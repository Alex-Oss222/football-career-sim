## January Staff Review: Three-page system

**Design only.** These files are proposals for football-career-sim; they do not change a canonical game, job, contract, calendar, or personnel decision.

**Cycle illustrated:** Review of the 2014 Jacksonville Jaguars coaching staff, carried out on dates actually established after Jacksonville's last game in the 2014 NFL season. Relevant work may occur in January or February 2015. Do not invent a review day from the month name, or treat January 1 as an assistant's proven contract-expiration day.

### Documents

The page names are the registry's (`runtime/decisions.py`, `january_staff`), and each file name comes from `decisions.file_name`: `Staff_Status_Report.md`, `My_Staff_Decisions.md` and `Staff_Moves.md`.

1. [`staff_status_report_template.md`](../foundation/templates/january_staff/staff_status_report_template.md): the general manager and the coordinators present verified staff responsibilities, evidence, contracts and open questions. It is a staff status report, not a new 53-player season review.
2. [`my_staff_decisions_template.md`](../foundation/templates/january_staff/my_staff_decisions_template.md): Stone's fixed-heading fill-in page, his one-time answer to the Staff Status Report. A decision to pursue renewal is not an agreed contract.
3. [`staff_moves_template.md`](../foundation/templates/january_staff/staff_moves_template.md): the January phase's closing account and **index** of completed independent staff results, not a pooled substitute for them.
4. [`staff_result_template.md`](../foundation/templates/january_staff/staff_result_template.md): the staff form of the shared [event result](../foundation/templates/event_inserts/event_result_template.md), one separate, dated reader-facing outcome for **each resolved staff event**. A completed renewal, rejected renewal offer, termination, contract lapse, interview permission disposition, resignation, hire or material role change gets its own event result if it actually occurs. It keeps every item the shared result requires, including the event ID in machine metadata and the event-record line, so one template owns each staff event.
5. This document: authority, dates, routing, immutable issuance, event records, status transitions and tests.
6. [`january_staff_2014_into_2015.md`](examples/january_staff_2014_into_2015.md): the real current staff as recorded October 5, 2014; unresolved 2014-season football; no invented future contract or employment outcome.

### Place in the season

The 2014 team's football season review and coach exit interviews belong to the 2014 `Season_Review/`, and from 2014 on so do the January ownership review and the Club Decision on Stone's employment ([season end design](season_end.md)): they are the closing season's record. The **January 2015** staff phase pages (`Staff_Status_Report.md`, `My_Staff_Decisions.md`, `Staff_Moves.md`, and the phase's `My_Responses.md` for later inserts) live in `career/2015/Early_Offseason/`, the registry's `early_offseason` place, with links back to the 2014 exit-interview evidence. Each per-event staff result lives in `career/2015/Team_Operations/Staff/` (the folder map's `team/coaching_staff` record) and is linked from Staff Moves. Contract terms stay only in the coach records under `data/people/coaches/` and the payroll view built from them. The January 2015 carousel method and draw records, and any search that a real vacancy opens, live in `career/2015/Early_Offseason/staff_changes/` (a search at `staff_changes/<vacancy-key>/`, through the folder map; [staff vacancy design](staff_vacancy.md)). The frozen 2014 records in `career/2014/Early_Offseason/staff_changes/` (`timeline.md`, `hires.md`) are history and are never appended to. The current repository has not yet created the 2015 `Early_Offseason` directory; its implementation must route through `SeasonPaths` rather than hard-coding a raw path or prematurely switching the active playing season.

### Behavior

- Stone is the final staff-selection authority under his Jacksonville appointment (Authority Map row 2), within the promised but unquantified assistant budget. Caldwell confirms contract funding and related administration (row 6). No one can sign the coach's acceptance on his behalf.
- Review only after the 2014 Club Decision retains Stone as Jacksonville's head coach. If he is dismissed, he does not decide Jacksonville's next staff: David Caldwell answers for Jacksonville on open staff items, each answer recorded as his, until a successor head coach holds row 2, and renewals stay open for that successor.
- Only concluded events get separate individual outputs. Open proposals retain dated status and a link; a plan to retain nine coaches does not fabricate nine signed renewals.
- A staff review does not pre-decide the next `Candidate Report → My Hire → Staff Hire` workflow for genuine vacancies.
- Absent a staff concern, it is legitimate to keep the current duties intact. No praise/criticism quota, staff grade, or forced turnover.
- Who calls each phase is not a review question. The current delegation in the job record continues unless Stone changes it; the brief states the callers as a fact.

## A. Why the workflow exists

The *Staff Status Report* presents information from the general manager and the coordinators, the individual coach records, contracts, interviews and on-field evidence. Stone's *My Staff Decisions* supplies only his authorized actions. *Staff Moves* is an index/status account; every separate resolved coaching event has its own dated result. The status report is not an auto-written endorsement of incumbents and the result index is not a second contract owner.

**Scope:** Evaluating the current staff, deciding football assignments, pursuing retention/nonrenewal and confirming actual vacancies. The separate `Candidate Report → My Hire → Staff Hire` workflow owns a new hiring search after a real opening arises; Caldwell brings its Candidate Report, with the affected unit's coordinator as adviser. Head-coach employment is settled first by Khan and Caldwell. This review is not the team season-review report, player assessments or the staff exit-interview transcript.

**Era-specific point:** For 2014-15, an assistant seeking a head-coach job was different from an assistant seeking another assistant/coordinator job (rules T1 to T3). Moves from position coach to coordinator were not automatically protected; NFL recognition of bona fide coordinator promotions came later, by resolution of May 19, 2020 (T4). A nominal title or a play-calling promise gave no protected status (T5). The Rooney Rule had not been expanded to coordinator searches in 2013 (R3), and the 2014-15 method must not enforce later requirements. Consult the documented period-specific interview windows and the separately sourced 2014-15 calendar ([career/2015/Calendar.md](../career/2015/Calendar.md): bye clubs' assistants through the Wild Card games of January 4, Wild Card winners' through the Divisional games of January 11, a Super Bowl assistant's second interview by January 25). An outside coach's 2015 employment starts from his last verified job before the career's divergence plus this career's own events; no real 2015-cycle staff news is read.

**Jacksonville's permission rule.** The job record holds the user's standing instruction `jacksonville-assistant-interview-permission` ([state/current_job.json](../state/current_job.json), given October 20, 2026, in force from the October 5, 2014 position): grant an assistant's head-coach interview and a position coach's coordinator interview; refuse a move to the same title elsewhere; alike whatever the asking club. It answers a request without asking Stone, and the request and Jacksonville's answer are FOR YOUR INFORMATION. It applies only after that season's league rules: a head-coach request cannot be refused once Jacksonville's season is over (T2); a live club's assistant may be interviewed only for a head-coach job and only inside the windows (W4, W5, W9); lateral talks with a live club's assistant wait until that club is eliminated (W10); and no hire is made while the employer club is alive (X1). For a request the instruction does not name, this design's rule is: a real step up the staff ladder (assistant to position coach, position coach to coordinator, any assistant to head coach) is granted; a nominal title at the same level (such as passing-game coordinator or assistant head coach for a position coach) counts as lateral and is refused; a different position at the same level goes to Stone as a staff-permission item. The frozen January 2014 default in `data/2014/Early_Offseason/staff_changes/carousel_method.json` (stage 4) is 2014 history; it closes nothing in 2015.

## D. Format and validation

### Staff Status Report

- Built or issued from current, date-appropriate coach terms, salary, authority, documented role work and actual season results. Displays entire staff **once** in its contract-control table.
- Comes from the job record's parties: Caldwell for contracts and budget, the coordinators for football work on their own units' position coaches. No football-operations author is named, because no record holds one.
- Separately highlights only coaches with real expiry issues, performance concerns, legitimate revised assignments or genuine outside contacts. No mandatory positive and negative entries per coach, number of paragraphs, interview quotes or constructed disagreement.
- Meaningful evidence points to actual film/practice/game/event entries and identifies what was assigned, what was observed and what is inferred. The report must not invent a missing protection diagnosis, staff conversation, coaching motive or future real-world hire.
- States who calls and who prepares each phase as a fact from the job record. Calling never appears under "Decisions requiring attention" unless Stone raised it.
- Puts an event under ACTION REQUIRED only when it needs Stone's answer: a request outside the standing instruction's cases, a renewal offer or counter needing his direction, an urgent expiration, a real vacancy, or an extension offer to Stone himself. A request the standing instruction answers goes under FOR YOUR INFORMATION with Jacksonville's answer and its result link. No fake offers from other teams. The date of issue cannot precede a result it quotes.

### My Staff Decisions

- Exactly the fixed numbered headings from the template. Parse each explicit coach-specific action and verify current employment, control, contract status and stated contingencies.
- `No change` is valid for existing job assignments and contracts still in force. For an expiring contract it is insufficient to create a renewal; name pursue, do not pursue, lawful lapse or a pending fact.
- A shared action (e.g. 'Pursue renewal for all nine expiring assistants') may be compiled to the nine named individuals **only** when the explicit target set and conditions are unambiguous and shown to Stone for validation. It yields nine independent negotiation matters, never nine automatic signatures.
- Defer/awaiting information is meaningful and remains **open** with reason and applicable deadline; a blank field or silence never means approval, denial or waiver. If a deadline has real independent effect, resolve the clock according to the actual governing mechanism, not an invented agent assent.
- **One exception to "blank is unanswered":** the calling lines in section 4 are optional. A line left out means the current delegation in `state/current_job.json` continues, and no calling question is raised.
- A changed caller or role is a separate authorized football action with dated update to `state/current_job.json` and relevant coach records. Its effects are not inferred from a renewal.
- **A play-caller who leaves.** Stone takes interim direction of that phase automatically on the departure, with no clock stop. Calls Stone held only as interim cover pass to the new coach on his effective date unless My Hire says otherwise; calls Stone held before the vacancy stay with him.
- My Staff Decisions is a one-time answer to the Staff Status Report. An event raised after it is answered on the phase's one `My_Responses.md` in `career/2015/Early_Offseason/`. No separate answer page for every assistant.

### Staff Moves and separate event outputs

- Render the phase's one Staff Moves index from immutable individual concluded events. Index can be revised as new events occur but cannot be primary employment evidence.
- Each finalized individual event creates exactly one reader-facing result, in `Team_Operations/Staff/`, including a declined renewal, expired contract at a verified time, permitted/denied interview, release, resignation, material reassignment or actual hiring. A formal continuation with unchanged terms may also warrant a result if it was explicitly raised as a consequential decision. Unchanged coaches who never had a separate event do not receive fake 'retained' transaction files.
- Each result carries its event ID in machine metadata and its event-record line, so the year's Record.md lists it in football words.
- A counteroffer, revised terms and meeting updates belonging to the **same open negotiation** are versioned process facts, not repeatedly final results. Separate distinct requests from two outside clubs each get their own outcomes.
- A vacancy resulting from an event leads to Candidate Report later; do not record a hired replacement here before an actual offer is accepted.
- A proposed contract creates no new coach employment entry, no scheduled salary, and no fake cap charge; on actual execution update coach record and the coaching payroll. Coaching salaries do **not** consume the player salary cap.
