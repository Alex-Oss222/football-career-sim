# 2014-2015 NFL Draft research: law, contemporaneous process and information gates

**Scope:** Historical scheduling, the 2011 CBA, clubs' contemporary scouting and decision practices, and the Jacksonville career's separately established 2014 draft precedent. This is an implementation research reference, **not** an in-career scouting report or a license to use real future Jaguars acquisitions. The real NFL dates and law are permitted history; the real Jaguars' 2015 picks, positions and finished season are not Stone-career facts.

### Sources whose facts are **not** transferable into the career

Contemporary Jacksonville reports discuss the real 2014 general manager with head coach Gus Bradley, the real Jaguars' No. 3 2014 selection, Blake Bortles, and the real 2014 draft class. They confirm **how professional scouting was practiced**, not who Alex Stone hired, who Caldwell selected in this fictional career, how its room voted or what real opponents the branch played. Those actual personnel transactions diverge from January 2013 onward and are excluded as factual career inputs. Likewise, the real 2015 NFL draft's Jacksonville No. 3 pick and real draft-day results cannot set the simulated club's 2015 selection order, scouting opinions or roster.

Some sources published **after** the in-career October 5, 2014 clock are legal/historical research aids to a design authored in 2026. They cannot be portrayed as information Stone received on October 5. The same goes for later accounts of a prior date: later verification proves when the event happened, but not when it was disclosed to the team at the earlier date.

## Period-correct scouting and decision process

**College scouting:** Area and national scouting should contribute genuine observations from college assignments, competition, event timing, athletic testing, medical and personal-background information where the club actually obtained it. A source must identify at least the game, workout or publication sufficient for the assertion. Never write a generic scout quote or private team interview the records do not establish.

**Role projection by position coaches:** An offensive tackle's third-down pass-set depth and inside-recovery ability, a guard's double-team fit and pass-off, a running back's protection declaration and pickup, a receiver's route-landmark discipline versus specific coverage, a defensive lineman's front assignment, or a corner's help/leverage rules constitute useful football questions. Tie them to the actual college evidence before describing a likely transfer. The result is a proposed first job and a test, **not** a hidden talent score, universal college-to-NFL conversion or guaranteed skill gain.

**Caldwell's decision room:** His final board may rank players differently from Stone's recommendation. Historical front-office discussion can use scenario planning, pick availability and trade compensation; disagreement does not require dramatic dialogue. A required Stone franchise-QB consultation is an actual written football projection and discussion before a leading quarterback commitment, not a permanent veto.

**Timing:** The college scouting board changes as legal eligibility becomes known, combine data emerges, pro days occur, clubs conduct their own verified work, and the league's selections resolve. Every release to Stone is dated. The presence of a final 2015 board in research materials does not authorize reading it during an October 2014 meeting.

**Undrafted recruiting:** Close the league draft before treating a player as undrafted. The prospect can choose another team, refuse, negotiate, withdraw or remain unsigned. An ignored offer is not a signed rookie. Each genuinely closed pursuit needs its own dated output regardless of success.

## 2014 Jacksonville career precedent: what is canon, what is not generalized

The branch completed its 2014 draft May 8-10 with nine Jacksonville selections. It had already acquired Washington's 2014 first and Arizona's 2014 second, while Washington held Jacksonville's original second. Its actual pick numbers and selected players belong solely to **2014**, and its tenable method for year-specific order/compensatory picks must not become a 2015 default.

Relevant practical tests established by that record:

- **Exhausted board at No. 205:** E. J. Gaines' real No. 188 selection and Ross Cockrell's real No. 109 selection made both unavailable at Jacksonville No. 205. The lack of a third instruction caused a new Stone recommendation of Jemea Thomas. Caldwell selected Thomas at No. 205. That must not be silently replaced with a fabricated third choice in future drafts.
- **No prearranged trade:** The 2014 board authorized no additional draft-day trades and the draft completed without them. A 2015 trade cannot be introduced for entertainment.
- **Rights versus executed contracts:** Nine drafted players were actually signed May 11, the day after the final draft round, not on their selection dates.
- **Competing undrafted offers:** The branch called 24 named players after the draft, signed 17, had six declines and held James Hurst without calling. A declined pursuit and a hold have different dispositions. The exact recruiting terms and medically necessary hold remain in the original May 10 record.
- **Historical rails collision:** The ordinal swap sent real Jaguars selections to the real recipient clubs of the branch choices where possible. It left Aaron Colvin unplaced on a self-pair and did not duplicate branch-owned Telvin Smith to Green Bay. Both are explicit precedent for future unique-identity checks.

### Rules and uncertainties not to bury

1. The present repo's 2014 compensatory formula is a **simulation method**, not a public reconstruction of the NFL Management Council's unpublished valuation. 2015 needs its own fixed-before-decision method. Treat exact resulting compensatory picks as unresolved until the branch has a defensible method and complete year-specific inputs.
2. The historical availability condition (real selection at or after N, or undrafted) deliberately uses **future real-draft metadata as a hidden constrained eligibility check**. That narrow user-approved exception is not permission to use later performance as a college scout's evaluation. The provenance and restrictions must be testable.
3. The exact timing of when Jacksonville's real 2015 rookie minicamp schedule became known remains unverified. The researched May 8-10 dates can be used as calendar rails, but an October 2014 coach-facing report cannot cite a future May announcement as if already communicated.
4. The NFL's draft clock is real; this user's Markdown interaction is not a timed live NFL card submission. Choose and test the treatment of between-pick pauses and approved fallback rules before claiming live clock realism.
5. A club's actual top-30 visits, prospect interviews, medical board, staff poll or in-house videotape must have a contemporaneous record to appear in the branch. General contemporary reports establish how clubs may operate, not that Stone's Jaguars conducted a specific interview.
6. The precise chronology of late-draft contracting and participation remains case-specific: an assigned slot, tendered contract, signed contract, official clearance, and permission to join a later football period are distinct facts, not one automatic event.

## Verification method and limitations

The source check compared original 2011 CBA language, the NFL's October 2014 site/date announcement, the NFL's January and March 2015 league bulletins, independent April 29/30 2015 schedule reports, and contemporaneous 2013-14 Jacksonville front-office interviews. It separately reconciled the repo's 2014 completed records, 2015 date reference, 2014 active career and general decision registries. This was a **design/research check**, not an executed playthrough, medical examination, scouting database build or repository test run. A future additional private team information source cannot be assumed merely because NFL clubs sometimes possessed it.
