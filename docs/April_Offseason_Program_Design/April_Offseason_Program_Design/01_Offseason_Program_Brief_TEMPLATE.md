| **First OTA / Phase Three: [date when legitimately confirmed]** | First opportunity for the specified non-contact team offense-versus-defense work | Do not quietly insert these reps into the April program. The OTA plan has its own later decision and report. |

The schedule must have been filed with the NFL and NFLPA by the applicable agreed deadline, no later than 21 days before its start; the actual submission and any changes must be confirmed by club operations. During ordinary non-OTA, non-minicamp program days, at most four hours at the facility on at most four weekdays, at most 90 minutes on the field, with at most two specifically directed hours of attendance. Staff meetings, treatment, individual work and coaches' requests must fit those limits. Club operations and the performance staff will monitor compliance.
