# April program: rigorous assessment and implementation blockers

**Status:** Review of `Alex-Oss222/football-career-sim` at its committed 2014 Week 5 checkpoint, based on read-only inspection of the GitHub `main` tree and contemporary rules. No GitHub change or simulated career outcome has been made. This document is an engineering/research assessment, not an in-world head-coach briefing.

## The design that holds up

The proposed three-page April workflow already exists by exact name in `runtime/decisions.py`, so inventing a new public structure would be needless. The intended workflow must cover the first **five weeks** of the nine-week voluntary program: two weeks of Phase One, three weeks of Phase Two. Phase Three, OTAs, the mandatory minicamp and the rookie minicamp belong to their separate owners. The calendar heading “April” is when the program begins, not when a five-week report must magically be completed.

The repository's 2014 Phase One/Two reports are unusually specific football evidence. For example, the first Boot Flood free-edge meeting produced conflicting answers; Bates and Tice reconciled the instruction and the April 29 different-diagram explanation was consistent. The QB/center/back double-A-gap point-reset cues were brought into agreement April 24. The subsequent **unopposed** Phase Two movement provides a different kind of evidence, but still not a real rush test. Crennel's team worked on defined coverage/check communication, and Westhoff kept punt operation, kick and coverage distinct. These are usable examples because the original dated branch records say they occurred. They are not a menu of mandated 2015 coaching successes.

The staff/GM division is equally clear. Stone can specify practice priorities, teaching emphasis, staff structure inside his budget, and role/depth decisions when those choices actually arise. Caldwell decides player contracts, draft selections, trades and acquisitions. Medical personnel decide clearance and limits. Stone's special franchise-quarterback input is written consultation, not approval rights. A practice plan does not supersede anyone's authority. Stone's existing four-year fully guaranteed head-coach agreement, game-call arrangement and standing practice-lead instruction must not be recreated annually or reset.

The existing `docs/event_inserts.md` rule is also settled: any event that actually reaches disposition gets **one separate individual dated output**, including failed or declined negotiations. A Phase One/Two report is not the owner of a contract decision; the monthly Program Report is not the owner of a rejected trade. That requires integration, not a revised canon decision.

## Corrections required by actual period rules

1. **Phase One classroom teaching and field workouts are different legal activities.** Contemporary Chiefs and Packers sources support classroom football coaching after the program begins. The 2011 CBA restricts field activity to qualifying strength/conditioning work and rehabilitation. There is no ordinary Phase One coached punt operation or snapper-kicker session. Stone's April 18, 2014 statement suggesting that narrow field work was lawful is not sufficient to override the CBA. The existing completed 2014 record correctly kept its Phase One kicking and snapping teaching in the room. Preserve his football intention while enforcing the lawful boundary.
2. **Phase Two still has no opposed football.** Coaches may legally teach individual and separate-unit football on the field. A draw of a changing front or a different formation is not a real pass rush, contested receiver release or tackling situation. Do not treat correct assignments on air as improved player ability or proof of competitive execution.
3. **The program is voluntary.** Its meetings and physical work are not tests of willingness to participate. A player who does not attend cannot be graded as disloyal or penalized through an attendance-based role conclusion. Legal participant eligibility is a separate question: an unsigned tender or expired contract is not evidence that the person signed an Offseason Workout and Minicamp Participation Agreement.
4. **Dates are for the relevant NFL year.** 2014 returning-HC start April 21, 2015 returning-HC start April 20, with a 21-day-before-start outer limit on calendar filing. For April 20, 2015 the outer deadline is March 30. The actual NFL/NFLPA mutually agreed submission date and a particular Jacksonville filing confirmation are not established. An archived NFL announcement published May 15 cannot, by itself, establish what an April 20 briefing could have quoted as public knowledge. A genuinely filed and communicated **internal** schedule may establish internal knowledge if its original dated source exists.

## Engineering blockers and their significance

| Gap | What inspection shows | Required response before treating workflow as implemented |
|---|---|---|
| Three-page orchestration | `runtime/decisions.py` registers `offseason_program` with `implemented=False`; no working April open/answer/close path was demonstrated | Build actual issuance, user decision, legal session application, completed close and derived report. Do not claim implementation from the registry alone. |
| Action gating | `runtime/decision_queue.py` knows `practice_plan` but its present blockers do not hold a future April practice start or a concluded plan by themselves | Add narrow phase-specific dependencies and validate standing instructions. Do not block unrelated draft/Caldwell work. |
| 2015 source of truth | 2015 calendar exists, but branch 2014 has played only Week 5; no 2015 current signed roster, completed January review or actual medical status follows from this | Require season handoff/activation plus up-to-date roster, signed participant eligibility and real medical permissions. |
| Schedule compliance | CBA controls activities and hours, but a confirmed 2015 actual filing and day-by-day session plan were not found in the active career | Separate scheduled from actually held. Validate 4 weekdays/week, 4 hours/day at facility, 2 specified hours, 90-minute field limit and coach/phase restrictions per player. Do not fabricate filing proof. |
| Credible observations | Existing 2014 training reports are completed records, not a demonstrated general 2015 simulation evaluator | Require actual dated opportunity, legal mode, assignment, participant, coached correction if recorded, later retest only if done and stated evidence limits. Do not roll generic ratings or forced improvements. |
| Program Report duplication | Existing `docs/season_structure.md` gives Phase One and Phase Two one authoritative `staff_plan.md` and one `training_report.md` each | Build `Program_Report.md` as a sourced readable synthesis, never an editable competing evaluation. |
| Individual closed events | Existing distributed event metadata and action-insert templates exist, but April-phase event closure wiring was not demonstrated | Create one dated readable output **immediately when each distinct matter closes**, at its appropriate medical, staff, trade or contract owner. Failed matters count. Report only indexes. |
| Issued briefing history | `docs/event_inserts.md` requires preserving each originally issued brief | Store source cutoff and issue record, publish new edition when genuine facts change; never replace what Stone originally saw. |
| Public page hygiene | `runtime/clean_pages.py` prohibits technical IDs, system terms, code links and policy chatter in rebuilt career pages | Keep provenance and technical controls out of coach prose, but preserve machine-owned event metadata. |
| Accepted validation | Current repository test suite primarily proves the existing game/preseason and other modules; April tests have not been implemented or run | Add and execute the **46 proposed tests** in the specification plus repository validation before claiming production readiness. |

The last point is consequential: a plausible Markdown report is not a tested offseason simulation. In particular, no means for independent, protagonist-blind **non-game observations** has been demonstrated for the new workflow. Do not introduce arbitrary scores or a hidden “drills generate player improvement” rule to fill that gap. Until an evidence-producing mechanism is defined and validated, the program may report actual lawful teaching and dated corroborated observations, but cannot claim player progression from design intent.

## Final limits of knowledge

The 2015 actual real Jaguars held nine of ten announced OTA practices, according to the club's June 18 report. That is evidence about **the real-world club**, not about whether Stone in this career later cancels a practice. The March 2015 Jacksonville personnel status, May 2015 participant list and 2014 season final record do not currently exist in the simulated career. Keep those fields unfilled. Other-club historical personnel rails are a narrowly defined exception, not authority to import any real-world Jacksonville move or future result.

The finished deliverable is a **template and implementation specification package**, not a season output or a claim that code was changed. The first next design task on the six-workflow list is the **NFL Draft**. Its staff report, user recommendations and results should be researched independently rather than prematurely mixed into the April program.
