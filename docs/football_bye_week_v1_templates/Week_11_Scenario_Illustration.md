# Week 11, 2014: what a credible bye page should do

**This is a worked illustration of format and information boundaries, not an issued briefing or a canonical Week 11 decision.** Week 10 has not been played in the current record reviewed for this design. The real 2014 Jaguars' Week 10 and Week 12 outcomes must **never** substitute for this career's generated outcomes.

## Situation already known from the published schedule

- Week 10: November 9, Jacksonville's designated-home game against Dallas at Wembley Stadium, London.
- Week 11: November 10-16, no Jacksonville game.
- Week 12: November 23, Jacksonville at Indianapolis.
- Four consecutive mandatory player days off must include Saturday and Sunday under the 2011 CBA. For illustration only, Thursday, November 13 through Sunday, November 16 would meet the basic requirement for this Sunday Week 12 schedule. The coach must choose and record the actual lawful dates.

## A credible staff briefing entry

**Issue:** Our ability to protect the ball when possessions reach scoring territory.

**Actual older evidence available even now:** In the closed Week 5 Pittsburgh game, Jacksonville committed four turnovers, including an interception on the possession following its recovery at Pittsburgh's 17 and a fumble at the Pittsburgh 20 in the fourth quarter. The score and event details are in the Week 5 game record.

**What cannot yet be stated:** That turnovers remained a trend through Week 10; that a named Week 10 player has an injury; that a new protection or coverage error caused the play; or that today's film proved the fix.

**Question for the Week 11 staff:** Across **all** closed games through Week 10, separate the turnover events by down, situation, concept and any *recorded* read, protection or ball-security observation. Is there a common football problem, or were these separate events? When unknown, bring the play and the question, not a manufactured cause.

## How Stone can answer

**Keep:** The current taught base offense unless the actual Week 10 self-scout establishes a material reason to alter it.

**Correct:** If the actual staff cut-up confirms a repeated situation, designate an eligible meeting or practice to rehearse the assignment that needs correction; name the player's job, defense/look and pass/fail check. Otherwise no permanent change.

**Work window:** Preserve the players' required consecutive days off. Staff film and scouting may proceed separately. Medical treatment is controlled by the medical staff.

**Into Week 12:** Tell Tice and Crennel to prepare Indianapolis material using available, dated game information. Do not freeze specific Colts calls, medical assumptions or the Game Plan until the Week 12 briefing.

## How the result might read without inventing work

> The staff completed its self-scout using the games on file through the Dallas game. Stone carried the base offensive structure and assigned further turnover review to the offensive staff; the named corrective practice was not completed before the players' scheduled break. No depth change was ordered. The players' four consecutive days off were observed. The club's record was unchanged because Jacksonville did not play. The specific correction will be evaluated during Week 12 preparation.

This is a **hypothetical writing example**. It can become a real result only if those events and dates actually occurred and were recorded. A different simulated Week 10 game might justify entirely different football work, or no change at all.
