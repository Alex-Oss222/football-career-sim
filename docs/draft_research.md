#### Sources whose facts are **not** transferable into the career

Contemporary Jacksonville reports discuss the real 2014 general manager with head coach Gus Bradley, the real Jaguars' No. 3 2014 selection, Blake Bortles, and the real 2014 draft class. They confirm **how professional scouting was practiced**, not who Alex Stone hired, who Caldwell selected in this fictional career, how its room voted or what real opponents the branch played. Those actual personnel transactions diverge from January 2013 onward and are excluded as factual career inputs. Likewise, the real Jaguars' 2015 draft slot and real draft-day results cannot set the simulated club's 2015 selection order, scouting opinions or roster.

Some sources published **after** the in-career October 5, 2014 clock are legal/historical research aids to a design authored in 2026. They cannot be portrayed as information Stone received on October 5. The same goes for later accounts of a prior date: later verification proves when the event happened, but not when it was disclosed to the team at the earlier date.

### Period-correct scouting and decision process

**College scouting:** Area and national scouting should contribute genuine observations from college assignments, competition, event timing, athletic testing, medical and personal-background information where the club actually obtained it. A source must identify at least the game, workout or publication sufficient for the assertion. Never write a generic scout quote or private team interview the records do not establish, and never name a scout as an author unless he is a party in the job record.

**Role projection by position coaches:** An offensive tackle's third-down pass-set depth and inside-recovery ability, a guard's double-team fit and pass-off, a running back's protection declaration and pickup, a receiver's route-landmark discipline versus specific coverage, a defensive lineman's front assignment, or a corner's help/leverage rules constitute useful football questions. Tie them to the actual college evidence before describing a likely transfer. The result is a proposed first job and a test, **not** a hidden talent score, universal college-to-NFL conversion or guaranteed skill gain.

**Caldwell's decision room:** His final board may rank players differently from Stone's recommendation. Historical front-office discussion can use scenario planning, pick availability and trade compensation; disagreement does not require dramatic dialogue. Stone's franchise-quarterback consultation is an actual written football projection and a meeting before Caldwell acts on any of the job record's four row 3a matters (the first-round pick used on a quarterback, premium draft capital traded for one, a major multiyear commitment to one as the intended starter, a major disposition of the starting quarterback), not a permanent veto.

**Timing:** The college scouting board changes as legal eligibility becomes known, combine data emerges, pro days occur, clubs conduct their own verified work, and the league's selections resolve. Every release to Stone is dated. The presence of a final 2015 board in research materials does not authorize reading it during an October 2014 meeting.

**Undrafted recruiting:** Close the league draft before treating a player as undrafted. The prospect can choose another team, refuse, negotiate, withdraw or remain unsigned. An ignored offer is not a signed rookie. Each genuinely closed pursuit needs its own dated output regardless of success.

### 2014 Jacksonville career precedent: what is canon, what is not generalized

The branch completed its 2014 draft May 8-10 with nine Jacksonville selections. It had already acquired Washington's 2014 first and Arizona's 2014 second, while Washington held Jacksonville's original second. Its actual pick numbers and selected players belong solely to **2014**, and its tenable method for year-specific order/compensatory picks must not become a 2015 default without being restated with 2015 inputs and dates (the [design](draft.md), section D).

Relevant practical tests established by that record:

- **Exhausted board at No. 205:** E. J. Gaines' real No. 188 selection and Ross Cockrell's real No. 109 selection made both unavailable at Jacksonville No. 205. The lack of a third instruction stopped the pick for a new Stone recommendation, Jemea Thomas. Caldwell selected Thomas at No. 205. That must not be silently replaced with a fabricated third choice in future drafts.
- **No prearranged trade:** The 2014 board authorized no additional draft-day trades and the draft completed without them. A 2015 trade cannot be introduced for entertainment.
- **Rights versus executed contracts:** Nine drafted players were actually signed May 11, the day after the final draft round, not on their selection dates.
- **Competing undrafted offers:** The branch called 23 of the 24 names on the board after the draft, signed 17, had six declines and held James Hurst without calling. A declined pursuit and a hold have different dispositions. The exact recruiting terms and medically necessary hold remain in the original May 10 record. Those calls were resolved by judgment, a one-off; 2015 uses a market draw (the [design](draft.md), section F).
- **Historical rails collision:** The ordinal swap sent real Jaguars selections to the real recipient clubs of the branch choices where possible. It left Aaron Colvin unplaced on a self-pair and did not duplicate branch-owned Telvin Smith to Green Bay. Both are explicit precedent for future unique-identity checks.

#### Rules and uncertainties not to bury

1. The present repo's 2014 compensatory formula is a **simulation method**, not a public reconstruction of the NFL Management Council's unpublished valuation. 2015 restates it (value points, version 2) with 2014 league-year inputs and 2015 dates, committed before March 23, 2015 (the [design](draft.md), section D). Treat exact resulting compensatory picks as unresolved until that method and its complete year-specific inputs exist.
2. The historical availability condition (real selection at or after N, or undrafted) deliberately uses **future real-draft metadata as a hidden constrained eligibility check**. That narrow user-approved exception is not permission to use later performance as a college scout's evaluation. The provenance and restrictions must be testable.
3. The exact timing of when Jacksonville's real 2015 rookie minicamp schedule became known remains unverified. The researched May 8-10 dates can be used as calendar rails, but an October 2014 coach-facing report cannot cite a future May announcement as if already communicated.
4. The NFL's draft clock is real; this user's Markdown interaction is not a timed live NFL card submission. Settled by the lead: Stone's page is checked and frozen before the first round; a pick stops for him only when every player he named there is gone and his page gives no further instruction (unless it tells Caldwell to use his own board), as at No. 205 in 2014; no NFL clock time runs during a stop, and pages show the era's clock limits only.
5. A club's actual top-30 visits, prospect interviews, medical board, staff poll or in-house videotape must have a contemporaneous record to appear in the branch. General contemporary reports establish how clubs may operate, not that Stone's Jaguars conducted a specific interview.
6. The precise chronology of late-draft contracting and participation remains case-specific: an assigned slot, tendered contract, signed contract, official clearance, and permission to join a later football period are distinct facts, not one automatic event.

## NFL Draft redesign: repository assessment, conflicts and implementation blockers

### A. What is sound and should stay

**Closed football canon:** The 2014 draft is already executed and recorded. Nine selected players, their distinct draft dates, Caldwell's final authority, the No. 205 reopened Stone recommendation, May 11 rookie contracts, and actual 2014 undrafted calls are fixed past events. The redesigned draft **starts prospectively**. Nothing in a new template changes a 2014 result, adds a 2015 player or advances past October 5, 2014.

**Existing phase shape:** `runtime/decisions.py` already names the exact three pages and maps `draft` to the year-specific Draft folder. This design requires an implementation and tests, not another unrelated naming scheme or a separate questionnaire replacing the user's natural recommendation page.

**Single-ownership model:** `runtime/events.py`, `docs/season_structure.md`, `docs/rebuild_plan.md` and the existing generated annual record define how real events own their dated outcomes. `docs/event_inserts.md` and the new `EVENTS` registry define where genuine consequential business appears. A draft report may display a short summary and **link** to each independently resolved outcome, but never become its substitute.

**Head-coach powers:** `state/current_job.json`, the executed Stone contract and Document 3 already distinguish Stone's football recommendations from Caldwell's binding choices. The written-projection and meeting right on row 3a's four quarterback matters is narrower than a draft veto. No fake shared GM authority is necessary.

**Historical league rails:** The approved 2014 method already specifies availability-at-N, ordinal pairing with the real Jaguars' k-th selection, no duplicated Jaguars-controlled players and explicit unplaced counterparts. The 2014 completed pairing demonstrates the collision rules. They are not ordinary draft mechanics and must remain transparently labelled as this simulator's specific historical-personnel exception in the technical spec.

**Actual 2015 inventory:** As of the October 5 state, Jacksonville holds nine 2015 picks: its own second, third, fifth, sixth and seventh, Miami's third, Houston's fourth, Arizona's fourth and Seattle's seventh. Jacksonville's original first/fourth are gone. The own third, fifth, sixth and seventh are held with no transfer recorded, pending the 2015 all-club audit. These original-club identifiers are sufficient for current planning. Numbers cannot be honestly supplied yet.

### C. Genuine legal and design ambiguities

**1. Real clock versus asynchronous coach approval.** The original 2015 draft allotted 10/7/5/4 minutes depending on round and four minutes for compensatory choices. A written free-form decision page is not a live NFL phone-room interface. The **recommended alternative** is to obtain Stone's ranked preferences, trade ceilings, first-job projections and explicitly authorized fallback instructions before day one; show the true pick clock during play and prompt only for real exceptions. **Settled by the lead:** the page is frozen before the first round; a pick stops for Stone only when every player he named there is gone and his page gives no further instruction, unless it tells Caldwell to use his own board (the 2014 rule at No. 205); at a pick where he named no one, Caldwell selects by his frozen board method; no NFL clock time runs during a stop, and pages show the era's clock limits only. This applies the user's rule that only an answer due before the event it holds stops the clock.

**2. The real compensatory algorithm is not fully public at the chosen historical point.** The 2014 project already adopts a measured branch formula using signed contract values, playing time, honours, cancellations and league totals. That method is an approved *simulation approximation*, not proof of the league's secret weights. **Settled:** 2015 restates the value-points method, version 2, with 2014 league-year inputs and 2015 dates, committed before March 23, 2015. It cannot inherit real-world 2015 recipients. No silent replacement.

**3. Historical availability rule deliberately introduces a narrow future-fact dependency.** A 2015 player's actual real draft position can be consulted behind the scenes **only** to enforce availability-at-N under the user exception. This fact must not be shown in a January or April pre-draft talent discussion, or converted into a scouting evaluation. A separate history rails validator handles it.

**4. A specific prospective rookie's eligibility, interview and medical status must be independently established.** Even when a public prospect's real college identity is known in October 2014, declaring him NFL draft eligible ahead of the Jan 2015 process or naming his later measured combine performance is hindsight. No invented medical clearance. College scout film and professional role judgments can be authored only from actual available evidence.

**5. Who speaks for Stone when a pick is due.** The existing emergency-practice succession has no delegated draft-selection authority. Tice or Crennel cannot be made a draft-day proxy because one of them may run practice when Stone is unavailable. Caldwell can make his **own** draft decisions by his frozen board method where Stone named no one, but a pick that stops for Stone waits for his answer, and a missing Stone response to an actual mandatory franchise-QB consultation cannot be manufactured into consent.

**6. Per-event versus grouped historical outcomes.** The current 2014 selection/UDFA records are genuine completed history under their prior archival method. Adding nine retroactive individual result files now would create new authoritative-looking events or change the 2014 historical view, violating the Week 5 preservation rule. Make one dated result per pick and per closed recruiting/contract/trade matter **from the next implemented draft onward**. Do not fake new May 2014 outputs.

**7. Same-day completed pursuits and source-date precision.** The completed 2014 undrafted file establishes May 10 for many signings/declines, but not a minute-by-minute timestamp for each call. A prospective separate result in 2015 must use exactly the known resolution date and time if recorded, rather than invent one. The lack of a time does not erase the individual event.

**8. Unverified future transfers and staff remain open.** The 2015 draft may eventually involve a changed employment role, roster, medical situation, pick owner or signed contract as the career advances. No template can assume that Stone is still Jacksonville's coach in April 2015 solely because he had a four-year deal in October 2014; absent a future club decision the current contract stands, but new information must be checked at the actual draft issue time. **Settled:** if the club decision retains him, he gets the Jacksonville pages; in another job, that job's version with its scope; out of work, no Jacksonville brief or My page, and Caldwell drafts on his frozen method.

### D. Ownership and implementation order

1. Verify active current job, season and the frozen October 5, 2014 history; preserve 2014 draft and game records byte-identical.
2. Generalize year-local order and ownership *without* rewriting the 2014 draft scripts' historic output. Add full 2015 original-club transaction audit, conditions and compensatory method before any correct overall pick number can be published.
3. Establish dated 2015 prospect eligibility and genuinely supported scouting evidence. Keep the restricted historical real-pick availability metadata separate from exposed evaluation.
4. Implement issue/version history for the report, natural recommendation page and immutable recorded Stone response with known fallback/priority semantics and formal franchise-QB consultation gate.
5. Implement Caldwell's independent pick/negotiation decision from explicitly authorized inputs, draft-day trade constraints and the settled stop rule. Never ask the assistant to improvise a player or source result.
6. Implement one canonical per-selection event with its dated reader result, and event-specific failed and successful transaction/UDFA outputs through existing owners and `My_Responses` inserts.
7. Implement ordinal historic-pick pairing, missing partner behavior, legal rookie rights, subsequent financial execution and annual-record reconciliation.
8. Build the summary and new season handoff **from canonical results**; use the proper roster, medical and future program gates. Do not write outcomes during Markdown render or open.
9. Test on a synthetic year fixture, validate clean pages and file ownership, and check 2014 records plus Oct 5 2014 state remain unchanged. Run the actual repo test suite only in a future authorized implementation PR.

### E. Acceptance/validation position

The accompanying implementation contract lists **72 proposed regression tests** (64 uploaded, eight added by the process check), including no-hindsight, era clock, original asset lineage, tradeable assets, user authority, failed negotiations, per-player recruiting results, pick self-pairings, role separation, signed-contract effects, and preserving the 2014 archive. These are **not passed tests**. The Markdown package is design-only; it is neither a complete runnable draft scheduler nor evidence that GitHub has integrated the pages.

**Result of assessment:** The proposed three-page NFL Draft experience is feasible within the repository's established boundaries, but the 2015 selection runner, complete ownership, compensatory methodology, dated scouting and the stop rule's implementation are **implementation blockers**. Treating those missing pieces as “a language model will figure it out when we get there” would manufacture precisely the football realism and user agency the redesign is intended to protect.
