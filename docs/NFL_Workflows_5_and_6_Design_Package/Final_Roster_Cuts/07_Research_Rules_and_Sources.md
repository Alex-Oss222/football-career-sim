## Two independent verification passes and explicit conflict resolution

**Pass 1:** Read original Article 29/33 text, NFLPA's Aug 19, 2014 expansion announcement, league schedule bulletins and the original 2015 Jaguars reports. **Pass 2:** Check 75/53 clock times against different contemporary sources: Seahawks July 15 league announcement, Jaguars Sept 4/5 club news, Patriots and Vikings period accounts. The NFLPA agreement is the controlling source of the ten-player practice squad exception, not a modern website's current 16-player rules.

A Raiders-hosted 2015 league-date PDF excerpt surfaced a **6 p.m.** Saturday Sept 5 53-man time, in conflict with the concordant original 2015 Seahawks bulletin, Jaguars Sept 4/5 reports and other league-calendar transcriptions that say **4 p.m. ET**. The design uses **4 p.m. ET** and preserves the discrepancy for any future implementer checking a league-office original. It would be wrong to choose the conflicting hour silently or to use a 2016/2022 calendar. The injured-reserve designated-return first permitted window is Sep 6 after 4 p.m. ET per 2015 CBS, NBC and league calendar reproductions, notwithstanding an inconsistent Raiders scan showing Sep 8.

## Necessary football decisions, as opposed to roster paperwork

The 2015 Jaguars contemporary explanation of preserving a swing tackle illustrates a **real roster problem**, not a universal theory of who should be cut: an injury can increase the value of someone who can cover both tackle sides and keep the offense functional. Similar questions exist for center, quarterback, defensive communication, nickel/dime and core coverage. A fourth-team offensive receiver's summer catch count without exposure to coverage or returns cannot by itself decide his total roster worth. The simulator must show what job is lost with a particular cut, the evidence actually available and who has the authority.

Stone, under his negotiated head-coach role, decides coaching depth and game day. Caldwell decides player retention, contracts, trades and cutdown compliance. Medical staff control clearance. Other clubs' waiver claims, free-agent contracts and player acceptances are independent, not Stone's choices. A user recommendation may shape Caldwell's information, never grant him an unsigned contract by assertion.

## Error boundaries that materially affect results

- No real future Jaguars cuts, retirements (except the repository's explicit date-gated league-wide retirement exception), practice-squad names, draft or free-agency additions are copied into 2015 career personnel.
- Other 31 clubs' historical player movements follow the limited, dated rails. Jacksonville players are protected from overwriting; when they leave Jacksonville they take a real next move only if the same kind and window criteria are met, otherwise unplaced until an actual authorized move.
- 'Waived with injury' is not the same event as 'placed on injured reserve': a claim or failure to clear may intervene. Medical diagnosis is not a coach's narrative choice.
- The first 75 stage can produce **zero cuts**; it still requires a legal count. The final 53 is a roster limit, not a rigid requirement to carry a particular number of QBs, RBs, linebackers, corners or special teams types.
- A separate personal result is required for each independently concluded event, including a failed waiver claim, rejected trade, withdrawn offer or unsuccessful practice-squad negotiation. Final 53 is a linked current roster view, not an omnibus disposition report.
- New 2015 game-day active 46, practice-squad 10 and reserve rules must be the **2015** rules; no 2020/2022 roster elevations, 48-man game day or modern IR return mechanics.
