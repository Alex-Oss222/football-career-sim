**Historical illustration of the career as it was played, not a new personnel decision.** The 2014 Jacksonville results below were already closed before the October 5, 2014 live checkpoint. The 2015 league's dates are usable for future planning but the rest of 2014 and all 2015 transactions remain unplayed. Never import the real Gus Bradley Jaguars' August/September 2015 cuts into Stone's team.

## A. The two distinct 2014 stages

### August 26: the 75-player check

The 2014 branch had **74 players under signed contracts** plus **four unsigned tenders** (Cameron Bradfield, Toney Clemons, Mike Brown and Austin Pasztor), treated in that historical roster record as rights rather than signed Active List players. The 75-player limit was met at 74. Caldwell **made no release, waiver or reserve-list move** to meet that deadline. The 2014 four-game preseason continued, with game four against Atlanta on August 28.

This is important for the future design: a 'first cut' is a real counting and legal-compliance decision point even when no cut is needed. The simulation must never release one of Stone's players to make a report look busy. Rights to an unsigned player also do not establish that he participated in that preseason game.

### August 28–29: Atlanta and the actual cutdown briefing

Atlanta beat Jacksonville **31–13 in the simulated career's final preseason game**. The recorded game generated medical incidents for Hakeem Nicks, A.J. Bouye, Gator Hoskins and Jeris Pendleton. Nicks was out on a multi-week instruction, and the decisions could not lawfully treat his availability as if he had passed a future examination.

At the August 29 meeting, the staff carried Stone's existing roster/depth package to Caldwell. The original record contains no dialogue to reconstruct. The two technical concerns were the appropriate waived/injured route for Cameron Brate and Jerrell Jackson, and the practice-squad eligibility of the proposed alternates. Stone's football concerns still included the X receiver, guard combinations, pass-rush/contain, and the long snapper. The original 2014 position-battle cards each kept their own detailed practice and game evidence.

### August 31: separate waiver and practice-squad events

At the branch's **noon August 31** waiver result, the waived Jaguars players all cleared according to the career's recorded method. Brate and Jackson reverted to Reserve/Injured in their separate completed cases. Caldwell then signed eight eligible practice-squad players: **Tyler Bray, Richard Murphy, Matt Feiler, Tyler Shatley, Jackson Jeffcoat, Jerome Long, Jemea Thomas and Adrian Phillips**. Two of the permitted ten places remained vacant. These signings followed waiver clearance, eligibility and their actual simulated acceptances. A recommendation to pursue an alternate was not itself a signed player.

The practice-squad names are the recorded 2014 branch outcomes. They must **not** be used as the 2015 squad or as evidence that everyone waived elsewhere would clear waivers for Stone.

### What must remain blank on My Cut Recommendations

Stone has not instructed how to rank unknown 2015 candidates. ### Why the Final 53 cannot be filled in advance

Caldwell's 2015 action cannot be known, and the roster itself will depend on the unplayed remainder of 2014, the January staff and season review, March free agency, the April/May draft, roster additions and departures, preseason game participation and medical outcomes. A blank year-specific Final 53 is *design*, not a completed league submission. The 2015 NFL's real Jaguars cuts are no more valid as the branch's answer than the club's real 2014 NFL preseason score was the score in Stone's career.

## C. Individual outcomes required once 2015 reaches this phase

Suppose the club later independently resolves three different matters on one day: a player waives/clears, a separate trade inquiry is rejected, and a third player declines a proposed practice-squad agreement. That is a **scenario to test the workflow, not 2015 career history**. It requires three separately dated outcomes, at roster, trade and contract owners respectively, plus links in the cutdown view. The Final 53 may report that none of those later outcomes modified a player's role, but can never serve as their only written result.

The 2014 worked record illustrates the mechanism already used in that season. No new result, waiver draw, injury return, role change or cap transaction has been created here.
