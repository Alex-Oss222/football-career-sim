## Period-specific rules to implement, not just cite

**Maximum four preseason games:** Article 23 §7; a Hall of Fame fifth applies only to a participating club. Jacksonville's 2015 schedule lists four. No game fifth is created to fill the calendar.

**Practice is separate from games:** Article 23's one-padded-practice and field-hour constraints apply to practice plans; the preseason game is a separate scheduled competition. The game-review writer cannot turn an unobserved padded practice into evidence merely because a full-contact exhibition was played.

**Exhibition participation:** Before the applicable roster cut, the camp/offseason eligible player list, medical restrictions and team-specific actual game actives control who may be used. Do not apply the regular-season 46-man game-day active limit automatically to the preseason. After Sep 1, game-four eligibility must reflect the 75 limit and any legitimately applicable roster exemptions, not a saved 90-man sheet.

**Medical and independent actor boundaries:** An injury is recognized at the game's actual time; a replacement that belongs to Stone pauses when material. Diagnosis, restrictions and clearance come from the appropriate medical authority. A roster-list response or replacement acquisition requires Caldwell's own dated action. A real 2015 Jaguars player cannot enter Stone's 2015 roster because the real Jaguars later signed him.

**Playing rules:** The May 19, 2015 PAT adoption is materially important. Test line of scrimmage, defense return possibility, two-point try location, and scorer credit independently of the score narrative. Never carry the first-two-week 2014 PAT trial forward as a 2015 blanket rule.

**Information cutoff:** A 2015 preseason game brief may refer to legitimate game-date opponent pro scouting and actual 2015 club sources *only* by the relevant dates; the stored historical results of the real 2015 games are not scouting evidence. NFL team media sometimes retroactively show modern names for Washington or venues; use 2015 Redskins, FedEx Field, EverBank Field.

## Research uncertainties / limitations

1. **Opposing preseason participation:** The league schedule says who plays whom, not which real-life opponent starters or camp free agents would have dressed in this career. The 2014 stored opponent-camp depth evidence cannot be extrapolated to 2015.
2. **Recorded film:** The 2011 CBA contains a club-recording requirement, but it does not give this simulator a specific film finding. A box score is not video review.
3. **2015 preseason release readiness:** The existing 2014 implementation and its PAT tests cannot certify a playable 2015 service. That is a real integration blocker.
4. **Roster-deadline interleaving:** September 1 changes eligibility before the September 3 game. Do not finish all four games before applying the 75-player reduction.
5. **Different owner/presentation layers:** A new `Preseason_Game_Report.md` can preserve the existing eight-section full football report only after the result owner and stat renderer are reconciled. No second full summary may be created.

**Two-pass procedure:** The initial pass checked original 2011 labor language and 2014–15 official team/NFL calendar. The second used the NFL league schedule against Jaguars' dated club release, and independently used the Seahawks league deadline announcement plus the period Patriots/Chiefs reports for contemporary operating practices. Shared league bulletins are not independent rulemakers; sources marked independent here are different organizations or primary agreement versus practice.
