### Period-specific rules to implement, not just cite

**Maximum four preseason games:** Article 23 §7; a fifth game applies only to the two clubs in the Hall of Fame Game or an American Bowl game scheduled around its date. Jacksonville's 2015 schedule lists four. No fifth game is created to fill the calendar; a season's page sets follow its fixtures.

**Practice is separate from games:** Article 23's one-padded-practice and field-hour constraints apply to practice plans; the preseason game is a separate scheduled competition. The camp days of §6(a) (the first day, then the two days without contact or pads) are counted from each player's own report day. The game-review writer cannot turn an unobserved padded practice into evidence merely because a full-contact exhibition was played.

**Exhibition participation:** Before the applicable roster cut, the camp and offseason eligible player list, medical restrictions and Stone's game-day holdouts control who may be used. Do not apply the regular-season 46-man game-day active limit or the standing healthy-inactive rule to the preseason: the whole controlled roster may dress. After Sep 1, game-four eligibility must reflect the 75 limit and any legitimately applicable roster exemptions, not a saved 90-man sheet.

**Medical and independent actor boundaries:** An injury is recognized at the game's actual time; a replacement that belongs to Stone pauses when material. Diagnosis, restrictions and clearance come from the appropriate medical authority. A roster-list response, a waiver claim or a replacement acquisition requires Caldwell's own dated action, on Stone's recommendation. A real 2015 Jaguars player cannot enter Stone's 2015 roster because the real Jaguars later signed him.

**Playing rules:** The try rule adopted May 19, 2015 for the 2015 season is materially important. Test line of scrimmage, defense return possibility, two-point try location and scorer credit independently of the score narrative. Never carry the 2014 preseason PAT trial (the Hall of Fame Game and the first two preseason weeks, library X2) forward as a 2015 blanket rule.

**Information cutoff:** A 2015 preseason game brief may refer to legitimate game-date opponent pro scouting and actual 2015 club sources *only* by the relevant dates; the stored historical results of the real 2015 games are not scouting evidence. Other clubs' players follow their real moves on their real dates, each usable only from its real public date. NFL team media sometimes retroactively show modern names for Washington or venues; use 2015 Redskins, FedEx Field, EverBank Field.

### Research uncertainties / limitations

1. **Opposing preseason participation:** The league schedule says who plays whom, not which real-life opponent starters or camp free agents would have dressed in this career. The 2014 stored opponent-camp depth evidence cannot be extrapolated to 2015; the 2015 opponents' rosters are researched at the August 2015 clock.
2. **Recorded film:** The 2011 CBA requires clubs to film camp activities (§10), but that gives this career no specific film finding. A box score is not video review.
3. **2015 preseason release readiness:** The existing 2014 implementation and its PAT tests cannot certify a playable 2015 service. That is a real integration blocker.
4. **Roster-deadline interleaving:** September 1 changes eligibility before the September 3 game. Do not finish all four games before applying the 75-player reduction.
5. **Different owner and presentation layers:** From 2015 the Preseason Game Report is the one result page for each game, and it carries the eight-section report's football substance (preparation, media, the story, the coaching review, the box score last). It is built only after the result owner and stat renderer are reconciled. No second full summary may be created.
6. **Dropped sources:** the uploaded register's Chiefs article of August 24, 2014 (preseason game takeaways) could not be reached or confirmed in two passes, and the Patriots' August 26, 2014 Belichick transcript was confirmed by one search only, with the page itself unreachable. Neither is in a library register, so both are dropped, as the [final roster cuts register](final_roster_cuts_research.md) drops the Patriots transcript; neither decided a rule.
7. **2015 practice observations:** how they are produced is awaiting the user's decision (design, section H, Q-P); until then no 2015 practice observation is produced, and a page cites only observations a training report already holds.

**Two-pass procedure:** The initial pass checked original 2011 labor language and 2014–15 official team and NFL calendars. The second used the NFL league schedule against Jaguars' dated club release, and independently used the Seahawks league deadline announcement plus the Chiefs' August 8, 2014 report for contemporary operating practices. Shared league bulletins are not independent rulemakers; sources marked independent here are different organizations or primary agreement versus practice. The process check re-read the April 9, 2015 Jaguars release, the NFL 2015 preseason schedule page, the May 19, 2015 try-rule article (with separate reports that the rule was adopted for 2015 and would then be reviewed) and Article 23 §§5 to 11 of the original agreement; it confirmed the Patriots transcript by search and could not retrieve the Chiefs August 24 article.

## Preseason Games: repository assessment and implementation blockers

### What already works

- The 2014 career has four distinct completed preseason games under `career/2014/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Preseason_Games/Game_01` through `Game_04`, each with a dated public `output.md`, stored game inputs, unique game result, rotations and individual medical consequences.
- `runtime/preseason.py` separates exhibition roster, opponent lineup, participation and game inputs from regular-season accounting. `runtime/rotation.py` already captures the important truth that a planned snap ceiling does not instantaneously terminate a possession.
- The private production resolver and `scripts/close_preseason_game.py` provide one-time closure and consequential Jacksonville injury pause. The preseason stat renderer and `tests/test_preseason_game.py`, `tests/test_preseason_rotation.py` cover several invariants.
- `foundation/templates/preseason_output_template.md` already insists on the game narrative, then real football analysis, then complete box score, with a cross-game coaching review inside the final game output. Do not throw this work away.
- `runtime/game_report.py`, `runtime/game_plan.py` and `scripts/freeze_pregame.py` already check the Game Report's layout and pregame freeze and compile the Game Plan; the preseason pages reuse them.
- Stone's play-calling, roster and role, medical authority and private actor independence are recorded in `state/current_job.json` and active canon. The dated owner and index system and event-insert design already state individual-outcome rules.

### Must change behind the scenes

1. **Decision-registry routing:** `runtime.decisions.folder('preseason_game', year)` currently uses the shared `training_camp_and_preseason` parent; it does **not** accept a game number or resolve `Game_0N`. Add a per-game page set using `SeasonPaths.preseason_folder(n)` while retaining unrelated camp and final-cut routes.
2. **New-season page ownership:** Current code writes to `Game_0N/output.md`. From 2015 the result page is `Preseason_Game_Report.md`, chosen by a `SeasonPaths.preseason_report(n)` that returns `output.md` for 2013 and 2014. Both should not be generated as rival full reports.
3. **Coach-plan compiler:** Add a football-language input accepting the Preseason Game Plan's fixed headings for one game, resolving personnel, named calls and rotation choices to existing game input formats, with a 2014 round-trip proof. Any call in the active book may be named for any situation; its fourth-down, two-point, kickoff and defensive lines are recorded intent only. Never ask the user for JSON, numeric IDs or software concepts.
4. **Preseason-specific decision gate:** The present queue's `practice_plan` class has no input or close blocker; add per-game events and E2 dependencies, and hold game 4's inputs until the reduction to 75 is recorded. Do not let an unrelated hiring or contract response hold a playable game absent a real dependency.
5. **2015 rules and release:** Add a verified 2015 rules and calendar release, the 15-yard try rule adopted for 2015, the 2015 fixture set and new-season opponent, strength and medical inputs before making the new four games playable. The 2015 release record, all seven gates with the player-state table and binding, is needed before preseason game 1 on August 14, 2015, and the preseason release inputs drop the Week 1 depth library. A 2014 release is not proof.
6. **2015 cutdown link:** The first reduction to 75 must be executed *before* the final preseason game; the fourth game's roster may differ from the third's. The final 53 comes after the fourth game, not before it.
7. **Sourced coaching review:** Use source-limited play observation, role and snap exposure and cautious attribution. A writer cannot invent film details, coaches' conversations, special-teams fault, progress grades or causal connections the resolution never models. Players are judged only on taught material.
8. **Event ownership:** Consequential injuries, player contracts, trades and any rejected proposals need separate dated owners, each built on the shared event result form. The game report's after-action part and the folder index link these without duplicating a medical or transactional narrative.
9. **Immutable issued history:** New important information reissues the briefing under the one reissue rule ([event inserts](event_inserts.md), section A, item 6) while preserving the original edition. A post-freeze answer cannot silently modify the frozen plan.
10. **Preserved completed work:** No rewritten 2014 scores, preseason player statistics, grades, career record, two-point rules, standings or draft results. Regressions must compare original pages and game-result bytes.
