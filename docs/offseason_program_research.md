### Main findings, supported and unsupported

#### Phase boundaries and legality

The controlling source is the [August 4, 2011 NFL/NFLPA collective bargaining agreement](https://nflps.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/collective-bargaining-agreement-2011-2020.pdf), Article 21, printed pp. 131-137 and Article 22 (minicamps); Appendix G covers offseason-workout conduct. PDF pages 146-152 in the one-based viewer for Article 21. The agreement in force for 2014-15 is **not** the later 2020 CBA. The [2014 offseason phase rules](../library/2014_offseason_phase_rules_verification.md) record sections 2(a) and 2(c) and the Phase One classroom basis in two passes; the other readings in this table were checked against the 2011 text by the process check and await a two-pass library entry.

| Claim | 2011 CBA basis | Operational effect |
|---|---|---|

| New head coach club may start first Monday April; returning head-coach club third Monday at earliest | Article 21 §2(c) | 2014 Jaguars returning start April 21; 2015 returning start April 20. New-head-coach exception does not arise solely from drafting a new page. |

| Program divided into Phase One first 2 weeks, Phase Two next 3, Phase Three last 4 | Article 21 §2(b) | April decision handles first five weeks; OTA workflow starts Phase Three. |
| Phase One strength, conditioning and physical rehab only for physical activities | Article 21 §2(b)(i) | No position-coach field practice, observation, coached snapping or kicking, helmets or ordinary footballs. Narrow quarterback-to-uncovered-receiver exception is not a coached field drill. |

**Important correction from the repository's own history:** Stone's April 18, 2014 direction includes the proposition that Phase One punters could kick and snappers could snap into a net. That sentence conflicts with Article 21's prohibition on footballs in Phase One except the narrow QB provision. The actual recorded 2014 Phase One correctly kept all kicking and snapping teaching in the classroom. Preserve the *rest* of Stone's intent: Westhoff controls punt units, four game situations are distinguished and the three parts of a punt receive separate technical attention. Do not replace the legal rule with Stone's older informal reading.

The CBA is not a daily schedule. It controls what may happen. A club's posted program schedule, actual filing and executed sessions supply whether and when that specific activity occurred. A staff outline is not a completed workout, and scheduled OTAs are not necessarily ten held practices.

### The design that holds up

The proposed three-page April workflow already exists by exact name in `runtime/decisions.py`, so inventing a new public structure would be needless. The intended workflow must cover the first **five weeks** of the nine-week voluntary program: two weeks of Phase One, three weeks of Phase Two. Phase Three, OTAs, the mandatory minicamp and the rookie minicamp belong to their separate owners. The calendar heading "April" is when the program begins, not when a five-week report must magically be completed.

The repository's 2014 Phase One/Two reports are unusually specific football evidence. For example, the first Boot Flood free-edge meeting produced conflicting answers; Bates and Tice reconciled the instruction and the April 29 different-diagram explanation was consistent. The QB/center/back double-A-gap point-reset cues were brought into agreement April 24. The subsequent **unopposed** Phase Two movement provides a different kind of evidence, but still not a real rush test. Crennel's team worked on defined coverage/check communication, and Westhoff kept punt operation, kick and coverage distinct. These are usable examples because the original dated branch records say they occurred. They are not a menu of mandated 2015 coaching successes.

The staff/GM division is equally clear. Stone can specify practice priorities, teaching emphasis, staff structure inside his budget, and role/depth decisions when those choices actually arise. Caldwell decides player contracts, draft selections, trades, claims, cuts and acquisitions. Medical personnel decide clearance and limits. Stone's special franchise-quarterback input is written consultation, not approval rights. A practice plan does not supersede anyone's authority. Stone's existing four-year fully guaranteed head-coach agreement, game-call arrangement and standing practice-lead instruction must not be recreated annually or reset.

The existing `docs/event_inserts.md` rule is also settled: any event that actually reaches disposition gets **one separate individual dated output**, including failed or declined negotiations. A Phase One/Two report is not the owner of a contract decision; the Program Report is not the owner of a rejected trade. That requires integration, not a revised canon decision.

### Corrections required by actual period rules

1. **Phase One classroom teaching and field workouts are different legal activities.** Contemporary Chiefs and Packers sources support classroom football coaching after the program begins. The 2011 CBA restricts field activity to qualifying strength/conditioning work and rehabilitation. There is no ordinary Phase One coached punt operation or snapper-kicker session. Stone's April 18, 2014 statement suggesting that narrow field work was lawful is not sufficient to override the CBA. The existing completed 2014 record correctly kept its Phase One kicking and snapping teaching in the room. Preserve his football intention while enforcing the lawful boundary.
2. **Phase Two still has no opposed football.** Coaches may legally teach individual and separate-unit football on the field. A draw of a changing front or a different formation is not a real pass rush, contested receiver release or tackling situation. Do not treat correct assignments on air as improved player ability or proof of competitive execution.
3. **The program is voluntary.** Its meetings and physical work are not tests of willingness to participate. A player who does not attend cannot be graded as disloyal or penalized through an attendance-based role conclusion. Legal participant eligibility is a separate question: an unsigned tender or expired contract is not evidence that the person signed an Offseason Workout and Minicamp Participation Agreement.
4. **Dates are for the relevant NFL year.** 2014 returning-HC start April 21, 2015 returning-HC start April 20, with a 21-day-before-start outer limit on calendar filing. For April 20, 2015 the outer deadline is March 30. The actual NFL/NFLPA mutually agreed submission date is not established; in this career the Jacksonville filing is the club's own dated event, made by Caldwell's office before that limit. An archived NFL announcement published May 15 cannot, by itself, establish what an April 20 briefing could have quoted as public knowledge. A genuinely filed and communicated **internal** schedule establishes internal knowledge from its filing date.
