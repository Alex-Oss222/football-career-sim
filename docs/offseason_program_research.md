### Main findings, supported and unsupported

#### Phase boundaries and legality

The controlling source is the [August 4, 2011 NFL/NFLPA collective bargaining agreement](https://nflps.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/collective-bargaining-agreement-2011-2020.pdf), Article 21, printed pp. 131-137 and Article 22 (minicamps); Appendix G covers offseason-workout conduct. PDF pages 146-152 in the one-based viewer for Article 21. The agreement in force for 2014-15 is **not** the later 2020 CBA.

| Claim | 2011 CBA basis | Operational effect |
|---|---|---|
| Program is voluntary, including classroom instruction | Article 21 §1; §5(a) | Never penalize voluntary nonattendance, demand justification or assign a commitment grade. Separate one permitted mandatory veteran minicamp under Article 22. |
| New head coach club may start first Monday April; returning head-coach club third Monday at earliest | Article 21 §2(c) | 2014 Jaguars returning start April 21; 2015 returning start April 20. New-head-coach exception does not arise solely from drafting a new page. |
| Program scheduled and filed by the mutually agreed date, no later than 21 days before start; changes advanced in writing | Article 21 §2(c) | Derived latest Apr 21 2014 filing date Mar 31; Apr 20 2015 latest Mar 30. Actual accepted filing dates and transmissions require their own proof. |
| Program divided into Phase One first 2 weeks, Phase Two next 3, Phase Three last 4 | Article 21 §2(b) | April decision handles first five weeks; OTA workflow starts Phase Three. |
| Phase One strength, conditioning and physical rehab only for physical activities | Article 21 §2(b)(i) | No position-coach field practice, observation, coached snapping or kicking, helmets or ordinary footballs. Narrow quarterback-to-uncovered-receiver exception is not a coached field drill. |
| Phase One **classroom** football coaching is possible | Article 21 §§1, 2 and §5 distinguish classroom instruction from restricted on-field work; 2014 Chiefs first-hand explanation below | Teach protection, call language and film on lawful program days, not pre-program and not on the field. |
| Phase Two individual and separate-unit instruction without opposition | Article 21 §2(b)(ii) | No offense against defense, receiver/DB competition, OL/DL or contact; no helmets. The punt operation may be rehearsed separately from a return team. |
| Phase Three OTAs allow 7-on-7, 9-on-7 and 11-on-11, with no live contact | Article 21 §2(b)(iii); Appendix G | The first legal offense-versus-defense tests are later, and even these are non-contact. |
| Time maximum and player control of hours | Article 21 §5(b) | In non-OTA/non-minicamp program days: at most 4 h in facility, 4 weekdays/week, 90 min field/day; at most 2 h/day prescribed by club. Validate actual individuals and club calendar. |
| Required Tender/UFA participation agreement | Article 21 §§3,9 | Tender alone is not proof a player may work; confirm signed contract or lawful offseason participation agreement. |
| Record filming and head-coach compliance exposure | Article 21 §§5(c),8 | Club retains field-session footage for NFLPA review through thirty days after season starts; illegal coaching creates real club/head-coach liability, not a reward for effort. |
| No post-program organized coached football outside allowed exception | Article 21 §6 | Do not covertly extend April or Phase Three workouts into the pre-camp break. |

**Important correction from the repository's own history:** Stone's April 18, 2014 direction includes the proposition that Phase One punters could kick and snappers could snap into a net. That sentence conflicts with Article 21's prohibition on footballs in Phase One except the narrow QB provision. The actual recorded 2014 Phase One correctly kept all kicking and snapping teaching in the classroom. Preserve the *rest* of Stone's intent: Westhoff controls punt units, four game situations are distinguished and the three parts of a punt receive separate technical attention. Do not replace the legal rule with Stone's older informal reading.

The CBA is not a daily schedule. It controls what may happen. A club's posted program schedule, actual filing and executed sessions supply whether and when that specific activity occurred. A staff outline is not a completed workout, and scheduled OTAs are not necessarily ten held practices.

Stone's special franchise-quarterback input is written consultation, not approval rights. A practice plan does not supersede anyone's authority. The existing `docs/event_inserts.md` rule is also settled: any event that actually reaches disposition gets **one separate individual dated output**, including failed or declined negotiations. A Phase One/Two report is not the owner of a contract decision; the monthly Program Report is not the owner of a rejected trade. That requires integration, not a revised canon decision.

### Corrections required by actual period rules

1. **Phase One classroom teaching and field workouts are different legal activities.** Contemporary Chiefs and Packers sources support classroom football coaching after the program begins. The 2011 CBA restricts field activity to qualifying strength/conditioning work and rehabilitation. There is no ordinary Phase One coached punt operation or snapper-kicker session. Stone's April 18, 2014 statement suggesting that narrow field work was lawful is not sufficient to override the CBA. The existing completed 2014 record correctly kept its Phase One kicking and snapping teaching in the room. Preserve his football intention while enforcing the lawful boundary.
2. **Phase Two still has no opposed football.** Coaches may legally teach individual and separate-unit football on the field. A draw of a changing front or a different formation is not a real pass rush, contested receiver release or tackling situation. Do not treat correct assignments on air as improved player ability or proof of competitive execution.
3. **The program is voluntary.** Its meetings and physical work are not tests of willingness to participate. A player who does not attend cannot be graded as disloyal or penalized through an attendance-based role conclusion. Legal participant eligibility is a separate question: an unsigned tender or expired contract is not evidence that the person signed an Offseason Workout and Minicamp Participation Agreement.
4. **Dates are for the relevant NFL year.** 2014 returning-HC start April 21, 2015 returning-HC start April 20, with a 21-day-before-start outer limit on calendar filing. For April 20, 2015 the outer deadline is March 30. The actual NFL/NFLPA mutually agreed submission date and a particular Jacksonville filing confirmation are not established. An archived NFL announcement published May 15 cannot, by itself, establish what an April 20 briefing could have quoted as public knowledge. A genuinely filed and communicated **internal** schedule may establish internal knowledge if its original dated source exists.
