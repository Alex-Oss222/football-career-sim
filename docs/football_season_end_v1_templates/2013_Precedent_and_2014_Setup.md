# Season-end examples tied to existing career facts

These are examples of the new **layout**, not a replay or revision of an old event. The closed 2013 season and employment decision remain unchanged. **The 2014 season has not ended at the repository's current checkpoint.** Do not present the 2014 placeholders as completed facts.

**Source:** [`career/2013/season_review/owner_and_gm_review.md`](https://github.com/Alex-Oss222/football-career-sim/blob/main/career/2013/season_review/owner_and_gm_review.md). That source explicitly calls its longer manuscript interpretive reconstruction, not a transcript. The frozen decision is the controlling historical record.

### How a compact club result could read, as a formatting illustration

> **Club Decision: January 15, 2014**
>
> Khan and Caldwell retained Alex Stone as Jacksonville's head coach under the contract signed in January 2013. The club credited the functioning program, the improved record and playoff victory, the establishment of a starting quarterback and the working relationship between football operations and the coaching staff. It did not treat the 18 interceptions, 38 sacks or three substantial defeats as resolved problems. The existing contract and distribution of authority were unchanged.
>
> **Next football matters:** Caldwell identified scoring margin and quarterback ball security and protection as early measures for 2014. Future player contracts, draft decisions and assistant appointments remained subject to their existing decision authorities.

This is enough for the *employment result*; the detailed 2013 team review belongs in its original football owner. No separate employment raise/extension output is created because none was offered or agreed.

## Example 3: How the future result separates other events

The following illustrates document boundaries only; **these are not events that occurred**.

- If Khan and Caldwell decide to retain Stone on his existing agreement, the outcome is the single `Club_Decision.md`. The word “retained” creates no raise, no new years and no additional authority.
- If Stone separately requests an extension, the club may decide whether to negotiate. If it issues a written offer, that offer becomes its own action notice and the negotiation later gets its **own standalone dated contract result**. The season-end club decision need not be rewritten to manufacture a second employment decision.
- If Khan and Caldwell dismiss Stone, that dismissal is recorded in `Club_Decision.md` **when it actually occurs**. The club cannot wait for Stone to consent, and no missing prior meeting is invented. After the effective date, his agent's report and any subsequent real interview, offer or hiring results live in the career owner, not in Jacksonville's confidential game brief.
- If an assistant is granted permission to interview another club or leaves during the same period, that matter gets its **own dated staff/permission output**. It is not bundled inside `Club_Decision.md`.

## The short version of what the head coach should see

A *real, issued* ownership briefing for the next completed season should be short enough to read at a meeting: the record, previously stated standards, the club's actual questions and the specific response sought. Stone's page contains only his own responses. The result states what Khan and Caldwell decided. The detailed team season review and player exit interviews stay linked, not copied into each page.
