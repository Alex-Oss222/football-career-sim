# Worked precedent and next-cycle setup: Jacksonville assistant vacancies

**Scope:** Grounded in the already completed 2014 special-teams search. This is *not* a new search, new interview, actual 2015 vacancy or authorization to hire anyone. Nothing in this file changes the 2014 career.

## Why this older event is a useful test

The actual branch record shows that Alan Lowry departed to become Atlanta's head coach on January 12, 2014. The special teams coordinator position was open. Stone covered the kicking game temporarily. At the February 2 planning checkpoint Stone had a target order and terms; his first target could not be reached, so the authorized sequence continued.

| Date in 2014 | What the actual branch records |
|---|---|
| January 12 | Lowry leaves Jacksonville, opening the special teams coordinator job. Stone directs special teams on an interim basis. |
| February 2 | The working plan selects Bobby April first, Mike Westhoff second, Bruce DeHaven third, with bounded terms already authorized. |
| February 3-4 | Jacksonville requests permission to speak to April; Oakland refuses the request. No interview with April occurs. |
| February 5 | Jacksonville contacts unattached Mike Westhoff. |
| February 7 | Westhoff agrees to an interview. |
| February 10 | Westhoff interviews. The record does **not** contain a verbatim interview transcript or individual answers, so no invented dialogue belongs in the result. |

## How the proposed Candidate Report would have read on February 2, 2014

### Special teams coordinator opening

**Why the job is open:** Alan Lowry departed January 12. Stone is temporarily directing the kicking game.

**Work to assign:** Punt and punt-return units, kickoff and kickoff return, place-kick protection and operation, personnel preparation, coverage-lane teaching, specialist coordination and game-day special teams calls. Stone retains team-level game management and Caldwell retains player acquisitions.

**Bobby April:** Established NFL special-teams coaching record. Current Oakland contract/permission requires confirmation before an interview. He is Stone's first target, not a guaranteed appointment.

**Mike Westhoff:** Veteran former Jets special-teams coordinator, retired after 2012. Whether he wishes to return must be established by contact; he is not presumed willing.

**Bruce DeHaven:** Experienced coach currently associated with Carolina in the source record, but exact branch contract status and permission need checking if the search reaches him.

**Decision requested:** Confirm the target order and financial bounds. Do not treat 'call April' as 'hire April'. No named answer to an interview or financial counter can be supplied in advance.

This illustration uses the actual plan known at the relevant branch checkpoint. It does not insert the later February 11 result back into the February 2 briefing.

The Staff Hire index may link all four, but cannot replace them.

## January 2015: what the system actually knows

As of the existing October 5, 2014 career checkpoint:

- Mike Tice and Romeo Crennel have 2015 contractual salaries and ongoing coordinating jobs. Stone currently calls both offense and defense, and the two coordinators prepare their respective sides. A new coordinator appointment must not silently transfer Stone's call control.
- Mike Westhoff has a 2015 contract with non-guaranteed 2015 salary. That is a real existing agreement, not an automatically vacant position.
- Nine other assistants' recorded terms run through 2014. A pending renewal or contract year boundary is **not yet** proof of a vacancy; a completed departure/expiry/termination must actually create it. The exact lapse date for the expiring assistants remains an identified rules/contract verification task.
- No 2014 end-of-season coaching result, staff departure, interview, January 2015 candidate interest, offer or hiring outcome has been established. Therefore no new 2015 Jacksonville hire may be written now.

**If a vacancy actually occurs:** Open an ACTION REQUIRED insert in the active offseason/season briefing, then issue a Candidate Report after the controlling vacancy and Stone's staff authority are established. The next stage is Stone's My Hire instructions, not an immediate candidate draw and automatic contract.

## A football interview for a specific role, without prewriting the answer

For a hypothetical *quarterbacks-coach* vacancy after the coach's existing terms legally end, a serious interview might include:

- Explain the quarterback's protection identification against a six-man pressure, including the line/backs' responsibilities and the built-in throw answer.
- Describe how you teach an early throw against off coverage and how you distinguish quarterback footwork from receiver route depth when timing fails.
- Show how you would coordinate with Tice and Stone given that Stone calls the offense.

For a hypothetical *defensive-backs* vacancy:

- Explain a Quarters match answer to motion into a bunch, including the safety and nickel communication and the force player against the run.
- Describe how a position coach takes a repeated exchange mistake from film to individual work and later checks it with a different presentation.

These are proposed interview questions, not actual 2015 interviews or verified candidate demonstrations. No candidate needs a numerical score, and no performance improvement is assumed from hiring a recognizable name.
