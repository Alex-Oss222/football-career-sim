## A. The two distinct 2014 stages

### August 26: the 75-player check

The 2014 roster had **74 players under signed contracts** plus **four unsigned tenders** (Cameron Bradfield, Toney Clemons, Mike Brown and Austin Pasztor), treated in that roster record as rights rather than signed Active List players. The 75-player limit was met at 74. Caldwell **made no release, waiver or reserve-list move** to meet that deadline. The four-game 2014 preseason continued, with game four against Atlanta on August 28.

This matters for the design: a 'first cut' is a real counting and legal-compliance point even when no cut is needed. The career must never release one of Stone's players to make a report look busy. Rights to an unsigned player also do not establish that he played in that preseason game. Under the 2015 design the same position asks Stone nothing: a FOR YOUR INFORMATION line gives the count.

### August 28 to 29: Atlanta and the cutdown meeting

Atlanta beat Jacksonville **31-13 in the career's final 2014 preseason game**. The game produced medical incidents for Hakeem Nicks, A.J. Bouye, Gator Hoskins and Jeris Pendleton. Nicks was out on a multi-week instruction, and no decision could treat his availability as if he had passed a future examination.

At the [August 29 meeting](../../career/2014/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/training_report.md#august-29-the-atlanta-review-and-the-cutdown-meeting), the staff carried the August 28 package's 53 recommendation and depth baseline to Caldwell unchanged. No quotation is recorded and the meeting decided nothing. The two technical items were the waived/injured route for Cameron Brate and Jerrell Jackson, and the practice-squad eligibility of Richard Murphy, a target, and Antwon Blake, an alternate. The WR1, WR3, left-guard, right-guard alternative, Edge 1 and long-snapper battles were still open; the record attributes no concern about them to Stone. Each 2014 position-battle card kept its own practice and game evidence.

### August 31: separate waiver and practice-squad events

At the **noon August 31** waiver deadline, every waived Jacksonville player cleared: the rails record no real cutdown-window claim for any of them, and the rails rule for other clubs' claims (design rule 17) clears a player with none. Brate and Jackson reverted to reserve/injured in their separate cases. Caldwell then signed eight eligible practice-squad players from the target list in order as eligibility allowed: **Tyler Bray, Richard Murphy, Matt Feiler, Tyler Shatley, Jackson Jeffcoat, Jerome Long, Jemea Thomas and Adrian Phillips**. Two of the ten places stayed open. The signings followed waiver clearance and the eligibility checks; the record has no acceptance step. The record also never checked for real practice-squad signings elsewhere in the same window: its rails check covered cutdown-window claims, and the Week 1 depth-chart library holds no practice squads. At least three of the eight (Feiler, Jeffcoat and Thomas) really signed to other clubs' practice squads in that window, so under design rule 19 each would have needed the market draw. The 2014 signings stay as written and are not a precedent for rule 19 or a fixture for the uncontested case. In 2015 an approach to a player who really signed elsewhere in the same window is decided by the market draw, and any other approached player signs (design rule 19). A recommendation to pursue an alternate was not itself a signed player.

The practice-squad names are the recorded 2014 outcomes. They must **not** be used as the 2015 squad or as evidence that every player waived elsewhere would clear waivers for Stone.

A blank Final 53 is *design*, not a league filing. The real 2015 Jaguars' cuts are no more the career's answer than the club's real 2014 preseason scores were the scores in Stone's career.

## C. Individual outcomes required once 2015 reaches this phase

Suppose the club resolves three different matters on one day: a waived player clears, a separate trade inquiry is rejected, and a third player whom Jacksonville approached for the practice squad signs with the club he really joined in that window, after the market draw. That is a **scenario to test the workflow, not 2015 career history**. It needs three separately dated outcomes, in the roster, trades and free-agency folders respectively, each linked from the cutdown index. Final 53, frozen as filed on September 5, shows none of them; none of them can have the index as its only written result.
