These are examples of the new **layout**, not a replay or revision of an old event. The closed 2013 season and employment decision remain unchanged. **The 2014 season has not ended: the last closed game is Week 5, October 5, 2014.** Do not present the 2014 placeholders as completed facts.

### How a compact club result could read, as a formatting illustration

> **Club Decision: January 15, 2014**
>
> Khan and Caldwell retained Alex Stone as Jacksonville's head coach under the contract signed in January 2013. The club credited the functioning program, the improved record and playoff victory, the establishment of a starting quarterback and the working relationship between football operations and the coaching staff. It did not treat the 18 interceptions, 38 sacks or three substantial defeats as resolved problems. The existing contract and distribution of authority were unchanged.
>
> **Next football matters:** Caldwell identified scoring margin and quarterback ball security and protection as early measures for 2014. Future player contracts, draft decisions and assistant appointments remained subject to their existing decision authorities.

This is enough for the *employment result*; a real Club Decision also carries the shared result fields and its event-record line. The detailed 2013 team review belongs in its original football owner. No separate employment raise/extension output is created because none was offered or agreed.

## Example 3: How the future result separates other events

The following illustrates document boundaries only; **these are not events that occurred**.

- If Khan and Caldwell decide to retain Stone on his existing agreement, the outcome is the single `Club_Decision.md`. The word “retained” creates no raise, no new years and no additional authority.
- If Stone separately requests an extension, Khan and Caldwell decide whether to make an offer. If they make a written offer, it arrives as an ACTION REQUIRED notice in the active brief, Stone answers it on that phase's My Responses page, and it gets its **own standalone dated result**, the New Contract or Unchanged page in `career/Stone/`. The season-end club decision need not be rewritten to manufacture a second employment decision.
- If Khan and Caldwell dismiss Stone, that dismissal is recorded in `Club_Decision.md` **when it actually occurs**. The club cannot wait for Stone to consent, and no missing prior meeting is invented. After the effective date, his agent's report and any subsequent real interview, offer or hiring results live in `career/Stone/`, not in Jacksonville's confidential game brief.
- If another club asks to interview an assistant, Jacksonville's standing instruction answers where it applies (a head-coach interview or a position coach's coordinator interview granted, a move to the same title refused) and the briefing shows it for information; a request the instruction does not answer goes to Stone as a staff-permission item. Either way, and if the assistant leaves, that matter gets its **own dated staff/permission output**. It is not bundled inside `Club_Decision.md`.
- If another club asks permission to contact Stone, Shad Khan alone decides. Stone's standing answer is that he is not open to job offers, so the request and Khan's answer reach him as news; no answer from Stone is asked for.

## The short version of what the head coach should see

A *real, issued* ownership briefing for the next completed season should be short enough to read at a meeting: the record, previously stated standards, the club's actual questions and the specific response sought. Stone's page contains only his own responses. The result states what Khan and Caldwell decided. The detailed team season review and player exit interviews stay linked, not copied into each page.
