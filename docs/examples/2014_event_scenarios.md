# Event inserts: illustrative 2014 scenarios

These are demonstration formats, **not new events, offers, transactions or facts about the simulated season**. Keep simulated Jacksonville records and existing Week 6 brief unchanged until their actual event triggers occur.

## Example 1: Week 8 trade-deadline alert without an offer

### COMING UP: Trading deadline

**Deadline:** Tuesday, October 28, 2014, 4 p.m. ET.

**From:** David Caldwell.

**Situation:** Jacksonville has no confirmed new incoming offer in this example. The deadline is near, and Caldwell is reviewing his own target and availability board.

**Your part:** No decision is due merely because the calendar is approaching the deadline. If Caldwell asks for a football evaluation on a genuine proposal, its terms and response time will appear as a new ACTION REQUIRED item.

**Result:** No trade outcome is created by this notice.

## Example 2: Genuine trade inquiry, once an event exists

### ACTION REQUIRED: Your football view on [other club]'s proposal

**Received:** [Time/date ET]. **Reply due:** [actual proposal deadline, if supplied].

**Caldwell says:** [Club] proposes [specific assets] in return for [specific assets]. Their offer is [formal / exploratory / incomplete] and [has/has not] been accepted by any club.

**Football consequence:** [Likely on-field role lost or gained, based on named eligible players and current chart].

**Financial and asset consequence:** [Verified contract cap consequences and draft-pick ownership or “Awaiting verified terms.”]

**Requested from Stone:** Support, oppose, or conditionally recommend the transaction. Caldwell decides and negotiates.

**Sample short response:** “I support moving [asset] only if we retain a starting-caliber [position] option. Otherwise oppose.”

**Result:** “[Caldwell accepted / countered / rejected / no agreement]. Effective [date]. [Changes].” No execution without a completed transaction record.

## Example 3: Player extension eligibility, not an invented offer

### COMING UP: Eligibility review for Adam Thielen

**Window:** After the final regular-season game of 2014, subject to his actual contract history and CBA classification.

**Known:** The repository's season-cycle guide identifies Thielen's extension window as opening after Jacksonville's last 2014 regular-season game. October 2014 is therefore not an eligible time to offer him a rookie-contract renegotiation.

**Now:** No negotiation or agent demand is implied. Caldwell may review his performance, contract and cap before the legal window opens.

**Later ACTION REQUIRED only if warranted:** “Caldwell recommends beginning talks. Does Stone have a football retention recommendation?”

### CAREER ACTION REQUIRED: Directions to your agent

**From:** [Representative], after a valid employer notice or verified contractual end.

**Employment status:** [Dismissed effective date / contract concluded]. This is a club action or a contractual fact, not a decision Stone can veto.

**Terms that still apply:** [Exact guarantees and other signed contractual rights, if sourced. For Stone, the end date, offset and dismissal terms are reconstructions completed October 19, 2026, and the page says so.]

**Routing:** `career/Stone` career desk. No private Jacksonville scouting or medical report reaches Stone after the employment change.

## Example 6: Assistant interview from another club

### FOR YOUR INFORMATION or ACTION REQUIRED: [Club] asks about [assistant]

**Known:** [Actual date, requesting club, position, employment and playoff status].

**Rule check:** In 2014 only a head-coach interview was protected; a position coach's coordinator interview and a same-title move could be refused. Use the 2014 rule, not the 2020 one.

**Standing answer:** Jacksonville's standing instruction grants head-coach and position-coach-to-coordinator interviews and refuses same-title moves, so the request and Jacksonville's answer appear under FOR YOUR INFORMATION. Ask Stone only if a concrete request falls outside that rule, for example a title change the rule does not name.

## Example 7: Multiple independent results inside one Week 8 briefing

This is an entirely hypothetical illustration and does not add events to Jacksonville's 2014 season.

- **One `Game_Week_Briefing.md`:** it displays two separate trade inquiries and a player-contract proposal under ACTION REQUIRED.
- **One `My_Responses.md`:** Stone opposes one trade, supports the other with conditions and recommends waiting on the extension.
- **Three separate dated outputs, as the events conclude:** the first trade's rejection file; the second trade's completed transaction (or no-deal) file; and the extension discussion's resolved or withdrawn result, if that discussion actually closes.
- **Week report/index:** links separately to each output. No `All_Event_Results.md` substitute and no fake final extension result while talks remain open.

Each event's result must be available when the event is resolved, not postponed until the ordinary game report.
