### Acceptance examples

- Two contract negotiations resolved on June 2 require **two individual results**, including any refusal.
- A trade approach rejected June 4 receives its own trade result, whether or not it affects the next OTA block.
- A minicamp report is not the output of a June 17 medical evaluation or roster transaction; link that event's actual result.
- One minor in-practice teaching correction is part of the practice report, not a fabricated contract, medical or personnel event.
