# Training Camp Position Battles: research and Markdown design package

This is **workflow 4 of 6**, designed for the upcoming 2015 Jacksonville season from the existing career evidence, using the original 2011 CBA and credible 2014–15 club reporting. The simulator's active career is after Week 5, October 5, 2014. All 2014 preseason games and camp comparisons are previously completed, their contents are not replayed. **No GitHub files were modified or 2015 depth, camp or roster results created.**

## Main three pages

1. [Position Battle Report](01_Position_Battle_Report_TEMPLATE.md), a staff briefing that offers a genuine, detailed comparison of the jobs actually being contested, the work observed and an explicit decision status.
2. [My Depth Calls](02_My_Depth_Calls_TEMPLATE.md), Stone's editable answer, allowing role appointments, meaningful tests, deferrals and a separate note to Caldwell without pretending that a coaching choice executes a cut.
3. [Depth Chart](03_Depth_Chart_TEMPLATE.md), the phase's readable **derived** output from the single current depth owner, linked to each actual individual battle resolution.

## Supporting documents

4. [Individual Battle Closure](04_Individual_Battle_Closure_REFERENCE.md), the one-event result material that belongs in that battle's own original card.
5. [Implementation and Validation Specification](05_Implementation_and_Validation_Spec.md), authority, 2011 CBA contact rules, temporal controls, source ownership, state flow and **68 proposed tests**.
6. [Worked Jacksonville Example](06_Worked_Jacksonville_Example.md), full three-page retrospective from the established 2014 camp with six separate contest links, plus 2015 date-only setup.
7. [Research and Source Register](07_Research_and_Source_Register.md), original CBA, period NFL/Jaguars reporting and canonical repository sources.
8. [Repository Assessment and Blockers](08_Repository_Assessment_and_Blockers.md), what works and precisely what remains unimplemented or unsupported.

## Unchanged owners

The existing `foundation/templates/position_battle_card_template.md` and individual 2014 battle cards remain the canonical case-specific evidence/result model. The camp staff plan and training report own instruction and actual observed work. The team depth owner owns current assignments. Caldwell's roster cuts and waiver results stay at their own transaction owner. The shared consequential-event insert/response process and requirement for one separately dated result per event apply during camp exactly as during any other phase.
