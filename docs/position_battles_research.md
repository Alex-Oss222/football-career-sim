## Training camp and position battles: 2011 CBA and contemporary source register

**Research scope:** NFL practice rules and coaching evaluation methods applicable during 2014-15. Sources establish era rules, actual league scheduling, the difference between spring and camp evaluation, and how teams publicly discuss contested roles. They do **not** import real 2015 Jacksonville draft selections, injuries, personnel, results or coaching decisions into Stone's simulated career.

### Primary governing agreement

**NFL and NFLPA, [2011 Collective Bargaining Agreement, signed August 4, 2011](https://nflps.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/collective-bargaining-agreement-2011-2020.pdf)**. Original contemporary agreement hosted by the NFL Physicians Society, not the later 2020 CBA or a current article transcription. It is source S1 of the library's [CBA practice record](../library/2014_cba_practice_rest_and_rookie_renegotiation.md) (same file, SHA-256 a7518753...; printed page = PDF page minus 15).

- **Article 23 §6(a), printed pages 140-141, PDF pages 155-156:** "The first day of a Club's preseason training camp shall be limited to physical examinations, meetings, and classroom instruction; no on-field activities shall be permitted other than running and conditioning." On the second and third days "no contact shall be permitted and no pads shall be worn". Thereafter two-a-days are permitted: no more than four hours on the field per day; no more than one padded practice per day, no longer than three hours of on-field activities, counted "as soon as position coaches begin to coach players on the field"; at least a three-hour break after it; and a second practice limited to the remaining time and to "walk-through" instruction ("no helmets, full-speed pre-snap, and walking pace after the snap"). The agreement contains no conditioning-test rule and no word "punish"; Jacksonville's conditioning standard is Stone's ([coaching profile](../career/Stone/Coaching_Profile.md); the 2014 camp plan's [entry standard](../career/2014/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/staff_plan.md#camp-entry-standard), item 8). The rule grants eligible practice procedures, not guaranteed contact work at the first opportunity.
- **Article 23 §6(b), p. 141, and Article 24 §1(c), p. 143:** a padded practice is one at which players are required to wear helmets and shoulder pads, with the quarterback, kicker, punter, long-snapper, head-injury and helmet-communication exceptions of §6(b). A padded practice cancelled within sixty minutes for a reason beyond the club's control does not count (§6(a)).
- **Article 23 §5, p. 140 (reporting):** no veteran other than quarterbacks and injured players may be required to report earlier than fifteen days before the club's first preseason game, including one day for physical examinations, meetings, classroom instruction, running and conditioning, or July 15, whichever is later.
- **Article 23 §9, p. 141:** Preseason Training Camp, for that Article, is "the entire period from the beginning of training camp for any player through the last weekend of preseason games". The design counts camp days from each player's own report day, the stricter reading ([design, section 3](position_battles.md#3-historical-2014-and-2015-calendar-cba-and-practice-law)).
- **Article 35 §1, p. 165 (preseason days off):** from the veterans' mandatory reporting date to the last weekend of preseason games, at least one day off every seven days, except a club with a game on the fifth day or sooner after its prior game, and never fewer than five days off in all. Article 23 has no roster or rest clause.
- **Contact:** Article 23 bans no drill in a padded camp practice. Tackling appears only in the offseason and minicamp contact bans: Article 21 (p. 133), Article 22 §5 (p. 139) and Appendix G (p. 278). The contact level of a padded camp period is the head coach's practice decision.
- **Article 21, printed pages 131-137 (the Phase Three rules, §2(b)(iii), on p. 132):** voluntary offseason work/Phase Three contact restrictions differ sharply from camp. Non-contact spring protection and coverage against an opposed team can identify assignments but is not a padded camp rep. Keep earlier report evidence within the permitted spring inventory.

**Important source warning:** The present-day Over The Cap webpage titled "Article 23" at `https://overthecap.com/collective-bargaining-agreement/article/23` publishes later CBA terms (e.g., days two through five restrictions and different practice lengths). Do not use that current transcription in place of the original 2011 rules for the simulator's 2014-15 era.

### Uncertainty and source hygiene

1. The 2011 CBA has a legally meaningful year-specific practice inventory. Do not silently substitute today's NFLPA/Over The Cap wording. The original 2011 PDF is controlling; a 2020-era online transcription is a warning, not independent confirmation.
2. Other clubs' historically real roster transactions operate under the user's restricted historical-league-rails exception and date gate. This cannot supply Jacksonville's 2015 player assessments or injury decisions.
3. An unknown 2015 late-August daily camp timetable stays unknown. Public initial camp announcements stop August 12. The simulated program can only schedule additional lawful activities on actual recorded Stone decisions.
4. No private coach conversations, player feedback, diagnostic details or unrecorded film outcomes can be cited as if recorded. Dated public football context and dated simulated private evidence are distinct sources.
5. Whether Art. 23 §6(a)'s days are counted from the club's first report day or each player's own is not settled by the text; the design counts from each player's own report day, which is lawful under either reading.

## Training camp position battles: rigorous repository assessment and blockers

**Inspected:** GitHub `Alex-Oss222/football-career-sim`, default branch `main`, tree/commit `75fd41fbfc5ed0363d9f6cb68b5bef37a6583f24`, October 20, 2026. Read-only source inspection. No pull request, commit, career clock movement or 2015 depth call has occurred in this package.

### What already works and should be preserved

1. **Documented authority:** `state/current_job.json` grants Stone depth-chart, package roles, lineups, game-day inactives, special-teams decisions and practice work; Caldwell executes acquisitions, draft decisions, contracts, cuts, claims and transactions; medical staff own diagnosis and return status. The four-year guaranteed head-coach contract and standing delegation remain canon. The coach does not get a power to cut a player simply by writing a depth chart.
2. **Actual battle evidence and dated sources:** Six full 2014 battle cards with substantive evidence, four real completed preseason game outputs in the simulated career and an unusually detailed camp training report. The individual 2014 cards are under the correct `Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Position_Battles/` tree, all closed August 30. The old report has no general claim that a no-target series identifies receiver quality. No 2014 battle is to be reopened for design aesthetics.
3. **Current depth owner:** the depth data in `data/YEAR/Team_Operations/Team/Depth_Chart/` (`game_depth_chart.json`, `working_depth_chart.json`, `update_log.json`) own the order and its chronology; `render_club_pages.py` builds `depth_chart.md` and its change history from them. `Roster_Decisions/roster_decisions.md` is the 2014 camp decision history only. The older 2014 roster reduction belongs to `final_roster_cuts.md`, not to a coach-comparison card.
4. **Specific coaching method:** `career/Stone/Coaching_Profile.md` defines his pace, split-group opportunities, sustained drives, corrections and his conditioning standard (an individual plan and retest after a failed test), and the current job record protects standing instructions. Those practices can be used as already-adopted intent, not automatic success.
5. **Existing presentation approach:** `foundation/templates/position_battle_card_template.md`, `foundation/templates/training_camp_and_preseason/training_camp_report.md` and `docs/camp_and_preseason_reports.md` already distinguish teaching, controlled contact, support, genuine field execution and preseason. Reuse them rather than introducing alternate grades.
6. **Three-page workflow is registered:** `runtime/decisions.py` already names `Position Battle Report`, `My Depth Calls`, `Depth Chart`, key `camp`, but `implemented=False`. `folder('camp', 2015)` gives `career/2015/Training_Camp_and_Preseason`, and `preseason_game` and `final_cuts` resolve to the same folder today; the preseason games and final cuts designs move them to their own folders, so camp alone keeps that folder's `My_Responses.md`.
7. **Dated events and clean pages:** `runtime/events.py` and `scripts/render_annual_record.py` support each event at its rightful owner; `docs/event_inserts.md` fixes one separate output per resolution and immutable briefing editions. The current clean-page rules forbid technical IDs, system paths and administrative notes on the coach's pages.

### No false resolutions

- Do not generate a WR1 battle for the real 2015 Jaguars' publicly known players. In Stone's October 5, 2014 career, those 2015 roster moves and draft choices have not happened.
- Do not "award" an offensive lineman after three clean non-contact OTA reps and claim he defeated an NFL defensive tackle.
- Do not make 40 fault-free snapper reps mean that the one chosen has a higher fabricated snap-accuracy rating.
- Do not force an open contest to close after a single preseason game when Stone has not answered.
- Do not convert a cut recommendation into Caldwell's executed waiver.

### Pre-execution acceptance bar

Before implementing a 2015 camp session: correct legally eligible roster and medical snapshot, active coach/assistant assignments named from the coach records, season-valid playbook, correctly released camp calendar, valid first-day and padded scheduling, Stone's actual plan, a user-approved procedure for practice observations or actual independently recorded observations, individual battle cards, single source of truth for depth, specific pending user decisions, separately owned completed events, and passing clean-page/annual-record/season-handoff tests. This package supplies the document contract but does not assert those runtime capabilities exist.
