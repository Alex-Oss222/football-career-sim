# Alex Stone coaching documents: revised proposals

Prepared October 3, 2026. Career evidence through September 28, 2014.

These are three complete examples of the proposed formats, using Stone's established character and career. They replace the earlier worksheets in this proposal folder. They remain drafts for selection and do not change the adopted profile, career records or employment decisions.

| Read | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Coaching profile](01_coaching_profile_template.md) | A sustained portrait of how Stone approaches football, teaching, people and the head-coaching job |
| [Head coach dossier](02_nfl_coach_sheet_template.md) | A GM's assessment of his experience, actual responsibilities, suitability and unresolved hiring questions, followed by career reference material |
| [Ownership review](03_performance_and_job_security_template.md) | An owner-perspective assessment of the inherited club, progress, recurring problems and continued employment |
| [Research and writing method](research_and_writing_method.md) | Primary NFL and writing sources, the reasoning behind each format, reusable structure and guidance for future updates |

The original drafts kept interrupting the documents with instructions about how to write them. The revised profile stays with Stone. The dossier puts the hiring case before the chronology. The review stays with ownership's judgment, including the responsibility of the GM and the club itself. Authoring guidance is collected in the companion.

The chronology is explicit: Stone ran New Orleans as interim HC/OC in 2012 and accepted Jacksonville's job January 15, 2013. Jacksonville's 2-14 predecessor season is compared with his 10-6 first season. The four games of 2014 are a separate update. The review's first-person voice is labeled editorial reconstruction; it creates no Khan quotation or new club decision.

Each proposal can be selected independently. Sources are pinned to the career version used for this review where appropriate. The external research informs the formats and writing, while the approved profile and career records supply the facts about Stone. The adopted documents remain unchanged until a later selection is authorized.
