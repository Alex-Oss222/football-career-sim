This is **workflow 4 of 6**, designed for the upcoming 2015 Jacksonville season from the existing career evidence, using the original 2011 CBA and credible 2014-15 club reporting. The simulator's active career is after Week 5, October 5, 2014. ### Main three pages

1. [Position Battle Report](../foundation/templates/position_battles/position_battle_report_template.md), the staff's briefing: a genuine, detailed comparison of the jobs actually being contested, the work observed and an explicit decision status. The brief proposes; it decides nothing.

## 1. Design contract and the three pages

Reuse `runtime/decisions.py` key `camp` with exact titles:

- `Position_Battle_Report.md`: the staff's current, evidence-anchored briefing. It is issued at the review points the season's camp plan sets (in 2014, the August 2 position review and then each game review; [2014 camp plan](../career/2014/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/staff_plan.md)) and reissued in between only under the one reissue rule ([event inserts](event_inserts.md), section A, item 6): a new event needs an answer from Stone, a dated finding materially changes a choice still pending, or an answer falls due before the next scheduled issue. Published at `runtime.decisions.folder('camp', Y)`, which resolves to `career/Y/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/`, with the camp's own `My_Responses.md` beside it. Each issued edition is immutable and carries its Issued and Edition lines; the current page is the latest edition, and every earlier edition is kept beside it with its date in the name, as the Game Week Briefing's are.
- `My_Depth_Calls.md`: a genuine editable answer from Stone (or the holder of depth authority in a different job). It may set initial groups, rehearsal rotations, package roles, current depth, temporary injury replacement and final named contests. It must allow an explicit "not decided; next test" answer. Do not auto-fill it from a staff recommendation. Each edition is answered once and then stays as the record of that decision; a later round of depth calls is a new edition, opened with the brief edition that asks for it, and the earlier one is kept beside it with its date in the name. A line left empty is unanswered: silence closes nothing.
- `Depth_Chart.md`: the **result** of Stone's applied depth calls, never a second mutable depth register. It is built from his applied My Depth Calls and the depth data as they stood on its issue date, checked against the depth chart's update log on that date, and never follows the current chart afterwards. It links each contest his calls closed to that card's dated closure, preserving prior decisions. No page exists until his first applied My Depth Calls; until then the camp index says that no depth chart has been issued. Each later application issues a new edition, the earlier kept beside it with its date in the name. The final edition, issued at the reduction to 53 once Final 53 is filed, carries the season depth chart and links Final 53; Final 53 links the team depth chart page.

`Position_Battle_Report` is not a transcription of each individual contest card. It presents only the important comparisons and choices for Stone at the current date. Its tables are built from the stored facts: availability from the roster record and the medical owner, the work completed from the camp report's dated blocks (linked, not copied), and the position coach's and coordinator's lines from the current cards' stored rows with their author and date. It never authors a second assessment. The detailed work and process belong under the camp `training_report.md` and the actual games, one complete comparison per dedicated position-battle card. Nothing invents a competing third annual player evaluation. The annual opening assessment carries dated updates; final annual assessment follows the season review.

## 3. Historical 2014 and 2015 calendar, CBA and practice law

### Original 2011 CBA Article 23, not later agreements

Use the August 4, 2011 NFL-NFLPA agreement, **Article 23 §6** (printed pp. 140-141, PDF pp. 155-156; the section numbering and pages were re-read from the agreement's text). Article 21 (printed pp. 131-137, the Phase Three rules on p. 132) governs the spring offseason program and Article 22 the minicamps. The camp sequence:

1. First day of camp, in the agreement's words: "The first day of a Club's preseason training camp shall be limited to physical examinations, meetings, and classroom instruction; no on-field activities shall be permitted other than running and conditioning" (Art. 23 §6(a), p. 140). Any other on-field activity on day one is refused. The agreement sets no conditioning-test standard. Jacksonville's standard is Stone's: players and coaches meet written physical standards, and a failed test leads to an individual plan and retest, never punishment running ([coaching profile](../career/Stone/Coaching_Profile.md); the 2014 camp plan's [entry standard](../career/2014/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/staff_plan.md#camp-entry-standard), item 8).
2. Days two and three: "No contact shall be permitted and no pads shall be worn during the second and third days of Preseason Training Camp" (§6(a)). These days can have lawful football instruction and ordinary practices within limits.
3. Days four onward: two-a-day practices are permitted, with players on the field for **no more than four hours per day**, **no more than one padded practice** per day, that practice **no longer than three hours of on-field activities** (the limit begins "as soon as position coaches begin to coach players on the field"), **at least a three-hour break** after it, and a second practice limited to the remaining on-field time and to "walk-through" instruction ("no helmets, full-speed pre-snap, and walking pace after the snap") (§6(a), pp. 140-141). A padded practice is one at which players are required to wear helmets and shoulder pads (Art. 24 §1(c), which §6(a) adopts), with the exceptions of §6(b); a padded practice cancelled within sixty minutes for weather or another cause beyond the club's control does not count. A completed preseason **game** is a distinct contest, not a camp-practice drill.
4. Reporting and days off: no veteran other than quarterbacks and injured players may be required to report earlier than fifteen days before the club's first preseason game (including one day for physical examinations, meetings, classroom instruction, running and conditioning) or July 15, whichever is later (Art. 23 §5, p. 140). From the veterans' mandatory reporting date to the last weekend of preseason games, all players have at least one day off every seven days, except a club with a game on the fifth day or sooner after its prior game, and never fewer than five days off in all (Art. 35 §1, p. 165). Players need the correct camp report eligibility and actual medical instructions. An eligible contracted player's reporting is different from a rookie with an unsigned deal, an authorized tryout, a reserve-list player or someone not under club control. A stated right to practice is not proof of participation.
5. Contact: the 2011 agreement limits padded camp practice by number, length and break, and bans no drill. Tackling appears only in the offseason and minicamp contact bans (Art. 21, p. 133; Art. 22 §5, p. 139; Appendix G, p. 278); the prohibited-drill list belongs to a later agreement ([CBA practice record](../library/2014_cba_practice_rest_and_rookie_renegotiation.md), section 5). Inside a lawful padded practice, each period's contact level is Stone's practice decision (row 11): it comes from the recorded practice instruction and is never assumed from the word "padded". Do not silently upgrade every padded period into full tackling to the ground or a competition against an outside NFL team.

**How camp days are counted.** Days one to three are counted from each player's own report day. This is the stricter reading and is lawful under either reading. It can refuse pads on a veteran's second and third days that a club-wide count from the first report day would allow: §9 defines Preseason Training Camp, for that Article, as "the entire period from the beginning of training camp for any player", and only §5 gives each veteran his own day of physicals.

The library becomes the owner of these rules once Art. 23 §5, §6(a) and §9 and Art. 35 §1 are transcribed into the [CBA practice record](../library/2014_cba_practice_rest_and_rookie_renegotiation.md) or a 2015 entry ([section 7](#7-build-integration-points-and-the-files-to-change)); this section then links that entry instead of restating it.

**Important calendar difference:** 2014 Jaguars rookies reported July 21, veterans July 24, team first practiced July 25, had first full pads July 30, and recorded an August 2 scrimmage. These events already happened in this career; older 2014 camp cards keep their written chronology even where the historical open-day schedule later proved imperfect. For 2015 the [calendar](../career/2015/Calendar.md) holds the announced schedule: quarterbacks, rookies and first-year players report July 27, the other players July 30, practices July 31 onward, an August 4 day off, August 5 the announced first **full** pads, an August 8 stadium scrimmage, August 9 off; the initial posted schedule ends August 12. The 2015 preseason opener is August 14 vs Pittsburgh, then August 22 at NY Giants, August 28 vs Detroit, September 3 at Washington. Those are public calendar anchors, not played Stone events. Equipment on any camp day within Art. 23 §6 is Stone's decision. The real club's own equipment choices are post-divergence coaching decisions and never Jacksonville anchors, just as the 2014 career did not copy the real club's July 27, 2014 shoulder-pad day. The first *eligible* padded day does not compel a team to use pads on it.

The 2015 reduction deadlines are September 1 before 4 p.m. ET to 75, September 5 before 4 p.m. ET to 53. They belong to Caldwell's distinct cut workflow ([final cuts design](final_roster_cuts.md)). The preliminary 90-player camp roster limit and any contemporaneous special exemptions or exempt statuses require a **dated legal roster check**, not a blanket assumption that every roster lists 90 or that every player can dress.

## 4. Competition eligibility and state machine

A *battle* is opened only from an actual dated authority/evidence record satisfying all three predicates:

1. Named, precise football job genuinely contested, such as **first-team left guard beside the named center** or **long snapper for the punt and place-kick operation**; a roster spot, fallback role or formation alignment alone is not always a battle.
2. At least two legally available or conditionally eligible **actual candidate people** under club control or authorized participation, with each candidate's limitations recorded. A medically unavailable player may remain a candidate but cannot be scheduled unlawfully.
3. An actual comparative coaching intent and real available opportunity to examine the relevant assignment; not just an unassigned roster vacancy, player requested workout, camp speculation or an assumed competition to create drama.

A card shows one of the card template's statuses: **Open**, a **conditional alternative**, **Decided [date]**, or **Ended without a decision [date]** when the job itself disappears. Technical states may exist in the data, but no status code appears on a reader page. A provisional role choice does not necessarily close a live contest; a decided competition must point to the My Depth Calls edition that decided it and its effective date. Each card's data names its canonical spot, eligible candidate set, first-group starting point and sources. Every card still open closes with Stone's season depth chart at the reduction to 53, at the latest ([final cuts design](final_roster_cuts.md)); a later change is an ordinary in-season depth decision through the depth chart's update log.

For each actual contested position, keep ONE individually owned readable document in `career/Y/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Position_Battles/{Offense|Defense|Special_Teams}/{position}/{spot}.md`; use `foundation/templates/position_battle_card_template.md`, not a parallel battle-card format. The [battle closure](../foundation/templates/position_battles/battle_closure_template.md) is that same card's closing section, folded into the card template at the build. A contest is not closed by silently removing it from the open list. Retain dated original evidence and status history. Do not reopen the six 2014 cards after August 30.

Each session's facts go only in the camp `Training_Camp/training_report.md` (actual work), and the battle card cites them; `Training_Camp/staff_plan.md` keeps intended work and receives no finding. From 2015 a practice records only its facts, never a result or a verdict on how a player looked ([section 11](#11-the-users-decisions)); a battle's performance evidence is the plays the games produce. For a preseason game, or a scrimmage run as a game through the same game path, read its own `Preseason_Games/Game_0N/` completed `Preseason_Game_Report.md` ([preseason games design](preseason_games.md)) or the preserved 2014 `output.md`, plus participation data if available. The source must support assignment attribution. If the game record shows only a sack *slot*, do not invent a failed punch or busted protection by a named player.

## 5. Football evidence and fair comparison rules

**From 2015, the evidence a battle is decided on.** The user's decision of October 20, 2026 ([section 11](#11-the-users-decisions)) fixes it: the play-by-play of the preseason games and of any scrimmage run as a game through the same game path; each candidate's health and availability; and Stone's depth calls. A practice contributes only its session facts (date, session type, equipment, who took part and in what capacity, what was installed and which periods were run, time on the field), never a result, and staff assessment text on the brief and the cards cites only those facts and those plays. The rest of this section applies to the game plays, and to the closed 2014 records as written.

The meaningful record for each candidate and question: exact date, work form (unopposed, nonpadded, padded controlled contact, scrimmage, external preseason game), first or reserve group, adjacent blockers and receiver/back group, defensive opponent and alignment, coverage help/pressure design, assignment actually asked, intervention from staff, observed result and whether the cause is established. Where a sample has no target, protection rep, rush or fault assigned, explicitly report that it failed to test the requested skill. Statistics (including sacks and catches) are evidence of a play, not an automatic personalized grade or a direct counterfactual about a player's NFL ability.

Players are evaluated only on taught material. Any call in the active playbook may be named on a game plan, preseason included, for any situation; only the no-hindsight lock refuses a call from a later book. A comparison judges a player only on the calls and techniques the staff has taught.

Use comparable contextual opportunities when lawful and sensible, but **do not force mathematically equal rep shares** that ignore practice teaching, availability and the game plan. A late entering reserve can supply important evidence of cold operation that the starter never faced. Cross-training at a second position does not start a new battle until the separate job actually becomes contested. A player competing at WR1 and WR3 gets one personal evidence history and two independent role comparisons.

No universal performance win points, repeating elite/plus/etc grades, simulated 10-point labels, arbitrary trust bars, automatic development gains, automatic rookie failure or guaranteed "earned" leap. Personnel-grade permissions in the canonical annual-card method do not license unsourced new camp numbers. Coach decisions under mixed evidence are allowed, but the report must label the decision as Stone's judgment rather than manufactured proof.

1. Read actual active simulated date, career job, roster rights, medical status, current depth and active playbook. Do not load real future 2015 Jaguars' transactions, final outcomes or performance as if they were career facts.
2. Obtain actual settled spring reports and latest camp/game evidence, including any pending comparison promised from a prior block; no unplayed session generates facts, and a 2015 practice adds only its session facts ([section 11](#11-the-users-decisions)).
3. Validate each contest against its exact job and participants; when valid, create one card under the existing by-unit/by-position path. Preserve open/conditional status and first-group source.
4. Build the `Position_Battle_Report.md` from the *as-of* owner facts, organized by useful decisions, at the camp plan's review points. Save an immutable issued edition with its Issued and Edition lines. Reissue only under the one reissue rule; an informational event that affects no pending answer waits for the next scheduled issue, and its own result page is readable as soon as it is written.
5. Events inside camp are inserts at the top of the active brief, under its title block, in the shared [insert template](../foundation/templates/event_inserts/brief_action_inserts_template.md)'s three sections: ACTION REQUIRED for an answer Stone owes, FOR YOUR INFORMATION for news and for what a standing instruction or someone else has already answered, COMING UP for a window ahead; each section is omitted when empty. Camp overlaps the preseason games and the cuts, so two briefs may carry inserts at the same time; each event appears once, on its own phase's brief, by the [August routing table](#which-brief-carries-an-event-in-august) below. Stone answers each inserted event on the My Responses page of the brief that carries it, never on My Depth Calls.
6. Stone fills `My_Depth_Calls.md` once per edition; the check confirms names against the roster record, current eligibility, medical availability, positions, packages and roles, and whether he is making a decision or explicitly deferring, and tells him exactly what is missing or invalid before anything is applied. It must not force closure or silently adopt a coach's view. A temporary depth replacement is a depth call on this page, not an inserted event; when it is due before the next scheduled issue, the brief is reissued under the reissue rule's third case. It becomes an insert only when a roster transaction by Caldwell is involved. Persist the exact answer and its issue date.
7. Apply Stone's actual depth or rotation instruction the way the Game Plan is applied (`apply_game_plan.py`): the game and working depth charts, one dated `update_log.json` entry with its sources, and an applied record whose one event line sits on the camp index and lists in `Record.md`. If several contests are decided at once, the charts are written once, one line goes to the record, and each contest gets its *own dated closure* in its individual card, linking his page. Reject duplicate owners or conflicting role assignments.
8. Build `Depth_Chart.md` as the phase result from the applied record as of its issue date, the roster record, medical constraints and the dated card closures. Validate it against the update-log state on its own issue date, never against the current chart, so a later dated change (a Week 1 move, say) updates the team depth chart page and leaves the issued edition as it was. Show open contests as open, not winners.
9. If Stone recommends a cut at the 75 or 53 deadline, the recommendation goes on My Cut Recommendations in the [final cuts workflow](final_roster_cuts.md); the actual cut, reserve status, waiver, claim and signing each close under the Caldwell-owned transaction owners and Final 53. A roster move outside those deadlines (a signing, release or waiver claim during camp) is an inserted event, decided by Caldwell on Stone's recommendation. A claim on another club's waived player follows the waiver order. A cut can change the candidate pool but cannot retroactively prove a training outcome or automatically choose a replacement starter for Stone.
10. Every individual event (including a rejected player contract negotiation, a refused trade, an unsuccessful coach pursuit or a later waived player) resolves on its proper simulated date in one dated result page, a version of the event result template, in its owner's folder: roster moves in Team_Operations/Team/Roster, signings and undrafted pursuits in Team_Operations/Free_Agency, trades in Team_Operations/Trades, staff in Team_Operations/Staff, Stone's own career in career/Stone. An existing standalone owner record, such as a completed trade, is that event's result. One shared `My_Responses.md` does **not** mean one shared event-result file. The phase's index and result page may link them, never stand in for them. Only an answer due before the event it holds stops the clock: a camp depth answer due before a preseason kickoff holds that game's inputs and nothing else, and an open battle or event never holds unrelated practice or other clubs' games.
11. Any newly communicated medical restriction constrains the lineup at the instant legally required; it does not backdate an earlier practice nor change the appointed role unless Stone decides. Return-to-activity stays with medical staff.
12. A legitimate later change to a closed depth role is a *new dated Stone adjustment*, not a rewrite of a past August comparison. Historic game inputs, old closed battle cards and Week 5-and-earlier content stay intact.

## 8. Regression tests proposed, not executed

### Era calendar, camp contact and safety

1. 2015 rookies/quarterbacks/first-year players have their July 27 conditional report route; ordinary veterans July 30; these dates are not automatic attendance.
2. The published July 7, 2015 club camp schedule is not presented as already public in the October 2014 head-coach briefing, and the preseason fixtures are not presented before their April 20, 2015 release.
3. July 31 first practice, the August 5 announced first full pads and the August 8 stadium scrimmage are schedule facts only, used no earlier than July 7, 2015; no real club equipment choice (such as the real club's shoulder-pad days) becomes a Jacksonville anchor or test value.
4. August 4/9 announced days off cannot become scheduled compulsory team practices without a separately lawful and recorded amendment, and any schedule keeps Art. 35 §1's one day off in every seven and five in all.
5. 2015 camp does not import the real Jaguars' 2015 player evaluation, injury, personnel or starter.
6. Any day-one on-field activity other than running and conditioning is refused under Art. 23 §6(a), day one counted from the player's own report day.
7. A padded or contact session on a player's second or third camp day, counted from his own report day, is refused.
8. Permitted non-padded group and team work on legal later days is accepted.
9. Only one padded practice per day per player can be committed.
10. A second daily session is a nonhelmet walkthrough within four combined on-field hours.
11. A padded practice longer than three hours of on-field activities, counted from when position coaches begin coaching on the field, is refused.
12. A second practice with less than the required three-hour break is refused.
13. A period's contact level comes from the recorded practice instruction and is never assumed from the word "padded"; tackling to the ground in a lawful padded practice is accepted when the instruction calls for it.
14. A completed preseason game is accounted as its own actual game record, not an OTA or padded camp-practice entry.
15. Camp roster count and status are checked against the 2015 era's 90-player rules and exemptions, not an assumed actual attendance count.
16. Medical staff's uncommunicated or projected clearance never qualifies a player for contact.
17. A medically excused absence or a lawful contract hold is not classified as low effort or a lost battle.
18. Summer player-directed preparation is not represented as a coach-run June/July team practice.
19. The 2014 closed camp practices and reports remain byte-immutable through the Week 5 cutoff.
20. The 2014 camp historical off-day discrepancy is flagged for old-record research rather than silently shifting a completed practice date.

### True competition and football evidence

21. A roster vacancy with no two genuine contenders creates no battle card.
22. A cross-training look for a reserve player creates no fictional competition unless the target job itself is contested.
23. The program refuses to compare a name absent from the club's lawful participants.

## 9. End-to-end acceptance scenario

A legitimate 2015 position contest is *first* created only after Stone has reached 2015 camp on his own results, lawful club participants and two real contender roles with a dated coaching instruction. Sessions X and Y add their session facts (padded, both candidates full participants, the periods run); preseason game 1's play-by-play supplies each candidate's snaps and plays; the opening cards supply prior evidence. The current battle card is updated with date and situation, without authoring a universal player grade. The issued camp brief shows a football comparison, not a fictional "100 point" race.

If Stone chooses the starter, his applied `My_Depth_Calls` writes the depth data and one update-log entry, gives the battle's original card **one dated closure** that can be read alone and links his page, and the Depth Chart edition issued that day links the card. Caldwell may or may not later make a different cut/contract decision; if he does, it gets its own result. An active trade proposition can be inserted in the brief and answered on the camp `My_Responses.md`, but no trade happens until Caldwell and the counterpart actually close it. The camp index links that trade's own result page. Reopening the brief or re-rendering the team chart cannot change the event, change an issued Depth Chart edition or produce a second owner.

## 10. Realism limits and blockers

1. **No practice resolution, by decision**: 2014's historical record is detailed, but there is no game-quality process for practice, and under the user's decision of October 20, 2026 none is needed: a 2015 practice records its facts, and performance evidence comes from the games ([section 11](#11-the-users-decisions)). The workflow accepts a valid user plan and records open questions; it never asserts a practice outcome.
2. **Current depth versus phase presentation**: the depth data are the one source of truth; each card carries its own dated closure; each Depth Chart edition is checked against the update log on its own issue date. A second mutable `Depth_Chart.md`, or one that follows the live chart, would create contradictions.
3. **Card closures and the annual record**: the 2014 records have individual cards and one combined August 30 event line in `roster_decisions.md`. From 2015 the same shape holds: one record line per applied set of depth calls, carried on the camp index, and one dated closure on each card it closed. Inserted events still get one result page each.
4. **2015 roster and medical unknown**: no prospective true-contender list, future injury, cut, camp win or replacement is valid yet. The historical 2015 Jaguars' publicly reported competitions are examples of NFL practice, not Stone's roster.
5. **Public camp calendar vs club choice**: the July 7 announcement does not prove every practice was held or a player participated; the initial announced practice list ends August 12. Do not invent the remainder.
6. **Authority crossover**: conditional lineups after cuts require a Stone answer and a separate Caldwell transaction. Automatic "best 53" selection or medical clearance is prohibited.
7. **Single bulk preseason turn**: Document 7 §5.1 still resolves the preseason as one consolidated turn presented at the transition into the regular season, and Document 1 line 229 names the eight-section preseason output template, while this design issues camp pages during camp. The user approved rewording both on October 20, 2026; the rewording is done in the step 8 build, when the per-game preseason pages exist ([section 11](#11-the-users-decisions)).
