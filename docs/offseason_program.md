## 4. 2011 CBA legality model

Use the August 4, 2011 CBA, not a later NFL collective bargaining agreement. Relevant text: Article 21 sections 1, 2(a)-(c), 3, 5(a)-(c), 6, 8-9; Article 22 for minicamps; Appendix G. Always select actual year rules and club calendar in future seasons. The library owns these rules. Today it holds section 2(a), section 2(c) and the Phase One classroom basis ([2014 offseason phase rules](../library/2014_offseason_phase_rules_verification.md)); the readings below of sections 3, 5(b), 5(c), 6, 8 and 9 and Appendix G were checked by the process check against the 2011 text and await a two-pass library entry (section 7). Until that entry exists they are this design's reading, not a library record.

**Before the formal program:** under Article 21 section 2(a), club-supervised workouts, meetings with coaches, coach-supervised group or individual film/playbook study and directed strength sessions are prohibited. Independent personal workouts with facility access are a different matter; they are not a club program or staff evaluation. Earlier preparation by coaches is permissible; a coach's undated prepared film packet is not a delivered player packet. Do not silently assume passive playbook or film distribution was permitted before the formal start if the method was not verified.

**Phase One:** physical work limited to strength, conditioning and rehabilitation. Only full- or part-time strength/conditioning coaches with no other club coaching responsibilities may coach/participate in/observe field activity. No helmets or footballs except the agreement's narrow quarterback throwing exception (an uncovered receiver, no defending player), which is not a position-coached pass drill. The CBA names classroom instruction as part of the voluntary program (sections 1, 3 and 5(a)) but nowhere expressly permits classroom coaching in Phase One; the basis for **football teaching in classrooms** once the program begins is the contemporary Packers (June 1, 2013) and Chiefs (April 21, 2014) club explanations, recorded in the [2014 offseason phase rules](../library/2014_offseason_phase_rules_verification.md). No punt snap/kick unit drill is assumed lawful in Phase One merely because an old Stone note proposed it. Reconcile any discrepancy to the actual CBA before execution.

**Phase Two:** coaches may conduct individual football instruction and separate offense-only, defense-only or special-teams-only perfect-play periods. No helmets, live contact, offense versus defense, offensive/defensive 1-on-1s or opposed special-teams drills. A front drawn on a card is not a live opponent. An ordinary on-air protection success cannot be graded as sound protection under rush.

**Time and voluntariness:** Article 21 section 1 and 5(a) protect choice to attend both workouts and classes. On program days other than the ten OTA days and minicamps, a club may have each player at facilities at most 4 hours per day and 4 weekdays per week, with at most 90 minutes per day on the field (section 5(b)), and may schedule no more than four workouts a week for any individual player and none on weekends (section 2(a)). The club may specify no more than 2 hours per day; the player selects the remaining eligible workout time within open weight-room hours. The check runs on the club's schedule for each player, through the group schedule he is assigned to: no more than four weekday workouts a week, two club-specified hours a day, 90 field minutes and no weekend sessions, with contact type and phase checked the same way. No per-player attendance log is kept; the 2014 program recorded no attendance and used none as evidence. No contact work during these phases. The minimum workout compensation and any specially signed participation agreement are paid/handled by the contract and club operations departments, not decided by Stone.

**Participation eligibility:** signed under-contract players are invited. A player with an unsigned Required Tender, or an unrestricted free agent whose contract with the club has expired, may be invited, but may take part only after he and Caldwell's office sign an Offseason Workout and Minicamp Participation Agreement under Article 21 sections 3 and 9. Stone invites the player, under his practice authority (job record row 11); Caldwell's office executes the agreement (row 5), and the signing reaches Stone as FOR YOUR INFORMATION. Until he signs a contract or that agreement, the player takes part in neither the program nor its social events, and a catch-up plan is ready for the day he signs, as in 2014 (the user's May 1, 2014 instruction, [memo amendment](../career/2014/Early_Offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-unsigned-tenders-and-the-cross-training-list)). Do not assume an agreement exists. No draft choice becomes a contracted veteran merely by appearing on a draft list. Rookie entry additionally follows Article 22 and calendar/eligibility rules.

**Safety and legal records:** medical staff direct rehabilitation and clearance. Article 21 section 5(c) requires club filming of on-field sessions in all three phases and retention until 30 days after the start of the regular season; record necessary compliance material separately from a player grade. Under Article 21 section 8, the head coach and club can be held responsible for violations; a "better" football session does not justify a legal shortcut. Independent work after the program and before camp remains subject to Article 21 section 6, not a loophole for unsanctioned club practice.

The page is answered once and kept as the record of his decision; it is never rewritten after he answers. An answer to an inserted event, and any later change of direction Stone gives himself, goes on the April My Responses page as a dated entry that applies going forward.

Parser/validator behind the page normalizes **substantive decisions** (which teaching assignment, unit, legal phase, dates, named players and responsible staff) into private decision metadata. ### 6.3 Phase-level dependencies and time gates

The present `runtime/decision_queue.py` has a `practice_plan` class but no event-specific blocker for starting or closing these two offseason phases; its generic block kinds cover game inputs, call sheets and season staging/opening. A new guarded *phase workflow* is required. Implement either a carefully extended queue block type (keyed to season and `phase_one`/`phase_two`) or a separate atomic phase gate wired to queue semantics; do not pretend that merely adding prose to a current queue item stops a script.

Proposed sequence:

- `RESEARCH_ONLY`: upcoming dates known, no new team season activated, no report/assessment fabricated.
- `ELIGIBLE_TO_ISSUE`: prior season really closed, employee has authority and participating program has a legally valid scheduled start and a recorded club filing event. No event committed.
- `AWAITING_STONE` if an actual uncovered practice decision is open. An effective standing instruction can satisfy what it actually covers and cannot be silently extended to a new choice.
- `PLAN_ADOPTED`: Stone's decision fixed, first dependent session may be prepared if attendance, medical, contract and CBA checks pass.
- `PHASE_ONE_ACTIVE` and `PHASE_ONE_REVIEWED`: each actual session advances only when authorized; Phase One report and updated teaching questions verified before Phase Two is materially built.
- `PHASE_TWO_ACTIVE` and `PHASE_TWO_REVIEWED`: separate-unit sessions and the Phase Two report are resolved without an illegal opponent.
- `PROGRAM_REPORTED`: the Program Report issued only after actual Phase Two closure, or a documented partial/terminated program is reported explicitly as such. No silent completion merely because May 22 passes.

Guards must be **dependency-specific**. Only an answer due before the event it holds stops the clock: an open decision over a revised Phase Two installation blocks only the affected Phase Two work, not Caldwell's parallel draft, unrelated free agency or other clubs' league events. Do not reuse a global game-week pause to block April unrelated actions. Resuming twice must not add two sessions or two decisions. The bridge must preserve date order, past issued pages and an unplayed 2014 Week 6 while this is only design work.

The present week-game private kernel is **not** an April practice model. Keep an observation record with: practice date, phase and legal mode; person(s) actually eligible and participating; responsible coach; existing installed call/assignment and evidence source; what happened in a defined opportunity; correction made; later independent check, if any; medical limitations; and what cannot be inferred.
