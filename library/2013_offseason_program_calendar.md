# Library: Jacksonville 2013 Offseason-Program Calendar

**Status:** Research pass and separate verification pass completed September 19, 2026.  This file supplies the Jacksonville-specific dates needed to run the 2013 offseason phases. It does not import any post-divergence roster, signing, injury, depth-chart, or performance outcome.

## Scope and controlling rules

The 2011 NFL-NFLPA CBA Article 21 controls the offseason structure. Clubs with a new head coach may open Phase One on the first Monday in April rather than the third Monday, and may conduct one additional voluntary veteran minicamp. Article 21 also separates the nine-week program into two weeks of Phase One, three weeks of Phase Two, and four weeks of Phase Three, with up to ten OTA days in Phase Three. Rookie minicamp is separately permitted on one of the first two weekends after the draft. [2011 NFL-NFLPA CBA, Article 21](https://nflpaweb.blob.core.windows.net/website/PDFs/CBA/March-15-2018-NFL-NFLPA-Collective-Bargaining-Agreement-Final-Executed-Copy.pdf)

## Discrepancy carried forward without rewriting history

Jacksonville was a new-head-coach club and therefore had both the April 1 early program start and the April 16-18 extra voluntary veteran minicamp. That minicamp fell squarely between the March 12 league-year/free-agency opening and the April 25 draft. The active career ledger had already closed the March 12 free-agency batch and then compressed all pre-draft activity into a March 12-April 24 window without logging this football phase. Per the user's instruction, this research does **not** rewrite those closed entries or manufacture retroactive attendance/performance. The rookie-minicamp output records the calendar omission as a continuity discrepancy; it does not pretend the April event was run.

## Evidence limits

- The cited calendar establishes when activities were scheduled, not who attended or how anyone performed in this counterfactual branch.
- Phase One and Phase Two are derived from Article 21's fixed structure beginning April 1; the exact day-by-day Jacksonville internal itinerary is not independently recovered and is not invented.
- Later actual Jacksonville signings, injuries, roster decisions, and camp outcomes were not used.
