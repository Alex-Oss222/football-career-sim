# Library: 2013 NFL awards structure and calendar

**Status:** Research pass and separate verification pass completed September 27, 2026. This file records the **structure and calendar** of the NFL's 2013 weekly, monthly and season honours, so the simulation can award its own branch winners on the real rails. It is research only. It changes no canon, roster, standings or calendar fact.

**Method:** The project's two-pass discipline applies. The first pass found each claim. The second pass searched each claim again from scratch with different query wording and, where possible, a different outlet. Each item is labelled **Confirmed** (two or more independent sources agree, or one primary league/AP source plus an independent corroboration), **Corrected** (the first pass or one source was wrong; the correction and reason are stated), **Pattern** (consistent practice seen across several 2013 instances, but no governing rule text reached) or **Unverified** (one source only, a non-2013 source only, or an inference).

**Access limits (read before relying on this file):** As with `2013_suspension_reinstatement_rules.md`, outbound page fetches were blocked by this environment's egress policy (nfl.com team sites, en.wikipedia.org and others were tried). **Every source below was read through search-engine result text (title, URL, date metadata and extracted passage), not by opening the page.** A future session with page access should re-open the URLs and upgrade or correct the labels.

**Hindsight boundary:** Several source articles below are titled with, or announce, real 2013 winners. They are cited **only** for the date, category, selection method or games-covered facts they carry. No winner, finalist, vote total or statistical line from them is recorded here, and none may be imported as an answer key (Document 1 §8, Document 2 §4.3).

Notes:
- The fan-voted weekly awards are sponsor programmes, not league-office honours. Their weekly results fed season-end fan-voted awards (FedEx Air & Ground Players of the Year; Pepsi NEXT Rookie of the Year, chosen from finalists drawn from the weekly winners), both presented at NFL Honors. **Confirmed** (W6, W8, S4).
- Other sponsor or media weekly features (for example outlet-specific "teams of the week") are not league awards and were not surveyed.

## 2. Monthly awards

**Categories (2013):** AFC and NFC Offensive, Defensive and Special Teams Player of the Month (six), plus a league-wide (not conference-split) **NFL Offensive Rookie of the Month** and **NFL Defensive Rookie of the Month**. Selected by the league office. **Confirmed** (M1-M4; the October and December releases carry all categories).

- **Corrected:** one local-TV headline for December 2013 labelled the defensive rookie award as "AFC". The NFL.com monthly releases and the club's own site treat both rookie awards as single league-wide awards (M2, M4). The league-wide form controls.

| 2013 month | Weeks covered | Last game covered | Announced | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| September | Weeks 1-4 | Mon Sept 30 (Week 4 MNF) | **Thu Oct 3, 2013** | Weeks: **Confirmed** (the September release describes a player's figures as "the first four games of a season"). Date: **Confirmed** (M1 date metadata; second search independently returned Oct 3) |
| October | Weeks 5-8 | Mon Oct 28 (Week 8 MNF) | **Thu Oct 31, 2013** | Weeks: **Confirmed** (October release describes "four starts"; Cincy Jungle item dated 2013/10/31). Date: **Confirmed** (M2, M2b) |
| November | Weeks 9-12 | Mon Nov 25 (Week 12 MNF) | **Wed Nov 27, 2013** (the day before Thanksgiving) | Date: **Confirmed** (M3 metadata; Eagles club release same cycle). Week span: **Pattern** (inferred from the announcement falling after Week 12 and before Week 13) |
| December | Weeks 13-17 | Sun Dec 29 (Week 17) | **Thu Jan 2, 2014** | Date: **Confirmed** (M4, M4b ProFootballTalk URL dated 2014/01/02, CBS Denver URL dated 2014/01/02). Five-game span including Sun Dec 1 (Week 13) and Week 17: **Confirmed** (M4 describes "five December games"; M4b describes a 5-0 December record) |

Edge games:
- **Week 17 / late December:** 2013 Week 17 was played entirely on Sun Dec 29, so it sits inside the December award; there was no separate "January" award for 2013. **Confirmed** (M4).
- **Thursday Oct 31 (Week 9 TNF)** and **Thanksgiving Thu Nov 28 (Week 13)** fall in one calendar month but a week that belongs to the next award window. Whether the league assigns such games by NFL week or by calendar date was **not found** in any source. **Unverified**. The branch should state its rule before it closes the first affected month; assigning by NFL week is the reading consistent with the announcement dates above, but it is an inference.

### 3.4 Walter Payton NFL Man of the Year (note only)

Each club names one nominee (32 total); the national winner is presented at NFL Honors (February 1, 2014). It is a community and character award decided by a selection panel, not a football-performance award. Club-nominee structure and NFL Honors presentation: **Confirmed** (W10, A2). The 2013 panel membership, nominee-announcement date and finalist date: **Unverified** (sources found describe other cycles). The branch should not award it from simulated statistics.

### 3.5 Other honours presented at NFL Honors (note only)

FedEx Air & Ground Players of the Year and Pepsi NEXT Rookie of the Year (fan-voted, from the weekly programmes); the Deacon Jones Award (a statistical award to the regular-season sacks leader, not a vote). PFWA season awards were announced separately in mid-January 2014 by the writers' association. Existence: **Confirmed** (A8, S4, A9). None is an AP award.

## 5. What this file does not contain

- **No 2013 winner, finalist, nominee, vote total, captain pairing or Pro Bowl selection** for any weekly, monthly or season honour. Those are post-event outcomes and forbidden answer keys; the branch awards its own winners from its own simulated season.
- No winners from 2012 or earlier seasons.
- No Pro Bowl game result.
- No real 2013 statistical lines, even where a source quoted them to justify an award.
