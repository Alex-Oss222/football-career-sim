## Findings that change the planning instructions

**The schedule decision is due earlier than April 21.** Article 21 section 2(c) requires submission to the NFL/NFLPA by their agreed date, at least 21 days before the program begins, and advance written notice of changes. April 21 minus 21 days is March 31. That is the latest filing date, not proof that no earlier 2014 administrative deadline existed. Caldwell's office must establish that date and preserve the actual filing receipt. April 3 public publication is a different event.

**Pre-program coaching and passive distribution are different questions.** Article 21 section 2(a) prohibits coach-led meetings, film and playbook study before the program. The February 10 club report independently confirms the football-discussion restriction. No affirmative permission for the particular pending film-distribution process was established. Prepare internally; absent a verified earlier lawful route, propose delivery and discussion from April 21. Do not backdate January receipts. See the agreement and the February 10 Jaguars report below.

**Phase One can include classroom football teaching.** Contemporary club explanations distinguish classroom instruction from the restricted field period. This supports film discussion and protection-language teaching beginning with the program, within its time limits. It does not permit position coaches to conduct or observe field instruction in Phase One. See the contemporary Packers and Chiefs explanations below.
