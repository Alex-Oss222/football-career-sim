# 2014 NFL calendar

League dates for all clubs, followed by the 2014 season's January–February 2015 postseason. All times are Eastern. Team-specific appointments belong in the club calendar, including [Jacksonville's schedule](../career/2014/Calendar.md). Calendar dates establish when something may happen; they do not record participation, transactions, selections or results.

This consolidates the calendar portions of the former league-calendar/financial research and its verification addendum. Historical dates and fixtures control, including same-place opponents. Research checked September 28–October 1, 2026. The source section records when announcements were available; later information must not be used early in career play.

Each club's nine-week program comprises two weeks of Phase One, three of Phase Two and four of Phase Three. The club files its calendar under the [2011 CBA filing rule](2014_offseason_phase_rules_verification.md); phase dates follow its start date. Actual OTA and minicamp dates vary by club. The published [all-club offseason dates](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-offseason-workout-program-dates-announced-0ap2000000339264) and club amendments supply those dates, not a uniform national practice calendar.

### Corrections and remaining limits

The core December 2013 league-calendar publication was established in the earlier research, conservatively by December 21. The exact December 20 release date was not verified. For entries first recovered from the February club bulletins, February 2 replaces the old unsupported late-January assumption. May 7 matching and June 16 tender deadlines are directly confirmed. September opening weekend was already public by February 2, before the April fixture release.

The false April 20 Jacksonville start and Sunday OTA dates came from a rejected search summary. The dated club announcements now control. Phase One April 21–May 2, Phase Two May 5–23 and Phase Three May 26–June 22 are derived spans; daily staff meetings and program-day choices are club appointments. July 30 first pads and August 2 scrimmage were published July 24, not unsourced simulation selections.

The historical July 29 rest/July 31 practice schedule conflicts with Jacksonville's closed branch camp record. The club calendar identifies that discrepancy; this research does not relocate its observations. Atlanta's 6 p.m. kickoff is verified by August 25; an earlier notice remains unrecovered. The preseason weekend ranges follow the published schedule; they do not create additional games or practices.

The cap-carryover notice deadline remains unresolved between December 26 and 27 in the older sources; consult the [financial rules](2014_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md) before filing. December 26 in this calendar is the distinct waiver deadline. Reserve/PUP and Reserve/NFI practice-return sources agree on October 14 but conflict on the last entry day: the Bengals September release says after Week 9, while the [Jaguars August 26 notice](https://www.jaguars.com/news/jaguars-reduce-roster-to-75-13558266) says November 18, after Week 11. Do not use either conflicting end date to clear an individual case without the governing notice. Full-league release-era kickoff uncertainties remain with the fixture sources. Future-rule, medical, personnel and game-result information elsewhere on these source pages is excluded.
