# 2014 NFL financial rules and personnel procedures

This file owns salary-cap, contract, tender and draft-order research. Dates and their verification are consolidated in the [2014 NFL calendar](2014_nfl_calendar.md). [Jacksonville's calendar](../career/2014/Calendar.md) adds club appointments and games. No actual historical transaction, selection or result is a branch outcome.

The financial and draft research below originated in separate September 28, 2026 searches. Its original access was limited to indexed source text. Later direct checks are identified in the relevant sections; the calendar's newly opened sources do not upgrade unrelated financial figures. Confirmed means the stated research corroborated the claim; Unverified means it still must not be used as a certified amount or deadline. Existing adopted simulation contract reconstructions retain their separate basis in their contract owner.

The offseason roster limit is 90; the dated reductions are to 75 and then 53, with a 46-player game-day active list. These carry forward the [2013-era roster rules](2013_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md). Reserve/future contracts apply to the next league year and its offseason roster, subject to eligibility. The eight-player practice squad becomes ten only from the August 19, 2014 [NFL/NFLPA agreement](https://nflpa.com/press/nfl-nflpa-agree-to-expand-practice-squads). The agreement also changes service-counting and eligibility; consult its text for an individual signing rather than treating the roster size as its only condition.

## 3. 2014 scheduling formula (historical background)

**Formula (2002 realignment, in force for 2014): 16 games.**

| # | Component | Games | Label | Sources |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| S1 | Home and away against each division rival | 6 | Confirmed | https://operations.nfl.com/calendar-events/nfl-schedule/making-the-schedule ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NFL_regular_season |
| S2 | All four clubs of one other division in the same conference, on a **three-year rotation** (two home, two away) | 4 | Confirmed | Same as S1 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_NFL_season |
| S3 | All four clubs of one division in the other conference, on a **four-year rotation** (two home, two away) | 4 | Confirmed | Same as S1 |
| S4 | **Same-place-finish games:** one game against the club in each of the **two remaining same-conference divisions** (the two divisions not drawn in S2) that finished in the **same place** in its division in the previous season as this club did in its own (first plays first, second plays second, and so on). One home, one away. | 2 | Confirmed | Same as S1 ; https://www.nbcsportsboston.com/nfl/nfl-schedule-explained-how-it-works/609618/ |

**2014 AFC South rotation (standings-independent):** every AFC South club plays **all of the AFC North** (S2) and **all of the NFC East** (S3). The two remaining AFC divisions for S4 are therefore the **AFC East** and the **AFC West**. Label: **Confirmed** (Big Cat Country, dated URL December 29, 2013, for Jacksonville; ESPN NFL Nation's 2014 opponents list and the Titans' 2014 opponents for Tennessee; Jaguars.com's 2014 home-schedule release).

**Jacksonville's 2014 home/away split for the rotation games (standings-independent, set by the formula):** home against **Cleveland, Pittsburgh, New York Giants and Dallas** (the Dallas game designated as Jacksonville's London home game on November 9); away at **Baltimore, Cincinnati, Philadelphia and Washington**. For S4, Jacksonville **hosts the same-place AFC East club** and **visits the same-place AFC West club**. Label: **Confirmed** (Big Cat Country December 29, 2013, and the Jaguars.com home-schedule release; the Titans' reported road games at Baltimore, Cincinnati and Philadelphia fit the same grid).

Historical dates and actual opponents now control. Use the [2014 NFL calendar](2014_nfl_calendar.md) and the frozen league fixtures; do not recompute same-place opponents from branch standings. Branch standings continue to determine the branch's playoff qualification and draft order.

## 5. Verification pass: corrections and discrepancies

- **V2. Non-playoff pick range: Corrected.** One pass 2 search summary, quoting a Record and Fact Book, said non-playoff clubs "select in the first through 18th positions". That wording belongs to the 14-club playoff era (from the 2020 season). In the 2013 season's 12-club format there were 20 non-playoff clubs, so they selected **1 to 20**, and playoff clubs 21 to 32, as the 2014-era sources (Patriots Draft 101, Stripe Hype February 3, 2014) state.
- **V6. Minimum-salary tiers.** Pass 1 supplied candidate values for every tier in the query. Pass 2 found search text independently stating only the 0 and 10+ values; the 3, 4 to 6 and 7 to 9 values appeared once in text that may echo the query, and the 1 and 2 values not at all. Those tiers are downgraded to Unverified or left unrecorded (§2a) rather than kept.
- **V7. Transition tag table.** Pass 1 found QB, RB and WR values; pass 2 found the full table in one search text. Only the three twice-found values are Confirmed (§2c).
- **V8. Carryover deadline.** Three incompatible descriptions of the notice deadline were found (F4); none is adopted.

## 6. Remaining source limitations

- Transition tag values other than QB, RB and WR; the 2014 Rookie Compensation Pool total. The minimum-salary table is directly verified in section 2a; individual credited service still requires evidence.
- The cap-carryover notice deadline. The December 26 ordinary-waiver deadline is a different rule and does not settle carryover.
- Multi-club division/conference draft-tie detail, the compensatory fill-to-32 mechanism and the forfeited-pick mechanism. Tied-block rotation is verified in the separate draft-order source.
- Calendar publication limits, later notices and the precise post-draft signing moment are collected in the [NFL calendar](2014_nfl_calendar.md#corrections-and-remaining-limits), not maintained here a second time.
