# Library: 2013 NFL Draft Pool Registry

**Status:** Pre-selection eligibility registry  
**Purpose:** Track who is known to be eligible without using actual draft results as an answer key.

This is deliberately separate from `2013_draft_class.md`, which is the detailed scouting snapshot.

## Completeness rule

There was no single clean, official pre-draft public master table located that enumerates every draft-eligible senior plus every special-eligibility player.

Accordingly:

- the **73-player special-eligibility group is complete**, because the NFL published the official list after the January 15 application deadline;
- the **senior pool is operational, not claimed exhaustive** and must be assembled from contemporaneous eligibility, combine, all-star, school, and scouting sources;
- the 333-player combine invitation list is useful coverage but is **not** an eligibility master list;
- absence from this registry never proves that a player was ineligible;
- before an unlisted player can be selected in the simulation, his 2013 eligibility must be verified from evidence available before the real draft began.

This is intentionally more conservative than using a modern drafted/undrafted database, because such a database reveals selection outcomes.

## Senior-pool coverage

The senior side of the operational pool should be built from contemporaneous sources rather than from the eventual draft result.

Permitted source types include:

- NFL Scouting Combine invitation lists.
- Senior Bowl and other recognized all-star rosters.
- Dated pre-draft national or team scouting boards.
- College roster/class-year records.
- Contemporaneous pro-day participant reports.
- Other pre-April-25, 2013 sources that establish identity and eligibility without relying on selection outcome.

NFL.com reported 333 combine invitees on February 6, 2013:
https://www.nfl.com/news/manti-te-o-heads-list-of-nfl-scouting-combine-invitees-0ap1000000136883

The combine list expands the operational senior pool but does not define its outer boundary.

## Runtime selection rule

A club may select:

1. any player already verified in this registry or a dated companion scouting source; or
2. an unlisted player only after the simulator verifies that player's 2013 draft eligibility from a pre-selection source.

The simulation must never infer eligibility from the fact that a player was actually drafted, signed as an undrafted free agent, or later appeared in the NFL.
