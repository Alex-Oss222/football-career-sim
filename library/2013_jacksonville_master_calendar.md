## Correction to the prior offseason calendar

The earlier project calendar stated that Jacksonville's official offseason program began April 1, 2013. That is incorrect.

Jacksonville's own April 1 preview says the program **begins April 2**, and its period coverage identifies April 2 as the first day the new coaching staff could begin football-specific meetings and coaching. The April 16-18 voluntary veteran minicamp, May 3-5 rookie minicamp, OTA dates, and June 11-13 mandatory minicamp remain verified.

The counterfactual simulation had already advanced past the April 16-18 voluntary minicamp without running it. This research correction does not manufacture retroactive attendance, installation, or performance. The missed phase remains a continuity gap in the season ledger.
