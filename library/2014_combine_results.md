# Library: 2014 NFL Scouting Combine results (Jacksonville board prospects)

**Status:** Pre-selection evidence file. Research pass (pass 1 of 2) and verification pass (pass 2 of 2) complete for the 23 prospects on Jacksonville's current [draft board](../career/2014/draft/player_draft_board.md), its named comparisons and its undrafted line list. Full-class coverage is **not built**.
**Purpose:** Record combine measurables, drill results, non-participation and eligibility status as they became public, without using any draft result as an answer key.
**Research date:** September 29, 2026. Branch date at entry: February 25, 2014 (ledger Entry 88).

This file is modelled on [the 2014 draft pool registry](2014_draft_pool_registry.md). Date gates are defined in [the 2014 draft information gates](2014_draft_information_gates.md).

## Information gate

The Combine ran February 19 to 25, 2014, at Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis; on-field workouts ran February 22 to 25, with offensive linemen and tight ends on February 22 (gates file). Each result below is usable in the branch only from its **public date** (the "Public" column, February 21 to 25, 2014). Eligibility facts carry their own earlier dates. Nothing after February 25, 2014 is recorded: pro-day results, later workouts, later medical or drug-test reports and every later event are excluded.

**What this file does not contain.** No grade, ranking or evaluation: grades are Stone's evaluation and remain open. No draft slot, drafting club, NFL statistic, contract, award or career outcome for any player. Where a source that confirmed a figure also carries post-draft material in its title, that source is withheld from the citations below and the figure's status is reduced to what the remaining sources support (see section 4).

## Source-access limitation (both passes)

Direct page retrieval (WebFetch) was not used; every figure comes from search-result text attributed to the cited URL, not from a first-hand read of the full page. "Confirmed" means at least two independent sources agreed. Many sources are later republications (March to May 2014, or retrospective pages) of combine measurements; they are cited only for the combine figure, never for anything they say about later events.

**Labels:** Confirmed (two independent sources); Single source; Unverified (conflicting or unconfirmed; not to be relied on); Inference (reasoned from a stated fact, with the reasoning given); DNP (did not participate in that drill at the Combine).

## 4. Verification pass and exclusions

**Pass 2 corrections.** Trai Turner's broad jump, 3-cone and shuttle figures from pass 1 were later-workout marks and are removed (he did not take those drills at the Combine). DeMarcus Lawrence's weight is 251, not the later listed 254. Telvin Smith's 40 is 4.52; the 4.54 variant is rejected. Kyle Van Noy's aggregator-only arm length is withdrawn. Christian Jones's wingspan is dropped. Bryan Stork's non-participation is confirmed but its reported reason rests on post-gate sources and is not recorded. Charles Leno's arm length and Cornelius Lucas's measurements are downgraded to Unverified for conflicts; Ross Cockrell's vertical, broad and shuttle are downgraded to Unverified.

**Pass 2 upgrades to Confirmed.** Donald vertical and shuttle; Fuller shuttle; Bitonio vertical, broad, 3-cone and shuttle; Van Noy vertical, 3-cone and shuttle; Lawrence 40 and bench; Telvin Smith's missing combine bench; James Hurst's invitation; Travis Swanson's drill marks; Matt Feiler's 40, vertical and shuttle; Tyler Shatley's non-invite.
