# Library: coach hiring and anti-tampering rules for the January 2014 hiring cycle

**Status:** Research pass A, an independent research pass B, a calibration pass and a separate skeptical verification pass completed September 28, 2026. This file records the **rules and procedure** that governed requests to interview and hire NFL coaches in the hiring cycle that followed the 2013 season (the January 2014 cycle): which coaches a club could ask for, when, with whose permission, what the employer club could refuse, when a hire could be made, and what the Rooney Rule required. Section 7 adds base-rate calibration from the January 2013 cycle. It is research only. It changes no canon, roster, staff, standings, calendar or state fact, and it decides nothing about any branch request.

**Method:** Passes A and B each researched the rules from scratch with different query wording and, where possible, different outlets. The calibration pass counted the January 2013 candidate field from the repository's verified pre-hire library. The verification pass then (a) compared A and B claim by claim, treating an item the two passes reached through different publishers as two routes; (b) checked every date against the repository's verified calendar files; (c) redid all date arithmetic and all calibration arithmetic; and (d) downgraded any label that rested on one route, one publisher, or a source that could not be tied to a season. Where A and B disagreed and the disagreement could not be settled from the evidence both passes recorded, both readings are given and the item is Unverified.

Labels:

- **Confirmed**: two or more independent sources from different publishers agree, and either one of them is contemporaneous with the 2013 season or the January 2013 or January 2014 cycles, or sources on both sides of 2013 agree and one states that the rule ran unchanged until a dated later change.
- **Pattern**: documented before and after 2013, or in repeated practice, with no amendment found, but with no 2013 text seen or with fewer independent routes than Confirmed needs.
- **Unverified**: one source or one route only, a policy text whose version year is unknown, an inference, or absence of evidence.
- **Corrected**: a pass, a search summary, a source, a label or a task premise was wrong; the fix and the reason are stated here and in §9.

**Terms used here:** the *employer club* holds the coach's contract; the *inquiring club* wants to talk to him; a club is *alive* while it is still playing in the postseason; a *lateral* request is any request for a job other than head coach.

## 1. Who may be asked, and with whose permission

### 1.1 Tiers, the protected promotion and lateral moves

| # | Rule in the January 2014 cycle | Label and sources |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | For anti-tampering enforcement the league recognized **only two tiers: Head Coach and Assistant Coach.** Every other title (offensive, defensive or special teams coordinator, co-coordinator, passing-game or run-game coordinator, assistant head coach, associate head coach, position coach) was an assistant title. | **Confirmed** (A and B independently: E1, E2 before 2013; L1, L2 describing the pre-2020 policy as two tiers until the May 2020 change; E10 January 2015; E16 2025; P3 low weight) |
| T2 | **The one protected move was to head coach.** Once an assistant's own season was over, his employer club could not stop another club from interviewing him for its head-coach vacancy. For an assistant of a club still alive, only the §2 windows applied. | **Confirmed** (A and B: E3 January 2013 cycle, "the job must be a promotion"; E2; E9 January 2016; E18; L1; L2) |
| T3 | **Every other request was lateral and could be refused.** A request to interview an assistant under contract for any assistant job, including a move from position coach to coordinator, a play-calling role or an "assistant head coach" title, was a lateral move, and the employer club was "under no obligation to grant" it. It could grant permission voluntarily. | **Confirmed** (policy text in P1/P2 extracts; E1, E2, E8, E10, E11; L1, L2 and L3 date the first protection of coordinator interviews to May 2020) |
| T4 | **No "bona fide promotion" test existed in 2013.** The protection for a bona fide offensive, defensive or special teams coordinator job (not a shared post; disputes decided by the Commissioner; a written staff organization chart with coordinator job descriptions; no restrictive contract terms; no contract until the employer's season ended) was adopted by resolution on May 19, 2020. | **Confirmed** as a later rule that does not apply (L1, L3, L4; both passes) |
| T5 | A play-calling promise or an "assistant head coach" title gave no protected status. Later explainers say the two-tier rule existed partly to stop clubs using nominal titles to pry assistants loose. | **Confirmed** (follows from T1 and T3; L1 for the reason) |
| T6 | The mandatory head-coach permission ran "from the day of their last game through March 1"; after March 1 the employer club had discretion. | **Unverified** (P1/P2 extract whose version is not identified, and P3; both passes found only these routes) |
| T7 | Form of a head-coach request: the inquiring club's owner or operating head contacts the employer club's owner or operating head to "request written permission". | **Unverified**. Reading A: P1 extract (2009 text). Reading B: no source requiring writing or requiring notice to the league office was found. Both readings stand. |
| T8 | A club that grants one lateral request "cannot grant [it] selectively" and must grant every requesting club. | **Unverified, two readings.** Reading A: a policy extract of unknown year states it for lateral moves. Reading B: a grant-one-grant-all rule is documented only for the 2020s and tied to the newer in-season interview windows (L12). Not enforced as a 2013 rule (§8). |
| T9 | Clubs keep assistants blockable by extending ("rolling") their contracts so they stay under contract for the coming season; some assistants let contracts run out to gain freedom. | **Unverified** (single later source, L16, found by both passes; no pre-2013 base rate of grants or refusals was found) |

### 1.2 Contract status: unemployed, expired, resigned, own staff

| # | Rule | Label and sources |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | A coach not employed by an NFL club, including one his club has dismissed, needs no permission and may be interviewed and hired at once. | **Confirmed** (E1, E2 "the only possible coaches that can be hired with no delay are the coaches that are currently unemployed"; H12 December 2018 league memo; E17 later explainer) |
| E2 | A coach whose contract has expired is free to seek employment with any club, for any position. | **Confirmed** (E1; H12 "once an employee's contract has expired, the employee is free to seek other employment"; P1/P2 extract) |
| E3 | Detail of E2: an assistant whose contract has expired but who keeps working for his club as a non-contract employee may not be denied permission, whether or not he is still paid and whether or not the request comes after March 1. | **Unverified** (P1/P2 extract only) |
| E4 | For an assistant whose contract is expiring, the employer club keeps exclusive rights through the **second Tuesday after the club's last game** (changed from the third Tuesday in March 2007). | **Unverified** (pass A only, and one publisher only: H4 2007, L16 2020 and L17 2025, all NFL.com search text; pass B did not find it). If the branch uses it, the dates are in §6. |
| E5 | A coach who resigns or retires before his contract expires is **not** free without the prior club's consent. | **Pattern** (H8 January 2000 Commissioner ruling on a head coach; H12 December 2018 memo, which the league described as a reminder of existing procedure; pass B only) |
| E6 | Whether the prior club may condition that consent on compensation when the coach is an assistant. | **Unverified, conflicting.** Reading B: the December 2018 memo says consent may be conditioned on compensation. Reading A: the policy bars draft-choice or cash exchanges for anyone but head coaches and high-level executives (C1). The two cannot both apply to an assistant; which governed in 2013 is not established. |
| E7 | An assistant's contract may not contain a right of first refusal for his employer club. | **Unverified, conflicting.** A policy extract (version not identified) states the ban; May 2020 reporting (L1) describes the 2020 resolution as newly barring "restrictive contract language, like the right to match". |
| E8 | A club may ask the league office about any assistant's contract status without the employer club's permission; the league discloses the contract's term only, never financial terms. | **Unverified** (P3 forum transcription and a P1 extract; the same routes in both passes) |
| E9 | A club may interview its own staff at any time. | **Pattern** (pass A only: E1, E17) |

### 1.3 Head coaches under contract to another club

| # | Rule | Label and sources |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | During a club's playing season, including its postseason and excluding the Pro Bowl, no club may request permission to talk to its head coach, and the head coach may not discuss or accept other employment unless he has been dismissed. | **Pattern** (P2 extract; P3) |
| H2 | In the off-season a head-coach-to-head-coach move is not a promotion and needs the employer's consent, which it may refuse. | **Pattern** for the practical rule (A and B). Whether a request could even be made is read two ways: A says a request may be made and refused; B cites E3 (January 2013 cycle) that a club could not ask permission to interview another club's head coach for its head-coach job. Either way the coach is unavailable unless his club releases him. |
| H3 | Head coaches and high-level executives are the only employees for whom draft choices or cash may be exchanged. Pre-2013 precedents: January 2000 (after a Commissioner ruling, the clubs agreed compensation that included a first-round pick) and February 2002 (two first-round picks, two second-round picks and $8 million). | **Pattern** (H11 policy quotation, date not shown; P2 extract; H8, H9, H10 for the precedents, labelled with their seasons) |

### 1.4 Compensation

| # | Rule | Label and sources |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | "Except for head coaches and high-level club employees (club presidents, general managers, and persons with equivalent responsibility and authority), clubs are not permitted to exchange draft choices or cash for the release of individuals who are under contract to another organization." A club may not pay picks or cash to pry loose another club's coordinator or position coach. | **Unverified** as to the 2013 wording (H11 and a P2 extract, version year unclear; pass A only). Consistent with C2 and with the pre-2013 head-coach deals in H3. |
| C2 | **No league compensation** existed in 2013 for a club that lost an assistant. The first such rule (third-round compensatory picks when a minority coach or executive employed at least two years becomes a head coach or general manager elsewhere) was Resolution JC-2A of November 2020, reported as new. | **Confirmed** (L7, two publishers; pass B) |

### 2.2 Interview windows

| # | Rule | Label and sources |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | **In-season bar.** During a club's playing season, including its postseason and excluding the Pro Bowl, no other club may, directly or through an intermediary, contact its coaches, request permission or discuss their future employment, and no coach may discuss or accept other employment. The only exceptions are the head-coach windows W4 to W9. | **Confirmed** (A: H1 1998, P2 extract, E16; B: P2 extract, E16, E8 "coaches on a non-playoff team can interview once the regular season ends", E3 January 2013 cycle "any coach whose team has been eliminated is eligible") |
| W1a | Start of the bar: the lateral-move clause applies "at any time prior to the opening of the employer club's training camp", which implies no lateral talks once camp opens. | **Unverified** (P1/P2 extract only). Matters for the mid-2014 off-season, not for January 2014. |
| W1b | A Pro Bowl coaching assignment does not extend the bar: the season is measured "excluding Pro Bowl". | **Unverified** (P2 extract and P3; the same routes in both passes) |
| W2 | **Non-playoff clubs (20).** Requests for their assistants could be made once the employer club's season ended, which was its Week 17 game on Sunday December 29, 2013. Monday December 30, 2013 was the first business day and the customary day for dismissals; no source makes it the legal start. | Rule keyed to the end of the employer's season: **Confirmed** (A and B; P3 "from the day of their last game"; E8). The Black Monday framing: **Corrected** (see §9, item 1) |
| W3 | **No waiting period in 2013.** The only waiting rules found (a wait until the third day after an employed candidate's final game, virtual-interview rules, in-season interviews in the last two regular-season weeks) are all later. | **Confirmed** that no such rule applies (A dates them to 2021 and 2025; B dates the third-day wait to May 2022, L10; the passes differ on the exact year, which does not affect 2013) |
| W4 | **Bye clubs (4).** "Assistant coaches under contract to playoff clubs that have byes in the Wild Card weekend may be interviewed for head coaching positions through the conclusion of the Wild Card games": from the end of the regular season until the last Wild Card game on Sunday January 5, 2014. No further first interview while the club stays alive. | **Confirmed** (B: 2013-14 calendar text in K1, K2, K3, K4; A: P1 2009 text "interviews of its coaches must be conducted prior to the conclusion of Wild Card games", E5 and E4 practice in the January 2013 cycle, H6 practice in the January 2012 cycle, E13 and E14 for the January 2019 cycle) |
| W5 | **Wild Card clubs (8).** No interview during Wild Card week. If the club lost, its assistants were free after elimination (January 4 or 5) under W2. If it won, its assistants could be interviewed for head-coach jobs **after the Wild Card games** and "through the conclusion of Divisional Playoff games", that is from the evening of January 5 until the last Divisional game on Sunday January 12, 2014. | **Confirmed** (B: calendar text K1, K2, K4; A: P1 2009 text "after the Wild Card games and prior to the conclusion of Divisional Playoff games", E6 January 2013 cycle "the following week, even if their team was still playing"; E9 2016 "cannot interview during the week of the wild card game") |
| W6 | **One interview per inquiring club** while the assistant's club is alive; no second interview until elimination, except W9. | **Confirmed** (H3 January 2004; P1 2009 extract; E3 January 2013 cycle; E14 2018) |
| W6a | The interview is held at a time and place acceptable to the employer club. | **Pattern** (P1 2009 extract; H1 1998 one-off rule requiring employer approval of scheduling; E9 2016; E14 2018) |
| W6b | The interview is held in the employer club's city and the employer chooses the day. | **Unverified** (E9 2016 and E14 2018 only; the 2009 wording "place acceptable to the employer club" is broader) |
| W6c | While the club is alive there may be no other direct or indirect contact between the inquiring club and the assistant or his agent. | **Unverified** (E3 only) |
| W7 | **No initial interview after the Divisional weekend** for an assistant whose club is still alive, and the club cannot waive this. An assistant of a conference finalist who had not been interviewed in W4 or W5 had to wait until his club was eliminated or its season ended. | **Confirmed** (E4 January 14, 2013: an assistant of a Super Bowl club not already interviewed must wait until after the Super Bowl; E3 January 2013 cycle; E13 December 2018; E15 January 2019; the 2013-14 calendar lists no window for this week) |
| W8 | **Conference championship losers (2)** were eliminated on January 19, 2014 and their assistants were then free under W2. | **Confirmed** (follows from W1 and W7; both passes) |
| W9 | **Super Bowl clubs (2).** "An assistant coach, whose team is participating in the Super Bowl, who has previously interviewed for another club's head coaching job may have a second interview with such club no later than the Sunday preceding the Super Bowl": by Sunday January 26, 2014. No first interviews, and no interviews in Super Bowl week. | **Confirmed** (B: calendar text K1, K2, K3; A: H4 and H5, adopted at the March 2007 annual meeting; E4 applied in the January 2013 cycle) |
| W9a | The second interview also needs the employer club's permission. | **Unverified** (A states it; B found it in one outlet only) |
| W9b | The second-interview provision applies only in a year with at least a two-week gap between the conference games and the Super Bowl. | **Unverified** (E7, whose date is not shown). Moot for 2013-14, which had a 14-day gap. |
| W10 | **The windows are for head-coach jobs only.** An inquiring club that wants an assistant of a club still alive for any other job must wait until that club is eliminated, and then needs the employer's consent under T3. | **Confirmed** as a derivation (the calendar text in K1 to K4 and the 2009 text in P1 both limit the windows to head coaching positions; W1) |
| W11 | Whether a playoff club could refuse, rather than only schedule, a head-coach interview inside W4 or W5. | **Unverified** (E9 2016 says winners' coaches "must be allowed" to interview; P1 gives the employer control of time and place; nothing dated 2014 was found) |

Absence of any other 2012 to 2014 amendment to the coach-tampering rules: **Unverified** (absence of evidence) |

## 3. The Rooney Rule in the January 2014 cycle

| # | Rule | Label and sources |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | A club filling a **head-coach** vacancy had to interview at least one minority candidate before hiring (adopted 2003). The rule required an interview, not a hire. | **Confirmed** (A and B: Ra, Re, Rf; L4) |
| R2 | Since a Commissioner memo of June 15, 2009, the same requirement (at least one minority candidate) applied to **general manager** and equivalent senior football-operations jobs. | **Confirmed** (Ra, Rb; both passes) |
| R3 | **Coordinators and position coaches were not covered** in the January 2014 cycle. The Fritz Pollard Alliance proposed a coordinator extension in January 2013 (it would have exempted a newly hired head coach's first staff), and the league announced at the March 2013 Annual Meeting that the rule would not be extended to coordinators. | **Confirmed** (Rc January 22, 2013; Rd March 2013; Re; A and B) |
| R4 | Later coverage that must not be applied: an informal "best practice" for coordinator openings under a sitting head coach (December 2016), the formal coordinator requirement and two external minority candidates for head coach (May 2020 and 2021), and in-person interviews of external minority candidates (2020s). | **Confirmed** as later rules (L4, L5, L6, L9, L14, L15) |
| R5 | Whether an internal minority candidate satisfied the 2013 rule; whether the interview had to be in person; whether any exception existed. | **Unverified** (the requirement that candidates be external dates from 2020, which suggests an internal candidate counted in 2013, but that is an inference; the December 2002 recommendation allowed limited exceptions for some internal promotions, and whether any survived to 2013 was not found; Ri) |
| R6 | **Enforcement** was by Commissioner discipline. Precedent (2003 season): a club president was fined $200,000 in July 2003 for hiring a head coach without interviewing a minority candidate. Later statements that the league could also take picks or void a hire are not tied to 2013. | Fine precedent: **Confirmed** (Rh, three publishers). Later penalty scale: **Unverified** for 2013 |
| R7 | The league's 2013 response to the January 2013 cycle was to restart its Career Development Symposium in May 2013 at the University of Pennsylvania's Wharton School, not to amend the rule. | **Confirmed** (Rg, two publishers; pass A only). No amendment before the January 2014 cycle was found: **Unverified** (absence) |

## 8. What a simulation must enforce

Each rule applies to every club in the branch league in the same way, including Jacksonville as an inquiring club or as an employer club.

1. **Two tiers only.** Only a head-coach job is a promotion. Coordinator, co-coordinator, assistant head coach, play-caller and position-coach jobs are all assistant jobs.
2. **Head-coach requests cannot be refused** once the assistant's season is over; for a live club only the windows in rules 8 to 11 apply. After March 1 the employer may have discretion (Unverified: flag, do not assume).
3. **Every other request is lateral and may be refused** by the employer club, finally, with no appeal and no Commissioner review. It may also be granted voluntarily. No title, play-calling promise, salary or other inducement turns a lateral move into a protected one. Who inside the employer club makes that decision is a branch authority-map question outside this file.
4. **No payment for assistants.** No draft choices or cash may be exchanged to release an assistant (C1, Unverified wording), and no league compensation is owed to a club that loses one in 2014 (C2).
5. **Free coaches.** Unemployed and dismissed coaches, and coaches whose contracts have expired, may be interviewed and hired at any time without permission. A coach who resigns or retires under contract is not free without his club's consent (Pattern). The second-Tuesday exclusive window for expiring contracts (E4) is Unverified; if used, apply the §6 dates.
6. **Head coaches under contract elsewhere** are unavailable during their club's season and afterwards only with their club's consent; compensation may be negotiated for a head coach only.
7. **In-season bar.** No contact of any kind about future employment with a coach whose club is still playing, except rules 8 to 11.
8. **Bye clubs:** head-coach interviews only until the last Wild Card game ends.
9. **Wild Card clubs:** none during Wild Card week; winners' assistants only between the end of the Wild Card games and the end of the Divisional games; losers are free after elimination.
10. **After the Divisional weekend:** no first interview with any coach of a live club.
11. **Super Bowl clubs:** a second interview only with a club that already interviewed the assistant, no later than the Sunday before the Super Bowl; none in Super Bowl week.
12. **One interview per inquiring club** while the assistant's club is alive, at a time and place the employer accepts.
13. **The windows are for head-coach jobs only.** Lateral requests for a live club's assistant wait until that club is eliminated.
14. **No hire while the employer club is alive.** No contract agreed, signed or announced until the employer's season ends; an agreement in principle is not a hire.
15. **Rooney Rule:** at least one minority candidate interviewed before any head-coach or general-manager hire; no requirement for coordinator or position-coach hires in 2014.
16. **Log each stage separately:** request, permission granted or refused, interview completed, candidate's own interest or withdrawal, offer, and hire. A report of interest is not an interview, and an interview is not an offer.
17. **Result-blind and protagonist-blind.** The windows follow the branch's own playoff results. An employer's consent decision, an inquiring club's interest and a coach's choice are resolved from concrete, checkable inputs (the job, its authority and play-calling, pay, the coach's record and contract status, the clubs' situations), never from which side is the protagonist (Document 1 §9.1 label-swap test).
18. **Contact outside these rules is tampering** and a Commissioner discipline matter (D1, Unverified wording). A simulated club gains nothing from it.
19. **Do not import later rules** listed in §5 (bona fide coordinator protection, compensatory picks, virtual or in-person timing rules, waiting periods, in-season interviews, the grant-one-grant-all rule).

## 9. Corrections made in this pass

1. **Black Monday is not the legal start (A corrected by B).** Pass A tied the first request date to Monday December 30, 2013. The rule is keyed to the end of the employer club's season, which for a non-playoff club was its Week 17 game on December 29, 2013; December 30 was only the first business day.
2. **No "bona fide promotion" exception in 2013 (task premise corrected by B).** The bona fide coordinator test, the Commissioner's review and the organization-chart filing date from May 19, 2020.
3. **"Assistant head coach" workaround rejected (A).** A January 2010 explainer (E2) said a coordinator could be taken by hiring him as "Assistant Head Coach/Offensive Coordinator". Under the two-tier rule that title is still an assistant title, so the move is lateral and blockable.
4. **"Work out compensation" for a coordinator rejected (A).** A November 2009 explainer (E1) said clubs could always work out compensation for an under-contract coordinator. The policy text (C1) allows draft-choice or cash exchanges only for head coaches and high-level executives.
5. **Later rules offered as general rules rejected (A and B).** Search summaries presented 2020 to 2026 rules as current: three-hour virtual interviews, fixed January dates, in-person interviews only after the Divisional round, a third-day wait, in-season interviews in the final two weeks, and two external or in-person Rooney interviews. None applies to 2013.
6. **Head-coach permission is mandatory (A).** One summary said the employer "is under no obligation" to grant a head-coach interview. That merged in the lateral-move clause and is rejected.
7. **Super Bowl second-interview date (A).** Summaries dated it "around 2009" or "shortly after the 2002 season". H4 and H5 fix it to the March 2007 annual meeting. The December 2002 change (H2) concerned playoff assistants' interviews generally, not the Super Bowl second interview.
8. **Misplaced cycle (A).** One summary placed a bye-week head-coach interview by a coordinator in "January 2014". It belongs to the January 2012 cycle (H6); the January 2014 cycle is outside this record.
9. **2009 Rooney expansion wording (A).** One summary said the 2009 expansion required "a minimum of two external minority candidates". The 2009 sources say at least one; the two-external requirement dates from 2020 and 2021.
10. **Tampering penalty label (verification pass).** Pass B labelled the Commissioner's penalty menu Confirmed as coming from a version "bracketing 2013". The only text seen is the 2018 version, and the case cited is from 2016; nothing on the pre-2013 side was found. Relabelled Unverified for the 2013 wording.
11. **One-interview and no-initial-interview labels (verification pass).** Pass A labelled these Pattern. Combined with pass B's January 2013 cycle source (E3) and A's January 14, 2013 source (E4), they meet Confirmed.
12. **Second-Tuesday window label (verification pass).** Pass A labelled it Pattern. It rests on one pass and one publisher (NFL.com, 2007, 2020, 2025), and pass B did not find it. Relabelled Unverified.
13. **Playoff windows unchanged from 2013 to 2014 (verification pass).** Pass A labelled the absence of a 2012 to 2014 change Unverified. Pass B's dated calendars for both seasons (K5 and K1 to K4) carry the same three provisions in the same words, so that narrower claim is Confirmed. Any wider absence of change remains Unverified.
14. **Source dating (verification pass).** Pass B treated the Eagles.com article "Jumping on NFL's coaching carousel" (E7) as a January 2013 cycle explainer. The passes' record shows no date for it, and B also used it for a condition about the Super Bowl gap. It is treated here as undated and is not counted as a January 2013 source; the January 2013 attributions rest on E3 and E4. The calibration pass's statement that "the library states" 7 of 27 candidates had permanent NFL head-coaching experience was not found as a stated total in the library, so that total is relabelled Unverified.
15. **Rooney coordinator decision dated (B adds precision to A).** Pass A said the league declined the January 2013 coordinator proposal without a date; pass B dates the decision to the March 2013 Annual Meeting (Rd).
16. **Chicago hire-date discrepancy (calibration pass).** A search summary gave a January 2013 hire one day earlier than the club's appointment date; the gap is a publication timestamp against an appointment date. Both conventions are kept in §7.3, where no club-level date is shown.
17. **Conflicts left open, not corrected:** T7 (request in writing), T8 (grant-one-grant-all), E6 (compensation for a resigned assistant) and E7 (right of first refusal). Each is stated with both readings and labelled Unverified.
