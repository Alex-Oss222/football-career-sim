# Library: 2013 NFL suspension completion, reinstatement and roster exemption

**Status:** Research pass and separate verification pass completed September 27, 2026. This file covers the rules and practice for a player on Reserve/Suspended at the end of a fixed four-game regular-season suspension under the rules in force in 2013. It is research only. It changes no canon, roster, cap or calendar fact.

**Method:** The project's two-pass discipline applies. The first pass found each claim. The second pass searched each claim again from scratch through different outlets and different query wording, without relying on the first pass's citation. Each claim below is labelled **Confirmed** (two or more independent sources agree), **Pattern** (consistent 2012-2013 league practice across several independent cases, but the governing rule text was not reached) or **Unverified** (one source, a post-2013 source only, or an inference).

**Hindsight boundary:** No Justin Blackmon coverage from after October 6, 2013 was used. Every rule below comes from other players' 2012-2013 cases, the league's own announcement wording, and the NFL personnel-manual text as quoted by the league. Post-2013 sources appear only where marked, and they never control a 2013 conclusion.

## Answers

| Question | 2013 rule / practice | Label |
|---|---|---|
| (1) Return date | The suspension covers the club's four regular-season games. The player becomes eligible to return to the club, including practice, meetings and the active roster, on **the day after the club's fourth suspended game**. For a Sunday game that is Monday. The league states this date when it announces the discipline. The reinstatement transaction is recorded that day. | **Confirmed** |
| (2a) Roster exemption | **Not automatic.** Only the Commissioner may put a player on the Exempt List, and "no exemption, regardless of circumstances, is automatic." In practice, every 2012-2013 four-game case found received one: the league placed the player on the Exempt List on his reinstatement day. | Discretion: **Confirmed** (manual text quoted in 2014). Routine grant: **Pattern** |
| (2b) Length | The league sets the length when it grants the exemption. In all three 2012-2013 regular-season cases where the length was reported, it was **one week, expiring on the Monday after the club's next game**. | **Pattern**. Time of day in 2013 is **Unverified** |
| (2c) While exempt | The player does not count against the 53 and may practice and take part in team activities. He cannot play: a player on the Exempt List is outside the Active/Inactive List, so he cannot dress for a game. The club may activate him at any time before the exemption expires. | **Confirmed** |
| (2d) When it ends | The player must be on the Active (53-man) List or leave the roster. Every 2012-2013 club activated the player before expiry and cleared a spot if it had none: by release, waiver or an injured-reserve move. The express rule "activate or release by the deadline" was found only in a 2023 source. | Activation practice: **Confirmed**. Express either/or rule for 2013: **Unverified** |
| (3) Automatic vs league reinstatement | For a **fixed-term** suspension, whether under the substance-abuse policy, the performance-enhancing substances (PED) policy or the personal conduct policy, the return date is set in the discipline itself. The league records the reinstatement on that date without any reported application or hearing. An **indefinite or minimum-term** suspension is different. Examples are substance-abuse banishment of at least one year and indefinite conduct suspensions. In those cases the player must be reinstated by a Commissioner decision, which may come after the earliest eligibility date. | Fixed-term: **Pattern** (policy text not reached). Indefinite contrast: **Confirmed** |
| Game checks | A suspended player forfeits **one-seventeenth of his Paragraph 5 (base) salary for each game suspended**. The standard contract pays base salary in weekly installments across the 17-week regular season. So a four-game suspension costs 4/17. | **Confirmed** |

## Research pass

1. **Return date.** The league's announcement formula in 2012-2013 named the return date: "eligible to return to the [club's] active roster on Monday, [date] following the club's [date] game against [opponent]." That formula appears in S4 (Hill 2013, substance abuse), S7 (Brazill 2013, substance abuse), S8 (Haden 2012, PED) and S11 (Irvin 2013, PED). The PFR transaction logs record "reinstated from suspension" on that same date (S7, S8, S9). Players physically rejoined the club that Monday (S8 Haden; S9 Talib "at team facilities for the first time Monday").
2. **Roster exemption.**
   - The manual text quoted in S1 reads: "The Exempt List is a special player status available to clubs only in unusual circumstances. The List includes those players who have been declared by the Commissioner to be temporarily exempt from counting within the Active List limit. Only the Commissioner has the authority to place a player on the Exempt List; clubs have no such authority, and no exemption, regardless of circumstances, is automatic." It adds that the Commissioner determines in advance whether the time on the list will be finite, or will continue until the exemption is lifted "and the player returned to the Active List."
   - Five 2012-2013 four-game cases (S5/S6, S7, S8, S9, S10) show the player placed on the Exempt List on his reinstatement day.
   - Reported lengths: Talib, reinstated Monday November 12, expiring November 19 (S9). Hill 2013, one week expiring Monday (S5). Hill 2012, one week (S10). No length was found for Haden or Brazill.
   - While exempt, the player practiced and attended meetings without counting against the 53 (S5, S9). To play he had to be activated first (S5: "if he played ... would have to make a roster move"). Each club activated before the game and expiry, clearing a spot as needed: Talib (Branch released, S9), Hill 2013 (Ross to IR, Reynolds waived, S6), Hill 2012 (Coe to IR, S10).
3. **Automatic vs league reinstatement.** No 2012-2013 fixed-term case shows an application, hearing or separate reinstatement decision. The return date is fixed in the discipline notice, and the transaction is recorded on that date (S4, S7, S8). By contrast, the substance-abuse banishment of Tanard Jackson (S12) had an earliest-eligibility date. The league's reinstatement came about three weeks after that date. Vick's indefinite conduct suspension (S13) likewise ended only by Commissioner decision, in stages. A fixed-term personal-conduct suspension (S14) ended on its fixed date and was followed by the same exemption-then-activation sequence.
4. **Game checks.** Base (Paragraph 5) salary is paid in weekly or biweekly installments across the regular season (S3). The 2013 regular season ran 17 weeks. Published 2012-2013 forfeiture amounts equal exactly 1/17 of base per suspended game: Haden 4 × 1/17 (S15), Talib 4/17 (S16), Miller 6/17 under the substance-abuse policy (S17).

## Verification pass

Each claim was searched again from scratch with different wording and different outlets.

- **Return date, confirmed.** Haden's "Monday, Oct. 8, one day after [the Giants game]" was independently re-found in a second search. Brazill's "Sept. 30" was re-found through the SI wire (June 24, 2013) and NFL.com, separately from the first-pass PFR log. Hill 2013 appears in both the NFL.com and the Giants.com announcements. Three different clubs and two different policies share one formula.
- **Exemption granted in practice, pattern.** Talib's exemption was re-found through CBS Sports, which is independent of the first-pass ESPN blog. It gives the same "does not count ... participate in team activities ... expired on Nov. 19" facts and the Saturday activation with Branch released. Hill 2013 was re-found through ProFootballTalk (S6), independent of Giants.com. **Correction made during verification:** a first-pass Giants.com passage about Hill returning from a *performance-enhancing-substances* suspension belongs to his **2012** return, not his 2013 substance-abuse return. The two Hill cases are now kept separate (S5/S6 for 2013, S10 for 2012). The one-week length held in both.
- **Exemption discretionary, confirmed with a caveat.** The manual passage was re-found in a second outlet (S2) with the same wording. Both publications date from 2014. The 2013 edition of the manual was not reached. The label rests on the fact that the passage describes a long-standing manual provision, which is corroborated by 2009 (S13) and 2010 (S14) exempt-list use.
- **What happens at expiry, partly unverified.** No 2012-2013 source was found stating "activate or release" as a rule. The only express statement is S18 (2023). The 2012-2013 evidence is consistent with it: every club activated the player before expiry and cleared a spot. The manual's "returned to the Active List" wording also supports it. Label: activation practice **Confirmed**; the express either/or rule and the 4 p.m. ET time are **Unverified** for 2013.
- **Automatic reinstatement, pattern.** Re-searched for any 2012-2013 report of a four-game-suspended player needing a league decision to return; none was found. The indefinite-suspension contrast (S12) was confirmed through two sources. The policy text that would settle the question (the pre-September-2014 substance-abuse policy) was not reached, so the label stays **Pattern**. The personal-conduct fixed-term example (S14) has a single source and is **Unverified** as a separate PCP rule.
- **1/17, confirmed.** The first-pass figures were re-found independently. The arithmetic was re-checked: $5,765,505 ÷ 17 = $339,147.35; $1,852,500 × 4/17 = $435,882.35; $2,284,125 × 6/17 = $806,161.76. The Miller figure (substance-abuse policy, 2013) appears in two outlets (S17).

## Application to the branch (sourced rule only)

- **Eligibility.** The branch's last suspended game is Sunday, October 6. Under the 2013 announcement formula (return on the day after the club's final suspended game), Blackmon is eligible to be reinstated and to rejoin the club for practice and meetings on **Monday, October 7**. That date is derived directly from the sourced rule. No league application or hearing is needed for a fixed four-game suspension (**Pattern**).
- **Exemption.** Jacksonville may receive a roster exemption, but only by league grant; it is not automatic. The league sets the length. In every 2012-2013 regular-season case where a length was reported, it was one week, ending on the Monday after the club's next game. On the branch calendar, that is the Monday after the October 13 game. This is a **pattern**, not a rule text. The branch must record whatever grant it resolves.
- **During any exemption.** Blackmon does not count against the 53 and may practice. Because he is not on the Active/Inactive List, he can be neither one of the 46 active nor one of the game-day inactives. To be listed inactive, or to play, he must first be activated to the 53.
- **At the end of any exemption, or at once if none is granted.** He must be on the 53-man Active List, which uses the branch's existing open spot if it is still open, or leave the roster by release, waiver or trade. The express either/or rule is confirmed only in a post-2013 source.
- **Money.** The four suspended weeks forfeit 4/17 of his 2013 Paragraph 5 base salary (**Confirmed** rule). The dollar amount still depends on the unverified base-salary figure in `career/2013/offseason/current_cap_worksheet.md`. The signing-bonus effect remains unverified.
