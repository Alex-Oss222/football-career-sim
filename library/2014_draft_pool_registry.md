# Library: 2014 NFL Draft Pool Registry

**Status:** Pre-selection eligibility registry. Research pass (pass 1 of 2) and verification pass (pass 2 of 2) complete for the seven focus prospects only; full-class coverage is **not built**. Pass 2 corrections are applied in the body and listed in the "Verification pass" section at the end.
**Purpose:** Track who is known to be eligible without using actual draft results as an answer key.
**Research date:** 2026-09-28. Branch date at research time: November 24 to December 1, 2013.

This file is modelled on `library/2013_draft_pool_registry.md`. Date gates are defined in `library/2014_draft_information_gates.md`. No 2014 scouting snapshot exists yet.

## Source-access limitation (both passes)

Direct page retrieval (WebFetch) was blocked by the session's network egress policy during both passes; only a search engine was reachable. Every fact below, including every pass 2 confirmation, was taken from search-result text attributed to the cited URL, not from a first-hand read of the full page. "Confirmed" here means at least two independent search results agreed. Where they did not, the entry says **unverified**.

Many of the pages located are school biographies, reference pages or retrospectives that also contain later material. Only the pre-selection facts are recorded here. Nothing about any player's draft position, NFL team, NFL statistics, awards, contract or career is recorded, and none of it may be used. Where a pre-selection fact is known only from a source published after the draft, the entry says so.

## Completeness rule

- The official special-eligibility list for 2014 (98 players) exists but has **not been transcribed** here yet. It becomes usable only from its publication date (gate file: January 19, 2014, confirmed in pass 2).
- The senior pool is operational, not claimed exhaustive.
- The combine invitation list (335) is coverage, not an eligibility master list, and is usable only from its dated release (gate file: February 6, 2014, corrected in pass 2).
- Absence from this registry never proves ineligibility.
- Before an unlisted player can be selected in the simulation, his 2014 eligibility must be verified from evidence public before the real draft began.

## Eligibility categories used here

- **Senior (college eligibility exhausted after the 2013 season):** eligible for the 2014 draft without an application.
- **Underclassman with remaining eligibility:** eligible only if at least three years have passed since high-school graduation and the NFL grants his dated special-eligibility application (deadline January 15, 2014). A reported intention alone does not make him draftable.
- **Unresolved:** status cannot yet be classified from dated sources.
