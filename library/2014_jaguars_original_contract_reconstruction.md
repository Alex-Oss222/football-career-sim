# Jaguars original contracts carried into 2014

**Current supplement:** [Entry 91 contract completion](2014_jaguars_contract_completion.md) adopts missing term lengths, guarantees and working assumptions. It supersedes this earlier pass’s open Wilson/Grimes terms, futures duration and guarantee labels; its completed schedules persist between turns.

Administrative correction at February 28, 2014, Entry 90. Researched September 29, 2026. The user requested continuation from the existing 2013 contracts and explicitly allowed labeled approximations where original annual terms cannot be fully recovered.

The starting evidence is the [2013 initial cap sheet](../career/2013/offseason/initial_cap_sheet.md), the executed branch signings, draftees, trades and futures agreements. Public sources below recover original instruments. Later publication may document an old term; later real releases, suspensions, restructures and extensions are not branch events. Original scheduled charge, actual historical charge after a real transaction, and cash are different figures.

The [current contract table](../career/2014/finances/player_contracts/contracts.md) and [ten-year tracker](../career/finances/salary_cap/cap_tracker.md) carry these results. Exact-looking arithmetic within an **estimate** does not make the underlying assumption confirmed. Values are whole dollars; final-year rounding balances the original bonus where supported. Unrecovered guarantees remain distinct from paid signing bonus.

## Verification and accounting decisions

- Retain Washington's bonus when carrying Cousins' remaining salaries to Jacksonville; similarly, Asper's old Buffalo bonus stays with Buffalo.
- Do not prorate Posluszny's signing bonus into the sixth year. His original five-year allocation ends in 2015.
- Lewis and Posluszny retain their original future salaries, excluding their later real reductions/extensions. Scobee retains the original deal before any real trade adjustment.
- Blackmon's deferred roster cash was already treated as signing bonus for cap purposes. Preserve the original schedule as an estimate before the branch's unresolved four-game forfeiture accounting; no real indefinite suspension or tolling is imported.
- Proven-performance escalators require the 2011 agreement's participation test and branch snap evidence. The 2013 stat totals do not contain a certified offensive/defensive snap denominator. Budget the projected increased salaries for Shorts and Rackley, but label them estimates. Prosinski retains the original base estimate with the higher alternative disclosed. Later draft classes' escalators remain contingent.
- Existing branch deals are unchanged. No new free-agent offer, option, trade, retirement, release or extension occurs in this correction.

## Futures service and minimum salaries

Use [2011 CBA Article 26](https://nflps.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/collective-bargaining-agreement-2011-2020.pdf): the minimum-salary credited-season test requires three regular-season games on qualifying full-pay status. It excludes practice squad and injured-reserve service. That differs from the six-game accrued-season test for free agency.
