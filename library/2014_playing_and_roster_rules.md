# 2014 NFL playing and roster rules: research and verification

**Status:** research pass (pass 1 of 2) and separate verification pass (pass 2 of 2) completed October 1, 2026, at a master clock of August 1 to 4, 2014. This file is the sourcing record behind the 2014 adoption in Document 2 §7; it is not a second rule authority. It is research only: it changes no closed result, roster, calendar or state fact. It is modelled on `library/2013_playing_rules.md` and `library/2013_nfl_playing_rules_for_simulation.md` (what the 2013 executable layer verified) and on `library/2014_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md` (release-date gating and labels). Where this file and that calendar file overlap, the calendar file's row is cited rather than re-researched.

**Information gate.** Every item carries the date it became public. At the master clock (August 1 to 4, 2014) everything adopted at the March 24 to 26 and May 20, 2014 league meetings, the officiating points of emphasis announced to clubs in the spring and explained publicly by late July, the reduction deadlines published in the spring, and the pre-existing 2012 to 2013 roster mechanisms are public. The August 19, 2014 practice-squad agreement is **not** public at the clock and stays gated (G1 below). No August 2014 or later outcome of any rule (preseason kick statistics, in-season penalty counts) informs any branch decision; where such a figure appears here it is quoted only to settle which rule text was in force.

**Labels.** Confirmed: two independent named outlets agree, or the item is already Confirmed in a linked library file. Supported: one credible outlet, or an outlet plus consistent arithmetic or a consistent dated case. Unverified: a single, undated or conflicting source. Gated: public later than the master clock; usable only from its public date.

**Research access.** The session's network egress proxy refused every direct page fetch tried in both passes (nfl.com, operations.nfl.com, archive.org, profootballtalk/nbcsports.com, espn.com, washingtonpost.com, cbsnews.com, the club sites and the SB Nation and Gannett sites). Both passes therefore rest on the dated result text returned by named-outlet web searches, with the outlet and URL recorded for each claim, exactly as `career/2014/finances/player_contracts/contract_status.md` §5 records for its own research. No attempt was made to route around the proxy. The consequence is stated per item: the official 2014 rulebook text could not be opened, so rule-article numbering is given only where an officiating outlet quoted it, and the exact wording of several items is Supported rather than Confirmed.

## 1. Playing-rule changes adopted at the Annual Meeting, Orlando, March 24 to 26, 2014

The Competition Committee circulated 13 proposals on March 19 (the Jets' "13 Rule Change Proposals for 2014" and the Giants' "View proposed NFL rule changes" list them; the first seven were club proposals from Washington and New England, the rest Competition Committee proposals). The owners acted on March 25 and 26. Outlets count the outcome differently: ProFootballTalk counted "five rules changes" passing on March 26 after the roll-up block passed on March 25, the Baltimore Ravens' site counted "seven of 13" proposals passed over the meeting with three failed and three tabled, and Niners Nation counted five passed, three voted down and five tabled (its count includes bylaw and resolution items). The six playing-rule changes below are the ones every accessible outlet agrees were adopted; whether a seventh adopted item was a playing rule or a bylaw could not be settled (P7).

## 6. What changes for the engine and the branch roster process

The entries below are the adoption consequences for this project. They state what the rules require and what the installed runtime does; they authorize no kernel change (a result-changing change needs the register's release procedure).

1. **Preseason Weeks 1 and 2 try from the 15-yard line (X2).** Jacksonville's August 8 and August 14 games must resolve the one-point try as a 33-yard kick with the two-point try unchanged at the 2; the August 22 and 28 games and every regular-season and postseason game use the 2-yard line. **The installed kernel 2014.4 does not model this:** `runtime/kernel.py` resolves every try with the calibrated extra-point rate on the 2-yard-line try (`runtime/rules.py`, `pat_snap_yard_line` 2) and knows the game types regular, postseason and pro_bowl only; it has no preseason game type and no field-goal-distance try. This is recorded as a dated open item in `runtime/defect_register.md`. A preseason execution path is being built separately; until it is committed, accepted and installed, no preseason game may be closed with the ordinary try and presented as the August 8 or 14 result.
2. **Running clock after a sack (P3).** The kernel's drive clock replays sampled 2012 drive sequences (Document 7; `library/2012_drive_model_calibration.md`), in which a sack outside two minutes stopped the clock. The 2014 rule removes that stoppage, so a 2014 drive with a sack runs a few more seconds off the clock than the 2012 sample implies. This is a calibration note, not a rule the kernel can enforce per play; it is logged with the register's display and calibration items and changes no result.
3. **Points of emphasis (E1 to E3).** The kernel draws an aggregate penalty burden per drive at the 2012 rate (`penalty_per_play`), without foul types. Stricter illegal-contact and defensive-holding enforcement is a real 2014 condition the 2012 calibration does not carry. No recalibration is adopted here (it would be a result-changing kernel change, and the only measured 2014 rates are gated outcomes, E4); the gap is logged in the register as a calibration item. Narration may describe strict downfield enforcement; it may not invent a specific flag the receipt does not record.
4. **Previous-spot enforcement (P4), roll-up blocks (P2), taller uprights (P1), the replay consultation (P5) and loose-ball reviews (P6)** have no representation in the kernel (aggregate fouls, no replay model, no upright geometry) and need none for results; narration follows the 2014 rule where it describes such a play.
5. **Crossbar dunks (P8) and abusive language (E3)** are narration-only: the Locker Room and Media agents may describe them only from an event already logged.
6. **Roster process (R1 to R8, G1).** The branch's training-camp roster is 90 until August 26 (75) and August 30 (53), both at 4:00 p.m. ET; the practice squad is eight at the master clock and the ten-player rule becomes usable only on August 19; a Reserve/Injured designated-for-return move needs the player on the 53 after the August 30 cut and returns him to practice after six weeks and to the list after eight; the trade deadline is October 28; the game-day list is 46 of 53 in the regular season and postseason; a preseason game may dress the whole roster, which the weekly input gate's 46-player check (`runtime/game_runner.py`, 2013 rule) does not yet admit. The twelve-year cap tracker and the 2014 worksheet already count Top-51 through the first regular-season game (R8).
7. **Unchanged (U1 to U3):** overtime, the 12-club postseason and the 46-player list need no change to `runtime/rules.py`, `runtime/standings.py` or `runtime/postseason.py`.

## 7. Two-pass verification record

### Pass 1: research

Searches by topic (annual-meeting adoptions, the May spring meeting, the preseason extra point, points of emphasis, overtime and playoffs, injured reserve, roster reductions), taking each claim from the dated outlet text returned and recording the outlet and URL. Items already researched for the 2014 calendar file were taken from it rather than re-researched.

### Pass 2: separate verification, skeptical of pass 1

Each status-determining claim was searched again from scratch with different wording, preferring a different outlet (club sites, Football Zebras, the Ravens' and Jets' proposal lists) over the pass-1 citation.

- **Corrected, the extra-point yard line (X2, X3).** Pass 1 returned both a 20-yard-line (38-yard) plan and the 15-yard-line (33-yard) rule.
