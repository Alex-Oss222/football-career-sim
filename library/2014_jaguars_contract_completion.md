# Completing Jacksonville's existing contracts

Prepared September 29, 2026 for the February 28, 2014 checkpoint, Entry 91. This supplements [the original-contract reconstruction](2014_jaguars_original_contract_reconstruction.md). The user authorizes realistic simulation terms where public history does not supply a complete original instrument. These assumptions now stay fixed in the financial inputs until a dated correction or contract event changes them.

## Method

Carry the existing contract first, including its original end year. A trade or waiver claim transfers remaining salary, not the former club's paid signing bonus. A waiver followed by a practice-squad deal ends the old contract. A replacement futures signing is a new instrument. Use the branch's draft slot for rookie comparisons, not the player's actual historical selection. Future extensions, new free-agent deals and unexercised options do not fill later columns.

The main tables show whole-dollar amounts with a dollar sign. A blank player-year means that no currently recorded deal covers the year. The notes identify simulated terms; they are not presented as newly discovered historical facts. Existing annual allocations from Entry 90 remain the fixed working schedule, including the stated estimates for Nwaneri, Mincey, Mosley and Owens. No new league cap, ownership budget, payment receipt or club carryover is invented by completing player contracts.

Adopt a two-year 2013-2014 contract at $630,000 and $730,000, total $1,360,000, with no bonus or guarantee. The two-year length is a simulation reconstruction using the same period's [Ryan Davis futures contract](https://overthecap.com/player/ryan-davis/957) as a structural comparator. Wilson's Atlanta active/inactive seasons in 2009, 2010 and late 2011, followed by the branch's full 2013 season, support the four-credited-season 2014 minimum. His brief 2012 promotion is not another credited season. Minimum tiers follow Article 26 of the [2011 agreement](https://nflps.org/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/collective-bargaining-agreement-2011-2020.pdf).

Wilson remains under contract through 2014. Stone's earlier recommendation to let him go if expired no longer applies automatically; a release would be a separate decision. His QB3 position is unchanged.

## Remaining guarantees

Lane Johnson's existing branch pick-2 salary schedule uses the [fully guaranteed Luke Joeckel comparator](https://www.nfl.com/news/luke-joeckel-signs-jacksonville-jaguars-rookie-contract-0ap1000000214117). Kelce's existing pick-33 schedule matches [Johnathan Cyprien's original contract](https://overthecap.com/player/johnathan-cyprien/2244). Its $3,916,124 total guarantee includes the $2,357,528 signing bonus, $405,000 2013 salary, $653,596 2014 salary and $500,000 of 2015 salary. Adopt the remaining $1,153,596 guarantee for the branch contract. This is a contract-slot reconstruction, not Kelce's real pick-63 deal.

For Poyer and the remaining later-round rookies, adopt bonus-only protection with no unpaid guaranteed base after 2013. For inherited veterans where the remaining guarantee was missing, adopt no additional guaranteed 2014-and-later salary. Their bonus proration remains a real cap cost; a $0 remaining cash guarantee does not erase it. This is a simulation assumption, not a claim that every original legal clause was recovered.

Blackmon's original [suspension clause](https://www.espn.com/blog/afcsouth/post/_/id/39311/breaking-down-justin-blackmons-deal) removes future guarantees. Apply it to his already-completed branch four-game suspension. Retain the gross original salary/deferred-bonus cash schedule without presuming a later suspension, tolling or bonus recovery. Gross release exposure does not silently recognize an uncollected forfeiture credit.

Monroe's $11,654,000 tender is unsigned. Its cap charge is included once; guaranteed salary remains $0 until signing. The scheduled cash line is conditional on signing/retention, not a payment receipt.
