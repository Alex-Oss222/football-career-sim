# Library: Super Bowl XLVIII MVP and 2014 Pro Bowl procedure

**Status:** Research pass and separate verification pass completed September 28, 2026. This file records **procedure only** for three end-of-2013-season events: the Super Bowl XLVIII Most Valuable Player selection (February 2, 2014), the 2014 Pro Bowl draft (January 21 and 22, 2014) and the 2014 Pro Bowl game rules (January 26, 2014). It extends `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` (§3.3, Pro Bowl calendar and vote), `2013_season_honours_selection_structure.md` (§1, Pro Bowl slots, need players, alternates) and `2013_postseason_format_and_schedule.md` (§2, Super Bowl XLVIII date and site). It is research only. It changes no canon, roster, standings, calendar or award fact, and it draws no winner.

**Method:** The project's two-pass discipline applies. The first pass found each claim. The second pass searched each claim again from scratch with different query wording and, where possible, a different outlet or an arithmetic cross-check against quotas already confirmed in the two files above. Labels: **Confirmed** (two or more independent sources agree, or a league release plus an independent corroboration), **Corrected** (the first pass, a source or an existing library entry was wrong; the correction and reason are stated), **Pattern** (consistent practice seen in seasons on both sides of 2013-14 or in several instances, but no 2014-dated rule text reached) or **Unverified** (one source only, a league release with no independent corroboration, a source from another season only, or an inference). A league release and its club-site mirrors count as **one** source ("single league origin"); this is stricter than the convention in the two earlier files, and it is why some draft mechanics are Unverified here although they are the only rule text found.

**Access limits (read before relying on this file):** WebFetch was attempted on www.nfl.com, www.si.com and www.footballzebras.com and each was **blocked by this environment's egress proxy** (EGRESS_BLOCKED). No block was routed around; no cache, mirror or archive copy of a blocked page was used. **Every source below was read through search-engine result text (title, URL, extracted passage), not by opening the page.** Search summaries mixed seasons more than once (see §4); every season attribution below was therefore checked by a second query. A future session with page access should re-open the URLs and upgrade or correct the labels.

**Hindsight boundary:** Many pages below report the real Super Bowl XLVIII result, its MVP, the real Pro Bowl captains, the coin toss, draft picks and the Pro Bowl score, and several search extracts surfaced those facts unprompted. They were used **only** for the procedural statement cited. No Super Bowl XLVIII participant, winner, score, statistic, MVP or vote share, and no Pro Bowl captain, captain-group membership, coin-toss result, draft pick, draft order, assignment, need player, coach-to-team pairing, score or statistic is recorded here, and none may be imported as an answer key (Document 1 §8, Document 2 §4.3). Where a URL slug names such a person or result, the slug is withheld in the source list.

## 1. Super Bowl XLVIII Most Valuable Player (Pete Rozelle Trophy)

Game rails (already confirmed elsewhere): Sunday February 2, 2014, MetLife Stadium, FOX (`2013_postseason_format_and_schedule.md` B1).

| Item | Fact | Label |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Award and trophy | The Super Bowl MVP award carries the **Pete Rozelle Trophy**. The league announced the name on October 8, 1990 and first presented it at Super Bowl XXV (January 1991). SPORT magazine presented the award through the 1989 season. | Name and 1990 date: **Confirmed** (M1 Pro Football Hall of Fame, 1990 page; M2 Wikipedia). SPORT magazine era: **Unverified** (one extract, M2) |
| Selectors | A panel of **16** media members (writers and broadcasters covering the game) plus a **fan vote** | Standing procedure: **Confirmed** (M3 NFL.com league release for the 2009 game; M2 Wikipedia; M7 and M8 later-season explainers). ## 2. 2014 Pro Bowl draft (Tuesday January 21 and Wednesday January 22, 2014)

### 2.1 Who drafts

| Item | Fact | Label |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Alumni captains | Two Hall of Fame alumni captains lead the two teams and have final say over picks (named July 31, 2013) | **Confirmed** (`2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` §3.3) |
| Advisers | Each alumni captain is assisted by his two **active player captains** and one **NFL.com fantasy football champion** (a fan) | **Confirmed** (P1 NFL.com July 31, 2013 release: "with the help of two NFL.com fantasy users"; P8 Wikipedia; D6 NFL.com fantasy feature) |
| Head coaches | The staffs of the highest-seeded club in each conference to lose in the Divisional round coach the two teams; the alumni captains act as de facto general managers for them | Coaching convention: **Confirmed** (`2013_season_honours_selection_structure.md` §1.2). GM framing: one independent report (P6 CBS Sports): **Unverified** as a rule |
| Which coach joins which alumni captain | Not found | **Unverified**. The branch must state its pairing rule before the draft |
| Need players | Each head coach appoints one undrafted "need" player (a long snapper in 2014) | **Confirmed** (`2013_season_honours_selection_structure.md` §1.2) |

### 2.2 Active player captains and captain groups

| Item | Fact | Label |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Who | The top two offensive and top two defensive vote-getters among players whose clubs did not reach the conference championship round | **Confirmed** (`2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` §3.3) |
| Captain groups | **Group 1:** the top offensive vote-getter with the **second** defensive vote-getter. **Group 2:** the top defensive vote-getter with the **second** offensive vote-getter | **Unverified** (single league origin: D1 rules release and its Bills.com mirror; no independent restatement reached). It is the only rule text found and nothing contradicts it |
| Draft pool | Captains join their team with their group and are **not drafted**. 86 voted players less 4 captains = **82 draft-eligible** (64 maximum drafted + 18 minimum assigned) | **Confirmed** (D1; `2013_season_honours_selection_structure.md` §1.4 arithmetic) |

### 2.3 Coin toss and first pick

| Item | Fact | Label |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Toss | A coin toss at the Pro Bowl press conference on **Tuesday January 21** | **Confirmed** (D1; independent draft-day reports D2 ESPN and D3 Bleacher Report describe the toss deciding the captain groups) |
| Winner's choice | The toss winner chooses **either** a captain group **or** the first pick | **Confirmed** (D1; D2, D3) |
| Consequence | If the winner takes a captain group, the loser is assigned the other group **and has the first pick in both the Tuesday and Wednesday drafts**. If the winner takes the first pick, the loser chooses a captain group | Rule text: **Unverified** (single league origin, D1). One draft-day extract's account of the opening Tuesday pick does not fit this text as written; the conflict was not resolved, and its details are withheld under the hindsight boundary |

### 2.5 The two sessions

|:-|:-|:-|
| **Tuesday January 21** (taped, then aired the same day) | **22 players, 11 per team**, drafted or assigned **in position segments**: interior defensive linemen (3 per team), guards (3), centers (2), fullback (1), punter (1), special teamer (1). The first segment was the interior defensive line. Maximum 30 seconds per pick. The release gives the taping as 12:30 to 2 p.m. without a time zone; the broadcast was 8 to 9 p.m. ET (`2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` §3.3) | Session groups and 22/11: **Confirmed** (D1; P6 CBS Sports independently lists the same Tuesday groups). Position-by-position segments with the interior line first: **Confirmed** (D1 "in segments"; D4 draft-day reports). Order of the later segments as listed: **Unverified** (D1 only). 30-second limit and taping window: **Unverified** (single league origin) |
| **Wednesday January 22** (live, NFL Network, 8 p.m. ET, three hours) | **60 players, 30 per team**, drafted or assigned from 12 groups: wide receivers (4 per team), tackles (3), tight ends (2), quarterbacks (3), running backs (3), outside linebackers (3), inside/middle linebackers (2), cornerbacks (4), safeties (3), defensive ends (3), placekicker (1), returner (1). Two minutes per pick. Minimum 12 assigned, maximum 48 drafted in this session | Groups and 60/30: **Confirmed** by arithmetic (the 12 groups hold 32 slots per team, 64 in all; 64 less the 4 captains, who are placed by group, is 60; 22 + 60 = 82). | Quarterback limit | One extract said that once each team has two quarterbacks it may not take a third until the last round | **Unverified**. A re-search with the exact wording found no source; it may be a cross-season conflation. Not adopted |
| Session totals | Minimum assigned **18** (6 Tuesday + 12 Wednesday); maximum drafted **64** (16 Tuesday + 48 Wednesday) | **Confirmed** (D1; arithmetic from the confirmed quotas). ## 4. Corrections and discrepancies

1. **Existing library entry (flagged, not edited).** `2013_season_honours_selection_structure.md` §4 item 4 treated "minimum 12 assigned, maximum 48 drafted" and "60 players, 30 per team" as another season's figures or a summarizer error. They are the **Wednesday-session** figures of the 2014 release and reconcile exactly with its overall 18 and 64 (§2.5). The 2015 release used different Wednesday figures (56 players, 28 per team, 60 seconds per pick; D7). That entry's conclusion (18 minimum, 64 maximum overall) stands; only its explanation of the stray figures is wrong. This task writes one file only, so the earlier file was not changed.
2. **July 2013 roster wording superseded.** The July 31, 2013 announcement said rosters would "continue to consist of 43 players per squad" and that the kick return specialist would be replaced by an additional defensive back (P1, P2, P3). The 43 matches the voted players per team (86 / 2); with the need player the game roster is 44 (§2.6). The returner change did **not** carry through: the December 27, 2013 selection lists and the January 2014 draft rules both carry one returner per team (at least one December list labels the slot as a punt returner, consistent with a game without kickoffs), and the draft rules give 7 defensive backs per team (4 CB, 3 S). **Corrected:** the January draft rules control the 2014 roster. Whether the defensive-back count changed from the old AFC/NFC roster was not checked.
3. **Narrow goalposts misdated.** A first-pass summary tied narrow goalposts and longer extra points to the 2014 game. They were 2015 experiments (P10). **Corrected.**
4. **Season mixing in draft examples.** Worked examples of the assignment rule and the "possession ends at the end of the quarter" example in several extracts name the 2015 alumni captains. They were used only as confirmation that the rule continued in 2015, never as 2014 text.
5. **"15 on-site media members."** One undated extract; not traced; contradicted by the 2009 league release and all other sources (16). Not used.
6. **Draft-day wording.** One secondary item placed the draft on "Wednesday and Thursday"; the league release and the calendar in `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` give Tuesday and Wednesday. The secondary item is wrong on the days and was used only for the alternation statement it shares with D2.
7. **Pro Bowl kickoff time (outside this file's scope, flagged).** P6 CBS Sports gives 7:30 p.m. ET, matching `2013_postseason_format_and_schedule.md` B3; `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` §3.3 gives 7 p.m. ET. Not researched further here.
