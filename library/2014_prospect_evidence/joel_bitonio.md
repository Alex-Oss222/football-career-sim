## 4. Dated evidence usable at the branch date

**E1. August 2, 2013 (date from one search summary; unverified). NFL.com, "Mountain West 2013 season preview."** https://www.nfl.com/news/mountain-west-2013-season-preview-0ap1000000217973
Previewed Nevada at UCLA on August 31, 2013: if Bitonio could neutralize UCLA outside linebacker Anthony Barr, "Bitonio's draft stock will explode." Described him as moving quite well for his size and as having started 26 consecutive games. The content previews an August 31 game, so the piece predates that game.

**E2. December 10, 2013. 2013 All-Mountain West team: first team, offensive line.**
Date: dated URLs https://www.mwcconnection.com/2013/12/10/5196338/2013-mountain-west-all-conference-football-awards and https://unlvrebels.com/news/2013/12/10/trio_of_rebels_named_second_team_all_mw.aspx . The honor itself is confirmed by several sources, including Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Bitonio).

**E3. December 12, 2013. NFL.com, "Nevada OL Joel Bitonio a name to know ahead of Senior Bowl."** https://www.nfl.com/news/nevada-ol-joel-bitonio-a-name-to-know-ahead-of-senior-bowl-0ap2000000297211
- Senior Bowl executive director Phil Savage said he was "hearing from scouts that he is at least a top-three round offensive lineman" (quoted as shown in search text).
- 6-4, 315; Nevada averaged about 250 passing and 179 rushing yards per game.
- Started 38 consecutive games, including UCLA, Florida State and BYU in 2013.
- Barr (UCLA) and Kyle Van Noy (BYU) had combined for 14 sacks that season, and Bitonio did not concede a sack to either.

**Earlier honors (before the branch date; dates not re-researched here):** Phil Steele preseason second-team All-Mountain West, 2013 (see the registry).

**G5. Publication date unverified (content is pre-draft: "his versatility will likely help him come May"). Buffalo Bills, "Draft Profile: Versatility is Bitonio's calling card."** https://www.buffalobills.com/news/draft-profile-versatility-is-bitonio-s-calling-card-12844293

Bitonio quoted: "They want guys who can play multiple positions because they only dress seven for a game." **Not usable until a pre-draft publication date is verified.**

**G6. Not usable before April 25, 2014. Big Cat Country, "Joel Bitonio 2014 NFL Draft Profile."** Dated URL https://www.bigcatcountry.com/2014/4/25/5646064/joel-bitonio-2014-nfl-draft-profile . Content not shown in search text; listed only.

**G7. Not usable before April 28, 2014. Mountain West Connection, "2014 NFL Draft Profile: Nevada OL Joel Bitonio."** Dated URL https://www.mwcconnection.com/2014/4/28/5662324/joel-bitonio-nfl-draft-profile
- Strengths named: quickness and tenacity; gets to the second level in the run game; rarely beaten outside in pass protection because of quick feet and hands; plays to the whistle.
- Concerns named: not the ideal body type; 33 7/8 in arms relatively short for an NFL tackle; at times moves too quickly for his own good; played most of his Nevada career from a two-point stance.
- Versatility: could be tried at tackle, with guard or center as alternatives.
