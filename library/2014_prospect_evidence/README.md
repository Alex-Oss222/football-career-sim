# Library: 2014 Prospect Evidence Files

**Purpose:** Dated, sourced evidence for the seven 2014 draft focus prospects, for scouts to read. These files record facts and contemporaneous published observations with their public dates. They are **not evaluations**: nothing here grades, ranks or projects a player.
**Research date:** 2026-09-28. **Branch date:** December 16 to 22, 2013.
**Pass status:** research pass (pass 1) and verification pass (pass 2) complete for all seven files. Each file ends with its own "Verification pass" section (confirmed, corrected, unverified).
**Companion files:** `library/2014_draft_pool_registry.md` (eligibility status; controls whether a player is draftable) and `library/2014_draft_information_gates.md` (date gates). These evidence files do not change any eligibility status.

## Source-access limitation (all files)

WebFetch was blocked by the network policy in the original two passes, which used **search-result text**. "Confirmed" in those passes means at least two independent search results agreed; single-source items say so. A subsequent Week 16 full-page check corrected Smith's media All-ACC team and Butler's regional-release date, and verified Paradis's banquet award and pre-bowl starts. Those three files identify the checked items and retain the original limitations for other evidence. See the [Week 16 verification register](../../career/2013/scouting/2014_draft/sources_week16_2013_12_22.md).

## File layout (same in every file)

1. Identity and listed measurables
2. Position history by season
3. Season production, each season marked with the date it became public
4. Dated evidence usable at the branch date (items E1, E2 ...)
5. **Gated (not usable before DATE)** items (G1, G2 ...): anything published or occurring after December 22, 2013 and before the draft began (Thursday, May 8, 2014, 8:00 PM ET)
6. Located but not used (post-draft, undated or unattributable material)
7. Discrepancies (where present)
8. Verification pass

## Loading rules

- At any simulation date, load only E items and G items whose date is on or before that date. An item whose publication date is **unverified** stays unloaded until its date is verified, even if its content looks pre-draft.
- **Season completeness at the branch date.** Bitonio (Nevada, last game November 30, 2013) and, if the single-source November 23, 2013 date holds, Butler (West Alabama) have complete 2013 seasons. Harris did not play in 2013. Leno and Paradis (Boise State bowl, December 24, 2013), Turner (LSU bowl, January 1, 2014) and Smith (Florida State bowl, January 6, 2014) do **not**: their full-season totals are gated to those dates. No bowl result is recorded anywhere in these files.
- Nothing published after the draft began may be used. Pre-draft facts found only in post-draft sources are flagged as such and are gated or excluded.

## Items for other owners (not edited by this task)

- **Registry, Telvin Smith:** the registry cites the ESPN ACC blog "2013 Top 25 countdown No. 13" as preseason 2013 and draws "the middle linebacker job was his in 2013" from it. Pass 2 found that piece is a January 2014 postseason countdown (one summary dates it January 27, 2014), and three dated 2013 sources list Smith as the weak-side linebacker, with middle-linebacker time in 2011 and 2012. See `telvin_smith.md`.
- **Registry, Joel Bitonio:** the registry records "38 consecutive starts" from NFL.com; school and reference text say he started his last 39 games or made 39 consecutive appearances. Unresolved; see `joel_bitonio.md`.
