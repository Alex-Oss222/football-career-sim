## 4. Dated evidence usable at the branch date

**E1. August 2, 2013 (date from one search summary; unverified). NFL.com, "Mountain West 2013 season preview."** https://www.nfl.com/news/mountain-west-2013-season-preview-0ap1000000217973
Leno had started 26 consecutive games, moving to left tackle in 2012 after playing right tackle in 2011. Against BYU he "stonewalled" the pass-rush pair of Ezekiel Ansah and Kyle Van Noy but conceded a coverage sack to Ansah (paraphrase of search text).
