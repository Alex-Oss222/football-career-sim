## 4. Dated evidence usable at the branch date

**E0. Undated, but prospective to the 2013 season (labelled inference from content). ESPN, "Solo flight."** https://www.espn.com/colleges/fsu/football/story/_/id/9109954/florida-state-seminoles-linebacker-telvin-smith-ready-fly-solo
Describes two seasons as a platoon player at middle linebacker and 2013 as his chance to become an every-down player; he and Vince Williams combined for 123 tackles the previous year. Smith: "I'm just ready to play." **Publication date unverified.**

**E1. June 24, 2013. Bleacher Report, "Florida State Football: Why Telvin Smith Is the Most Underrated LB in the ACC."** https://bleacherreport.com/articles/1653457-florida-state-football-why-telvin-smith-is-the-most-underrated-lb-in-the-acc
Called him an extremely athletic, lean linebacker with exceptional range, outstanding instincts and hitting power; compared him with Alabama's C.J. Mosley, noting Mosley's roughly 25-pound size advantage and that Smith was then off the radar of many scouts. (Date from one search summary.)

**E2. August 5, 2013. Tomahawk Nation, "Florida State football camp 2013: Defense questions."** Dated URL https://www.tomahawknation.com/florida-state-football-fsu-noles/2013/8/5/4588866/florida-state-football-camp-2013-defense-questions
Smith and Christian Jones entrenched as starters at WLB and MLB; Jimbo Fisher said they would change depending on the situation.

**E3. August 19, 2013 (date from one search summary). ESPN FSU blog, "Noles D relishing Pruitt's aggressive style."** https://www.espn.co.uk/blog/colleges/fsu/post/_/id/9513/noles-d-relishing-pruitts-aggressive-style
New defensive coordinator Jeremy Pruitt's meeting-room accomplishment lists sparked a competition between Smith and Jones.

**E4. August 27, 2013. seminoles.com, "Instant Analysis: FSU's Two-Deep Depth Chart."** Dated URL https://seminoles.com/news/2013/8/27/instant-analysis-fsus-two-deep-depth-chart
Jones and Smith top the depth chart at middle and weak-side linebacker respectively.

**E5. October 25, 2013. Tomahawk Nation, "Telvin Smith continues to Improve & Impress."** Dated URL https://www.tomahawknation.com/f/2013/10/25/5028720/telvin-smith-continues-to-improve-impress
Improved from the previous year but could still get better; stronger than ever at the point of attack, playing like 238 pounds despite a listed 218; played predominantly at WILL, showing speed, instincts and downhill play.

**E6. December 2, 2013. ACC, "2013 All-ACC Teams Announced": first-team linebacker.** Dated URL https://theacc.com/news/2013/12/2/529ce42de4b0465167b4e15a_131480996780103312.aspx
Listing in search text: "LB, Telvin Smith, Florida State, 6-3, 218, Sr., Valdosta, Ga." Christian Jones on the second team.

**E7. December 4, 2013. ESPN (David Hale), feature on Smith rededicating himself in honor of former teammate Greg Reid.** https://www.espn.com/college-football/story/_/id/10077490/florida-state-seminoles-linebacker-telvin-smith-rededicates-honor-former-teammate-greg-reid
Senior linebacker; vocal leader of the defense (from the registry).

**E8. December 7, 2013. ACC Championship Game against Duke: diving interception** (Yahoo ACC "Must See Moment" page https://sports.yahoo.com/fsu-lb-telvin-smith-makes-044540275.html ; Wikipedia).
