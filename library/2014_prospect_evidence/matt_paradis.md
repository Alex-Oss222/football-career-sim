## Eligibility (from the registry)

Redshirt senior in 2013; automatically eligible. No declaration needed.

## 1. Identity and listed measurables

- **School and position:** Boise State, 2009 to 2013 (walk-on from 2008); center.
- **Hometown and high school:** Council, Idaho; Council High School, an eight-man football program.
- **High-school record (from OBNUG text, dated 2011 or 2012; see E1 and E2):** 186 tackles and 17 sacks over his junior and senior seasons; 1A Idaho Player of the Year in 2008; all-state as a sophomore and junior; no scholarship offers; invited walk-on at Boise State.
- **Listed college height and weight:** **unverified.** A reference infobox gives 6-3, 300, which may be a later listing; the Boise State roster page (https://broncosports.com/sports/football/roster/matt-paradis/4881) was not read.
- Combine measurements: height and weight **unverified**.
