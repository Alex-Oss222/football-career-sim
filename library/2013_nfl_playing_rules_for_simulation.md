# Library: 2013 NFL playing rules the game kernel models

**Status:** Research pass and separate verification pass completed September 28, 2026. This file covers the 2013 NFL playing rules that the shared game kernel (`runtime/kernel.py`, `runtime/field_position.py`, `runtime/drive_model.py`, `runtime/play_detail.py`, `runtime/rules.py`) touches, and records the audit of the kernel against them that produced kernel 2013.8. It is research only. It changes no canon, closed result, roster, standings or calendar fact.

**Method:** The project's two-pass discipline applies. The first pass found each claim. The second pass searched each claim again from scratch, with different query wording, looking for a different outlet, without relying on the first pass's citation. Each claim is labelled **Confirmed** (two or more independent sources agree and at least one is contemporaneous with, or describes, the rule as it stood in 2012-2013), **Confirmed (unchanged rule)** (two or more sources agree, but every source found post-dates 2013; the rule is long-standing and a later source records when it next changed, or that it has not), or **Unverified** (one source, or post-2013 sources only with no evidence the rule was in force in 2013).

**Hindsight boundary:** The current in-sim date is mid-November 2013. No 2013 regular-season or postseason game result, 2013 season statistic (for example the 2013 extra-point success rate) or post-2013 outcome is used as evidence of a rule. The illustrative games cited are 2012 games. Later sources are used only to date when a rule changed after 2013 (which shows the 2013 value), and they are marked.

**Access limits (read before relying on this file):** Page fetches were blocked by this environment's egress policy for every host tried: nfl.info (the league's own 2013 rule-book PDF, `https://www.nfl.info/download/2012mediaguides/2013%20nfl%20rule%20book.pdf`), archive.org (the archived copy, `https://archive.org/details/2013-nfl-rulebook`) and footballzebras.com. **Every source below was therefore read through search-engine result text (title, URL and extracted passage), not by opening the page.** The 2013 Official Playing Rules text itself (Rules 4, 6, 11, 15 and 16) was **not read directly**. Foundation Document 2 §7 already records these rules as "2013 playing rules, verified"; this file supplies the citations. A future session with page access should read the rule book at the URLs above and upgrade or correct the labels.

## Rules

| # | Rule (2013) | Label | First pass | Verification pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Regular-season and preseason overtime is one 15-minute period.** The regular-season period was not shortened to 10 minutes until 2017. | Confirmed | S1, S2 | S8 (2017 change dates the 15-minute period), S6 (2012 tie "after 15 minutes of overtime") |
| R2 | **Modified sudden death (regular season from 2012; postseason from 2010).** A coin toss decides the receiver. The game ends if the team that possesses first scores a touchdown on its first possession, or if the defence scores (a safety, or a defensive touchdown) on that possession. If the first possession ends in a field goal, the other team gets a possession (or the opportunity to possess): its touchdown wins, its field goal ties and leads to sudden death, and any other result ends the game for the field-goal team. If the first possession ends without a score, the next score of any kind wins. | Confirmed | S1, S2, S3 | S4, S5, S7 (Nov 18, 2012 Texans-Jaguars: both clubs kicked first-possession field goals, then sudden death) |
| R3 | **Regular-season tie.** If the period expires with the score level, the game ends in a tie. | Confirmed | S1 | S6 (Nov 11, 2012 Rams-49ers 24-24 after 15 minutes of overtime) |
| R4 | **Kicking-team recovery.** If the kicking team legally recovers the overtime kickoff (for example an onside kick), the receiving team is considered to have had its opportunity to possess. | Confirmed | S10 | S11 |
| R5 | **Postseason overtime.** 15-minute periods; a two-minute intermission between periods and no halftime after the second overtime period in the 2010-era rule text as reported; play continues until a winner, so a postseason game cannot end tied; the modified-sudden-death possession rule carries across a period break (a first possession still in progress when the first period ends continues into the second). | Confirmed | S9, S5 | S8 (post-2013, same structure) |
| R6 | **Overtime timeouts and reviews.** Each team has two timeouts in regular-season overtime; there are no coach's challenges in overtime (the replay official initiates every review). | Unverified for 2013 (post-2013 sources only). The no-challenge part matches Document 2 §7 | S8 | Foundation Document 2 §7 (review authority only) |
| R7 | **Kickoffs from the 35; kickoff touchback to the 20.** Kickoffs moved from the 30 to the 35 in 2011; the touchback stayed at the 20. It moved to the 25 only in 2016 (kickoffs and safety kicks), so 2013 is the 20. | Confirmed | S12 | S13, S14 (2016 change) |
| R8 | **Scrimmage-kick (punt) touchback to the 20.** | Confirmed (unchanged rule) | S15 | S14 (the 2016 change left "all other touchbacks, including punts and end zone turnovers" at the 20) |
| R9 | **Free kick after a safety from the kicking team's 20**, by punt, drop kick or place kick (no tee in the NFL before 2024). | Confirmed (unchanged rule) | S16 | S17 |
| R10 | **The try (extra point / two-point conversion) is snapped at the 2-yard line** in 2013: a kick scores one point, a successful run or pass two. The kick moved to the 15 only in 2015; the two-point snap stayed at the 2. | Confirmed | S18 | S19, S20 |
| R11 | **No try after a walk-off overtime touchdown** (the game ends on the score). | Confirmed (follows from R2: the game ends when the touchdown is scored). The engine already applies it | S2 | S4 |
| R12 | **Missed field goal:** the defence takes over at the spot of the kick (seven or eight yards behind the line of scrimmage) or its own 20, whichever is farther from its goal line (rule since 1994). | Confirmed (unchanged rule) | S21 | S22 |
| R13 | **Game clock:** four 15-minute quarters; 12-minute halftime; a two-minute warning in each half (and in overtime); two-minute intermissions between quarters. | Confirmed (unchanged rule); already verified in Document 2 §7 | S23 | S24 |
| R14 | **Play clock:** 40 seconds from the end of a play; 25 seconds after administrative stoppages (change of possession, charged timeout, two-minute warning, end of a period, penalty enforcement). | Confirmed (unchanged rule); already verified in Document 2 §7 | S25 | Document 2 §7 |
| R15 | **46 game-day actives, 7 inactives, 53-man roster.** Since 2011 the active list is 46 and the third-quarterback rule is gone. | Confirmed | S26 | S27 |
| R16 | **Instant replay in 2013:** two coach's challenges, a third after two successful ones (since 2004); the replay official initiates reviews inside two minutes of each half, in overtime, of every scoring play (2011) and every turnover (2012). From 2013 (the "Jim Schwartz rule"), a coach's flag on an automatically reviewed play no longer cancels the review. | Confirmed | S28, S29 | S30, S31 |
| R17 | **Three charged timeouts per half.** | Confirmed (unchanged rule); already verified in Document 2 §7 | Document 2 §7 | S23 |

## Engine audit (kernel 2013.7, fixed in 2013.8 where marked)

The kernel resamples real 2012 drives, kicks and transitions. 2012 and 2013 were played under the same rules for every item below (R2 took effect for the regular season in 2012; no rule in this table changed between the 2012 and 2013 seasons), so a real 2012 record is a legal 2013 play. The audit therefore checks the rules the kernel *applies itself*: the overtime state machine, the clock windows, the transitions and the game-day limits.

| Rule | Kernel 2013.7 behaviour | Status |
|---|---|---|
| R1-R3 regular-season overtime | **Defect.** The 2012 overtime pool marks a drive `final` when its score ended the game (a walk-off). `field_position._dynamic` / `draw_drive` treated every `final` overtime tuple as running out the whole window, so a first-possession field goal could consume the entire 15:00 period and the expiry rule then ended the game (Week 10 Washington at Minnesota, drive 26: `OT`, 900 to 0, opening-drive field goal, game over). In the 250-game synthetic sample (kernel 2013.7), 10 of 21 overtime games had a non-clock drive run out the rest of the period: one opening field goal ended the game with the other club never possessing, one fourth-down (downs) drive ran out the period and produced a tie, and eight walk-off scores (seven field goals, one touchdown) were clocked to 0:00 though the winner was right. | **Fixed in 2013.8.** In the overtime regime only a clock-expired drive ends the period (`field_position.ends_window`); every other 2012 overtime drive replays with its own scaled seconds and must fit the time left. Possessions alternate on a real clock; `rules.ot_status` applies R2; the game ties only when the period expires (R3). |
| R5 postseason overtime | **Defect (not yet reachable in 2013 canon).** A clock-expired postseason possession handed the ball to the other club at `100 - end`, and the continuation counted as a new possession in the modified-sudden-death history. | **Fixed in 2013.8.** Postseason periods lie on one continuous countdown (`RULES.postseason_ot_period_bound` periods of 15:00, an engine bound that fails closed if ever exhausted), so a possession carries across a period break and the game never ends tied; snap rows are labelled `OT`, `OT2`, ... by `play_detail._period_clock`. |
| R11 no try after walk-off touchdown | Applied (`walk_off` in `kernel.possess`; ledger class `xp_after_ot_walkoff`). | Match. |
| R16 review authority in overtime | `rules.review_authority` omitted overtime (booth authority only inside two minutes, scoring and turnovers). | **Fixed in 2013.8** (`overtime=True` returns booth). Not used by the outcome path. |
| R2 helper | `rules.overtime_ends` (unused) ended overtime on any lead after a completed possession, which ignores modified sudden death. | **Removed in 2013.8** (`ot_status` is the single implementation). |
| R15 46 actives | Background actives were trimmed to 46 by `week_inputs.game_day_actives`, but `game_runner.build_game_packet` accepted any number of actives (Weeks 1-2 receipts carry 47-53 players per club; Weeks 3-10 are within 46). | **Fixed in 2013.8.** `build_game_packet` fails closed above `RULES.active_limit` (46) before the private event is journaled. Synthetic calibration rosters used directly by the kernel (47 players) are unaffected. |
| R7 kickoff 35 / touchback 20 | Real 2012 own-35 kickoff records; a touchback start of 80 + enforcement. | Match. |
| R8 punt touchback 20 | `field_position.punt`: touchback start 80 + enforcement. | Match. |
| R9 safety free kick from the 20 | Real 2012 free-kick records from the 20 (13 records, all returned). | Match. |
| R12 missed field goal | `missed_fg_start = min(80, 110 - distance)` (kick spot = distance - 10 from the goal line, or the 20). | Match. |
| R10 try | Extra-point kick only, at the 2012 rate (1,229/1,237). | **Listed, not fixed:** the kernel never attempts a two-point try, although the 2012 drive-model artifact carries the two-point rate. A two-point decision rule is a football-choice model, not a small fix. |
| R4 kicking-team recovery | Not modelled: the kickoff pool holds no kicking-team recoveries (onside kicks are excluded), so every kick goes to the receiving club. | **Listed.** The overtime branch "kicking team recovers" cannot occur. |
| R2 defensive score on the first possession | Not modelled: interception, fumble and kick-return touchdowns do not exist in the transition pools, so the only defensive score is a safety. | **Listed** (already a documented 2013.7 limitation). |
| R6, R13, R14, R17 clock rules | Quarters, halves and the overtime period are exact windows (900/1800 s). Timeouts, the two-minute warning and the play clock are not simulated per snap; each drive's seconds are the real 2012 drive's scaled duration, which embeds them. | **Listed** (structural: no per-snap clock). Constants are in `runtime/rules.py`. |
| R13/R14 spikes | A spike stops a running clock. Every possession starts on a stopped clock (R14: a change of possession is an administrative stoppage), and an incompletion stops it. Kernels through 2013.8 shuffled a drive's real 2012 spikes into any snap slot, so a spike could open a drive or follow an incompletion (Week 12, Entry 53). | **Fixed in 2013.9** (`play_detail._seat_spikes`): a spike sits just after a run, sack or completion; snap order only, no result changes. |
| Neutral site | `kernel._edge` gives the 0.008 home term to the designated home club whatever the venue. | **Listed** (existing Entry 45 limitation). |

## Verification notes

- **R1/R2/R3.** The first pass (Bleacher Report, NFL.com, Fox News) and the second pass (Football Zebras, Niners Nation, two 2012 game accounts) agree on every branch of modified sudden death. The 2012 Texans-Jaguars game independently confirms the "both kick field goals, then sudden death" branch; the 2012 Rams-49ers game confirms the 15-minute period and the tie. A search result also described a November 2013 tie; it post-dates the in-sim clock and was not used.
- **R5.** The NFL.com postseason page and Niners Nation agree on continuation across periods and the two-minute intermission. Only post-2013 sources repeat the period length; the postseason rule has not changed since 2010 in the respect the kernel models (untimed until a score, 15-minute periods).
- **R6.** Two timeouts per team in overtime was found only in 2024 coverage. It is recorded as a constant (`RULES.regular_ot_timeouts`) but marked **Unverified** for 2013 and has no effect on outcomes (the kernel does not simulate timeouts).
- **R7-R10.** The 2011 kickoff change is confirmed by two 2011 reports; the later changes (2015 try, 2016 touchback) are each confirmed by two outlets and fix the 2013 values by elimination.
- **Extra-point rate.** A search result quoted a 2013 season extra-point success rate. It is a 2013 outcome and is not used; the kernel keeps its sourced 2012 rate (1,229/1,237).
- **No correction to Document 2 §7 was needed.** Every rule it states matches the findings above; this file only adds citations and the engine audit.
