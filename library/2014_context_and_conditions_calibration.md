# 2014 context and conditions calibration

[Pre-build specification](2014_6_pre_build_specification.md) · [League base](2010_2014_calibration_base.md) · [Engine decisions](../runtime/2014_engine_decisions.md#kernel-20146-decisions-dated)

**What this is.** The league record behind the kernel 2014.6 weather and venue terms (plan batch B3d, data only; read by batch B14, R20). It holds three artifacts, all built by `scripts/research/build_2014_conditions_calibration.py`, which has a `--check` mode:

- `library/data/2014_conditions_calibration.json`: the pooled condition slopes, the keep-rule record, the centres and the centring sample;
- `library/data/2014_venues.json`: static venue facts;
- `library/data/2010_2013_venue_climatology.json`: venue-by-month wind and cold means.

**Data window.** NFL regular seasons 2010-2013 and 2014 Weeks 1-4 (games through September 29, 2014), read only through the batch B2 source gate. The second pass reads nflscrapR's own events. nflscrapR carries no weather, so its rows are joined to the nflverse game conditions by game id. Only pooled slopes are stored. Club, kicker and punter fixed effects are absorbed in the fits and never written.

## Method

- **Covariates** (specification section 4):
  - indoor: the roof is dome or closed;
  - wind: outdoor miles per hour;
  - cold: max(0, 50 - temperature) / 10 outdoors;
  - altitude: a game at Denver's home venue;
  - turf: any surface other than grass;
  - precipitation: report only, single source.

  The game-edge terms are signed for the offense: the time-zone difference, the Denver edge, and the body-clock edge (a Pacific visitor at a 1:00 p.m. Eastern start). An outdoor game without temperature or wind leaves every weather model and is counted. Club time zones are static geography, and they are the only typed facts in the builder.
- **Forms.** Every model has season fixed effects. Field-goal make is a logit on kicker fixed effects with the kernel's own distance model as an offset: the league base drive model's band intercept plus its slope times the distance. Punt gross is least squares on punts snapped from the receiving side of midfield (yardline_100 of 55 or more), with yardline and punter fixed effects. Kickoff touchback is a logit on 2011-2014 Week 4 kickoffs from the 35, not onside, with kicker fixed effects; the kernel applies it as a likelihood ratio on the kickoff record draw. Drive touchdown share is a logit with start-bin, home, offense club-season and defense club-season fixed effects. Interception rate and passing-touchdown share are fitted and go live only if kept. Sack rate and yards are report only, because the drive replay has no channel for them.
- **Keep rule.** A term is kept only when all four tests hold: it has a preregistered sign; its full-sample estimate has that sign; its leave-one-stadium-out skill is positive (every stadium, with neutral sites as their own group, held out once with both models refit); and the second pass reproduces both. Kept terms are refit jointly, and a term whose joint sign flips is dropped, the lower single skill first. There is no shrinkage. Altitude terms stay at slope 0, because the rule cannot test a one-venue term (decision 1B.2).
- **Per-season stability.** Each term carries its per-season slopes and Cochran's Q. A Q test with p below 0.01 is flagged unstable and reported, not used as a gate.
- **Centring.** A kernel shift is slope × (x − centre), where the centre is the outcome's mean covariate over its base events, so the league mean stays on the base. The artifact also stores the per-game condition tuples of the base games, with no identifiers, as the centring sample.
- **Inputs at runtime** (decision 1B.3): static venue facts plus the 2010-2013 climatology. No 2014 weather file is read at runtime. A retractable roof uses (1 − its 2010-2013 closed share) × the open-roof means. A venue-month cell with fewer than five games uses the venue's all-season mean plus the league month offset (a labelled convention). A venue with no 2010-2013 outdoor game uses the league cell. Reader-facing prose states weather only when the basis is observed.

## Limits

- Stadium names are the nflverse spellings as published today, so some are anachronistic for 2014. Venues are keyed by the source venue id.
- The kickoff touchback raw bins are shown with Denver's home games excluded, because altitude would otherwise dominate the cold gradient.
- The offense plan test (early-down neutral pass rate against drive touchdown share) is reported in the design record only. It cannot be adopted in 2014.6.
