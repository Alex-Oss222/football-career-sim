# Library: 2013 season honours selection structure (Pro Bowl, AP All-Pro, AP awards)

**Status:** Research pass and separate verification pass completed September 28, 2026. This file extends `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` (which holds the calendar, voter pool and announcement dates) with the **selection structure** of three 2013-season honours: how many players at each position, how ballots worked, how ties and replacements were handled, and who was eligible. It is research only. It changes no canon, roster, standings or calendar fact.

**Method:** The project's two-pass discipline applies. The first pass found each claim. The second pass searched each claim again from scratch with different query wording and, where possible, a different outlet or an independent arithmetic cross-check. Labels: **Confirmed** (two or more independent sources agree, or a league/AP release plus an independent corroboration), **Corrected** (the first pass, the task premise or one source was wrong; the correction and reason are stated), **Unverified** (one source only, a source from another season only, or an inference). Where a rule is documented only for an earlier or later season, that is said explicitly and the item stays Unverified for 2013 unless a source ties it to 2013.

**Access limits (read before relying on this file):** WebFetch to en.wikipedia.org was attempted and **blocked by this environment's egress proxy** (EGRESS_BLOCKED). No block was routed around. **Every source below was read through search-engine result text (title, URL, extracted passage), not by opening the page.** Search summaries mixed seasons more than once (see Corrections); every count below was therefore checked against a second, independent route. A future session with page access should re-open the URLs and upgrade or correct the labels.

**Hindsight boundary:** Most source pages below list real 2013 honourees. They were used **only to count slots per position** or to read a procedural statement. No 2013 winner, finalist, vote total, Pro Bowl selection, alternate, captain, coach, long snapper or All-Pro name is recorded here, and none may be imported as an answer key (Document 1 §8, Document 2 §4.3).

Notes:
- **Unconferenced quotas:** the 2014 game was the first "Unconferenced" Pro Bowl; players were selected "without regard to conference" and every count above is a league total. **Confirmed** (R1, R2, and the July 31, 2013 format releases already cited as B1 to B3 in `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md`).
- **Position classification (3-4 versus 4-3):** the announcement grouped nose tackles with defensive tackles and used OLB and ILB/MLB groups. Exactly how the ballot classified a 4-3 weakside or strongside linebacker, or a 3-4 end, was **not found**. **Unverified**. The branch should classify each player by the ballot position his club lists before it runs the vote.
- **Week 17 is outside the Pro Bowl vote:** the fan ballot closed December 26, 2013 and selections were announced December 27, both before Week 17 (December 29). This is a date comparison of already-confirmed facts, not a separate source.

The "need" players are **not** part of the 86 voted selections; 86 voted + 2 need = 88 participants. **Confirmed** (R3, R4).

### 2.2 Voting

| Item | 2013 fact | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Voters | 50-member AP national media panel (same panel as the awards) | **Confirmed** (A3; `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` A4, P1) |
| Ballot | **Corrected premise:** in 2013 there were **no points and no separate second-team ballot.** Each voter cast **one vote per slot** (one quarterback, two wide receivers, and so on), naming a single team | **Confirmed** (A9 Football Perspective describes the process as 50 voters selecting their top players at each position; A10 ProFootballTalk, 2022: "Previously, it was one vote per position per voter") |
| First team | The top vote-getters at each position fill the first-team slots | **Confirmed** (A9, A10) |
| Second team | Formed from the **runners-up** in the same single vote (the next vote-getter, or the third and fourth at two-slot positions); voters did not vote a second team | **Confirmed** (A9; A10) |
| Empty second-team slot | If the first-teamer(s) took every vote, there is no runner-up and the second-team slot is left **vacant** (the 2013 announcement shows a position with "no second team") | **Confirmed** (A2 Patriots.com; A3 ESPN) |
| Second-team ties | Players tied for a second-team slot are **all** named (2013 second teams list more names than slots at several positions) | **Confirmed** (A2, A4, A5 show three or more second-teamers at two-slot positions) |
| First-team ties | Rule not found for 2013 | **Unverified** |
| Points system | A points system (first-team and second-team votes on one ballot) began only in **2022** | **Confirmed** (A10 ProFootballTalk; A11 Football Outsiders). Not applicable to 2013 |
| Postseason | Ballots follow the regular season; the teams were announced January 3, 2014, before the Divisional round | **Confirmed** (`2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` §3.2) |

| Item | 2013 fact | Label |
|---|---|---|

## 4. Corrections made in this pass

1. **All-Pro points (task premise corrected).** The request anticipated "points per first-team vote and second-team vote". That system dates from 2022. In 2013 each voter cast one vote per slot and the second team was the runners-up (A9, A10, A11).
2. **All-Pro punt returner and special teamer.** A first-pass summary listed a 2013 punt returner. Corrected: 2013 had only a kick returner; the punt returner and special teamer were added in 2016 (A6, A7).
3. **Pro Bowl tackle and center counts.** A roster page updated at game time lists 8 tackles and 5 centers because it includes injury and Super Bowl replacements. The December 27 announcement had 6 and 4, which also match the draft quotas (R2, R3).
4. **Pro Bowl draft numbers.** One search summary gave "minimum 12 assigned, maximum 48 drafted" and "60 players, 30 per team". Those figures do not reconcile with 88 participants and appear to be another season's rules or a summarizer error. The 2014 rules page (R3) gives maximum 64 drafted and minimum 18 assigned, which reconciles exactly (64 + 18 = 82 draftable; 82 + 4 captains + 2 need = 88). The existing `2013_nfl_awards_structure.md` entry therefore stands; no edit to it is needed.
5. **Wrong-season summary rejected.** One search summary for the "2014 NFL Pro Bowl Roster" page returned a roster from a different Pro Bowl year. It was discarded and not used for any count.
