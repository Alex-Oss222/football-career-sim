## Date semantics and coverage

An announcement, effective retirement, last game, release, reserve/retired transaction and one-day ceremonial contract are distinct. The sources below establish announcement dates or periods; an exact effective transaction date remains null unless separately verified. Unknown means not verified, not still active. Pending intentions (including possible future retirement plans) are not completed retirements.

Ten dates have official or corroborating reports. Blake has a retrospectively reported date without independent contemporary confirmation; Miller has conflicting dated reports. Forty-nine identities have no verified exact date in this audit. Do not replace those gaps with a last-season year.

## Verification notes

- **Chad Henne:** Announcement Sunday night; reports published February 13.
- **C.J. Anderson:** Club report includes direct confirmation from the player; separate report quotes the same-day announcement.
- **Maurice Jones-Drew:** The one-day Jaguars contract and ceremony were April 28, not the original retirement announcement.
- **Adam Thielen:** Vikings January 15 report specifies Wednesday; Reuters dates the event January 14.
- **Eugene Monroe:** Announcement date only; no later medical information is imported.
- **Brad Meester:** Season-end intent, not a December 18 removal.
- **Jeremy Mincey:** Public announcement; not last playing season.
- **Roy Miller:** July 1 CBS article says Saturday (June 30); July 2 RotoWire blurb says Monday. June 30 is the earlier dated report, not an independently settled exact date. No personal allegations retained.
- **Russell Allen:** Actual-history date only; source medical events do not apply to this branch.
- **Paul Posluszny:** Club published his retirement letter on March 13.
- **Josh Scobee:** March announcement and April ceremony are separate dates.
- **Antwon Blake:** Retrospective KROD report supplies the date. A matching reference is not independent contemporary confirmation; exact transaction date unresolved.
