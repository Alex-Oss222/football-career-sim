Tuesday January 14, 2014, at the stadium facility: summaries for defensive players and specialists outside the main core and core.

### Ryan Davis, DE

Present: Stone; Anthony Pleasant. Fourth edge rusher; age 24; under contract (reserve/future contract, final year 2014). Hurt in the Divisional (trunk, minor; out, projected return January 14); his exit physical is today, and clearance is the medical staff's. In camp he "earned rotational rush work," with "multiple generated rush finishes across the preseason." Inactive in Week 1, he dressed for the other 15 games and both playoff games: 1 tackle (assisted, Week 10 at Tennessee), no sack or pressure recorded.

Stone's message: the preseason rush evidence earned the job. The season record does not show how many rushes he got, so Stone would not call the gap between camp and games a finishing problem or an opportunity problem until Pleasant's review separates the two.

Davis's point: the fourth end's work came in pieces, and he asked what moves him up. Stone promised no role: 2014 evidence moves roles, and the spring order starts open.

Plan: voluntary. Pleasant offers a cutup of his preseason finishes beside his regular-season rushes. Once the medical staff clears him, strength work is suggested, with the performance staff's input on request within CBA limits; football instruction waits for the offseason program, no earlier than April 21.

Follow-ups: Pleasant to classify his 2013 rush reps and give him the result in writing.

### Lavar Edwards, DE

Present: Stone; Anthony Pleasant. Rookie (#135); age 23; under contract, final year 2016; available, no communicated restriction. At rookie minicamp he "knew the base edge/contain assignment and communicated it"; his rush lane widened on one team rep, "a technique/landmark loss rather than a processing failure," and he "corrected the lane on the repeat." He was inactive Weeks 1 to 11 and 13 and dressed Weeks 12 and 14 to 17 and both playoff games: 1 tackle (assisted, Week 14 against Houston).

Edwards's point: during the inactive weeks nobody told him what the staff saw in practice, and he asked what gets him dressed. Stone treated the first as possible staff teaching failure and promised no role on the second.

Plan: voluntary. Pleasant offers a cutup of his minicamp and game reps built on rush-lane landmarks; strength and movement work is suggested, not required.

Follow-ups: Pleasant to give him a written evaluation. Teaching note: a rookie went from minicamp to Week 12 without a recorded individual evaluation.
