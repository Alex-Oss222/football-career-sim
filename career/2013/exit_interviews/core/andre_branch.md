**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; Anthony Pleasant (defensive line).
**Status going in:** Edge 3: listed fourth in the Week 1 edge rotation, behind C.J. Wilson, and third in every recorded edge order from Week 2 through Week 8 (rotating in Weeks 1 and 2); Edge 3 on the season-end roster; seventh in the defensive-line depth order. Active 53; available, no communicated restriction; his exit physical is with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: 2012 second-round four-year rookie contract; final year 2015; under contract when the 2014 league year opens on March 11 (1 accrued season through 2012, which ended with him on Reserve/Injured at full pay; 2013 accruing in the branch). Age 24 (born July 14, 1989).

## The season in the record

Before 2013 he had a 2012 rookie record of 13 games and 3 starts, wearing 90, and was on Reserve/Injured at the close of 2012. Stone's roster evaluation of late January and February 2013 placed him in the "Defensive core/development" group. That label was not raised in this interview.

Camp. In the first physical work "Babin supplied the leading edge-rush pressure, while Branch and C.J. Wilson remained in the edge/strong-side rotation." No spring record, and no later camp or preseason record, names him. At camp close Crennel retained "Babin on the edge," and "Ryan Davis earned rotational rush work" (standouts: "Multiple generated rush finishes across the preseason").

Season. Crennel called the defense. The edge order was "Babin; Mincey, Wilson, Branch rotating" in Week 1, "Babin; Mincey, Branch, Davis rotating" in Week 2, and "Babin, Mincey, Branch, Davis" from Week 3 through Week 8, the last week file to list the edge order; the season-end roster lists him Edge 3. He dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games. The bye correction for the defense included "rush finish," and from Week 8 Stone's directives to Crennel put containment or the run fit ahead of the rush in most weeks.

Statistics (closed receipts):

- Regular season, 16 games: 4 tackles (2 solo, 2 assisted), 1 pressure; no sack, no tackle for loss.
- Postseason, 2 games: dressed for both; nothing credited.
- Credited weeks: Week 4 vs Indianapolis (W 31-10), 2 tackles (1 solo, 1 assisted), with the edge row "Babin and Branch two tackles each"; Week 8 vs San Francisco in London (W 20-13), 1 solo tackle; Week 15 vs Buffalo (L 16-45), 1 assisted tackle; Week 16 vs Tennessee (W 38-27), 1 pressure. Nothing was credited in Weeks 1 to 3, 5 to 7, 10 to 14 and 17.

No receipt injury, no weekly-award shortlist, no in-season honour.

## Stone's evaluation

Stone said it first and plainly: the written record cannot tell either of them how well Branch played in 2013. After the July 26-29 opening block of camp, no staff record evaluates him individually.

Strengths:

- Camp. He held his place in the edge/strong-side rotation through the first physical work.
- Availability. Dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games with no receipt injury; third in every recorded edge order from Week 2 through Week 8.
- Week 4, the one week an edge row's evidence column names him: "Babin and Branch two tackles each."

Development targets:

- Nothing written supports a technique finding, good or bad, and Stone would not invent one. The questions for his film are the room's: containment on the directive weeks, rush lanes, and finish.
- What he will be judged on in 2014 is the edge job as the program writes it: an edge knows "when they are responsible for the quarterback, the back, boot action or contain," and the Good, Better, Best descriptions of assignment, correction retention and changing looks.

Teaching state: none recorded. Stone did not assign one.

## The player's view

Branch was frustrated and said so. He came into his second year wanting to start, was never higher than third in any edge order the staff wrote down, and "couldn't tell you what I was graded on." He said he knew his jobs, nobody told him he had made a mistake that mattered, and to him that meant the order was set before the season began. Stone said the record shows the order from Week 1 but never why Branch was placed where he was, so he could neither prove nor disprove that; either way the missing explanation is the staff's failure, not his. Pleasant said he coached Branch's technique in the room and in individual periods but never gave him a read on where he stood or what would move him.

Asked how he learns fastest, Branch said from his own reps: the loss, a correct rep beside it, and the chance to do it again straight away. He asked for two things in writing: the specific points he has to show, and his own film, the good and the bad. He also asked whether he should add weight to play the strong side. Stone said nothing in the record says his weight was a problem, that a playing-weight target is an individual plan he can work out with the performance staff if he wants one, and that it is not a coach's diagnosis.

## Future and contract

He asked whether Jacksonville still sees him as a player it is building around, and whether it will draft an edge rusher. Stone said the draft and every acquisition are Caldwell's, and he would not discuss the board. Where a player was drafted does not decide who plays; that applies to Branch and to every player in the room. No 2014 role is set, and every job is decided on 2014 evidence. He will give Caldwell his football evaluation of Branch and would not say what it is. Branch asked whether he would get a real chance in camp. Stone said no player is owed a rep share and none is shut out by one; the work goes to what the evidence supports. He made no starting, rep or roster promise.

## Development plan

Voluntary throughout. Nothing is required before April 21 or in any voluntary phase, and declining any of it costs him nothing (readiness standard section 7).

- Film, his to keep: Pleasant's cutup of every rep he played in the regular season and both playoff games, sorted as lane or contain held on the call, won and finished, won and not finished, lost, or call against the look, with that week's directive at the top, and each loss paired with a correct rep where one exists. Built this month; delivered when Caldwell's office confirms what the CBA permits.
- Written points: after the sort, Pleasant writes him the two or three edge points the film supports, tied to reps, not general advice.
- Technique: individual work on those points, in the teaching cycle he described (loss, correct rep, re-rep), when the CBA permits group instruction.
- Physical: a playing-weight and strength plan with the performance staff, only if he asks; his own training otherwise.
- Medical: today's exit physical belongs to the medical staff.
- Support: Player Development's resources remain available on confidential terms; Stone did not ask whether he uses them.
