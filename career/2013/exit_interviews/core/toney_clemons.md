# Toney Clemons, WR: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; Darryl Drake (wide receivers).
**Status going in:** WR4, fourth on the season-end receiver depth chart (Shorts, Thielen, Blackmon, Clemons, Brown); WR3 from Week 2 through Week 7; kick returner by situation. Active 53; available, no communicated restriction; exit physical with the medical staff today. Contract: signed off Pittsburgh's practice squad in late November 2012; two-year Jacksonville contract, final year 2013; exclusive-rights free agent when the 2014 league year opens on March 11 (no accrued season before 2013; 2013 accruing in the branch, 1 total). Age 25 (born October 11, 1988).

## The season in the record

He entered 2013 on the 2012 final active roster, wearing 17, with a 2012 record of 4 games and no start. The spring phase records do not name him; the camp position-battle record reads "Brown/Clemons provide depth," with five receivers retained. When Blackmon's suspension began after Week 1, the Week 2 plan kept "the same concepts with the jobs moved among Shorts, Thielen, Clemons and Brown," and Clemons was WR3 from Week 2 through Week 7. From Week 8, when Blackmon dressed as WR3 and the outside Z receiver, Clemons was WR4.

Regular season, all 16 games dressed: 41 targets, 27 receptions, 306 yards, 5 touchdowns, 11.3 yards a catch, longest 43; 1 fumble, lost. Kick returns: 8 for 180, 22.5 a return; 1 punt return for 14 (team statistics view). Postseason, both games: 2 targets, 2 receptions, 42 yards; 1 kick return for 9. No receipt injury and no weekly-award shortlist.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- He answered when the role grew. He moved into the WR3 jobs in Week 2 with the concepts unchanged and scored in three of the next five games.
- Late in the year Mesh found him, and each of those four catches came on a scoring drive, two of them in the playoffs. The Cleveland touchdown put Jacksonville ahead in a game it won in overtime.
- Returns: eight kickoffs by situation. Stone did not grade them in the meeting; the return evaluation is a special-teams follow-up (Stone until the coordinator job is filled).

Development targets:

- Ball security after the catch. The Indianapolis fumble is the only one in his record; it was coached on the Monday film and did not recur. Stone classified it as a football mistake: coached and re-checked, not punished.
- The practice record is thin. No spring phase record names him, and camp recorded him as depth. Stone told him the staff cannot state a Good, Better or Best reading for him from the written record, and would not invent one. His 2014 evaluation needs practice evidence, not only game results.
- Two short catches lost yardage (Weeks 5 and 10). Nothing written classifies them, and Stone drew no conclusion from them.

## The player's view

Clemons was direct and not hostile. "Seattle, Indy, Denver. I did what the three is supposed to do." He said he understood what Blackmon is as a player and did not argue the Week 8 decision itself, but asked what, in his own work, would have kept him at WR3. Stone answered from the record: the change was his decision to dress Blackmon at Z with a defined job set (run blocking, Smoke, Slant-Flat, backside access, a vertical alert only if earned), and nothing written says Clemons lost the job for a mistake. Stone said the call was his to own.

On coaching, Clemons credited the room for the Week 2 move: the concepts were the same and only his job inside them changed, so he did not have to learn a new offense. His complaint came after Week 8: as the fourth receiver he did not know what he was being evaluated on from week to week. The weekly plans after Week 8 set no individual focus for him, and Stone treated that as a possible teaching failure first.

He raised the fumble himself: "One. I heard about it that Monday, and it didn't happen again."

## Future and contract

Clemons asked whether Jacksonville would tender him as an exclusive-rights free agent, and whether Stone saw him on the 53 next year. Stone said the tender decision is Caldwell's, due before 4 p.m. on March 11; that he will give Caldwell his football evaluation; and that he would not say what it is. He made no roster-spot, role or depth-chart commitment: 2014 roles open on 2014 evidence. Clemons also asked about the kick-return job. Stone said returner use is a special-teams decision within the head coach's authority, and that he directs special teams himself until the coordinator job is filled. It too opens on 2014 evidence, and he promised nothing. Stone reminded him that Player Development and the confidential support resources remain available, without asking whether he uses them.

## Development plan

Voluntary; nothing is required before April 21 or in any voluntary phase, and declining any suggestion costs him nothing.

- Drake's cutup of his 2013 targets, including the Week 4 ball-security rep paired with a corrected example and the late-season Mesh catches; reviewed in Phase Two if he is under Jacksonville control.
- Drake's written evaluation of his 2013 practice and game work, so the staff's view of him does not rest on game results alone; given to him when and as the CBA permits.
- The complete active playbook, every receiver spot including Z, is his to study. Learning another spot is his choice, and no role follows from it.
- Returns: a special-teams evaluation of his kickoff and punt returns (Stone until the coordinator job is filled).
- Physical: any program is the strength and performance staff's, on request. Medical: exit physical today; any finding belongs to the medical staff.

## Follow-ups

- Drake: 2013 target cutup and a written evaluation of Clemons's practice and game work, to Stone before Stone gives Caldwell his evaluation, and to Clemons when and as the CBA permits.
- Special teams coordinator (vacant since Lowry left for Atlanta on January 12; Stone until the job is filled): an evaluation of Clemons's 2013 kickoff and punt returns, to Clemons. No return-role decision is made before 2014 evidence.
- Stone: football evaluation of Clemons to Caldwell before the ERFA tender deadline (4 p.m. ET, March 11).
- Medical staff: exit physical, January 14. No finding is recorded in this interview.
- Teaching note: the spring and camp records carry no individual practice evidence for him beyond "depth." Depth receivers' practice work needs to be recorded so their evaluation does not rest on game results alone.
- Teaching note: when a role changes because of another player's availability, as in Week 8, the displaced player should hear at the time what the change does and does not say about his own work, and what he is evaluated on in the new role.
