# Brad Meester, C: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; George Yarno (offensive line).
**Status going in:** Reserve center, seventh on the season-end line order (behind the starting five and Bradfield). Active 53; available, no communicated restriction (his Week 2 injury cleared at its September 27 projection); exit physical with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: branch re-signing, one year ($1.50M), final year 2013; pending unrestricted free agent; entered the league in 2000, with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register: Confirmed). Age 36 (born March 23, 1977).

## The season in the record

2012: 16 games, 16 starts. Re-signed in March for one year with "no starting promise"; the deal "pays for veteran communication and transition value but contains no contractual starting promise; center remains subject to football evaluation." The staff's case for him included "veteran line communication" and "potential transition value while a younger center develops."

Spring and camp record him individually. OTAs: "the line its most consistent point and combination communication"; "again stabilized the point and made fewer coach-dependent corrections"; on a faster changed-front Power sequence, "Tice and Meester reduced the call to point, echo and confirmation," and "a later different front was handled without coach rescue." Mandatory minicamp standouts: "Stabilized common calls and finished changed-front/twist work without rescue," with "Physical combinations remain for camp." Camp: "Meester continued to stabilize the line."

He started Week 1 (W 31-13 vs Kansas City) and Week 2 (L 13-17 at Oakland), where he was hurt: upper extremity, out, projected return 12 days. Brewster started Week 3. Meester cleared September 27 and dressed as reserve center from Week 4: "a continuity decision, not a demotion, to be revisited on Monday"; Week 5, "now as an execution decision: one sack combined in two starts"; Week 6, Brewster confirmed. Stone said publicly in each of those weeks that Meester was ready. The bye locked him as reserve center, and Stone told Caldwell before the trade deadline that there was no need at center.

He dressed for 15 regular-season games (all but Week 3; bye Week 9) and both playoff games. Sacks allowed charged to him: 5 regular season (Weeks 1, 6, 7, 11, 16) and 1 postseason (Divisional). The 2013 engine charged each sack allowed to a randomly drawn dressed lineman (`runtime/defect_register.md`), so these are not evidence; after Week 2 the record does not show whether or when he entered a game. The Week 1 line that reads his charge as "the same physical and leverage losses as the preseason" rests on the charge and is not used. No weekly-award shortlist, no in-season honour.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- Communication. Point declaration, fewer coach-dependent corrections, the point-echo-confirmation reduction built with Tice, and changed-front and twist work finished without rescue.
- Readiness after clearance: ready as reserve every week ("Brad dresses and he's one snap from playing").

Development targets:

- Physical combinations: spring left them for padded work, and camp recorded only that he continued to stabilize the line. No individual physical finding exists, and Stone did not invent one.
- Stone told him the center job moved on Brewster's execution while Meester was injured, not on a finding against Meester.

Teaching state: for the spring phases, the communication record fits the framework's Best description (self-corrects routine problems, helps the man next to him without losing his own job). That state covers communication only: the technique half was left for padded work, and camp gave no individual finding on it. No in-season state can be given after Week 2; the record holds none.

## The player's view

Meester was direct. He knew the deal carried no starting promise and did not claim otherwise. His objection was the recorded reason: "After Seattle you said zero sacks was the whole line, not just Mike. Then in Week 5 the reason was one sack in two starts. That's the line's number too." The job was settled on "Three games is enough evidence," and he was hurt for the first of those games and the reserve for the other two.

Stone said the point was fair. The Week 5 reason as written is a unit number; the decision was to keep an operation that was working, and there is no finding against Meester to dress it up with. Stone accepted that the record should have said so.

On coaching, Meester said the spring method worked, point, echo and confirmation above all, and it is what he would teach any center. After September, he said, nobody asked what he saw from the sideline; the bye council did not include the reserve center. Stone asked him then. Meester said he would rather give it on the tape with Yarno than from memory.

## Future and contract

Meester said he has not decided whether he will play in 2014 and will not decide this week. He asked whether, if he does, there is a place for him in Jacksonville, and whether the club is looking for a center. Stone told him contracts and acquisitions are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is or what he will recommend. On role, Stone repeated March: no job is promised, and a 2014 role is earned on 2014 evidence.
