# Mike Harris, CB: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; Tony Oden (defensive backs).
**Status going in:** Starting outside cornerback opposite Brent Grimes from Week 11 (for the injured Alan Ball) through the Divisional game; a reserve corner before that. Dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games. Available, no communicated restriction; his exit physical is with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: 2012 Jacksonville sixth-round four-year rookie contract, final year 2015; under contract; 1 accrued season, with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register). Age 25 (born January 5, 1989).

## The season in the record

2012, his rookie year, when Oden also coached the secondary: 15 games, 6 starts. Stone's early-2013 roster evaluation listed him under "Defensive core/development." The spring records do not name him. Camp: comparable corner work (July 26-29), then "Grimes and Ball handled the leading outside work, with Harris in the next band" (July 31-August 2) and "Harris followed" (August 5-7). Preseason Game 1 vs Miami (W 33-17): with Anderson, Bouye and Prosinski, he "held punt-coverage lanes and confirmed substitutions."

Regular season, 16 games: 41 tackles (24 solo, 17 assisted), 1 sack, 1 tackle for loss, 1 pressure, 7 passes defended; 7 punt returns for 74 yards and 2 kickoff returns for 46 (120 return yards). Postseason, 2 games: 9 tackles (7 solo, 2 assisted), 2 passes defended.

- Weeks 1 to 10, reserve: tackles in Weeks 1, 3, 4 and 7; a pass defended in Week 3.
- Week 11 vs Arizona (W 29-7), first start. Stone that week: "Mike Harris outside. Jordan stays at nickel. One new job, not two." Directive to Crennel: "identify Fitzgerald every snap without leaving Harris alone." A sack and 3 tackles; held up: "Harris in Ball's spot."
- Week 12 at Houston (L 6-38): directive "know Andre Johnson's location without leaving Harris isolated." 5 tackles, 1 pass defended. Correction: "Andre Johnson (8-134, 3 touchdowns)." Stone afterwards, asked about Johnson's three touchdowns: "I'm not putting that on one player. We'll look at the calls, the leverage and the help, and we play them again in eleven days." Crennel's rematch note: alter first "where the help goes against Johnson."
- Week 13 at Cleveland (W 22-19, overtime): Bouye cleared; Stone: "He's our first corner off the bench. Mike Harris earned the start." 8 tackles, 3 passes defended.
- Week 14 vs Houston (W 21-20): directive "change the help location on Andre Johnson without abandoning Hopkins"; Johnson caught 6 for 67 and a touchdown ("Johnson still caught a touchdown"). 6 tackles, 1 pass defended.
- Weeks 15 to 17: 5 tackles, 1 for loss and 1 pass defended vs Buffalo (L 16-45); 5 vs Tennessee (W 38-27); 4 at Indianapolis (L 22-23).
- Wild Card at Kansas City (W 38-14): 5 tackles. Divisional at Tennessee (L 13-20): 4 tackles, 2 passes defended; held up, "Britt contained: 8 catches for 58, longest 19."

No directive names him after Week 12. Unit corrections in his starts, none naming him: Buffalo, "Manuel's four touchdown passes, two of them to Graham"; Tennessee, "Britt: 15 catches for 187" and "The 50-yard touchdown to Greene"; Indianapolis, "Late pass defense"; Wild Card, "Underneath pass defense." No weekly-award shortlist, in-season honour or receipt injury.

Evidence caveat: the receipts record no coverage responsibility; sacks and passes defended were credited by role share (`runtime/play_detail.py`; defect register item 19), and 2013 returners were drawn afresh on every kick, so his returns are not evaluated.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- He took an outside job mid-season on a week's preparation and kept it. "Harris in Ball's spot" held up in his first start; when Bouye was cleared, Stone kept Harris in the job on the evidence then, and he started through both playoff games.
- Available every week: dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games.

Development targets:

- Houston away first. The losses in his starts are written to the unit; his reps in Week 12, Week 14 and the Buffalo, Tennessee, Indianapolis and Wild Card corrections are classified by the state/05 categories before anything is assigned to him.
- What the help plan asked of him. Weeks 11 and 12 were built around not leaving him alone and no later directive names him; the record does not say why, so Stone read it as a finding neither way.
- Camp placed him in "the next band"; that is a comparison, not a finding. No record names a technique fault of his.

Teaching state: the record holds no individual phase evidence for him, so Stone gave none.

## The player's view

Harris was matter-of-fact and pressed one point. Camp was fair, he said: "Alan won it. I knew where I stood." He knew Arizona and Houston were built to give him help and did not mind: "First time starting outside for this staff. I'd have done the same." What bothered him was afterwards: "At some point it stopped being about me, and nobody said whether that's because I earned it or because Romeo needed the help somewhere else." Stone treated it as a possible teaching and communication failure first. Oden said whatever the plan asked of him each week should have been said to him in the room, in words, and that was his to fix.

On Houston he said, "Johnson had three and I was the new guy. I heard what you said after. I want to know if it was me." Stone said the record does not say, the cutup will, and if it was the call, it goes on the staff. On coaching, he said a second year with Oden meant "nobody had to start over with me."

## Future and contract

He asked whether the outside job is his in 2014, since Stone had said publicly he earned the start. Stone said he did earn it, on the evidence then; that is a fact about 2013, not a promise for September, and no 2014 role is promised to anyone. Harris asked whether the club would bring in corners. Stone said acquisitions and contracts are Caldwell's, and he would not discuss other players' contracts. Harris did not raise his own contract.

All voluntary. Jacksonville's 2014 offseason program may begin no earlier than April 21; declining any voluntary work costs nothing.

Priority: Week 12, Week 14, Buffalo, Tennessee in Week 16, Indianapolis, the Wild Card; Week 11 and the Divisional as reference. By January 31, 2014. No quiz.

- Physical: his own routine; performance-staff input on request within CBA limits.
