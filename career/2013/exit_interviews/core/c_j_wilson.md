# C.J. Wilson, DE: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; Anthony Pleasant (defensive line).
**Status going in:** Defensive end with no current role (the roster lists none; the edge order since Week 3 is Babin, Mincey, Branch, Davis). Active 53, kept on the active roster all season while injured; no reserve-list move was made. Not yet cleared: Week 2 trunk injury, projected return January 30, 2014; his rehabilitation is run by the medical and training staff, and his exit physical is with the medical staff today, with no finding recorded here. Contract: inherited 2010 Green Bay seventh-round four-year rookie contract, acquired by branch trade, final year 2013; pending unrestricted free agent; 3 accrued seasons (2010-2012 with Green Bay), with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register). Age 26 (born March 30, 1987).

## The season in the record

Acquired before the April 25 draft from Green Bay for Blaine Gabbert, player for player. "Caldwell and Stone treated Wilson as comparable defensive-front depth rather than as a guaranteed starter"; the trade record adds, "Wilson receives no promised starting job or package role." By the opening of the OTA block, every controlled veteran, Wilson included, had received the club's welcome process: standards package, support information, the complete active playbook, and follow-up contacts from Stone and the position coach. Mandatory minicamp: "All controlled players attended and completed assigned work." Camp, July 26-29: "Branch and C.J. Wilson remained in the edge/strong-side rotation." The preseason game records and the camp closeout do not name him.

- Week 1 vs Kansas City (W 31-13): dressed; edge "Babin; Mincey, Wilson, Branch rotating."
- Week 2 at Oakland (L 13-17): dressed; "defensive end C.J. Wilson (trunk, out, projected return 137 days)." The receipt classes it long-term. The record does not say when in the game it happened.
- Statistics: 2 games dressed; the receipts record no statistic for him. Postseason: none (inactive).

After Week 2: "C.J. Wilson's absence changes edge rotation depth," and "Whether Wilson moves to injured reserve is a Caldwell and medical matter; no move has been made." He was inactive from Week 3 through the Divisional game, and every later weekly table carries him only as "C.J. Wilson out," some with his projection. No weekly-award shortlist, no in-season honour.

## Stone's evaluation

Stone told him the record is thin and that he would not fill it in.

- Recorded: he held an edge/strong-side rotation job through the first physical camp block and was in the Week 1 edge rotation. That is the whole of his individual football evidence.
- Not recorded: any individual rep, correction or finding, good or bad, and any 2013 statistic. Stone gave no development target he could not tie to a rep.
- The injury is a medical status, not an effort, toughness or character finding. Diagnosis, restrictions and clearance belong to the medical staff; Stone did not comment on his recovery.

Teaching state: none can be given; the record holds no individual phase evidence.

## The player's view

Wilson was guarded. He said he came in a trade he did not ask for, learned a new book in the spring, "got Kansas City and part of Oakland," and then "the only thing anybody wrote about me after September was 'out.'" He said medical detail stays between him and the doctors; Stone said that is where it belongs and did not ask.

On coaching, he said camp was fair: he knew his job in the edge rotation and got the reps to show it. What did not work was the rest of the season: once he was hurt, he said, the football side and his rehabilitation ran separately, and nobody gave him a plan for staying in the defense within what the medical staff allowed. Stone said the record shows no such plan either way, and treated that as a staff planning failure, not a question of Wilson's commitment. Pleasant said the room should have had one.

## Future and contract

He asked three things. First, whether Jacksonville wants him back. Stone said contracts are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is or what he will recommend. Second, whether his rehabilitation can continue at the facility after his contract year ends. Stone said he did not know the answer and would not guess; it belongs to the medical staff and Caldwell's office, and he would ask them to answer Wilson directly before the league year opens on March 11. Third, whether the injury would be held against him. Stone said that on the football side an injury is not a finding against anyone, that clearance is the medical staff's, and that he would not speak for how any contract decision weighs availability, because that is Caldwell's.

## Development plan

Medical first; everything else voluntary.

- Medical: rehabilitation continues under the medical and training staff. January 30 is their projection; clearance is theirs, and Stone stays out of it.
- Film, his to keep: Pleasant's cutup of his camp edge and strong-side work and his Week 1 and Week 2 reps, with corrected reps where they exist. What can be sent to a pending free agent, and when, is for Caldwell's office to confirm.
- Football study: only within what the medical staff permits. The complete playbook remains his while he is under a Jacksonville contract.
- Offseason program: Jacksonville's 2014 program (no earlier than April 21) applies only if he is under a Jacksonville contract when it opens; nothing here assumes either way.
- Support: Stone reminded him that Player Development and confidential support go on no report, without asking whether he uses them.
