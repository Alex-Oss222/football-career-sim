# Tyson Alualu, DT: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; Anthony Pleasant (defensive line).
**Status going in:** Rotating defensive tackle behind the Miller/Marks starting pair (roster role "DT rotation"). Active 53; available, no communicated restriction; exit physical with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: 2010 first-round five-year rookie contract, final year 2014; under contract; 3 accrued seasons through 2012, with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register: Confirmed). Age 26 (born May 12, 1987).

## The season in the record

2012: 16 games, 16 starts. Stone's early-2013 roster evaluation listed him under "Defensive core/development." The spring and preseason game records do not name him individually. In camp's first physical block (July 26-29), "the defense's physical work showed Miller and Marks as the most stable interior run combination, with Alualu rotating across the front." At the August 3 scrimmage "Miller/Marks held the interior," and at camp close "Crennel retained Miller/Marks inside." The weekly defensive tables for Weeks 1 to 8 read "Miller, Marks; Alualu rotating"; Mosley joined the interior rotation in Week 11.

He dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games.

- Regular season: 24 tackles (18 solo, 6 assisted), 3 sacks, 3 tackles for loss, 7 pressures, 2 passes defended.
- Postseason: 4 tackles (2 solo, 2 assisted), 1 tackle for loss, 1 pass defended.

Recorded moments:

- Week 6 at Denver (W 26-10): a tackle for loss; the interior row reads "Rush finished."
- Week 7 vs San Diego (W 30-24): a sack; interior row "Run stopped"; the run defense allowed 34 yards.
- Week 11 vs Arizona (W 29-7): two sacks, listed among the standout performances; "run fits (76 rushing yards allowed)" held up.
- Week 14 vs Houston (W 21-20): two tackles for loss. Trailing 20-14, "Tyson Alualu, Daryl Smith and Brent Grimes stopped three straight Houston runs for losses"; the punt that followed gave Jacksonville the ball at the Houston 40, and Shorts's touchdown and Scobee's extra point made it 21-20. "The late three-and-out that set up the winning drive" is on the held-up list.
- Wild Card at Kansas City (W 38-14): a tackle for loss and a pass defended; 47 rushing yards allowed.
- Weeks 1, 2, 8 and 12: no statistic recorded for him.

Unit run-defense corrections in games he dressed for, none naming an individual: Week 10 (210 rushing yards allowed), Week 12 ("Foster's 29 carries"), Week 13 ("Richardson's 129 yards"), Week 15 ("237 rushing yards and 7.2 a carry"), the Divisional (Johnson 24 carries for 103). No weekly-award shortlist, in-season honour or receipt injury. No snap count is recorded, so no total is read as a rate.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- Finishing when the play came to him: the Week 7 sack, the two-sack Arizona game, and three tackles for loss, two of them in the Week 14 win over Houston, where he was one of the three players who stopped three straight Houston runs for losses.
- Carrying a job across the front. Camp recorded him "rotating across the front," and he held that job, available, every week.

Development targets:

- The starting order was a comparison, not a finding against him. Camp named Miller and Marks the most stable run combination; no record names a lost fit, a missed assignment or a technique fault of his. Stone told him that plainly and did not invent one.
- His individual evidence is missing: none of his reps is classified in any record. A blank line in a rotation game is not read as a flaw, and his reps inside the corrections of Weeks 10, 12, 13 and 15 and the Divisional are classified by the state/05 categories before anything is assigned to him.

Teaching state: the record gives no individual phase state, and Stone did not build one from box-score lines.

## The player's view

Alualu was direct. He accepted the camp call: "Roy and Sen'Derrick played the run the way Romeo wanted it that week. I'm not arguing July." His objection was that the order never moved after July, "and I had Arizona and I had that Houston series." He asked what, specifically, would have changed it in 2013. Stone answered from the record: it gives no reason for the order beyond the camp finding and the early weekly tables, which record the run defense holding in Weeks 1, 4, 7 and 8 (unit results, not the pair's alone); it records no problem found in Alualu; and nobody had told him what evidence would move him. Stone recorded the last part as the staff's failure.

On coaching, he said rotating across the front kept him on the field but split his individual work between alignments, and he never heard which one the staff was actually evaluating him at. He also said the Monday corrections were "the unit's number," not his reps. Pleasant did not dispute either point.

## Future and contract

He asked whether the club intends to extend him before his final contract year. Stone said contracts are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is or what he will recommend. Alualu then asked whether an interior starting job would be his if it opened. Stone said no job is promised to anyone, a 2014 role is earned on 2014 evidence, and he would not discuss another player's contract. Alualu: "Then put Arizona and Houston in front of him." Stone said the evaluation is built from the whole record.
