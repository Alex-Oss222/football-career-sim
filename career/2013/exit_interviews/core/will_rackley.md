# Will Rackley, G: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; George Yarno (offensive line).
**Status going in:** Starting right guard, won in camp and confirmed on the right side August 24, 2013. Active 53. Limited, no projected absence: a minor upper-extremity injury from Week 5, carried as limited through the Divisional game. Exit physical with the medical staff today; diagnosis, restrictions and clearance are theirs. Contract: under contract; 2011 third-round four-year rookie contract, final year 2014; 2 accrued seasons before 2013 (2012 on Reserve/Injured, full pay), with 2013 accruing in the branch. Age 24 (born October 11, 1989).

## The season in the record

He was on reserve/injured at the 2012 season close, wearing 65; no 2012 role is tabulated.

Spring. OTAs, June 4-5: "Rackley and Nwaneri were late once transferring the interior combination, but the line corrected without a coach supplying the second answer." Mandatory minicamp: "Rackley and Pasztor remained a genuine right-guard competition because neither separated across communication and technique."

Camp and preseason. First padded work: "Rackley communicated the combinations more consistently while Pasztor had the better isolated recovery after losing initial leverage; neither owned the job." Then: "Rackley earned the first right-guard work for the next block because his communication held late in practice." After the August 3 scrimmage: "Rackley stayed first at right guard but Pasztor's best rush pickup kept the contest live." Preseason Game 1 against Miami (August 9, won 33-17): he identified his protection job, lost leverage physically and continued the rep. Camp standouts, with Johnson: "Best cumulative first-unit communication; occasional leverage losses remained technique work, not effort findings." Position battle: "Rackley held the first-unit operation; Pasztor was medically interrupted."

Season. Right guard in every game: 16 regular-season games and both postseason games dressed, the line locked at the bye, "no reshuffle" for the Wild Card and "normal jobs" in the Divisional plan. At the bye Pasztor worked in his first practice since his availability was restored; the week file records that he was not moved on the depth chart and that his Week 10 role would come from that evidence and Week 10 practice. In Week 10 "Rackley stayed the starting right guard."

Receipt injuries: Week 4 against Indianapolis, minor trunk, limited, no projected absence; Week 5 at St. Louis, minor upper extremity, limited, no projected absence. Every week's availability record from Week 5 through the Divisional game lists him "limited (minor)." The record does not say what the limitation changed in his practice week.

His individual receipt line: 6 sacks allowed (Week 4 against Indianapolis, 1; Week 6 at Denver, 1; Week 8 against San Francisco at Wembley, 1; Week 13 at Cleveland, 2; Week 14 against Houston, 1), none in the postseason. The 2013 charge was drawn at random across dressed linemen (defect register) and is not used as evidence. No weekly-award shortlist, no in-season honour.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- Communication. It is what won him the job: the more consistent combination calls in the first pads, communication that "held late in practice," and the best cumulative first-unit communication in camp. That meets Good, and points to the communication part of Better.
- Availability: he dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both postseason games. He carried a limited listing from Week 4 on (minor trunk in Week 4, minor upper extremity from Week 5); Stone said that is a medical fact and not a toughness or effort finding in either direction.

Development targets:

- Recovery after losing initial leverage. Camp gave Pasztor the better isolated recovery, Miami recorded a leverage loss, and the standouts entry keeps "occasional leverage losses" as technique work. That is the recorded target, and it is the technique part of Better that the record does not yet show.
- The in-season record is thin: line listings, charged sacks and availability. Stone said his evaluation stops there until Yarno's sort exists, and that the sort will not read any rep as caused by the limitation without the medical staff.

Teaching state: Good, with the communication part of Better supported; the technique part of Better (reproducing technique through repeated reps) is not yet shown.

## The player's view

Rackley said dressing for every game after a 2012 spent on Reserve/Injured mattered to him. He left the injury there: he would talk to the trainers about it. He asked for one thing, that nobody watching his film decide what the limitation did or did not do. Stone agreed; that is medical, and it stays with the medical staff.

He said the camp competition was run straight: "I knew it was calls and recovery, and some days Austin recovered better than me." He objected to two things. First, the six charged sacks: they are public, and going into the last year of his contract, "people read the number." Stone said the charge sheet is not reliable about whose sack it was, that he cannot change what was published, and that neither his evaluation nor Yarno's uses it. Second, at the bye, when Pasztor was cleared, nobody told him where the job stood; he learned he was still the starter from the Week 10 depth chart. The bye file records that Pasztor was not moved on the depth chart and that his Week 10 role would come from the bye practice and Week 10 practice; nothing records anyone telling Rackley where the job stood. Stone treated it as a staff communication failure, and said any change in his role will come to him from Stone or Yarno directly, not from a depth chart.

## Future and contract

He asked whether there had been any talk of an extension, and whether right guard is his in 2014. Stone said contracts are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and would not speak for Caldwell or say what it contains. On the job: he won it on 2013 evidence, "A starting job is not lifetime tenure," and 2014 roles are decided by 2014 evidence, for him and for anyone who competes with him. No extension, role or roster commitment was made. Rackley said he understood.

Stone reminded him that Player Development's resources, including financial education, stay available on the same confidential terms, without asking whether he uses them.

## Development plan

Voluntary throughout; nothing is required before April 21 or in any voluntary phase, and declining any suggestion costs him nothing. Medical comes first.

- Medical: exit physical today. Any rehabilitation is run by the medical and training staff, and the work below proceeds only within what they clear.
- Film: Yarno's sort of his season, the six charged sacks in with everything else, nothing in his column by default, medical limitation kept as its own category and left to the medical staff, and a correct rep beside each loss where one exists; his camp recovery reps are added for the technique target. Delivered and discussed when and as the CBA permits.
- Technique, his choice of method and within clearance: recovery after losing initial leverage.
- If he takes part once the program opens (no earlier than April 21): individual work on what the sort shows.
- Physical: the strength and performance staff's input on request, coordinated with the medical staff.
