# Alan Ball, CB: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; Tony Oden (defensive backs).
**Status going in:** Starting outside cornerback with Brent Grimes until his Week 10 injury; inactive from Week 11 through the Divisional game, kept on the active 53 with no reserve-list move. Not yet cleared: Week 10 injury (trunk, long-term), projected return January 22, 2014. His rehabilitation is run by the medical and training staff; his exit physical is with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: branch signing, one year ($1.00M), final year 2013; pending unrestricted free agent; 4+ accrued seasons (entered 2007; signed from outside as a free agent in March 2013), with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register). Age 28 (born March 29, 1985).

## The season in the record

Signed March 12 on a one-year contract (the free-agency board's tier: "value / competition signing"); the contract "promises an opportunity to compete across secondary and special-teams roles, not a starting job or CB1 status." The spring records do not name him. Camp: after Grimes's return to full work, "Ball, Harris, Poyer and the other corners continued comparable role-specific work" (July 26-29), then "Grimes and Ball handled the leading outside work, with Harris in the next band" (July 31-August 2), and "Grimes/Ball outside" at camp close. Preseason Game 4 at Atlanta (W 33-27): with Thielen, Trawick and Allen, he "executed punt-coverage communication and pursuit."

Regular season, 9 games: 41 tackles (23 solo, 18 assisted), 1 tackle for loss, 9 passes defended, 1 interception for 8 yards; 4 kickoff returns for 88 yards. Postseason: none (inactive).

- Week 1 vs Kansas City (W 31-13): intercepted Alex Smith on a pass for Cyrus Gray; the 16-snap drive that followed scored. Two passes defended; standout list; corner row "Ball the week's cover player."
- Week 3 at Seattle (W 16-13): 6 tackles, 1 for loss, 2 passes defended.
- Weeks 5 to 8, the Grimes/Ball corner row: "Givens contained" (8 catches for 20), "Outside receivers held" ("Denver receivers 87 yards combined"), "Floyd contained" (6 for 30), "Boldin held short" (7 for 26). The rows do not split the two corners.
- Week 10 at Tennessee (L 11-41): 5 tackles; hurt, "trunk, long-term; out, projected 73 days, to January 22, 2014."
- Weeks 2, 4, 5, 6: 3, 5, 5 and 5 tackles, a pass defended in each; Week 7: 3 tackles; Week 8: 6 tackles, 1 pass defended.

Unit corrections in his games, none naming him: Week 3, Seattle's "last possession produced two completions of more than 20 yards" (Rice 44, Kearse 24); Week 5, "Cook catching 102 yards"; Week 7, "The 80-yard touchdown to Mathews." From Week 11 through the Divisional game he was inactive, and the weekly tables carry him only as "Ball out." No weekly-award shortlist or in-season honour.

Evidence caveat: the receipts record no coverage responsibility; interceptions and passes defended were credited by role share, with a pass defended on every interception (`runtime/play_detail.py`; defect register item 19), and 2013 returners were drawn afresh on every kick, so his returns are not evaluated.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- He won the job the contract only let him compete for, held it from the second camp block through camp close, and started every game until he was hurt.
- The Week 1 interception that became a touchdown drive; the Seattle game in a three-point road win; and four straight weeks (5 to 8) of positive reads for the outside pair.
- Teams portability, which the contract named: preseason Game 4.

Development targets:

- None the record can name. No record assigns him a coverage loss, missed tackle or technique fault, and Stone did not invent one.
- His reps inside the unit corrections (Seattle's last possession, Cook, the Mathews touchdown) are classified by the state/05 categories before anything is assigned to him.

Teaching state: the record holds a camp comparison and pair-level reads, not an individual Good/Better/Best state, and Stone did not build one from a stat line. The injury is a medical status, not an effort or toughness finding.

## The player's view

Ball was businesslike. The March promise was kept, he said: "You said competition. It was. Nobody handed Brent anything and nobody handed me anything." He called the first nine weeks the best football he had played in a while and wanted one answer: "Rice for 44, Kearse for 24, then they punt. I want to know if either one was mine."

What did not work: once he was hurt he became a line on the inactive list, and nobody laid out how he could stay in the defense within what the medical staff allowed. Stone said the record shows no such plan and called it a staff planning failure, not a question of Ball's commitment. Oden agreed. Ball said medical detail stays with the doctors; Stone did not ask.

## Future and contract

He asked whether Jacksonville wants him back. Stone said contracts are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is or what he will recommend. Ball asked to hear the answer from Caldwell's office, not through his agent; Stone said he would pass the request on and that how and when the club communicates is Caldwell's. Then he asked whether the job is his if he returns. Stone said Mike Harris started from Week 11 on the evidence then, that no 2014 role is promised to anyone, and that he would not discuss another player's contract. Ball: "Then I'm competing again. Fine."

## Development plan

Medical first; everything else voluntary.

- Medical: rehabilitation continues under the medical and training staff; January 22 is their projection, and clearance is theirs.
- Film, his to keep: Oden's cutup of his nine games from Crennel's call sheets, each snap where the ball went to a receiver in his responsibility tagged with the call, the help, his leverage, technique and result. Priority: Week 3's last possession, Weeks 5 and 7; keep reps from Weeks 1 and 5 to 8. What can be sent to a pending free agent, and when, is for Caldwell's office to confirm.
- Football study only within what the medical staff permits. Jacksonville's 2014 program (no earlier than April 21) applies only if he is under a Jacksonville contract then.
- Support: Stone reminded him that Player Development and confidential support go on no report, without asking whether he uses them.
