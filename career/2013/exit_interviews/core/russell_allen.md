# Russell Allen, LB: exit interview

**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone. Frank Bush (linebackers) was at his Indianapolis interview that day (ledger Entry 75).
**Status going in:** Base linebacker beside Daryl Smith since Week 14, in place of the injured Paul Posluszny; first linebacker off the bench before that; a defined coverage-unit job all season (roster role "Base LB (from Week 14, for Posluszny); coverage units"). Active 53; available, no communicated restriction; exit physical with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: 2012 three-year contract, final year 2014; under contract; 4 accrued seasons (2009-2012), with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register: Supported). Age 27 (born May 5, 1986).

## The season in the record

He entered 2013 as No. 50, an outside linebacker with a 2012 record of 16 games and 16 starts. Crennel kept Posluszny and Smith as the base linebackers through camp. Allen's camp evidence is on special teams: the standouts record lists him with Trawick, Rambo, Poyer, Bouye and Prosinski for "Repeated communicated coverage-lane execution and finish," and Lowry kept him in "defined core/backup coverage responsibilities." At Atlanta in the preseason, "Thielen, Trawick, Allen and Ball executed punt-coverage communication and pursuit."

Outside Week 4 he was the first linebacker off the bench through Week 13, and he held his coverage-unit job all year. In Week 4, with Posluszny out, he and Smith were the base pair ("Russell Allen takes the bigger role, and Daryl Smith runs the communication"), and the record reads "Without Posluszny the linebacker operation held"; Indianapolis ran for 65 yards. After Posluszny's Week 13 injury he started Weeks 14 to 17 and both playoff games ("Russell Allen starts. Daryl makes the calls. Russell plays his job, not Paul's."). Stone's Week 15 decision kept him there: "Correctly done. Keep it."

He dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games. No receipt injury, weekly-award shortlist or in-season honour.

- Postseason: 18 tackles (12 solo, 6 assisted), 1 tackle for loss, 1 pressure, 1 interception returned 37 yards, 1 pass defended.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- Ready when the job moved. His role changed on a week's notice twice, in Week 4 and Week 14. Week 4's record says the linebacker operation held, and Week 14's lists "Smith and Allen in the reshaped base defense" as held up. Stone repeated his own Week 15 note to him: correctly done.
- Finishing the plays that came to him: the Arizona interception that became three points before the half, the Wild Card interception that set up the first touchdown, and the Week 13 sack on Cleveland's first series.
- Coverage units. Camp recorded repeated, communicated lane execution and finish. By the framework's definitions, reproducing a communicated job through repeated reps is Better. Stone said that is the one teaching state the record supports for him, and only for that job in that phase.

Development targets:

- Run fits in his starts. By unit result they held in Weeks 14, 16 and 17 and the Wild Card, and were corrected in Week 15 and the Divisional. Neither correction has been classified by cause, so nothing in them is assigned to him until the review says whose fit broke and why (assignment, communication, technique, physical loss, processing, or the call).
- Linebacker evidence is otherwise unit reads and tackle totals. Stone did not build a linebacker teaching state from box-score lines and said so.

## The player's view

Allen was plain about the camp decision: "Paul and Daryl were the guys. I didn't love it after starting every game in 2012, but I understood it." He said the Week 14 message helped him more than any install that week: "Play your job, not Paul's. That was the right thing to tell me."

His objection was the Buffalo correction. "Run defense without Posluszny. I was the without." He said the line reads like the 237 yards belong to the man who replaced Posluszny, and he wants the tape to say which fits broke and whose they were. Stone did not dispute that the review had not been done fit by fit, agreed the wording named an absence instead of a cause, and recorded it as a teaching failure.

He also asked who takes the defensive calls if Smith goes down. "I'm one injury from being that guy and nobody's said it." Stone told him the record does not answer it; Crennel owes a proposal on how the communication is taught and to whom before the 2014 program starts.

What he needs: the fit-by-fit tape, and to know whether he stays on the coverage units if he is playing base. "Don't take teams away from me because I start."

## Future and contract

Allen asked whether the club intends to extend him before his final contract year. Stone said contracts are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is or what he will recommend.

He then asked whether the base job is his when Posluszny returns. Stone said no base job is promised to anyone in the room, him included, and 2014 roles are earned on 2014 evidence. He would not discuss another player's medical situation. Allen: "So I'm competing for it." Stone: everyone is. On special teams, Stone said the coverage jobs are set from 2014 evidence and workload by the special teams coordinator and him; with the coordinator job vacant since Lowry left for Atlanta on January 12, they are Stone's until it is filled. He made no promise either way.
