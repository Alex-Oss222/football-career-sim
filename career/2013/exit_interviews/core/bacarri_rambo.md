## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- Retention. Each correction the spring record gives him (the tackling angle, the substitution call, the exchange call) is recorded as held at the next test. That is what the program asks of a rookie.
- He earned a job nobody gave him: "no depth position set" in May, "without an established starting award" in camp, then the starting job beside Lowery, held all season.
- Special-teams communication: with Trawick the clearest multi-unit communicator in camp, and the coverage-lane work in the preseason.
- The goal-line interception, as a rep to keep once Oden's tape shows it came from the right spot in the call.

Development targets:

- Tackling angle. The one individual technique item on record, corrected in non-live work in May; the record holds no individual live tackling evaluation of him since. "Tackling is part of the scheme," so Oden's cutup sorts his 2013 tackle attempts by angle and leverage.
- The weekly help rule and the unit items above, classified before any of it is assigned to him.

Teaching state: Good at rookie minicamp is his only recorded state. The later records describe corrections retained and communication against changed pictures, which is how the framework describes Better, but no record assigns that state and Stone did not assign it after the fact.

## The player's view

Rambo was quieter than most and specific when asked. He said the season went faster than he expected and that he never felt the job was his: "Every week I figured I was one bad day from somebody else playing." Stone said that is an accurate description of how jobs work here; nothing was awarded in camp or since. Rambo said he preferred knowing it.

What worked, he said, was the retest: "You tell me once, I get it wrong, you walk it, I get it right the next day. That's how I learned all of it." What did not: in season he heard the pair's row and his tackle count, not what he had done right or wrong. And the weekly help rule was the hardest thing to carry from the meeting to the field; he said he leaned on Lowery for it. Stone treated both as teaching problems before anything else. Oden said the second matches what Lowery raised about the directives asking for two things at once.

He asked to keep his coverage jobs. Stone told him Alan Lowry left on January 12 to become Atlanta's head coach and the special teams coordinator job is open; until it is filled, unit jobs are Stone's call, and they are earned on 2014 work, like everything else.

## Future and contract

He asked whether he starts in 2014 and whether the club will draft a safety. Stone said the draft and signings are Caldwell's and he would not discuss them; no 2014 role is promised to anyone; in 2013 he was not promised one either and earned first-group work on the evidence, and the spring starts the same way. He did not raise his contract; he is under contract through 2016.

## Development plan

All voluntary. Jacksonville's 2014 offseason program may begin no earlier than April 21, and declining any voluntary work costs nothing.

- Film, his to keep: Oden's cutup of his 18 games from Crennel's call sheets, each snap tagged with the call, his help or fit assignment and the result, classified. Priority: his tackle attempts sorted by angle and leverage; then the unit items; keep reps: the Wild Card interception and the Week 7 and Week 8 tackles for loss.
- Help rules: once Crennel's written help account is done, Oden gives him a short teaching sheet, each 2013 help rule by opponent, what it asked of the second safety and which part came first, followed by a walkthrough when the program permits (framework section 2).
- Physical: his first NFL offseason. Before April 21 his training is his own, with performance-staff input on request within CBA limits; Phase One strength and conditioning from April 21 at the earliest. The record holds no physical finding for him, and Stone set no target.
- Special teams: coverage-unit film with the special teams coordinator (vacant since Lowry left for Atlanta on January 12; Stone until the job is filled) when the calendar permits.
- Support: Stone reminded him that Player Development and confidential support go on no report, without asking whether he uses them.
