**Date:** Tuesday January 14, 2014. **Present:** Alex Stone; George Yarno (offensive line).
**Status going in:** Starting center (confirmed Week 6); the season-end line order is Monroe, Nwaneri, Brewster, Rackley, Johnson, then Bradfield, Meester, Pasztor, Asper. Active 53; available, no communicated restriction; exit physical with the medical staff today, and no finding from it is recorded here. Contract: 2012 Jacksonville undrafted contract, three years, final year 2014; under contract; one accrued season before 2013 (2012: 12 games, 7 starts, then Reserve/Injured), with 2013 accruing in the branch (contract status register). Age 24 (born July 27, 1989).

## The season in the record

Inherited on the 2012 roster: 12 games, 7 starts, reserve/injured at season close. No OTA, minicamp or camp record names him individually. Preseason Game 2 at the Jets (August 17, lost 24-17): he "identified the protection assignment but lost leverage on the one sack; the effort evidence shows recovery after the loss."

Inactive in Weeks 1 and 2. With Meester hurt at Oakland, Stone started him at Seattle as "the center we have healthy." Monday's film covered "every Meester communication duty Brewster now inherited"; Tuesday and Wednesday were "center week for Brewster: front identification, the MIKE point, protection calls, exchanges, twist pickup, cadence and road communication." Seattle, which "had seven sacks in two games," had none. Stone kept him at center in Week 4 as "a continuity decision," in Week 5 "as an execution decision," and confirmed him in Week 6: "Three games is enough evidence." The bye review listed "Brewster at center" under Keep and locked the line. Week 4 practice had him on odd-front communication; the Thursday before Week 10 ran a pressure period in which he and Cousins "had to identify blocked, hot or kill."

He dressed for 14 regular-season games (Weeks 3 to 17; bye Week 9) and both playoff games. The receipts hold one individual line, sacks allowed: 4 regular season, 1 postseason. The 2013 engine charged each sack allowed to a randomly drawn dressed lineman (`runtime/defect_register.md`), so these charges are not used as evidence below. ## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- He took over the whole center job in one week and the operation held. Stone's caution from Seattle stands ("Zero sacks is the line and everyone on it, not just him"); the individual evidence is Stone's sequence of decisions: continuity, then execution, confirmed on three games, kept at the bye. Nothing in the record reversed it.
- Identification: the one individual technique note, from August, records the assignment identified correctly and recovery after the loss. That note is tied to the game's one sack, and preseason protection notes came in part from automated attribution (Preseason Game 3 record), so it is read only alongside center week and the in-season decisions, not alone.

Development targets:

- Leverage after a correct identification: the Preseason Game 2 rep (with the caveat above), the pattern camp recorded for the whole protection unit ("Assignments were generally identified; leverage and physical finish produced sacks"). Technique, not assignment or effort.
- Stone was plain that the in-season record on him is thin. After Seattle he said publicly he would "grade the calls on tape"; no grade is recorded, and the bye record reports the sort of sacks by cause only for the group. He did not substitute a verdict from memory.

Teaching state: Good, on the recorded decisions (he knew the job, communicated it and held it at game speed). Better, which asks for retained corrections and unprompted communication across changing looks, is not established: no individual grade of his calls exists.

## The player's view

Brewster said the year went further than he expected in August. He called center week the best coaching he had all season: "Two days where everything was about my calls. I didn't have to guess what mattered." He wants the Thursday identification period he ran with Cousins before Nashville as a regular period.

His complaint was one Stone accepted at once: "You told the press you'd grade my calls. I read that. I never saw it." He added that the sacks column was the only individual number anyone had shown him about his year. Stone told him that column is not evidence about him and will not be used; Yarno's cutup will be the answer. Stone treated both as teaching problems first. He also asked for exchange, cadence and silent-count work with the quarterbacks before camp.

## Future and contract

He asked whether center is his in 2014 and whether an extension had been discussed, with one year left on his deal. Stone made no role commitment: he earned the job on 2013 evidence, 2014's job is earned on 2014 evidence, and "a starting job is not lifetime tenure" for anyone. Contracts are Caldwell's; Stone will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is. Asked whether the club will draft a center, Stone said acquisitions are Caldwell's and he would not discuss the board.

## Development plan

Voluntary. Jacksonville's offseason program may begin no earlier than April 21; nothing before it or in any voluntary phase is required, and declining any suggestion costs him nothing.

- Film: Yarno's cutup of his season from Week 3, calls and protection reps sorted by the bye-review causes, the Seattle calls graded, a correct rep beside each loss where one exists. What can be sent before April 21 is for Caldwell's office to confirm.
- Identification: the blocked, hot or kill period, proposed in Cousins's exit plan as a standing OTA period (the 2014 OTA plan is not yet written), with Brewster as its center if he attends.
- Technique: leverage and finish after a correct identification, method his choice.
- Exchange, cadence and silent count with the quarterbacks when group instruction is permitted.
- Physical: his own routine; strength and performance work is available, voluntary, in Phase One from April 21. Medical: today's physical belongs to the medical staff. Stone reminded him that Player Development and confidential support go on no report, without asking whether he uses them.
