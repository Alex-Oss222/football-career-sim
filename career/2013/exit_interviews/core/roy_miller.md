## The season in the record

Before the season the staff viewed him "primarily as a run-defense / interior-rotation option rather than a pass-rush solution"; he signed after his market "produced interest in a defined early-down interior role." Camp: "the most stable interior run combination" with Marks (July 26-29), "Miller/Marks held the interior" at the August 3 scrimmage, and "Crennel retained Miller/Marks inside." The spring and preseason game records do not name him individually.

He dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games.

- Regular season: 49 tackles (30 solo, 19 assisted), 1 sack, 4 tackles for loss, 7 pressures, 3 passes defended.
- Postseason: 3 tackles (2 solo, 1 assisted), 1 tackle for loss.

Recorded moments:

- Week 2 at Oakland (L 13-17): six tackles; tackles for loss at Seattle (W 16-13) and St. Louis (L 24-26).
- Weeks 7 and 8 (wins over San Diego and San Francisco): a pass defended in each; "The run defense: 34 yards allowed," then "The run defense against Gore: 17 carries, 45 yards."
- Week 10 at Tennessee (L 11-41): "Roy Miller sacked him in the end zone for a safety, 7-2," a standout performance. The same game's correction: "The fourth-quarter run defense: Johnson's 52-yard touchdown and 210 rushing yards allowed."
- Week 16 vs Tennessee (W 38-27): seven tackles, a tackle for loss, two pressures; held up, "Run fit: 20 Tennessee runs for 77 yards (210 in Week 10)."
- Wild Card at Kansas City (W 38-14): a tackle for loss; 47 rushing yards allowed.

Unit corrections in games he dressed for, none naming an individual: Week 12 "Foster's 29 carries," Week 13 "Richardson's 129 yards," Week 15 "237 rushing yards and 7.2 a carry" without Posluszny, and the Divisional "Johnson 24 carries for 103; Tennessee held the ball 37:06." No weekly-award shortlist, in-season honour or receipt injury. No snap count is recorded.

## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- The job he was signed for. The interior run fit held in camp. In the season the weekly records list the run defense as holding in Weeks 1, 4, 7, 8, 11, 14, 16 and 17 and the Wild Card; those are unit results, like the corrections. The Week 16 rematch answered Week 10: his seven tackles in a game Tennessee ran 20 times for 77 yards.
- Available every week, and the Week 10 safety.

Development targets:

- The run games that went wrong (Week 10's fourth quarter, Weeks 12, 13 and 15, the Divisional) are recorded against the unit. His reps inside them are classified by the state/05 categories before any fault is his; Stone assumed none.
- The rush line (1 sack, 7 pressures) cannot be read yet. Several weekly directives put the run fit first ("fit Richardson before rushing past the run," "Johnson's run fit before the rush plan"), and the staff holds no classified rush reps for him.

Teaching state: for camp, his base run fit meets the Good description (he knew and executed the fit at the contact level camp allowed, in the most stable pairing). Nothing recorded individually reaches Better, and nothing recorded says he fell short of it.

## The player's view

Miller said he knew what he was signed for and thought he did it. He did not accept that the bad run games were the front's alone: "Two hundred thirty-seven isn't four guys. After Poz, the linebackers behind us changed. I want the tape to say whether we got reached or the fit behind us moved." Stone said that is a question for the tape, and a communication and teaching question before it is anyone's fault; Crennel's check of front-call timing from Week 14 on, already owed to Marks, would come to him too.

On coaching, he said the weekly directive gave the interior a clear first job, which helped. What did not help was a Week 17 directive that said both "fit the back before chasing Luck" and "finish rushes," with "rush finish" also the defense's bye-week focus: "Tell me which one the call wants." Pleasant agreed the directives said what to stop, not what the rush could give up. Miller asked whether he had been labelled a two-down player. Stone told him the truth: when Jacksonville signed him, the staff saw him first as a run defender; that was a signing view, not a permanent label, and roles move on evidence.

## Future and contract

Miller knows his 2014 base is not guaranteed and asked directly whether he will be on the team in March. Stone said contracts and roster decisions are Caldwell's; he will give Caldwell his football evaluation and did not say what it is or what he will recommend. Miller asked whether the club will add interior players. Stone said acquisitions are Caldwell's, whoever is here competes, and a 2014 role is earned on 2014 evidence. Miller: "That's a no-answer." Stone: "It's the true one."
