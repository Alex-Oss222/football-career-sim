## The season in the record

Stone's early-2013 roster evaluation (written late January to February 2013) listed him in the defensive core "(if healthy)"; the initial roster gives his 2012 as 9 games and 9 starts and lists him on reserve/injured at that season's close. The spring records do not name him. ## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- Communication, the one individual practice evaluation the record holds: the cleanest and "most stabilizing" safety communicator in camp, before he and Rambo took first safety work at the August 3 staff review.
- Available for all 18 games.
- The takeaways the held-up lists kept: London, Cleveland and the Week 16 interception that became a touchdown two plays later. Oden's tape decides whether each came from the right spot in the call; if it did, it is a rep to keep.

Development targets:

- None the record assigns to him. The pass-defense and run-fit corrections in the table are unit items, each classified (assignment, communication, technique, physical loss, processing, coaching or teaching) before any of it is his. Where a help directive asked for two things at once, the first question is whether it said which came first; Stone said the directives were his.
- Teaching state: the record holds camp communication reads, not a Good/Better/Best state, and Stone did not build one from a tackle total.

## The player's view

Lowery was even and went at the numbers first. "A hundred and nineteen tackles from a safety means I played well or the ball kept getting to the third level. I want to know which." He said he heard his tackle count every week and never once whether one of those tackles should have been a ball broken up or a run stopped five yards sooner.

His main point was the help. "When Brent asks where the help was, the help was us. Some weeks we were told two things, and the ball went to the one we weren't doing." He named the Houston and Buffalo directives and wants to see, from the call sheets, which rule each call gave priority to. Stone said those were his directives to Crennel; if they asked for two things without an order, that failure is his before it is Lowery's. Oden said Grimes had raised the same question from the corner side.

Stone asked him the council questions for the whole season. What should not change: taking out what does not work, as Crennel did with the second pressure in camp, "instead of making us live with it for a month." What was unclear: the priority rule. What wastes time: nothing he would cut; he would add individual film for the back end after games, not only unit cutups. He also asked to sit in on the run-defense review. "Johnson twice, Buffalo. I'm in those fits. If the fit's wrong, I'm wrong too."

On the coaching, he called the camp communication work the best teaching the back end had all year, and said that after camp the safeties became "a row with two names on it." Oden did not dispute it.

## Future and contract

He asked no contract question; he is under contract through 2015. He asked whether the club intends to add a safety and whether he and Rambo start the spring as the pair. Stone said signings and the draft are Caldwell's and he would not discuss them; no 2014 role is promised to anyone, Lowery included, and the spring evaluation starts from 2014 work. Lowery said that was what he expected and he would rather compete than be told.

## Development plan

All voluntary. Jacksonville's 2014 offseason program may begin no earlier than April 21, and declining any voluntary work costs nothing.

- Film, his to keep: Oden's cutup of his 18 games from Crennel's call sheets, each snap tagged with the call, his help or fit assignment and the result, classified. Priority: the Week 5, 6, 10, 12, 15 and 17 items and the Divisional run fit; keep reps: the Week 8 takeaway and the Week 13 and Week 16 interceptions.
- Help priority: the written account of each week's help plan that Crennel already owes Grimes (Week 11 through the Divisional), extended to the safeties, so both rooms get the same answer.
- Run fit: an invitation to the run-defense self-scout Crennel owes Posluszny, voluntary and when the calendar permits.
- Physical: his own routine; performance-staff input on request within CBA limits; Phase One from April 21 at the earliest.
- Support: Stone reminded him that Player Development and confidential support go on no report, without asking whether he uses them.
