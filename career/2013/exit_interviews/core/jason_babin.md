## Stone's evaluation

Strengths:

- The camp rush. "Leading edge-rush pressure" in the first physical work, the edge work before Miami, and the edge job at camp close.
- Week 14. His first-half sack forced the punt before the 80-yard touchdown drive in the 21-20 win over Houston, and he had two sacks that night. Week 15, the only sack of Manuel.
- Availability. Dressed for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games, first in every recorded edge order, no receipt injury.
- Quarterback containment in the late directive weeks held: Manuel, Locker (Week 16) and Luck ran for 4, 10 and 2 yards. The edge rows also record "Rush lanes held" (Week 3) and "Contained" (Weeks 6 and 7). Stone called these unit results and did not credit them to Babin individually.

Development targets:

- Finish. Pressures are credited in Weeks 2 to 4, then none until Week 14, and none in Weeks 16 and 17 or the playoffs; no sack through Week 13. Stone set out the whole picture first: the contain directives begin in Week 8, Weeks 5 to 7 carry neither a directive nor a credited pressure, and the Week 6 plan already recorded 2 sacks in five games. He called it a film question, not a verdict.
- Contain. Kaepernick's 30-yard run (Week 8), Schaub's 9 and 10 (Week 14) and Locker's 13-yard scramble on the Divisional go-ahead drive. None of those records names an edge, and none goes in his column before the film is sorted.

Teaching state: Stone told him the camp rush meets Good as a rusher at camp contact level. He did not name Better, because nothing written records his corrections or how he handled changed looks.

## The player's view

Babin was blunt and did not accept the sack total as the measure of his year. "Half the season the sheet said keep the quarterback in front of me. You don't get sacks doing that." He said he agreed with the plan against Kaepernick and Locker; what he wanted was to know, week by week, on which downs the rush was his to take. Pleasant said the weekly sheet recorded what the defense would stop, not what the rush was asked to give up for it. Marks raised the same point on January 13, and Stone treated it as a teaching failure first.

On Weeks 5 to 7 he did not concede: early opponents got the ball out quickly, he said, and the film would show it. Stone said the sort should answer that, and he would take its answer either way.

On the scrambles he asked that nobody assume the lane was his: "Check which side it went out." Pleasant agreed to classify every quarterback run by side and assignment before anyone's name goes on it.

What worked: the plan he heard in midweek meetings was the plan Crennel called on game day. What did not: "finish" arrived as a word for the whole defense, with none of his own reps attached. What he needs: the cutup, and to run his own training in the spring.

## Future and contract

He asked two things: whether Jacksonville would add an edge rusher in free agency or the draft, and whether he would still be first on the edge in September. Stone said acquisitions and the draft board are Caldwell's and he would not discuss either; that no 2014 role is set, his included, and every job is decided on 2014 evidence; and that he will give Caldwell his football evaluation of Babin and would not tell him what it is. He made no roster, starting or contract statement. Babin said he expected to compete and expected to win. He did not raise his contract.
