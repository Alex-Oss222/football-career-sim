# 2013 exit interviews: Practice squad summaries

Tuesday January 14, 2014, at the stadium facility: summaries for the eight practice-squad players.

### Tyler Bray, QB

Present: Stone; Jeremy Bates; Charlie Skalaski. Age 22; available; practice-squad contract expiring (the August 31 waiver ended his #208 rookie contract). No 2013 regular-season or postseason statistic. Through spring and camp his base cadence and footwork improved, but "his progression timing became late when presentation changed." In preseason Game 4 at Atlanta, "Bray's late processing produced the lone turnover," and Wilson "earned QB3 over Bray."

Stone's message: the base operation held, and at mandatory minicamp he "correctly communicated the protection on his re-test." As a teaching state that is Good on the reduced core; Better means executing against changing looks. The Game 4 turnover is that thread, not a new flaw. His practice-squad season is unrecorded and stays unevaluated.

Bray's point: the reduced menu hid what he could do, and he never knew what earned the next layer. The staff's written criterion was "until his progression timing survives a changed picture"; whether it reached Bray is a possible teaching failure. He asked about a reserve/future contract: Caldwell decides; Stone gives Caldwell his football evaluation without revealing it.

Plan: in the room, Bates and Skalaski walked his changed-picture reps with him. Club work needs a contract, starts no earlier than April 21 and is voluntary.

Follow-ups: Caldwell's office, the reserve/future decision; Bates, written expansion criteria if he is signed.

### Richard Murphy, RB

Present: Stone; Tim Spencer. Age 27; available, no communicated restriction; practice-squad contract expiring. He was on Jacksonville's 2012 final active roster (2012: 5 games, 0 starts). No 2013 regular-season or postseason statistic. The preseason record names him twice: against Miami, "assignment-correct route work from Reisner, Lewis, Grimes and Murphy sustained scoring drives"; at Atlanta he was among those who "sustained assigned route responsibilities." No spring or camp record names him.

Stone's message: those were correct reps, and the record says so. The August 31 recommendation to waive him was Stone's, and Caldwell accepted it after reviewing the whole offseason, camp and all four preseason games; the written record gives no individual reason, and Stone did not improvise one. Spencer owes him a written evaluation.

Murphy's point: at 27 he would rather have Jacksonville's answer early than wait into March. He asked directly about a futures contract. Stone: that is Caldwell's decision, and its timing is Caldwell's too; Stone will give Caldwell his football evaluation and would not predict the answer.

Plan: Stone suggested keeping up the route detail that showed in preseason. Everything is voluntary; club work needs a contract and waits until April 21 at the earliest.

Follow-ups: Spencer, written evaluation; Caldwell's office, the reserve/future decision.

### Brandon King, DB

Present: Stone; Tony Oden. Born January 28, 1987; available, no communicated restriction; practice-squad contract expiring. He entered 2013 on a reserve/future contract signed December 30, 2012, before Stone was hired; the August 31 waiver ended it. No 2013 statistic, and no spring, camp, preseason or season record names him.
