# 2013 exit interviews: Offense summaries

Tuesday January 14, 2014, at the stadium facility: summaries for offensive players outside the main core and core.

### John Parker Wilson, QB

Present: Stone; Jeremy Bates. QB3 all season; age 28; available, no communicated restriction. He dressed Weeks 1 to 4 with no recorded statistic and was inactive from Week 5 ("a game-day numbers decision") through the Divisional. Camp record: "Wilson's clean final-game work earned QB3 over Bray"; at Atlanta in preseason Game 4 he "directed the cleaner passing operation."

Stone's message: the evidence is practice and preseason, and it is consistent. He was "assignment-sound" in OTAs and "assignment-reliable" at mandatory minicamp; in camp he operated the core "without separating upward" and worked "with a narrower menu" in the stadium scrimmage. The regular-season receipts record no play for him, and Stone would not invent a flaw to fill the gap. The next step is the full menu against changing pictures and on third down.

Wilson's point: a third quarterback who does not dress needs his work to show up somewhere. He asked where he stands for 2014. Stone said contract and roster decisions are Caldwell's and that he would give Caldwell his football evaluation without predicting the answer.

Plan: voluntary. Bates offers a cutup of his camp and Game 4 reps; team work waits for the offseason program, no earlier than April 21.

Follow-ups: Caldwell's office to confirm his contract status to him; Bates to build the cutup on request.

### Mike Brown, WR

Present: Stone; Darryl Drake. WR5; age 24; available, no communicated restriction. He dressed 9 games (Weeks 1 to 8 and 10): 16 targets, 12 receptions, 112 yards, longest 22; kick returns 4 for 115, punt returns 3 for 32. He had a 22-yard catch at Seattle and 5 catches on 5 targets for 41 against San Diego, including the 15-yard Split Leak on the opening field-goal drive. He was inactive from Week 11 through the Divisional.

Stone's message: San Diego is the clearest game on his tape. The practice record is thin ("Brown/Clemons provide depth"). The Week 11 list that made him inactive also dressed Mosley and Rutland, with Ball out; nothing recorded is a finding against his play.

Brown's point: he learned he was inactive from the list, and after returning kicks in Week 10 he could not tell what it meant. He asked whether he will be tendered. Stone treated the first as a staff communication problem. On the second: the club lists him as a pending exclusive-rights free agent, any tender is Caldwell's decision, and Stone will give Caldwell his evaluation without saying what it is.

Plan: voluntary. Drake offers a route cutup.

Follow-ups: Stone and Drake to walk him through the Week 11 change; special teams (Stone until the coordinator job is filled) to review his return film on request.

### Mark Asper, G

Present: Stone; George Yarno. Interior line depth; age 28; available, no communicated restriction; under contract, final year 2015. He dressed 6 games as the interior reserve (Weeks 3 to 8). From Week 10 Stone dressed Pasztor as the primary interior reserve, a decision taken from Pasztor's bye-week and Week 10 practice; Asper was inactive through the Divisional. No spring, camp or preseason record names him. The receipts charge him 2 sacks allowed (Weeks 7 and 8); those charges are not evidence, and the record does not show whether he played a snap.

Stone's message: the record is thin and the evaluation stays thin. The Week 10 change rested on Pasztor's work; nothing recorded is a correction of Asper's assignment, technique or effort.

Asper's point: he has no individual evaluation to work from, and he asked whether taking center exchanges would help him dress. Stone promised no role; roles, including a second position, move on 2014 evidence, and whether center work enters his individual plan is Yarno's teaching call.

Plan: voluntary. Strength work with the performance staff is suggested, not required; Yarno's technique work begins with the offseason program, no earlier than April 21.

Follow-ups: Yarno to give him a written evaluation and an answer on center work; Tice with Yarno to classify his charged sacks by cause (the all-linemen follow-up).
