### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** J.J. Watt sacked Cousins on Jacksonville's first snap, and Randy Bullock kicked a 51-yard field goal after Arian Foster's 14-yard run, 3-0. Jacksonville drove from its 20 to the Houston 18: Jones-Drew caught 10 on Spacing and ran for 11 and 12 on 6OL Power, and Grimes ran 11 on Counter. On the next 6OL Power, Johnathan Joseph forced a Grimes fumble and recovered it. After Jason Babin's sack forced a Houston punt, Jacksonville went 80 yards in four plays: Jones-Drew 12 on 6OL Counter, Shorts 30 on Stick, Montell Owens 34 on 22 Heavy Snag, and Jones-Drew's 4-yard touchdown on Counter, 7-3. Houston answered with an 11-play, 80-yard drive (Owen Daniels 16 and 19, Andre Johnson 26, Foster 18 on a catch) and Ben Tate's 2-yard touchdown, 10-7. Jacksonville then held the ball for 12 plays and 89 yards, almost eight minutes: Jones-Drew ran 19 on the Inside access call and 18 on Split, Thielen caught 20 on Texas and Lewis 10 on TE Delay, and Jones-Drew caught a 17-yard touchdown on Stick with 1:59 left, 14-10.

**Second half.** Houston opened with a 10-play drive and Bullock's 55-yard field goal, 14-13. Brian Cushing sacked Cousins, and Keshawn Martin returned the punt 33 yards to the Jacksonville 49. Houston ran on seven of nine plays, Schaub scrambling for 9 and 10, and Johnson caught an 8-yard touchdown, 20-14. After a punt exchange, Tyson Alualu, Daryl Smith and Brent Grimes stopped three straight Houston runs for losses, and a 30-yard punt from the Houston 10 gave Jacksonville the ball at the Houston 40. On the fourth play, with 3:34 left, Shorts caught a 36-yard touchdown on Power Pass Post-Cross, and Scobee's extra point made it 21-20. Houston reached the Jacksonville 39, where Smith forced Johnson's fumble after an 8-yard catch and recovered it with 1:56 left. Cousins knelt twice.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville ran 25 times and dropped back 27 (24 passes, 3 sacks); Jones-Drew 13 carries for 89 and a touchdown, 2 catches for 27 and a touchdown.
- Cousins completed 15 of 24 for 226 yards, two touchdowns and no interception; sacked by Watt, Cushing and Willie Jefferson. Shorts caught 5 of 6 targets for 99 yards and the winning touchdown.
- Third down: Jacksonville 8 of 12, Houston 4 of 10.
- Foster ran 17 times for 40. Johnson caught 6 for 67 and a touchdown and lost the deciding fumble; Hopkins caught 4 of 8 targets for 51. Schaub was 20 of 28 for 216 and ran for 35.
- Daryl Smith had 13 tackles, a forced fumble and a recovery; Russell Allen 8 tackles, 3 for loss, in his first start in Posluszny's place; Babin 2 sacks.
- Scobee was 3 of 3 on extra points and attempted no field goal; Bullock made 51 and 55.
