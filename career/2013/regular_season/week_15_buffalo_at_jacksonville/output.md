### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** Buffalo opened with a 10-play, 84-yard drive, eight of them runs: Fred Jackson ran for 21 and 16, Spiller for 13, and Spiller caught a 7-yard touchdown, 7-0. Nigel Bradham sacked Cousins on Jacksonville's first series. Buffalo went 80 more yards, with Steve Johnson catching 20 and 30 and Spiller running 6 yards for a touchdown, 14-0. Jacksonville answered with a 10-play drive (Kelce 14 on Texas, Lewis 14 and 10 on Sprint Flood, Shorts 14 on Power Pass) and Scobee's 41-yard field goal. After a Buffalo punt downed at the 9, Manny Lawson forced and recovered C.J. Anderson's fumble at the Jacksonville 12, and Chandler caught an 8-yard touchdown on Buffalo's second play, 21-3. Scobee kicked a 27-yarder after a 9-play drive (Blackmon 17 on Mesh, Jones-Drew 16 on Snag). Buffalo then went 79 yards in seven plays, Spiller running 17 twice, and T.J. Graham caught a 10-yard touchdown with 28 seconds left, 28-6.

**Second half.**
- **Three interceptions:** Leodis McKelvin intercepted Cousins on Texas on the first series, and Buffalo scored in 12 plays from the Jacksonville 43, Graham's second touchdown, 35-6. After Buffalo punted from its own 4, Da'Norris Searcy intercepted Cousins on Jacksonville's second snap from the Buffalo 33, and Justin Rogers intercepted him deep in Jacksonville territory early in the fourth quarter. Spiller ran 13 yards for a touchdown on the next snap, 42-6.
- **Late scoring:** Jacksonville closed the gap with Scobee's 27-yard field goal after an 8-play drive (Cousins 12 on a scramble, Jones-Drew 10 and 9), and an 8-play, 69-yard drive ending in Jones-Drew's 26-yard touchdown catch on Texas, 42-16. Buffalo ran seven straight times and Dan Carpenter kicked a 33-yard field goal with 34 seconds left.

**The numbers.**
- Buffalo ran 33 times for 237 yards. Spiller had 24 carries for 147 and two touchdowns, plus a touchdown catch; Jackson had 8 for 86.
- Manuel completed 23 of 32 for 209 yards and four touchdowns with no interception, and was sacked once (Babin).
- Cousins completed 23 of 36 for 242 yards, a touchdown and three interceptions (McKelvin, Searcy, Rogers). Jacksonville lost four turnovers, and three of them led directly to Buffalo touchdowns.
- Jones-Drew had 12 carries for 58 and 6 catches for 76 and a touchdown; Shorts caught 6 of 10 targets for 63.
- Jacksonville ran 24 times against 37 dropbacks; it trailed 28-6 at halftime.
- Scobee made all three field goals.
