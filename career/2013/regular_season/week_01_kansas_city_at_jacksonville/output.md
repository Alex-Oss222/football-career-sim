### Game narrative: selected highlights

Jacksonville's diagnostic approach survived an early scoreless exchange and produced the game's decisive evidence on defense. Crennel's unit intercepted Kansas City five times, with Paul Posluszny, Julian Stanford, A.J. Bouye, Jordan Poyer and Bacarri Rambo each recording one. Kansas City gained 489 yards but repeatedly surrendered possession; Jacksonville converted the separation into a 21-point win.

The offense gained 431 yards, ran for 164 and allowed one sack. The prepared Dagger calls produced 75 yards and a touchdown, Mesh produced 76 yards, and the heavy Power call produced 45 yards and a touchdown. The vertical alert also produced a touchdown but contained Jacksonville's lone turnover, exactly the kind of mixed evidence that preserves the trigger rule rather than turning the shot into a mandatory call.

Kirk Cousins completed 16 of 24 passes for 169 yards, one touchdown and one interception. The kernel again attributed isolated series to Chad Henne and John Parker Wilson without a generated quarterback injury. Henne added 85 yards and a touchdown; Wilson added 13 yards and a touchdown. Cousins remains QB1, and Stone ended any ordinary multi-quarterback usage going forward.

Jonathan Grimes led the rushing work with 57 yards and a touchdown. Travis Kelce led Jacksonville receivers with 47 yards and a touchdown; Marcedes Lewis added 42 yards. Jacksonville scored four touchdowns, made both field-goal attempts, committed one turnover and was penalized seven times for 55 yards.

Montell Owens left with a minor trunk injury and is out pending the next medical reassessment. Bacarri Rambo left with a short-term trunk injury and is medically unavailable, with a four-day reassessment point and a generated return horizon extending beyond Week 2. These are medical dispositions, not coaching choices.
