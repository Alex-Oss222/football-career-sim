### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First quarter.** Jacksonville's first drive reached the Tennessee 4, helped by a 28-yard Stick to Shorts and an 18-yard Texas catch by Thielen, but Josh Scobee missed from 22 yards. On Tennessee's fourth snap, Paul Posluszny intercepted Locker. Jacksonville moved into range again and Scobee missed from 27. Tennessee then drove 80 yards in 12 plays, finishing in the first seconds of the second quarter on Locker's 6-yard pass to Kenny Britt. It was 7-0.

**Second quarter.** Posluszny sacked Locker, then Roy Miller sacked him in the end zone for a safety, 7-2. After the free kick Jacksonville drove six plays for Scobee's 44-yard field goal, 7-5. Then the turnovers started:
- Alterraun Verner forced a Jones-Drew fumble and recovered it at the Jacksonville 14, and Rob Bironas kicked a 33-yard field goal.
- On the next possession Jason McCourty intercepted a pass to Jones-Drew.

It was 10-5 at halftime.

**Third quarter.** Thielen (25) and Shorts (35) set up Scobee's 35-yard field goal, 10-8. Tennessee answered at once: Locker scrambled 41 yards, Johnson ran 19 and 13, and Britt caught a 7-yard touchdown, 17-8. Kelce caught a 36-yard pass on the next drive, but Zach Brown intercepted a throw to Thielen. Scobee's 48-yarder, set up by Thielen's 37-yard catch, made it 17-11.

**Fourth quarter.**
- Tennessee's 87-yard drive, begun late in the third quarter, ended early in the fourth with Johnson's 21-yard touchdown catch, 24-11.
- McCourty intercepted Cousins on a throw to Shorts at the Jacksonville 8, and Darius Reynaud scored from four yards on the third snap, 31-11.
- After a Jacksonville three-and-out, Johnson ran 52 yards for a touchdown on Tennessee's first snap, 38-11.
- Verner intercepted Cousins, and Bironas kicked a 33-yard field goal, 41-11.
- Pollard sacked Cousins and forced a fumble that he recovered himself, and Tennessee knelt out the game.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville turned the ball over six times: four interceptions (McCourty two, Zach Brown, Verner) and two lost fumbles.
- Cousins completed 21 of 35 for 285 yards and was sacked four times; Derrick Morgan had two sacks.
- Scobee made 3 of 5 field goals, missing from 22 and 27.
- Shorts caught 9 of 12 targets for 116 yards, and Thielen caught four for 82.
- Jones-Drew ran 15 times for 40 yards.
- Tennessee ran for 210 yards: Johnson 14 carries for 126 and a touchdown, plus three catches for 26 and a touchdown.
- Locker completed 23 of 31 for 179 yards and three touchdowns. Britt caught 10 passes for 77 yards and two touchdowns.
- Jacksonville's defense had three sacks, including the safety, and Posluszny's interception.
