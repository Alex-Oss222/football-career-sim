### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** Jacksonville went 89 yards on its first possession: Marcedes Lewis caught 42 on Spacing and Cecil Shorts 37 for a touchdown on Sprint Flood, but Scobee missed the extra point, 6-0. Russell Allen sacked Brandon Weeden on Cleveland's first series, and after a punt Scobee kicked a 51-yard field goal, 9-0. Cleveland answered with a 10-play drive, nine of them runs; Trent Richardson ran for 14, 16 and 13 before Billy Cundiff kicked from the 2 on fourth-and-1, 9-3. Scobee missed from 48 after Jones-Drew caught 23 on Stick and ran 14 on Counter. Cundiff kicked a 31-yarder, Ahtyba Rubin sacked Cousins for 10, and Cleveland went 75 yards in eight plays, with Davone Bess catching 31 and Richardson catching a 7-yard touchdown, 13-9. With 1:25 left Cleveland drove from its 10 to the Jacksonville 14, Bess catching 30, and Cundiff's 32-yarder on the last play of the half made it 16-9.

**Second half.** Johnson Bademosi returned the second-half kickoff 68 yards, but Cleveland lost 6 and punted to the Jacksonville 11. Jacksonville threw on all six snaps of the next drive, reached the Cleveland 48 and lost the ball on Joe Haden's interception, returned to the Jacksonville 34. Four plays later Dwight Lowery intercepted Weeden. After four punts, Jacksonville went 45 yards (Lewis 16 and 12, Thielen 13 on Texas) for Scobee's 35-yard field goal early in the fourth quarter, cutting Cleveland's lead to 16-12. After a Cleveland punt, Lewis caught 23 on Snag and Toney Clemons caught a 43-yard touchdown, putting Jacksonville ahead 19-16. Cleveland tied it with 2:46 left on Cundiff's fourth field goal, from 43, after Jordan Cameron's 19-yard catch. Jacksonville reached the Cleveland 28 on two Justin Blackmon catches of 23 and 18, and Haden intercepted Cousins's next throw with 20 seconds left.

**Overtime.** Cleveland received and went five plays for 8 yards and a punt. Lowery returned it 40 yards to the Cleveland 36. Because Cleveland's opening possession did not score, the next score won. Jones-Drew ran 12 yards on 22 Heavy Power and 8 on Counter, and Scobee kicked a 34-yard field goal on third down to win it, 22-19.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville ran 28 times and dropped back 36 times (34 passes, 2 sacks). Jones-Drew ran 16 times for 93 yards and caught 3 for 40.
- Cousins completed 20 of 34 for 328 yards, two touchdowns and two interceptions, both to Haden. Lewis caught 7 for 110; Blackmon 5 for 72.
- Richardson ran 25 times for 129 yards and caught a touchdown. Bess caught 6 of 14 targets for 71; Cameron caught 1 of 3 for 19. Weeden completed 24 of 43 for 210 with Lowery's interception and Allen's sack.
- Third down: Jacksonville 4 of 14, Cleveland 6 of 18.
- Kicking: Scobee made 3 of 4 field goals and missed an extra point; Cundiff made all four. Bademosi returned 4 kickoffs for 140 yards.
