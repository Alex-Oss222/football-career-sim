**Stone's decisions:**
- **Monday:** "We got beat. Film first." Each of the six Tennessee turnovers was shown once with a coaching label.

- **Jones-Drew:** stayed the lead back. His fumble was coached, not punished.
- **Ball's replacement:** Mike Harris started outside; Poyer stayed at nickel, with Bouye the first outside reserve. Rutland dressed.
- **Mosley:** dressed and joined the interior rotation with Marks, Miller and Alualu.
- **Inactives:** Ball, C.J. Wilson, Edwards, Pendleton, Asper, John Parker Wilson, Mike Brown.
- **Offensive thesis:** "Get back downhill," with 12 personnel first and Duo and Power the lead runs.
- **Directives to Crennel:** fit the run again; identify Fitzgerald every snap without leaving Harris alone; cover Housler and the backs.
- **2014 scouting directive:** Stone sent a coaching-focus list to Caldwell and the staff (see link above). It was scheduled around, never ahead of, Arizona preparation.

### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** Jacksonville opened with a nine-play, 68-yard drive, but Scobee missed from 30. Daryl Smith intercepted Carson Palmer on Arizona's third snap, and Scobee kicked a 27-yard field goal. Harris sacked Palmer on the next Arizona series, and Jay Feely missed from 32. Jacksonville then went 78 yards in seven plays: Shorts caught 19, Jonathan Grimes caught 28 on Power Pass Post-Cross, and Grimes ran 16 yards for a touchdown on Wham, 10-0. A 10-play, 81-yard drive, seven of its plays by Jones-Drew, ended in a 26-yard field goal. Russell Allen intercepted Palmer with 19 seconds left, Jones-Drew ran 35 yards, and Scobee kicked a 25-yarder on the last play of the half, 16-0.

**Second half.** Arizona opened with a 13-play drive, and Feely missed from 31. Jacksonville answered in five plays: Blackmon caught 18, Shorts caught 26 and 20, and Jones-Drew scored from 15 yards on the Inside access call, 23-0. Dwight Lowery sacked Palmer, Jordan Poyer intercepted him, and Scobee kicked a 26-yarder, 26-0. Arizona's only score was an 11-play, 80-yard drive that ended with Mendenhall's 22-yard touchdown catch early in the fourth quarter. Jacksonville closed with an eight-play, 77-yard drive of mostly runs, including Grimes for 18 and Jones-Drew for 14 and 31, and Scobee's 33-yard field goal with 27 seconds left.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville ran for 267 yards: Jones-Drew 25 carries for 191 and a touchdown, Grimes 7 for 62 and a touchdown.
- Cousins completed 19 of 24 for 219 yards with no interception and was sacked once. Shorts caught 8 for 90 and Blackmon 4 for 42.
- The defense had three interceptions (Daryl Smith, Russell Allen, Poyer) and four sacks (Alualu two, Harris, Lowery), and held Arizona to 76 rushing yards.
- Larry Fitzgerald caught 8 passes for 103 yards.
- Scobee made 5 of 6 field goals; Feely missed both his tries.
