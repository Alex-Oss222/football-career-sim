## 2. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context** (San Francisco's seven branch games; passing yards are gross, before sack yardage, as the receipts record them):
- **Record:** San Francisco came in 2-5.
- **Defense:** allowed 22.6 points and 358.9 yards a game (246.3 passing, 112.6 rushing), and opponents converted 43.0% of third downs. It had 14 takeaways (10 interceptions, 4 fumble recoveries) and 13 sacks.
- **Offense:** averaged 20.1 points and 302.1 yards (183.7 passing, 118.4 rushing) and converted 38.9% of third downs, with 10 giveaways and 18 sacks allowed.
- **Frank Gore** had run for 106 yards at St. Louis, 109 against Houston and 165 against Arizona.
- **Anquan Boldin** had receiving games of 91, 130, 96 and 62 yards.
- **Availability:** Ray McDonald was out from his Week 4 injury (projected return November 12).

Stone's reading: an inconsistent offense and a defense whose linebackers and secondary had produced the takeaways, against a Jacksonville offense three games without a giveaway.

**Stone's decisions:**
- **Theme:** "More people. Same language." The expansion came from personnel, formation and sequencing rather than new volume, because of the London trip.
- **Blackmon dressed** for the first time since his return, as WR3 and the outside Z receiver. The receiver order was Shorts, Thielen, Blackmon, Clemons and Brown. His jobs were run blocking, Smoke against cushion, Slant-Flat, backside access and, only if San Francisco earned it, a vertical alert or the Mills/Post role. He had no target quota and no ceremonial first call; the first scripted call was a run.
- **Thielen** became the movable receiver: H, slot, stack, Return and Zip motion, Whip, Mesh and Sprint Flood.
- **Kelce** joined the regular call structure, with some calls making him an intermediate read after his chip.
- **New call:** Inside with an access Smoke to Blackmon, from 11 Doubles and 11 Trips. Mills returned as a secondary shot behind Power Pass Post-Cross. Boot Flood stayed out.
- **Inactives:** Pasztor, Mosley, C.J. Wilson, Edwards, Pendleton, Rutland and John Parker Wilson. Blackmon and Mike Brown dressed, so five receivers were available.
- **Directives to Crennel,** who called the defense: fit Gore before chasing Kaepernick; collapse and contain Kaepernick rather than running past him; finish through the catch against Boldin; give Vernon Davis full tight-end preparation without distorting the plan.

**Practice / teaching emphasis:**
- **Monday:** the San Diego film. Keep the 407 yards, the clean ball security, the fast start and the run defense; fix the four sacks, 91 rushing yards, 3 of 10 on third down and the 80-yard touchdown to a running back.
- **Tuesday:** Tice's staff mapped how San Francisco matches 11 and 12 personnel, how Bowman and Willis react to backs, tight ends and Thielen's motion, what Aldon Smith does against chip help, and where the 14 takeaways came from.
- **Wednesday:** Blackmon's role install (not a re-install, since he had practised through Weeks 6 and 7), graded on blocking as much as routes. Thielen and Kelce's development work continued.
- **Thursday:** conflict sequences: Power, Blackmon Smoke and the Go alert; Thielen Mesh and Whip; Kelce Split, Split Leak and Y-Cross; 6OL Power and HOLD.
- **Friday:** reduce before travel. Any sequence that was not clean, or any alert Cousins and Blackmon read differently, came off the plan.
- **London:** no new install after the primary practice work. The London days were for walkthrough, retention, substitutions, communication, situations and recovery. The repository records no detailed travel itinerary, and none is assumed here.

### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First quarter.** Jacksonville's first two possessions ended in punts. Blackmon's first touch was a 1-yard catch on the opening series, and Ian Williams sacked Cousins on the first Mills call. The third possession went 73 yards in 10 plays. Jones-Drew caught a 15-yard pass, Shorts gained 16 and then 28, and Jones-Drew ran 13 yards on 6OL Heavy Right Power before scoring from four yards on 11 Trey Right Power with 52 seconds left in the quarter. It was 7-0.

**Second quarter.** After a San Francisco three-and-out, Thielen scored on a 20-yard Mesh from 11 Bunch Right on Jacksonville's second snap, making it 14-0. Then the turnovers started:
- Nnamdi Asomugha intercepted Cousins on a throw to Shorts. San Francisco drove eight plays for Phil Dawson's 41-yard field goal.
- Patrick Willis intercepted Cousins on another throw toward Shorts, after a 34-yard catch by Marcedes Lewis.
- San Francisco reached a 54-yard field-goal try on the last play of the half, helped by a 28-yard catch by Vernon Davis. Dawson missed.

It was 14-3 at halftime.

**Third quarter.** San Francisco opened with a nine-play, 86-yard touchdown drive. Kendall Hunter ran for 13 and 11, Gore caught a 19-yard pass and Quinton Patton scored on a 19-yard catch, making it 14-10. Jacksonville answered with a 13-play drive after Dwight Lowery's 34-yard kickoff return. Cousins completed eight passes on it, among them Kelce's first catch since Week 5, a 3-yarder on Y-Cross. Scobee kicked a 38-yard field goal to make it 17-10. On San Francisco's next possession Kaepernick ran for 30 yards, and on the next snap Lowery forced a fumble by Kyle Williams and recovered it.

**Fourth quarter.**
- Jacksonville's drive after the fumble lasted 14 plays and ended on downs early in the quarter. Thielen caught passes of 32 and 18 on it.
- After a San Francisco punt and a 19-yard return by Mike Brown, Montell Owens ran for 13 and Thielen caught a 19-yard Y-Cross. Scobee's 43-yard field goal made it 20-10 with 10:46 left.
- Dawson's 34-yarder made it 20-13 with 5:45 left.
- Aldon Smith intercepted Cousins on a throw toward Shorts at 3:27. Jeremy Mincey then sacked Kaepernick, and San Francisco punted.
- Taking over with 2:30 left, Jacksonville ran twice from 6OL Heavy, and on the next snap Tarell Brown intercepted a throw to Thielen.
- San Francisco got the ball with 2:07 left and moved 29 yards in eight plays. Kaepernick's last two passes, to Marlon Moore and Davis, fell incomplete.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville gained 400 yards (267 passing, 133 rushing) to San Francisco's 336.
- Cousins completed 23 of 38 for 267 yards and a touchdown and was sacked three times. His four interceptions ended the three-game turnover-free run; three came on throws toward Shorts.
- Thielen caught 7 of 10 targets for 109 yards and the touchdown. Shorts caught 7 of 13 for 81.
- Blackmon caught 1 of 2 targets for 1 yard and returned a punt 4 yards. Kelce caught 1 of 2 for 3.
- Gore ran 17 times for 45 yards. Boldin caught seven passes for 26 yards, and Davis caught six for 76.
- Jacksonville converted 4 of 13 third downs; San Francisco converted 8 of 18.
