### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** Tennessee scored first: Kenny Britt caught 48 and Damian Williams an 18-yard touchdown, 7-0. Shorts caught 55 on Texas to set up Scobee's 47-yard field goal, 7-3. Jacksonville then went 89 yards in nine plays: Shorts caught 36 on Spacing, Jones-Drew ran 12 on the Inside access call and 8 on Wham, and Thielen caught a 25-yard touchdown on Texas, 10-7. C.J. Mosley sacked Jake Locker and Tennessee settled for a 34-yard field goal. Jonathan Grimes caught 39 on 22 Heavy Snag and ran 15 and 18 on consecutive Wham calls for a touchdown, 17-10. Britt caught 49 to set up a 22-yard field goal on the last play of the half, 17-13.

**Second half.** Jacksonville scored three touchdowns in the third quarter:
- **Opening drive:** Kelce caught 28 on Power Pass Post-Cross, Thielen 50 on 22 Heavy Snag, and Shorts a 2-yard touchdown on the same call, 24-13.
- **After a punt from Tennessee's end:** Clemons caught 32 on Mesh and Jones-Drew scored from a yard on 6OL Counter, 31-13.
- **Takeaway:** Dwight Lowery intercepted Locker on Tennessee's next snap, and two plays later Lewis caught a 10-yard touchdown on Texas, 38-13.

Tennessee answered with Shonn Greene's 50-yard touchdown catch and a 13-play drive ending in Chris Johnson's 8-yard touchdown catch, 38-27 with 5:49 left. Tennessee's last drive ran 14 plays to the Jacksonville 1, where it turned the ball over on downs with 44 seconds left.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville ran 36 times against 34 dropbacks and committed no turnover. Jones-Drew had 25 carries for 93 yards and a touchdown; Grimes ran 4 times for 37 and a touchdown and caught 2 for 42.
- Cousins completed 16 of 32 for 356 yards and three touchdowns with no interception; sacked twice (Morgan, Mike Martin). Shorts caught 4 of 12 targets for 104 and a touchdown; Thielen 2 for 75 and a touchdown; Lewis 4 for 59 and a touchdown.
- Tennessee ran 20 times for 77 yards; Johnson had 13 carries for 59. Locker completed 35 of 57 for 432, three touchdowns and one interception; Britt caught 15 of 24 targets for 187.
- Daryl Smith and Lowery had 10 tackles each; Mosley had the sack.
