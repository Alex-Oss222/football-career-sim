## 2. Week setup and game preparation

**Roster move:** Justin Blackmon's league suspension ended with Week 5. He was reinstated on Monday, October 7, the day after Jacksonville's fourth suspended game, as the sourced 2013 rule sets it (`library/2013_suspension_reinstatement_rules.md`). Jacksonville activated him to the roster spot Caldwell had held open since the suspension, so no league roster exemption was needed. The active roster is back to 53. Blackmon practiced this week and was one of Stone's seven game-day inactives, under the Week 6-7 decision recorded in Entry 36.

**Opponent context:**
- **Denver defense** came in 2-3. It had allowed 25.4 points, 427.2 yards (305.2 passing, 122.0 rushing) and a 43.1% third-down rate a game. It still had 11 sacks, spread over eight players, and 7 takeaways.
- **Denver offense** averaged 23.6 points, 353.2 yards and a 45.9% third-down rate, with 7 giveaways and 9 sacks allowed.
- **Players to watch:** Peyton Manning had thrown three interceptions at Dallas. Ronnie Hillman had 115 rushing and 143 receiving yards there, including an 86-yard catch.
- **Jacksonville's rush** had 2 sacks in five games.

**Stone's decisions:**
- **Line:** Brewster is now the starting center, no longer a temporary replacement. Meester is reserve center, Bradfield swing tackle and sixth offensive lineman, Asper interior depth.
- **Inactives:** Blackmon, Pasztor, Mosley, C.J. Wilson, Edwards, Rutland and John Parker Wilson. Henne dressed as QB2 and Thielen returned as WR2.
- **Development emphasis:** Kelce and Thielen, through assignments rather than target quotas. For Kelce: attached blocking, split-flow, the new H chip-release tag, TE Delay, Snag and Y-Cross. For Thielen: motion, Mesh, Smoke, Spacing and Drive.
- **Menu changes:** Sprint Flood entered as a new launch point; Boot Flood stayed out.

**Practice / teaching emphasis:**
- **Monday:** graded the St. Louis film piece by piece, keeping what worked and cutting what didn't.
- **Tuesday:** Tice's staff mapped Denver's front, pressure and response to 12 personnel and motion.
- **Wednesday:** the core offense first, then Kelce's chip landmark and release timing and Thielen's motion landmarks and route depths.
- **Thursday:** varied scout looks so Kelce and Thielen had to identify their own answers.
- **Friday:** tested each new tag for timing.
- **Saturday:** mental work.

### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** The first quarter was scoreless. Jacksonville's opening drive ran ten plays over 5:33 and ended in a turnover on downs. After that the sides traded punts, and Josh Scobee missed a 51-yard field goal early in the second quarter. Denver scored on a three-play drive: Ronnie Hillman ran for 36 yards, then 41, and Peyton Manning threw a one-yard touchdown to Wes Welker. Matt Prater missed a 56-yarder as the half ended, and Denver led 7-0.

**Third quarter.**
- Scobee opened the scoring from 53 yards.
- On Denver's next possession Knowshon Moreno was tackled for a 12-yard loss by Posluszny, and the ledger records a safety. The possession began after a 28-yard kickoff return, so that safety could not have happened as recorded (see section 8).
- After the free kick, Cousins found Adam Thielen for 38 yards on 12 Trey Right Sprint Flood R. Jones-Drew then scored from 11 yards on 21 Pro Left Counter R, making it 12-7.
- Prater answered with a 51-yard field goal early in the fourth quarter.

**Fourth quarter.** Jacksonville put the game away with two touchdown drives:
- The first went 13 plays. Jonathan Grimes caught a 25-yard Power Pass Post-Cross, and Toney Clemons caught passes of 18 on 12 Ace Shift Empty Spacing and 20. Marcedes Lewis scored on a 14-yard 12 Trey Right Stick with the H chip-release tag.
- After Sen'Derrick Marks's second and third sacks forced a Denver punt, the second drive went 10 plays. Jones-Drew ran for 13, 7, 13, 7 and 10 on Counter, 6OL Power, Split, Inside and 6OL Power again. Clemons caught a 19-yard touchdown on the chip-release Stick call with 3:28 left.

**The numbers.**
- Denver went 0 for 13 on third down and gained 195 yards, 87 of them passing.
- Jacksonville ran 79 plays to Denver's 45 and held the ball 38:41.
- Cousins completed 28 of 42 for 276 yards and two touchdowns without an interception, and was sacked three times.
- Jones-Drew ran 22 times for 125 yards.
- Neither team turned the ball over.
- Travis Kelce was not targeted. Under kernel 2013.6, targets follow the tight-end depth order and play-call labels are not tied to the receiver. The TE Delay label landed on throws to Clemons and Mike Brown.
