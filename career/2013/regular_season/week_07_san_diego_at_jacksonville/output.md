## 2. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context:**
- **San Diego** came in 4-2.
- **San Diego defense** It had allowed 19.7 points, 354.5 yards (232.0 passing, 122.5 rushing) and a 40.0% third-down rate a game. It had 10 takeaways and 8 sacks; Derek Cox had an interception, a forced fumble and a recovery against Indianapolis.
- **San Diego offense** averaged 21.7 points and 362.0 yards and converted 37.5% of third downs, with 13 sacks allowed and 7 turnovers.
- **Philip Rivers** had a five-touchdown game and a three-interception game.
- **Malcom Floyd** had topped 90 receiving yards in four of six games.
- **Ryan Mathews** had contributed as a runner and receiver.

Stone's reading: San Diego was an opportunistic defense more than a dominant one, so Jacksonville's two-game turnover-free streak mattered.

**Stone's decisions:**
- **Lineup:** unchanged from Denver, with Brewster at center. The seven inactives carried over: Blackmon (in his final inactive week), Pasztor, Mosley, C.J. Wilson, Edwards, Rutland and John Parker Wilson.
- **Offensive theme:** "block it, show it, leak it."
- **Kelce's development** moved from block and chip-release to Wham (a new change-up run) and Split Leak with TURNBACK protection. That is a leak off the split-flow action San Diego would already have seen him block.
- **Thielen** added Whip/Pivot to his motion, Smoke, Mesh and Sprint Flood work.
- **Shots:** Power Pass Post-Cross stayed the primary shot. A Bubble/Smoke Pump Go alert was allowed only if San Diego jumped the short game.
- **Kept off the sheet:** Boot Flood.

**Practice / teaching emphasis:**
- **Monday:** coached the Denver film. Keep the turnover-free football, the run game and the defensive third downs; fix the three sacks, the scoreless first half and Hillman's long runs.
- **Tuesday:** Tice's staff mapped San Diego's front against 12 personnel, the edge's reaction to split-flow, Weddle's run fits and where the ten takeaways came from.
- **Wednesday:** Kelce's progression (Power, Split, Wham, chip-release, Split Leak, Y-Cross) and Thielen's release tempo.
- **Thursday:** complementary sequences.
- **Friday:** the cut rule applied to each new call.

### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First quarter.** Jacksonville scored on its first three possessions. The opening drive went six plays, the longest a 15-yard Split Leak completion to Mike Brown, and Josh Scobee kicked a 41-yarder. The second went nine plays, with a 31-yard catch by Marcedes Lewis and a 17-yarder by Cecil Shorts on 12 Ace Shift Empty Spacing, and Scobee made another 41-yarder. The third ended with four seconds left in the quarter. Adam Thielen caught a 17-yard Snag, then Maurice Jones-Drew caught a 15-yard touchdown on 12 Trey Right Stick with the H chip-release tag. It was 13-0.

**Second quarter.** San Diego answered with a six-play drive: five runs gained nothing, then Philip Rivers threw an 80-yard touchdown to Ryan Mathews. Jacksonville then drove 85 yards in 12 plays. Lewis gained 21 on a chip-release Stick, Jones-Drew 17 on Sprint Flood, and Shorts scored on a 24-yard Snag, making it 20-7. Two Eddie Royal catches of 27 and 35 yards set up a 39-yard Nick Novak field goal before halftime.

**Third quarter.** San Diego opened with a touchdown: Royal caught a 26-yarder and Mathews ran it in from three, making it 20-17. Jacksonville's answer used the week's new calls:
- Cousins was sacked on one Split Leak.
- Toney Clemons caught 21 yards on the next one.
- Jones-Drew ran 15 on Counter.
- Jonathan Grimes scored from 17 yards on 12 Wing Right Wham R.

It was 27-17.

**Fourth quarter.**
- Dwight Lowery forced and recovered a fumble after a Malcom Floyd catch early in the quarter.
- Jacksonville punted, then later drove 12 plays for Scobee's 43-yard field goal, making it 30-17 with 6:32 left.
- San Diego scored on a two-play drive after the kickoff; Ronnie Brown ran it in from the one. The ledger cannot reconcile that drive with the kickoff return before it (see section 8).
- Kendall Reyes sacked Cousins twice on Jacksonville's next possession, forcing a punt.
- San Diego's last drive moved 31 yards in eight plays before time ran out.

**The numbers.**

- San Diego ran 22 times for 34 yards, and Floyd caught six passes for 30.
