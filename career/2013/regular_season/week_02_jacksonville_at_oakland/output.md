## 2. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context:** Oakland lost 34-14 at Indianapolis in Week 1. Indianapolis ran for 179 yards and converted 12 of 21 third downs, but Oakland still sacked Andrew Luck four times, with four different players, and took the ball away zero times. Terrelle Pryor threw for 148 yards with two interceptions and was sacked five times; Darren McFadden ran 19 times for 80. Stone drew one conclusion from one game: Jacksonville could run on this front, but its pressure could still end a possession.

**Blackmon:** the league's four-game suspension took effect after Week 1. Justin Blackmon moved to Reserve/Suspended for Weeks 2-5; he may attend meetings and use the facility under the league's rules but does not practice. Stone has also set him game-day inactive for Weeks 6 and 7, as his own availability decision. The public statement: Blackmon is unavailable, and Jacksonville will announce any change in his playing status. Caldwell kept the open roster spot.

**Stone's priorities:** Keep the run structure that produced 180 yards and avoid another 25-third-down game. More 12 personnel and two-back football, less 10 personnel; the same concepts with the jobs moved among Shorts, Thielen, Clemons and Brown. Empty came off the sheet and Spacing moved to 12 Doubles. Grimes stays RB2, the line stays Monroe-Nwaneri-Meester-Rackley-Johnson, and Ryan Davis is active.

**Practice / teaching emphasis:** Monday and Tuesday studied Oakland's front, pressure and safety response to 12 personnel, and self-scouted both Week 1 interceptions, the three sacks and the Boot Flood read. Wednesday repped the core runs and quick game with the receivers in their new alignments. Thursday paired Power with Counter and Post-Cross, Outside with Boot, and Inside/Bubble with the vertical alert, then ran the first 15 as a mock game. Friday and Saturday reduced.

## 3. Pregame media Q&A

**Reporter:** Is Justin Blackmon with the team this week?

**Stone:** He's unavailable. If his playing status changes, we'll announce it.

**Reporter:** Lane Johnson gave up two sacks in the opener. Any change on the right side?

**Stone:** No. We know why they happened and we worked on it all week. He starts.

**Reporter:** How does the offense change without your leading target from Week 1?

**Stone:** The plays don't change. Who runs them does, and we'll use more two-tight-end sets so we're not leaning on our thinnest position.

### Game narrative: selected highlights

The opening drive reached field-goal range on the run, the last snap a 17-yard Jones-Drew carry on Split Zone, and Scobee made it 3-0. Oakland answered with a 12-snap drive that Pryor finished with a nine-yard touchdown pass to tight end Jeron Mastrud, then added a Sebastian Janikowski field goal after a 41-yard completion to Jacoby Ford.

Down 10-3, Jacksonville went to the 12-personnel Spacing call that replaced Empty on this sheet, and Marcedes Lewis caught it for a 72-yard touchdown. Oakland took the lead back through the half: McFadden ran for 15 and Rashad Jennings scored from the four early in the third quarter. Scobee's second field goal, after Nick Roach sacked Cousins on a Boot Flood look, cut it to 17-13.

The fourth quarter belonged to Jacksonville's defense and went nowhere for its offense. A 13-snap possession that began late in the third quarter included a 19-yard vertical-alert completion to Lewis and ended without points; Charles Woodson's sack on another Boot Flood set it back. Oakland then punted on three straight possessions, one of them after Sen'Derrick Marks sacked Pryor.

Jacksonville's next-to-last drive moved 66 yards on a 24-yard catch by Lewis and a 15-yard Dagger throw to Adam Thielen, and ended in a punt with 3:15 left, still trailing by four. The defense forced another punt. The offense got the ball back with 1:07 to play, and on the first snap Cousins fumbled on a run; Christo Bilukidi forced it and recovered.

The Week 1 corrections held in part. Jacksonville faced 13 third downs instead of 25 and converted five, and Cousins threw no interception (23 of 39, 281 yards, one touchdown). The run game gained 120 yards on 25 carries. Protection did not improve: Oakland sacked Cousins three times, with Lane Johnson charged with two for the second straight week and Uche Nwaneri with one.

## 5. Postgame media Q&A

**Reporter:** You punted down four with a little over three minutes left. Why not go for it?

**Stone:** That was my call. Our defense had stopped them three straight times, and we got the ball back with over a minute to play. Then we put it on the ground on the first snap. That's the play that decided it.

**Reporter:** What happened on the fumble?

**Stone:** I'll look at it on tape before I tell you whose it was. Kirk didn't throw an interception today, and that part of last week got fixed.

**Reporter:** Johnson was charged with two more sacks.

**Stone:** Same issue as last week, and it didn't get fixed. That one's on us as coaches as much as him. We'll work it again.

**Reporter:** Lewis had 150 yards. Is that the plan without Blackmon?

**Stone:** The plan was to use two tight ends and let the coverage decide who gets the ball. Today it was Marcedes.

**Reporter:** Anything on Meester?

**Stone:** He's with the medical staff. We'll know more this week.

**Next-week carry-forward:** Seattle preparation starts with a center decision (Meester is out, with a projected 12-day return) and C.J. Wilson's long absence at defensive end. Blackmon remains on Reserve/Suspended.
