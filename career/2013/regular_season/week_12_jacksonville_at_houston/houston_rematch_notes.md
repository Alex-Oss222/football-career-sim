# Houston rematch notes (recorded after Week 12)

**Recorded:** November 24, 2013, after Houston 38, Jacksonville 6, before the coaches left, as Stone's Week 12 plan directed. **Closed** at recording. These are notes for the Week 14 rematch (Thursday, December 5), not a Week 14 plan.
**Evidence:** the Week 12 full receipt (`stats/game_receipts/week_12_jacksonville_jaguars_at_houston_texans.json`) and the [Week 12 output](output.md). The game record carries Jacksonville's calls but not Houston's scheme labels, so no note names a Houston front, coverage or pressure the record cannot show.

## Tice (offense)

1. **What Houston showed that was new:** the record does not show Houston's calls. It does show where the sacks came from: Watt twice (10 yards on Mesh in the second quarter, 3 on Snag in the fourth) and Mays once on TE Delay.
2. **What Jacksonville forced Houston to change:** nothing the record can show. When Jacksonville ran, it gained 7.2 a carry for Jones-Drew, including 25 and 34 on Split in the third quarter.
3. **What Houston took away:** the finish. Four drives reached the Houston 24 or closer and produced a field goal, two turnovers on downs and Reed's interception on Snag. Shorts drew 17 targets for 61 yards; the passing game averaged 4.2 yards an attempt.
4. **What Jacksonville would alter first:** the run-pass balance once behind (17 runs against 45 dropbacks), and the red-zone and fringe menu that failed on all four trips.

## Crennel (defense)

1. **What Houston showed that was new:** Andre Johnson as the finisher: 8 catches for 134 and three touchdowns, including a 73-yarder on the first snap after a turnover on downs.
2. **What Jacksonville forced Houston to change:** only its first possession (Mincey's 10-yard stop on Foster and Marks's sack forced a punt from the 16) and Grimes's interception on Houston's first second-half series.
3. **What Houston took away:** the pass rush (one sack) and third down (Houston converted 6 of 11). Foster carried 29 times for 133 and caught 3 for 52.
4. **What Jacksonville would alter first:** where the help goes against Johnson, and the fit against Foster on the long runs (30, 14 and 14 yards).

## Lowry (special teams)

1. **What Houston showed that was new:** Keshawn Martin's 26-yard punt return, which set Houston up at the Jacksonville 43 before a touchdown.
2. **What Jacksonville forced Houston to change:** nothing the record shows. Every Houston kickoff was returned; Anderson's 68-yard return set up a start at the Houston 35.
3. **What Houston took away:** field position after two punts. The 26-yard return and a punt from the Houston 49 downed at the line of scrimmage both preceded Houston touchdown drives.
4. **What Jacksonville would alter first:** punt coverage lanes and plus-territory punt execution; Rutland's teams load is reviewed against his first-reserve corner role.
