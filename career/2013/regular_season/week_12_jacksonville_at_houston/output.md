### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** Jacksonville went three-and-out to start, then Jeremy Mincey dropped Arian Foster for a 10-yard loss and Sen'Derrick Marks sacked Matt Schaub to force a Houston punt from its 16. Jacksonville took over at the Houston 49; Shorts caught 15 on Snag and Scobee kicked a 49-yard field goal, 3-0. Houston answered with a 12-play, 80-yard drive on which Foster touched the ball six times, and Owen Daniels caught an 8-yard touchdown with 14 seconds left in the quarter. Jacksonville's reply ran 62 yards: Marcedes Lewis caught 23 on Sprint Flood, Jones-Drew ran 10 on 6OL Counter and C.J. Anderson 10 on 6OL Power, but stalled at the 15 and Scobee kicked from 33, 7-6. J.J. Watt sacked Cousins for 10 yards on the next series; Keshawn Martin returned the punt 26 yards to the Jacksonville 43, and Foster scored from a yard on the sixth play, 14-6. Houston's last first-half drive reached the Jacksonville 30 and ran out of time.

**Second half.** Brent Grimes intercepted Schaub on Houston's third snap and returned it 26 yards to the Jacksonville 46, but the drive gained 5 yards and Anger's punt from the Houston 49 was downed at the line of scrimmage. Houston went 51 yards in nine plays, and Andre Johnson caught an 8-yard touchdown, 21-6. C.J. Anderson returned the kickoff 68 yards to the Houston 35; Jacksonville threw on all nine snaps, reached the 23 and turned it over on downs on fourth-and-4. On the next snap Johnson caught a 73-yard pass to the Jacksonville 4 and then a 4-yard touchdown, 28-6. Jones-Drew ran 25 and 34 yards on Split to the Houston 22, and on the next snap Brooks Reed intercepted Snag intended for Shorts and returned it 46 yards. Houston then ran seven times in a nine-play drive, and Johnson caught his third touchdown on the last snap, 35-6. Randy Bullock added a 32-yard field goal. Jacksonville's last two drives ended on downs at the Houston 24 and on Johnathan Joseph's interception.

**The numbers.**
- Cousins completed 25 of 42 for 176 yards with two interceptions and was sacked three times (Watt two, Joe Mays). Shorts caught 10 of 17 targets for 61 yards.
- Jones-Drew ran 12 times for 86 yards; Jacksonville ran 17 times in all and threw 45 times (including sacks).
- Jacksonville reached the Houston 24 or closer four times and scored no touchdown: a field goal, two turnovers on downs and an interception.
- Foster ran 29 times for 133 yards and a touchdown and caught 3 for 52. Andre Johnson caught 8 for 134 and three touchdowns. Schaub was 20 of 31 for 271, four touchdowns and one interception.
- Houston converted 6 of 11 third downs; Jacksonville 4 of 14.
