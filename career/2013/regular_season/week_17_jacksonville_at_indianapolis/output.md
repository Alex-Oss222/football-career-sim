### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First half.** Pat McAfee pinned Jacksonville at its own 5 on the game's first punt, and Jacksonville's 12-play reply ended in a punt from its 42. Chris Prosinski's 12-yard punt return set Jacksonville up at the Indianapolis 40, and Scobee kicked a 45-yard field goal. On Indianapolis's next snap Sen'Derrick Marks sacked Andrew Luck and forced a fumble at the Colts 8, but Jacksonville lost a yard and settled for a 26-yard field goal, 6-0. Adam Vinatieri kicked a 32-yarder after Reggie Wayne caught 24. Jacksonville then went 83 yards in eight plays: Toney Clemons caught 29 on Mesh, Montell Owens 19 on 22 Heavy Snag, Justin Blackmon 29 on Spacing, and Jonathan Grimes a 6-yard touchdown on Spacing, 13-3. Adam Thielen caught 20 to set up Scobee's 31-yarder on the last play of the half, 16-3.

**Second half.** The third quarter belonged to the defenses. Kelvin Sheppard and Jerrell Freeman sacked Cousins, and Vinatieri's 30-yard field goal made it 16-6. Scobee's 28-yarder, after catches by Shorts (18 on Power Pass) and Jones-Drew (19), made it 19-6 with 8:13 left. Luck answered in seven plays, with catches of 26, 15 and 25 before Wayne's 12-yard touchdown, 19-13. Shorts caught 28 on Texas and Scobee kicked his fifth field goal, from 27, 22-13 with 2:23 left. Luck then drove Indianapolis 71 yards in ten plays, throwing to Wayne on six of them, and Vinatieri kicked a 28-yarder with 22 seconds left, 22-16.

**The finish.** Jacksonville took the touchback, ran three times for 6 yards and punted from its own 26 with 3 seconds left. T.Y. Hilton returned the punt 26 yards to the Jacksonville 43. Luck completed 36 yards to Dwayne Allen and then a 7-yard touchdown to Wayne as time expired, and Vinatieri's extra point won it, 23-22.

**The numbers.**
- Jacksonville ran 32 times against 38 dropbacks and committed no turnover. It held the ball 36:35 and converted 7 of 17 third downs, but reached the end zone once: Scobee made all five field goals (45, 26, 31, 28, 27).
- Cousins completed 24 of 35 for 278 yards and a touchdown with no interception; sacked three times (Freeman, Sheppard, Cam Johnson). Jones-Drew ran 21 times for 83.
- Indianapolis ran 19 times for 49 yards; Ballard had 12 carries for 48. Luck completed 28 of 38 for 308 yards and two touchdowns; Wayne caught 11 of 16 targets for 114 and both touchdowns.
- Marks had the strip-sack; Daryl Smith led with 8 tackles.
