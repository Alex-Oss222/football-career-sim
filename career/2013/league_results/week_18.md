## Dallas 40, New Orleans 10

The first quarter produced three lost fumbles and a 52-yard Garrett Hartley field goal. Dallas then scored on four of its next five possessions: a 12-play, 76-yard touchdown drive, Dan Bailey field goals from 42 and 45, and an 11-play, 68-yard touchdown drive for 20-3. In the fourth quarter Morris Claiborne's interception set up Bailey's 19-yarder, and DeMarcus Ware returned an interception of Drew Brees 86 yards to the New Orleans 23 to set up another touchdown. A lost Saints fumble led to one more Dallas score for 37-3 before Benjamin Watson caught Brees's touchdown pass, and Bailey's fourth field goal closed it. DeMarco Murray ran for 130 yards and a touchdown but lost two fumbles, both forced and recovered by Curtis Lofton. Tony Romo, Lance Dunbar and Miles Austin accounted for the other Dallas touchdowns, and Brees was sacked three times.

**Standouts:** DeMarco Murray (28 carries, 130 yards, TD); DeMarcus Ware (86-yard interception return); Dan Bailey (4 of 4 field goals).

## Buffalo 33, Pittsburgh 30 (OT)

Pittsburgh led 13-3 at the half on a 71-yard touchdown drive and two Shaun Suisham field goals. Buffalo then scored touchdowns on three of its four second-half drives, of 80, 57 and 80 yards, for 24-13. Pittsburgh answered with an 80-yard touchdown drive. After Manny Lawson and Cortez Allen traded interceptions, the Steelers went 45 yards for a 27-24 lead with 2:13 left. Dan Carpenter's 41-yard field goal on the last play of regulation tied it. In overtime Suisham's 25-yarder ended Pittsburgh's 13-play opening possession, and Carpenter matched it from 27. Suisham then missed from 48, and Carpenter's 32-yarder won it 40 seconds into the second overtime period. E.J. Manuel threw for 415 yards and three touchdowns, two to Steve Johnson (10 catches, 166 yards). Ben Roethlisberger completed 43 of 60 for 408 yards; Heath Miller caught both of his touchdown passes, and Emmanuel Sanders caught 16 passes.

**Standouts:** Steve Johnson (10-166, 2 TD); Dan Carpenter (4 of 4 field goals, including the tying and winning kicks); E.J. Manuel (30-52, 415 yards, 3 TD).

## Philadelphia 30, Tampa Bay 20

Tampa Bay led 13-0 early in the second quarter after a short-field touchdown and two field goals set up by Lavonte David's and Leonard Johnson's interceptions of Michael Vick. Philadelphia answered with a 12-play, 78-yard touchdown drive and a 14-play drive that ended in a 20-yard field goal at the half, 13-10. A 68-yard touchdown drive and a field goal made it 20-13, and Tampa Bay tied it after a long kickoff return, but the Eagles' 80-yard touchdown drive and a 21-yard field goal made it 30-20 with 9:01 left. Cary Williams then intercepted Josh Freeman near the Philadelphia 10 and returned it 86 yards. The Eagles lost a fumble on the ensuing possession, and Nate Allen's interception ended Tampa Bay's next one. LeSean McCoy ran for 125 yards and a touchdown and caught a touchdown pass among 6 catches for 96. Fletcher Cox had two sacks.

**Standouts:** LeSean McCoy (30 carries, 125 yards, TD; 6-96, TD); Cary Williams (86-yard interception return); Fletcher Cox (2 sacks).
