## AFC Championship: Buffalo 34, Tennessee 3

Buffalo, without E.J. Manuel (Divisional injury), started Jeff Tuel and scored on its first possession, an eight-play, 80-yard touchdown drive. Justin Rogers intercepted Jake Locker to set up a nine-play touchdown drive for 14-0 after one quarter. Dan Carpenter's 29-yard field goal and Rob Bironas's 54-yarder on the last play of the half made it 17-3. A three-play, 52-yard touchdown drive and Carpenter's 30-yarder in the third quarter put it away, and a 76-yard drive late in the fourth made it 34-3. C.J. Spiller ran 27 times for 214 yards and a touchdown, and Buffalo ran for 236 without a turnover. Tuel completed 23 of 32 for 207 yards and two touchdowns, to Steve Johnson and Trevor Graham. Kyle Williams had three of Buffalo's five sacks.

**Standouts:** C.J. Spiller (27 carries, 214 yards, TD); Kyle Williams (3 sacks); Jeff Tuel (23-32, 207 yards, 2 TD, no interception).

## NFC Championship: Minnesota 20, Philadelphia 7

Minnesota opened with a 17-play, 83-yard touchdown drive that used 7:30, and Philadelphia answered with a 16-play, 81-yard drive, DeSean Jackson catching Michael Vick's touchdown pass, 7-7. An interception of Vick set up Blair Walsh's 36-yard field goal, and an eight-play, 55-yard drive made it 17-7 at the half. In the second half Philadelphia reached the Minnesota 16, 20 and 8 and came away with nothing: a turnover on downs, a lost fumble (Marvin Mitchell forced and recovered it) and an interception. Walsh's 43-yard field goal after the first of them made it 20-7, and a last Eagles drive ended on downs at the Minnesota 41. Christian Ponder completed 20 of 28 for 195 yards, threw a touchdown pass to Kyle Rudolph and ran for another. Adrian Peterson ran 22 times for 76 and caught four passes for 52. LeSean McCoy ran 21 times for 101, and Jackson caught 11 passes.

**Standouts:** Christian Ponder (20-28, 195 yards, TD; rushing TD); Chad Greenway and Josh Robinson (an interception each); Marvin Mitchell (forced fumble and recovery in the red zone).
