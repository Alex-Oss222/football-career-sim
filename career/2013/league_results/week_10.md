## Minnesota 16, Washington 13, overtime (Thursday)

Washington turned the ball over on downs on each of its first two possessions, the first at the Minnesota 17, and Minnesota answered it with an 83-yard touchdown drive capped by Jarius Wright's catch. Blair Walsh's 47-yard field goal made it 10-0, Kai Forbath answered from 35, Walsh missed from 45 before halftime, and Josh Morgan's touchdown catch tied it 10-10 in the third quarter. After E.J. Biggers intercepted Christian Ponder in the fourth, Walsh's 33-yarder gave Minnesota a 13-10 lead with 1:05 left, Forbath tied it from 43 on the last play of regulation, and Walsh's 20-yard field goal ended a 77-yard drive on Minnesota's first overtime possession. Robert Griffin completed 35 of 51 passes for 339 yards but was sacked seven times, and Greg Jennings caught nine passes for 154 yards.

**Standouts:** Chad Greenway (2 sacks, 13 tackles); Greg Jennings (9-154); Pierre Garcon (8-117).

## Pittsburgh 17, Buffalo 14

Troy Polamalu intercepted E.J. Manuel on Buffalo's first possession, and Pittsburgh scored first on a 59-yard drive. Buffalo tied it 7-7 on an 80-yard drive, then Cameron Heyward forced and recovered a Manuel fumble at the Buffalo 12, and Pittsburgh scored on a 3-play drive for a 14-7 halftime lead. Buffalo tied it again at 14-14 with 7:35 left on an 89-yard, 12-play drive, and Pittsburgh answered with an 11-play drive that ended in Shaun Suisham's 42-yard field goal as time expired. Ben Roethlisberger threw touchdown passes to Emmanuel Sanders and Isaac Redman, and C.J. Spiller ran for 95 yards and caught one of Manuel's two touchdown passes, Steve Johnson catching the other.

**Standouts:** Emmanuel Sanders (10-88, TD); C.J. Spiller (95 rushing yards; 8-44, TD); Cameron Heyward (sack, forced fumble, fumble recovery).

## Baltimore 17, Cincinnati 10

The first quarter was scoreless, and Cincinnati turned the ball over on downs on its second and third possessions; Baltimore went 67 yards for a touchdown after the second. Haloti Ngata and Jimmy Smith each forced and recovered a fumble, from Giovani Bernard and Ryan Whalen, and the first, at the Cincinnati 13, led to Justin Tucker's 35-yard field goal on the last possession of the half. Mike Nugent's 32-yarder made it 10-3, and Lardarius Webb's 37-yard interception return set up a one-play touchdown drive from the Cincinnati 6 for a 17-3 lead. BenJarvus Green-Ellis ran for Cincinnati's touchdown at the end of a 91-yard drive with 1:18 left, and the Bengals' last possession ended on downs at their own 40. Andy Dalton threw for 260 yards without a touchdown, while Joe Flacco threw for 105 and Ray Rice scored both rushing and receiving.

**Standouts:** Lardarius Webb (interception, 37-yard return); Ray Rice (53 rushing yards, TD; 2-6, TD); Josh Bynes (sack, 10 tackles).

## Detroit 38, Chicago 30

The teams traded the lead through the first half, which ended 17-17 on Robbie Gould's 25-yard field goal; Gould had also missed from 51. Glover Quin and Louis Delmas each intercepted Jay Cutler in the second half, and Detroit scored a touchdown on a 5-play drive from the Chicago 23 after the second for a 31-20 lead. Chicago got within 31-27 with 7:26 left, Detroit answered with an 84-yard, 14-play touchdown drive with 1:29 left, and Gould's 41-yarder with 13 seconds left made it 38-30. Reggie Bush ran for 114 yards and scored rushing and receiving, though Shea McClellin forced and recovered his fumble, Joique Bell ran for two touchdowns, and Matthew Stafford threw touchdown passes to Calvin Johnson and Bush. Cutler threw for 394 yards and three touchdowns, to Earl Bennett, Matt Forte and Tony Fiammetta, and Brandon Marshall caught 13 of 16 passes thrown his way for 179 yards.

**Standouts:** Reggie Bush (114 rushing yards, TD; 2-3, TD); Brandon Marshall (13-179); Calvin Johnson (12-117, TD).

## New York Giants 30, Oakland 17

Charles Woodson intercepted Eli Manning on the Giants' first possession, but Mathias Kiwanuka forced and recovered a Rod Streater fumble on Oakland's next possession, and the Giants scored from the Oakland 30. Oakland tied it on an 82-yard drive and led 10-7 on Sebastian Janikowski's 46-yard field goal, then the Giants scored the next 23 points: two touchdowns around Josh Brown's 31-yard field goal at the end of the half, then field goals of 35 and 32 yards by Brown, each after an interception of Terrelle Pryor. Antrel Rolle and Prince Amukamara intercepted Pryor, the second ending an Oakland drive at the Giants 16, and Usama Young intercepted Manning. Manning and Henry Hynoski ran for touchdowns and Victor Cruz caught Manning's touchdown pass; Denarius Moore's 66-yard touchdown catch on a one-play drive made it 30-17 with 2:07 left.

**Standouts:** Josh Brown (3 of 3 field goals); Victor Cruz (7-82, TD); Denarius Moore (7-105, TD).

## Green Bay 10, Philadelphia 6

Brandon Graham forced and recovered a John Kuhn fumble on Green Bay's first possession, setting up Alex Henery's 45-yard field goal, and the score stayed 3-0 until Mason Crosby's 20-yarder on the last possession of the half ended a 14-play drive. Green Bay opened the second half with an 80-yard touchdown drive, Eddie Lacy scoring, and Henery's 48-yarder made it 10-6. Philadelphia turned the ball over three times in the second half: Brad Jones and Tramon Williams each forced and recovered a fumble, from Brent Celek and Bryce Brown, Williams also intercepted Michael Vick, and the Eagles' last possession ended in a lost fumble with 52 seconds left. Philadelphia converted two of 13 third downs.

**Standouts:** Tramon Williams (interception, forced fumble, fumble recovery); Eddie Lacy (98 rushing yards, TD); Clay Matthews (sack, 10 tackles).

## Atlanta 28, Seattle 12

Stephen Hauschka kicked field goals of 35, 30, 51 and 39 yards, and Seattle never reached the end zone. Atlanta turned the ball over on downs at the Seattle 18 on its first possession and trailed 3-0, then scored touchdowns on drives of 80, 81 and 90 yards, the last opening the second half, for a 21-9 lead. Tony McDaniel forced and recovered a Steven Jackson fumble, and Hauschka's fourth field goal made it 21-12 with 7:32 left, but Atlanta scored again with 30 seconds left. Matt Ryan completed 14 of 20 passes with touchdowns to Bradie Ewing, Tony Gonzalez and Harry Douglas, whose only catch was a 7-yard score; Jacquizz Rodgers ran for the other touchdown and Jackson ran for 134 yards.

**Standouts:** Steven Jackson (134 rushing yards); Matt Ryan (14-20, 179 yards, 3 TD); Stephen Hauschka (4 of 4 field goals).

## St. Louis 23, Indianapolis 10

Jerrell Freeman forced and recovered a Daryl Richardson fumble at the Indianapolis 20 on St. Louis's first possession, and Greg Zuerlein's 23-yard field goal opened the scoring. LaRon Landry returned an interception of Sam Bradford 49 yards to the St. Louis 2, and Andrew Luck threw a 2-yard touchdown pass to Reggie Wayne on the next play for a 7-3 lead; Zuerlein's 20-yarder on the last possession of the half made it 7-6. St. Louis opened the second half with an 83-yard touchdown drive, and Vick Ballard lost two fumbles in the third quarter, to Cortland Finnegan and Michael Brockers, the second leading to a touchdown from the Indianapolis 25 for a 20-7 lead. Adam Vinatieri kicked a 53-yard field goal and Rodney McLeod intercepted Luck. Bradford threw touchdown passes to Chris Givens and Stedman Bailey, Richardson ran 31 times for 148 yards, and St. Louis held the ball for 38:34.

**Standouts:** Daryl Richardson (148 rushing yards); Chris Givens (8-106, TD); Jerrell Freeman (15 tackles, sack, forced fumble, fumble recovery).

## Carolina 19, San Francisco 9

Graham Gano kicked field goals of 36 and 41 yards for a 6-2 lead, and Phil Dawson missed from 42 on the last possession of the half. Frank Gore's touchdown catch put San Francisco ahead 9-6 in the third quarter, Gano's 47-yarder tied it, and DeAngelo Williams's touchdown run ended an 80-yard drive for a 16-9 lead early in the fourth. Gano's fourth field goal, from 27, made it 19-9 with 3:51 left, and San Francisco turned the ball over on downs on its next possession. Williams ran 28 times for 104 yards and Carolina held the ball for 36:36.

**Standouts:** Graham Gano (4 of 4 field goals); DeAngelo Williams (104 rushing yards, TD; 3-41); Tarell Brown (interception, 6 passes defended).

## San Diego 28, Denver 10

Denver led 3-0 on Matt Prater's 47-yard field goal and 10-7 on Demaryius Thomas's touchdown catch, and San Diego scored touchdowns on its first three possessions, the third two plays after an interception of Peyton Manning, for a 21-10 lead. Prater missed from 54 on the last possession of the half, and San Diego's 52-yard, 11-play touchdown drive made it 28-10 in the third quarter. Derek Cox and Shareece Wright intercepted Manning and Rahim Moore intercepted Philip Rivers; Denver's last drive reached the San Diego 19 and ended on downs. Ryan Mathews ran for 121 yards and caught two passes, for touchdowns of 22 and 6 yards, and Rivers and Ronnie Brown ran for the others.

**Standouts:** Ryan Mathews (121 rushing yards; 2-28, 2 TD); Philip Rivers (15-22, 173 yards, 2 TD; rushing TD); Jarret Johnson (14 tackles).

## Arizona 33, Houston 17

Arizona scored on its first two possessions, a Jay Feely field goal and a 15-play touchdown drive, and led 17-7 at halftime after scoring a touchdown with 13 seconds left; Shiloh Keo had forced and recovered a Rashard Mendenhall fumble at the Houston 16 in between. Houston got within 27-17 early in the fourth quarter on an 84-yard, 12-play touchdown drive, then Feely kicked his third and fourth field goals and Houston turned the ball over on downs on each of its last two possessions. Mendenhall ran 25 times for 174 yards, Carson Palmer threw two touchdown passes to Rob Housler and ran for another, and Arizona ran for 218 yards. Arian Foster ran for 103 yards and a touchdown, and Greg Jones caught a 7-yard touchdown pass, his only catch.

## New Orleans 20, Dallas 3

Dallas opened with a 13-play drive that ended in Dan Bailey's 45-yard field goal and did not score again. New Orleans turned the ball over on downs on its first possession and lost a Luke McCown fumble to Jason Hatcher on its second, then took the lead with a 64-yard touchdown drive. Garrett Hartley kicked field goals of 40 and 51 yards, and an 80-yard, 5-play touchdown drive made it 20-3 in the third quarter. Pierre Thomas ran for 118 yards and a touchdown and Marques Colston caught McCown's touchdown pass, while Dallas threw for 78 yards, converted one of 12 third downs and punted eight times.

**Standouts:** Pierre Thomas (118 rushing yards, TD); Marques Colston (7-93, TD); Brodrick Bunkley (sack, 9 tackles).

## Tampa Bay 37, Miami 21 (Monday)

Tampa Bay led 23-0 at halftime: Rian Lindell kicked field goals of 38, 22 and 19 yards, and Dekoda Watson and Mark Barron each forced and recovered a fumble, from Lamar Miller and Dion Sims, the second leading to a 60-yard touchdown drive. Miami opened the second half with an 83-yard touchdown drive, Tampa Bay answered with an 80-yard one, and after Akeem Spence intercepted Ryan Tannehill and Lindell missed from 34, Mike Gillislee ran 76 yards for a touchdown on the next snap to make it 30-14. Tampa Bay's fourth touchdown made it 37-14, Miami turned the ball over on downs at the Tampa Bay 5, and Miami's last touchdown came with 1:56 left. Brian Leonard ran for two touchdowns, Josh Freeman threw touchdown passes to Kevin Ogletree and Tom Crabtree and ran for 56 yards, and Tannehill threw touchdown passes to Charles Clay and Mike Wallace.

**Standouts:** Josh Freeman (22-35, 243 yards, 2 TD; 56 rushing yards); Brian Leonard (35 rushing yards, 2 TD); Mark Barron (forced fumble, fumble recovery, 11 tackles).
