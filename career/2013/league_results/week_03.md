## Kansas City 16, Philadelphia 9

Justin Houston sacked Michael Vick three times, and Ryan Succop's three field goals plus a Jamaal Charles touchdown were enough. Vick threw for 291 yards without a turnover, but Philadelphia's only points were three Alex Henery field goals.

**Standouts:** Justin Houston (3 sacks); DeSean Jackson (10-118); Ryan Succop (3 of 3 field goals).

## New Orleans 20, Arizona 16

Arizona outgained New Orleans 442 yards to 220 and settled for three Jay Feely field goals. Drew Brees threw for 125 yards, and Pierre Thomas ran for 74 and a touchdown; Malcolm Jenkins intercepted Carson Palmer.

**Standouts:** Rashard Mendenhall (102 rushing yards); Pierre Thomas (74 rushing yards, TD; 3-42); Malcolm Jenkins (interception).

## Minnesota 27, Cleveland 17

Christian Ponder completed 38 of 50 for 238 yards and three touchdowns, 14 of the completions to Greg Jennings. Adrian Peterson ran for 111. Cleveland lost a fumble and an interception.

**Standouts:** Greg Jennings (14-102, TD); Christian Ponder (38-50, 3 TD); Adrian Peterson (111 rushing yards).

## Detroit 38, Washington 28

Matthew Stafford threw three touchdown passes, one each to Calvin Johnson and Reggie Bush, and Joique Bell and Bush also scored on the ground. Roy Helu ran for two Washington touchdowns and Ryan Kerrigan had two sacks.

**Standouts:** Matthew Stafford (28-44, 204 yards, 3 TD); Reggie Bush (62 rushing yards, TD; 7-53, TD); Ryan Kerrigan (2 sacks).

## Green Bay 24, Cincinnati 20

James Starks ran for two touchdowns and Eddie Lacy for 104 yards. Aaron Rodgers threw for 294; B.J. Raji sacked Andy Dalton twice, and Morgan Burnett intercepted him.

**Standouts:** Eddie Lacy (104 rushing yards); B.J. Raji (2 sacks); Randall Cobb (8-79).

## Baltimore 27, Houston 24

Michael Huff intercepted Matt Schaub twice and James Ihedigbo once, and Houston turned it over four times. Andre Johnson caught two touchdowns among nine catches for 103 yards; Torrey Smith had 122 receiving yards for Baltimore.

**Standouts:** Michael Huff (2 interceptions); Torrey Smith (8-122); Andre Johnson (9-103, 2 TD).

## New York Giants 31, Carolina 17

David Wilson ran for four touchdowns and 87 yards, and Antrel Rolle intercepted Derek Anderson twice. Victor Cruz caught nine passes for 145 yards. Anderson threw for 311 yards and two touchdowns but turned the ball over three times.

**Standouts:** David Wilson (87 rushing yards, 4 TD); Antrel Rolle (2 interceptions); Victor Cruz (9-145).

## San Diego 27, Tennessee 20

Philip Rivers threw three interceptions, two to Bernard Pollard, and San Diego still won behind Ryan Mathews' 97 rushing yards and a touchdown and a 101-yard day from Malcom Floyd. Tennessee's first loss.

**Standouts:** Ryan Mathews (97 rushing yards, TD; 6-60); Malcom Floyd (5-101, TD); Bernard Pollard (2 interceptions).

## Dallas 23, St. Louis 20

Dan Bailey's three field goals decided it. Tony Romo threw two touchdown passes, one to Jason Witten; Sam Bradford threw two for St. Louis, to Brian Quick and Chris Givens.

**Standouts:** Dan Bailey (3 of 3 field goals); Brian Quick (8-75, TD); Tony Romo (21-32, 206 yards, 2 TD).

## New England 24, Tampa Bay 10

Stevan Ridley ran for two touchdowns and Shane Vereen for one. New England intercepted Josh Freeman twice, through Devin McCourty and Jake Bequette.

**Standouts:** Stevan Ridley (72 rushing yards, 2 TD); Devin McCourty (interception); Vincent Jackson (6-64).

## Atlanta 31, Miami 13

Matt Ryan threw three touchdown passes and ran for another. Lamar Miller ran for 109 yards in the loss, and Dannell Ellerbe forced and recovered a fumble.

**Standouts:** Matt Ryan (286 yards, 3 TD; rushing TD); Roddy White (5-78); Lamar Miller (109 rushing yards).

## Buffalo 31, New York Jets 24

C.J. Spiller ran for two touchdowns and caught eight passes, 173 yards from scrimmage in all, and E.J. Manuel threw two touchdowns. Muhammad Wilkerson sacked Manuel twice.

**Standouts:** C.J. Spiller (96 rushing yards, 2 TD; 8-77); E.J. Manuel (30-43, 2 TD); Muhammad Wilkerson (2 sacks).

## Indianapolis 20, San Francisco 9

Indianapolis sacked Colin Kaepernick five times, two by Cory Redding, and intercepted him twice. Phil Dawson's three field goals were San Francisco's only points, and Andrew Luck threw two touchdown passes despite three Indianapolis turnovers.

**Standouts:** Cory Redding (2 sacks); Andrew Luck (22-32, 2 TD); Navorro Bowman (interception, forced fumble, fumble recovery).

## Chicago 38, Pittsburgh 20

Brandon Marshall caught two of Jay Cutler's three touchdown passes and finished with 115 yards. Cutler also threw three interceptions, and Matt Forte ran for 100 yards and a touchdown.

**Standouts:** Brandon Marshall (8-115, 2 TD); Matt Forte (100 rushing yards, TD); Jay Cutler (3 TD, 3 INT).

## Denver 26, Oakland 13

Matt Prater kicked four field goals and Peyton Manning threw for 321 yards. With Sebastian Janikowski still on a medical hold, punter Marquette King kicked both of Oakland's field goals. Pat Sims sacked Manning twice.

**Standouts:** Peyton Manning (28-39, 321 yards, 2 TD); Wes Welker (9-107); Matt Prater (4 of 4 field goals).
