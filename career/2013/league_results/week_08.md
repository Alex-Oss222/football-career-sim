## Carolina 26, Tampa Bay 23 (Thursday)

Carolina lost a fumble on its first snap, when Dashon Goldson forced and recovered one from Kenjon Barner, and trailed 17-3 in the second quarter. Cam Newton threw touchdown passes of 89 yards to Armanti Edwards and 8 yards to Ted Ginn, each receiver's only catch, and Graham Gano kicked four field goals, the last from 48 yards with 1:49 left for a 26-20 lead. Rian Lindell's 40-yard field goal on the final play cut the margin to three. Doug Martin ran for 126 yards and caught a touchdown pass, and Steve Smith caught seven passes for 115 yards.

**Standouts:** Luke Kuechly (16 tackles, 3 tackles for loss); Graham Gano (4 of 4 field goals); Doug Martin (126 rushing yards; 4-39, TD).

## New Orleans 45, Buffalo 31

Luke McCown threw for 407 yards and five touchdowns, each to a different receiver, and Pierre Thomas ran for 104 yards and scored both rushing and receiving. New Orleans led 42-17 late in the third quarter; Buffalo scored the next two touchdowns to get within 42-31 before a 15-play drive ended in Garrett Hartley's 23-yard field goal with 1:42 left. Marques Colston caught 10 passes for 137 yards. Steve Johnson caught both of E.J. Manuel's touchdown passes, Mario Williams had two sacks, and Kyle Williams had a sack and Buffalo's only interception.

**Standouts:** Luke McCown (30-44, 407 yards, 5 TD); Marques Colston (10-137); Pierre Thomas (104 rushing yards, TD; 2-28, TD).

## Kansas City 23, Cleveland 13

Mike Devito had three of Kansas City's four sacks of Brandon Weeden, who threw for 113 yards. Alex Smith threw touchdown passes to Dwayne Bowe and Anthony Fasano, Jamaal Charles ran for 115 yards and Ryan Succop kicked three field goals, the longest from 49. Cleveland trailed 23-6 until Davone Bess's touchdown catch with 32 seconds left, after Billy Cundiff had missed from 45; Joe Haden intercepted Alex Smith, and Sean Smith intercepted Weeden.

**Standouts:** Mike Devito (3 sacks); Dwayne Bowe (8-93, TD); Jamaal Charles (115 rushing yards).

## Detroit 35, Dallas 10

Detroit led 21-0 in the second quarter and 28-7 at halftime. Reggie Bush ran for two touchdowns, and Matthew Stafford threw three, to Calvin Johnson, Nate Burleson and Joique Bell, and ran for 72 yards on four carries. David Akers missed both of his field-goal tries, from 53 and 43 yards. Miles Austin caught 10 passes for 71 yards and Dallas's touchdown, and Brandon Carr intercepted Stafford.

**Standouts:** Reggie Bush (56 rushing yards, 2 TD); Matthew Stafford (27-45, 243 yards, 3 TD; 72 rushing yards); Nate Burleson (8-97, TD).

## New England 28, Miami 13

Miami scored on its opening drive, capped by Lamar Miller's touchdown catch, then managed only two Caleb Sturgis field goals. New England's four touchdowns included Tom Brady passes to Julian Edelman, Shane Vereen and Stevan Ridley, and Ridley also ran eight times for 105 yards and a touchdown. Each quarterback threw two interceptions: Cameron Wake and Reshad Jones picked off Brady, and Alfonzo Dennard and Steve Gregory picked off Ryan Tannehill. Miller ran 27 times for 105 yards, and Miami held the ball for 36:02.

**Standouts:** Stevan Ridley (105 rushing yards, TD; 2-38, TD); Julian Edelman (7-88, TD); Lamar Miller (105 rushing yards; 4-16, TD).

## New York Giants 30, Philadelphia 20

The Giants ran for 268 yards. David Wilson carried 28 times for 149 yards and two touchdowns but lost two fumbles, both forced and recovered by Nate Allen, and Henry Hynoski ran for the other touchdown. Josh Brown kicked three field goals, one from 53 yards, and New York led 23-3 in the third quarter. LeSean McCoy and Bryce Brown ran for Philadelphia's touchdowns, and DeMeco Ryans had a sack and 13 tackles.

**Standouts:** David Wilson (149 rushing yards, 2 TD); Nate Allen (2 forced fumbles, 2 fumble recoveries, 11 tackles); Josh Brown (3 of 3 field goals).

## New York Jets 40, Cincinnati 34

The Jets won in overtime on a touchdown with 59 seconds left in the extra period, after Nick Folk had missed from 42 yards and Terence Newman had intercepted Geno Smith earlier in overtime. New York led 34-24 in the fourth quarter before Cincinnati tied it on a touchdown and Mike Nugent's 55-yard field goal with 36 seconds left in regulation. Smith threw for 298 yards and four touchdowns, two to Santonio Holmes, who caught seven passes for 145 yards. Andy Dalton threw for 357 yards and three touchdowns without an interception, and BenJarvus Green-Ellis ran 36 times for 120 yards.

**Standouts:** Santonio Holmes (7-145, 2 TD); Geno Smith (24-40, 298 yards, 4 TD); BenJarvus Green-Ellis (120 rushing yards; 6-82, TD).

## Pittsburgh 17, Oakland 14

Pittsburgh intercepted Terrelle Pryor four times, three of them in the first quarter, with Chris Carter, Curtis Brown, Lawrence Timmons and Ryan Clark taking one each. The Steelers led only 10-7 after three quarters and went up 17-7 early in the fourth; Isaac Redman and Derek Moye scored their touchdowns, and Shaun Suisham kicked a 48-yard field goal. Darren McFadden ran for both Oakland touchdowns, the second pulling Oakland within 17-14 with 1:53 left, and Pittsburgh's last drive ended in a missed 66-yard field-goal try on the final play. Emmanuel Sanders caught 10 passes for 109 yards but lost a fumble that Jack Crawford forced and recovered.

**Standouts:** Lawrence Timmons (sack, interception, 8 tackles); Emmanuel Sanders (10-109); Darren McFadden (76 rushing yards, 2 TD).

## Arizona 34, Atlanta 19

Rashard Mendenhall ran for 104 yards and two touchdowns and caught Carson Palmer's only touchdown pass, and Stepfan Taylor added 84 rushing yards and a score on six carries as Arizona ran for 242 yards. Matt Bryant kicked four field goals and Julio Jones caught a touchdown pass, pulling Atlanta within 27-19 with 5:09 left, but Matt Ryan was intercepted on the next possession and Arizona scored again. Atlanta turned the ball over three times: Antoine Cason and Patrick Peterson intercepted Ryan, and Darnell Dockett forced and recovered a Roddy White fumble.

**Standouts:** Rashard Mendenhall (104 rushing yards, 2 TD; 2-32, TD); Stepfan Taylor (84 rushing yards, TD); Matt Bryant (4 of 4 field goals).

## Denver 30, Washington 24

Alfred Morris scored all three Washington touchdowns, running for 141 yards and two of them and catching the third, and London Fletcher sacked Peyton Manning three times. Manning completed 19 of 23 passes for 224 yards and two touchdowns, to Ronnie Hillman and Eric Decker, and Hillman ran for 156 yards. The teams traded four touchdowns in the fourth quarter before Matt Prater's third field goal, from 20 yards with 2:51 left, made it 30-24; Washington's final drive ran out of time. Champ Bailey intercepted Robert Griffin.

**Standouts:** Alfred Morris (141 rushing yards, 2 TD; 4-16, TD); Ronnie Hillman (156 rushing yards; 3-46, TD); London Fletcher (3 sacks).

## Minnesota 31, Green Bay 17

Adrian Peterson ran for 104 yards and a touchdown and caught two passes for 92 yards and another. Green Bay tied it 17-17 early in the fourth quarter, Minnesota went back ahead with 8:06 left, and after Eddie Lacy lost a fumble that Marvin Mitchell forced and recovered, Minnesota scored again as time ran out. Christian Ponder threw three touchdown passes without an interception, and Jamarca Sanford intercepted Aaron Rodgers. Jordy Nelson caught six passes for 133 yards for Green Bay.

**Standouts:** Adrian Peterson (104 rushing yards, TD; 2-92, TD); Christian Ponder (16-30, 240 yards, 3 TD); Jordy Nelson (6-133).

## St. Louis 17, Seattle 6 (Monday)

St. Louis sacked Russell Wilson five times, one each by five players, and held Seattle without a touchdown although Seattle outgained the Rams 371 yards to 255. Stephen Hauschka missed from 43 and 41 yards in the first half, which ended 3-3, and Sam Bradford threw third-quarter touchdown passes of 2 yards to Lance Kendricks and 15 yards to Tavon Austin, each receiver's only catch. Janoris Jenkins intercepted Wilson and Will Witherspoon forced and recovered a Sidney Rice fumble; Earl Thomas and Kam Chancellor intercepted Bradford, and Daryl Richardson ran for 102 yards.

**Standouts:** Will Witherspoon (sack, forced fumble, fumble recovery, 10 tackles); Janoris Jenkins (interception, 4 passes defended); Daryl Richardson (102 rushing yards).
