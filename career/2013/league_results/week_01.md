### Baltimore Ravens 27, Denver Broncos 26

**Standouts:**
- Ray Rice — 134 rushing yards and 141 receiving yards.
- Peyton Manning — 239 passing yards.

### New England Patriots 23, Buffalo Bills 31

Buffalo won despite New England gaining 513 yards. C.J. Spiller’s 154 rushing yards and 93 receiving yards gave the Bills the finishing production that separated a game in which each side committed two turnovers.

**Standouts:**
- C.J. Spiller — 247 scrimmage yards.
- Tom Brady — 385 passing yards in defeat.

### Tennessee Titans 17, Pittsburgh Steelers 27

**Standouts:**
- Ben Roethlisberger — 412 passing yards.
- Chris Johnson — 146 rushing yards and 98 receiving yards.

**Standouts:**
- Tony Gonzalez — 202 receiving yards.
- Matt Ryan — 318 passing yards.
