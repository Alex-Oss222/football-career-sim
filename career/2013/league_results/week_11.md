## Indianapolis 38, Tennessee 17 (Thursday)

Tennessee led 7-0 on a 90-yard touchdown drive, then Antoine Bethea intercepted Jake Locker on a one-play possession and Indianapolis scored from the Tennessee 34 to tie it. The Colts added touchdown drives of 75 and 69 yards for a 21-7 lead, and Rob Bironas's 20-yard field goal on the last possession of the half made it 21-10. Ropati Pitoitua forced and recovered a Reggie Wayne fumble in the third quarter and Tennessee scored from the Indianapolis 32 to get within 21-17, but Indianapolis answered with touchdown drives of 81 and 67 yards, the second for a 35-17 lead with 4:17 left. Bethea intercepted Locker on the next snap, and Adam Vinatieri's 31-yard field goal closed the scoring. Andrew Luck threw four touchdown passes, two to T.Y. Hilton, whose only two catches both scored, one to Wayne and one to Dominique Jones, whose only catch was an 18-yard score, and ran for another; Tennessee ran for 30 yards.

**Standouts:** Andrew Luck (19-32, 287 yards, 4 TD; rushing TD); Antoine Bethea (2 interceptions); Reggie Wayne (7-114, TD).
