**Buffalo Bills win Super Bowl XLVIII.** The AFC's sixth seed won four postseason games: 33-30 in overtime at Pittsburgh, 26-24 at the Jets, 34-3 at Tennessee and 31-20 over Minnesota.

## Buffalo 31, Minnesota 20

Buffalo opened with a nine-play, 79-yard touchdown drive, and Minnesota answered with an 80-yard drive in six plays, 7-7. Minnesota then lost three possessions in a row on turnovers: fumbles at the Buffalo 16 and 23 and an interception near the Minnesota 35, which set up Dan Carpenter's 37-yard field goal, 10-7. Christian Ponder was intercepted again late in the half, and the return reached the Minnesota 1 with 51 seconds left; and Buffalo knelt twice to end the half (see the note below). Buffalo went 66 yards for 17-7 in the third quarter, and Minnesota answered with an 80-yard drive, Ponder's second touchdown pass, 17-14. The teams traded lost fumbles early in the fourth quarter, and Blair Walsh's 38-yard field goal tied it 17-17 with 7:38 left. Buffalo then drove 68 yards in four plays for 24-17 with 3:43 left. On its next possession Minnesota lost a fumble at its own 15, and Tashard Choice ran 15 yards for a touchdown on the next snap, 31-17. Walsh's 28-yarder with 58 seconds left made it 31-20.

Jeff Tuel, in his second postseason start in place of the injured E.J. Manuel, completed 22 of 34 for 225 yards and a touchdown to C.J. Spiller. Spiller and Fred Jackson also ran for touchdowns. Buffalo forced six turnovers: two interceptions and four lost fumbles, recovered by Alan Branch, Alex Carrington, Kyle Williams and Marcell Dareus, each of whom also forced one. Ponder threw for 257 yards and two touchdowns, to Greg Jennings (7 catches, 97 yards) and Jarius Wright, but was intercepted twice and lost two fumbles. Adrian Peterson ran 22 times for 105.

**Standouts:** Aaron Williams and Leodis McKelvin (an interception each; McKelvin also returned four punts for 70 yards); Nigel Bradham (11 tackles, sack); Jeff Tuel (22-34, 225 yards, TD).
