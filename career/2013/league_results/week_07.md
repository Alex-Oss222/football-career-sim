## Seattle 45, Arizona 20 (Thursday)

Seattle scored six touchdowns. Marshawn Lynch ran for 129 yards and one of them, Russell Wilson threw for 323 yards and two touchdowns and ran for another, and Sidney Rice caught seven passes for 110 yards and a score. Larry Fitzgerald scored both Arizona touchdowns, one rushing and one receiving; Calais Campbell had two sacks.

**Standouts:** Marshawn Lynch (129 rushing yards, TD); Sidney Rice (7-110, TD); Larry Fitzgerald (rushing TD; 4-37, TD).

## Buffalo 20, Miami 13

C.J. Spiller ran for 144 yards and Steve Johnson caught both of E.J. Manuel's touchdown passes. Buffalo intercepted Ryan Tannehill twice, once each by Jim Leonhard and Justin Rogers. Lamar Miller ran for 98 yards and Miami's touchdown, and Philip Wheeler made 17 tackles.

**Standouts:** C.J. Spiller (144 rushing yards); Steve Johnson (8-94, 2 TD); Philip Wheeler (17 tackles).

## Chicago 17, Washington 7

Jay Cutler threw touchdown passes to Alshon Jeffery and Joe Anderson, and Matt Forte ran for 91 yards. Chicago held Washington to 62 rushing yards, and Chris Conte intercepted Robert Griffin. Roy Helu scored Washington's only touchdown; DeAngelo Hall made 15 tackles.

**Standouts:** Alshon Jeffery (4-87, TD); Matt Forte (91 rushing yards); Josh Morgan (5-97).

## Detroit 21, Cincinnati 17

Detroit came back from 17-0 with three straight touchdowns. Matthew Stafford threw all three, two to Calvin Johnson and a 56-yarder to Reggie Bush. Cincinnati outgained Detroit 388 yards to 253, but Mike Nugent missed from 48 and 37 yards. A.J. Green caught 10 passes for 138 yards and BenJarvus Green-Ellis ran for 136.

**Standouts:** Calvin Johnson (7-82, 2 TD); A.J. Green (10-138); BenJarvus Green-Ellis (136 rushing yards).

## Philadelphia 28, Dallas 24

**Standouts:** LeSean McCoy (56 rushing yards, TD; 4-112, TD); Barry Church (2 interceptions); DeMarco Murray (89 rushing yards, TD; receiving TD).

## New York Jets 23, New England 20

**Standouts:** Muhammad Wilkerson (2 sacks, 15 tackles); Nick Folk (3 of 3 field goals); Devin McCourty (2 interceptions).
