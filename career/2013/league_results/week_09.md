## Miami 17, Cincinnati 6 (Thursday)

Cincinnati never reached the end zone: Mike Nugent kicked field goals of 33 and 36 yards and missed from 55, and Miami answered the miss with a touchdown drive for a 7-3 second-quarter lead. Each quarterback threw two interceptions, Robert Geathers and Vontaze Burfict picking off Ryan Tannehill and Jamar Taylor and Philip Wheeler picking off Andy Dalton. Leading 10-6 after Dalton's second interception, Miami drove 99 yards in 14 plays and scored with 51 seconds left. Daniel Thomas ran for one Miami touchdown and Dion Sims caught a 20-yard touchdown pass, his only catch.

**Standouts:** Chris Clemons (16 tackles); Philip Wheeler (interception, 10 tackles); BenJarvus Green-Ellis (92 rushing yards; 4-30).

## Atlanta 23, Carolina 10

Atlanta went 80 yards in three plays on its opening possession and led 14-3 in the second quarter and 17-3 at halftime. Matt Ryan threw touchdown passes to Roddy White and Tony Gonzalez without an interception, Steven Jackson ran for 101 yards and Matt Bryant kicked three field goals. DeAngelo Williams ran for Carolina's touchdown, which made it 20-10 in the third quarter. Carolina lost two fumbles, by Cam Newton and Williams, with Corey Peters and Sean Weatherspoon each forcing and recovering one; the second came at the Atlanta 8 with 2:51 left.

**Standouts:** Tony Gonzalez (5-106, TD); Steven Jackson (101 rushing yards); Corey Peters (sack, forced fumble, fumble recovery).

## Kansas City 22, Buffalo 20

Ryan Succop kicked five field goals in six tries, the last from 42 yards with 23 seconds left. Buffalo led 10-6 at halftime and 13-12 early in the fourth quarter, then drove 94 yards in 14 plays for a 20-12 lead with 3:51 left. Kansas City answered with an 80-yard touchdown drive, capped by Knile Davis's run, to get within 20-19; Buffalo went three-and-out, and Kansas City started the winning drive at the Buffalo 40. Alex Smith threw for 338 yards without a touchdown or an interception, and Jamaal Charles caught four passes for 118 yards, one of them for 78. Fred Jackson ran for 122 yards and a touchdown and Scott Chandler caught Buffalo's other touchdown, but E.J. Manuel was intercepted by Johnathan Cyprien and Sean Smith, and Jackson lost a fumble to Tyson Jackson.

**Standouts:** Ryan Succop (5 of 6 field goals); Jamaal Charles (4-118; 45 rushing yards); Fred Jackson (122 rushing yards, TD).

## Minnesota 27, Dallas 17

Minnesota scored on its opening drive, Dallas tied it 7-7 in the first quarter, and after Dan Bailey missed from 33 yards, Adrian Peterson ran 77 yards for a touchdown on the next snap for a 17-7 lead with 2:03 left in the half. Peterson ran 11 times for 130 yards and two touchdowns and caught seven passes for 63 yards, and Christian Ponder completed 26 of 35 passes, with a touchdown to Kyle Rudolph. Mistral Raymond intercepted Tony Romo early in the fourth quarter, setting up Blair Walsh's second field goal for a 27-10 lead. Romo threw for 310 yards and a touchdown to Jason Witten, and DeMarco Murray ran for Dallas's other touchdown. Dallas's second touchdown ended a 90-yard drive with 1:07 left.

**Standouts:** Adrian Peterson (130 rushing yards, 2 TD; 7-63); Christian Ponder (26-35, 175 yards, TD); Jason Witten (3-66, TD).

## New York Jets 30, New Orleans 13

The Jets ran for 303 yards: Bilal Powell carried 25 times for 162 and Alex Green four times for 84. Geno Smith threw three touchdown passes, to Powell, Jeff Cumberland and Stephen Hill, and Nick Folk kicked three field goals. New Orleans was intercepted on its first possession, tied it 7-7 on Pierre Thomas's touchdown run and trailed 17-10 at halftime; Curtis Lofton forced and recovered a Powell fumble to open the third quarter, and Garrett Hartley's field goal made it 17-13. The Jets then scored on each of their last three possessions. Antonio Cromartie and Kyle Wilson intercepted Luke McCown, and Marques Colston caught five passes for 101 yards.

**Standouts:** Bilal Powell (162 rushing yards; 3-12, TD); Geno Smith (16-29, 132 yards, 3 TD); Curtis Lofton (11 tackles, 4 tackles for loss, forced fumble, fumble recovery).

## San Diego 20, Washington 20

The game ended in a tie after a scoreless overtime. Kai Forbath's 33-yard field goal gave Washington a 20-17 lead with 1:41 left, and Nick Novak answered with a 46-yarder on the last play of regulation. Washington went three-and-out to open overtime, and San Diego's only overtime possession reached the Washington 8 before ending on downs as the period ran out. Philip Rivers threw for 341 yards and ran for a touchdown, John Phillips caught a 12-yard touchdown pass, his only catch, and Malcom Floyd caught seven passes for 143 yards, but Josh Wilson intercepted Rivers twice. Robert Griffin threw touchdown passes to Josh Morgan and Aldrick Robinson, whose only catch was a 31-yard score; Marcus Gilchrist intercepted Griffin, Washington converted one of 13 third downs, and San Diego held the ball for 43:07.

**Standouts:** Malcom Floyd (7-143); Josh Wilson (2 interceptions); Eric Weddle (14 tackles).

## St. Louis 38, Tennessee 17

Daryl Richardson ran 26 times for 141 yards and three touchdowns and caught four passes for 112 yards and a fourth. St. Louis led 14-0 in the first quarter and 28-7 at halftime, and Sam Bradford threw for 306 yards and ran for a touchdown. Jake Locker threw two touchdown passes to Kendall Wright but was intercepted three times, twice by Janoris Jenkins and once by Cortland Finnegan; Akeem Ayers intercepted Bradford. The Rams had 33 first downs.

**Standouts:** Daryl Richardson (141 rushing yards, 3 TD; 4-112, TD); Janoris Jenkins (2 interceptions); Cortland Finnegan (interception, sack, 12 tackles).

## Philadelphia 23, Oakland 14

**Standouts:** Bryce Brown (106 rushing yards, TD); DeSean Jackson (8-82, TD); Denarius Moore (11-80, TD).

## Seattle 23, Tampa Bay 17

Seattle led 17-0 in the first quarter, the second touchdown coming three plays after Bobby Wagner forced and recovered a Vincent Jackson fumble, and 20-3 at halftime. Stephen Hauschka kicked three 45-yard field goals, and Russell Wilson threw touchdown passes to Golden Tate and to Marshawn Lynch, whose only catch was a 5-yard score. Josh Freeman threw for 298 yards and touchdowns to Luke Stocker and Doug Martin but was sacked four times, twice each by Red Bryant and Tony McDaniel. Tampa Bay's second touchdown made it 23-17 with 1:55 left, and its final drive reached the Seattle 6 as time ran out. Mark Barron intercepted Wilson.

**Standouts:** Stephen Hauschka (3 of 3 field goals); Red Bryant (2 sacks, 7 tackles); Luke Stocker (7-93, TD).

## Cleveland 24, Baltimore 13

Ward intercepted Joe Flacco. Brandon Weeden threw three touchdown passes, two to Davone Bess and one to Greg Little, and Cleveland led 24-6 early in the fourth quarter before Ray Rice's touchdown run. **Standouts:** D'Qwell Jackson (2 sacks, interception, 8 tackles); Davone Bess (8-122, 2 TD); Brandon Weeden (19-29, 270 yards, 3 TD).

## New England 34, Pittsburgh 0

Stevan Ridley ran 30 times for 171 yards and three touchdowns, Tom Brady threw a touchdown pass to Danny Amendola, and Rob Gronkowski caught eight passes for 126 yards. Pittsburgh turned the ball over on downs twice, Shaun Suisham missed from 32 yards, Aqib Talib intercepted Ben Roethlisberger, and Rob Ninkovich forced and recovered a Heath Miller fumble. Brandon Spikes had two of New England's four sacks, and New England held the ball for 38:33.

**Standouts:** Stevan Ridley (171 rushing yards, 3 TD); Rob Gronkowski (8-126); Brandon Spikes (2 sacks, 8 tackles).

## Houston 34, Indianapolis 28

Houston led only 13-7 after three quarters, and the teams then scored six touchdowns in the fourth. Coby Fleener's 60-yard touchdown catch pulled Indianapolis within 34-28 with 2:29 left, and after a Houston punt, Andrew Luck's final drive reached the Houston 12 before he was intercepted as time ran out. Arian Foster ran for 151 yards and a touchdown, Matt Schaub threw touchdown passes to DeAndre Hopkins and Keshawn Martin, and Schaub also ran for a touchdown and 96 yards on four carries, one of them a 75-yard run, as Houston ran for 279 yards. Luck threw for 304 yards and three touchdowns, two to Darrius Heyward-Bey, and was intercepted by Brooks Reed and Johnathan Joseph; LaRon Landry intercepted Schaub, and Adam Vinatieri missed from 40 yards.

**Standouts:** Arian Foster (151 rushing yards, TD); Matt Schaub (16-27, 227 yards, 2 TD; 96 rushing yards, TD); Darrius Heyward-Bey (5-72, 2 TD).

## Green Bay 30, Chicago 13 (Monday)

Jordy Nelson ran 71 yards for a touchdown on Green Bay's first snap, and the Packers led 27-0 at halftime. Clay Matthews intercepted Jay Cutler to set up Mason Crosby's first of three field goals, Eddie Lacy ran for a touchdown and Aaron Rodgers threw one to Randall Cobb. Matt Forte's third-quarter touchdown run was Chicago's only touchdown. Brandon Marshall caught eight of 16 passes thrown his way for 81 yards, Major Wright intercepted Rodgers, and Morgan Burnett made 13 tackles.

**Standouts:** Jordy Nelson (76 rushing yards, TD; 3-17); Clay Matthews (sack, interception); Mason Crosby (3 of 3 field goals).
