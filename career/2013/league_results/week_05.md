## Cleveland 10, Buffalo 6

**Standouts:** Jim Leonhard (2 interceptions); C.J. Spiller (79 rushing yards); Phillip Taylor (sack).

## Miami 21, Baltimore 3

**Standouts:** Ryan Tannehill (30-47, 284 yards, 3 TD); Brian Hartline (7-91); Charles Clay (7-80, TD).
