# 2013 season review: Stone with Shad Khan and David Caldwell

**Function:** the dated record of the head coach's end-of-season review with ownership and the general manager, and of the club's retention decision. Organization-side actions are resolved from the criteria frozen in Entry 1 before Stone's review was read (the hiring-search discipline, `career/2013/offseason/hiring_search.md`, and Document 3 row 1).
**Date of the meeting:** Wednesday January 15, 2014, at the stadium, four days after the Divisional loss at Tennessee.
**User's instructions (September 28, 2026):** Stone presents an honest season review and his 2014 direction and asks for nothing new: no extension, raise, authority change or staff budget. The meeting is held January 15.

## Entry 1: Criteria freeze

**Recorded:** September 28, 2026, before the meeting was written. **Freeze state:** CLOSED.

**Decision authority.** Hiring, retaining, suspending or firing the head coach belongs to owner Shad Khan and general manager David Caldwell (Document 3, authority row 1). Stone may negotiate, object or resign; he cannot decide it.

**Contract.** Four years from January 15, 2013, fully guaranteed for all four years. No option, extension, buyout or termination mechanics beyond the full guarantee were set (`career/2013/offseason/head_coach_contract.md`). Compensation is undetermined in the record.

**The club's criteria for the hire (recorded before Stone was hired).** From `library/2013_coaching_market_pre_hire.md` section 3.4 and `hiring_search.md` Entry 1:
1. a functional working relationship with a newly hired general manager;
2. whole-program leadership suited to a 2-14 roster, not a single schematic repair;
3. a credible quarterback evaluation and development plan;
4. building a staff and operating structure from a near-clean reset;
5. roster-development judgment under a high draft position and broad reconstruction.

**Stone's own first-year standard (said in his interview, `hiring_search.md`).** "Evidence of a functioning program, player development, fewer preparation and communication failures, a candid quarterback evaluation, and a clearer long-term roster direction, not a promised playoff appearance." He also accepted Caldwell's control of scouting, contracts, the cap and draft administration.

**The 2013 record the club reviews (branch canon at January 15, 2014).**
- **Results:** 10-6 in the regular season, second in the AFC South (Tennessee won the division on division record), AFC fifth seed. Won the Wild Card 38-14 at Kansas City; lost the Divisional 20-13 at Tennessee. Preseason 2-2. The club was 2-14 in 2012.
- **Scoring:** 356 points for and 346 against in the regular season. Three losses by 28 or more: 11-41 at Tennessee (Week 10), 6-38 at Houston (Week 12), 16-45 against Buffalo (Week 15). Six regular-season losses in all: at Oakland 13-17, at St. Louis 24-26, at Tennessee 11-41, at Houston 6-38, against Buffalo 16-45, at Indianapolis 22-23 (Week 17, with the division at stake).
- **Quarterback:** Caldwell traded Jacksonville's 2014 second-round pick to Washington for Kirk Cousins, and Blaine Gabbert to Green Bay for C.J. Wilson (`career/2013/trades/trades.md`). Cousins won the open competition and started as QB1 all season: 3,981 passing yards, 23 touchdowns, 18 interceptions and 38 sacks (regular season, receipts).
- **Draft class:** Lane Johnson (#2) started at right tackle; Travis Kelce, Jordan Poyer, Sio Moore and Bacarri Rambo held roles; Adam Thielen, an undrafted signing, became WR2 (`career/2013/roster.md`).
- **Staff:** the staff Stone built under the budget in his contract (`career/2013/coaching_staff.md`) completed the season; no departure is recorded.
- **Conduct and availability:** Justin Blackmon's four-game league suspension and October reinstatement were handled under the club's standards (ledger Entries 36 and 42). Paul Posluszny is on an independent medical hold from Week 13.
- **Honours:** Stone was a Coach of the Year finalist on the branch shortlist (Entry 71; Rex Ryan won the draw). Maurice Jones-Drew was Comeback Player of the Year.

**Labelled inferences.**
- The club measures the year against its own hire criteria and against the standard Stone set for himself, not against a playoff promise that was never made.
- A 10-win season with a playoff win exceeds what either standard asked for. The thin scoring margin and the three lopsided losses are legitimate questions for an owner and a general manager to raise, and the interception and sack totals bear on criterion 3.

**Unknown private weighting.** Khan's private expectations for 2014, any ownership concern not in the record, the 2014 football operations budget, and any appetite on either side for extending the contract are not established and are not supplied.

**Controls.** The decision must be the one the club would reach for any first-year head coach with this record, contract and evidence (the label-swap test, Document 1 section 9.1). No real-world Jacksonville 2013 or 2014 event is used. Stone's delivery, confidence or phrasing is not an input to the decision.
