## 1. Operating chain of command

### Team level

- **Alex Stone, head coach:** final football authority within the head-coach powers already established in Document 3. Stone sets team priorities, practice structure, depth chart, game management and the coaching-staff organization. He remains the ordinary offensive play-caller.
- **David Caldwell, general manager:** not a member of the coaching staff. Caldwell retains final player-personnel, contract, salary-cap, scouting-administration, acquisition and draft authority under the accepted Stone-Caldwell division.
- Assistant coaches do not receive independent player-personnel authority from their titles. They evaluate, recommend and teach; acquisition authority remains where Document 3 places it.

### Phase control

| Phase | Day-to-day lead | Game-day caller / operational controller | Stone's retained role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offense | Mike Tice coordinates the unit and weekly offensive process | **Alex Stone calls offensive plays** | Final offensive menu, game-plan approval, situational direction, game management |
| Defense | Romeo Crennel | **Romeo Crennel calls the defense** | Final team principles, coordinator supervision, major situational/game-management decisions |
| Special teams | Alan Lowry | **Alan Lowry directs the kicking game** | Final team-level priorities, roster/use decisions within Stone's authority, game-management interface |

The delegation above is real delegation. Stone does not hire Crennel or Lowry and then secretly call their units snap by snap.

## 3. Common assistant-contract framework

Unless an individual row below says otherwise, each executed assistant contract uses the following branch terms.

### Guaranteed compensation

- Scheduled salary is guaranteed for the stated contract term if Jacksonville terminates the coach without cause.
- If the coach later earns compensation from another NFL or major-college football coaching job during the guaranteed period, Jacksonville receives a **dollar-for-dollar offset** against what it still owes.
- Outside broadcasting, speaking or passive income does not offset the coaching guarantee unless a later written amendment says otherwise.

This structure reflects the period reality that clubs often continued to owe fired coaches under existing commitments while offset language reduced duplicate football compensation.

### Termination for cause

Cause is limited to a material employment breach such as serious intentional misconduct, material dishonesty to the club about football employment, a major rules violation based on the coach's own conduct, or intentional refusal to perform assigned duties after notice where cure is possible.

Ordinary football disagreement, poor unit statistics, a losing season, a scheme dispute, or Stone changing a player's role are **not automatically "cause."**

### Head-coach change

An assistant's title does not guarantee continued employment if Stone ceases to be Jacksonville's head coach.

A new head coach may release or reassign assistants subject to club contract obligations. If the club terminates an assistant without cause, the salary guarantee/offset provisions still apply.

### Outside opportunities

- No assistant has an unrestricted right to leave for a lateral job while under contract.
- Outside interviews and releases follow the applicable NFL anti-tampering, permission and postseason-interview rules.
- Jacksonville may grant a release for a genuine promotion.
- No contract here promises a future coordinator or head-coach interview.

### Football authority

No assistant contract:

- guarantees a starter;
- guarantees a draft choice or free-agent signing;
- gives the assistant final personnel authority;
- gives an assistant control of the player salary cap;
- overrides Stone's head-coach authority;
- overrides Caldwell's personnel authority.

### Duties and reassignment

Stone may adjust ordinary football duties within the reasonable scope of the coach's title as the staff evolves.

A material demotion that strips the core job the club hired the coach to perform is not treated as an ordinary reassignment merely to evade the salary guarantee.

## 4. Offensive staff

### Mike Tice — Offensive Coordinator

**Contract:** 3 seasons, $3.45M total; $1.10M in 2013.  
**Reports to:** Stone.  
**Play-calling:** Stone retains final offensive play-calling.

#### Pre-hire résumé relevant to the appointment

By January 2013, Tice had:

- played extensively in the NFL as a tight end;
- coached the Minnesota offensive line before becoming the Vikings' head coach;
- served as Minnesota's permanent head coach from 2002 through 2005 after an interim stint;
- worked on Jacksonville's offensive staff earlier in his coaching career;
- coached Chicago's offensive line before becoming the Bears' offensive coordinator for 2012.

The appointment therefore buys unusually senior offensive and head-coaching experience without pretending Tice is the game-day play-caller in Stone's offense.

#### Primary responsibilities

Tice owns the **offensive operating process** beneath Stone:

- weekly run-game architecture;
- protection coordination with Bates and Yarno;
- offensive installation calendar;
- coordination of position-coach input into one weekly plan;
- first- and second-down run/pass structure;
- short-yardage and four-minute run planning;
- offensive personnel-package organization;
- self-scout of run tendencies and protection problems;
- practice-script recommendations;
- game-plan quality control before Stone finalizes the call sheet.

Tice may recommend calls and sequences during games. Stone makes the final offensive call unless Stone explicitly delegates a period or emergency succession later.

## 5. Defensive staff

### Romeo Crennel — Defensive Coordinator

**Contract:** 3 seasons, $4.95M total; $1.60M in 2013.  
**Reports to:** Stone.  
**Play-calling:** Crennel controls the defensive call sheet and calls the defense.

#### Why the contract is at the top of the assistant market

Crennel is not being paid like an ordinary first-time coordinator.

By January 2013 his résumé already included:

- decades of NFL coaching;
- extensive Bill Parcells/Bill Belichick tree experience;
- multiple Super Bowl championship staffs;
- New England defensive coordinator experience;
- Cleveland head-coaching experience;
- Kansas City defensive-coordinator and head-coaching experience.

The three-year term and $1.6M opening salary compensate him for returning immediately to a coordinator role after a head-coaching job and for taking genuine operational ownership of Stone's defense.

#### Defensive authority

Crennel owns:

- defensive scheme installation;
- weekly game plan;
- fronts, coverages and pressure package;
- defensive personnel packages;
- defensive meeting structure;
- defensive practice priorities inside Stone's overall schedule;
- game-day defensive play-calling;
- in-game defensive adjustments;
- recommendations on defensive roster roles.

Stone retains final head-coach authority and The Prowl's established team-level defensive principles.

#### Scheme boundary

The contract does not force a particular base front before roster evaluation.

Crennel may draw heavily on his multiple-front / 3-4 background, but the 2013 Jacksonville defense is installed from the roster Stone and Crennel actually inherit and build.

## 7. Staff collaboration rules

### Weekly planning

The three phase leads do not operate separate franchises.

- Tice and Stone reconcile offensive personnel, protection and run-game priorities.
- Crennel presents the defensive plan and any roster/schematic problems that require head-coach attention.
- Lowry identifies bottom-of-roster special-teams requirements early enough to matter in personnel discussions.
- Position coaches bring evidence from film, practice and meetings rather than hidden player ratings.

### Personnel input

Assistant coaches may:

- grade players;
- recommend roles;
- recommend retention/release priorities;
- identify scheme fits;
- flag developmental needs;
- participate in draft/free-agent football evaluations when Caldwell/Stone assign them.

They may not:

- negotiate player contracts;
- promise roster spots;
- promise starting jobs;
- authorize trades;
- commit draft capital;
- present themselves as final personnel decision-makers.

### The Prowl

All assistants operate under the same program rules:

- football mistakes are coached before they are moralized;
- preparation and repeated correctable failures can change responsibility;
- medical authority remains medical;
- offseason voluntariness remains voluntary;
- private mental-health, financial and family-support information is not a coaching grade;
- the staff is accountable upward as well as players downward.

## 8. Contract accounting and future changes

Assistant contracts are maintained outside the NFL player salary cap.

Any later:

- extension;
- raise;
- title change;
- coordinator promotion/demotion;
- play-calling transfer;
- resignation;
- firing;
- outside interview permission;
- contract buyout or settlement,

must be recorded as a dated staff transaction.

The values in this file remain the active assistant contract register until superseded by such a transaction.
