## Live-board availability and autonomous league action

The clubs between Jacksonville's turns selected autonomously from their own needs and contemporaneous boards. The actual historical 2013 draft was never loaded as an answer key. Jacksonville's decision points resolved as follows:

- **Before #2:** Kansas City selected Eric Fisher. Luke Joeckel and Lane Johnson remained available; Caldwell chose Johnson. No acceptable trade-down proposal reached Jacksonville.
- **Before #33:** the broad first-round tackle, defensive-front, corner, and skill-position cluster came off the board in varied club-specific order. Kelce, Allen, Poyer, Bakhtiari, Moore, Edwards, Rambo, and Bray remained available.
- **Before #64:** Keenan Allen and the leading remaining receiver/front alternatives were selected. Poyer, Bakhtiari, Moore, Edwards, Rambo, and Bray remained.
- **Before #98:** Bakhtiari was selected. Moore, Edwards, Rambo, and Bray remained.
- **Before #135:** Moore was no longer available after Jacksonville selected him; Edwards remained through the intervening selections.
- **Before #169:** Edwards was no longer available after Jacksonville selected him; Rambo and Bray remained.
- **Before #208:** Rambo was no longer available after Jacksonville selected him; Bray remained.

This is the availability record needed to adjudicate Jacksonville's choices, not a disguised copy of the real draft order and not a complete league transaction ledger. No non-Jacksonville pick creates a Jacksonville cap or roster entry.

## Draft-day trade record

No Jacksonville draft-day trade closed. The Cousins and Gabbert/Wilson transactions were completed before the draft and did not use a 2013 selection. Caldwell heard the draft-day market at each turn, but no proposal compensated the club for leaving the best available choice on its own board. Jacksonville therefore exercised all seven selections.
