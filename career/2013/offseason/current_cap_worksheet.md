# Jacksonville Jaguars — 2013 Regular-Season Cap Reconciliation

**Status:** RECONCILED WITH SOURCE-BOUNDED UNCERTAINTY.
**Effective date:** October 7, 2013, after Justin Blackmon's reinstatement and activation.

## Governing treatment

The 2013 league cap remains **$123.0M**. Offseason Top-51 treatment has ended. Jacksonville now accounts for the active 53, existing bonus/dead-money obligations, guarantees, reserve mechanisms when used, and practice-squad compensation as incurred. The practice squad is not part of the active 53.

## Branch transition

- The May 5 Top-51 planning range was approximately **$7.0M-$7.4M**.
- Moving from the Top 51 to the active 53 adds the two previously excluded minimum-level active contracts: approximately **$810,000** of scheduled 2013 base obligations.
- Eight practice-squad contracts carry the verified 2013 minimum of **$6,000 per week** while the player remains on the squad. A full 17-week season for the opening eight would be **$816,000**, but this is not falsely booked as guaranteed full-season cash because membership can change weekly.
- The eleven August 31 waivers/releases do not erase signing-bonus prorations or guarantees already included in the branch's prior accounting. Public-source limitations prevent a penny-precise allocation of inherited minimum-contract termination-pay elections or later injury-settlement consequences. No unverified settlement is invented.
- Pasztor and Mosley remain on the active 53, so no reserve-list cap mechanism has been used at this checkpoint. The three outside waiver claims create no new Jacksonville salary obligation. Practice-squad contracts are separate current obligations.

## September 4 working position

Before week-by-week practice-squad charges, the reconstructed working room is approximately **$6.2M-$6.6M** (`$7.0M-$7.4M` less approximately `$810,000`). If the opening eight remain for all 17 weeks, that full-season exposure would reduce the comparable planning range to approximately **$5.4M-$5.8M** before any later transaction, injury settlement, reserve move or termination-pay election.

This range is the honest current branch answer; an exact club ledger does not exist publicly for the counterfactual roster. The May range is not carried forward unchanged, and Top-51 language is historical only.

## September 9: Blackmon to Reserve/Suspended

Justin Blackmon serves a four-game league suspension (Weeks 2-5) on Reserve/Suspended. He forfeits his base-salary game checks for those four weeks. How the forfeiture is credited against the 2013 cap has not been verified here, and the exact 2013 base salary behind his cap charge is not verified in this worksheet (`initial_cap_sheet.md` does show the split, a $1,231,455 base and $2,975,818 proration, but as an older archive transcription its September 18, 2026 audit did not re-verify; administrative correction, [Monroe franchise designation](../../2014/00_Team_Operations/Free_Agency/signings.md)), so the forfeited amount and its cap effect are **unresolved**; no figure is booked. His signing-bonus proration is unaffected by this worksheet; whether any of it is recoverable is also unresolved. The working range above is unchanged until that reconciliation is done. Jacksonville's active roster is 52 with one open spot; no replacement has been signed.

## October 7: Blackmon reinstated and activated

Blackmon was reinstated from Reserve/Suspended and activated to the open 53rd spot on October 7 ([Blackmon reinstatement](../regular_season/week_06_jacksonville_at_denver/output.md)), so his base-salary game checks resume from Week 6. The sourced 2013 rule (`library/2013_suspension_reinstatement_rules.md`, confirmed) sets the forfeiture for the four suspended games at 4/17 of his 2013 Paragraph 5 base salary. The dollar amount and how it is credited against the cap remain **unresolved**, because the transcribed base/proration split behind his cap charge has not been re-verified; no figure is booked and the working range is unchanged. The active roster is 53 with no open spot.
