# Free agency — Stone's plan

**Status:** Stone's dated targets and price ceilings for the March 12 opening of free agency (`library/2013_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md`). Caldwell holds final authority over contract negotiation/execution and cap management (Authority Map rows 5-6) — this board is what Stone and his staff are recommending, not a guaranteed signing. Whether a targeted player actually signs with Jacksonville, at these terms, is resolved live by Document 7 §6.2's free-agency procedure (real market pool, other clubs' genuine competing pursuit) when that period is actually reached — not assumed here.

## Priority 1 — Michael Bennett (DE)

The one exception Stone is asking Caldwell to spend real money on: Jacksonville's pass rush is a genuine need.

- **Offer:** approximately 1 year, $5.5-6 million.
- **Ceiling:** approximately $6-6.5 million. If another club goes higher, walk away rather than chase.
- **Pitch:** starting role, heavy snaps, inside/outside rush usage, a one-year opportunity to rebuild his market value, and a Florida return.

## Priority 2 — Sen'Derrick Marks (DT)

Inexpensive, 1-year prove-it structure for interior defensive-line competition.

## Priority 3 — Roy Miller (DT)

Run-defense piece. Modest 2-year structure.

## Priority 4 — Alan Ball (CB)

Cheap veteran corner — not being sold as a long-term CB1, but real competition, insurance, and special-teams/secondary depth.

## Priority 5 — Justin Forsett (RB)

Inexpensive insurance behind Maurice Jones-Drew. Signing him removes the need to spend a meaningful draft pick at running back.

## Priority 6 — Brad Meester (C)

One-year return for the veteran center, to help install the new system while the club develops his eventual successor.

## Own free agent — Daryl Smith (LB)

Offer a short, incentive-heavy deal with a real ceiling. If another club offers more security, let him walk — his history with the organization is not by itself a reason to overpay.

## Contingency — Brent Grimes (CB)

Plan B only, if Bennett doesn't sign — not a second mandatory target. If Bennett goes elsewhere, a comparable offer (roughly 1 year, $5.5-6 million) is defensible for Grimes, but the stated preference is to spend the one premium free-agent push on pass rush rather than split it.
