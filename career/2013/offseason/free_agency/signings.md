## Pursuit results

### Michael Bennett — offer declined; premium contingency activated

Jacksonville offered a one-year, **$6.25M** contract with **$4.0M guaranteed**, inside Stone's stated one-year walk-away. Bennett's representative communicated credible multi-year interest elsewhere and preferred greater security rather than Jacksonville's one-year prove-it structure. Caldwell did not cross the one-year ceiling or manufacture a second premium bid; Bennett declined, and Jacksonville closed the pursuit without recording his eventual destination.

That loss activated the already-written Brent Grimes contingency. It did not authorize simultaneous premium spending on both players.

### Sen'Derrick Marks — signed

Marks accepted the one-year, **$1.5M** prove-it agreement shown above. Jacksonville offered a clear interior-rotation opportunity without a starting guarantee, while the short structure preserved Marks's chance to return to market. The limited guarantee and first-year charge fit the value-pursuit instruction.

### Roy Miller — signed

Miller's market produced interest in a defined early-down interior role. Jacksonville secured him with the two-year, **$5.0M** agreement shown above; only the **$1.5M** signing bonus is guaranteed. The second year gives the player more security than a one-year offer while keeping Jacksonville's 2013 counted charge to **$1.75M**.

### Alan Ball — signed

Ball accepted the one-year, **$1.0M** competition contract shown above. The agreement promises an opportunity to compete across secondary and special-teams roles, not a starting job or CB1 status.

### Justin Forsett — offer declined

Jacksonville offered one year at **$1.1M**, with **$0.3M guaranteed**, for a reserve, passing-down and change-of-pace role. Forsett received a credible opportunity elsewhere with a clearer path to offensive touches and chose that role. Caldwell did not raise the offer into starter money; no cap charge was booked and no eventual destination was imported into this branch.

### Brad Meester — re-signed

Meester accepted the one-year, **$1.5M** agreement shown above. The deal pays for veteran communication and transition value but contains no contractual starting promise; center remains subject to football evaluation.

### Daryl Smith — re-signed

Jacksonville's club medical review was satisfactory for a short return, but Smith's representative sought more than a one-year rehabilitation bet. The sides closed on the two-year, **$6.0M** structure shown above, with only the **$1.0M** signing bonus guaranteed and no starting guarantee. That controlled exposure stayed within the board's annual range while acknowledging real competing interest.

### Brent Grimes — contingency signed

After Bennett declined, Jacksonville completed its medical review and offered Grimes a one-year, **$5.5M** contract with the full amount guaranteed. Grimes accepted the short structure and an immediate opportunity to compete for a major role. The club priced the Achilles uncertainty through term rather than asserting a guaranteed recovery or future performance.
