## Rejected counterproposal (non-operative)

Stone proposed exchanging performance vesting in Years 3–4 for a limited concurrence right on franchise-quarterback commitments. Caldwell declined. The operative agreement is therefore the original four-year, fully guaranteed offer; none of the proposed benchmarks or concurrence language survives into the executed terms.
