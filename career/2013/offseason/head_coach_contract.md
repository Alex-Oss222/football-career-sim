## 2. Market calibration for the simulation compensation

Stone's dollar terms were not fixed in the original hiring-search ledger. The current user instruction expressly authorizes realistic figures, so this section closes that previously open field without changing the already-accepted football terms.

The $4.5 million average was selected from the actual 2013 coaching market rather than from a modern salary scale:

- Doug Marrone's 2013 Buffalo contract was four years and $16 million, or $4 million per season.
- Bruce Arians' initial Arizona deal paid just over $3 million per season.
- Mike McCoy was reported around $3 million per season in the same coaching generation.
- Chip Kelly received five years and $32.5 million from Philadelphia, or $6.5 million per season.
- Sean Payton's January 2013 New Orleans extension was expected to exceed $8 million annually at the top of the market.

Stone enters this branch with a stronger demonstrated NFL head-coaching result than a typical first-time coordinator candidate because he has just completed a 12–4 interim season and a playoff win. He is still taking his first permanent NFL head-coaching job and explicitly told clubs he would sacrifice some personal compensation to secure a stronger assistant budget. A $4.5 million average therefore places him above the ordinary first-time-hire band without pricing him like Kelly, Payton, or another established franchise-level coaching star.

## 3. Assistant-coach budget

Stone's accepted offer expressly included a funded assistant-coach budget strong enough to pursue the staff he proposed, and Stone had already stated that he was willing to reduce his own compensation in exchange for assistant resources.

The completed operating term is:

| Provision | Executed term |
|---|---|
| 2013 assistant-coach salary pool | **$7,000,000 minimum** |
| Annual escalation | **3% minimum annual increase** for the remaining contract term |
| Covered personnel | Coordinators, position coaches, quality-control/assistant coaches and coaching assistants hired to Stone's football staff |
| Excluded from pool | Stone's own salary; player payroll; scouting/front-office payroll; medical/performance departments not reporting as football coaches; legacy severance owed to pre-Stone employees |
| Allocation inside pool | Stone may allocate the pool among his selected coaches without Caldwell choosing the assistants |
| Exceeding pool | Requires owner/club approval; refusal to exceed the guaranteed pool is not interference with Stone's staff-selection authority |
| Unused amount | Does not convert to Stone compensation and does not carry forward as a personal benefit |

The $7.0 million figure is deliberately a **strong 2013 staff budget**, not a claim about the actual Jaguars' historical assistant payroll. Public NFL assistant salaries were and remain much less transparent than head-coach salaries. Contemporary examples show elite coordinators and senior assistants could command seven-figure compensation, while one 2013 Dallas report estimated roughly $5 million for only four prominent veteran assistants. Stone's proposed staff included experienced coordinator-level targets, so a full-staff pool materially above that four-coach figure is realistic.

## 5. Guarantee and termination mechanics

### 5.1 Termination without cause

If Jacksonville terminates Stone without cause before the end of the 2016 football season:

- all unpaid scheduled salary through the end of the four-year term remains guaranteed;
- the club continues paying on the ordinary contract schedule unless the sides later agree to a lump-sum settlement;
- Jacksonville receives a **dollar-for-dollar offset** for compensation Stone actually earns from another NFL or major-college football coaching or football-executive position during the remaining guaranteed term;
- broadcasting, speaking, academic work, endorsements and passive investment income do not offset the guarantee.

Offset language was common in NFL coaching contracts of the period. Reporting on the Browns' 2013 staff specifically noted that most coaching contracts contained offsets. The guarantee remains economically real because Jacksonville bears the difference between Stone's scheduled salary and qualifying subsequent football compensation.

- CBS Sports, January 2014 discussion of 2013 Browns coaching-contract obligations and common offset provisions:  
  https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/browns-coaching-carousel-expected-to-cost-haslam-upward-of-50-million/

### 5.2 Termination for cause

The full guarantee may be voided only for a material for-cause event, limited to ordinary major employment breaches such as:

- fraud or intentional material dishonesty toward the club concerning football employment;
- conviction of, or guilty/no-contest plea to, a felony or similarly serious offense materially affecting employment;
- intentional refusal to perform the head-coach duties after written notice and reasonable opportunity to cure where cure is possible;
- material violation of NFL rules that results in a substantial league suspension and is based on Stone's own post-contract conduct;
- material breach of the agreed authority/loyalty/confidentiality obligations that remains uncured after notice where cure is possible.

Poor win-loss record, ordinary football disagreement, unpopular decisions, failure to make the playoffs, or Caldwell disagreeing with Stone's player evaluations are **not cause**.

This file does not invent conduct by Stone that would trigger the clause.

### 5.3 Resignation

A voluntary resignation by Stone ends future guaranteed salary unless Jacksonville materially breached the contract first or the parties negotiate a separate release. There is no unilateral opt-out clause.

## 6. Staff-selection safeguards

Stone's staff-selection authority means the GM may not select coordinators for him. It does not guarantee that a named target:

- is available;
- wants the job;
- can be released from another contract;
- accepts Jacksonville's compensation;
- passes ordinary club background/employment review.

Stone may pursue Kromer, Spagnuolo, Crennel or other candidates mentioned in the interview record, but none is appointed by this contract. Each hiring is a later simulation event.

An assistant contract extending materially beyond Stone's own 2016 term requires owner approval for the excess guaranteed years. That is a financial commitment rule, not a power for Caldwell to choose Stone's staff.

## 7. Ownership and budget protections

The club may not satisfy Stone's staff-selection term by nominally allowing him to choose assistants while providing an assistant pool materially below the executed floor.

For 2013, **$7.0 million is the guaranteed minimum assistant-coach salary pool**. Jacksonville may voluntarily increase it.

The player salary cap is entirely separate. Increasing or decreasing coaching payroll does not create player cap room.

## 8. Rejected counterproposal — non-operative

The rejected proposal creates no surviving benchmark, vesting trigger, concurrence right, veto right or reduced guarantee. The operative agreement remains the four-year fully guaranteed contract described above.
