# Jacksonville Jaguars — 2013 OTAs Output

## May 13-15 — OTA block 1

**Event status:** COMPLETE through the end of May 15, 2013.
**Event boundary:** This record closes only the May 13-15 voluntary OTA block. The May 20-21 block has **not** been run. The missed April 16-18 voluntary veteran minicamp remains a continuity gap and was not backfilled.

### Control, onboarding, participation and medical gate

Jacksonville opened the block with the reconciled **64-player controlled roster**. Before football participation, the staff completed the established welcome process for every controlled veteran who did not already have a closed onboarding record: each received the organizational standards package, support information and complete active 2013 Iteration I playbook; Stone and the applicable position coach completed the individual follow-up contacts. Repeated requests for simpler cross-room vocabulary were routed back to the coordinators. The outreach created no role, attendance or character grade.

Qualified medical personnel supplied a current football-use status before work began. Daryl Smith was cleared for the permitted OTA work and participated without a communicated restriction. Brent Grimes participated in meetings and medically directed rehabilitation/individual work, but was withheld from offense-versus-defense team periods throughout this block; the staff received no new diagnosis or return date. Every other controlled player was cleared for the work assigned during the block and participated. Rookie-minicamp clearance was not treated as automatic May clearance. No new injury or medical restriction was produced or communicated during May 13-15.

Participation was voluntary. No absence, rehabilitation assignment or declined optional activity was used as commitment evidence or a hidden personnel grade.

### Work performed and teaching progression

Each day followed **Explain -> Show -> Walk -> Rep -> Correct -> Rep again -> Retain -> Add complexity**. Meetings and walkthroughs established common personnel, formation, strength, point and coverage language before non-contact individual, group, team and special-teams periods. The staff evaluated only this taught menu:

- offense: huddle/cadence, basic formation portability, Power and Inside Zone rules, Stick and Drive jobs, the protection point and back responsibility, and a limited purposeful motion presentation;
- defense: base front/alignment, run-fit and leverage communication, core coverage distribution and a limited pressure presentation;
- special teams: substitution language, punt/kickoff lane and leverage rules, protection/release fundamentals and specialist operation.

The team did not reach a basis for broad situational installation or PRESS tempo. No preset snap share, touch allocation, personnel percentage or depth-chart probability controlled the work.

### Unit and player evidence

**Quarterbacks and offensive operation.** Kirk Cousins supplied the cleanest block of huddle operation and protection-point communication. His first-day timing in Drive improved after the receivers and quarterbacks re-walked the landmark together and the correction carried into May 15. Chad Henne was generally assignment-sound and accurate in the short taught menu; on changing pictures he was slower to reset the protection answer, then retained the simplified communication on the final day. Tyler Bray needed repeated help marrying cadence, footwork and progression timing; the issue was processing within the taught picture rather than unwillingness or a medical limit. John Parker Wilson remained steady without creating a role change. No quarterback order was awarded.

**Offensive line and skill groups.** Brad Meester gave the line its most consistent point and combination communication. Lane Johnson carried his rookie-minicamp assignment reliability into veteran work and corrected his spacing on a non-contact twist exchange, but the evidence remains an opening at right tackle, not a starting decision. The interior group did not consistently transfer Power combination timing when the defensive presentation changed. Tice and the line staff reduced the next installation step and returned to one point declaration and one shared combination vocabulary rather than blaming physical execution for a teaching/communication problem.

Cecil Shorts was the most consistent receiver at preserving the taught Stick and Drive landmarks against changed leverage. Travis Kelce handled attached-to-flex alignment changes correctly, while his hand placement and base on the taught run-block fit still needed correction; the correction improved in individual work but was not yet stable across the block. C.J. Anderson showed assignment reliability in the installed back tracks and check responsibility. None of that created a target, workload or roster guarantee.

**Defense.** Paul Posluszny stabilized front and fit calls, and Daryl Smith executed his assigned non-contact work without a communicated limitation. Sio Moore initially chased backfield action past his coverage landmark, accepted the correction and retained it when the same picture returned May 15. Jordan Poyer carried forward his leverage discipline and communicated cleanly through a changed receiver distribution. With Grimes withheld from team periods, the secondary rotated the available corners through outside and inside teaching positions; that was a temporary evaluation experiment, not a depth-chart move.

The defense became less reliable when disguise and pressure presentation were added before the base coverage exchange was settled. Crennel removed the extra presentation, re-taught the underlying coverage ownership, and the unit finished the block with the base communication intact. The postponed pressure variation is not installed team football yet.

**Special teams.** Brynden Trawick and Adam Thielen provided useful evidence across more than one taught coverage/return responsibility, particularly in alignment and lane communication. Bacarri Rambo needed an initial substitution reminder and retained the corrected unit call on the following days. These are provisional special-teams evaluations, not final-unit assignments or roster decisions.

### Coaching and install decisions at block close

- Retain the common huddle, cadence, point, formation and base coverage language.
- Carry Power, Inside Zone, Stick and Drive forward for recall rather than expanding every available family.
- Re-teach interior combination timing and quarterback/line protection communication before adding presentation.
- Keep motion limited to a football purpose; do not add variation merely because another OTA block is scheduled.
- Postpone the added defensive pressure disguise until base coverage exchange is reliable without coach rescue.
- Continue Grimes only within the next qualified medical instruction; no coach-created timetable applies.

All role movement remains provisional. Cousins, Meester, Shorts, Posluszny, Johnson, Poyer, Moore, Trawick and Thielen produced meaningful positive evidence in the work described above; Kelce, Bray, Henne, Rambo and the interior offensive unit leave with specific retained or still-open corrections. A clean rep was recorded as clean rather than given a manufactured flaw.
