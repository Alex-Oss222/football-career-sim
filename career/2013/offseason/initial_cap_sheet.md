## Governing 2013 controls

The accounting rules below are the rules that govern the branch once the 2013 league year begins. The January 15 front office may plan against reasonable cap projections, but it does not get to know a final league announcement before that announcement historically existed.

| Control | 2013 treatment |
|---|---|
| Final unadjusted league salary cap | **$123,000,000** per club; announced February 28, 2013 |
| League year / free agency | March 12, 2013, 4:00 PM ET |
| Offseason Top-51 accounting | Applies during the league-year offseason until the regular season; the 90-man roster is not simply summed as 90 full cap charges |
| Veteran signing-bonus proration | Straight-line over the contract term, **maximum five years** under the general CBA rule |
| Rookie signing-bonus proration | Maximum **four years** under the rookie-contract rules |
| Pre-/on-June-1 termination | Remaining unamortized bonus generally accelerates into the current league year |
| Post-June-1 termination | Future unamortized amounts generally fall into the following league year; club may use up to two qualifying post-June-1 designations |
| Cash-spending floor | 89% per-club cash-spending minimum measured over the **2013–2016 aggregate period**, not a 2013-only floor |
| Player roster limits | 90 offseason; 75 after first cut; 53 regular-season roster; 46 game-day actives; 8-player practice squad in 2013 |
| Head-coach / assistant compensation | Club operating expense; **not charged to the NFL player salary cap** |

The previous version's four-year maximum for all signing-bonus proration was too broad. Four years is the rookie limit; ordinary veteran bonus proration can run up to five years.

### What this sheet now supports

This sheet is sufficient to say, without inventing precision:

- Jacksonville can pursue normal free agents and extensions from a position of substantial flexibility.
- A release, restructure or trade involving a high-cost veteran can be evaluated from that player's contract mechanics and then applied to the planning range.
- Expiring March 12 players must be treated separately from players already under 2013 contract.
- The club is **not** required to manufacture cap room merely to become compliant.
- Cap space is not a reason by itself to retain or release a player.

### What still requires exact reconciliation

Before claiming an exact post-transaction cap balance to the dollar, reconcile:

1. every contract that will enter the March 12 Top-51;
2. every reserve/future contract signed before the league year;
3. pre-existing dead-money charges;
4. league credits/debits and benefit adjustments;
5. the exact approved carryover rather than the rounded $19.5M report;
6. any branch transaction already executed.

Until then, transaction outputs should report a **best-supported cap effect** and an updated planning range, not fabricate a club-certified balance.

### No-hindsight rule

Actual post-January-15 Jaguars decisions are not imported. In particular, the real releases of Dawan Landry, Aaron Ross, Laurent Robinson, later free-agent signings, the real 2013 draft class, and the later Eugene Monroe trade are not branch actions.

Later historical sources may be used only to recover contract terms that already legally existed on January 15. They may not tell Stone or Caldwell what transaction to make.
