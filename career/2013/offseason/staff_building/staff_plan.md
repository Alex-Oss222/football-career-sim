# Staff building — Stone's plan

**Status:** Stone's own candidate targets, dated late January 2013. This is a recommendation he can actually execute — the accepted contract gives him final hiring authority over his own coaching staff (Authority Map row 2) — but every named person below is a **candidate Stone intends to call, not a commitment**. Whether any of them actually accepts is a real, unresolved question for the interactive staff-building turn, not something to assume from this list.

Real availability windows below (who was actually let go or hired elsewhere, and when) are legitimate contemporaneous market facts, the same kind the coaching-search library already used — they tell Stone who is genuinely reachable and for how long. They are not a guarantee any of them says yes to Jacksonville.

Earlier interview-only staff conversations already on record (not commitments either): Aaron Kromer (offensive), Steve Spagnuolo / Romeo Crennel (defensive) — see `hiring_search_brief/05_interview_positions.md`. Crennel appears again below as the leading DC target.
