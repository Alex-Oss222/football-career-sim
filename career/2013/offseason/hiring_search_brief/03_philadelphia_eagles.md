# Philadelphia Eagles — priority 3

**Pitch:** the fastest offensive rebuild of the four — existing pieces, less demolition needed.

**Non-negotiables (beyond the universal terms in `00_overview_and_universal_terms.md`):**
- Michael Vick's existing contract is not assumed guaranteed, and the starting job is not assumed his.
- Nick Foles gets a real, fair evaluation and is kept unless there is a real football reason not to.
- No forced quarterback selection at #4 overall.
- Preference for a 5-year deal; will accept 4 (still subject to the universal minimum-4-guaranteed-years term).

**Negotiable / collaborative points:**
- GM Howie Roseman keeps contracts, cap, scouting, and trades.
- Quarterback and other top personnel decisions are joint between Stone and Roseman.

**Walk-away conditions:**
- Vick's contract or starting job is mandated as-is, without a real evaluation.
- Play-calling authority is taken away.
- Roseman picks the coordinators instead of Stone.

**Automatic acceptance / rejection conditions:** none specified — any offer must come back to the user.
