# Jacksonville Jaguars — priority 1

**Pitch:** a four-year rebuild.

**Non-negotiables (beyond the universal terms in `00_overview_and_universal_terms.md`):**
- No guarantee that Blaine Gabbert is the starting quarterback.
- No forced quarterback selection at the #2 overall pick.
- Stone controls the depth chart.
- Minimum 4 guaranteed contract years.
- Ownership funds a genuinely strong coaching staff (see the universal salary-for-staff-budget trade).

**Negotiable / collaborative points:**
- GM David Caldwell keeps contracts, cap, scouting, and draft administration.
- Quarterback decisions are collaborative, with Stone holding a real HC veto — not unilateral either way.

**Walk-away conditions:**
- Gabbert is mandated as the starter.
- A quarterback is forced at #2 regardless of Stone's evaluation.
- The GM picks the coordinators instead of Stone.
- The coaching staff is underfunded.
- Play-calling authority is taken away.
- Contract term comes in under 3 years, or with no real guarantees.
- Ownership expects an immediate playoff push in year 1.

**Automatic acceptance / rejection conditions:** none specified — see the open question in `00_overview_and_universal_terms.md` regarding a partial/vesting guarantee counter. Any offer must come back to the user.
