The normal onboarding sequence was compressed honestly into the short post-draft window:

- Each player received the same welcome package promptly after Jacksonville obtained his rights or executed his agreement: Stone's letter, Prowl expectations, medical/readiness authority, support-resource map, rookie roadmap, position material, and the **complete active Iteration I playbook**.

No call was backdated.
- The calls were receipt, learning, and support conversations—not quizzes. Repeated questions about how personnel labels connect to formation jobs led the offensive staff to slow the first language walkthrough. That is recorded as a teaching adjustment, not eleven player errors.
- Medical staff completed entry screening and communicated no camp restriction for these eleven. That camp clearance is limited to this event; it is not a season-long health prediction.

## Work performed

The staff used the planned **Explain -> Show -> Walk -> Rep -> Correct -> Rep again -> Retain -> Add complexity** cycle. The offensive menu stayed narrow: huddle operation, formation/strength and eligible labels, cadence, exchange, a base Inside Zone presentation, Power footwork, Stick, and a small play-action/Boot Flood teaching segment. Protection work stayed with the language required by that menu. No later Stone terminology or future iteration entered the building.

Defense installed alignment, leverage, one primary run-fit rule, one primary coverage responsibility, pursuit, and basic rush-lane/contain communication. Special teams taught alignment, release/protection, lane leverage, block avoidance, return communication, ball security, and emergency substitution procedure. Work was controlled and non-live where required; camp was not converted into a punishment or conditioning test.
