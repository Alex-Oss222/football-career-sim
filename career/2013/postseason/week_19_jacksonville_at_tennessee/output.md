**First half.** Jones-Drew opened with 15 yards on 22 Heavy Power. The drive reached the Tennessee 40, and Anger's punt was downed at the 9. Tennessee went three and out, and C.J. Tennessee answered with 14 plays and 80 yards: Nate Washington caught three passes, Craig Stevens caught 23 to the 14, and Chris Johnson carried four times inside the 15 and scored from the 3, 7-3. Jacksonville went 80 yards in nine straight passes. Toney Clemons caught 25 on Mesh, and two snaps later, also on Mesh, Cecil Shorts caught a 40-yard touchdown, 10-7, early in the second quarter. Rob Bironas then missed twice: from 41 after a 21-yard Washington catch, and from 42 on the last play of the half after Darius Reynaud caught a 55-yard pass. Jacksonville led 10-7 at the half, but its two possessions after the touchdown gained 6 yards.

**Third quarter.** Tennessee went three and out to start the half. Jacksonville moved 34 yards: Thielen caught 15 on the Power Pass, Anderson ran 12 on 21 Pro Left Counter, and Scobee made a 51-yard field goal, 13-7. Tennessee answered with a 14-play drive that used 9:26. Kendall Wright caught 28 and Britt 16, and Johnson ran five times inside the Jacksonville 34. The drive stalled at the 11, and Bironas made a 29-yarder, 13-10. The kickoff return put Jacksonville at its own 5. Three runs gained 6, and Anger punted from the 11.

**Fourth quarter.** After a Tennessee three and out, Jacksonville's next possession also stalled when Moise Fokou sacked Cousins, and Anger's punt went out of bounds at the Tennessee 38. Locker then drove Tennessee 62 yards in ten plays, with a 13-yard scramble, and Britt caught a 4-yard touchdown, 17-13 with 7:13 left. Thielen returned the kickoff 24 yards to the Jacksonville 19. Zach Brown sacked Cousins for 9 on the TE Delay, and two snaps later Thielen caught a 6-yard Power Pass throw. Patrick Bailey forced the ball out and recovered it at the Jacksonville 18. Tennessee ran the clock to 3:27 and Bironas kicked a 26-yarder, 20-13.

**The finish.** Jacksonville started at its 20 with 3:27 left, down 7. Five snaps reached the 30. On fourth-and-10 with 2:22 left, the engine drew a punt from its real 2012 late-game cell (trailing by 4-8, 121-300 seconds). Tennessee took over at its own 10. Johnson ran for 9 and 10 yards and Locker completed three short passes; the drive used 2:15 before Tennessee punted with 7 seconds left. Jacksonville's last snap, from its own 8, gained nothing.
