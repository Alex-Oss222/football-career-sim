### Game narrative: selected highlights

**First quarter.** Kansas City took the touchback and moved to its own 47, helped by a 24-yard catch by A.J. Jenkins. On the seventh play Alex Smith threw for Donnie Avery and Russell Allen intercepted, returning it 37 yards. Jacksonville started at the Kansas City 29. Jones-Drew lost 4 on the opening Duo; on the next snap he ran Split left 33 yards for a touchdown, 7-0. Kansas City went three and out. Cousins then threw on all five snaps of a 76-yard drive: Shorts caught 27 and 14 on Snag and 22 on Mesh, and Marcedes Lewis caught a 13-yard touchdown on Texas, 14-0 with 9:29 left in the quarter. On the third snap after Thielen's punt return, DeVito forced a Jones-Drew fumble on the Access call and recovered it. Kansas City could not use it: Colquitt's punt was downed at the Jacksonville 7, and the two sides traded punts.

**Second quarter.** From his own 11, Jones-Drew ran 21 yards on Draw and 32 on the Trips Inside call. After a 4-yard Anderson carry on 6OL Power, Lewis caught a 32-yard Sprint Flood touchdown, 21-0. Kansas City answered with an 84-yard drive. McCluster caught 20 and Knile Davis 39 to the 7, and Charles scored from there, 21-7. Lowery returned the kickoff 31 yards. Three snaps after a Derrick Johnson sack, Cousins found Travis Kelce for 53 on Mesh to the 15. Jones-Drew took it to the 4 on a Sprint Flood checkdown and a 6-yard run, and Shorts ran the last 4 for a touchdown, 28-7 with 3:45 left. Marks sacked Smith on the next series, and the half ended there.

**Third quarter.** Jacksonville took the second-half touchback and scored in seven plays. Lewis caught 33 on Mesh, and Montell Owens caught 29 to the 13. After a 1-yard 6OL Counter, the Power Pass shot came off the run as the plan intended: incomplete to Blackmon, then Shorts on the Post-Cross for a 12-yard touchdown, 35-7. Daryl Smith's sack started a Kansas City three and out. After an exchange of punts, Jacksonville's 11-play drive used six minutes: Clemons caught 17 on Mesh, Grimes ran 10 on Draw, Jones-Drew ran 12 on 6OL Counter and Shorts caught 21 on Texas to the 4. The drive stalled there, and Scobee kicked a 22-yard field goal, 38-7.

**Fourth quarter.** Charles broke a 25-yard run to open Kansas City's 80-yard drive. Smith then hit Avery, McCluster and Bowe underneath, and Bowe caught a 3-yard touchdown, 38-14 with 11:15 left. Jacksonville's eight-play reply stalled at the Kansas City 48, and Anger's punt was fair-caught at the 13. Smith completed seven straight passes, six to Avery or Charles, to reach the Jacksonville 8, and Jenkins's catch took it to the 3. On the next snap Bacarri Rambo intercepted a throw for Fasano at the goal line and returned it 18 yards. At 3:41 and 38-14, Jacksonville gained 27 yards on four snaps and Cousins knelt three times to end it.

**The numbers.**
- **Jacksonville offense:** 504 yards, 179 rushing and 325 passing. It converted 7 of 13 third downs, against 25 attempts in September, and lost one turnover.
- **Passing:** Cousins completed 20 of 30 for 325 yards and three touchdowns, with no interception and one sack.
- **Backs:** Jones-Drew ran 22 times for 144 yards and a touchdown and lost one fumble.
- **Receivers:** Lewis caught 5 for 91 and two touchdowns. Shorts caught 6 for 99 and a touchdown and ran for another. Kelce's one catch went 53 yards.
- **Kansas City:** 47 rushing yards; Charles had 13 carries for 48 and a touchdown. Smith completed 31 of 44 for 279 yards, a touchdown and two interceptions, and was sacked twice. Avery caught 12 of 13 targets for 111, with a longest catch of 22.
- **Jacksonville defense:** Daryl Smith had 14 tackles and a sack, Allen 9 tackles and the opening interception, and Marks a sack and two tackles for loss.
- **Kick returns:** Quintin Demps returned three kickoffs for 78 yards, the longest 36.
