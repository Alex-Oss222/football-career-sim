## Exclusions and publication traps

- The Gulf South PDF's storage path is not its publication date. Using December 2 would misdate the admitted statistical release.
- Conference media teams and coaches' teams are distinct. Turner's update specifies AP; the ACC comparisons specify the media ballot. Fuller's conference and national selections concern different scopes.
- Later-updated biographies can contain bowl results, full-career totals, invitations, attendance and honors not yet public at the cutoff. Only the isolated, independently supported historical facts listed above are used.
- Senior Bowl invitation evidence is separate from attendance, practice performance, measurements and the game. Nothing from those future activities is imported.
- Turner's award is not an eligibility event. Fuller's award is not medical clearance. Harris's later professional record cannot supply a missing contemporaneous status determination.
- No December 14 or later college event is used in the December 13 first-read addenda. The already closed December 15 Jacksonville checkpoint is used only for branch roster/record context.
- No new market consensus or exact pick range was established. The earlier November opinions retain their original dates and limitations.
