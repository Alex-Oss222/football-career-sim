# 2014 draft: Phase III, verify then write (Week 13)

**Status:** Plan. Issued by Alex Stone in Week 13 preparation (November 25 to December 1, 2013). It records no evaluation result.
**Authority:** Caldwell retains the board. No round movement, no final tier, no selection recommendation.
**Parent directives:** [../2014_draft_focus_directive.md](../2014_draft_focus_directive.md) and [phase_ii_role_validation.md](phase_ii_role_validation.md), both unchanged. The focus list is not expanded.

## Starting point

Every focus prospect file reads NOT STARTED, and no evaluation entry is canon. Stone does not pretend Phase II produced conclusions. Phase III creates the first legitimate dated entries: one evidence entry per focus file, not rankings.

## Verify first

Before any coach writes a football evaluation, the scouting department verifies school, current class/status, draft eligibility and a dated information source. Unresolved eligibility is written as unresolved. The sourcing record is `library/2014_draft_pool_registry.md` read through `library/2014_draft_information_gates.md`: only facts public by the entry's date may be used.

## Deliverable (by Saturday, November 30)

For each focus file, the scouting department prepares:

1. verified identity, school and status where supportable;
2. a dated source list;
3. the requested tape cut-up;
4. unresolved information, explicitly marked;
5. the coach's first-entry window, prepared.

## Workload rule

Houston follows on Thursday, December 5. Position coaches get one scouting window this week; the scouting department carries everything else. No coaching prospect work Sunday after Cleveland or Monday through Wednesday of Houston week. "The short week belongs to the roster we have."
