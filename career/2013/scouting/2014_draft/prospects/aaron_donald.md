### December 1, 2013 evidence cutoff: comparative research report

#### Assessment and evidence

Donald is included to test the opportunity cost of concentrating the first-round discussion on offensive linemen. Pitt's November 25 release documents nationally prominent backfield production and finalist recognition, while listing a comparatively compact defensive tackle. These are contemporary facts and school-listed dimensions, not future testing or an NFL outcome. [S24](../sources_2013_12_01.md).

The defensible hypothesis is an interior disruption role. Jacksonville's Under front has a weak-side three-technique assignment, which makes that hypothesis concrete. It does not follow that Donald is a nose tackle, a closed end, an immediate replacement for Marks or an already established complete starter.

**Strengths supported by tape:** Original full-game charting is absent. The source establishes disruption in college; it does not distinguish every win caused by penetration, an unblocked assignment, pursuit or a teammate's action. **Weaknesses supported by tape:** No particular recurring flaw is certified. Sustaining the assigned fit against combinations is the most important unresolved translation issue.

#### Run defense and pass rush

For a three-technique, a first-step advantage is useful only when it creates pressure or closes the intended run path without exposing the adjacent fit. A defender who penetrates indiscriminately can open a cutback or remove the linebacker’s protection. The evaluation should pair each backfield play with snaps where the offense deliberately runs at him or uses his upfield movement against him.

Against a combination, separate holding the line from surviving by slipping into another gap. The latter may be useful on a called movement but wrong on a base assignment. Compact build creates questions about reach and mass; it does not by itself answer whether leverage, hands and recognition allow the defender to retain useful ground.

Pass-rush review should isolate the initial entry, the blocker’s response and the next move. Interior rushers have a shorter path to the quarterback, but a failed first move can leave them locked up with little room. A useful pressure role requires a counter, awareness of escape lanes and coordination with the ends. Draws and screens are part of the same evaluation, because aggressive movement can be exploited.

#### First job and development cost

**Projected first job to investigate:** Rotational three-technique and an interior rush assignment, contingent on evidence that he can execute the weak B-gap fit. That is a study recommendation, not a new Jacksonville package or depth-chart change. Miller's nose responsibilities are a different job; adding one prospect does not make those responsibilities disappear.

**Development cost:** Moderate for a bounded interior role; substantial if the team expects every-down work against varied blocking surfaces immediately. Pleasant's teaching would center on get-off without losing assignment, strike/separation, response to combinations and rush-lane discipline. Crennel would need to decide which situations actually permit the attacking freedom under discussion.

**Coach's answer:** Open. A production résumé cannot show that a player has learned the branch defense. The active path is useful rotational snaps after assignment and contact standards are met, not an award-based entitlement to playing time.

#### Value and objection

The strongest objection is paying an early price for disruption that requires too much protection elsewhere in the front. The strongest reason to keep investigating is that reliable interior pressure could improve a four-man rush without changing the coverage structure. Both statements are role-based inferences, not completed grades.

Compare Donald with Reid for the interior-development question and Harris only conditionally, since Harris's status remains unresolved. Do not use those names to imply equal ability or equal price. **Tier:** Not yet graded. **What would change the view?** Consistent assignment control, viable counters and effective work when the offense targets him would support a larger role. Production dependent on free paths or uncontrolled penetration would narrow it. The source record needs additional independent film evaluation before any early-round recommendation.
