#### Coverage translation

The first study is outside corner in the current Cover 1/Cover 3 structure. Those calls share some demands but do not evaluate the same skill. Cover 1 asks whether he can maintain the required leverage through the release and route break without relying on zone help that is not there. Cover 3 asks whether he can protect the deep area, process route combinations and close underneath without being drawn out of position.

Press work should be judged through the feet as well as the hands. A forceful jam that opens the wrong shoulder can surrender the route immediately. Off coverage requires a controlled transition and correct response to the receiver's stem. Ball skills matter only after the defender remains in a position to play the pass.

The physical reputation makes run support worth studying, but collision willingness and tackling reliability are different traits. Oden needs the approach angle, relationship to inside help, wrap and finish. A corner who abandons outside leverage to seek a hit can make the rest of the fit fail.

#### First job, teaching cost and roster path

**Projected first job to investigate:** Outside corner, with the initial side and technique menu chosen after film review. There is no automatic nickel projection. Short-area route traffic, two-way releases and interior run responsibilities create a separate evaluation at nickel.

**Development cost:** Moderate for a bounded outside role; greater if rehabilitation or interrupted practice limits repetition. That statement describes a planning dependency, not a prediction about Fuller's recovery. The teaching sequence is leverage rules, release response, route transitions, then combinations and motion. A medical clearance would permit participation; it would not certify mastery of those tasks.

**Coach's answer:** Open. Grimes, Harris, Poyer and the other current corners remain in their existing branch roles. Fuller must earn a place in a future competition; a college reputation does not establish immediate superiority to the roster.

#### Value objection and alternatives

The strongest objection is combining an early acquisition cost with unresolved health and assuming a broad secondary role will justify both. The reason to investigate is that a credible outside-corner projection can be a different roster purchase from a late developmental corner. Compare his coverage evidence with Cockrell's and Butler's, while comparing acquisition cost separately.

**Tier:** Not yet graded. **What would change the view?** Dated medical information, subsequently available participation evidence and repeatable coverage technique could strengthen the case. Dependence on a narrow coverage setting would reduce flexibility. The second-round market signal means Jacksonville must account for the missing pick, rather than casually scheduling this option for later.
