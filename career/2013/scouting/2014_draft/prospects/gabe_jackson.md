#### Translation to Stone's offense

The useful run-game projection is an interior blocker whose contact can help establish the runner's initial lane. That hypothesis must be tested in the right context. A favorable down block against a defender already moving away is different from controlling a defender aligned directly over the guard. The latter asks for independent leverage, hand placement and sustained feet.

On ACE or DEUCE combinations, the question is whether his initial contact benefits the adjacent blocker and leaves a clean route to the linebacker. Winning the first collision while losing the second-level assignment can still spoil the play. On Outside, he must gain position without opening the back door. On Counter, the pulling path and ability to adjust at the point of contact deserve separate evidence.

For Cousins, immediate interior pressure matters because it disrupts the available throwing space. Jackson's evaluation should distinguish a firm initial anchor from an anchor that survives a second move. The guard also has to avoid chasing a looping defender while leaving the penetrator free. HALF and SOLID demand coordination; a blocker who wins alone but misreads exchanges can still create a pressure problem.

#### First job and development burden

**Projected first job to investigate:** Left guard, with right-guard cross-training only after the first side is functional. His background gives that study a clearer starting point than a new interior conversion. It does not establish that the college language, footwork or rush environment transfers unchanged.

**Development cost:** Moderate for one guard role. The major work would be pass-set timing, independent hand use, combination departures and the team's protection declarations. A reserve needs enough second-position competence to justify a game-day place, but adding that expectation early increases the teaching load.

**Coach's answer:** Open. The research recommendation is to compare Jackson's existing interior experience with the work required to move Bitonio or Martin inside. Tice and Yarno must determine whether the apparent reduction in conversion work results in actual earlier usefulness.

#### Acquisition argument and strongest objection

Jackson is a relevant comparator even if the final board puts him out of reach. His presence asks whether Jacksonville wants to spend its early resource on a guard, a tackle or a defensive contributor. The club cannot assume it will address tackle in round one and then obtain this particular guard in round three.

The best objection is treating a power-based reputation as proof of complete guard play. A narrow run-game benefit may not justify an early investment if pass-protection help and movement restrictions consume the gain. **Tier:** Not yet graded. **What would change the view?** Reliable isolated protection, sound exchanges and effective movement would strengthen a broad guard projection. Repeated late feet or dependence on favorable contact angles would narrow the call-sheet fit.
