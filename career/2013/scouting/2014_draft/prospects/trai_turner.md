#### Assessment and first job

The first job to investigate is right guard. It keeps the projection close to his documented college work and creates a direct evaluation beside Brewster. There is no present reason to attach center or tackle to the label. A conditional guard competition is defensible; a promise that he will replace Rackley is not.

The appeal is a player with an existing guard background and contemporaneous praise for pulling movement. The unresolved issue is whether his pass protection and communication can sustain the entire offense. Jacksonville cannot purchase only the attractive running plays and ignore the exposed dropbacks.

#### Documented evidence

LSU's biography records a 2011 redshirt and seven starts at right guard during the 2012 season. An independent August 2013 LSU position preview confirms that starting background and describes quick feet, effective pulling and improvement after difficult early experience. These are attributed contemporary assessments, not a newly conducted Jacksonville film evaluation. LSU's later-updated biography also contains bowl totals and subsequent material; none of that is admitted here. [S03–S04](../sources_2013_12_01.md).

The prior playing history supports familiarity with an interior position and meaningful college exposure. It does not reveal LSU's private protection calls, identify who made every point, or prove that Turner can use Stone's terminology. A school blocking statistic is not a substitute for distinguishing a combination assist from an isolated win.

**Strengths supported by tape:** Direct charted film is not available in this packet. The positive technical evidence is the August writer's pulling and footwork assessment. Treat it as one evaluator's opinion to verify against complete games.

#### Run-game translation

Power and Counter are attractive research fits because a guard must clear the line, locate the changing target and strike without losing the ability to redirect. The evaluation must distinguish a kick-out assignment from a wrap assignment. A hard collision against the wrong shoulder is a failed block even if it looks forceful.

In ACE work with Brewster, Turner must generate enough initial help to secure the down lineman without staying attached after the linebacker becomes his responsibility. In DEUCE work with Johnson, he must feel whether the tackle can take over before climbing. Those relationships demand timing with actual teammates. His college experience reduces novelty; it does not eliminate that development work.

The short-yardage file should test displacement without a run-up. The space file should test whether he can stop and fit a moving linebacker. If one is stronger than the other, the report should preserve that distinction rather than flattening it into a single run-blocking label.

#### Pass protection and contrary case

The central concern is pocket integrity. An interior rusher has a short path to Cousins; an early concession inside can defeat a play before an edge rusher arrives. The anchor test therefore concerns usable pocket depth, hand replacement and recovery after the first contact, not body weight alone.

Against line games, require the correct initial point, an audible exchange with Brewster or Johnson, and a square handoff. Evaluate the repetition after a mistake separately: correction retained, same technical error, or new problem. Do not turn a repeated error into a character allegation.

**Weaknesses supported by tape:** There is insufficient direct evidence to diagnose chronic lunging, poor anchor or slow recognition. The preseason source describes early struggle followed by improvement, but it does not supply a current failure rate. The strongest football objection is that the attractive run-game projection is ahead of the verified pass-protection evidence.

#### Development cost and game-day role

**Practice cost: moderate for right guard alone; substantial if asked to solve multiple interior positions immediately.** Begin with stance, first two steps, inside-hand placement and recovery. Then pair him with the center for MIKE, ACE and protection changes, and with the tackle for DEUCE and twist exchanges. Test the same rule against a different defensive presentation to distinguish learning from memorizing a picture.

The game-day pathway is earned guard depth, then competition for a starting job. A reserve guard who cannot handle another position creates a dressing constraint, but that constraint should be acknowledged rather than solved by inventing versatility. In a roster with Brewster starting and Meester available as reserve center, a guard need not become the entire interior backup at once.

**Can the job be taught?** The protection vocabulary and blocking techniques are teachable. His learning speed is unknown without legal contact, actual instruction and retained performance. No interview, board session or response from Turner has occurred in this report.

#### Acquisition reasoning

Stone's third-round focus is an evaluation target, not a price quotation. Compare Turner with Jackson and Richardson for direct guard evidence, and with Thomas for the cost of a tackle-to-guard route. If another guard has a more complete protection case, Turner's preferred status cannot cancel that advantage. If Turner stays in college, the alternatives must remain usable without an emergency rewrite.

**Tier:** Not yet graded. Identity and the prior right-guard role are well supported; NFL anchor, communication, exact market range and eligibility remain distinct unresolved items. No medical conclusion is drawn from the absence of a current report.

**What would make us change our mind?** Verified special eligibility is necessary before selection. On football, a stable pocket against isolated interior power, clean exchanges and correction retention would strengthen the case. Repeated quick pressure through his assigned gap would weaken it even if the pull-blocking tape is attractive. A changed declaration status changes availability, not ability.
