The central uncertainty is whether the tackle projection survives professional edge speed and counters.

#### Protection and run-game translation

The tackle assessment should begin with true pass sets in which the blocker cannot rely on the ball leaving immediately. Distinguish a controlled set that keeps the inside lane protected from a wide initial gain that exposes an inside counter. A successful first punch matters less if the feet stop and the rusher wins the second exchange. Judge the pocket's depth and width, not only whether a sack occurs.

An interior projection changes the problem. The rusher reaches the blocker sooner; the guard has less space to recover and must protect the center-tackle relationship. HALF and SOLID work require a clear distinction between helping an adjacent blocker and abandoning an immediate threat. A college tackle's familiarity with the edge does not demonstrate that compressed timing.

In the run game, compare down-block leverage with reach-block positioning. Stone's Power and Counter require dependable contact before any second-level climb. Inside and Outside require enough control to keep the defender from crossing the runner's track. These are separate movement demands; an all-purpose label must not hide a weakness in one family.

#### First job, teaching cost and active role

**Projected first job to investigate:** Tackle reserve with one primary side. Guard is a separate conversion study, not a promised second job. Jacksonville already has Monroe and Johnson as starters and Bradfield in the reserve/extra-lineman role. Martin must be compared with the actual value of that arrangement rather than assigned a vacancy that does not exist.

**Development cost:** Moderate for one tackle assignment; substantial if the plan requires immediate guard work and emergency duty on both edges. Tice and Yarno would need to establish set landmarks, hand replacement, recovery after losing initial leverage, and the team's exchange language. Cross-training comes after the first position is stable.

**Coach's answer:** Open. This report supplies a teaching sequence but no evidence that either coach has delivered it or that Martin has absorbed it. Dressing as a reserve requires credible emergency protection, not only a long-term starting projection.

#### Value objection and decision trigger

The best objection is paying an early price for nominal versatility while the first usable job is still unresolved. Compare Martin and Bitonio at tackle before comparing either with Richardson or Jackson at guard. If Martin proves the better tackle and the team values that role, his interior possibility is a bonus. If only guard is convincing, value him against the guards.

**Tier:** Not yet graded. **What would change the view?** Sustained edge protection and recoveries against counters would strengthen the tackle case. Repeated dependence on help would narrow it. Interior work would need its own evidence before changing the first-job recommendation. The contemporary market signal makes waiting until round three a risk, not proof of unavailability.
