#### Assessment and first job

Keep Bitonio at tackle for the initial evaluation, with a separately tested guard conversion. The evidence available now supports a college tackle prospect; it does not establish that an NFL guard conversion has already succeeded. For Jacksonville, the practical first role to investigate is reserve tackle with a route into interior competition if the guard work proves stronger. Monroe and Johnson currently occupy the tackle jobs. That roster context changes the route to playing time, not Bitonio's ability or grade.

This is a deliberate distinction from simply calling him versatile. A reserve who can execute one protection assignment correctly is more useful than a player described as covering several positions without evidence at any of them. Stone's first-round interest warrants an early-investment comparison against Martin, Richardson and Jackson, but it does not establish first-round value.

#### Evidence supporting the case

Nevada's 2013 biography identifies a senior tackle entering the season with 26 consecutive starts and describes established starting work in 2011 and 2012. Its preseason listing is 6-foot-4, 315 pounds, a school measurement rather than a verified NFL measurement. The school's previous-season blocking recognition supports sustained college relevance, not a numerical NFL grade. The NFL's preseason Mountain West preview separately praised his movement. [S01–S02 in the source register](../sources_2013_12_01.md).

That combination makes the movement-to-contact question worth serious investigation. In Stone's offense, tackle value includes reaching an edge, handling the backside of Inside, and arriving square for DEUCE combinations. Guard value includes an ACE combination with the center and the ability to redirect on Power/Counter pulls. Athletic movement can help each task, but success at one does not prove the others.

**Strengths supported by tape:** No independently charted tape observation is being claimed. The documented starting experience and attributed movement assessment are the supporting evidence. There is no verified pressure-allowed rate, isolated matchup win rate or snap-by-snap grade in this packet.

#### Protection and technique questions

The tackle evaluation should separate three losses that demand different corrections. Losing outside because the set is late calls for a change in set angle and timing. Giving up the inside after opening early calls for control of the inside half and independent hands. Being driven backward after establishing position is an anchor question. Calling all three problems slow feet would conceal the actual teaching task.

For speed-to-power, the deciding evidence is whether he maintains a useful base after contact and can reset his hands without surrendering the pocket. For twists, distinguish a missed declaration from a correctly identified exchange lost physically. The active book's HALF and SCAT protections create different timing demands; a short college throw cannot establish protection on a longer NFL concept.

**Weaknesses supported by tape:** Not established here. In particular, the packet does not assert short arms, weak anchor, chronic overextension or inability to play tackle. Those would require measurements or observed repetitions. The main limitation in the evaluation is the unproven translation between positions and levels.

#### Run-game fit and guard test

The guard trial should answer a narrow question: can he preserve the pocket and produce useful movement in reduced space better than he can protect the edge? On combinations, require control of the first defender before climbing. On a pull, distinguish reaching the target from arriving balanced enough to change its path. On the backside, determine whether he can cut off penetration without lunging across a defender's face.

The guard file should be graded independently. If the strongest evidence remains at tackle, retain that projection even if Jacksonville has an immediate interior need. If guard becomes the better first job, the report must identify the particular blocks and protection situations that justify the change. Need cannot supply missing evidence.

#### Development cost and game-day pathway

**Practice cost: moderate at one position; substantial if tackle and guard are taught simultaneously.** Start with one stance, one side and the core protection rules. Add the opposite side only after set landmarks, inside-counter recovery and exchanges remain sound on unfamiliar looks. Guard work adds different contact timing and center communication, not merely a new place to stand.

A rookie reserve must prove a complete emergency assignment set. A sixth-lineman package can expose useful blocking traits, but a successful extra-OL snap does not certify an emergency tackle. Carrying him while he develops displaces another reserve; dressing him requires an actual contingency job. No starting place or game-day activation is awarded in this report.

**Can the projected job be taught?** The rules and mechanics can be taught. His retention and ability to execute under pressure remain untested. The cost is repeated individual correction plus paired work with the adjacent guard, followed by team repetitions with a changing front.

#### Best objection, value and decision conditions

The strongest objection is paying for a first-round starting lineman while the best-supported present case is still a college tackle with an unresolved NFL position. Jacksonville lacks its second-round pick, making a mistaken first selection harder to compensate for. That is a reason to compare complete football roles, not a reason to reach automatically.

**Tier:** Not yet graded. Confidence is stronger in the college identity and experience than in the NFL position or acquisition range. No dated source reviewed here establishes a December 1 first-round consensus for Bitonio.

**What would make us change our mind?** Repeated independent tackle evidence against speed, counters and power would strengthen the outside projection. A better guard file in isolated anchor, combinations and exchanges would change the first job. Failure at both would reduce the acquisition case. A later testing result or later published evaluation enters only when its information gate opens.
