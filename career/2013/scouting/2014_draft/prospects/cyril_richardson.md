Movement efficiency and protection against quick interior counters require direct review rather than assumptions from body type.

#### What the guard role requires

The attraction is a prospect who could make the interior run game more dependable without changing Stone's offensive identity. For Power, ask whether he can establish displacement with useful leverage and maintain the block when the defender attempts to cross his face. For a combination, evaluate the moment of departure: leaving too soon exposes the first-level defender; staying too long allows the linebacker to close.

Counter and the pulling assignments add a different requirement. The blocker must clear traffic, arrive on the correct shoulder and remain balanced through contact. A powerful collision at the wrong entry point can compress the lane for the runner. Short-area footwork and timing are therefore as important as the eventual force of the block.

Pass protection is the decisive limit on a guard-first early selection. In SCAT, the quarterback cannot count on a spare protector to rescue repeated interior losses. Richardson would need to keep the near hip connected, reset his hands and recover without opening an immediate lane. In HALF, the exchange with center and tackle must survive a late change in the front. None of those team-specific skills follows automatically from a college accolade.

#### First job and teaching cost

**Projected first job to investigate:** One guard position, followed by a competition for useful interior snaps. His college tackle history is context, not an emergency-tackle certification. Begin the comparison with Rackley, Nwaneri and the reserve interior arrangement; do not displace anyone through this research report.

**Development cost:** Moderate if one guard position and a limited set of combinations form the initial menu. It rises if the team expects a pulling specialist, both guard positions and tackle cover immediately. Teach stance and first two steps alongside the protection vocabulary, then assess whether those habits persist when defenders change their approach.

**Coach's answer:** Open. Tice and Yarno would need to decide whether the movement demands of the actual call sheet are teachable within the expected roster role. A favorable answer requires observed correction and repetition, not a general belief that a large lineman can be coached.

#### Comparison and objection

Richardson versus Turner is partly an eligibility and resource question: the former is a senior comparison; Turner remains conditional. Richardson versus Bitonio or Martin is primarily a role question: guard confidence may be more useful than a speculative tackle/guard combination, but it purchases different roster flexibility.

The best objection is paying for college displacement without confirming isolated pass protection and movement on the intended runs. **Tier:** Not yet graded. **What would change the view?** Controlled pass sets against quickness, effective pulls through traffic and properly timed combinations would support broader usage. Protection requiring persistent adjacent help would reduce the value even if the run blocking remains attractive.
