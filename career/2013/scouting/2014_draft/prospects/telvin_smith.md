### December 1, 2013 evidence cutoff: comprehensive research report

**Chronology note:** This retrospective research supplement retains a December 1 evidence cutoff. It does not replace or close the December 13 Phase IV coach-read entry above.

**Report type:** Ex-ante research and conditional coaching analysis, not a completed Crennel/Bush/Lowry meeting. Senior linebacker; selection clearance follows the registry. [Source register](../sources_2013_12_01.md).

#### Assessment and first job

Investigate weak-side linebacker first, with a limited sub-package assignment built around pursuit and back coverage. Do not make him the projected replacement for every responsibility Posluszny currently carries. The Week 13 injury creates a current-roster decision, whereas Smith is a future prospect. The two problems must remain separate.

The active Under defense gives WILL a weak A-gap fit, pursuit responsibilities and back-match work. That provides a concrete first job. It also exposes the central test: can his movement get him to the correct leverage before a blocker closes the path? A fast arrival in the wrong gap does not help the defense.

#### Supporting evidence and role clarification

Florida State's 2013 material identifies a senior linebacker. Contemporary reports describe Smith and Christian Jones sharing or changing linebacker responsibilities. The August Phil Savage observations identify Smith's speed as a distinguishing quality. That supports investigating a space-oriented role, but it does not justify treating him as a permanently fixed college MIKE or assuming he owns an NFL communication job. [S05–S07](../sources_2013_12_01.md).

**Clarification of the November 30 entry:** Its simple middle-linebacker label is too narrow to carry the full projection. The earlier entry remains intact; the contemporary descriptions support movement between responsibilities. The report must evaluate the actual assignment on a play, not infer it from the roster abbreviation.

**Strengths supported by tape:** No direct full-game chart is being claimed. Speed and role breadth are attributed source observations. Coverage results, missed-tackle rate and block-defeat consistency are not quantified or assumed.

#### Run fits and contact translation

The decisive run sequence begins before pursuit. Identify the key, maintain the assigned fit, then close. On a flowing run, he must avoid overrunning the cutback. Against a climbing guard, the useful question is whether he can preserve a shoulder and escape into the fit, rather than simply whether he makes contact.

His lighter college build creates a legitimate projection question about repeatedly facing NFL blockers. It does not prove he cannot play base defense. Evaluate early recognition, approach angle, pad level and separation independently. If the fit can be protected only by asking the line to play a different structure every time he enters, the package carries a real cost.

Tackling must be evaluated at the finishing point: balanced approach, leverage relative to help, wrap and control. Chasing down a runner after a poor angle demonstrates recovery speed, not a successful initial fit. A tackle total would not resolve that distinction.

#### Coverage, pressure and package limits

For zone work, require an appropriate drop landmark, awareness of routes entering and leaving the area, and a response to play-action that does not surrender the intermediate window. For man work, separate carrying a back on a straight release from matching an option route or a late direction change. Long speed cannot certify the second task.

Pressure should be a later addition to the first assignment set. Crennel can consider a defined rush or replacement responsibility once Smith understands the coverage consequence. Teaching base fits, every linebacker position, safety work and a full pressure menu simultaneously would make a versatile prospect harder to evaluate.

**Weaknesses supported by tape:** Insufficient direct evidence to establish a particular recurring technical defect. The live objection is role limitation: if he cannot control a fit against blocks and cannot match routes reliably, pursuit alone leaves too narrow a defensive job.

#### Development cost and special teams

**Practice cost: moderate for one linebacker role; substantial for a full MIKE/WILL/coverage conversion.** Start with the weak-side key and leverage rules, then add the paired fit with the adjacent lineman and linebacker. Progress to back releases and motion only after the initial assignment remains stable. Re-test after correction rather than awarding learning credit for an articulate explanation.

Special teams is a plausible route to dressing, not an established credential. Lowry needs evidence of lane integrity, block avoidance, finishing angles and adjustment to the returner's path. Speed alone cannot make someone a kickoff-cover player. A punt assignment also requires its own release and containment evidence; the report does not invent a gunner role.

The early defensive pathway would be backup WILL plus a clearly bounded sub-package if earned. He must also meet the coverage-unit standard. If neither job is ready, carrying him consumes a development spot without an immediate active contribution.

#### Best objection and alternatives

The strongest objection is selecting a space athlete while assuming the difficult contact and route-recognition work will take care of itself. Compare him with Christian Jones for role breadth and with Van Noy for a more edge-oriented allocation of linebacker resources. Those are different football purchases, not interchangeable labels.

**Tier:** Not yet graded. The speed case is better supported than a claim of complete coverage technique or every-down readiness. Stone's fourth-round focus is not evidence that Smith will last until Jacksonville's fourth selection window.

**What would make us change our mind?** Repeated correct fits through traffic and controlled finishes would strengthen the base role. Reliable route recognition would support a larger sub-package. Consistent displacement by blockers or false steps that erase the movement advantage would narrow the role. Verified special-teams performance can establish an active path; it cannot erase a defensive limitation.

**Answer to the Phase IV question:** Investigate WILL first in Jacksonville's Under defense. That is a professional role hypothesis, not a claim that Smith played only WILL in college. The newly verified middle-linebacker listing should remain in the record alongside the earlier evidence of changing responsibilities.

The new game contributes evidence of involvement against the run, in pressure and on a pass turnover. It does not establish that one player can immediately own every linebacker responsibility. A sack does not certify an independent edge-rush role; an interception does not certify consistent back-match coverage. The useful translation question remains whether recognition and leverage allow his movement to produce correct weak-side fits.

**Teams pathway:** Still OPEN. None of the new sources establishes kickoff lane integrity, punt coverage releases or a repeatable tackling assignment for Lowry. Defensive production cannot fill that field.

**Tier:** Not yet graded. **Next decision-changing evidence:** Full-game confirmation of keys, contact and coverage responsibility around the recorded positive plays, including ordinary and unsuccessful snaps.
