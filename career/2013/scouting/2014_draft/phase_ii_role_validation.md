# 2014 draft: Phase II, role validation (Week 12)

**Status:** Plan. Issued by Alex Stone in Week 12 preparation (November 18-24, 2013). It records no evaluation result.
**Authority:** Caldwell and the scouting department own the formal board. Stone gives no numerical grade.
**Parent directive:** [../2014_draft_focus_directive.md](../2014_draft_focus_directive.md), unchanged. No player is added to the focus list.

## Purpose

Week 12 is role-validation week. For each focus player the scouting department tries to disprove the first projected role: a tackle projection looks for the tape that argues guard, an outside-corner projection looks for the tape that argues he cannot survive outside, a developmental-center projection looks for evidence that the teaching cost is too high. Stone does not want confirmation scouting.

## Deliverable: interim card (not the final report)

One page per focus player, due Friday of Week 12.

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| Initial projected job | One job |
| Strongest supporting evidence | Tape-based |
| Strongest contrary evidence | Tape-based |
| Technique requiring most work | Specific |
| Practice cost | Low / moderate / substantial, with explanation |
| Early game-day pathway | Offense / defense / special teams / none yet established |
| Open question | What remains unknown |
| Change-our-mind trigger | Specific evidence |

Where reliable film cannot answer a field, the card says **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**; projection never fills the blank. Cards are appended to each prospect's file as dated entries under the rules in [README.md](README.md).

## Workload rule

Position coaches get two defined scouting windows in Week 12. No prospect work during the Houston opponent meeting, install, practice preparation, corrections or individual player meetings. The scouting department carries the bulk. "We're drafting in the spring. We're playing Houston Sunday."
