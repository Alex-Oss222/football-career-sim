## Next evidence that would change the reports

For the offensive line, record the protection context, assistance, initial contact and recovery on complete snaps. For centers, include the point and snap in the same observation. A block cannot be evaluated independently of the assignment when that assignment is unknown. Do not infer private calls from a gesture alone.

For linebackers, connect the key and first steps to the fit or coverage responsibility. Distinguish a correct assignment from a late recovery that happens to produce a tackle. For corners, separate release leverage, route response, catch-point work and run support. Lowry's teams question needs evidence of the specific unit and task, not merely speed or a defensive position.
