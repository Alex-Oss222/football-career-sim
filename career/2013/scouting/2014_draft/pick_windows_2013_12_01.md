## Decision 1: what does an early offensive lineman add?

Monroe and Johnson occupy the starting tackle jobs. Bradfield already provides reserve/extra-lineman utility. Nwaneri, Brewster and Rackley occupy the starting interior positions, with Meester and other reserves behind them. Those are the current branch assignments, not promises about next season. The comparison must begin with that actual room rather than a fictional vacant line.

For Bitonio and Martin, separate three claims: can he play tackle; can he play guard; can he perform more than one role early enough to justify a game-day place? Evidence for the first does not complete the second or third. Calling a player versatile before establishing his first useful position makes the projected benefit larger while hiding the teaching cost.

Richardson and Jackson provide guard-specific benchmarks. If the interior projection is materially more convincing, an early guard can be a coherent purchase. If Bitonio's or Martin's tackle work is the stronger reason to select him, the report should explain why that future or reserve benefit justifies the resource despite the current starters. The answer cannot simply be that Stone requested the name.

Donald offers a different use of that resource. The comparison is not a fabricated cross-position ranking. It asks whether the eventual evidence supports a more consequential contribution from an interior defender than from an offensive lineman whose first role remains developmental. His three-technique study must still demonstrate fit control, not only backfield disruption.

**Research conclusion:** Resolve the first-job question before settling the first-pick preference. There is not enough direct film evidence in this packet to make a final selection recommendation.

## Decision 2: protect the plan against the missing second round

The gap between the first and third selections makes it risky to build a sequence around a specific player who may be valued in between. It does not justify reaching automatically. The practical alternatives are to choose a different position with the first resource, accept a later developmental option, or investigate a trade with an explicit cost. No trade is recommended or executed here.

Turner creates a separate uncertainty because the December record does not establish a declaration. The guard plan needs to work if he is not in the pool. Thomas provides an interior study with prior positional breadth; Jackson and Richardson supply stronger contemporary market warnings against treating them as automatic fallbacks. Their exact future grades and availability remain unresolved.

If Turner declares later, the football questions still remain: can he anchor independently, exchange interior movement and execute the intended Power/Counter assignments without excessive protection help? A declaration changes eligibility, not the technique evaluation. If he does not declare, the report should close the acquisition branch without moving another player upward merely to preserve Stone's preferred sequence.

**Research conclusion:** Carry an interior-role comparison and an alternative-position use of the third-round resource. Do not make the whole offensive-line plan dependent on one conditional entrant.

## Decision 3: choose the linebacker job before the linebacker

Smith's proposed first study is WILL: weak A fit, pursuit and back-match responsibilities. Jones and Van Noy begin as SAM studies, where contact at the surface, force relationships and complementary pressure matter more directly. The comparisons are useful precisely because those jobs differ.

If the need is a space-oriented WILL, require Smith to show correct leverage before rewarding his movement. If the need is a strong-side player who can contribute pressure, evaluate Jones and Van Noy on that sequence instead. Neither alternative should inherit the MIKE's declaration responsibility simply because he has played several college positions.

Posluszny's current injury is a present-tense roster problem. Daryl Smith is the current branch's defensive communication lead; Telvin Smith's prospect report creates no change to that arrangement. The injury cannot be solved by a future prospect report, and it cannot inflate that prospect's grade. Coverage-team work is another distinct source of possible value. Lowry must establish the actual assignment, release, lane and finish, rather than accepting an athletic label as a dressing pathway.

**Research conclusion:** One base job plus one earned complement is a more assessable rookie plan than an undefined all-purpose linebacker role. Formal coach answers remain open.

## Decision 4: spend late picks on a workable development plan

Leno and Hurst should be compared on the reserve floor before the long-term ceiling. The important questions are what happens after losing initial leverage, whether the inside counter remains protected and how much of the normal passing plan survives an emergency substitution. Right- and left-side work need separate evidence. College starts alone cannot certify a swing tackle.

Paradis, Stork and Swanson should be compared on the complete center sequence: front recognition, point/combination communication, snap and block. A mistake in any part can erase the value of the others. Swanson's conflicting preseason contact assessments make isolated versus assisted work a specific comparison rather than a general argument about toughness. No prospect is given Brewster's job, and Meester's presence does not guarantee an effortless succession plan.

Harris's study remains behind a status gate. Historical interior production permits research but does not clear eligibility, establish current conditioning or authorize a first-practice alignment. Reid provides an interior comparison without requiring that missing status to be guessed. Donald supplies an earlier-resource benchmark, not an assumed equivalent.

Butler and Cockrell require an outside-corner foundation before any nickel expansion. Interceptions and conference recognition justify investigation, but the professional projection depends on release technique, leverage, transitions, tackling and communication. Return work or a blocked-kick return cannot be silently converted into a coverage-unit résumé.

**Research conclusion:** A late-round project should have a named first role, a realistic teaching sequence and a reason to occupy a roster place while developing. Several attractive projects can still form an impractical class if all need the same protected practice time and none can dress.

The correct present answer is not to choose both on paper. Keep the two role studies distinct, improve the evidence, and compare the actual available players when the window exists. If one focus target is gone, the remaining target does not receive a grade bonus by default.

For each film finding, record the game, play context, alignment, assigned task, observed result and alternative explanation. Repeated independent wins can support a strength; repeated comparable losses can support a weakness. A highlight, school statistic or secondhand opinion alone cannot establish either at the same confidence.
