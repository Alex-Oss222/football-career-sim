## Answers the evidence can and cannot support

**First position:** The working research proposals remain tackle first for Bitonio, right guard for Turner, WILL for Smith, a one-side tackle foundation for Leno, center for Paradis and outside corner for Butler. These are explicit hypotheses to test, not completed staff determinations. Harris receives no first alignment while his status remains unresolved.

**Teachability and cost:** The mechanism is clearer than the individual learning outcome. Bitonio and Leno need one stable position before broad cross-training. Turner needs contact and protection work paired with the adjacent linemen. Smith needs a dependable base key and fit before an expanded package. Paradis needs communication and physical execution assessed together. Butler needs leverage and route transitions before nickel expansion. The reports do not pretend that an actual correction was delivered or retained.

**Strengths and concerns:** Smith's game record and Butler's statistical record establish dated outcomes, not every cause behind them. The former still needs assignment and block context; the latter still lacks target and route denominators. For the offensive linemen, recognition is useful context but cannot establish pressure rates, anchor consistency or the share of a combination block each player won.

**Game-day usefulness:** Developmental value and dressing value remain separate. A tackle needs an emergency protection package; a center needs the entire snap/point/block sequence; a linebacker or corner needs a defensive role or an independently established teams contribution. No source in this update makes those jobs automatic.

**Tier and authority:** All twenty reports remain not yet graded. Caldwell retains the board, round movement and selection decisions. No player is promoted because Stone named him, because a comparison player received an award or because the branch suffered an injury or loss.
