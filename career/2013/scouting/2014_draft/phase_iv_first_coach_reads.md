## Rule for every read

A coach's read may answer the question or say **OPEN** or **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**. Any answer must cite specific, dated football evidence in the prospect's entry. "Versatile" and "athletic" are not answers. An honest blank beats a confident invention.
