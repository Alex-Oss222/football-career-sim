## Evidence boundaries

- Full-page reading improves the verification of a published statement. It does not turn that statement into independent film analysis.
- A national or regional selection establishes the named recognition. It cannot establish isolated block success, coverage efficiency, learning response or an exact draft price.
- Archived biographies and later articles encountered during search remain excluded when their relevant public date cannot be established. Search results about future professional outcomes are not evidence for this decision.
- This report does not use a later bowl result, later full-season total or a later all-star performance. The scheduling announcement in N05 is prospective information only.
- The official media ballot, school team award, AP list, FWAA list and AFCA selection remain distinct. No combined numerical score or final ranking is constructed.
- Existing twenty-prospect entries remain verbatim. New material answers what changed, what carries forward and what evidence would change the decision.
