# December 1, 2013 scouting evidence and source register

**Prepared:** September 28, 2026. **Information cutoff:** December 1, 2013. This register supports the expanded [prospect reports](README.md) and [pick-window analysis](pick_windows_2013_12_01.md). It records research, not elapsed simulation time or completed staff work.

## Reading the evidence correctly

The new entries are research reports with conditional football analysis. They do not claim that Jacksonville's staff watched full games, charted snaps, interviewed prospects, reviewed private medical files or met during the short-week blackout. Public assessments are attributed. Proposed roles and teaching sequences are the report author's inferences for later staff review.

Four evidence categories remain distinct:

- **Documented:** A cited source supports the particular identity, historical role, dated event or prior-season fact. School-listed measurements remain school measurements.
- **Attributed assessment:** A contemporary evaluator or school describes a trait. Repeating the description does not turn it into an independently verified Jacksonville film finding.
- **Projection:** The report explains how a possible trait or background could relate to the active 2013 playbook. This is conditional analysis, not a result.
- **Open:** Eligibility, medical clearance, technique consistency, learning response, availability or another conclusion is not established by the admitted evidence.

Archived roster pages often combine an authentic preseason section with material added after the season. Only explicitly earlier history, preseason text and applicable identity fields are used. A page carrying "2013" in its title is not sufficient proof that every sentence was available on December 1. Publication dates that cannot be established are marked below rather than invented.

Only college events and releases available by the cutoff may inform the reports. Later declarations, final awards, bowl events, all-star results, testing, injuries, professional careers and actual draft outcomes are excluded. Sources with later updates are usable only where an earlier dated subsection or historical fact can be separated reliably. Uncertain later material is left out.
