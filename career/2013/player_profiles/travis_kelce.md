## Best traits

- Attached/wing-to-flex alignment corrections held through spring; moving routes earned second-group work and transferred to preseason assignments.
- Alignment and assignment. Rookie minicamp recorded Good on the attached/wing alignment menu. In OTAs he "handled attached-to-flex alignment changes correctly," and the standouts record reads "Alignment knowledge traveled." No phase record shows an assignment error; every recorded correction was technique.
- Route and movement work that transferred. Camp: "Kelce's best movement/route work earned a defined second-group role." Preseason: held his route assignment on a touchdown drive against the Jets; transferred his assigned route job against Philadelphia; sustained it at Atlanta. He carried three weeks of in-season additions and was in the regular call structure from Week 8.
- His four catches from Week 10 through the Wild Card went for 36 at Tennessee, 14 against Buffalo, 28 against Tennessee and 53 at Kansas City.

## Main weaknesses

- Run-block base, hands and pad level needed repeated correction after alignment changes. These are recorded technique losses, with no in-game blocking retest.
