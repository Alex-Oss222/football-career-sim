- The Week 2 held-up line, the Wild Card fair catch at the 13, the Divisional punt downed at the 9 and the late Divisional punt after which Tennessee started at its own 10 are noted, not claimed: without the special-teams film review Stone could not separate them from the spot and the coverage.

## Main weaknesses

- Catch-to-kick time, hang and direction are not measured. Sampled punt outcomes, return responsibility and the Week 12 zero-distance entry do not classify his skill.
- Plus-territory punting. Lowry's own rematch note named "plus-territory punt execution" after the punt downed at the line from the Houston 49. That punt is the one the record names; its cause (snap, protection, contact or kick) is not recorded, and Stone did not name one.
- Punt and coverage on the three returns that went on correction lists: Martin 26 (Week 12), Martin 33 (Week 14, after "better punt lanes" was the plan) and Hilton 26 on the last snap at Indianapolis. Each is classified as punt (hang and placement against the intended punt), coverage (lane and leverage) or call.
- Teaching state: none is recorded for him; the records speak to the unit, and Stone did not build one from the averages.
