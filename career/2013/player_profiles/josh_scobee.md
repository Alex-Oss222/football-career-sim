## Best traits

- The record includes a strong bye-week practice make result and perfect recorded kicking from Week 14 through the Divisional game.
- The kicks that tied games or won them went in: the Week 3 tying kick with 5:41 left and the winner on the final snap, the third-quarter 47 that tied Week 5, the Week 13 overtime winner, and the Week 14 extra point that put Jacksonville ahead with 3:34 left.
- The finish. He made every field goal and extra point he attempted from Week 14 through the Divisional, including five field goals in Week 17.
- The first half of the season: 14 of 15 at the bye, with the weekly record showing no operation issue in any of those games.

## Main weaknesses

- The midseason short/long misses and missed try have no kick/snap/hold cause. The old flat long-distance model cannot grade leg range or pressure consistency.
- Apart from the 51 at Denver, the misses clustered from Week 10 to Week 13: 22 and 27 at Tennessee, 30 against Arizona, 48 and an extra point at Cleveland. All five went on the weekly correction lists, the Tennessee pair as "Two missed field goals inside 30 yards." None is classified by cause. Stone told him he would not call any of them a kick problem, a snap, a hold or protection until the special-teams review says which, and told him the Week 10 to 13 tape had not been broken down that way.
- Teaching state: the record gives none for a kicker, and Stone did not build one from make totals.
