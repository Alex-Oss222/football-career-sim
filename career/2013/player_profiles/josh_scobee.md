- **My judgment: Snap-hold operation — 7.0 /10.** Strong NFL trait.

**Recorded support:**
- The record includes a strong bye-week practice make result and perfect recorded kicking from Week 14 through the Divisional game.
- The kicks that tied games or won them went in: the Week 3 tying kick with 5:41 left and the winner on the final snap, the third-quarter 47 that tied Week 5, the Week 13 overtime winner, and the Week 14 extra point that put Jacksonville ahead with 3:34 left.
- The finish. He made every field goal and extra point he attempted from Week 14 through the Divisional, including five field goals in Week 17.
- The first half of the season: 14 of 15 at the bye, with the weekly record showing no operation issue in any of those games.
