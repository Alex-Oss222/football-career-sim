## Season production in context

Regular season: 357 of 564, 3,981 yards, 23 touchdowns, 18 interceptions,
84.5 passer rating, 38 sacks, 37 rushes for 106 yards and two lost fumbles.
The 2013 NFL passing environment averaged 61.2% completions, 7.1 yards per
attempt and an 86.0 passer rating. Cousins therefore produced near the league
average in aggregate passing efficiency, with a better-than-league completion
rate, league-average yards per attempt and a slightly below-average passer
rating. The branch engine's known attribution defects mean those totals are
context, not direct grades for processing, protection or placement.

The postseason was cleaner statistically: 38 of 59 for 480 yards, four
touchdowns and no interceptions across two games. That supports the final
season picture but does not erase the regular-season inconsistency.

## What made him this player in 2013

- Clean huddle, cadence, protection-point and two-minute operation won and held the QB1 job.
- He functioned best as a rhythm passer when timing and the initial defensive picture stayed coherent.
- Changed-picture decisions, pressure answers and some placement remained inconsistent rather than physically unavailable throws.

## Play style

Rhythm-oriented pocket quarterback. He wants to operate on time, keep the
offense organized and win with timing, leverage and ordinary NFL arm talent
rather than with rare arm strength or explosive running ability.

## Best traits

- Taught offensive operation, cadence and command.
- Short/intermediate rhythm timing and functional pocket movement.
- Ability to correct a specific mistake quickly when the cause is clear.

## Main weaknesses

- Changed-picture processing and pressure decision consistency.
- Deep/outside placement and high-difficulty throws are less reliable than the short/intermediate rhythm game.
- Turnover clusters show that his best decision-making was not yet repeatable across the full season.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Branch evidence used:** [2013 exit interview](../exit_interviews/main_core/kirk_cousins.md), its linked offseason/camp records, 16 regular-season game receipts, two playoff games and the [generated statbook](../stats/team_player_stats.md).
- **Historical benchmark sources:** [2013 NFL environment, qualified leaders and physical testing](../../../library/annual_player_sheet_benchmark_method.md#research-sources); testing dates differ and do not measure 2013 game speed.
- **What is established:** NFL-sufficient arm, functional pocket movement, strong taught operation and a credible starter-level 2013 season.
- **What remains uncertain:** Exact causes of several interceptions and sacks, and how much of the pressure inconsistency belongs to QB decision, protection, receiver or caller.

**One-line description:**  
A viable 2013 NFL starter whose command and rhythm passing were ahead of his changed-picture consistency, with enough arm and mobility to run the offense but no elite physical trait carrying him through bad decisions.
