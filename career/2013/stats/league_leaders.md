# 2013 NFL statistical leaders

**Version:** `NFL-2013-W01-LEADERS-WITHHELD-1`  
**Through:** Week 1, September 9, 2013.  
**Status:** **FORMAL LEAGUE RANKINGS WITHHELD.**

The committed Week 1 background roundup did not preserve complete player-level box scores for all fifteen non-Jacksonville games. Ranking the preserved standout lines as if they were the complete league would create false leaders.

Known Week 1 statistical lines are available in [league_player_stats.md](league_player_stats.md). Formal passing, rushing, receiving, sack, interception and tackle leaderboards begin only after the Week 1 coverage gap is canonically backfilled or otherwise reconciled.

Starting with subsequent closed games, full public stat receipts are required so future leaderboards can be rebuilt mechanically.
