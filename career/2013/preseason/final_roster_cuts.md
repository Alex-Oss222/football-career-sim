# 2013 final roster cuts

**Status:** COMPLETE through September 4, 2013.

## August 27 — 75-player deadline

Jacksonville controlled 64 players and was already compliant. **No release was required or invented.**

## August 31 — final 53

Caldwell accepted Stone's football recommendations after reviewing the entire offseason, camp and all four branch-simulated games. The club released/waived: Tyler Bray, Richard Murphy, Will Ta'ufo'ou, Jerrell Jackson, Isaiah Stanback, Austen Lane, Jerome Long, D'Anthony Smith, Brandon Marshall, Brandon King, Antwon Blake.

Execution and observable effort were evaluated separately. Special-teams credit required communicated responsibility, lane/leverage and pursuit evidence; acquisition or draft status supplied no preference. Pasztor was retained while medically held, and Mosley was retained despite his medical limitation; availability was not treated as effort.

## September 1 — waivers and practice squad

Autonomous clubs claimed **Austen Lane, Brandon Marshall and Isaiah Stanback**. The remaining eight preferred, eligible and available players cleared and signed to Jacksonville's practice squad: Tyler Bray, Richard Murphy, Will Ta'ufo'ou, Jerrell Jackson, Jerome Long, D'Anthony Smith, Brandon King, Antwon Blake.

The practice squad is separate from the active 53. No waived player was presumed available before the claim period closed.
