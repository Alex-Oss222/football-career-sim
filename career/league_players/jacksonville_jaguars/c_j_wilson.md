## Staff assessment

C.J. Wilson was a rotational edge defender for Jacksonville in 2013, working in the edge and strong-side rotation in camp before a long injury absence left him active for two games. The staff saw a rotational run defender whose power, edge setting and block shedding were his strengths. His bend and speed offered less than his power, and the staff saw limited reason to feature him as a wide speed rusher.

*Staff view, March 31, 2014.*
