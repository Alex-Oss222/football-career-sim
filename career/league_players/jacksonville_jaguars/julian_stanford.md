## Staff assessment

Julian Stanford was a reserve linebacker for Jacksonville in 2013, dressing for all 16 regular-season games, with his role expanding late that season. In 2014 he has played as the third linebacker in the base defense, with 29 tackles in six games. He is mobile, can cover space and pursue, and can handle a defined replacement assignment. His main limitations are taking on blocks, reading and fitting the run, and the detail of his coverage, especially in man coverage.
