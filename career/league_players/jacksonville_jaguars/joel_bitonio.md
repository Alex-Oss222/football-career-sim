## Staff assessment

Joel Bitonio is a rookie who has started six games on Jacksonville's line. A four-year college starter at both tackle spots, he has a heavy lower body, good short-area quickness and a finished set, and his strength makes him a forceful drive blocker in the run game. His length falls short of the staff's standard for a tackle. In camp he anchored against Sen'Derrick Marks, then over-set once and opened the inside lane, and George Yarno's correction of his set angle and inside hand held.

*Staff assessment originally dated May 11, 2014; 2014 results current through October 12.*
