## Staff assessment

Telvin Smith is a rookie linebacker who has made four special-teams tackles in six games. His case is speed, range, pursuit and ball skills: he played weak-side linebacker at Florida State in 2013 and ran the second-fastest forty among linebackers at the combine. His lean frame is the question against blockers in the run game; in camp he followed the flow on a counter and left the wrap gap, then fit the same misdirection correctly after the correction.
