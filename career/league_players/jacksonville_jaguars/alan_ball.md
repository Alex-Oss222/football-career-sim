## Staff assessment

Alan Ball won Jacksonville's outside cornerback job in the 2013 camp and started the first nine games until he was hurt. Jacksonville signed him again in September 2014 after ten months out of football; he has played five games since, and by early October Stone had made him the outside reserve behind Aqib Talib and Alterraun Verner. He is a long outside corner who can handle a defined starting coverage job and communicate exchanges. He has a balanced profile, with less playmaking and route anticipation than the better corners.
