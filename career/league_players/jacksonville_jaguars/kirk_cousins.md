## Staff assessment

Kirk Cousins has proven he is an NFL starting quarterback. He started all 16 regular-season games for Jacksonville in 2013, led the team to the playoffs and remains the starter in 2014. He runs the offense well, particularly its protection calls and short-to-intermediate passing game. Interceptions remain his biggest concern: 18 in 2013 and nine through the first seven games of 2014.

*Staff assessment originally dated March 31, 2014; 2014 results current through October 19.*
