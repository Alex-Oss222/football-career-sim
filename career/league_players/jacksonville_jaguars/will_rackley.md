# Will Rackley

**Jacksonville Jaguars · Guard · Age 24 · Former player**

6' 3", 310 lb · Born October 11, 1989 · Lehigh University · Drafted in 2011, third round, by Jacksonville

As of January 11, 2014, after the Divisional round.

## Staff assessment

Will Rackley won Jacksonville's right-guard job in camp in 2013 and held it in every game, all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games. His communication on combination blocks won him the job because it held late in practice, and he brought functional strength inside. Individual leverage and recovery after losing it were his main limitations.

*Staff view, March 31, 2014.*
