## Staff assessment

Josh Scobee has proven himself a strong NFL starting kicker. He made 36 of 41 field goals in the 2013 regular season and all three in the playoffs, and he has made 10 of 13 in seven games in 2014. His strengths are a leg with useful long-distance range and a dependable, established kicking operation. Kickoff placement trails his range, and the staff is less confident in his consistency over repeated pressure kicks than in his leg.

*Staff assessment originally dated March 31, 2014; 2014 results current through October 19.*
