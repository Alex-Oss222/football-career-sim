## Staff assessment

Brynden Trawick was a reserve and kicking-game player for Jacksonville in 2013, dressing for all 16 regular-season games. In the Pittsburgh game he played every defensive snap at safety next to Bacarri Rambo, and he has 27 tackles in eight games in 2014. He is a physical, developmental safety whose run support and communication on the coverage units are his best tools. His recognition of route combinations, his positioning in deep coverage and his man coverage in the slot are less developed.

*Staff assessment originally dated March 31, 2014; 2014 results current through October 26.*
