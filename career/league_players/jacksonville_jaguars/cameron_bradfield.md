# Cameron Bradfield

**Jacksonville Jaguars · Offensive tackle · Age 26 · Former player**

6' 4", 308 lb · Born September 14, 1987 · Grand Valley · Undrafted

As of January 11, 2014, after the Divisional round.

## Staff assessment

Cameron Bradfield was Jacksonville's swing tackle throughout 2013 and, from the fifth week, the sixth lineman in its six-lineman package, dressing for all 16 regular-season games and both playoff games. He could recognize his assignment, including twists and blitzes, and cover either edge in a reserve role, and his assignment responses held in padded camp work. His hand use, movement blocking and recovery against NFL rushers were behind that assignment work, and in a 2013 preseason game he lost leverage physically after identifying his protection job. He never signed Jacksonville's 2014 restricted free-agent tender, and the club withdrew it at the final roster cut.

*Staff view, March 31, 2014.*
