## Staff assessment

Marcedes Lewis has proven himself a strong NFL starting tight end. He was active for all 16 regular-season games for Jacksonville in 2013, catching 61 passes for 720 yards, and he has four touchdown catches in eight games in 2014. He is a big, complete attached tight end whose blocking, catch radius and dependable operation anchor the position, and in camp he sustained his attached blocks on the recorded reps at controlled contact. He gives the offense more structure and physical usefulness than sudden separation, and his speed and yards after the catch are below an NFL starter's standard.

*Staff assessment originally dated March 31, 2014; 2014 results current through October 26.*
