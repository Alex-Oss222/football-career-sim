## Staff assessment

Aaron Donald is a rookie defensive tackle who has been active for seven games in his first NFL season. He is undersized but explosive, with a first step and hand quickness that beat blockers before they set; in camp his first step beat Trai Turner on a one-on-one rep. His anchor against double teams is below a starter's standard and holding his gap on early downs is still unproven at his size; in camp the Mike Brewster and Trai Turner double team moved him off the strong A-gap, and he split it only once.

*Staff assessment originally dated May 11, 2014; 2014 results current through October 19.*
