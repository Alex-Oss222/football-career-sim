# Alex Stone: performance and job security

**Through August 4, 2014. Edited October 1, 2026.**

[Coaching profile](alex_stone_coaching_profile.md) | [NFL Coach Sheet](alex_stone_nfl_coach_sheet.md)

**Last employment decision: retained.** Khan and Caldwell retained Stone on January 15, 2014 under his original four-year, fully guaranteed contract. There was no extension, raise or change of authority. No later employment decision is recorded. The [owner and GM review](../2013/season_review/owner_and_gm_review.md) owns that decision.

Two playoff appearances in two completed head-coaching seasons give Stone a successful start. They do not yet establish sustained contention or a pattern of stagnation. He has not coached a 2014 preseason or regular-season game at this cutoff.

## What the 2013 review supports

**Reasons to keep him.** Jacksonville made the playoffs, advanced a round and established a workable Stone-Caldwell relationship. Stone assembled his staff, ran a quarterback competition and gave younger players meaningful roles. Tice handled the weekday offensive process and Crennel retained defensive calling responsibility. Ownership kept that arrangement in place.

**Results that need to improve.** The 10-6 record came with only a 356-346 scoring advantage and a minus-10 turnover differential. Three losses were by at least 28 points. Cousins threw 18 interceptions and took 38 sacks. In the owner review, Caldwell named scoring margin, quarterback ball security and protection as the first measures for 2014. No minimum win total or playoff ultimatum was established.

**Coaching problems behind the numbers.** At [Houston](../2013/regular_season/week_12_jacksonville_at_houston/output.md), Stone departed from a usable running game and finished with seventeen runs and forty-five dropbacks. His subsequent calling check addressed a real mistake; its consistent use remained unproven. The player exits also exposed unfinished development work: [Thielen's individual period](../2013/exit_interviews/main_core/adam_thielen.md) had disappeared after the bye, [Cousins](../2013/exit_interviews/main_core/kirk_cousins.md) wanted clearer diagnosis and film examples, and [Kelce](../2013/exit_interviews/core/travis_kelce.md) lacked a clear completion standard for his blocking work.

The 2013 results count in his career record. The season's equal-strength simulation and documented clock limitations restrict what they can establish about his individual coaching ability or late-game judgment.

## August 4, 2014 update

Follow-through has improved. The [spring handoff](../2014/02_Offseason_Training/Mandatory_Minicamp/training_report.md) records delivered feedback and requested teaching material. The [camp report](../2014/04_Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/training_report.md) adds contact work, delayed retests and in-person block reviews. Kelce's repeated-effort blocking issue remains open; film packets remain unissued. [Westhoff's February appointment](../2014/01_Early_Offseason/staff_changes/hires.md) filled the special-teams vacancy.

Those are useful corrections, but practice cannot establish whether the team will protect the ball, avoid another lopsided loss or sustain individual development through game weeks. There is no new result here that changes Stone's employment status.
