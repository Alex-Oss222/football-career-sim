# Coaching profiles

[Alex Stone's profile](alex_stone_coaching_profile.md) | [Performance and job security](alex_stone_performance_review.md) | [NFL Coach Sheet](alex_stone_nfl_coach_sheet.md) | [Assistant assessments](staff_profiles.md) | [Writing template](profile_template.md)

This folder separates Stone's user-defined coaching identity, the assessment of his performance, and his career record. The profile was revised October 1, 2026 to adopt the user's edited draft, tone guidance and separate offensive and defensive approaches, each with passing and running sections. Each performance assessment and career record retains its own dated coverage. The [current season state](../../state/05_Current_Season_State.md) supplies the career date, and the [staff record](../2014/00_Team_Operations/Staff/coaching_staff.md) owns current assignments. Revising the profile does not complete a practice, install a concept or change an appointment.

## Each document's job

| Document | Reader's question | What belongs here |
|---|---|---|
| `alex_stone_coaching_profile.md` | Who is Stone as a head coach, and how does he approach decisions? | The user's adopted identity, preferences, teaching, relationships, preparation, tactics, delegation and public conduct. Describe the choices these produce without promising success. |
| `alex_stone_performance_review.md` | Is he progressing, sustaining success or falling short, and what has the club decided? | A short season review, the trend across seasons, material unresolved problems, ownership's stated expectations and actual employment decisions. Detailed teaching observations stay in season reports. |
| `alex_stone_nfl_coach_sheet.md` | What is on his resume, and what have his teams produced? | Background, appointments, calling responsibility, wins and losses, playoff finishes, season statistics, defined league ranks and rates, contract and authority. Preserve completed seasons as the career grows. |
| `staff_profiles.md` | What has each assistant demonstrated? | The existing assistant assessments and their dated changes. Current appointments belong in the season staff record. |
| `profile_template.md` | How should an assessment be written and updated? | Short prompts for the three functions, with no requirement to fill unsupported fields. |

Rosters, injuries, contracts, game logs, playbooks, position manuals and weekly plans stay with their existing owners. Link to them when needed. Do not recreate their tables here or add empty opponent, equipment or game-week fields.

## Evidence and time

Use the source that owns the fact: identity and authority canon for stable background; accepted contracts and appointment records for employment; event outputs for what happened; plans for what was intended. The active playbook defines available scheme, not proof that a page was installed or used. Read only the iteration permitted for the current season. The prehire dossier remains frozen history.

In the performance review, distinguish team results from Stone's contribution. Attribute coordinators' and position coaches' work accurately. Team results do not by themselves establish coaching effectiveness; the equal-strength 2013 engine and documented management defects particularly limit that inference. The profile defines his approach, not an ability rating or a numeric coaching grade, XP or probability bonus.

A pattern may justify a clear football judgment. An isolated incident establishes what happened on that occasion. Distinguish missing evidence from failure, a requested correction from delivered instruction, and successful practice from transfer to a game. State specific limitations where they affect the conclusion. Do not repeat a generic disclaimer under every heading.

Only the user establishes Stone's intended beliefs, motives and philosophy. Preserve the adopted profile when assessing whether that approach worked. Recorded choices can support an assessment of his habits; they cannot silently replace his identity or supply invented private thoughts, dialogue, family conflict or future development. Later real-world careers and future playbook editions supply no answers to current questions.

## Update procedure

1. Record practice, teaching and game observations in the report for that work. Check the date, conditions and responsible coach. Routine phase completion does not require another entry in Stone's performance review.
2. Update the performance review after a season, an employment decision or evidence that materially changes a standing concern. Compare results across seasons; name recurring problems and the response. Keep earlier season conclusions, but link to detailed observations rather than repeat the practice diary. An analyst's view of job security is not a club decision.
3. Change Stone's approach in the profile when the user adopts or revises it. Refresh established job or delegation facts when their source changes. Earlier phase plans calling for a review entry at every handoff or a profile rewrite after an assessment are superseded by this procedure; preserve their dated plans and reports.
4. Add completed seasons to the NFL Coach Sheet and update sourced career or employment facts. Separate regular season from playoffs, label net yardage and define rate denominators. Name the statistic behind each top-ten finish. Keep approximate or incomplete figures clearly identified. The source owner changes first; a review cannot appoint a coach, award a role or advance the clock.

Keep current-season exit interviews and team reviews in that season's `07_Season_Review/`. Carry unresolved teaching and staff follow-through, with the source, responsible coach and next observation, in the existing season handoff. A season boundary does not reset the assessment.

The user-defined [camp approach](alex_stone_coaching_profile.md#how-he-wants-training-camp-run) is maintained in the profile and operational season plan. A change to that approach is not evidence that a practice or fitness test occurred.

## Writing standard

In Stone's profile, use practical football language to connect the user's traits to preparation, teaching and decisions. Preserve the approved human details and distinguish a preference from guaranteed success. Keep performance reviews and season statistics in their separate records. In assessments, use football actions and their consequences: what he called, removed, taught, delegated or failed to revisit. Explain who was responsible and what the player needed. Avoid unsupported superlatives, invented motives and repeating the same anecdote under several competencies. Include an unknown only when it affects an actual conclusion or decision.

## Research behind this structure

The division above is an editorial choice for this repository, informed by public examples and coaching guidance:

| Source | Application here |
|---|---|
| [San Diego Chargers 2013 Media Guide](https://res.cloudinary.com/nflclubs/image/upload/chargers/zsoiuz3ov42bxwoupclx.pdf), Mike McCoy biography, printed pages 13-14 | A factual coach reference can use a compact career chronology, relevant achievements and personal background. Club publicity language is not a standard for judging coaching quality. |
| [Dick Cass on Baltimore's head-coach search](https://www.baltimoreravens.com/news/behind-the-scenes-with-dick-cass-7748197), January 18, 2008 | Firsthand NFL hiring evidence covers teaching, judgment, communication, staffing and running practices as well as scheme. Biography, interviews and references serve different purposes. The profile therefore addresses the whole head-coach job. |
| [Sarah McQuade, USA Football: evaluating coaching performance](https://blogs.usafootball.com/blog/1255/evaluating-your-own-coaching-performance), October 31, 2016 | Review specific behavior and whether a correction worked. Keep the detail in season reports and carry material findings into the performance review; its suggested numeric scale is not adopted. |
| [Vince DiGaetano, USA Football: creating a position manual](https://blogs.usafootball.com/blog/1595/creating-a-position-manual-to-enhance-your-teaching), March 25, 2016 | Technique, rules and drills belong in teaching material. Existing playbooks and development plans own those details; the profile states Stone's approach. |
| [NFL Guide for Statisticians](https://www.nflgsis.com/gsis/documentation/stadiumguides/guide_for_statisticians.pdf), pages 5-6 and 13-15 | Define first downs, third-down efficiency and net passing yardage. Use this career's receipts for the actual numbers and league comparisons. |
| [National Archives plain-writing checklist](https://www.archives.gov/open/plain-writing/checklist.html) | Organize around the reader's question, state the point early, use active verbs and remove unnecessary words. |

These are references for editing the documents, not sources for Stone's biography or evidence that he adopted later guidance in 2014. Public material does not establish a universal NFL internal assessment form.
