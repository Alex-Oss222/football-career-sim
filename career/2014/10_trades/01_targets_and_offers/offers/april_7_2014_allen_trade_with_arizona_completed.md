# April 7, 2014: Allen trade with Arizona (completed)

[All targets and offers](../README.md) · [Source offer log](../trade_offers.md)

The agreement of March 24 closed under its own conditions, with no further negotiation. Paul Posluszny was cleared on April 5. Allen passed Arizona's physical on April 7 and the trade was processed that day. Arizona's unconditional 2015 fourth is Jacksonville's. See [completed trades](../../02_completed_trades/trades.md).
