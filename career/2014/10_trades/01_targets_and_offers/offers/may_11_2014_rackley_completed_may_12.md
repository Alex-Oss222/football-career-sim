# May 11, 2014: Rackley (completed May 12)

[All targets and offers](../README.md) · [Source offer log](../trade_offers.md)

Caldwell, on Stone's instruction to shop Will Rackley for an unconditional 2015 seventh and keep Mike Brewster, called the six clubs on the line-needy list. The calls are in the [trade record](../../02_completed_trades/rackley_to_seattle_2014-05-12.md).

- San Francisco, Carolina, New England and Houston, passed: each had drafted an interior lineman in the draft that had just ended.
- Chicago, passed: it re-signed its starting left guard, Matt Slauson, in March.
- Seattle, completed: its 2013 starting left guard, Paul McQuistan, left for Cleveland on March 24 and it drafted no interior lineman. Seattle accepted the unconditional 2015 seventh without a counter, subject to its physical. Rackley passed on May 12 with his Week 5 limitation disclosed; processed May 12. No fallback was needed. See [completed trades](../../02_completed_trades/trades.md).
