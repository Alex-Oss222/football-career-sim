# Nos. 82 and 194 to Washington for Jacksonville's 2015 second: March 31, 2014

Recorded September 30, 2026 (2014 ledger Entry 105). Branch date: March 31, 2014.

**Stone's instruction** ([memo amendment](../../01_early_offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-trade-the-colts-picks-for-a-2015-pick)): trade the two picks received from Indianapolis, No. 82 and No. 194, for a 2015 pick.

**Method.** The same as the [Arizona trade for No. 38](../01_targets_and_offers/package_i_negotiation_2014-03-20.md): Caldwell sets the ask, and the other club answers on its own need and pick value. There is no private draw. The same offer from any other club would get the same answer. Pick value uses the Jimmy Johnson chart, with a pick one year out counted one round later.

## Why Washington

- Washington already holds Jacksonville's own 2015 second, from the Cousins trade, and that pick has no conditions.
- Washington has no 2014 first; it went to Jacksonville in the same trade. Its first 2014 selection is No. 45, and it also holds No. 58, No. 77 and one pick in each later round. An extra third-round pick gives it another early choice in this draft.
- Jacksonville has no 2015 first or second. Its 2015 first and fourth went to Arizona, and the second is the one Washington holds. Getting it back restores a 2015 second.

## Values

| Asset | How it is counted | Chart value |
|---|---|---:|
| No. 82 (2014, round 3) | As is | 180 |
| No. 194 (2014, round 6) | As is | Under 20 |
| Jacksonville's 2015 second | Slot unknown until the 2014 season ends. Counted one round later, a middle-to-late second is worth what a 2014 third at the same slot is worth | About 130 to 190 |

Jacksonville pays a small premium for taking the pick a year later. That is the usual cost of trading into a future year.

## The calls (March 31)

1. **Caldwell's opening:** No. 82 alone for Jacksonville's 2015 second.
2. **Washington's answer:** No. 82 alone does not cover a second, even one a year away, if Jacksonville's 2015 season goes badly and the pick lands early. It asks for No. 194 as well.
3. **Caldwell:** Stone authorized both picks. He accepts.

## The trade

| Jacksonville sends | Washington sends |
|---|---|
| 2014 third-round pick, No. 82 (Indianapolis original, round 3, slot 18) | Jacksonville's original 2015 second-round pick, unconditional, slot set by the 2014 season |
| 2014 sixth-round pick, No. 194 (Indianapolis original, round 6, slot 15) | |

- **Picks:** both 2014 picks are clean ordinary picks, received from Indianapolis earlier the same day with no condition.
- **Players and cap:** no players move, and nothing changes on the cap. Jacksonville's rookie class shrinks from 11 selections to 9.
- **Processed:** March 31.
