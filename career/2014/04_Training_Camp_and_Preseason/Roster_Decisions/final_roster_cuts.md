## August 31, noon: waivers, the reserve placements and the practice squad

**Waivers.** The branch cannot know other clubs' claims except through the rails: a waived player whose real next move was a waiver claim in this window goes to that club; otherwise he clears. The Week 1 rails library records no cutdown-window claim for any of the 19 waived players (none of the 17 ordinary waivers and neither injured-designation waiver), so every Jacksonville waiver cleared at noon. Brate and Jackson reverted to Jacksonville's reserve/injured list on August 31 under the period procedure (Reserve/Injured from Waived/Injured); their contracts continue, they are off the 53 and neither carries the designated-return tag.

**Practice squad.** Caldwell signed from the package's target list in order as eligibility allowed. The squad is ten places under the August 19 agreement (rules library [G1](../../../../library/2014_playing_and_roster_rules.md), Confirmed: ten players; at most two with up to two accrued seasons; a practice-squad season counts toward the three-season limit only with at least six games on the squad). The 2013 eligibility terms in R5 (no accrued season, or one accrued season on fewer than nine games on the 46, or fewer than three prior practice-squad seasons) are Unverified in the library and were applied conservatively: a player whose accrued count is not established was signed only where G1's two-player allowance covers him.
