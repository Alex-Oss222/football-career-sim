### August 31, 2014: the Week 1 inactives (Stone, Document 3 row 8)

Seven of the 53 are inactive for Week 1 at Philadelphia so that 46 dress (rules library R4): Hakeem Nicks and Gator Hoskins by medical instruction, and five by Stone's standing rule for the healthy inactives, the deepest healthy reserve on the chart in each of the five largest healthy position groups, never below a legal game-day unit, re-set each week: Malcolm Butler (tenth defensive back), Corey Linsley (ninth lineman), Kasim Edebali (eighth defensive lineman), Todd Davis (seventh linebacker) and Taylor Gabriel (fifth healthy receiver). A.J. Bouye cleared August 31, his projected date, and is active. The list is in the game input and may be changed by Stone in the Week 1 preparation; the 46 pass the game-day unit check.

### August 31, 2014: the season captains (Stone)

Stone named six season captains, three on offense and three on defense by position group, with no player vote: **Kirk Cousins** (quarterback), **Eugene Monroe** (offensive line) and **Maurice Jones-Drew** (skill group) on offense; **Sen'Derrick Marks** (defensive line), **Daryl Smith** (linebackers) and **Dwight Lowery** (secondary) on defense. The structure is the one Stone chose in the spring ([staff decisions](../../02_Offseason_Training/staff_decisions.md#choose-season-captains-before-week-1)): the third defensive group is the linebackers. The appointment is Stone's decision from the whole offseason and camp; it is not a depth-chart award and changes no role. The captains are shown in the [roster](../../00_Team_Operations/Team/Roster/roster.md) Role column and the [depth chart](../../00_Team_Operations/Team/Depth_Chart/depth_chart.md#captains-august-31-2014).
