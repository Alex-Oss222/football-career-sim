## What separates the candidates

**After game 3:** Turner's starting point stands on 37 clean snaps by the record across five combinations; neither alternative took a right-guard snap, and the one right-guard loss of the night was Asper's with the reserve core. Open as a conditional.

**After August 21:** Turner's starting point stands and the conditional alternative was not rehearsed at right guard this week; Asper took the right guard in Henne's mixed group and Wilson's groups, a depth arrangement and not the conditional. Open as a conditional.

**After game 2:** Turner's starting point stands on 19 clean snaps by the record; neither alternative took a right-guard snap. Open as a conditional.

**After August 13:** Turner's starting point stands and the conditional alternative was not rehearsed at right guard this week. Open as a conditional.

**After game 1:** Norwell's game work was at left guard; no right-guard snap recorded.

This is an established conditional competition, not a claim that Turner is being replaced. The scrimmage added a secured combination and one split; the evidence supports continued evaluation and preserves his starting point.
