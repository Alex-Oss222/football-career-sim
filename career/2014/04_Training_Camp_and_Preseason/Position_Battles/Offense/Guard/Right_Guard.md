## What separates the candidates

**Decision, August 30, 2014 ([roster decisions](../../../Roster_Decisions/roster_decisions.md)):** Trai Turner is the starting right guard on Stone's season game depth chart. The conditional alternative (Bitonio or Norwell if Turner struggled) was never triggered on the record: one loss charged to his slot in 61 preseason snaps, the August 28 Boot Flood sack on a movement call with the cause unassigned, which stays Yarno's film item and is not a chart condition. Feiler and Asper, the depth right guards of the preseason, left the roster August 30 (Feiler to the practice squad). The rows above are preserved as dated history; this card is resolved and off the open list.

**After game 4:** Turner's starting point stands with the first loss charged to his slot, a sack on a movement call with the cause unassigned; neither alternative took a right-guard snap in four games, and Feiler's and Asper's clean right-guard work came in depth arrangements. Open as a conditional, with the film review of the Boot Flood sack Yarno's first item.

**After August 27:** Turner's starting point stands and the conditional alternative was not rehearsed at right guard this week; Feiler and Asper took the right guard in the developmental and reserve lines, depth arrangements and not the conditional. Open as a conditional.

**After game 3:** Turner's starting point stands on 37 clean snaps by the record across five combinations; neither alternative took a right-guard snap, and the one right-guard loss of the night was Asper's with the reserve core. Open as a conditional.

**After August 21:** Turner's starting point stands and the conditional alternative was not rehearsed at right guard this week; Asper took the right guard in Henne's mixed group and Wilson's groups, a depth arrangement and not the conditional. Open as a conditional.

**After game 2:** Turner's starting point stands on 19 clean snaps by the record; neither alternative took a right-guard snap. Open as a conditional.

**After August 13:** Turner's starting point stands and the conditional alternative was not rehearsed at right guard this week. Open as a conditional.

**After game 1:** Norwell's game work was at left guard; no right-guard snap recorded.

This is an established conditional competition, not a claim that Turner is being replaced. The scrimmage added a secured combination and one split; the evidence supports continued evaluation and preserves his starting point.
