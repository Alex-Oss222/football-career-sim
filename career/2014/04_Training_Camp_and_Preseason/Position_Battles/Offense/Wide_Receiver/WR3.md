## What separates the candidates

**After August 27:** Rehearsed inside the quarter structure, Adams in both Cousins quarters and Hawkins in the second and the second half; nothing on the record separates them. Open.

**After game 3:** A catch at Z for Adams for a first down and two catches inside for Hawkins with Cousins; one incompletion to each on a third down. Both operated beside different neighbors at Cousins's pace without a recorded fault; nothing in it separates them. Open.

**After August 21:** Rehearsed with the surrounding receivers changed and with both quarterbacks; nothing on the record separates them. Open.

**After game 2:** One target to each candidate in the WR3 role, Adams's for 10 and a first down, Hawkins's for 2; a catch and a run for Hawkins outside the role. The comparison received its first game football and nothing in it separates them. Open.

**After August 13:** Rehearsed again in both arrangements; nothing on the record separates them. Open.

**After game 1:** Neither candidate was thrown to in the WR3 role; the comparison was not advanced. Hawkins's production with Henne is useful receiver evidence, not WR3 evidence.

Both have retained a specific correction in pads and in series. The record does not establish a WR3 winner. Adams is also competing for WR1, so the staff must judge the combinations it actually uses.
