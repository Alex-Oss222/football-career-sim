## What separates the candidates

Nicks has the steadier recorded timing through this work; Adams's correction has now held on its delayed retest and in series, with his releases against Talib still mixed. Three controlled-contact days and a scrimmage against their own defense do not settle WR1. Their shared opponents and actual assignments matter more than a tally of catches.

## Next work and decision

The next useful test is an opponent: Stone's frozen rotation gives Nicks the WR1 work in the first first-unit possession and Adams the WR1 work in the second, with Thielen the constant, and the August 8 game against Tampa Bay tests releases, route timing and the answer when the first path is denied against press that does not stop at the whistle. No new role has been awarded. [Roster decisions](../../../Roster_Decisions/roster_decisions.md) owns any eventual change.
