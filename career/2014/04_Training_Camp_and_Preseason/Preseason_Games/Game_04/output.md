**Stone:** I will know more at seven o'clock. The first quarter is the whole operation with the group that starts the season, from the huddle, with the normal third downs and the normal red zone, and it is the only quarter they get. We have not started on Philadelphia. That starts Monday.

**Stone:** He plays the first half with Kirk and he is on the field to be thrown to. The cross, the stick from the shifted picture, the mesh and the matchup when the field gets short.
