## 3. Pregame media Q&A

**Reporter:** How much are we going to see of Cousins and the first group tonight?

**Stone:** Two possessions, give or take the length of the first one. If the first drive runs long, I do not need a second full series. If we go three-and-out, they come back out. I am not leaving starters on the field because the quarter has not ended.

**Reporter:** You have rookies at both guard spots and a second-round receiver in the first group. Is tonight an audition for those jobs?

**Stone:** It is an opportunity, not a verdict. The combinations you will see were set a week ago, before anyone knew how the game would go, and they move during the first two series on purpose. One good series does not win a job and one bad one does not lose it. I want to see whether what we taught survives a defense that does not cooperate.

**Reporter:** The try is from the 15 this week. Does the longer kick change how you handle a touchdown?

**Stone:** It changes the kick, not the decision. A 33-yard field goal is a kick we expect to make. Westhoff has the operation ready with both snappers. We will go for two when the situation calls for it, not to put something on film.

### Preseason game 1: Tampa Bay at Jacksonville | August 8, 2014

**Final:** Jacksonville 21, Tampa Bay 30 | L

### Game narrative: selected highlights

Tampa Bay's first unit solved the game in three throws. The first defense had already made one stand, forcing a punt after McCown's eleven-play opening drive stalled at the Jacksonville 36 on two Mincey tackles and a Marks tackle for loss. Then Martin went 21 yards on first down and McCown hit Murphy for a 57-yard touchdown with 6:50 left in the first quarter. In the second quarter it was Martin for 12 and McCown to Owusu for 68 and a score, two plays after a Jacksonville punt. The third touchdown drive was the long one: ten plays, 71 yards, carried by a 48-yard completion to Myers on third-and-13. Three completions produced 173 of McCown's 197 yards; the extra point after the third score, a 33-yard try under this week's rule, was missed.

Cousins's two series produced six snaps and two punts. After an incompletion and a 5-yard Y-Cross completion to Thielen, Clayborn sacked him on a Boot Flood on third-and-5, the loss charged to the right-tackle slot. The second series ended the same way: Jones-Drew for 3 on the Counter, a Boot Flood throw to Jones-Drew for nothing, then Melvin, a corner, sacked Cousins for 9 on the slow screen, again over the right tackle. Lane Johnson's injury was logged at the end of the first series; he was not removed and finished his assigned block at right tackle.

Jacksonville's first points came off a Tampa Bay mistake. Facing fourth-and-16 at its own 47, Tampa Bay handed to Martin; Talib forced and recovered the fumble at the Tampa Bay 48. Henne, behind the first tackles with Norwell, Linsley and Bitonio inside, needed two snaps: Y-Cross to Hawkins for 31, then Anderson 17 yards for the touchdown on Inside Zone with the access bubble attached, 7-7 with 18 seconds left in the quarter.

Henne's two possessions behind the second line went the other way. Gholston sacked him over Lucas at right tackle on third-and-7, and from the Jacksonville 7 a drive that reached the 39 ended when Fletcher intercepted a third-and-8 throw aimed at Brate on the Stick call with the H in RETURN motion. Brate's injury was logged at the end of that possession and he was removed. Glennon took over at his own 20 after the interception and went 78 yards in 13 plays against the first defense, which held at the 2 for three downs (Harris on the ball twice) and conceded only Barth's 19-yard field goal as the half expired: 23-7.

Wilson opened the second half with the night's cleanest possession, 80 yards in eight plays: Hurns for 19 on Drive, Murphy for 17 on third-and-1 from Spacing, Hurns for the 17-yard touchdown on Bunch Mesh. Kreiter's first game snap held up on the try. The next time Wilson threw, from the Jacksonville 21, Major Wright intercepted a first-down Stick throw to Nicks and Tampa Bay started at the Jacksonville 31; James scored from the 5 four plays later, 30-14.

With 12:51 left and down 16, Jacksonville punted on fourth-and-2 from the Tampa Bay 45. Shaw's first series answered anyway: 60 yards in eight plays, Jackson for 17 on third-and-3 from the Ace Y-Cross, Gabriel for 10 on third-and-10, Owens for the 7-yard touchdown with 5:54 left. The reserve defense gave the ball back twice more (Todd Davis's sack, then three Tampa Bay runs for minus 2), but a three-and-out from the Jacksonville 10 and Anger's 61-yard punt left 19 seconds. Shaw found Owens for 28 and Munoz stripped him; Tampa Bay knelt.
