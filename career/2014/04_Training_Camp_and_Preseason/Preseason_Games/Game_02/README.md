## Questions going into Chicago

**Written before the game from Stone's plan and the week's record; preserved as the ex-ante questions.** Two examinations. For the first groups: whether Lucas's correction changes the edge result on ordinary dropbacks and then on the boot and the screen; whether the first offense stays on the field long enough to operate (the exit rule is the second first down and the sequence after it, roughly 8 to 10 snaps or two possessions, whichever first); whether the WR1, WR3 and guard comparisons receive real football in both arrangements; whether a long completion against the first defense repeats with the same cause, after a review that could not classify the three from Tampa Bay; whether Branch's lane stays intact while he rushes from Edge 1 with the veteran structure around him. For the rookies and backups: assignment, portability, cold entry, technique at speed, the physical result, conditioning late in the block, the reset after coaching, ball security and a second Sunday job, and above all repeatability: whether Tampa Bay was one good rep or is becoming who the player is.
