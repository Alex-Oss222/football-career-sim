## 3. Pregame media Q&A

**Reporter:** Last week you said two possessions for Cousins. What is different this week?

**Stone:** The exit rule. Last week it was two series. This week it is two first downs and the sequence after them, or about ten snaps, or two possessions, whichever comes first. Six snaps told me nothing about the offense and not much about Kirk. I want the group to stay on the field long enough to have to operate.

**Reporter:** Cornelius Lucas gave up a sack at right tackle last week and now he is starting there. Why him?

**Stone:** Because he played the job and I have tape to correct. If I move a young tackle over from the left, I am starting a new evaluation at a position he has not played for us. Cornelius has had the correction all week. It is one game, in Lane's absence. It is not a depth-chart decision.

**Reporter:** Is Lane Johnson out because of the injury or because of you?

**Stone:** Me. His date was today and I was not going to build a plan around a man being cleared on game day. He does not play tonight whatever the trainers say this afternoon.

**Reporter:** Three long completions against the starters last week. Did the film tell you what happened?

**Stone:** It told us what the throws were. It did not tell us one cause we could put a name on, and I am not going to make one up for you. We went back and coached the help and the communication in the coverages we already play. If it happens again the same way, then we have a pattern. One possession tonight for that group.

## 5. Postgame media Q&A

**Reporter:** Kirk's group scored and then he came out. Was there a thought of leaving him in after the touchdown?

**Stone:** No. The rule was two possessions or the second first down and the sequence after it, and he got both. He had fourteen snaps, both series got to their 33 or better, one ended with seven points and one ended with a pick. That is enough to coach. The rest of the night belonged to the other guys and I said so before the game.

**Reporter:** The interception. What did you see?

**Stone:** A first-down throw at their 33 that we did not get to keep. I will look at the route and the read before I say which it was. It was a call I would not expect from that spot on the field, and that is on my sheet before it is on Kirk.

**Reporter:** Three missed field goals, two from 43, one with nine seconds left. Is that a Scobee problem?

**Stone:** It is a kicking-operation problem until Mike shows me it is not. Four operations, two snappers, one make from 53. I am going to look at the snap, the hold and the protection on all four before I put it on Josh. If they are clean, then it is Josh, and he knows that.

**Reporter:** Cornelius Lucas played every snap at right tackle. Did he do what you needed?

**Stone:** The slot that gave up three last week gave up none tonight, in seventy-four snaps against three different edges. That is what I asked him for. It was one game and I told him that. Lane's situation is Lane's situation, and we will talk about next week after the film.

**Reporter:** You punted from their 41 with a minute and change left in a tie game. Why not go for it, or try the long kick?

**Stone:** Fourth-and-5 from the 41 is a 59-yard try and I had just missed two from 43. I kicked it out of bounds at the 10 and gave the defense the ball, and they sacked him twice and got it back for us with nine seconds. Then we missed the kick. I will look at the decision. I will say now that in a game I said was for evaluation, that was a fourth-down rep I did not give that group.

**Reporter:** The 76-yarder in overtime. Same problem as Tampa Bay?

**Stone:** I do not know yet, and I will not say it is the same thing until I have seen the call and the help on it. Tampa was the first group. **Reporter:** Jerrell Jackson and Richard Murphy both left. Anything you can tell us?

**Stone:** Jerrell is going to miss time; the trainers will put a number on it next week, not me. Richard is in the protocol and that is the doctors' decision, not mine or his. Anything about the roster is Dave's.

**Reporter:** Seven sacks again, seven different players again. Are you starting to believe that group?

**Stone:** I believe the ones who did it against the lines they did it against. Last week it was Tampa's second and third lines, tonight it was Chicago's second and third lines, and I noted which slots they came over. It is good work and it is the right players doing it twice. The lane and the fit behind the sack are what I grade, and the game book does not grade them for me.

### Preseason review through August 14, 2014

This is an interim review after two of four games, with the [August 8 review](../Game_01/output.md#preseason-review-through-august-8-2014) and the camp findings through August 13 as its baseline. What the staff can now trust across two games: the taught passing concepts (Stick, Spacing, Drive, Y-Cross, Mesh, the Power Pass) convert third downs for every quarterback who has run them against an opponent; the reserve rush wins at the line; the specialist operation has not recorded a fault in 22 kicking snaps across two snappers; Hawkins fields cleanly.
