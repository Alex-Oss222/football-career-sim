## Maurice Jones-Drew

**Facts on the table.**
- Age and history: 29 (born March 23, 1985). Eight Jacksonville seasons; a Bay Area native.
- Branch 2013 use: 1,334 rushing yards and 12 total touchdowns. The branch season honours named him Comeback Player of the Year. These are equal-strength engine totals: they show use, not proven ability.
- Recorded practice strengths: run tracks and protection.
- His role here: continuity while Jonathan Grimes and C.J. Anderson develop. No starting promise.

**Exchanges.**
1. **Caldwell opens (March 25):** two years, $6.0M, $2.5M guaranteed. He keeps below Stone's ceiling because the working difference must still absorb the rookie class.
2. **Representatives counter (March 26):** two years, $8.5M, $4.0M guaranteed. They cite the 2013 season and a multi-year Oakland offer at home. The guarantee is above Stone's ceiling, so Caldwell cannot accept it.
3. **Caldwell's final offer (March 27):** two years, $7.0M, $3.25M guaranteed. That is inside the ceiling on both length and money. He declines a third year.
4. **Jones-Drew accepts (March 28).** The comparison:
   - **Money:** Oakland pays more years and total money for the full three years ($7.5M) but guarantees only $1.2M. Jacksonville guarantees $3.25M, pays $3.5M a year against Oakland's $2.5M, and pays $3.35M in 2014 cash against Oakland's $2.5M.
   - **Other factors:** the pull of home is real. Continuity with a staff and system that just used him as its lead back is the other side of it.
   - **Decision:** the extra $2.05M of guaranteed money and the higher average decide it. The same terms from any club, set against Oakland's, would give the same answer.

- **Release exposure:** releasing him before 2015 leaves $750,000 of dead money and removes the $3,650,000 of 2015 cash.
- **No other terms:** no void years, escalators, incentives or trade clauses. No starting promise.

## C.J. Wilson

**Facts on the table.**
- Age and history: 27 on March 30 (born March 30, 1987). Four accrued seasons: 2010-2012 with Green Bay and 2013 in the branch.
- 2013: injured in Week 2 in the branch, never moved to a reserve list. His projection was reached January 30. His current physical, taken March 26, shows no communicated restriction; no diagnosis is invented.
- Evidence: too little individual evidence to promise a role.
- His role here: a roster competition on a front that lost Babin and Alualu.

**Exchanges.**
1. **Caldwell offers (March 25):** one year at the $730,000 minimum for four credited seasons, no guarantee.
2. **Representatives (March 26)** ask for two things: a $65,000 signing bonus to match Oakland, and a stated rotation role.
3. **Caldwell (March 27):** he declines any role promise, under Stone's instruction. He adds the $65,000 bonus. It is the minimum-contract bonus size, not the substantial guarantee Stone ruled out.
4. **Wilson accepts (March 28).** The money now matches Oakland's. With it equal, the open places on Jacksonville's front and a staff that has his medical history decide it. The same terms from any club would give the same answer.

**Executed contract:** one year, $795,000 ($730,000 base plus a $65,000 signing bonus, the only guarantee). The 2014 cap charge is counted in full at $795,000. The minimum-salary benefit is not applied, the same treatment as Cain's contract, until his eligibility is verified. Releasing him leaves $65,000 of dead money.
