# Alan Ball: Stone's conversation, May 12, 2014

Recorded September 30, 2026 (2014 ledger Entry 110). Branch date: May 12, 2014.

**Stone's instruction:** [memo amendment](../early_offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-alan-ball).

**The conversation.** Stone called Ball after the draft. He said Jacksonville had no clean role to offer after what it added at corner, that this was not because Ball failed here, and that his 2013 season, when he won the outside job and started until his injury, stood on its own. He asked Ball and his agent to name three clubs where he would have a real opportunity, said any club that called Jacksonville would get an honest account of his work, and told him to take the best situation he found rather than wait. If nothing developed and Jacksonville's room changed, they would talk again.

**Ball's answer.** He wanted to play and would look. His agent gave Caldwell three clubs; Caldwell made the calls the same day and gave each the same evaluation.

**Where it stands.** Ball is an unplaced free agent. Under the [league rails](../league/personnel/method.md), other clubs' rosters follow their real 2014 movements, and no club's real history includes a Ball signing: his own real 2014 move was with the real Jaguars, which the branch never made. So no club signs him in the branch, and he stays available. Jacksonville keeps him first on its veteran-corner call list. The three clubs and the calls are recorded here as Stone's and Caldwell's actions, not as a market result.
