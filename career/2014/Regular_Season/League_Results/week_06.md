## Tampa Bay 35, Baltimore 13

Doug Martin ran for three touchdowns and Josh McCown threw for 200 yards and a touchdown to Vincent Jackson without a turnover, and Tampa Bay's defense took the ball away four times: Bradley McDougald returned an interception 58 yards for a touchdown, Mason Foster and Mark Barron also intercepted Joe Flacco, and an interception at the Baltimore 5 and Da'Quan Bowers's strip sack, recovered by Gerald McCoy at the Baltimore 17, each set up a short touchdown drive. Baltimore held the ball nearly 39 minutes, made 27 first downs and converted 13 of 19 third downs, Flacco completing 36 of 54 for 348 yards and a touchdown to Bernard Pierce and Torrey Smith catching 12 of 19 targets for 98, but Michael Johnson sacked Flacco twice among four sacks, Justin Tucker missed from 31 and the Ravens scored one touchdown. Standouts: Martin, McDougald, Michael Johnson. Tampa Bay's Clinton McDonald left with an injury projected at forty-five days and Lavonte David on a head hold projected at five.

## Carolina 27, Cincinnati 24

Cincinnati led 24-13 in the third quarter, its first touchdown coming two plays after Reggie Nelson recovered Jonathan Stewart's fumble at the Carolina 18, before Carolina scored two fourth-quarter touchdowns and Thomas DeCoud intercepted Andy Dalton on the Bengals' last possession. Cam Newton completed 29 of 43 for 286 yards and touchdowns to Jerricho Cotchery and Greg Olsen without an interception, Cotchery catching 11 of 15 targets for 124, and DeAngelo Williams ran for Carolina's other touchdown. Dalton threw touchdown passes to Jermaine Gresham and Brandon Tate, who also returned four kickoffs for 110, and Vontaze Burfict made eleven tackles with a sack, but the Bengals converted two of eleven third downs and punted eight times. Standouts: Cotchery, Newton, Burfict. Carolina's Stewart left with an injury projected at eighty-six days.

## Atlanta 16, Chicago 10

Devonta Freeman ran 24 times for 154 yards and a touchdown and Matt Bryant made all three of his field goals as Atlanta held the ball more than 38 minutes, made 26 first downs and led 16-3 after three quarters; Matt Ryan completed 26 of 38 for 271 without a touchdown or an interception, Julio Jones catching 11 of 18 targets for 125. Jared Allen made twelve tackles and sacked Ryan, but Chicago converted none of its seven third downs and turned the ball over three times, Nathan Stupar and Javier Arenas intercepting Jay Cutler and Matt Forte, who ran 15 times for 109 with a 50-yarder, losing a fumble. Shea McClellin's recovery of a Ryan fumble set up Cutler's touchdown pass to Senorise Perry with 3:06 left, but the Bears' last possession ended on a failed fourth-and-1 at their own 37. Standouts: Freeman, Julio Jones, Allen. Atlanta's Lamar Holmes left with an injury projected at forty-seven days.

## Seattle 41, Dallas 14

Seattle took the ball away six times: Michael Bennett, Bobby Wagner and Earl Thomas intercepted Tony Romo, Thomas returning his 69 yards for a touchdown that made it 14-0 in the first quarter, and Wagner, Byron Maxwell and Malcolm Smith recovered Dallas fumbles, Wagner forcing two. Marshawn Lynch ran 22 times for 125 and a touchdown and caught a touchdown pass, Russell Wilson threw for 273 yards and three touchdowns, and Doug Baldwin caught all seven of his targets for 139, a 77-yard touchdown among them, as Seattle led 28-7 at the half and held the ball nearly 40 minutes. Lance Dunbar ran seven times for 70 and a touchdown, Cole Beasley caught Romo's touchdown pass and Brandon Carr intercepted Wilson, while Morris Claiborne made thirteen tackles. Standouts: Wagner, Lynch, Baldwin. Seattle's Kam Chancellor left with an injury projected at eight days and Dallas's C.J. Spillman with one projected at five.

## Denver 23, N.Y. Jets 17

Ronnie Hillman ran for two second-quarter touchdowns and Brandon McManus made all three of his field goals as Denver led 20-0 at the half, the third coming after the Broncos recovered a surprise onside kick at the Jets 49 and making it 23-3 in the fourth quarter; Peyton Manning completed 23 of 38 for 294 yards without a touchdown or an interception, Emmanuel Sanders catching six for 97. Saalim Hakim, who returned four kickoffs for 165 yards, then set the Jets up at the Denver 14 with a 94-yard return, Geno Smith threw a 21-yard touchdown pass to Greg Salas on fourth-and-17, and Chris Johnson's touchdown run at the end of an 11-play drive brought New York within 23-17 with 1:48 left. The Jets recovered the onside kick that followed, but that possession ended in Smith's second interception, Danny Trevathan and T.J. Ward having one each, and New York ran for 36 yards and drew ten penalties for 70. Standouts: McManus, Hakim, Manning. Denver's Isaiah Burse left on a head hold projected at twelve days and Bradley Roby on one projected at two, and the Jets' D'Brickashaw Ferguson and Sheldon Richardson on head holds projected at two days each.

## Detroit 24, Minnesota 17

Detroit led 14-0 at the half and 24-3 after George Winn's 39-yard touchdown run in the third quarter, Joique Bell having run for a touchdown with a 50-yard carry among his 73 yards and Matthew Stafford having thrown one to Brandon Pettigrew, and the Lions ran for 162 yards. Minnesota failed on fourth-and-goal from the 2 at the end of a 78-yard drive in the second quarter, then scored two fourth-quarter touchdowns on Matt Cassel's passes to Jarius Wright and Greg Jennings, the first after Sharrif Floyd forced Bell's fumble and Captain Munnerlyn recovered at the Detroit 21; Detroit recovered the onside kick with 1:37 left. Ndamukong Suh made ten tackles with two sacks and three tackles for loss, and Tahir Whitehead forced Chase Ford's fumble for Ezekiel Ansah to recover. Standouts: Suh, Winn, Floyd. Detroit's Stephen Tulloch left with an injury projected at fifty-one days.

## Miami 33, Green Bay 14

Green Bay led 14-6 early in the third quarter on Aaron Rodgers's touchdown passes to John Kuhn and Jordy Nelson, and Miami scored the game's last 27 points: two of Caleb Sturgis's four field goals, Rishard Matthews's touchdown on a blocked punt, Knowshon Moreno's touchdown run and Louis Delmas's 52-yard interception return with 17 seconds left. Lamar Miller ran 25 times for 112, Ryan Tannehill completed 22 of 31 for 204 and was not sacked, Brandon Gibson caught eight for 96, and the Dolphins converted nine of 15 third downs and held the ball nearly 38 minutes. Clay Matthews made fifteen tackles and A.J. Hawk fourteen, but after their second touchdown the Packers punted four times, the first of them blocked, before Delmas's interception; Mason Crosby missed from 49 and a fourth-and-3 at the Miami 39 failed in the first half. Standouts: Miller, Sturgis, Delmas. Green Bay's Ha Ha Clinton-Dix left with an injury projected at seventeen days.

## Houston 16, Indianapolis 0

Houston shut Indianapolis out: Arian Foster ran 23 times for 109 yards as the Texans ran for 175, Ryan Fitzpatrick threw a touchdown pass to DeAndre Hopkins just before the half, and Randy Bullock made three of five field goals, the last after Kendrick Lewis intercepted Andrew Luck. Mike Mohamed, Kareem Jackson and Whitney Mercilus each sacked a Colts quarterback, Brooks Reed forced Dan Herron's fumble for Elbert Mack to recover, and Indianapolis's last threat ended on a failed fourth-and-goal from the Houston 5 with 2:08 left. Luck completed 15 of 25 for 131, Ahmad Bradshaw ran 16 times for 70 and Cory Redding forced and recovered Jay Prosch's fumble, but the Colts drew ten penalties for 91 yards. Standouts: Foster, Lewis, Mohamed. Indianapolis's Luck left on a head hold projected at three days and Houston's Damaris Johnson on one projected at one day.

## New England 34, Buffalo 31

New England led 24-10 at the half, the last of those points on a two-play, 99-yard touchdown drive after Buffalo failed on fourth-and-goal at the 1, and Duron Harmon's 45-yard return of a Kyle Orton interception made it 31-10 early in the third quarter. Stevan Ridley ran 19 times for 146 and two touchdowns, with a 53-yarder, Julian Edelman caught six for 123 and Tom Brady's touchdown pass, and Stephen Gostkowski's 46-yard field goal made it 34-17 early in the fourth quarter. Orton threw for 372 yards and four touchdowns, to C.J. Spiller, Frank Summers, Sammy Watkins and Scott Chandler, Robert Woods caught nine of 13 targets for 137 and Jerry Hughes sacked Brady three times, and Buffalo scored touchdowns with 5:14 and 0:47 left. Standouts: Ridley, Orton, Hughes.

## Philadelphia 27, N.Y. Giants 24 (OT)

Cody Parkey's 45-yard field goal won it in overtime after Cedric Thornton recovered Rashad Jennings's fumble on the Giants' first overtime possession; Parkey had missed from 47 before the half and from 43 with 56 seconds left in regulation. Prince Amukamara's 45-yard interception return put the Giants ahead 17-14 early in the fourth quarter, and after Parkey's tying field goal Bradley Fletcher intercepted Eli Manning and the Eagles scored on the next play, before Manning, who threw touchdown passes to Rueben Randle and Daniel Fells, led an 80-yard drive that tied it at 24 with 3:40 left. Nick Foles completed 33 of 40 for 294 yards, LeSean McCoy ran 20 times for 91 and a touchdown and Brad Smith ran for one and caught another as Philadelphia held the ball nearly 39 minutes and converted 11 of 18 third downs, while Jason Pierre-Paul sacked Foles twice and Jacquian Williams made fourteen tackles. Standouts: Foles, Amukamara, Pierre-Paul. Philadelphia's Jason Kelce left with an injury projected at seventeen days and Bennie Logan on a head hold projected at five, and the Giants' Larry Donnell on a head hold with no time projected lost.

## Cleveland 33, Pittsburgh 30

Billy Cundiff's 59-yard field goal as time expired won it, after his 47-yarder at the end of a 16-play drive had tied it at 30 with 3:13 left; he made all four of his attempts. Buster Skrine returned Antonio Brown's fumble 30 yards for the touchdown that put Cleveland ahead 27-23 in the fourth quarter, Barkevious Mingo forcing it among his fourteen tackles, and Eric Martin's recovery of Brown's muffed punt had set up an earlier field goal; Ben Tate ran 20 times for 96 and a touchdown and Brian Hoyer threw one to Jordan Cameron as Cleveland converted 11 of 20 third downs without a turnover. Ben Roethlisberger threw for 394 yards and a touchdown to Brown, who caught nine for 133, Le'Veon Bell and LeGarrette Blount ran for touchdowns and Shaun Suisham made all three of his field goals, and the Steelers answered Skrine's touchdown with an 83-yard drive to lead 30-27. Standouts: Cundiff, Skrine, Roethlisberger. Pittsburgh's Markus Wheaton left with an injury projected at 115 days and Cleveland's Ahtyba Rubin with one projected at five.

## San Diego 41, Oakland 7

Philip Rivers completed 23 of 31 for 261 yards and three touchdowns without an interception, to Keenan Allen, who caught nine of 13 targets for 113, Ryan Mathews and Seyi Ajirotutu, Mathews also running 22 times for 76 and a touchdown, and Dontrelle Inman returned a punt 100 yards for another touchdown as San Diego led 17-7 at the half and scored the game's last 24 points without a turnover. Manti Te'o sacked Derek Carr twice among five sacks and Shareece Wright forced Marcel Reece's fumble for Brandon Flowers to recover, setting up one of Nick Novak's two field goals. Carr threw Oakland's touchdown to James Jones late in the first half, but the Raiders converted four of 13 third downs, punted eight times and drew ten penalties for 70 yards, with Tarell Brown making eleven tackles. Standouts: Rivers, Inman, Te'o. San Diego's Malcom Floyd left with an injury projected at twenty-five days and Eddie Royal on a head hold projected at twenty, and Te'o and King Dunlap were limited with no time projected lost.

## San Francisco 38, St. Louis 17

San Francisco broke a 17-17 tie with three fourth-quarter touchdowns, Colin Kaepernick completing 27 of 38 for 338 yards and four touchdowns, two to Michael Crabtree, who caught ten of 13 targets for 129, and one each to Anquan Boldin, who caught eight for 115, and Vernon Davis, while Frank Gore ran for another and the 49ers converted 13 of 17 third downs. Perrish Cox forced Zac Stacy's fumble on the Rams' first possession for Antoine Bethea to recover, Cox, Eric Reid and Bethea intercepted Shaun Hill, and St. Louis failed on fourth-and-1 at the San Francisco 15 with 6:36 left, trailing 31-17. Hill threw touchdown passes to Austin Pettis and Lance Kendricks before the half and Greg Zuerlein's field goal tied it in the third quarter; Janoris Jenkins made thirteen tackles and intercepted Kaepernick and William Hayes sacked him twice. Standouts: Kaepernick, Crabtree, Cox. San Francisco's Nick Moody left with an injury projected at seventeen days, Dan Skuta on a head hold projected at seventeen and Joe Staley on one with no time projected lost; St. Louis's Davin Joseph left on a head hold projected at six days.

## Arizona 20, Washington 10

Chandler Catanzaro made six of seven field goals, from 19 to 48 yards, and Arizona's defense added a third-quarter safety, so the Cardinals won without a touchdown, converting two of 14 third downs while holding the ball more than 39 minutes. Carson Palmer threw for 303 yards, Rob Housler catching eight of nine targets for 71 and Michael Floyd three for 77, and Kevin Minter, Antonio Cromartie, Sam Acho and Tony Jefferson each sacked Robert Griffin while Matt Shaughnessy made eleven tackles, three for loss. Washington managed eleven first downs, 39 rushing yards and one conversion in eleven third downs and failed on fourth-and-11 at the Arizona 24 before Griffin's touchdown pass to Alfred Morris with ten seconds left; the Redskins recovered the onside kick that followed. Standouts: Catanzaro, Shaughnessy, Housler. Arizona's Floyd left on a head hold projected at twelve days.
