## Oakland 27, Arizona 13

Oakland scored on four of its five first-half possessions and led 24-6 at the half, Sebastian Janikowski's 42-yard field goal opening the scoring before Latavius Murray and Taiwan Jones ran for touchdowns and Derek Carr threw one to Denarius Moore; Carr completed 14 of 19 for 234 yards without an interception, James Jones catching all six of his targets for 89, and the Raiders converted six of ten third downs. Jonathan Dwyer ran 21 times for 157 yards, with a 35-yarder, as Arizona ran for 193, and Carson Palmer's touchdown pass to Larry Fitzgerald finished a 13-play, 83-yard drive that made it 27-13 late in the third quarter. The Cardinals then reached the Oakland 5 before Neiko Thorpe forced Robert Hughes's fumble and Charles Woodson recovered, and after Kevin Minter recovered Taiwan Jones's fumble at midfield, Arizona failed on fourth-and-2 at the Oakland 42 with 4:49 left. Standouts: Carr, Dwyer, James Jones. Arizona's Stepfan Taylor left with an injury projected at ten days, Ted Ginn on a head hold projected at seven and Rashad Johnson on one projected at three.

## Baltimore 25, Atlanta 19 (OT)

Baltimore took the overtime kickoff and drove 67 yards in 10 plays for the winning touchdown, after Matt Bryant's four fourth-quarter field goals, the last from 36 as time expired, had brought Atlanta back from 19-7 to tie it at 19; the first of them followed the Ravens' failed fourth-and-1 at their own 10. Atlanta led 7-0 on Matt Ryan's touchdown pass to Steven Jackson at the end of an 11-play opening drive before Baltimore scored the next 19 points on an 88-yard touchdown drive and four Justin Tucker field goals, one after Darian Stewart intercepted Ryan and another after Marlon Brown recovered Devin Hester's fumbled kickoff return at the Atlanta 19. Bernard Pierce ran 24 times for 97 yards and a touchdown, Kyle Juszczyk ran for the other and Terrell Suggs made thirteen tackles with a sack, while Devonta Freeman ran 15 times for 112 and Joplo Bartu made twelve tackles for the Falcons, and the Ravens drew eleven penalties for 101 yards. Standouts: Pierce, Bryant, Suggs. Atlanta's Justin Blalock left with an injury projected at sixteen days and William Moore on a head hold projected at two, and Baltimore's Dennis Pitta with an injury projected at four.

## Green Bay 47, Carolina 30

Aaron Rodgers completed 25 of 34 for 357 yards and four touchdowns without an interception, two to Randall Cobb, who caught seven for 101, and one each to Jordy Nelson and Richard Rodgers, and Eddie Lacy ran 24 times for 132 and a touchdown as Green Bay scored on each of its first six possessions and led 34-10 at the half. Sam Shields intercepted Cam Newton twice, on Carolina's first possession and with 3:14 left, each time setting up a score, and Clay Matthews's strip sack, recovered by Tramon Williams, gave the Packers the ball at the Carolina 1, though they lost seven yards and settled for the first of Mason Crosby's four field goals. Newton threw for 348 yards and four touchdowns, to Kelvin Benjamin, who caught seven for 106, Brenton Bersin, DeAngelo Williams and Corey Brown, whose only catch went 55 yards, and the Panthers scored touchdowns on their first three possessions of the second half to get within 34-30, but Graham Gano missed an extra point, Green Bay sacked Newton seven times, Brad Jones twice, and the Packers scored the last 13 points. Standouts: Aaron Rodgers, Shields, Cobb.

## Indianapolis 30, Cincinnati 23

Ahmad Bradshaw's 46-yard touchdown run with two seconds left won it, on the play after Dan Herron returned the kickoff 57 yards to the Cincinnati 46, Mike Nugent having tied it at 23 with a 50-yard field goal with 21 seconds left. T.Y. Hilton caught four passes for 142 yards and both of Andrew Luck's touchdown passes, the first on the opening drive, and Gregory Toler intercepted Andy Dalton twice and Mike Adams once in the first half, Toler's second, with 18 seconds left in the half, setting up Adam Vinatieri's 55-yard field goal for a 13-3 halftime lead. Dalton threw for 350 yards and a touchdown to Dane Sanzenbacher, who caught six for 106, and Jeremy Hill ran for another as the Bengals came back from 23-13 in the fourth quarter, while Bradshaw finished with 128 yards on 21 carries and Cory Redding made nine tackles, three for loss, with a sack. Standouts: Bradshaw, Hilton, Toler. Cincinnati's Giovani Bernard left with an injury projected at thirty-five days and Cedric Peerman was limited with no time projected lost, and Indianapolis's D'Qwell Jackson left on a head hold projected at fifteen days.

## Pittsburgh 38, Houston 31

Ben Roethlisberger threw for 357 yards and four touchdowns without an interception, two to Justin Brown and one each to Heath Miller, who caught two passes for 76 yards, and Le'Veon Bell, and LeGarrette Blount ran for another as Pittsburgh converted 12 of 16 third downs and scored touchdowns on five of its first eight possessions, the third a two-play, 76-yard drive in the last 19 seconds of the half for a 24-14 lead; Antonio Brown's 62-yard punt return had set up Shaun Suisham's field goal. Houston did not turn the ball over either: Ryan Fitzpatrick completed 25 of 33 for 262 yards and two touchdowns to DeAndre Hopkins, who caught 11 of 14 targets for 133, and Alfred Blue ran five times for 80 yards and two touchdowns, his 68-yarder coming on a two-play, 72-yard drive that tied it at 7. After Suisham missed from 55 with 2:05 left, the Texans drove 55 yards to score with 23 seconds left and trail 38-31, but Pittsburgh recovered the onside kick. Standouts: Roethlisberger, Hopkins, Blue. Pittsburgh's Antonio Brown left on a head hold projected at eight days and Ramon Foster on one with no time projected lost.

## San Diego 23, Kansas City 20 (OT)

Nick Novak's 38-yard field goal on San Diego's first possession of overtime won it, and Kansas City's answer, a 13-play drive, ended when Alex Smith was intercepted on fourth-and-10 at the San Diego 44; Novak, who also made from 23 and 31, had missed from 54 with 1:58 left in regulation. After Kansas City went ahead 13-7 early in the third quarter, Donald Brown returned the kickoff 105 yards for a touchdown that put the Chargers ahead 14-13, Philip Rivers having thrown a touchdown pass to Ryan Mathews at the end of a 12-play, 88-yard drive just before the half. Kansas City had led 6-0 on Cairo Santos's field goals, the first after Rivers was intercepted and the second after San Diego failed on fourth-and-16 at the Kansas City 35, and Jamaal Charles and Joe McKnight ran for the Chiefs' touchdowns, the second putting them ahead 20-17 early in the fourth quarter. Derrick Johnson and Marcus Cooper intercepted Rivers, Brandon Flowers and Marcus Gilchrist intercepted Smith, Manti Te'o sacked Smith three times among his ten tackles and Dontari Poe sacked Rivers twice. Standouts: Donald Brown, Te'o, Novak. San Diego's Jahleel Addae left on a head hold projected at six days, Richard Marshall on one projected at four and Shareece Wright on one projected at three, and Kansas City's Albert Wilson with an injury projected at four.

## Chicago 23, Miami 16

Robbie Gould made five of six field goals, from 38 to 50 yards, the fourth after an interception gave Chicago the ball at the Miami 20 with 33 seconds left in the half, and Jay Cutler's touchdown pass to Alshon Jeffery, who caught seven of 12 targets for 149, and his two-point pass to Martellus Bennett put the Bears ahead 23-16 with 8:20 left. Miami had led 7-0 on its 11-play opening drive and went ahead 16-15 with 11:27 left on a five-play, 80-yard drive that included Lamar Miller's 60-yard run, Miller finishing with 121 yards on 18 carries, but the two-point pass failed; Knowshon Moreno ran for one Dolphins touchdown and Ryan Tannehill threw the other to Brian Hartline. Philip Wheeler forced and recovered Bennett's fumble at the Miami 5 in the third quarter, and Charles Tillman and Brock Vereen each intercepted Tannehill once, the later of the two interceptions coming with 3:27 left before Chicago ran out the clock. Standouts: Gould, Jeffery, Wheeler. Chicago's Kyle Fuller left on a head hold projected at 100 days, Matt Forte with an injury projected at thirty-eight and Roberto Garza on a head hold projected at three.

## Buffalo 35, Minnesota 21

Kyle Orton completed 29 of 42 for 345 yards without an interception, throwing touchdown passes to Robert Woods and Scott Chandler, who had 93 receiving yards each, while C.J. Spiller ran for two touchdowns and Fred Jackson for another, and Buffalo scored touchdowns on four straight possessions to turn a 14-0 deficit into a 28-14 lead, converting nine of 16 third downs and holding the ball more than 36 minutes. Minnesota had led 14-0 after the first quarter on Matt Cassel's touchdown passes of 34 yards to Rhett Ellison and 14 to Matt Asiata, and Asiata's 81-yard punt return brought the Vikings within 28-21 late in the third quarter, but the Bills answered with a 14-play, 85-yard touchdown drive, and Keith Rivers intercepted Cassel with 18 seconds left after Minnesota had reached the Buffalo 11. Chad Greenway made fourteen tackles and Jeff Locke punted eight times, five inside the 20. Standouts: Orton, Spiller, Asiata. Minnesota's Brian Robison left with an injury projected at twenty days.

## New Orleans 35, Detroit 28

Mark Ingram ran 22 times for 126 yards and a touchdown and caught seven passes for 102 and another as New Orleans, held scoreless until the last two minutes of the first half, scored touchdowns on five straight possessions, from a 10-play, 90-yard drive that ended with five seconds left in the half to a four-play, 58-yard drive on the ground that made it 35-21 with 2:19 left. Drew Brees threw touchdown passes to Jimmy Graham, Marques Colston, who caught eight for 98, and Ingram, and after Keenan Lewis intercepted Matthew Stafford at the end of a 13-play drive in the third quarter, the Saints went 97 yards to tie it at 14; Curtis Lofton sacked Stafford twice. Detroit led 14-0 early in the second quarter, Stafford throwing for 363 yards with touchdown passes to Brandon Pettigrew and Jed Collins and Golden Tate catching 11 of 13 targets for 165, and Darius Slay intercepted Brees and Dwight Bentley recovered Ingram's fumble, forced by Nick Fairley, but Matt Prater missed from 45 and 52 and New Orleans recovered the onside kick after the Lions' last touchdown with 1:09 left. Standouts: Ingram, Brees, Tate. New Orleans's Brodrick Bunkley left with an injury projected at 110 days.

## Dallas 38, N.Y. Giants 31

Dallas led 35-10 with 4:15 left in the half on Tony Romo's touchdown passes to Jason Witten and Terrance Williams, Barry Church's 40-yard interception return, Dwayne Harris's 105-yard kickoff return and DeMarco Murray's touchdown run, and Dan Bailey's 38-yard field goal made it 38-17 in the third quarter. Eli Manning completed 32 of 42 for 386 yards and ran for a touchdown, Henry Hynoski and Peyton Hillis also running for scores and Victor Cruz catching seven of eight targets for 131 and a touchdown, and the Giants held the ball more than 35 minutes and finished touchdown drives of 80 and 96 yards to get within 38-31 with 2:59 left, but their last possession ended at the Dallas 42 as time ran out. Henry Melton and Nick Hayden each sacked Manning twice, Church made twelve tackles and Antrel Rolle intercepted Romo on fourth-and-9 before the half, while Dallas converted two of eight third downs and drew ten penalties for 91 yards. Standouts: Church, Harris, Cruz. Dallas's Brandon Carr left on a head hold projected at four days and Melton on one projected at one day, and the Giants' John Jerry on one projected at three.

## N.Y. Jets 34, New England 31 (OT)

Nick Folk's 45-yard field goal won it in overtime, four plays after the Patriots went three and out on the first possession and Jeremy Kerley returned their punt 31 yards; Stephen Gostkowski had missed from 59 at the end of regulation. New England led 31-17 with 5:45 left after Logan Ryan returned a Geno Smith interception 41 yards for a touchdown, and the Jets scored touchdowns on their next two possessions, the second after a punt gave them the ball at the New England 44, to tie it with 52 seconds left. Smith completed 25 of 35 for 283 yards and three touchdowns, two to Kerley, who caught eight of 11 targets for 123, and one to Chris Ivory, who also ran for one, and Chris Johnson's 42-yard run came on the 85-yard drive that tied it at 17 early in the fourth quarter; Tom Brady threw for 339 yards and touchdown passes to Julian Edelman and Aaron Dobson, James White ran for the Patriots' other touchdown and Darrelle Revis made twelve tackles. Standouts: Kerley, Ivory, Logan Ryan. New England's Brandon Bolden left on a head hold projected at nineteen days, Rob Ninkovich on one projected at fourteen and Dont'a Hightower on one projected at two, and Jerod Mayo with an injury projected at one day.

## Denver 30, San Francisco 21

San Francisco's 17-play drive in the fourth quarter, kept alive on fourth-and-10, ended on a failed fourth-and-6 at the Denver 6, and after a Denver three-and-out, Manning's last series, the 49ers' next possession began at the Denver 36 and ended in an interception after reaching the Denver 11, Chris Harris and T.J. Ward each intercepting Colin Kaepernick. Anquan Boldin caught Kaepernick's touchdown pass and ran for another and Frank Gore ran for one, and a five-play, 72-yard drive extended on fourth-and-13 brought the 49ers within 27-21 with 2:48 left, but Denver recovered the onside kick and Brandon McManus made his third field goal, from 54, with Brock Osweiler at quarterback; Michael Crabtree caught nine for 110 and Michael Wilhoite made eleven tackles with a sack. Standouts: Manning, Julius Thomas, McManus. Denver's Manning left with an injury projected at twenty days.

## Seattle 31, St. Louis 30

Tarvaris Jackson, who replaced the injured Russell Wilson late in the first quarter, threw touchdown passes to Doug Baldwin and Jermaine Kearse without an interception and Marshawn Lynch ran 27 times for 102 yards and two touchdowns, and after Greg Zuerlein's third field goal, from 45, put St. Louis ahead 30-24 with 6:27 left, Seattle drove 80 yards in 10 plays to lead 31-30 with 2:10 left; the Rams' last possession ended on a failed fourth-and-15 at the Seattle 49. Rodney McLeod intercepted Wilson to give the Rams the ball at the Seattle 2, setting up the first of Shaun Hill's two touchdown passes to Kenny Britt, and McLeod, who made twelve tackles, forced Lynch's fumble on Seattle's first snap after the Rams tied it at 17, setting up another Zuerlein field goal; Tavon Austin's 29-yard punt return then set up the touchdown that made it 27-17 early in the fourth quarter. Jeron Johnson and K.J. Wright forced the Rams' two lost fumbles, DeShawn Shead and Jeremy Lane recovering them, and each led to a Seattle touchdown. Standouts: Lynch, McLeod, Tarvaris Jackson. Seattle's Wilson left with an injury projected at thirty-seven days, Christine Michael with one projected at fourteen and Bobby Wagner on a head hold projected at ten.

## Tennessee 27, Washington 24

Shonn Greene ran 26 times for 207 yards and all three Tennessee touchdowns as the Titans ran for 238, the last of them ending a four-play, 76-yard drive on the ground that made it 27-16 with 20 seconds left, after Washington, having converted one fourth-and-4, failed on another at the Tennessee 24 with 2:27 left. Pierre Garcon's 80-yard touchdown catch on the Redskins' next play and a two-point pass made it 27-24 with seven seconds left, but Tennessee recovered the onside kick. Washington had led 5-0 after Kai Forbath's 46-yard field goal and a safety on the Titans' first possession, and tied it at 13 in the third quarter when Keenan Robinson forced Greene's fumble, David Amerson recovered at the Tennessee 31 and Robert Griffin threw a touchdown pass to Jordan Reed and a two-point pass; Ryan Succop missed from 37 after Andre Roberts's muffed punt gave Tennessee the ball at the Washington 20, and Griffin threw for 313 yards without an interception. Standouts: Greene, Garcon, Keenan Robinson. Tennessee's Brett Kern left with an injury projected at fifty-three days, and Washington's DeAngelo Hall on a head hold projected at nine and Alfred Morris with an injury projected at two.
