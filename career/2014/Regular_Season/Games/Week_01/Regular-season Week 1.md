Your August 31 practice-squad plan remains contingent rather than presumed. The September 23 date for changing waiver priority to current-season standings is established; Jacksonville being exactly 26th before then remains a working prior-draft-order assumption until the personnel office verifies the applicable ordering.

I would carry your 53 recommendation into Caldwell's meeting unchanged. ### 2. Philadelphia defense: begin with rules, not guessed percentages

Public evidence available before the branch date establishes Billy Davis's defense as primarily a 3-4 structure with 4-3 looks and substantial multiplicity. Davis also described the base ends as predominantly two-gap players and the 3-4 structure as useful against spread width. That supports preparing for changing surfaces and pressure presentations, but it does **not** tell us what Philadelphia will call against Jacksonville on September 7. ### 3. Defense: Philadelphia tempo

Crennel prepares the defense for a Kelly offense without pretending we know the Week 1 menu. The emphasis is operational: get the call, identify eligible distribution, set the front, communicate motion, and be ready to play without substituting.

The carried defensive vocabulary remains **Under, Nickel Even, Cover 1, 1 Robber/Rat, Cover 3, Quarters, four-man rush and the installed five-man pressure**. No line game is added merely because Philadelphia may play fast. The previous sheet did not establish one as taught. Game_1_Context_and_Research

### 4. Special teams

Westhoff builds the opening operation around Scobee, Anger and Kreiter, Hawkins as KR/PR and Hurns as the second punt-return option. The Atlanta coverage work carries forward: lane responsibility and kick placement are graded separately. Bouye cannot be built into a coverage unit until medical clears him.

### 5. Quarterback input

Before the final reduction of the offensive sheet, Bates and Tice ask Cousins for the calls he trusts against pressure and the calls he does not want protected only by theory. The specific candidates to discuss are **Stick, Mesh, Drive, Y-Cross, Dagger, Boot Flood and the Slow Screen**.
