## 1. Preparation and final game-day decisions

Stone's [Week 7 Game Plan](Game_Plan.md) kept the calling arrangement: Stone calling the offense and the defense, Romeo Crennel preparing the defense and sending the call in, Daryl Smith declaring the front and Mike Westhoff calling the kicking game. It changed the depth chart. C.J. Anderson moved ahead of Maurice Jones-Drew at running back, Travis Kelce ahead of Marcedes Lewis at tight end, and the receivers ran Davante Adams, Adam Thielen, Andrew Hawkins, Allen Hurns, Taylor Gabriel and Hakeem Nicks. On the line Andrew Norwell took left guard and Corey Linsley center, with Joel Bitonio listed last while he was out. Aaron Donald moved to second on the defensive line, between Jeremy Mincey and Sen'Derrick Marks, with Roy Miller fifth, and Telvin Smith to third at linebacker. Alan Ball took the nickel and Malcolm Butler the outside reserve corner job while A.J. Bouye was out. With Nicks sixth among the receivers, the plan took the Nicks X relief variation off its two carried calls. Fourth downs, two-point tries and kickoffs stayed on Stone's standing rules. After the five sacks at Tennessee the plan put the left-side and center protection communication first among the practice priorities, then Wide Zone, Boot and the shifts, the man and zone answers, Donald's interior rush, the A-gap mug with matched coverage, the run fits and the kicking units; the Flea Flicker was practice only.

Monday, October 13, was the walkthrough under Stone's standing practice schedule, and Tuesday the players' day off. Wednesday's practice was the week's one padded practice, in helmets and shoulder pads; Thursday and Friday were in helmets, and Saturday, October 18, the walkthrough. No one was injured during the week, no new restriction was communicated and the club made no roster move.

Bitonio and Bouye were reassessed on Wednesday, October 15, the date their instructions named, with no change: out, projected return October 22 ([medical history](../../../Team_Operations/Medical/medical_history.md#october-13-to-18-bitonio-and-bouye-reassessed-no-change)). Neither took part in the Wednesday, Thursday or Friday practices. As Stone's plan provided, Norwell took the first-unit left-guard work beside Eugene Monroe and Linsley, and Ball the nickel with Butler as the outside reserve. The league's report listed both as non-participants all three days and out for the game.

**Plan in force:** On offense, one formation shown to Cleveland as several plays: Kelce and Lewis shifting, Outside Zone with its Boot from the same Wing look, Counter after Power, and a back or tight end released into the space the pressure leaves, with the protection counted for the new left guard. On defense, Donald and Marks inside, a four-man rush with the A-gap threats shown, and Lee and Cameron first in the coverage count ([Game Plan](Game_Plan.md)).  
**Game-day availability:** Bitonio and Bouye out under their medical instructions. Stone's standing inactive rule sat the deepest healthy player of the five largest healthy groups: Jordan Poyer, Charles Leno Jr., Kasim Edebali, Todd Davis and Hakeem Nicks.
