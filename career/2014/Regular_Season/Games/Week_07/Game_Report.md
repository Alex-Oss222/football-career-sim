## 1. Preparation and final game-day decisions

Stone's [Week 7 Game Plan](Game_Plan.md) kept the calling arrangement: Stone calling the offense and the defense, Romeo Crennel preparing the defense and sending the call in, Daryl Smith declaring the front and Mike Westhoff calling the kicking game. It changed the depth chart. C.J. Anderson moved ahead of Maurice Jones-Drew at running back, Travis Kelce ahead of Marcedes Lewis at tight end, and the receivers ran Davante Adams, Adam Thielen, Andrew Hawkins, Allen Hurns, Taylor Gabriel and Hakeem Nicks. On the line Andrew Norwell took left guard and Corey Linsley center, with Joel Bitonio listed last while he was out. Aaron Donald moved to second on the defensive line, between Jeremy Mincey and Sen'Derrick Marks, with Roy Miller fifth, and Telvin Smith to third at linebacker. Alan Ball took the nickel and Malcolm Butler the outside reserve corner job while A.J. Bouye was out. With Nicks sixth among the receivers, the plan took the Nicks X relief variation off its two carried calls. Fourth downs, two-point tries and kickoffs stayed on Stone's standing rules. After the five sacks at Tennessee the plan put the left-side and center protection communication first among the practice priorities, then Wide Zone, Boot and the shifts, the man and zone answers, Donald's interior rush, the A-gap mug with matched coverage, the run fits and the kicking units; the Flea Flicker was practice only.

Monday, October 13, was the walkthrough under Stone's standing practice schedule, and Tuesday the players' day off. No one was injured during the week, no new restriction was communicated and the club made no roster move.

Bitonio and Bouye were reassessed on Wednesday, October 15, the date their instructions named, with no change: out, projected return October 22 ([medical history](../../../Team_Operations/Medical/medical_history.md#october-13-to-18-bitonio-and-bouye-reassessed-no-change)). ## 2. The game

Cleveland won 34-31 at EverBank Field on Jordan Cameron's 5-yard touchdown catch at 0:17 and Brian Hoyer's two-point pass to Marqise Lee. Jacksonville intercepted Hoyer four times, turned the first two into touchdowns in the first quarter and scored on Paul Posluszny's 37-yard return of the third, and it led 31-26 after Josh Scobee's 50-yard field goal with 10:43 left. It did not score again. Twice in the last five minutes, leading by five, it went for it on fourth down on its own side of midfield under Stone's standing rule and was stopped, the second time at its 33 with 0:43 left, and Cleveland needed five snaps from the Jacksonville 32. Cleveland ran 88 plays to Jacksonville's 51.

### First quarter

Cleveland's first possession reached midfield on Hoyer's 25-yard throw to Ben Tate on third-and-5, and on the next snap Alterraun Verner intercepted Hoyer's throw for Lee and returned it 12 yards to the 50. Kirk Cousins found Adam Thielen for 15 and Davante Adams for 16, and C.J. Anderson ran to the 13. A Cleveland offside on third-and-5 and Lane Johnson's false start left first-and-goal at the 13, and after Anderson's 2-yard run and an incompletion for Adams, Joe Haden's pass interference on third-and-goal put the ball at the 1. Anderson scored on the next snap at 9:22, 7-0.

On Cleveland's first snap after the kickoff, Aqib Talib intercepted Hoyer's throw for Lee and returned it 4 yards to the Cleveland 17. Justin Gilbert's holding foul moved the ball to the 12, Anderson's 8-yard run on third-and-7 reached the 1, and after Phillip Taylor stopped Anderson for a loss of 2, Cousins found Marcedes Lewis for a 3-yard touchdown at 6:57, 14-0. Andrew Norwell was hurt on that drive and did not return; Cornelius Lucas played left guard the rest of the way.

Cleveland answered with eight plays. Hoyer ran 18 yards on the first snap, Glenn Winston ran 14 on third-and-8, and Marlon Moore's 27-yard catch reached the Jacksonville 19, where the drive stalled and Billy Cundiff kicked a 36-yard field goal at 3:03, 14-3. Talib was hurt on that series and did not return; Verner moved to his spot and Malcolm Butler came on outside. Jacksonville's next possession went backward: Barkevious Mingo sacked Cousins for a loss of 7 on third-and-8, and Bryan Anger's 54-yard punt was returned 2 yards to the Cleveland 33.

### Second quarter

Cleveland's 13-play drive from its 33 converted on third-and-4 with Cameron's 17-yard catch and on third-and-5 with Travis Benjamin's 11-yard catch, which left first-and-goal at the 1. Hoyer threw three incompletions, for Cameron, Ray Agnew and Jim Dray, and on fourth down Verner sacked him for a loss of 7 at 10:33. Daryl Smith was hurt on the series and did not return; Posluszny moved to his spot, Telvin Smith to Posluszny's and Sio Moore came on.

From its 8 Jacksonville threw an incompletion and lost a yard on each of two runs, and Anger punted 63 yards from the 6; Benjamin returned it 20 yards to the Jacksonville 49. Cleveland scored in six plays. Lee caught a 22-yard pass on third-and-2, Telvin Smith's face-mask foul on third-and-9 at the 18 gave Cleveland first-and-goal at the 9, and Hoyer found Benjamin for the touchdown at 5:57, 14-10. Josh Gordon was hurt on the drive and did not return.

Allen Hurns's 14-yard catch and Ahtyba Rubin's neutral-zone foul took Jacksonville to the Cleveland 49, three incompletions followed, and Anger's 46-yard punt was downed at the Cleveland 3. Tate and Winston moved Cleveland to its 16, and on the next snap Posluszny intercepted Hoyer's throw for Winston and returned it 37 yards for a touchdown at 2:10, 21-10.

Cleveland used the last two minutes for points. Marlon Moore caught a 19-yard pass on third-and-4, and Sio Moore forced a fumble that Gary Barnidge recovered for Cleveland. Winston ran 15 yards, Terrance West's 7-yard catch reached the 30, and Cundiff's 48-yard field goal at 0:43 made it 21-13. Jacksonville knelt to end the half.

### Third quarter

Jacksonville's first possession of the half reached its 36 on Adams's 14-yard catch and Thielen's 6-yard catch, and after an incompletion for Anderson on third-and-4, Anger punted 64 yards into the end zone. Cleveland's nine-play drive from its 20 converted on third-and-8 with Benjamin's 15-yard catch, and Tate ran 25 yards, Winston 10 and West 14 to the Jacksonville 14. An illegal-formation foul on third-and-2 at the 6 pushed Cleveland back to the 11, Cameron's 3-yard catch left fourth-and-4 at the 8, and Cundiff kicked a 25-yard field goal at 8:14, 21-16.

On Jacksonville's second snap after the kickoff, Tashaun Gipson intercepted Cousins's throw for Adams and returned it to the Cleveland 47. Benjamin's 11-yard catch and, after an illegal substitution against Cleveland, Verner's 30-yard pass-interference foul put the ball at the Jacksonville 17. After an incompletion, Tate ran for 8 and 5 to first-and-goal at the 4, Hoyer threw three incompletions, and Cundiff's 23-yard field goal at 5:15 made it 21-19.

Jacksonville answered on the first snap after the touchback: Cousins found Thielen for an 80-yard touchdown at 4:57, 28-19. Cleveland then went 80 yards in 13 plays. Tate carried three straight times from the 20, the third for 7 yards on third-and-7, and on fourth-and-5 at its 48 with 1:10 left in the quarter Hoyer found Cameron for 6. Tate ran 13 yards to the Jacksonville 33 on the last snap of the quarter.

### Fourth quarter

Jeremy Mincey sacked Hoyer on the first snap of the fourth quarter, and on second-and-11 Tate caught a 34-yard touchdown pass at 14:04, 28-25; Cundiff's kick made it 28-26. Jacksonville answered with a field goal. Anderson ran for 9 and Adams for 10, Cousins hit Travis Kelce for 17 to the Cleveland 37 and Anderson ran 7 more, but an incompletion for Adams and Karlos Dansby's stop of Anderson for a loss of 2 on third-and-3 left fourth-and-5 at the 32. Scobee kicked a 50-yard field goal at 10:43, 31-26.

Lee's 19-yard catch on third-and-4 took Cleveland to the Jacksonville 48, but Lee's illegal block on Benjamin's 3-yard catch left third-and-14 at the Cleveland 48, the throw for Lee fell incomplete, and Spencer Lanning's 40-yard punt was fair-caught at the Jacksonville 12. Anderson ran 15 yards on first down, and three plays later Jacksonville faced fourth-and-1 at its 36 with 4:52 left. It went for it under Stone's rule, and Rubin stopped Maurice Jones-Drew for no gain. Cleveland reached the 25 before Bacarri Rambo intercepted Hoyer's throw for Cameron and returned it 1 yard to the Jacksonville 14 with 2:46 left.

Jacksonville ran six plays from there. Anderson ran for 4 and 7 yards to a first down at the 25, Cousins scrambled for 2 and Jones-Drew ran for 1, and Anderson's 5 yards on third-and-7 left fourth-and-2 at the Jacksonville 33 with 0:43 left. The rule sent the offense again, and Armonty Bryant stopped Anderson for a loss of 1. Cleveland took over at the Jacksonville 32 with 0:38 left. After an incompletion for Benjamin, Agnew caught a 27-yard pass to the 5, Hoyer spiked the ball at 0:23, and after another incompletion for Benjamin, Cameron caught the 5-yard touchdown on third-and-goal at 0:17, 32-31. Cleveland went for two, and Hoyer's pass to Lee was good, 34-31. Andrew Hawkins returned the kickoff 15 yards with five seconds left, Chris Prosinski's holding foul put the ball back at the Jacksonville 23, and Mingo sacked Cousins for a loss of 16 on the last play.

### The calls and decisions that shaped the game

Stone's Game Plan kept fourth downs, two-point tries and kickoffs on his standing rules. The fourth-down rule reads only the distance and the spot: fourth-and-2 or less is a go with offensive coordinator Mike Tice's first call on that down's menu; fourth-and-3 or 4 is a go on the opponent's side of midfield; otherwise it is a field goal when the kick is 52 yards or less, from the opponent's 34 in, and a punt beyond it. It decided all seven of Jacksonville's fourth downs: four punts, one field goal and two tries for the first down, neither converted.

At fourth-and-10 at the Cleveland 49 late in the first half the kick would have been 67 yards, so the rule called for the punt. Anger's punt was downed at the 3, and three snaps later Posluszny's interception return made it 21-10.

At fourth-and-5 at the Cleveland 32 with 10:43 left and Jacksonville ahead 28-26, the kick was 50 yards, inside the rule's line, and Scobee made it.

The rule's first line has no term for the score, the clock or field position. At fourth-and-1 at the Jacksonville 36 with 4:52 left and a five-point lead it sent the offense, and the call it names for the distance was 12 Ace, Power Right. Rubin stopped Jones-Drew for no gain, and Cleveland started at the Jacksonville 36; Rambo's interception on second-and-9 at the 25 ended that possession.

Jacksonville's last series began at its 14 with 2:46 left and reached fourth-and-2 at its 33 with 0:43 left, still ahead 31-26. The same line of the rule applied, with the same call, and Bryant stopped Anderson for a loss of 1. Cleveland took over at the Jacksonville 32 with 0:38 left, and Agnew's 27-yard catch on its second snap put it at the 5.

Cleveland's touchdown at 0:17 left it ahead 32-31, and it went for two rather than kick; Hoyer's pass to Lee made the lead three. Jacksonville's own two-point rule, a kick except in the fourth quarter when a touchdown leaves it down 2, up 1 or down 5, kicked after all four of its touchdowns, each in the first three quarters. No onside situation under the kickoff rule arose.
