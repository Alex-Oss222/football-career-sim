## 1. Preparation and final game-day decisions

Stone's [Week 6 Game Plan](Game_Plan.md) kept the arrangement of the Pittsburgh game: Stone calling the offense and the defense, Romeo Crennel preparing the defense and sending the call in, Daryl Smith declaring the front and Mike Westhoff calling the kicking game. It made no depth or role change. Fourth downs were to follow the rule Stone gave during the Pittsburgh game from the first one on; in Week 5 he had answered the first himself and given the rule after it. Two-point tries and kickoffs stayed on his standing rules. After the four turnovers against Pittsburgh, the plan put securing the ball and defining Kirk Cousins's answer against pressure first among the week's practice priorities, and it added nothing new to the practice menu: no Throwback Screen, Halfback Pass, WR Pass or new motion family.

Monday, October 6, was the Pittsburgh review and the Tennessee introduction, and Tuesday the players' day off. Under Stone's standing practice schedule Week 6 had no padded practice, and the Wednesday, Thursday and Friday practices were in helmets. On Saturday, October 11, the team held its walkthrough and travelled to Nashville. No one was injured during the week, no new restriction was communicated and the club made no roster move.

Each of the three players under a medical instruction at the start of the week was cleared or released on the date projected for him ([medical history](../../../Team_Operations/Medical/medical_history.md#october-6-to-11-christian-jones-and-trawick-cleared-poyer-released)). Christian Jones was reassessed and cleared on Monday, took the review and every session after it in full, and was not on the league's injury report. Brynden Trawick was reassessed on Tuesday with no change, out with a projected return of October 11, and took no part in the Wednesday, Thursday and Friday practices. As Stone's plan provided, Chris Prosinski took his deep-safety work and Adrian Phillips the next safety and coverage duties while he was out. The medical staff cleared Trawick on Saturday, and he took the walkthrough in full. The independent medical process released Jordan Poyer's hold on Thursday, October 9. He had not practiced before the release and took part in full on Thursday and Friday and in the walkthrough, with no communicated limitation. The league's report listed Trawick as a non-participant all three days and questionable for the game, and Poyer as a non-participant Wednesday and full Thursday and Friday with no game designation. Because each came on its projected date, the alternatives Stone had written into the plan for a different result were not needed: Todd Davis in Jones's linebacker and coverage work, and the inactives and the punt, kickoff, dime and emergency-safety groups rebuilt without Trawick.

**Plan in force:** On offense, the same 12-personnel look shown to Tennessee several ways: Maurice Jones-Drew running at the actual box count, Marcedes Lewis and Travis Kelce shifting to test the linebackers' coverage rules, Outside Zone paired with Boot Flood and Power with Counter and Power Pass. On defense, Shonn Greene's run fits first and four rushers holding their lanes against Jake Locker unless Stone calls the five-man counter ([Game Plan](Game_Plan.md)).  
**Game-day availability:** No change from the Week 5 game-day unit. Jones and Trawick, both hurt against Pittsburgh, were cleared, and Stone's plan named the same seven inactives as in Week 5: Poyer, Malcolm Butler, Jonathan Grimes, Corey Linsley, Kasim Edebali, Taylor Gabriel and Gator Hoskins. Poyer was on the list by Stone's decision, not by medical instruction, as Butler had been in Week 5 after his own release.

## 2. Pregame media

Stone met reporters after practice on Friday, October 10, the day before the walkthrough and the flight to Nashville.

**Reporter:** Brynden Trawick didn't practice this week. Where is he?

**Stone:** He was looked at again Tuesday and nothing changed, so he didn't practice, and he's questionable. He's cleared when the doctors clear him, not before. Christian was cleared Monday and he's done everything since, so he's off the report.

**Reporter:** And Poyer?

**Stone:** The independent process released Jordan yesterday. He didn't practice before that. He went full yesterday and today, and there's no designation on him.

**Reporter:** So he plays Sunday?

**Stone:** He's eligible. You'll get the inactives Sunday with everybody else.

**Reporter:** If Trawick isn't cleared, who's next to Rambo?

**Stone:** Chris took Brynden's work at deep safety this week, and Adrian had the next safety job and the coverage duties behind it. If the doctors don't clear Brynden, we'll go back through all 46 and set it from there. I'm not going to name a starter ahead of a medical decision that hasn't been made.

**Reporter:** You were inside the Pittsburgh 30 five times and came away with 10 points, and you turned it over four times. What has to change?

**Stone:** We moved the ball, and then we gave it to them four times, and too many good drives came back with nothing. That starts with me: the calls, the plan and the spots I put players in. Tennessee is a division team. We have to protect the football, tackle and execute the situations we practice.

**Reporter:** Three of the four were Kirk's interceptions. How is he handling it?

**Stone:** You'll have to ask Kirk. I'm not going to take those apart up here one at a time and hang them on him. What we say about them stays in the quarterback room.

**Reporter:** Tennessee is 1-4, but all four losses have been one-score games, the last two by a point at Indianapolis and by three to Cleveland. What do you see?

**Stone:** That they're close, and they're at home. The record doesn't tell you much about Sunday. Shonn Greene has 81 carries in five games, so it starts with fitting the run and tackling him. Kendall Wright is their leading receiver, and you'd better know where Delanie Walker is. And you can't give Jake Locker a lane. Once he's out of the pocket, the coverage has to hold a lot longer than anybody wants it to.
