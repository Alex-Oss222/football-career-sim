Write your decisions on the [Game Plan](Game_Plan.md); the staff checks it and tells you what is missing before anything is set for the game.

**Standing directions:**

- **Calling arrangement:** You call, delegate or take back any phase at any time; the arrangement stands until you change it. [Who calls what](../../../Team_Operations/Staff/README.md#who-calls-what)
- **Inactives, from August 31, 2014:** The deepest healthy reserve on the chart in each of the five largest healthy position groups, never below a legal game-day unit, re-set each week. [Roster decisions](../../../Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Roster_Decisions/roster_decisions.md)
- **Two-point tries:** Kick, except go for two in the fourth quarter when the touchdown leaves Jacksonville down 2, up 1 or down 5. [Week 5 report](../Week_05/output.md)
- **Kickoffs:** Onside when trailing by 1 to 16 with 2:00 or less left; onside when trailing by 9 to 16 with 5:00 or less left; deep otherwise. [Week 5 report](../Week_05/output.md)
- **Fourth downs:** Fourth-and-2 or less, go, with Tice's first call on the matching menu; fourth-and-3 or 4, go on the opponent's side of midfield, otherwise the field goal or the punt by the field-goal line; fourth-and-5 or more, a field goal when the kick is 52 yards or less, otherwise a punt. [Week 5 report](../Week_05/output.md)

**What the club, not Stone, decides:**

- **Medical diagnosis.** Team medical staff; you have no part.
- **Medical clearance and return to practice and play.** Team medical staff; you have no part.
- **The injury report and game-status designations.** Team medical staff and team communications staff; you supply information.
- **Trades, waivers, claims, releases, the reserve lists and the practice squad.** David Caldwell; you recommend.

**If facts change:** The staff issues a newly dated briefing, and this one is kept as what you knew when you answered.
