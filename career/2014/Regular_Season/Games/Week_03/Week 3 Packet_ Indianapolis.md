# Staff meeting brief | proposed September 15

## Jacksonville self-scout

Washington did not expose a broad protection failure. Jacksonville ran for 171 yards and allowed one sack in 33 dropbacks. Counter Right went 7 for 41 with a touchdown, Outside Zone Left 7 for 43 with a touchdown, Power Right 5 for 31, Trey Stick was 4 of 4 for 37 and Bunch Mesh 3 of 3 for 28. Those stay.

The passing corrections are more important than adding plays. Cousins threw three interceptions. Dagger with HALF was sent on third-and-1 even though the sheet put that situation in the Power, Counter, Mesh and access folder. Another throw was sent from the Washington 11 without a sheet label. Snag again escaped its low-red-zone folder twice. The first opening call was not sent for the fifth consecutive game. The issue is now call control, not insufficient menu.

The MAX Power Pass produced the 51-yard Adams gain but finished 1 of 4. It remains a shot with a setup, not a volume call. Y-Cross HALF produced the only sack and a zero-yard completion; Y-Cross SOLID was available but never sent. Week 3 should put the protected version in front of Stone earlier.

## Indianapolis, branch evidence

Indianapolis is 1-1. Across Denver and Philadelphia, Andrew Luck is 44 of 79 for 657 yards, four touchdowns and four interceptions, with five sacks. Trent Richardson has 48 carries for 272 yards. T.Y. Hilton has 22 catches for 259 yards and two touchdowns. Reggie Wayne has 158 receiving yards, and Dwayne Allen has 131. Richardson, Hilton, Wayne and Allen have functioned as full-snap offensive pieces; Fleener has been the additional tight-end component.

The defense has produced five sacks. Cory Redding owns three, Cam Johnson two. Vontae Davis comes off a Week 2 game with an interception and four passes defended. The two defensive results are too different to declare a stable weakness: Denver produced 449 yards and 41 points, Philadelphia 175 yards and six points.

Castonzo suffered a minor lower-extremity injury in Week 2, with a projected return September 18. Holmes was removed under an independent head/neck medical hold, with a projected return September 20. Those dates are not clearances. Jonathan Harrison replaced Holmes for 47 snaps.

## Staff thesis

Offensively, make Indianapolis answer 12 personnel, shifts to Empty, Bunch and the Power/Counter/Outside Zone family before chasing a theoretical matchup. Use the quick game and SOLID protection to keep Redding and the edge rush from dictating the day. Do not force throws at Davis merely to involve a receiver.

Defensively, Richardson has earned the run-defense attention, but selling out to stop him gives Luck exactly what he wants with Hilton. Start with sound Under fits and capped coverage. Four-man rush first. The Colts have allowed four of their five sacks over the Cherilus and Thornton protection slots, so Crennel should test that side before adding a fifth rusher.

Special teams remains an immediate correction. Jacksonville has conceded 261 combined return yards in two games. Herron produced 120 kickoff-return yards against Philadelphia.
