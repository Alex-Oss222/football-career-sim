Stone's [Week 8 Game Plan](Game_Plan.md) kept the calling arrangement: Stone calling the offense and the defense, Romeo Crennel preparing the defense and sending the call in and Mike Westhoff calling the kicking game, with Paul Posluszny declaring the front while Daryl Smith is out. ## 2. The game

Miami won 29-23 in overtime at EverBank Field. It started overtime at its 5, went 95 yards in 12 plays, and Ryan Tannehill's 13-yard touchdown pass to Lamar Miller on third-and-1 ended the game under the season's overtime rule before Jacksonville had the ball. Regulation finished 23-23 after Josh Scobee's third field goal, from 26 yards with 1:24 left, and Caleb Sturgis's miss from 50 with 1:02 left, his second miss of the day. Jacksonville scored both of its touchdowns in the first half, Montell Owens's 6-yard run and Kirk Cousins's 1-yard run after Davante Adams's 59-yard catch. Twice it went for it on fourth-and-2 on its own side of midfield under Stone's standing rule, at its 38 in the second quarter and its 44 on the first snap of the fourth, and C.J. Anderson was stopped both times; Miami turned the second stop into the field goal that tied the game 20-20.

Cousins found Adam Thielen for 6, Anderson ran 4 for a first down, and Thielen's 53-yard catch reached the Miami 28. Two snaps later Brent Grimes intercepted Cousins's throw for Owens and returned it 28 yards to the Jacksonville 48. Grimes was hurt on the series and did not return. Miller ran for 11, 15 and 4 yards to the 18, Telvin Smith dropped him for a loss of 6 on second-and-6, and Sturgis kicked a 39-yard field goal at 9:53, 3-0.

Jordan Tripp forced a fumble on Hawkins's 24-yard kickoff return, Hawkins recovered it, and Jacksonville began at its 31. Travis Kelce's holding foul left second-and-20, Anderson ran 11, and on third-and-9 Cousins found Adams for 14, but Cortland Finnegan forced a fumble and Olivier Vernon recovered it for Miami at the Jacksonville 47. Kelce was hurt on that drive and stayed in the game. Around Andre Branch's offside and face-mask fouls, Brian Hartline caught a 16-yard pass and Miller a 9-yard pass, and Miami had first-and-goal at the 9. Telvin Smith sacked Tannehill for a loss of 2, Charles Clay's unnecessary-roughness foul after Miller's 5-yard run left third-and-21 at the 21, Miller's 15-yard catch made it fourth-and-6 at the 6, and Sturgis missed from 24 at 5:04.

### The calls and decisions that shaped the game

Stone's Game Plan kept fourth downs, two-point tries and kickoffs on his standing rules. The fourth-down rule reads only the distance and the spot: fourth-and-2 or less is a go with offensive coordinator Mike Tice's first call on that down's menu; fourth-and-3 or 4 is a go on the opponent's side of midfield; otherwise it is a field goal when the kick is 52 yards or less, from the opponent's 34 in, and a punt beyond it. It decided all ten of Jacksonville's fourth downs: five punts, three field goals and two tries for the first down, neither converted.

At fourth-and-2 at the Jacksonville 38 with 9:14 left in the second quarter and Miami ahead 10-7, the rule's first line applied, and the call it names for the distance was 12 Ace, Power Right. Delmas stopped Anderson after 1 yard, and Miami started at the Jacksonville 39; Telvin Smith's interception on its second snap ended that possession.

The three field goals came from inside the rule's line: fourth-and-7 at the Miami 12 in the second quarter; fourth-and-12 at the 12 in the third, after the clipping foul had taken first-and-goal at the 6 back to the 21; and fourth-and-8 at the 8 with 1:24 left, after Misi's sack on third-and-goal at the 2. Scobee made all three, from 30, 30 and 26 yards.

On the first snap of the fourth quarter, ahead 20-17, Jacksonville faced fourth-and-2 at its 44. The same line of the rule applied, with the same call, and Vernon stopped Anderson for a loss of 1. Miami started at the Jacksonville 43, and five plays later Sturgis's 24-yard field goal tied the game.

All five punts came from Jacksonville's side of midfield. At fourth-and-4 at its 26 with 11:03 left and the score 20-20, the rule's second line covers only the opponent's side, so Anger punted; Landry muffed the punt out of bounds, and Miami kept the ball and drove for its go-ahead field goal. At fourth-and-11 at its 39 with 0:20 left in a tied game, the rule called for the punt again, and after the touchback the game went to overtime.

Miami kicked at fourth-and-goal at the 1 with 6:18 left and the score 20-20, and the 19-yard field goal put it ahead 23-20. Its last regulation possession began at the Jacksonville 26 after Owens's foul, lost 6 yards on first down and ended in Sturgis's 50-yard miss on fourth-and-16 at the 32, which gave Jacksonville the ball at its 40 with 0:44 left.

Jacksonville kicked after both of its touchdowns, each in the first half. Its two-point rule, a kick except in the fourth quarter when a touchdown leaves it down 2, up 1 or down 5, and its kickoff rule, deep unless it trails late in the fourth quarter by the margins the rule names, sent no two-point try and no onside kick.
