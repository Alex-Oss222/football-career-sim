## 1. Preparation and final game-day decisions

Stone's [Week 8 Game Plan](Game_Plan.md) kept the calling arrangement: Stone calling the offense and the defense, Romeo Crennel preparing the defense and sending the call in and Mike Westhoff calling the kicking game, with Paul Posluszny declaring the front while Daryl Smith is out. It changed two groups on the depth chart. On the line Joel Bitonio returned to left guard, between Eugene Monroe and Corey Linsley, with Cornelius Lucas the first reserve and Andrew Norwell listed last while he is out. At linebacker Posluszny moved first, ahead of Telvin Smith, Sio Moore, Julian Stanford, Christian Jones and Todd Davis, with Daryl Smith listed last. Alan Ball stayed the nickel and Malcolm Butler the outside reserve corner. Fourth downs, two-point tries and kickoffs stayed on Stone's standing rules. After Cleveland's 32 first downs and Jacksonville's one conversion in eight third downs, the plan put the left-guard and edge protection against Cameron Wake and Olivier Vernon first among the practice priorities, then Wide Zone with its Boot and Counter, third-down separation and protection, Aaron Donald's rush with four connected lanes, the A-gap mask with simple coverage checks, the fourth-down and endgame operation and the return and coverage substitutions; the Flea Flicker came off the week's practice menu.

Monday, October 20, was the walkthrough under Stone's standing practice schedule, and Tuesday the players' day off. Wednesday, Thursday and Friday were practices in helmets, with no padded practice in the week, and Saturday, October 25, the walkthrough. No one was injured during the week, no new restriction was communicated and the club made no roster move.

Davante Adams and Alterraun Verner were reassessed on Monday, October 20, the date their instructions named: Adams cleared on his projected return date and Verner's participation limit ended, and both took every session in full ([medical history](../../../Team_Operations/Medical/medical_history.md#october-20-to-25-adams-verner-talib-bitonio-and-bouye-back-norwell-and-daryl-smith-out)). Aqib Talib's independent medical hold was unchanged at his October 20 reassessment and was released on Wednesday, October 22, its projected date, the day Bitonio and A.J. Bouye cleared on theirs. The three missed the Monday walkthrough and took the Wednesday, Thursday and Friday practices and the Saturday walkthrough in full. Norwell and Daryl Smith were reassessed on Thursday, October 23, with no change: Norwell's hold runs to a projected return of November 2, and Smith is out with a projected return of October 31. Neither took part in any session, and the league's report listed both as non-participants Wednesday through Friday and out for the game. As Stone's plan provided, Posluszny took the front declaration with Telvin Smith and Sio Moore beside him, Talib returned outside opposite Verner with the independent clearance, and Ball kept the nickel. Dwight Lowery's designation for return allowed practice from October 20, but his medical instruction has not cleared him, so he did not practice.

**Plan in force:** On offense, Kelce and Lewis moving inside the same 12-personnel eleven: Wide Zone and its Boot from the Wing look, Counter and Power when Miami's backside chases, the quick game and the back screen against Wake and Vernon, and third down won with protection and spacing. On defense, Donald and Marks inside with four connected rush lanes, the A-gap threats shown with a four-man rush, and Mike Wallace's deep release first in the coverage count, Brandon Gibson's intermediate routes next and Charles Clay handed between the linebackers and safeties ([Game Plan](Game_Plan.md)).  
**Game-day availability:** Norwell and Daryl Smith out under their medical instructions. Stone's standing inactive rule sat the deepest healthy player of the five largest healthy groups: Jordan Poyer, Charles Leno Jr., Kasim Edebali, Hakeem Nicks and Todd Davis.
