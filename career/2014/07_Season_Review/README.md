# Season review and 2015 (after Jacksonville’s final game)

**NOT_STARTED.** [The handoff manifest](season_handoff.json) is the single checklist and source map. Do not create a second transition packet. After Jacksonville's final actual game, close team reviews and player/staff exit interviews, then stage the next year. League postseason, awards and statistics may still need later closure; keep those gates visible without freezing permitted team offseason work.

Use the [exit-interview template](../../../foundation/templates/season_review/exit_interview.md) for actual player and staff meetings. Their completed records belong in `Exit_Interviews/Players/[player].md` and `Exit_Interviews/Coaches/[coach].md`. The [season-review template](../../../foundation/templates/season_review/season_review.md) produces `season_review.md`, the team's football review and actual ownership decisions. Create these records when the reviews happen. The separate final player assessments belong in `Player_Assessments/[player].md`, using final mode of the [2014-onward player sheet](../../../foundation/templates/player_sheet_2014_onward_template.md); they link the interviews and preserved opening cards rather than copying their accounts.

## What transfers

| Record | Treatment at the year boundary |
|---|---|
| Player contracts | Preserve every remaining salary, bonus allocation, guarantee, option, tender status and expiry. Reconcile actual service/escalator triggers. No automatic extension or release |
| Player cap | Preserve dead money and future obligations; verify the new league cap, carryover election, adjustments, Top-51/full-roster transition and actual cash. Unknown room remains unknown |
| Organization finances | Carry Stone/coordinator/position-coach terms, paid and unpaid compensation, remaining guarantees and verified offsets; retain pending renewal decisions |
| Roster, roles and depth | Carry controlled players and supported assignments, distinguish unsigned rights, expired deals and pending choices. Reconcile on actual effective dates; opening working depth is not a legal game roster |
| Medical and availability | Preserve holds, restrictions, projections and the clearance authority. A January date or new folder never clears a player |
| Draft and league ownership | Carry future traded/conditional picks and prior ordinal swaps. The k-th actual Jacksonville selection pairs with the k-th historical Jacksonville pick, not the same round; follow existing collision rules |
| Player and coach development | Carry demonstrated strengths, unresolved corrections, delivered/undelivered feedback and next legal observation. Do not reset returning players to rookie status |
| Stats, awards and history | Keep prior receipts, standings, awards and season summaries under their original season. New-season counters start with no receipts; career history links closed seasons rather than duplicating totals |
| Calendar and rules | Source the next historical calendar and dated changes. Actual games/dates/byes stay historical; players, outcomes and playoff qualification remain branch-dependent |

## Close and stage

1. Run `python scripts/season_handoff.py prepare 2014`. Repeating it preserves entered gates and decisions while refreshing the source inventory; adding or removing an owner reopens that inventory review. Every future active season uses the same command with its year.
2. Complete each team gate in the existing review/event owners. Record the evidence path and SHA-256 in the manifest only after reviewing it. Set each completed gate to `COMPLETE`; leave unfinished league-statistics/award gates open until their real closure. Add unresolved user decisions to `pending_decisions`.
3. Review each `reference_inventory` group: individual development, film and undelivered feedback, medical records, coach development, and draft ownership. The listed files remain their owners. Set a group to `COMPLETE` only with hashed `evidence` covering every listed source; if a group has no files, cite the actual closeout finding that establishes there is no outstanding work. Do not create empty plans or duplicate incident histories to satisfy a list. At team closeout, freeze every `carry_forward` source in `source_sha256` and set `status` to `TEAM_CLOSED`. Source changes require renewed review. Complete and review the authored final assessments before staging. `check 2014` checks the manifest's current structure and reviewed evidence; `stage` additionally requires the team and carry-forward reviews to be complete.
4. Verify the historical 2015 calendar, then run `python scripts/season_handoff.py stage 2014`. This writes eight provisional baselines: roster, staff, working depth, contracts, contract status, cap worksheet, development roster profiles and staff decisions. It rebases links and removes copied event-owner comments, preserves prior files and refuses conflicting destination content. Individual teaching plans, film obligations, medical incidents, coach development and draft ownership remain source references awaiting explicit reconciliation. No previous game receipts, statistics, awards, battle cards or phase reports are copied. Existing successor receipts block staging instead of being silently treated as reset.
5. Staging queues the returning players' new opening assessments in `career/2015/opening_handoff.json`; it does **not** write them. Author each opening from the reviewed 2014 final, preserving earlier regular-season and playoff rows from the previous card. Record its reviewed hash and set its queue status to `COMPLETE`. A new arrival needs an entry assessment and hashed entry evidence; a queued player no longer controlled needs `NOT_CARRIED` and the actual departure evidence. Prior opening and final cards stay in 2014.
6. Reconcile the seven opening areas in that same manifest: roster/roles, contracts/cap, medical, staff/authority, draft assets, development/film, and calendar/era. Each `COMPLETE` gate needs current evidence hashes, including its actual successor current-state sources. Outstanding medical incidents remain linked at their original owners; prepare the new current-participation view from their communicated instructions. Do not clear a player, renew a contract or decide an open job just to finish the checklist. Set `opening_review.status` to `COMPLETE` only after these reviews.
7. Run `python scripts/season_handoff.py check-opening 2015`. It rejects missing or unfinished cards, changed reviewed sources, missing inherited statistics and unresolved opening reviews. Then `review-opening 2015` runs the same checks and stores a compact acceptance digest in the existing opening manifest and prior handoff. It changes neither the live map nor the clock. The accepted manifest is a frozen review snapshot; later ordinary roster, medical and statistics updates remain possible in their current owners.
8. Only the subsequent authorized administrative event activates 2015, reconciling both state documents, the live repository map and financial owners together. Record that event at its real owner and regenerate `Record.md` and age/finance views. Repository validation requires the accepted opening review when an active season has this handoff. Codex performs this bookkeeping; Stone retains consequential football and contract choices. Prepare the newly active year's own handoff afterward. The same route applies to 2015 onward; source each historical calendar without opening future playbooks or inventing dates.

Opening readiness rereads the current 2014 handoff, so reopening a team gate or changing a reviewed source cannot be hidden by the staged copy. Later league-history gate completion is allowed. For an actual choice that prevents opening, use `{"id":"opening-contract-choice","status":"OPEN","blocks_opening":true}` in `pending_decisions`; record its resolution and change the status to `RESOLVED` before review. Ordinary carried questions, future work and `requires_user` alone remain nonblocking.

## Simulation after transfer

Passing a handoff check does not release games. Each season needs its own registered kernel, era rules, fixtures, legal rosters, financial control and closure acceptance. The existing six game-release gates remain in force. Stats and receipts are always season-qualified, and a missing successor input never falls back to 2013. The user's choice to start the next season cannot silently select draft targets, renew contracts, change roles or clear injuries.
