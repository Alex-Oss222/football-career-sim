## Current synthesis: build from the player he has become

Cousins enters 2014 as a quarterback who has run this offense through a full regular season and two playoff games. Washington supplied part of his entry background; it is no longer a sufficient description of him. His starting experience, relationships with these receivers and linemen, situational responsibilities and responses to coaching belong in the assessment. They are not erased because the old game engine cannot establish talent from its results.

**Working staff inference, open to revision:** the clearest supported direction is an organized rhythm passer with an established command of taught operation and some situational work, developing more independent answers when the first picture changes. Huddle, cadence, protection-point communication, taught timing and two-minute operation have repeated practice support. His season gives a much larger set of situations to examine, including sustained drives, difficult stretches and occasions where the offense needed another answer. That does not settle whether he will become primarily a timing distributor, a more aggressive anticipator, an adaptable multi-answer operator, or some combination. We do not choose the destination now.

The next work should both use his strengths and let new ones appear. Do not make him repeat a beginner course, rebuild working mechanics or become a conservative game manager to reduce interceptions. An available aggressive throw can be the correct answer. Equally, a successful throw cannot establish a good read without the actual assignment and picture. Record decision, execution and outcome separately.

**What the full season changes:** he has experience of preparation, starting responsibility, changing teammates/support, opponent adjustments, two-minute situations and playoff preparation. It justifies asking richer questions and providing appropriate opportunities to demonstrate command. It does not automatically raise an ability tier. Reliable dated practice/communication observations can refine capability; defective outcome totals and generic engine explanations cannot.

**His already recorded perspective:** he learns well from a concise phrase with two or three relevant right/wrong examples. He wanted coaching on the bad throw rather than simply removing throws from his menu, and a chance to raise call-confidence questions before the final sheet. These are statements from his exit interview, not a new conversation. His Cleveland risk disagreement remains a useful question to examine with him, not a character label.

### What to preserve, explore and leave undecided

| Current thread | Working interpretation | Opportunity to learn more |

| Situational command | Repeated two-minute practice support and game experience justify a meaningful command assignment | Explore clock, field position and risk with more than one legal answer; review Stone's call and supporting jobs too |
| Independent problem-solving | Some immediate and later corrections are documented; broad adaptability is not established | Invite his own solution before the coach supplies a cue, then observe it in a suitable taught problem |
| Mechanics and physical response | No complete cause review supports a general mechanical overhaul | Preserve functioning motion; change a specific component only when actual evidence identifies it |
| Offensive identity | His preferred way of seeing/solving a problem may become clearer during spring and camp | Let him select relevant clips and describe what he notices, when contact is permitted. Record disagreement and revised coaching views honestly |

These are concurrent strands, not milestones he must pass in sequence. Explore one appropriate new possibility while maintaining useful existing work; an unresolved question elsewhere does not lock the entire offense. Material install or role decisions still belong to Stone and the actual event record.

## Teaching packets

Start with a balanced season conversation when permitted: work he trusts, an adaptation he recognizes, something he would solve differently and a question he has for the staff. Invite examples; do not invent his choices or require a quiz to receive promised film. Include ordinary correct operation and useful aggressive answers, not just highlights and errors. If no media exists, provide an honestly labeled written review and source locators.

Bates builds a short player-facing explanation with two or three genuinely relevant examples where they exist. Cousins explicitly said that paired examples work better for him than a lone correction phrase. Show the question rep, a documented correct answer, then change the presentation in the later lawful test. If no matching correct rep exists, label a diagram or teaching demonstration as such; do not rename a completion “correct” solely because it gained yards.

## Staff accountability and stop rules

- Stone's review includes Week 12 call balance, Week 13 calls relative to his stated re-anchor, the Divisional punt and menu, and Boot Flood removal. Those are staff questions, not automatic deductions from Cousins.
- Classify identification, progression, late decision, unseen defender, placement, pressure/mechanics, receiver error, deflection and justified risk using the 2013 plan. Multiple contributions can coexist. “Undetermined” is a valid outcome when the assignment or film is missing.
- Preserve functioning mechanics. If decision and feet separate only when the picture changes, test processing and instruction before imposing a cosmetic throwing rebuild.
- Reduce only the failing layer, teach it, retest it, then reconsider expansion. Read-ahead access to the full active book remains unrestricted; installed/evaluated work remains the taught subset.
- Participation is voluntary where the governing phase says so. Medical authority controls physical and cognitive work. No contract, draft, role or future signing promise is embedded in this plan.
