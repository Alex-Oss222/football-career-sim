# Living player profiles: 2014 entry

**Current-use note:** this is the frozen 61-player 2013 exit-review baseline, not the live roster or a progression eligibility list. The [current roster](../roster/roster.md) has 55 controlled players as of March 28, 2014; [progression_roster.json](progression_roster.json) derives the current development cohort from that roster and uses [2013_exit_player_index.json](2013_exit_player_index.json) only to identify Jacksonville continuity. Preserve departed-player feedback obligations as history; add actual newcomer work here without treating missing observations as failed performance.

[Development method](README.md) · [Cousins in detail](kirk_cousins.md) · [Film obligations](../film/player_queue.md) · [Annual calendar](../../calendar.md)

**Evidence cutoff:** completed branch 2013 season and exit interviews; current control/medical status must be checked against February 2, 2014 Entry 79 and later actual events. These are staff syntheses and proposed opportunities, not new player statements or 2014 practice results. The linked interviews identify their underlying practice/game sources and distinguish documented facts from open questions.

**All 61 players are represented:** 52 active, the 8 from the 2013 practice squad (six now on 2014 reserve/future contracts; King and Ta'ufo'ou left, Entry 85) and retired Meester for archival obligations. This inventory neither re-signs an expiring player nor guarantees a place in camp. Allen's departure/retirement and every later transaction change teaching eligibility; promises already made remain tracked.

A profile describes the player now, not the type he must become. Carry useful strengths forward; allow more than one plausible direction. Thin evidence means the staff needs to observe, not that the player is poor. A veteran needs opportunities to learn, and a reserve needs an evaluation that does not depend on getting a box-score statistic. Full-season experience matters even where the old engine's numbers cannot establish ability.

**Using the last column:** these are invitations and planned evaluation opportunities, not claims that a player answered or that a role was approved. Ask during permitted contact, make voluntary participation genuinely optional and respect a player's choice not to discuss or submit film. Where an interview promised a take-home cutup with no required quiz, that promise stands. A technical question in lawful teaching is not a condition for receiving the promised material.

Each phase output records what actually happened. At handoff, revise only the relevant profile with date/source, player perspective if expressed, observed behavior, support/opposition, confidence, alternative explanation and next opportunity. Record no change when that is the honest conclusion. Keep previous assessments recoverable in history. There are no automatic stage promotions, permanent archetypes or assigned growth arcs.

## Handoffs that discover rather than prescribe

| Context | What can become clearer | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Film / permitted conversation | How the player reads his season, what he recognizes, successful adaptations, useful teaching method and unanswered staff decisions | Self-report and diagrams cannot certify physical execution; no fictional conversation |
| Phase One | Current permitted physical preparation and the performance/medical plan | No football-skill upgrade from participation or fitness alone |
| Phase Two | Technique, communication and different solutions within legal individual/group work | Unopposed work does not prove answers against live opposition |
| OTAs / mandatory minicamp | Changed-picture decisions, shared assignments, independent correction and emerging preferences within non-contact football | Physical competition and game transfer remain separate questions |
| Camp / preseason | Actual contact, pace, recovery, fatigue context and different supporting personnel; discover strengths not visible in the spring | Samples differ; one play or role is not a whole-player identity |
| Season / actual bye / season close | Which strengths endure, what the player has learned to adapt, where the staff changed its view and what still has not been tested | No automatic growth, failure narrative or talent grade from team record |

New players get this same method after the branch actually acquires or invites them. They do not inherit the departed player's profile. Update the film queue, owner and lawful next opportunity together; keep medical details and transactions in their authoritative records.
