## Source notes

The [NFL calendar](../../library/2014_nfl_calendar.md#sources-and-publication-dates) contains the dated sources and remaining uncertainties. Spring phase spans follow the published April 21 start and the CBA's two-, three- and four-week structure. Jacksonville's program-day allocations and staff appointments come from the linked club plans.

The historical July 29 rest day and July 31 practice conflict with the closed [branch camp report](04_Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/training_report.md), which records a July 29 practice and July 31 off. This calendar shows the verified historical schedule. The recorded observations have not been moved, deleted or counted as an additional completed practice; reconciliation belongs in the camp record. July 30 first full pads and August 2 scrimmage were both published on July 24. Atlanta's 6 p.m. kickoff was undetermined in April and is conservatively available from August 25. Cap-carryover notice timing remains unresolved in the financial sourcebook; December 26 above is the separate waiver deadline.
