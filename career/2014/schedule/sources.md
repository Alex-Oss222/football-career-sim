# 2014 opponent rotation: evidence and verification

**Policy update, Entry 101:** historical fixtures and dates now control all seasons, including same-place pairings. Prior branch-recomputation discussion below is superseded. Source facts remain useful; no historical results become branch results.

**Research checkpoint:** February 2, 2014 branch; checked September 29, 2026. Scope: opponent construction, designated home/away assignment and already announced London dates. No real 2013 record, playoff seed, strength-of-schedule number, 2014 result, player movement or coaching change enters the build.

## Two separate passes

**Pass one:** Kevin Kelley, FBSchedules, [2014 NFL Opponents Announced](https://fbschedules.com/2014-nfl-opponents-announced/), December 30, 2013, supplies the published home/away lists. Extract only division rotation pairs, home assignment within those pairs, and the home-division direction of the two same-place games. Discard the actual clubs filling the standings-dependent slots.

**Pass two:** separately re-searched and checked Nate Bouda, NFL Trade Rumors, [2014 Opponents For Each NFL Team](https://nfltraderumors.co/2014-opponents-nfl-team/), January 3, 2014. All eight four-by-four rotation grids and all eight directed same-place division pairings agree with pass one. These are separate publications of the NFL opponent list, not independent determinations of the NFL's formula. No discrepancy in the imported matchups was found.

**Primary confirmation:** the Raiders/NFL release [2014 Opponents Determined](https://www.raiders.com/news/2014-opponents-determined-12314752), December 30, 2013, confirms six divisional, four conference-rotation, four interconference-rotation and two same-place games, with dates released later. Its Oakland list checks the AFC East/AFC West and AFC West/NFC West home assignments for Oakland. Carolina's [2014 opponents determined](https://www.panthers.com/news/2014-opponents-determined-12329818), December 31, 2013, independently checks its two rotation grids and the NFC South-to-West home / East-away direction. The complete-grid second pass is the NFL Trade Rumors list, not an assertion that those two club releases cover all 32 clubs.

## Stored transformation

[rotation_2014.json](rotation_2014.json) stores eight grids. For each club in the first division, two members of the other division visit; the remaining two host it. Reciprocal entries are generated, never independently typed. The fixed rotations cover 128 games; division home-and-away supplies 96. The remaining 32 are generated by equal **branch** division place along these directions:

| Conference | Designated home division → away division |
|---|---|
| AFC | East → North; North → West; West → South; South → East |
| NFC | East → South; South → West; West → North; North → East |

The generator recomputes all eight division orders from the 256 closed [2013 branch receipts](../../2013/stats/game_receipts/README.md) through `runtime.standings.Season`. It refuses incomplete club/week coverage or a division tie that would otherwise fall back to alphabetical order. Winning records are used only for the NFL scheduling rule, never as talent ratings. Division place includes the established tiebreak procedures; it is not draft order or playoff elimination order.

Jacksonville is second in the AFC South: Buffalo visits and Jacksonville visits San Diego. The full league transformation also changes other teams' opponents. The generated matrix cannot be reproduced correctly by replacing Miami with Buffalo in Jacksonville's historical October slot alone.

## London dates already public

The Raiders/NFL [2014 International Series Dates Confirmed](https://www.raiders.com/news/2014-international-series-dates-confirmed-11979383), November 28, 2013, and a separately checked [Wembley Stadium notice](https://www.wembleystadium.com/news/2013/nov/28/2014-international-series-dates-confirmed), November 28, 2013, confirm:

| Week | Date | Visiting club | Designated home club |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 4 | September 28 | Miami | Oakland |
| 8 | October 26 | Detroit | Atlanta |
| 10 | November 9 | Dallas | Jacksonville |

All three are at Wembley. These pairings survive the branch rotation; their dates are fixed before the April release. The Raiders notice defers game times while Wembley displays times on the dated page. Because the page may have been updated, this build retains only the independently confirmed date/week/venue facts and leaves kickoff confirmation to the schedule release. Designated home does not mean a normal home-field advantage at a neutral venue.

## Check and remaining boundary

`render_2014_opponents.py --check` verifies that both generated views match the input digest and branch receipt digest. Structural checks cover 256 distinct fixtures, 16 games/eight designated home/eight away per club, 6/4/4/2 components, reciprocal divisional games and equal branch division place. Tests also exercise changed standings, invalid/duplicate pairings and incomplete source coverage.

Exact fixture placement, byes, rest and shared-stadium conflicts remain work for the dated [release procedure](release_plan.md). The existing [annual-calendar verification](../../../library/2014_full_calendar_verification.md) remains the authority for other league dates. No future fixture or outcome is inferred from this opponent build.

## April 23, 2014 fixture release (prepared September 30, 2026)

[fixtures.json](fixtures.json) and its [readable view](fixtures.md) hold the 256-game regular-season release. They were prepared at the March clock and became usable when the career clock passed **April 23, 2014, 8 p.m. ET** ([ledger](../ledger.md)). The data holds schedule facts only: week, date, weekday, Eastern kickoff, clubs, site, stadium and a season-qualified `game_id` equal to the runtime event id. No score, result, player, injury or later performance fact is stored.

**Pass one (extraction).** nflverse `games.csv` ([nfldata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nflverse/nfldata/master/data/games.csv), retrieved September 30, 2026, SHA-256 `b83b61ed…0708a`, recorded in full in the data). Only the columns week, gameday, weekday, gametime, away_team, home_team, location and stadium were read, as for 2013. nflverse reflects the season as played, so its values were checked against release-era sources before use.

**Pass two (separate comparison).** All 256 rows were compared field by field with two independently compiled feeds: the NFL.com schedule feed preserved in the nflgame project's `schedule.json` ([BurntSushi/nflgame](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BurntSushi/nflgame/master/nflgame/schedule.json)) for week, date, clubs and kickoff time, and nflscrapR's NFL game-centre list ([reg_games_2014.csv](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ryurko/nflscrapR-data/master/games_data/regular_season/reg_games_2014.csv)) for week, clubs and game-centre id. The id encodes the date. All three agree on the same 256 week/away/home fixtures and on every game-centre id. Only eight rows differ on date or time, each resolved below. Jacksonville's 16 games and bye were also checked row by row against the [Jaguars' April 23 release](https://www.jaguars.com/news/2014-jaguars-schedule-released-12908209) as transcribed in the [regular-season view](../regular_season/README.md): every date, opponent, home/away and kickoff agrees. Direct web access to Wikipedia, Pro-Football-Reference and club sites was blocked for this pass, so the spot checks below came from search-indexed contemporaneous reports.
