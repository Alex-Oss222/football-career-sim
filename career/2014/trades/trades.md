## Jacksonville / Arizona: No. 38 (March 20, 2014)

| Club | Receives |
|---|---|
| Jacksonville | Arizona's original 2014 second-round pick, No. 38 |
| Arizona | G Uche Nwaneri; Jacksonville's original 2015 first-round, 2015 fourth-round and 2016 fifth-round picks |

- Negotiation: Jacksonville offered the three picks, Alualu and Nwaneri on March 18; Arizona countered without Alualu on March 19, and Caldwell accepted ([negotiation record](package_i_negotiation_2014-03-20.md)).
- Closing: Nwaneri passed his physical; branch medical disclosure showed no communicated restriction. Processed March 20, before his March 25 roster bonus, which passes to Arizona.
- Accounting (Jacksonville): about $5,894,500 of 2014 charge removed; $2,189,000 of bonus proration (the 2014 and 2015 allocations) accelerates into 2014 dead money; his 2015 working charge ($5,894,500) is removed.
- Picks: [ownership register](../draft/pick_ownership.json) and [draft order](../draft/draft_order.md) updated. Jacksonville holds no 2015 pick before round 3.
- Draft plan: No. 38 is Davante Adams; No. 26 becomes Joel Bitonio, then Kyle Van Noy. Availability at each pick is decided at the draft by the rails rule.
- Ledger: [2013 ledger, Nwaneri traded for No. 38](../../2013/ledger.md#entry-99-cain-re-signed-nwaneri-traded-to-arizona-for-no-38-package-i).

## Recording a completed trade

Write the effective date and time, clubs and approval; what each club sends; the linked offer and final terms; physicals, branch medical disclosure and conditions satisfied; league processing; sourced contract, cash and cap treatment with explicit unknowns; any future conditional obligation and its review date; and the resulting roster, control and pick changes. Then link the ledger entry and every affected current-state and accounting view.

A contemplated trade, an agreement awaiting conditions, a retirement, a waiver or a release gets no row here; each belongs to its own event record. When a condition on a completed trade matures, append its dated outcome without erasing the original terms.
