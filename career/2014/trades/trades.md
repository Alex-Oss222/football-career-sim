## Completion record, when a trade happens

Write the actual effective date/time, clubs and approval; players/picks each sends; linked offer and final accepted terms; physicals, branch medical disclosure and conditions satisfied; league processing; sourced contract/cash/cap treatment and explicit unknowns; any future conditional obligation and review date; and the resulting roster/control and pick changes.

Then link the atomic ledger entry and every affected current-state/accounting view. No completed row is added for a contemplated package, successful negotiation awaiting conditions, retirement, waiver or release. Those events belong to their proper event owner. Once a condition on an already completed trade matures, append its dated outcome without erasing the original instrument.
