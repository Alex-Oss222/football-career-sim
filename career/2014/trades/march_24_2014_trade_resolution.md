## PACKAGE G: RUSSELL ALLEN

**Status: AGREED IN PRINCIPLE - NOT COMPLETED**

### Jacksonville would send
- LB Russell Allen

### Arizona would send
- Arizona's unconditional 2015 fourth-round pick

### Closing conditions
The clubs have agreed to the compensation. No further trade negotiation is required if the conditions are satisfied.

The transaction may close only if:

1. LB Paul Posluszny receives **actual medical clearance**.
2. The clearance occurs no later than **April 21, 2014**.
3. Russell Allen passes Arizona's acquiring-club physical.
4. Normal league trade processing is completed.

### Important medical rule
The projected April 5 return date does **not** constitute clearance.

Only an actual medical clearance recorded in the branch satisfies the condition.

### Current status
Until the condition is satisfied:

- Russell Allen remains a Jacksonville-controlled player.
- Allen remains on Jacksonville's roster and depth chart.
- Jacksonville continues carrying Allen's contract and cap charge.
- Arizona continues owning its 2015 fourth-round pick.
- Jacksonville may not spend or count Arizona's fourth as an acquired asset yet.

### Automatic closing rule
If Posluszny receives actual clearance on or before April 21 and Allen passes Arizona's physical:

**Russell Allen
for
Arizona 2015 fourth**

closes without another club-compensation negotiation.

If Posluszny is not actually cleared by April 21, the agreement expires and does not automatically execute.
