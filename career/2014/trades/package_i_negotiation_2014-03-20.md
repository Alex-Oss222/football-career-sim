# Package I: Arizona negotiation, March 2014

Recorded September 29, 2026 (ledger Entry 99). Branch dates: March 18 to 20, 2014. Method: judgment-based negotiation under the March 2014 replay instruction, which superseded Entry 94's first-pass chart draw. Arizona's answers are authored simulation decisions made on the same criteria any club would use; they are not recovered historical conversations.

## Offer (March 18, Stone's instruction, Entry 98)

Jacksonville's original 2015 first, 2015 fourth and 2016 fifth, plus DL Tyson Alualu and G Uche Nwaneri, for Arizona's original 2014 second-round pick, No. 38, to select Davante Adams. Deadline: before Nwaneri's $1,000,000 roster bonus on March 25; otherwise he is released.

## Arizona's evaluation

- **Picks.** On the Jimmy Johnson chart, with each future pick counted one round later per year out, the three picks are worth 462 points against No. 38's 520 (a ratio of 0.89). On picks alone the offer is short, which is why the first pass was declined.
- **Nwaneri.** Arizona finished 6-9-1 in the branch. Daryn Colledge, a starting guard, was released on March 11 (a real move, designated post-June 1), leaving a starting guard spot open. Nwaneri, 29, started at left guard for a 10-6 playoff team in 2013 and is under contract through 2015 at about $4.8M a year in cash. For Arizona he fills a real need without spending a pick or bidding in free agency.
- **Alualu.** Arizona's interior line is set (Calais Campbell, Darnell Dockett, Dan Williams), and Alualu's $2.7M of 2014 cash buys depth it does not need.

## Negotiation record

1. **March 18.** Caldwell presents the full package. Nwaneri passes Arizona's review of his contract and branch medical disclosure (no communicated restriction). A physical is required before processing.
2. **March 19.** Arizona counters: the three picks and Nwaneri for No. 38, without Alualu. It values Nwaneri as a starter at his salary and will not take Alualu's.
3. **March 19.** Caldwell accepts the counter. It matches Stone's instruction to trade Nwaneri with the picks. Alualu stays in Jacksonville and returns to package E (Marks is re-signed, so its condition is met).
4. **March 20.** Nwaneri passes his physical and the trade is processed, before his March 25 roster bonus.

## Jacksonville accounting

- **Nwaneri, pre-June 1 trade:** his 2014 charge of about $5,894,500 (base $3,775,000, $1,000,000 roster bonus, $25,000 workout, $1,094,500 proration) comes off. The $1,094,500 allocations for 2014 and 2015 accelerate into 2014 as $2,189,000 of dead money. The March 25 roster bonus becomes Arizona's.
- **2014 effect:** the scheduled charge falls by $3,705,500. Because Nwaneri leaves the Top 51, one displaced minimum returns to the count, so the working difference improves by $2,850,500.
- **2015 effect:** his $5,894,500 working charge comes off.
- **Draft capital:** Jacksonville holds 13, 26, 38, 90, 122, Detroit's fifth, its own fifth, sixth and seventh. It holds no 2015 pick before round 3 (Washington owns its 2015 second).
- **Draft plan consequence (the package I amendment):** 38 is Adams, 26 becomes Joel Bitonio (then Kyle Van Noy), 13 stays Donald (then Fuller). Adams's availability at 38 is decided at the draft by the rails rule; nothing promises he is there.
