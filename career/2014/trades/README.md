Branch 2013 volume statistics may explain Stone's original call list but cannot supply a talent grade, fair-market price or guaranteed willingness to trade.

## Run the window

1. Check the [calendar](../calendar.md), actual roster/control, contract clauses, medical information and the package's trigger. Trading opens **March 11 at 4 p.m. ET**. Preparation today is not contact with a buyer.
2. Verify the requested asset by **draft year, round and original club**, its current owner and every prior encumbrance. The audited 2014 register does not certify all 2015/2016 assets; check the particular future pick before agreeing. A conditional pick is one contingent obligation, not two assets.
3. Record dated actual contact and any counter in the offer log. Keep offered, agreed-in-principle, conditional and processed distinct. Terms outside Stone's authorized alternatives need a new choice, not an invented concession.
4. Close only after applicable medical disclosure, physicals, contract review, Caldwell's approval, counterparty acceptance and league processing. In particular, finish Allen by **April 21** under package G's retained clearance condition.
5. Write the completed trade first. Reconcile the applicable season ledger, controlled roster, depth/role changes if actually decided, contracts/cap, current-state documents, league database corrections and pick ownership in the same event commit. Regenerate the draft order if affected. Unknown cap terms remain explicitly unresolved; never book the memo's rough savings as verified money.
6. Check the next calendar deadline and validate the repository. Private snapshot advancement follows a merged canon-changing event; this planning build does not change it.

Prior completed deals remain in [2013 trades](../../2013/trades/trades.md). Their outstanding assets remain effective, including the corrected Cousins exchange. Do not replay them as 2014 transactions.
