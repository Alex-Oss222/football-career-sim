# Jacksonville Jaguars: 2014 trade targets

This page is the trade plan: what each trade Stone recommended to Caldwell is, what it may accept, and where it stands at March 31, 2014. Stone's intent comes from [section 5 of his February 2 memo and its amendments](../offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#5-2014-trade-targets), which control where this summary is silent. No player or pick changes control through this page. Actual contacts are in the [offer log](trade_offers.md), finished deals in [completed trades](trades.md), and the March 24 outcomes in the user's [trade resolution log](march_24_2014_trade_resolution.md).

## Open trades

### Allen trade with Arizona

- Closes only if Paul Posluszny receives actual medical clearance no later than April 21, 2014, Allen passes Arizona's physical, and league processing completes. No further compensation talks are needed.
- Posluszny's April 5 return projection is not clearance. If clearance prevents an April 21 close, Caldwell brings that specific linebacker-coverage decision to Stone before expiry; it is not waived silently ([timing amendment](../offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-28-2026-amendment-package-g-timing)).
- Until it closes, Allen stays on the roster and depth chart with his cap charge, and Arizona's fourth is neither owned nor counted. If it expires, Allen's April 22 retirement applies while Jacksonville controls him.

### Rackley-or-Brewster trade

- Opens only when Jacksonville has actually drafted two offensive linemen. Joel Bitonio and Corey Linsley are targets, not replacements; neither a target nor an unsigned agreement counts.
- Caldwell then chooses which one player to shop, to the clubs in the original Nwaneri call order (San Francisco, Carolina, New England, Seattle, Chicago, Houston). The ask is a 2015 seventh; the authorized alternatives are a 2016 seventh, or a 2015 seventh conveyed only on the games condition. Trading both needs a separate Stone authorization.
- Games condition: the traded player appears in at least half of the acquiring club's 2014 regular-season games, counted from that club's branch receipts, never snaps, starts or real 2014 participation. The accepted instrument records the exact trigger.
- The player stays a Jaguar and nothing is booked until a buyer accepts. Reassess interior-line coverage from actual acquisitions before offering; no player loses his role through this plan.

## Money, control and priority

No memo savings are booked. Each trade's remaining salary, guarantees, proration, Top-51 displacement and any returning obligation is reconciled in the [contract register](../offseason/contract_status_register.md) and the [cap tracker](../../finances/jaguars_cap.md), with sourced amounts kept apart from estimates. Future picks are not cash or cap room, and no acquired pick is presumed available for another offer. The plan authorizes outbound players for picks only: no incoming player and no further outgoing Jacksonville pick. A return outside the authorized terms is a new decision for Stone and Caldwell. Jeremy Mincey and Brent Grimes are not on this board; the memo handles their expiring rights separately.
