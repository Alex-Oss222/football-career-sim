# Will Rackley to Seattle for a 2015 seventh: May 12, 2014

Recorded September 30, 2026 (2014 ledger Entry 110). Branch dates: May 11 to 12, 2014.

**Stone's instruction** ([memo amendment](../../early_offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-shop-will-rackley-keep-mike-brewster)): shop Will Rackley for an unconditional 2015 seventh-round pick; fall back to a 2016 seventh or a conditional 2015 seventh only if necessary. Brewster stays.

**Method.** The same as the other 2014 trades: Caldwell makes the ask, and each club answers on its own need and the price. There is no private draw, and the same offer to any club in the same position would draw the same answer. The trade became available on May 10, when Jacksonville drafted four offensive linemen.

## What the clubs were buying

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Player | Will Rackley, guard, 24 (born October 11, 1989); 2011 third-round pick |
| Contract | Final year: $1,431,000 base, no guarantee. Unrestricted after 2014 |
| 2013 in the branch | Starting right guard for a 10-6 playoff team. His line calls were consistent; leverage and recovery technique were the open issue in his 2013 review |
| Medical | Limited by a Week 5 upper-extremity injury, minor, with no projected absence |

Jacksonville keeps his final $154,868 of signing-bonus allocation as 2014 dead money whatever happens; a buyer takes only the $1,431,000 base.

## The calls (May 11)

Caldwell called the six clubs on Stone's line-needy list: San Francisco, Carolina, New England, Seattle, Chicago and Houston.

- **San Francisco, Carolina, New England and Houston** passed: each added an interior lineman in the draft that had just ended.
- **Chicago** passed: it re-signed its starting left guard, Matt Slauson, in March.
- **Seattle** had the need. Its 2013 starting left guard, Paul McQuistan, signed with Cleveland on March 24 ([ProFootballTalk](https://profootballtalk.nbcsports.com/2014/03/24/browns-bring-paul-mcquistan-back-for-one-year/); [Dawgs By Nature](https://www.dawgsbynature.com/2014/3/24/5542666/browns-sign-og-paul-mcquistan-former-starter-on-the-seattle-seahawks)), and Seattle drafted no interior lineman. A 24-year-old with a season of starts, on a one-year minimum-range salary, for the last pick in the draft is an easy price. Seattle accepted the unconditional 2015 seventh without a counter, subject to its physical. No fallback was needed.

## The trade

| Jacksonville sends | Seattle sends |
|---|---|
| G Will Rackley | Seattle's own 2015 seventh-round pick, unconditional |

- **Physical:** Rackley passed Seattle's physical on May 12. The Week 5 limitation was disclosed; it carries no projected absence.
- **Processed:** May 12, 2014. Seattle takes his contract from that date.
- **Rails:** a trade is not Rackley's real 2014 move, so he does not follow one; he is a branch player on Seattle's roster.
