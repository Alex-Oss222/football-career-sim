## Chicago 27, Buffalo 23

Jay Cutler threw three touchdown passes on 39 attempts, ten of his completions to Brandon Marshall for 56 yards and a score, and Matt Forte broke a 58-yard run on his way to 93 yards on 15 carries. Buffalo turned the ball over three times, two of them E.J. Manuel interceptions, and Manuel was sacked four times, two by Shea McClellin; C.J. Spiller's 131 yards on 22 carries went unrewarded. Standouts: Forte, McClellin (two sacks, eleven tackles), Spiller. Buffalo lost Jerry Hughes for several weeks after his sack.

## Tampa Bay 26, Carolina 23

Patrick Murray kicked four field goals in four tries and Josh McCown found Vincent Jackson twice for touchdowns, 81 yards on six catches, to outlast a Carolina side that led the ground game through DeAngelo Williams's 97 yards. Greg Hardy sacked McCown twice of Carolina's four, and Lavonte David forced and recovered the fumble that cost Carolina a possession; Cam Newton threw two touchdowns and an interception. Standouts: Jackson, Murray, Hardy. Carolina left with Thomas Davis and Charles Johnson under short instructions.

## Baltimore 27, Cincinnati 26

Bernard Pierce ran 18 times for 133 yards and a touchdown and added 55 receiving, and Justin Forsett's 52-yard run set the tone of a 236-yard rushing day. Andy Dalton threw for 407 yards on 43 attempts, 136 of them to Jermaine Gresham on a 61-yard catch among five, but was sacked four times and intercepted once, and Mike Nugent missed one of five field goals in a one-point game. Standouts: Pierce, Gresham, Dalton. Baltimore lost Elvis Dumervil for the better part of two months.

## Cleveland 36, Pittsburgh 24

Ben Roethlisberger threw four interceptions and Pittsburgh turned it over five times; Cleveland did not turn it over once and Billy Cundiff made all five field goals. Brian Hoyer threw two touchdowns on 27 attempts and Isaiah Crowell scored on a 19-yard catch, with Ben Tate carrying 19 times for 76. Standouts: Cundiff, Crowell, Ryan Shazier (14 tackles and a sack in defeat). Pittsburgh's Sean Spence and Cleveland's Miles Austin left with multi-week injuries.

## Seattle 34, Green Bay 28, overtime

Russell Wilson threw for 398 yards and three touchdowns and Marshawn Lynch ran for 97 and two scores in a game that went to the extra period; Aaron Rodgers was sacked six times, two by Cliff Avril, and threw for 334 with two touchdowns, 129 of the yards to Randall Cobb. Seattle turned it over three times and still won. Standouts: Wilson, Lynch, Avril. Green Bay's Jarrett Boykin and Morgan Burnett were ruled out for more than two weeks.

## Denver 41, Indianapolis 20

Montee Ball carried 21 times for 138 yards and four touchdowns behind Peyton Manning's 289 passing yards on 26 attempts; Denver did not turn the ball over. Andrew Luck threw for 369 yards and two scores, 133 to T.Y. Hilton on eleven catches, but was intercepted twice and Adam Vinatieri missed two of four field goals. Standouts: Ball, Hilton, Julius Thomas (97 yards).

## St. Louis 33, Minnesota 20

Tavon Austin caught ten passes for 145 yards and a touchdown and Zac Stacy scored twice on 95 rushing yards as St. Louis ran for 187. Matt Cassel threw for 313 and two touchdowns, including a 60-yarder to Jerick McKinnon, with one interception; Adrian Peterson carried nine times for 26 and left with a short-term injury. Standouts: Austin, Stacy, Janoris Jenkins (forced and recovered a fumble).

## New England 35, Miami 9

Stevan Ridley ran for 126 yards and a touchdown on 22 carries and Tom Brady threw three touchdowns on 29 attempts. Ryan Tannehill threw 52 times for 296 yards and three interceptions with no touchdown, 129 of the yards to Brandon Gibson on twelve catches from 22 targets. Standouts: Ridley, Brady, Gibson. New England's Jerod Mayo left with an injury projected at three weeks.

## Atlanta 42, New Orleans 21

Matt Ryan threw for 437 yards and five touchdowns on 37 attempts, Devonta Freeman caught seven for 125 and a score on top of 80 rushing yards, and Roddy White had 110 and a touchdown on four catches. Drew Brees threw for 341 without a sack but with one touchdown; Mark Ingram and Khiry Robinson scored on the ground. Standouts: Ryan, Freeman, White. Atlanta's Robert Alford and White both left under short instructions.

## New York Giants 34, Detroit 6

Rashad Jennings ran 21 times for 186 yards and two touchdowns, 92 of them on one carry, and the Giants sacked Matthew Stafford six times, two each by Devon Kennard and Mathias Kiwanuka. Stafford was intercepted twice; Detroit turned it over three times and Nate Freese missed one of three field goals. Standouts: Jennings, Kennard, Kiwanuka. Detroit's Dwight Bentley left with a multi-week injury.

## Oakland 31, New York Jets 20

Derek Carr threw three touchdowns on 33 attempts without an interception and Darren McFadden had 110 rushing yards with a 42-yard run and a score, plus 88 receiving on four catches with a 52-yarder. Geno Smith threw three touchdowns of his own on a day neither side turned the ball over. Standouts: McFadden, Carr, Eric Decker (87 yards and a score). The Jets' David Harris left under a short instruction.

## Arizona 27, San Diego 10

Andre Ellington ran 24 times for 185 yards with a 41-yard run and Arizona rushed for 247; Calais Campbell sacked Philip Rivers twice of four. Arizona turned the ball over three times and the score held; Rivers threw for 225 with a touchdown and an interception. Standouts: Ellington, Campbell, Larry Fitzgerald (85 yards).

## San Francisco 20, Dallas 14

Dallas turned it over four times, two Tony Romo interceptions among them, and San Francisco did not turn it over at all. Colin Kaepernick threw 45 times for 313 yards and two touchdowns, a 75-yarder to Brandon Lloyd, and Ian Williams sacked Romo twice; Phil Dawson missed two of four field goals. Standouts: Kaepernick, Williams, Anquan Boldin (seven catches, 80 yards). Dallas's Brandon Carr left with a short-term injury.

## Kansas City 24, Tennessee 17

Jamaal Charles ran for 109 yards and two touchdowns on 20 carries and Alex Smith threw a 50-yard touchdown to Donnie Avery; Kansas City did not turn the ball over. Jake Locker threw 44 times for 308 yards and two touchdowns with an interception and was sacked three times, Justin Houston making 14 tackles. Tennessee turned it over three times and Ryan Succop missed one of two. Standouts: Charles, Houston, Nate Washington (83 yards).

## Houston 27, Washington 23

Ryan Fitzpatrick threw three touchdowns and three interceptions on 41 attempts, 339 yards in all, and Kendrick Lewis forced and recovered a fumble among his ten tackles. Robert Griffin threw for 254 and two touchdowns with an interception, 60 yards to DeSean Jackson; Alfred Morris caught a 59-yard pass on top of 64 rushing yards and Kai Forbath made all three field goals. Standouts: Fitzpatrick, Lewis, Arian Foster (83 yards). Washington's Brian Orakpo left with an injury projected at a month, which carries into its Week 2 game against Jacksonville.
