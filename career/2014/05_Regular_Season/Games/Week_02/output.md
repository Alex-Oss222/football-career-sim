## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** The staff had one Washington game of its own to study, and it was a loss: Houston 27, Washington 23 at Houston on September 7, the [league roundup](../../League_Results/week_01.md) and its game book. Robert Griffin threw 38 times for 254 yards, two touchdowns and an interception and took one sack in 71 snaps; Alfred Morris carried 24 times for 64 and caught four passes for 68, the 59-yarder the longest play of Washington's day, and lost a fumble; DeSean Jackson caught six of nine targets for 60 and the touchdown; Pierre Garcon two of seven for 13; Jordan Reed four for 37 from the tight-end spot; Kai Forbath made all three field goals and Tress Way averaged 47.7 on six punts. Washington ran 71 offensive snaps, converted 7 of 16 third downs, turned the ball over twice and allowed three Houston touchdowns, Ryan Fitzpatrick's three interceptions keeping the game close. On defense Ryan Kerrigan had the sack with three pressures and a tackle for loss in 64 snaps, Jason Hatcher two tackles in 44, Bashaud Breeland two of the three interceptions with three passes defended at the second corner, Perry Riley eight tackles. Brian Orakpo left after two defensive snaps with an injury the record projects at a month, and that is the one Washington availability fact the staff had; Garcon and Darrel Young were logged with short projections that run out before Sunday, so the plan treats both as available. One game gives evidence and not a tendency profile, and the staff said so. The system evidence is older and public: Jay Gruden entered the season trying to develop Griffin as a pocket passer while leaning on Morris and the run game, with Griffin, Morris, Young, Jackson and Garcon among the listed starters, and Jim Haslett's defense stayed a three-four structure with a more aggressive approach and Hatcher brought in to move across the front and rush inside on passing downs, all from the Washington Post's September 2 previews that the packet cites. Washington's Week 1 chart puts Trent Williams and Tyler Polumbus at the tackles, Kory Lichtensteiger at center, Kerrigan and Orakpo at the outside linebackers with Trent Murphy behind Orakpo, Chris Baker, Barry Cofield and Hatcher across the front, Keenan Robinson and Riley inside, DeAngelo Hall and David Amerson at the corners with Breeland behind Amerson, Ryan Clark at free safety, and Andre Roberts returning kicks and punts, four kickoff returns for 84 at Houston. What the staff did not have was Washington's Week 2 lineup or any film of what it would call against twelve personnel without Orakpo; the opener was built to find that out.

**Stone's priorities and decisions.** Stone issued the [September 7 packet](Regular-season%20Week%202.md) as the Week 2 plan and it governed the six days within his authority, and by issuing it he adopted the four decisions at its foot. The roster decisions went to Caldwell on Monday morning and were executed the same day ([September 8 transactions](../../../00_Team_Operations/Team/Roster/transactions_2014-09-08.md)): Mike Harris to ordinary reserve/injured and Dwight Lowery to reserve/injured with the club's one return designation, the designation preserved for the harder job to replace; Alan Ball called first for the corner room, examined and signed on a one-year minimum contract with no role promise ([signing record](../../../00_Team_Operations/Free_Agency/alan_ball_signing_2014-09-08.md)); Adrian Phillips promoted from the practice squad for safety and coverage-unit depth, so Jemea Thomas was not needed. The secondary for Washington is the packet's: Talib and Verner outside, Rambo and Trawick at safety, Poyer at nickel, Bouye the first outside reserve, Butler the next corner, Prosinski the safety reserve, Phillips added depth, Ball the reserve outside corner listed last in his first week with any expansion to be earned through the work. Poyer stays at nickel because moving him would make two changes to repair one injury. Lowery remains a captain; Daryl Smith remains the defense's relay and Oden and Crennel used Monday and Wednesday to build the secondary echo behind him without touching the six captain appointments. On offense the week led with Power and Counter, moved Inside Zone down the opener until the front and count are seen, kept Stick, Mesh, Drive, the access bubble and the slow screen prominent against pressure, held Boot Flood Left out unless Wednesday earned it back, kept Boot Flood Right under its edge rule, carried the Power Pass with MAX as a conditional shot rather than a replay of the two Philadelphia touchdowns and kept Dagger behind its protection gate. The first HC direction was that every Week 1 sack be classified before Wednesday and that no unidentified protection survive Friday. The backfield direction was that Anderson and Grimes take the available work until medical updated Jones-Drew, with no projection treated as a clearance and no touch quota assigned. On defense the direction was to fit Morris first without selling out the secondary, to keep rush lanes against Griffin as important as first pressure, to simplify communication with Verner and Trawick entering the first group, to establish leverage and help rules on Jackson and Garcon rather than brackets by reputation, and to add nothing to the installed coverage families. Westhoff's direction was a dedicated coverage correction after the 134 return yards at Philadelphia, placement and lane responsibility graded separately. The call sheet is the packet's: an opener of thirty calls built on nineteen distinct calls with eleven planned returns to a picture already shown, Snag on the low-red-zone menu only, twenty distinct calls in all; third down from Power, Counter, Mesh and the access package in short yardage through Stick, Mesh and Drive in the middle distances to Y-Cross with SOLID, Drive, the gated Dagger and the slow screen in long; the red zone from Power, Counter and the Cross high and from Bunch Mesh, Snag, Power, Counter and Boot Flood Right low. What came off the menu came off before the week by the packet's own exclusions, none of them taught in the 2014 program: the Choice tag, the Y-Iso alignment, the dropback Sail, TE Delay, the Alert Post tag, Texas and Angle, Split Leak, the static Empty and the six-lineman package. The Friday rule removed nothing, for the reasons in the reduction table below, and Boot Flood Left stayed off for the reason given there. The frozen sheet is [call_sheet.json](call_sheet.json).
