## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** The staff had one Washington game of its own to study, and it was a loss: Houston 27, Washington 23 at Houston on September 7, the [league roundup](../../League_Results/week_01.md) and its game book. Robert Griffin threw 38 times for 254 yards, two touchdowns and an interception and took one sack in 71 snaps; Alfred Morris carried 24 times for 64 and caught four passes for 68, the 59-yarder the longest play of Washington's day, and lost a fumble; DeSean Jackson caught six of nine targets for 60 and the touchdown; Pierre Garcon two of seven for 13; Jordan Reed four for 37 from the tight-end spot; Kai Forbath made all three field goals and Tress Way averaged 47.7 on six punts. Washington ran 71 offensive snaps, converted 7 of 16 third downs, turned the ball over twice and allowed three Houston touchdowns, Ryan Fitzpatrick's three interceptions keeping the game close. On defense Ryan Kerrigan had the sack with three pressures and a tackle for loss in 64 snaps, Jason Hatcher two tackles in 44, Bashaud Breeland two of the three interceptions with three passes defended at the second corner, Perry Riley eight tackles. Brian Orakpo left after two defensive snaps with an injury the record projects at a month, and that is the one Washington availability fact the staff had; Garcon and Darrel Young were logged with short projections that run out before Sunday, so the plan treats both as available. One game gives evidence and not a tendency profile, and the staff said so. The system evidence is older and public: Jay Gruden entered the season trying to develop Griffin as a pocket passer while leaning on Morris and the run game, with Griffin, Morris, Young, Jackson and Garcon among the listed starters, and Jim Haslett's defense stayed a three-four structure with a more aggressive approach and Hatcher brought in to move across the front and rush inside on passing downs, all from the Washington Post's September 2 previews that the packet cites. Washington's Week 1 chart puts Trent Williams and Tyler Polumbus at the tackles, Kory Lichtensteiger at center, Kerrigan and Orakpo at the outside linebackers with Trent Murphy behind Orakpo, Chris Baker, Barry Cofield and Hatcher across the front, Keenan Robinson and Riley inside, DeAngelo Hall and David Amerson at the corners with Breeland behind Amerson, Ryan Clark at free safety, and Andre Roberts returning kicks and punts, four kickoff returns for 84 at Houston. What the staff did not have was Washington's Week 2 lineup or any film of what it would call against twelve personnel without Orakpo; the opener was built to find that out.

## 2. Pregame media Q&A

**Reporter:** Two defensive backs went on injured reserve Monday and you signed Alan Ball the same day. Walk us through the roster moves.

**Stone:** Mike went on injured reserve and Dwight went on with the designation to return, so he can practice in six weeks and play in eight if the doctors clear him. Both exams Monday said what Sunday night said. Alan took a physical and signed, and Adrian Phillips came up from the practice squad. That is Dave's part of it and I'll leave the terms to him.

**Reporter:** Who steps in for them, and what are you asking of Verner and Trawick this week?

**Stone:** Alterraun outside with Aqib, Brynden next to Bacarri, Jordan stays at nickel. Alterraun has started a lot of football games. The job this week was the communication, not the coverage: Daryl relays it, the safeties echo it, Alterraun declares his help. We ran that Monday and Wednesday before we ran anything else.

**Reporter:** Did you think about moving Poyer outside?

**Stone:** No. That is two changes to fix one injury.

**Reporter:** What's the status of Maurice Jones-Drew?

**Stone:** He was reassessed Monday and nothing changed, and his date was Friday. The trainers cleared him Friday. He did not practice this week.

**Reporter:** Did he practice Friday?

**Stone:** No. We had already practiced when he cleared and I was not going to put him in the last period.

**Reporter:** He hasn't had much practice time. Can he play without the reps?

**Stone:** He took the walkthrough Saturday, all of it. C.J. and Jonathan took the week's work and that order is rehearsed behind him. If he dresses he dresses to play, and there is no number on it.

**Reporter:** What's the approach in preparing for Robert Griffin and that offense?

**Stone:** Fit Alfred Morris first. Then the lanes. Four rushers who keep their relationship to him matter more than the first man there, because the first man there creates the lane he runs through. The fifth rusher comes only when Romeo can name the protection it attacks.

**Reporter:** Washington lost at Houston, so they're 0-1. Does that change anything about how you see them?

**Stone:** One game is one game. They threw it 38 times, Morris caught a 59-yarder, and they were in it to the end. We have one Washington game of our own to study and that is all I will say about tendencies.

## 4. Postgame media Q&A

**Stone:** We won by seventeen on the road with four sacks and three takeaways and we ran the ball for 171. We also threw it to the other team three times, one of them from their 11, and gave up 127 return yards for the second week running. The three throws are mine before they are Kirk's. Go ahead.

**Reporter:** Just defensively, I think there were three takeaways and four sacks --

**Stone:** Three and four, and seventeen points off the three. Julian had two of the sacks over their left tackle, Roy and Jeremy had the other two and both of those were strips they recovered themselves. Paul's pick at our 42 was the one that turned it, because C.J. scored two plays later. They had one drive, the first one, and after that nothing got inside our 30.

**Reporter:** What did you think of Kirk's performance, the three interceptions, his progression at all?

**Stone:** Eighteen of 32, one touchdown, three picks, and one sack after four last week. The 51 to Davante from our 16 was the throw that got us off our goal line and into the tying drive. The three interceptions came on three third downs, and two of those were calls that should not have left the sideline in that situation, which is my procedure. The read on each of the three I have not seen. I will not split them between him and me until I have.

**Reporter:** Was Kelce supposed to get the ball on third-and-1?

**Stone:** He was the target. Dagger on third-and-1 is not the sheet. The sheet has Power, Counter, Mesh and the access run there, and he got the gated call instead. That is the sideline, not the quarterback.

**Reporter:** How big was the sequence before the half? You had it at the 11 and you turn it over there.

**Stone:** We ran it down to 1:18 from our 38 and came away with nothing from the 11, and then they got to our 30 before the half ran out. The defense and the clock covered that one. The offense did not.

**Reporter:** What was working about the run game?

**Stone:** Counter and the outside zone off it. Both touchdowns came on those two calls, and Power Right carried the drive before the half. Mike and the rookies at guard moved them, and Maurice ran it 24 times without a practice rep this week.

**Reporter:** Maurice, 112 yards, no practice all week. How did he hold up?

**Stone:** He played every snap and the trainers have nothing logged on him today. He went in with no number and he earned the 24. I will see him Monday like everybody else.

**Reporter:** What did you think of C.J.?

**Stone:** The 24-yard touchdown and the 12 to the 11 on the tying drive. Eight snaps, seven carries, 50 yards. He ran the outside zone the way Mike taught it in August.

**Reporter:** Stanford had two sacks today. What have you seen from him?

**Stone:** Two over their left tackle and both got them off the field. He also got a hand on the first throw of their two-minute drive. He was logged with something after their first drive and played through it; the trainers see him tomorrow. The lane and the fit behind the sack are what I grade, and I have not graded them yet.

**Reporter:** Lane Johnson looked like he was favoring something after that sack. Anything?

**Stone:** Lower body. He finished the game, every snap. He is seen tomorrow and the trainers put the date on it, not me.

**Reporter:** How is he?

**Stone:** He walked off. That is what I know.

**Reporter:** Andre Roberts had 105 kick-return yards and the 35 at the end. What happened on the coverage?

**Stone:** It was 134 last week and 127 this week, and we spent two practices on it. I do not know yet whether it was the lanes or the placement, and we grade those separately. Mike has it tomorrow and I will have an answer that is not the same as last week's.

**Reporter:** Three takeaways. Is that a start?

**Stone:** It is three. Roy and Jeremy knocked the ball out themselves and fell on it themselves, and Paul read a throw. We had none last week.

**Reporter:** 2-0, two road wins. What does that mean?

**Stone:** It means we come home 2-0 and play Indianapolis, who scored 31 today. The three throws and the returns come home with us.
