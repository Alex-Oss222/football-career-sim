### Washington

Washington is **0-1 after a 27-23 branch loss at Houston**. One game gives us evidence, not a tendency profile: Griffin threw for 254 with two touchdowns and an interception; Alfred Morris added 64 rushing and a 59-yard reception; DeSean Jackson had 60 receiving yards. Brian Orakpo's branch injury projects him out for this game.

The useful pre-Week-1 system evidence is broader. Jay Gruden entered the season trying to develop Griffin more as a pocket passer while expecting the offense to lean substantially on Morris and the run game. [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/redskins-offense-seeks-an-identity-in-first-season-under-coach-jay-gruden/2014/09/02/5326ff9a-2eb4-11e4-9b98-848790384093_story.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com) Washington listed Griffin, Morris, Darrel Young, Jackson and Garçon among the offensive starters before opening day. [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/sf/sports/wp/2014/09/02/redskins-starters/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Defensively, Jim Haslett remained in a 3-4 structure but entered 2014 with an explicitly more aggressive approach. Jason Hatcher was acquired to move across fronts and provide interior rush, particularly in passing situations. [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/redskins-defense-mixes-new-and-familiar-faces-aims-to-be-more-aggressive-better/2014/09/02/25d1f0a4-2ee6-11e4-9b98-848790384093_story.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Staff thesis:** Orakpo being out does not permit us to relax the protection plan. Locate Kerrigan and Hatcher, solve the interior movement, and make Washington prove it can remain sound against Power, Counter, quick access and shifts before we spend the game dropping Cousins into long-developing concepts.
