## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** The staff had no Philadelphia game of its own to study. Jacksonville has not played the Eagles under Stone, and the only Philadelphia football on the club's record is the 2013 season: 8-8, the NFC East title on the division record, a wild-card win over Tampa Bay, a divisional win at St. Louis and the conference championship loss at Minnesota, with 318 points scored and 330 allowed. What the staff did have was public before the branch: Chip Kelly's offense plays at a tempo that denies the defense its substitution, and Billy Davis described his defense as a three-man-front structure with four-man looks, the base ends predominantly two-gap players and the structure chosen for spread width. That told the staff what kind of system to prepare for and not what Philadelphia would call on September 7. The club's declared Week 1 lineup was not in the staff's hands when the plan was built, so the week prepared for the system rather than for a named quarterback or a named front, and the opening menu was built to find the answers on the field. The players the plan had to account for were its own: Nicks out and Hoskins out under the August 28 instructions, Bouye cleared and the 46 otherwise whole.

**Stone's priorities and decisions.** Stone issued the [August 28 packet](Regular-season%20Week%201.md) as the Week 1 plan and it governed the six days within his authority. Its first direction was that no new concept family enters the plan this week: the game is won or lost through formation, shift, sequencing and protection with the taught sheet, and installation time goes to execution. Its second was that every major dropback is rehearsed against a live rush picture, with the short side of SCAT, HALF, SOLID and MAX identified before a call is approved. Its third was that twelve personnel makes Philadelphia declare how it matches Lewis and Kelce together, attached first and then shifted to space without a substitution. On the receivers, the offense runs normally with Adams at X, Thielen at H and Hawkins at Z, no target quota replaces Nicks, and the relief job belongs to Hurns, the fourth receiver, because Gabriel is inactive under Stone's standing rule. The starters are the season game depth chart of August 30 with no change: Monroe, Bitonio, Brewster, Turner and Johnson; Jones-Drew; Lewis, Kelce and Jensen at tight end; Cousins. The call sheet is the packet's: eleven and twelve personnel as the base, an opener of thirty calls built on twenty-one distinct calls with nine planned returns to a picture already shown, each return asking whether Philadelphia's first answer was a rule or a reaction; third down from Power, Counter, Mesh and the access package in short yardage through Mesh, Stick and Drive in the middle distances to Y-Cross with SOLID, Dagger with HALF, Drive and the slow screen in long; the red zone from the compact runs and the Cross high and from Bunch Mesh, Snag, the boot, Power and Counter low. On defense Crennel carries the vocabulary the unit owns, Under and Nickel Even, Cover 1, 1 Robber/Rat, Cover 3 and Quarters, the four-man rush and the installed five-man pressure, with one no-substitution escape call for base personnel and one for nickel against Philadelphia's tempo and no line game, none having been taught. Westhoff's opening operation is Scobee, Anger and Kreiter, with Hawkins returning kicks and punts and Hurns the second punt returner. What came off the menu came off before the week began, by the packet's own exclusions: the Choice tag, the Y-Iso alignment, the dropback Sail, TE Delay, the Alert Post tag, Texas and Angle, Split Leak, the static Empty and the six-lineman package, none of them taught in the 2014 program. The Friday rule, that any call with unresolved protection, formation communication or route spacing comes off and is replaced from the same situation, removed nothing, for the reasons in the reduction table below. The frozen sheet is [call_sheet.json](call_sheet.json).

**The week, day by day.** Monday, September 1, was the opponent introduction and the correction day, in helmets at walkthrough pace. The offense walked the packet's four base pictures against Tice's scout look, the Inside Zone from Doubles against an odd front, Power from Ace against the odd front and then the reduced front, Stick with SCAT against two-high and then a pressure stem, and the shift from twelve personnel to Empty with the base personnel staying on the field, two repetitions each, the point of the walkthrough being whether the identification language survives a front change and whether everyone is set after the shift. The defense walked its call procedure against a scout offense aligning immediately: the call, the eligible count, the front, the strength and the motion adjustment, with Daryl Smith relaying. The Atlanta corrections were taken one item at a time, the Boot Flood sack at Turner's slot the one offensive film item still open, coached by Yarno as the backside edge on the boot and nothing else. Jeremy Cain's Chicago signing came onto the league record on this date, which ends Jacksonville's involvement with the long-snapper question; Kreiter is the snapper. Tuesday, September 2, was the players' day off, and the staff built the Wednesday script and the opponent scout cards from the Monday walkthrough. Wednesday, September 3, was the main installation practice, in pads: the inside run and the gap run against the odd and shaded fronts, the twelve-personnel declaration with the shift, the movement pair from Wing with the scout edge alternately squeezing and chasing, the Drive, Y-Cross and Dagger dropbacks each against the rush picture the packet names for it, the access package against a changing box, and a scripted drive from the own 25 with the calls from the sideline, the substitutions, the huddle, the cadence and the play clock, Stone sending the calls himself. The cap-compliance filing closed at 4 p.m. ET and the Top-51 count ended at midnight, as the calendar carries it. Thursday, September 4, was the competitive situational practice, in pads: third down by distance in the packet's order, Power and Counter in short yardage against a loaded interior and a fast-flow linebacker, Mesh against the scout's man and match, Stick with the extra rusher threatened, Drive against zone pressure, Y-Cross with SOLID against a mugged interior, Dagger against a four-man rush and split safeties, the slow screen against an aggressive rush; the defense against tempo on its two escape calls and on the installed five-man pressure with the protection issue named before each call. Friday, September 5, was the scoring and clock day in helmets: the high and low red zone, the backed-up series, the two-minute sequence by signal and word with the sideline and clock procedure, and the four-minute series with the Y-Cross and the boot against the sellout; Westhoff rehearsed the hands and onside units from the 46. Bates and Tice heard Cousins on the pressure calls after practice and Stone applied the reduction rule after that. Saturday, September 6, was the opener and operation walkthrough at no-contact pace, the thirty calls in the packet's order interrupted deliberately by a third down, a backed-up snap and a low-red-zone snap so the offense rehearsed leaving the opener and returning to it; Stone confirmed the lineup, the inactives and the sheet, and the team travelled to Philadelphia.

**Availability.** Hakeem Nicks, wide receiver, did not practice Wednesday, Thursday or Friday under his August 28 instruction; the medical staff reassessed him Thursday, September 4, on the day the instruction named, and communicated no change: out, projected return September 20. Status for the game: out. Gator Hoskins, tight end, did not practice Wednesday, Thursday or Friday under his August 28 instruction; reassessed Wednesday, September 3, on the day the instruction named, with no change: out, projected return September 16. Status for the game: out. A.J. Bouye, cleared August 31 on his projected date, was a full participant all week with no communicated limitation. No other player was limited or held out, no injury arose in practice, and no practice-squad elevation was made; the two reserve/injured players, Brate and Jackson, are not on the 53. The inactives for the game are the seven set on August 31 and confirmed by Stone on September 6 with no change: Nicks and Hoskins by medical instruction, and Butler, Linsley, Edebali, Todd Davis and Gabriel by his standing rule for the healthy inactives, the deepest healthy reserve in each of the five largest healthy position groups. Forty-six dress.

### The quarterback's input

Held Friday after practice, as the packet schedules it, Bates and Tice asking Cousins which of the seven candidate calls he trusts against pressure and which he does not want protected only by theory. His answers are his reported views from the recorded work of camp and this week, offered when invited, not quotations and not a grade. The calls he trusts are the quick game and the HALF-protected core he has operated all year, Stick, Mesh and Drive from Doubles, Bunch and Trey, the Y-Cross from both Trey pictures and the Boot Flood's free-edge answer from Wing, with the slow screen when the rush gives a reason for it. The call he wants protected by the picture and not by theory is Dagger with HALF against a mugged interior, where the dig timing is the longest on the sheet; his answer and the staff's are the same, the packet's own gate on the call and the SOLID Y-Cross as the same-situation answer when the pressure picture shows. Nothing he and Brewster see differently in protection is open. Stone's response was the reduction below; he changed no starting point, added no call and set no series count.
