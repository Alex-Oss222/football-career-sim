## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** Pittsburgh came in 3-1 from four games of its own: Cleveland 36, Pittsburgh 24 at Heinz Field on September 7, Pittsburgh 41, Baltimore 28 at Baltimore on Thursday, September 11, Pittsburgh 34, Carolina 24 at Carolina on the night of September 21 and Pittsburgh 26, Tampa Bay 17 at Heinz Field on September 28 ([Week 1](../../League_Results/week_01.md), [Week 2](../../League_Results/week_02.md), [Week 3](../../League_Results/week_03.md) and [Week 4](../../League_Results/week_04.md) roundups and their game books). Le'Veon Bell has 83 carries for 581 yards and eight touchdowns, 27 for 194 and four touchdowns at Baltimore, 26 for 202 with the 93-yarder at Carolina and 16 for 137 and two touchdowns against Tampa Bay, and 15 catches on 19 targets for 217 and two more touchdowns, the 55-yarder at Baltimore the long one; he lost the one fumble of his season in the opener. ## 4. Postgame media Q&A

**Stone:** Four turnovers, and they scored 17 off them. We had it 10-6 in the third quarter and we moved it most of the day. The two fourth downs you're going to ask about are mine. Go ahead.

**Reporter:** How would you sum up the offense today?

**Stone:** We moved it. Three hundred and sixty yards, 30 runs, and they didn't sack Kirk. Then three interceptions and the ball on the ground at their 20. Two of those four came down there with a chance at points, and you don't beat this team doing that.

**Reporter:** What was the thinking on kicking the field goal in the third quarter, fourth-and-1 at the 2?

**Stone:** I'd made that call before the third down. Third-and-16 at the 17 is a kick for me. Maurice took the screen to the 2, it was fourth-and-1, and the call stood. By the rule I go by, fourth-and-1 is a go. Then we got the delay on top of it. That's three points where I should have given us a shot at seven, and it's mine.

**Reporter:** And the punt on fourth-and-4 at their 26 in the second quarter?

**Stone:** Same thing. Third-and-14 at the 36 is a 54-yard kick, so the punt was set. Travis got 10, it was fourth-and-4 at the 26, and we punted. That's a go down for me too. Bryan put it on the 2 and we got it back at their 42, and we didn't do anything with that either.

**Reporter:** How did Kirk play?

**Stone:** Twenty-one of 33, no sacks, three interceptions. Two were going to Travis, and one to Andrew at their 15 right after Daryl got us the ball. I'll look at all three before I put any of it on the throw, the route or the read. He hit Davante for 64 on the touchdown drive.

**Reporter:** Travis had three balls come his way and two of them got picked off. What did you see from him?

**Stone:** He caught the third-and-14 for 10 that got us to their 26. The two that were picked were thrown his way, and I'm not putting them on him from up here.

**Reporter:** What happened on Maurice's fumble at their 20?

**Stone:** He'd gained 8 on Power and the ball came out. Timmons got it out and Polamalu was on it. We were down 10 with nine and a half to go. He ran it 24 times for 110 today.

**Reporter:** Bell's run on fourth-and-1, was that the backbreaker?

**Stone:** We had them stopped short on third-and-13 and he went 24 on fourth-and-1. They scored on the next snap. Then we threw it to them on our next series and they scored again.

**Reporter:** Did the defense wear down on that last touchdown drive? They ran it eleven straight times.

**Stone:** Eleven snaps and they never threw it. Bell had a 30 in the middle of it. Whether we fit those runs is what I want to know, and I don't know it standing here.

**Reporter:** Two sacks and they were 4 of 14 on third down. What did you like from the defense?

**Stone:** Third-and-long, they made one, the catch by Bell on the first drive. Jeremy and Andre got the sacks. Daryl took the ball away at their 17 before the half and we gave it right back. Brynden made eight tackles.

**Reporter:** Anything on Christian Jones and Brynden Trawick?

**Stone:** Both were looked at and neither came out. Christian gets seen tomorrow and Brynden on Tuesday. That's what I have.

**Reporter:** Did Pittsburgh do anything that surprised you?

**Stone:** No. They took it away five times at Carolina. Today they had four and we handed them all four.

**Reporter:** You're 3-2 and still on top of the division. How do you regroup from this?

**Stone:** Monday is this tape. Then it's Tennessee, on the road.

### Addendum, October 18, 2026: the fourth-down rule stands for every game

Stone gave the rule during this game, after the first fourth down, and it decided the other five ([material game-management decisions](#player-findings-and-material-decisions)). The user's instruction of October 18, 2026 made it his standing rule for every game. In the excerpt on record, with its bracket and omission: "i dont need to [play each one]; universal rule: 4 and 2 and below we always go for it; 4 and 3 or 4 ... go; 4 and 5 or above field goal or punt". The user clarified it the same day: field goals to 52 yards, go past midfield, Tice's first call.
