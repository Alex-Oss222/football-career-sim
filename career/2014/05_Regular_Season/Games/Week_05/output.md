## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** Pittsburgh came in 3-1 from four games of its own: Cleveland 36, Pittsburgh 24 at Heinz Field on September 7, Pittsburgh 41, Baltimore 28 at Baltimore on Thursday, September 11, Pittsburgh 34, Carolina 24 at Carolina on the night of September 21 and Pittsburgh 26, Tampa Bay 17 at Heinz Field on September 28 ([Week 1](../../League_Results/week_01.md), [Week 2](../../League_Results/week_02.md), [Week 3](../../League_Results/week_03.md) and [Week 4](../../League_Results/week_04.md) roundups and their game books). It has scored 125 points and run for 859 yards, 68 in the opener and 302, 232 and 257 since. Le'Veon Bell has 83 carries for 581 yards and eight touchdowns, 27 for 194 and four touchdowns at Baltimore, 26 for 202 with the 93-yarder at Carolina and 16 for 137 and two touchdowns against Tampa Bay, and 15 catches on 19 targets for 217 and two more touchdowns, the 55-yarder at Baltimore the long one; he lost the one fumble of his season in the opener. LeGarrette Blount has 20 carries for 187 and a touchdown, nine of the carries for 96 of the yards at Baltimore. Ben Roethlisberger is 82 of 134 for 916 yards with five touchdowns and five interceptions, four of the interceptions in the opener, when Pittsburgh turned the ball over five times; Pittsburgh has given it away once in the three games since. He has been sacked nine times for 50 yards: one charged to Kelvin Beachum's slot at left tackle, two to Ramon Foster's at left guard, three to Maurkice Pouncey's at center, none to David DeCastro's at right guard and three to Marcus Gilbert's at right tackle. The staff treated that split as the packet does, a reason to study the center and right-side relationships first, and not as proof of who made a protection error or a reason to rush the same side on every snap. The passing game runs through more than one man: Markus Wheaton 19 catches on 28 targets for 225 and two touchdowns, seven for 110 against Tampa Bay; Heath Miller 15 on 31 targets for 166; Antonio Brown 15 on 23 for 139 and a touchdown, held to 11 snaps at Baltimore after an injury logged at the end of Pittsburgh's opening possession, with a hold projected at five days, and on the field for every snap of the two games since; Justin Brown nine for 104. On defense Ryan Shazier has 45 tackles, three of Pittsburgh's ten sacks, an interception and a fumble he forced and recovered at Carolina; Cameron Heyward and Cam Thomas have two sacks each and Daniel McCullers, Steve McLendon and Jason Worilds one. Pittsburgh has seven takeaways, five of them at Carolina and two at Baltimore, with interceptions by Shazier, Michael Mitchell, Will Allen and Cortez Allen. Cleveland ran for 142 yards against it, Baltimore 178, Carolina 84 and Tampa Bay 156, and the four opponents scored 105 points; their tight ends caught 21 of 39 targets for 210 yards and no touchdown, Greg Olsen's seven for 67 at Carolina the most. Dri Archer has returned 15 kickoffs for 334 yards; Darrius Heyward-Bey has returned three punts for 35 and Antonio Brown one, so the staff did not assume who fields Sunday's punts. Shaun Suisham is nine of nine on field goals and 14 of 14 on tries, and Brad Wing has punted 13 times for 615 yards with five inside the 20. Pittsburgh's one roster change since the opening week is the September 23 signing of James Harrison, the outside linebacker who had announced his retirement September 5, and he has no snap in the four game books; the September 30 release of Adam Podlesh on the league's record changed nothing, Podlesh not being on Pittsburgh's roster ([in-season league moves](../../../../../library/2014_inseason_rails.md)). Two Pittsburgh injury projections in the game books reach this week: Sean Spence, the inside linebacker, held since the first quarter of the opener on a multi-week projection to October 10 with no snap since, and Will Allen, the safety, a minor projection to September 29 from an injury logged in the third quarter against Tampa Bay, where he was not removed; the staff treated both as unknown until Pittsburgh's own list. What the staff did not have was Pittsburgh's coverage and pressure choices: the four game books record outcomes and participation, the sack, the rusher and the slot, and not a formation-by-formation pressure rate, a coverage chart, a response to motion or a protection declaration, so no tendency percentage was built into the plan, and the opener was written to learn from the first twelve-personnel presentation and shift who stays with each tight end, who widens with the back and whether Pittsburgh bumps or travels.

**Stone's priorities and decisions.** Stone issued the [September 28 packet](Week_05_Pittsburgh.md) as the Week 5 plan and it governed the six days within his authority. The calling arrangement begun at San Diego continues: Stone calls both sides; Crennel prepares the defense, gives him the situation and protection information and transmits his call, Daryl Smith making the front declaration; Tice tracks the offensive situation and the next eligible opening call, Bates supplies quarterback and protection information without issuing a competing call, Skalaski the clock, timeout, fourth-down and challenge information, and Westhoff directs the kicking game. No emergency caller succession was appointed.
