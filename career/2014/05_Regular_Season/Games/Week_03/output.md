## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** The staff had two Indianapolis games of its own to study and they pointed in different directions: Denver 41, Indianapolis 20 at Denver on September 7 and Indianapolis 31, Philadelphia 6 at Indianapolis on September 14, the [Week 1 roundup](../../League_Results/week_01.md), the [Week 2 roundup](../../League_Results/week_02.md) and their game books. Across the two, Andrew Luck is 44 of 79 for 657 yards, four touchdowns and four interceptions with five sacks taken; Trent Richardson has 48 carries for 272, 22 of them for 165 and a touchdown against Philadelphia; T.Y. Hilton 22 catches on 33 targets for 259 and two touchdowns, eleven catches in each game; Reggie Wayne nine for 158 with a touchdown; Dwayne Allen six for 131 with a touchdown, the 51-yarder at Denver the long one; Coby Fleener two for 53 in 43 offensive snaps as the second tight end. Richardson, Hilton, Wayne and Allen played every one of the 147 offensive snaps, and Mario Harvey took 36 at fullback. On defense Cory Redding has three sacks and five pressures, Cam Johnson two sacks, both at Denver, and Vontae Davis comes off a game with an interception and four passes defended; D'Qwell Jackson and Jerrell Freeman are the tacklers inside, Jackson with two tackles for loss. The two defensive results are not one picture: Denver gained 449 yards and scored 41, Philadelphia gained 175 and kicked two field goals, and the staff declined to call either the tendency. The protection evidence is more specific: of the five sacks Indianapolis has allowed, four are charged to the Gosder Cherilus and Hugh Thornton slots on the right side and one to Anthony Castonzo's. Castonzo left the Philadelphia game with a lower-extremity injury the record projects at four days, to September 18, and Khaled Holmes was removed under a head and neck hold projected at six days, to September 20, with Jonotthan Harrison playing 47 snaps at center behind him; both dates are projections and not clearances, and the staff prepared for either lineup. Dan Herron has five kickoff returns for 139 branch yards, 120 of them against Philadelphia, and Griff Whalen eight punt returns for 93. The Week 1 chart puts Castonzo and Cherilus at the tackles, Jack Mewhort and Thornton at guard, Holmes at center with Harrison behind him, Redding, Josh Chapman and Arthur Jones across the front, Bjoern Werner and Erik Walden outside, Freeman and Jackson inside, Davis and Greg Toler at corner with LaRon Landry and Mike Adams at safety, Adam Vinatieri kicking and Pat McAfee punting; Cecil Shorts and Justin Blackmon, traded there in March, are on the roster with no snap recorded in either game. What the staff did not have was Indianapolis's defensive matching rule against twelve personnel, which neither game book records, and that is what the opener was built to find out.

**Stone's priorities and decisions.** Stone issued the [September 14 packet](Week%203%20Packet_%20Indianapolis.md) as the Week 3 plan and it governed the six days within his authority; by issuing it he adopted the four decisions at its foot. The first is the call-control procedure, and the Monday review changed its footing before the week began. The packet was written against the play-by-play of the first two games, which carried Snag outside its folder, the gated Dagger on third-and-1, a throw from the Washington 11 with no sheet call and the opener's first call unsent, and the Week 1 and Week 2 reviews had treated those as the fifth game of a sideline-procedure item. The staff record now establishes that the call labels in those game books were attached by the record's labelling method without reading the down, the distance, the field zone or the opener's order, and that method is corrected from this game; the two reviews' finding that the sideline sent those calls is withdrawn, the games and every statistic in them stand ([Week 2 page](../Week_02/README.md), the note of October 2, 2026; engine note in section 7). Stone's response on Monday was to keep the procedure anyway, as his own decision on its merits and not as a correction of a fault the film does not show: Tice tracks the next eligible opener number and the situation folder beside him and is the one voice that tells him when the situation has overridden the opener; Bates gives quarterback and protection information only; Charlie Skalaski relays the clock, the timeouts, the next fourth-down decision point and incoming booth replay and challenge information; Stone makes every call and every decision. The second decision is the offensive plan: lead with Power, Counter and Outside Zone and use Ace, Trey, Wing and the shift to Empty early to make Indianapolis declare whether it stays big, substitutes or exposes a linebacker or safety on Kelce; the Inside Zone from Doubles an information call until the box earns it; the first Y-Cross with SOLID and the HALF version only against a clean count; Dagger confined to first-and-10, second-and-4-to-6 and third-and-6-or-more and removed from the third-and-1 card; one conditional MAX shot after the Power reaction is established and no second one because the first misses; the six-call two-point folder from Friday; Boot Flood Left off unless actual Wednesday work earned it back; Jones-Drew the lead back with Anderson prepared on the complete normal-down and protection menu so that a full series or selected snaps change no play family, and no touch quota.
