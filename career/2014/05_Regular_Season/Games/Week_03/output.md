## 4. Postgame media Q&A

**Stone:** We scored 38 and we are 3-0. We also gave up 21 in the fourth quarter with three corners in the locker room, and I gave them the ball back with 2:12 on the clock after three runs for 2 yards. That last part is mine. Go ahead.

**Reporter:** What do you see with your defense right now? Thirty-four points, 21 of them in the fourth quarter.

**Stone:** Thirteen through three quarters and two picks, both on third-and-long. Then 62, 66 and 85 yards on three of the last four drives. Jordan went out in the third, Aqib after the first of those three, Malcolm after the last one. I know who was on the field. I do not know yet what beat them, the call, the help or the man, and I will not name a player from the sideline.

**Reporter:** How big was that sequence at the end? You had it at your 36 with 2:42 left and you punt, and they go 85 yards.

**Stone:** Three runs, 2 yards, punt at 2:22. They scored at 1:01. If one of those runs goes for a first down the game is over. It did not, and I had an eleven-point lead with a secondary that had lost two starters, and I chose to put the ball in their hands. That is the decision I review first.

**Reporter:** What was the thought process on punting on fourth-and-8 with 2:22 left?

**Stone:** Fourth-and-8 from my 38, up eleven. A failed try gives them a field goal and a one-score game at midfield. I punted it to their 15. They went 85. I will look at it, and I will look at the three calls before it more than the punt.

**Reporter:** What did you think of Kirk's performance, the four touchdowns, his progression at all?

**Stone:** Eighteen of 31, four touchdowns, no sack, and he ran one in on third-and-goal. The Mesh from Bunch was the call of the day, and he hit Davante for 42 and Marcedes for 60 on it. The one throw I want back is the one for Montell on third-and-1 before the half, from our 49. I have not seen the read. The two picks last week and that one are the only turnovers he has had in three games with no sack today.

**Reporter:** Was Marcedes supposed to get the ball on the 60-yarder?

**Stone:** He was the read on that picture, and he ran it a long way. Second-and-10, same call that got Davante the 42 in the first half. Jerry's room gets credit for the spacing twice.

**Reporter:** Three defensive backs went down. Talib, Poyer, Butler. Anything you can tell us?

**Stone:** All three are head and neck and all three are in the independent process. Jordan after their three-and-out in the third, Aqib after the drive that made it 38-20, Malcolm after the last touchdown. The doctors see them, the dates are theirs, and none of them comes back until that process releases them.

**Reporter:** How is he?

**Stone:** Aqib walked off. That is all I have.

**Reporter:** Who was out there at the end? Prosinski?

**Stone:** A.J. took Jordan's spot by the chart, and Chris took Malcolm's for the two kneel-downs. I made no change to that order during the game and the chart carried it. What we do next week waits on tomorrow's exams.

**Reporter:** Allen had three touchdowns. Is that the one where you were supposed to switch or stay with it?

**Stone:** Nine catches, 140, three scores, from the tight end. We built the week to cap Hilton, and Hilton had 75. I cannot tell you from the sideline whether Allen beat a linebacker, a safety or the help, and that is the first cutup Romeo has tomorrow.

**Reporter:** Richardson had 133, the 47 in the second quarter. Is that a fit problem?

**Stone:** Nineteen for 133. The 47 came on second-and-15 from his 14 and got them to our 39, and they scored four plays later. We fit him from the base front with no extra man all day, on purpose, so Luck would not have Hilton and Wayne one-on-one. I do not know yet whether that one beat the force or the cutback. Paul had two for loss and Daryl one. The fit is on the film and not on the sheet.

**Reporter:** Andrew Hawkins had 165 kick-return yards and the 57 set up your first points. What did you see on the returns?

**Stone:** The 57 to their 40 is the one. We had been giving up those; today we got one. He fielded all six and brought the last one back 34 so we could kneel. Mike's units gave up 41 after we went up 38-13, and that is the one he grades first.

**Reporter:** 3-0, first in the division. What's your message to the team?

**Stone:** We are 3-0 and we go to San Diego with three corners in the protocol. The first thing I say tomorrow is about the last five minutes, not the first fifty-five.
