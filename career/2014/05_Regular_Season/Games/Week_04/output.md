## 4. Postgame media Q&A

**Stone:** One point. We kicked from 47 on the first drive and missed, we got three out of the takeaway, and we started the last drive at our 6 with no timeouts left. The decisions in there are mine. Go ahead.

**Reporter:** You got the Hawkins touchdown and then the stop. Did you think things were turning?

**Stone:** I thought we had a chance. They punted it to our 6 with 1:39 left, Adam caught the third-and-3 and we were at our 46. Then four balls on the ground.

**Reporter:** What did you make of the sequence at the end? Near the end there where you used your timeouts --

**Stone:** We spent two on their last possession and got the ball at our 6 with 1:39 and none left. I'll look at how those were spent.

**Reporter:** On fourth-and-4 at their 29 on the first drive, did you think maybe of going for it?

**Stone:** No score, first drive, fourth-and-4 at their 29. I kicked it and Josh missed from 47. Mike goes through the snap, the hold and the protection with him before anybody puts it on the kick.

**Reporter:** What was the thought process on going for it on fourth-and-2 at their 34?

**Stone:** Down eight, 5:55 left. Three points there still leaves us needing a touchdown. Maurice got 3 on the Counter and we scored six plays later.

**Reporter:** What's it like as a play caller on defense when they go 80, 74 and 75 for touchdowns?

**Stone:** The first one had a fourth-and-6 run for 26 and a third-and-13 run for 15 in it, and both kept it alive. Their other possessions got them nothing, and one of them ended with A.J.'s strip. The calls were mine, and which ones stays in the room.

**Reporter:** Talk about A.J. Bouye, the sack, the fumble. What can you say about him?

**Stone:** Third-and-11, he got to the quarterback, took the ball and came up with it at their 29. We got three out of it, and that part isn't his.

**Reporter:** How would you assess Kirk's performance today?

**Stone:** No turnovers. Twenty-four of 40, 267 and two touchdowns, and he got sacked three times, the first one on the first snap of the game backed up at our 10. The third-and-3 to Adam on the last drive is the one I'd show anybody. The last four didn't connect.

**Reporter:** Marcedes had eight targets, three catches, 8 yards. What have you seen from him?

**Stone:** Eight came his way. The Y-Cross is built around him and it went to Davante four times and to Marcedes twice. He lost 5 on a third-and-18. Whether he was open on the ones that didn't come off, I'll see on the tape.

**Reporter:** The delay to Lewis at the 20. Why go to it there?

**Stone:** It was on the card for that part of the field and we repped it Thursday. Incomplete, and we scored three plays later.

**Reporter:** What did you think of the push you were getting in the run game? Leaned on it as much as you could have late in the game?

**Stone:** Twenty-one runs for 107 and they didn't drop us for a loss on one of them. Maurice had the 20 on second-and-1 and the 3 on fourth-and-2. We threw it 40 times. That mix is what I called.

**Reporter:** Are any of those injuries today potentially serious?

**Stone:** Paul, Julian and Adrian were all looked at and all three finished. The trainers see them in the morning. That's what I have.

**Reporter:** In a game like this, does it damage your team's morale, spirit?

**Stone:** It shouldn't. We lost a one-point game on the road with the ball in our hands at the end. We're 3-1, and Pittsburgh is here Sunday.
