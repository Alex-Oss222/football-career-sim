## 1. Week setup and game preparation

**Opponent context.** San Diego came in 2-1 from three games of its own: Arizona 27, San Diego 10 at Arizona on Monday, September 8, San Diego 31, Seattle 12 at Qualcomm Stadium on September 14 and San Diego 24, Buffalo 21 at Buffalo on September 21 ([Week 1](../../League_Results/week_01.md), [Week 2](../../League_Results/week_02.md) and [Week 3](../../League_Results/week_03.md) roundups and their game books). Philip Rivers is 53 of 87 for 809 yards with seven touchdowns and four interceptions, and he has been sacked 12 times, four at Arizona, five against Seattle and three at Buffalo; six of the twelve are charged to King Dunlap's slot at left tackle, two each to D.J. Fluker's and Johnnie Troutman's, one each to Nick Hardwick's and Chad Rinehart's. The staff treated the six as a reason to test San Diego's left-side declarations and not as proof that Dunlap is the problem. Ryan Mathews has 52 carries for 322 and the touchdown against Seattle, 22 for 133 that day and 21 for 118 at Buffalo; an injury was logged against him at the end of San Diego's opening possession at Buffalo and he finished the game, all 70 snaps, but he carries an eighteen-day projection to October 9, and the staff prepared for Danny Woodhead (ten carries for 41 with a lost fumble at Buffalo) and Donald Brown behind him. Keenan Allen has 12 catches on 20 targets for 237 and a touchdown in each game; he carries a minor lower-extremity projection to September 27, and the staff treated his availability as unknown until San Diego's own list. Eddie Royal has seven catches for 150, the 49-yarder against Seattle the long one, Malcom Floyd ten for 91 on 20 targets, Seyi Ajirotutu five for 95 with a touchdown at Buffalo, and the tight ends Antonio Gates seven for 92 with a touchdown and Ladarius Green two for 28. The matchup the staff built the passing game around was the tight end against San Diego's coverage: Arizona's Rob Housler, Seattle's Zach Miller and Buffalo's Scott Chandler combined for 12 catches, 169 yards and two touchdowns on 22 targets. On defense Kendall Reyes has four sacks, three against Seattle, with Corey Liuget, Sean Lissemore and Brandon Flowers one each; Manti Te'o has 39 tackles, Flowers 30 with two interceptions, and Melvin Ingram two tackles for loss and the fumble he forced and recovered at Buffalo. Arizona ran for 247 yards in the opener, Seattle 179 and Buffalo 85, and the three opponents scored 60 points. Brown returns kickoffs (six for 119) and Royal punts (four for 30); Nick Novak is three of three on field goals and Mike Scifres has punted 13 times. What the staff did not have was San Diego's coverage and pressure choices: the three game books record the sack, the rusher and the slot, not the coverage call or how often San Diego sent more than four, so no percentage was built into the plan, and the opener was written to find out who carries the tight ends when twelve personnel spreads out.
