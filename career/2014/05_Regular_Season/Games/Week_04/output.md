## 1. Week setup and game preparation

Stone took over the defensive calls for the trip to San Diego. Romeo Crennel remained the coordinator and relayed the calls to Daryl Smith. The week began with Indianapolis's fourth-quarter touchdown drives and Jacksonville's failed four-minute possession, then moved to Quarters, Cover 6 and the four-man Replace pressures. Monday's relay work and Saturday's walkthrough rehearsed Stone switching between the offensive and defensive sheets.

The [Week 4 packet](Week%204%20Packet_%20at%20San%20Diego.md) also settled the backfield order: Maurice Jones-Drew led, C.J. Anderson relieved him by series or package, and Jonathan Grimes was third. Lewis and Kelce were to get more designed receiving work, with coverage deciding where Cousins threw. TE Delay and the Flea Flicker survived practice. The Throwback Screen was cut because its receiver, side and releasing blockers had never been specified; number 26 remained empty on the opener. The [frozen call sheet](call_sheet.json) contained 22 distinct calls.

San Diego entered 2-1. Rivers had been sacked twelve times, six against the left-tackle slot, giving the staff a place to begin its protection study. That distribution did not establish who had missed an assignment. The Chargers' first three opponents had found production through their tight ends, which informed Jacksonville's passing plan. With Mathews's availability in question, the defense also prepared for Woodhead and Brown.

Talib and Kelce were cleared Thursday and Adams on Friday. Poyer and Butler remained out. Nicks completed the full practice week Stone had required, but Stone had not decided whether to restore him at X. Adams kept that job. No transaction or game-day change followed the Saturday freeze.

## 3. Game

**Week 4: Jacksonville Jaguars at San Diego Chargers**

Jacksonville had the ball near midfield with a chance to win, but four straight incompletions ended its last possession. San Diego held on 21-20, handing the Jaguars their first loss. The Chargers finished all three of their red-zone trips with touchdowns. Jacksonville finished with two touchdowns and two field goals after missing its first attempt.

**First quarter.** Cousins was sacked on the opening snap, then found Adams for a third-down conversion that kept an eleven-play drive alive. Scobee missed from 47 yards. Bouye immediately gave Jacksonville another opportunity, stripping Rivers and recovering at the San Diego 29. The offense gained only seven yards before Scobee made a 40-yarder. San Diego's next drive nearly ended at its own 24, but Woodhead ran for 26 on fourth-and-6 and the Chargers carried the possession into the second quarter.

**Second quarter.** Royal's run on third-and-13 extended that drive again, and Woodhead scored from 19 yards. Jacksonville answered with its best possession of the half. Adams caught three passes for 66 yards, including a 29-yarder to the 1, and Jones-Drew caught the touchdown. The lead lasted one possession: Allen's 38-yard catch helped set up Gates's touchdown, putting San Diego ahead 14-10. The Chargers reached the Jacksonville 34 before halftime, but the clock expired without a kick.

**Third quarter.** Neither offense scored on its first possession. Posluszny's third-down sack then forced another San Diego punt, and Jones-Drew carried the next Jacksonville drive with two long catches and a third-and-8 run to the Chargers' 15. Liuget's sack and two incompletions stopped it there. Scobee's 36-yard field goal cut the deficit to one. San Diego answered with a sustained drive, using Allen downfield and Woodhead to keep moving on third down.

**Fourth quarter.** Allen's short touchdown finished that fourteen-play drive and made it 21-13. Jacksonville went backward on its next possession, then held San Diego without a first down despite giving it the ball at the Jaguars' 33. Starting at its own 12, Jacksonville drove 88 yards. Jones-Drew converted fourth-and-2, Adams moved the ball to the 7 on third-and-9, and Hawkins caught the touchdown with 2:53 left. Scobee's extra point made it 21-20. San Diego picked up two first downs before punting, leaving Jacksonville at its own 6 with 1:39 and no timeouts. Thielen's 33-yard catch brought the Jaguars to their 46. They did not complete another pass; the fourth-down throw for Hawkins fell incomplete with 35 seconds left.

## 4. Postgame media Q&A

**Reporter:** After Thielen gets you out near midfield, what happened on those last four throws?

**Stone:** They didn't connect. We had a chance there. Adam got us out from our end, and we couldn't finish the drive.

**Reporter:** You got the ball back with no timeouts. Would you handle that sequence differently?

**Stone:** I need to look at it. We started at the 6 with 1:39 and didn't have one left.

**Reporter:** The opening drive got to their 29. Did you consider leaving the offense out there?

**Stone:** I sent the field goal team out on fourth-and-4. Josh missed it. Mike will go through the whole operation with him, the snap and the hold too, before we put it all on Josh.

**Reporter:** Later you went on fourth-and-2. What changed?

**Stone:** We were down eight with less than six minutes left. A field goal still leaves us needing a touchdown. Maurice converted, and we finished that drive.

**Reporter:** Your first day calling the defense, they had three long touchdown drives. What bothered you about those?

**Stone:** On the first one, they ran for a first down on fourth-and-6 and again on third-and-13. We had chances to get off the field. The calls were mine.

**Reporter:** What did you have called on those two runs?

**Stone:** I'm keeping that in the room.

**Reporter:** Bouye gave you the ball at their 29. How much more did you need from that possession?

**Stone:** More than three. A.J. got to Rivers, knocked it out and recovered it. He did his part.

**Reporter:** How did Kirk play, apart from the finish?

**Stone:** He took care of the ball and threw two touchdowns. We gave up three sacks, starting on the first play. That throw to Adam on third-and-3 at the end gave us a chance. We didn't complete the next four.

**Reporter:** You wanted more from the tight ends this week. Lewis had eight targets but only eight yards. Why didn't that produce more?

**Stone:** We threw his way. I need to see whether Marcedes was open on the ones we didn't complete before I can tell you why.

**Reporter:** Maurice was getting yards. Could you have stayed with the run more?

**Stone:** He had the long run and the fourth-down conversion on that last touchdown drive. We didn't have a run for a loss all day. We threw forty times, and that mix was mine.

**Reporter:** What have you been told about Paul, Julian and Adrian?

**Stone:** All three were looked at and finished. The trainers see them again in the morning. That's all I have.

**Reporter:** Does a loss like this take anything out of the team?

**Stone:** It shouldn't. We had the ball with a chance to win a one-point game on the road. We're 3-1. Pittsburgh comes here Sunday.
