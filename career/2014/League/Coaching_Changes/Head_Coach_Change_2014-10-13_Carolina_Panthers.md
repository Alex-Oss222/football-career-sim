# Head Coach Change: Carolina Panthers

**Event date:** Monday, October 13, 2014

**Resolved on:** Monday, October 13, 2014

**Involving:** The Carolina Panthers and their head coach, Ron Rivera.

**What happened:** The Carolina Panthers made a change at head coach on October 13, 2014, the day after their sixth game of the season, which left them 2-4. An interim head coach from the club's staff takes over.

**Stone's answer:** No response required.

**Final decision:** The Carolina Panthers' own decision.

**Outcome:** Executed.

**Changes effective:** From October 13, 2014, an interim head coach from the Carolina Panthers' staff leads the club for the rest of the season.

**Outstanding issue:** None.
