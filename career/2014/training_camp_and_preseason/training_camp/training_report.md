## Legal work, participation and medical method

- **Report dates.** The league published the 32 clubs' camp report dates on July 14 (library item C26, publication date confirmed). Jacksonville's dates are the historical dates selected under Entry 101 and cited in the [plan](staff_plan.md#historical-camp-sequence-and-simulation-stops): rookies July 21, veterans July 24, first practice July 25, first full pads July 30, scrimmage August 2. They satisfy rule R8 of the [league calendar research](../../../../library/2014_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md): a veteran may not be required to report earlier than 15 days before the first preseason game or July 15, whichever is later, and with Tampa Bay on August 8 that is July 24 exactly; rookies and first-year players may not be required to report earlier than seven days before the veteran date, and July 21 is inside that lead. Any quarterback or injured-player exception is unverified in the library, so the veteran quarterbacks reported with the veterans. The report and first-practice dates are on the plan's cited club sources; the first-full-pads and scrimmage dates are the plan's selected dates and are not separately verified in the library, so they are carried as selected, not confirmed.
- **Who reported when.** A first-year player is a player under contract with no accrued season. Of the six reserve/future players, Bray, Jerrell Jackson and Long have no credited season and reported July 21 with the 26 rookies; Murphy, D'Anthony Smith and Blake each carry one credited season and their accrual is unresolved in the [contract status register](../../finances/player_contracts/contract_status.md), so the club did not require them before July 24. The remaining 45 signed players reported July 24. Every signed player reported on his date; no absence is on record.
- **Unsigned tenders.** Bradfield, Clemons, Brown and Pasztor had not signed their tenders by July 29. They are not under contract, and a player without a contract may not report to camp or practice until he signs; the library's calendar files record the tender deadlines and no separate camp-participation article, so the operative basis is contract status, and Stone's May 1 instruction keeps them outside the program and the social calendar until they sign. No work, meeting or invitation is recorded for them, and no "fell behind" language attaches. Caldwell's office owns the contracts.
- **Controlled roster:** 78 throughout: 74 signed and four unsigned tenders, of the 90-player limit. No transaction between June 28 and July 29.
- **Physicals and the entry standard.** The medical staff examined each reporting group on its report day (July 21 and July 24) and refreshed football-use instructions for camp. No restriction was communicated for any player. Cornelius Lucas's foot check was consistent with the June 16 physical: no communicated restriction; the performance-staff review item is carried, because nothing on record resolves it. The position-adjusted conditioning test was written before any player took it, applied consistently by football job, and produced no failure requiring an individualized retest plan; the test is an entry standard, not a depth verdict.
- **Acclimation and contact.** The [calendar](../../calendar.md) records the first three camp days as required acclimation with no contact; the library does not record the helmet and shell sequence within them, so it is not stated here. July 25, 26 and 27 were the acclimation days. July 28 and 29 ran short of full pads under the club's schedule, whose first full-pad practice is July 30. No contact evidence is claimed for any of the five days: every observation below is of the same class as the spring's, recognition, communication, timing and spacing against an opposing look, and cannot establish blocking, a won rush, a finished tackle or a physical matchup. Off days, hours and drill rules were applied within the calendar; no day is invented or removed from the plan's July 25 to 29 interval.
- **Injuries.** The program has no engine injury draw for practices. The 2013 camp record ([2013 output](../../../2013/offseason/training_camp/output.md)) recorded only what the medical staff communicated and drew nothing, and the same method applies: no injury and no new restriction was communicated July 21 to 29, so none is recorded.
- **Staff.** Tice coordinated the offense, Crennel the defense and Westhoff special teams, with Stone present throughout; the emergency practice lead was not used. Consultation cadence: the 25-minute meeting July 25, the 15-minute post-session meetings each day, and the full staff review after the third practice, July 27. The player council's fortnightly cadence resumes in camp; no council meeting is recorded July 25 to 29.
