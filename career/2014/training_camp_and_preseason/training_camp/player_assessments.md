# Jacksonville | Camp player assessments | Through August 4, 2014

The [first five practices](training_report.md) showed that the taught spring work survived the break and a change of formations and personnel; those are non-contact findings. The padded practices of July 30, August 1 and August 4 added physical evidence at controlled contact, and the August 2 scrimmage added series work with the chains, the play clock, substitution on the move and the coaches off the field, under the same script: blocks secured and lost, releases won and late, fits made and corrected, the instructed tackle fitted and wrapped, with no period live to the ground and no score kept. These assessments follow the [assessment method](../../../../foundation/templates/training_camp_and_preseason/player_assessment.md): the taught job first, then the recorded work, with knowing the call, recognizing the picture, executing the technique and winning the matchup kept as separate findings. Nine of the eleven padded corrections held on their delayed retest and two recurred and were corrected again with one clean repeat since; none is closed, and the preseason is the next test.

## Offense

### Cousins, Henne, Wilson, Bray and Shaw | Quarterback operation

Cousins operated the situation and progressed beyond the first answer when it was removed, on August 1 did the same on the clock with a live rush in front of him, and in the scrimmage did it with the call arriving from the sideline, the personnel changing on the clock and no coach on the field: he snapped legally through the changes, made the boundary-or-middle decisions and went to the checkdown when the paired Cover 3 took the back's vertical away. Henne handled the two-minute and four-minute menus without another explanation of the situation and operated the mixed series; its one error was the second tight end's late substitution, which he answered by holding the snap until the group was legal, and Tice's point went to the substitution, not to him. Wilson retained his Sprint Flood depth and ran a reserve series on the core; the record gives Bray and Shaw one reduced-menu series each, not a comparative verdict. What practice could not test is a defense that does not stop at the whistle; that is the preseason question.

### Bitonio and Norwell; Turner | Left guard and right guard

The shared identification is settled at contact speed: HALF went to the overload against the mug and the five-man pressure on every recorded padded team rep, Brewster resetting the point, with Norwell correct from either guard and from the alternative arrangement rehearsed August 1. The physical findings are mixed in the way Block 2 expects. Bitonio anchored against Marks on the inside combination and over-set once, opening the inside lane; Yarno corrected the set angle and inside hand, and the repeat held, including at right guard. Norwell carried the ACE combination with Brewster through to the linebacker on the recorded Power reps and recovered after losing initial leverage once to Miller in protection. Turner held his anchor against Mosley and C.J. Wilson, lost one one-on-one rep to Donald's first step with his hands late, and held the repeat; the Brewster-Turner combination moved Donald on the recorded reps and was split once. The scrimmage was the repeated work the room wanted: Bitonio's set angle held in the early and the late series against Marks; Norwell carried the combination through in the mixed series and was walked back once by Marks late, recovering; Turner's hands were on time against Donald on the recorded reps and the Brewster-Turner combination moved Donald with one split, as on July 30. August 4 added nothing new. Each competitor has a secured combination and an occasional lost rep over three padded days and a scrimmage, in the same mixed shape; Stone kept the starting points at the block review because the record contains no football reason for a move. The plan's "struggles" trigger for Turner was not met. Yarno and Tice now need the combinations against an opponent and a collapsing pocket before the guard competition can be assessed on that basis.

### Henne and Linsley | Relief protection pairing

Linsley's point with Henne was on time in every camp rep, including at contact speed and, in the scrimmage, behind the first tackles against the first defense with the call from the sideline; he lost initial leverage once to Mosley one-on-one on July 30 and recovered, his anchor held on the August 1 retest, and in the later scrimmage series he lost initial leverage once to Marks with the rep whistled at the quarterback's depth, a lost rep under the script and not a sack. That is useful evidence for the pairing's communication and its first evidence under a pocket that was moving. Shatley and Larsen held their recorded combination blocks with Wilson and no correction. Protection over a game with a collapsing pocket is untested.

### Nicks and Adams; Adams and Hawkins; Thielen | WR1, WR3 and the H job

Nicks held the taught landmarks against changed leverage and, with press lawful, won leverage against Talib with his hands while keeping the quick-concept timing, in the padded periods and in the scrimmage series; Talib re-routed him on other reps. Adams kept his assignment while having mixed release-path results against Talib and Verner, and with contact won the release while losing the timing, the extra step the book's release rule forbids; Drake corrected it, on August 1 he kept the timing against Harris and lost one release to Talib, and in the scrimmage and on August 4 the timing held on every recorded rep with his releases against Talib still mixed. Hawkins corrected the short Y-Cross dig twice for two different causes, spacing on July 26 and Poyer's jam on July 30, held the depth on both delayed retests and through two scrimmage series against Poyer; Drake kept the causes separate. Thielen carried the H assignment against Poyer and kept his crossing depth through contact and in series. Drake has evidence about spacing, assignment reliability and release technique; three padded days and a scrimmage separate no one at WR1 or WR3.

### Jones-Drew, Jonathan Grimes and Anderson | Track, protection and ball security

Jones-Drew pressed the Power landmark and read the puller, not open grass, on the recorded reps, and his ball security held through the controlled strip attempts in the padded periods and in series. Anderson bounced one Inside Zone rep before pressing the interior; Spencer corrected it, and the August 1 and scrimmage reps were correct. Jonathan Grimes's track was correct with no correction. The scrimmage gave the backs' protection its first individual findings: Jones-Drew scanned to the five-man pressure's extra rusher and fitted him on the recorded reps; Anderson identified the same rusher and lost the block once to Telvin Smith, a physical loss with the assignment right.

### Kelce; Lewis; Brate, Jensen and Hoskins | The attached block

Kelce's attached blocking, the assessment promised in 2013, now has a block of camp evidence. His assignment and first step were right on the Power down block and the TREY combination with Johnson; his pad level rose late in the period and the closed end got into his chest on the later reps, the base and hand item from 2013. Zernhelt corrected the base; on August 1 the early reps held and the late-period rise recurred against Te'o-Nesheim; in the scrimmage, the repeated-effort test, the base held through the first inside-run series and rose again in the late series against Te'o-Nesheim and Davis, the third day; on August 4, in a shorter period after the day off, it held early and late for the first time, and Zernhelt noted that the period was shorter than the scrimmage's series run. The role the evidence supports is unchanged, TE2 with movement value, and the attached job late in a run of series is the open repeated-effort question; the preseason is the next run of series. Lewis sustained his attached blocks against Davis on the recorded reps and in series. Brate sustained the assigned block against the reserve ends on every recorded day; Jensen and Hoskins were assignment-correct with mixed finish, and Jensen's late arrival as the second tight end on one 12-personnel call in the scrimmage is a substitution-timing finding, not a blocking one. TE3 remains open.

## Defense

### Marks, Miller, Mosley, C.J. Wilson and Donald | The interior fit and block defeat

The Under fit was named before every padded snap, from the sideline signal in the scrimmage, and held on the recorded Power and Inside Zone reps: Marks owned the weak B-gap and Miller controlled the shade. Marks also produced the interior's two scrimmage wins in protection, walking Norwell back once late and taking initial leverage from Linsley once, both recovered or whistled. Donald's first step, the spring item, is a contact finding: it won one one-on-one rep against Turner on July 30, and the Brewster-Turner combination moved him off the strong A-gap on the recorded reps with one split on July 30 and one in the scrimmage; Pleasant's point was pad level on the double team. Mosley and C.J. Wilson were anchored by the guards they faced on the recorded reps, and Mosley took initial leverage from Linsley once before he recovered. None of this is a place.

### Mincey and Branch; Davis, Te'o-Nesheim, Edebali and Jeffcoat | Edge 1 and the edge rotation

Mincey kept contain on every recorded rep against the live set, in the padded periods and in series, and won the edge once a day against Johnson with the long arm. Branch produced the better isolated wins, beating Johnson around the corner on July 30 and inside in the scrimmage, and twice left the lane doing it, on a July 30 team rep and late in his Edge 1 scrimmage series, when Cousins stepped up through the open escape lane before the whistle; the book grades both as pressure errors. Crennel made the point after each, and after each Branch kept contain on the retest while still producing pressure, August 1 and August 4. PEEL was executed correctly by both. Mincey is the steadier lane and Branch the better isolated win with a lane item that has recurred once; the competition stays open, and the preseason is the next test. Davis and Te'o-Nesheim set the edge on BOX against the attached blocks on every recorded day; Edebali and Jeffcoat won and lost in their lanes, Edebali beating Lucas around the edge once in the reserve series; no reserve place is set.

### Posluszny, Daryl Smith, Stanford, Moore and Telvin Smith | Fits, the relay and the back matchup

Posluszny and Daryl Smith fit the strong B and weak A as the Under fit assigns, and the relay worked through Smith, Posluszny and Stanford with the preceding communicator removed from the drill, and in the scrimmage from the sideline signal with the clock running, each relay complete. Crennel tested the linebacker-on-back vertical in man: Daryl Smith carried Jones-Drew's wheel with the taught leverage on three days and Lowery's post help arrived where the rule puts it each time. Stanford held his wheel leverage once against Jonathan Grimes on July 30 and lost the top of the route once; Bush's correction was the June 18 leverage point, the August 1 reps held against Anderson, the scrimmage did not present the matchup in his series because the script called the paired Cover 3, and on August 4 he held the leverage at the top against Jonathan Grimes in the group period. Moore arrived as the next support defender on SPILL. Telvin Smith fit Counter with the flow on July 30 and the wrap came through the gap he left; Bush corrected the puller read, and he fit the same misdirection correctly on August 1 and in two scrimmage series, one beside the regulars; in protection work he also beat Anderson's block once as the extra rusher. Christian Jones and Todd Davis were assignment-correct in comparable reps with mixed physical results. Fit and wrap to the whistle was executed as instructed; no tackle to the ground was scored for anyone, by design.

### Talib, Verner, Harris, Poyer, Butler, Thomas and Phillips | Press, recovery and the rat

Rambo's motion trigger, Harris's rub path, Poyer's rat vision and Stanford's wheel leverage held on their camp retests, and the padded days and the scrimmage added technique in series: Talib re-routed Nicks at the line on recorded reps and lost others to Nicks's hands; Verner lost leverage to Adams at the line once on July 30, recovered to the dig, and held his leverage in the scrimmage with one more recovery; Harris held his rub path with contact and through series; Poyer's rat vision held under the live five-man rush and in series. Butler's corrected transition from press was late once against Hurns, Oden corrected it, the August 1 rep was on time, and in the scrimmage's press series it was on time against Hurns and Gabriel. Thomas and Phillips were assignment-correct with mixed results. The reserve defense's one scrimmage error was a late nickel substitution against a personnel change, handled by Crennel's sideline call to stay in base with the paired Cover 3, which the group played correctly; it is a group timing finding, not a coverage bust and not an individual's. These are particular jobs in the called coverage, not a claim that any player can cover every matchup. Rambo's and Lowery's support fits were correct. The corner places of Brent Grimes and Ball and the reserve corner remain open.

## Special teams and reserve places

Cain and Kreiter both kept the operation clean, and Anger put the recorded situational punts to the instructed side. Against a controlled rush on July 30 both snappers' operations held and the interior protection passed an A-gap rusher late on one recorded rep; Westhoff corrected the exchange, the August 1 reps were clean in either snapping order, and in the scrimmage, at the actual changes of possession with Kreiter snapping first and Cain later, the exchange was correct on every recorded rep; August 4 the same in either order. The emergency snapper path was executed inside a series with Cain removed by script. Coverage lanes held against a hold-up in the padded periods and in series; the punts were fielded under the fit-and-wrap rule and no return is scored. The field-goal operation held against the rush at the end of two scrimmage series. Neither snapper has separated; no returner is designated, and the question now waits for the preseason.

The reserve groups received meaningful padded work against veterans and their own scrimmage series, with mixed series beside regulars for Linsley, Norwell, Branch, Posluszny, Stanford and Telvin Smith, and assignment-correct observations with mixed physical results for rookies who had no separate correction recorded: Lucas's set landmark held against the rookie rushers and in his reserve series, and he was beaten around the edge once by Branch and once by Edebali, with his foot review item unchanged; Hurns and Gabriel had mixed release results in press and off. There is no basis to turn silence about an individual into failure or exceptional performance. All five named competitions remain open after the block review, and no reserve place was awarded.
