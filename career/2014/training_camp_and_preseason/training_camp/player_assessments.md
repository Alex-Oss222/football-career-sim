The [first five practices](training_report.md) showed that the taught spring work survived the break and a change of formations and personnel; those are non-contact findings. The two padded practices of July 30 and August 1 added the first physical evidence at controlled contact: blocks secured and lost, releases won and late, fits made and corrected, the instructed tackle fitted and wrapped, with no period live to the ground. These assessments follow the [assessment method](../../../../foundation/templates/training_camp_and_preseason/player_assessment.md): the taught job first, then the recorded work, with knowing the call, recognizing the picture, executing the technique and winning the matchup kept as separate findings. Each padded correction has one clean repeat behind it and none is closed; the scrimmage and preseason are the delayed retests.

## Offense

### Cousins, Henne, Wilson, Bray and Shaw | Quarterback operation

Cousins operated the situation and progressed beyond the first answer when it was removed, and on August 1 did the same on the clock with a live rush in front of him, uncontacted by the script. Henne also handled the two-minute and four-minute menus without another explanation of the situation. Wilson retained his Sprint Flood depth; the record gives Bray and Shaw reduced-menu work, not a comparative game verdict. What pads could not test is a defense that does not stop at the whistle; that is the preseason question.

### Bitonio and Norwell; Turner | Left guard and right guard

The shared identification is settled at contact speed: HALF went to the overload against the mug and the five-man pressure on every recorded padded team rep, Brewster resetting the point, with Norwell correct from either guard and from the alternative arrangement rehearsed August 1. The physical findings are mixed in the way Block 2 expects. Bitonio anchored against Marks on the inside combination and over-set once, opening the inside lane; Yarno corrected the set angle and inside hand, and the repeat held, including at right guard. Norwell carried the ACE combination with Brewster through to the linebacker on the recorded Power reps and recovered after losing initial leverage once to Miller in protection. Turner held his anchor against Mosley and C.J. Wilson, lost one one-on-one rep to Donald's first step with his hands late, and held the repeat; the Brewster-Turner combination moved Donald on the recorded reps and was split once. Each competitor has a secured combination and one corrected lost rep. The plan's "struggles" trigger for Turner was not met, and the August 1 arrangement with Bitonio at right guard was an evaluation opportunity, not a change. Yarno and Tice still need the combinations under repeated work and a collapsing pocket before the guard competition can be assessed on that basis.

### Henne and Linsley | Relief protection pairing

Linsley's point with Henne was on time in every camp rep, including at contact speed; he lost initial leverage once to Mosley one-on-one and recovered, and his anchor held on the August 1 retest. That is useful evidence for the pairing's communication and a first physical finding. Shatley and Larsen held their recorded combination blocks with Wilson and no correction. Protection over a game with a collapsing pocket is untested.

### Nicks and Adams; Adams and Hawkins; Thielen | WR1, WR3 and the H job

Nicks held the taught landmarks against changed leverage and, with press lawful, won leverage against Talib with his hands while keeping the quick-concept timing; Talib re-routed him on other reps. Adams kept his assignment while having mixed release-path results against Talib and Verner, and with contact won the release while losing the timing, the extra step the book's release rule forbids; Drake corrected it, and on August 1 he kept the timing against Harris and lost one release to Talib. Hawkins corrected the short Y-Cross dig twice for two different causes, spacing on July 26 and Poyer's jam on July 30, and held the depth on both delayed retests; Drake kept the causes separate. Thielen carried the H assignment against Poyer and kept his crossing depth through contact. Drake has evidence about spacing, assignment reliability and now release technique; two padded days separate no one at WR1 or WR3.

### Jones-Drew, Jonathan Grimes and Anderson | Track, protection and ball security

Jones-Drew pressed the Power landmark and read the puller, not open grass, on the recorded reps, and his ball security held through the controlled strip attempts. Anderson bounced one Inside Zone rep before pressing the interior; Spencer corrected it, and the August 1 reps were correct. Jonathan Grimes's track was correct with no correction. The backs' protection against a live rush was run inside the team periods with no individual finding recorded.

### Kelce; Lewis; Brate, Jensen and Hoskins | The attached block

Kelce's attached blocking, the assessment promised in 2013, has its first camp evidence. His assignment and first step were right on the Power down block and the TREY combination with Johnson; his pad level rose late in the period and the closed end got into his chest on the later reps, the base and hand item from 2013. Zernhelt corrected the base; on August 1 the early reps held and the late-period rise recurred against Te'o-Nesheim. The role the evidence supports now is the one he has, TE2 with movement value, and the attached job is a repeated-effort question, not a one-rep one. Lewis sustained his attached blocks against Davis on the recorded reps. Brate sustained the assigned block against the reserve ends on both days; Jensen and Hoskins were assignment-correct with mixed finish and no correction beyond the general teaching. TE3 remains open.

## Defense

### Marks, Miller, Mosley, C.J. Wilson and Donald | The interior fit and block defeat

The Under fit was named before every padded snap and held on the recorded Power and Inside Zone reps: Marks owned the weak B-gap and Miller controlled the shade. Donald's first step, the spring item, is now a contact finding: it won one one-on-one rep against Turner, and the Brewster-Turner combination moved him off the strong A-gap on the recorded reps and was split once; Pleasant's point was pad level on the double team. Mosley and C.J. Wilson were anchored by the guards they faced on the recorded reps, and Mosley took initial leverage from Linsley once before he recovered. None of this is a place.

### Mincey and Branch; Davis, Te'o-Nesheim, Edebali and Jeffcoat | Edge 1 and the edge rotation

Mincey kept contain on every recorded rep against the live set and won the edge once against Johnson with the long arm. Branch produced the better isolated win, beating Johnson around the corner, and on one July 30 team rep left the lane doing it, which the book grades as a pressure error; Crennel made the point once, and on August 1 Branch kept contain at Edge 1 and still produced pressure on a recorded rep. PEEL was executed correctly by both. Mincey is the steadier lane and Branch the better isolated win; the competition stays open. Davis and Te'o-Nesheim set the edge on BOX against the attached blocks; Edebali and Jeffcoat won and lost one-on-one reps in their lanes; no reserve place is set.

### Posluszny, Daryl Smith, Stanford, Moore and Telvin Smith | Fits, the relay and the back matchup

Posluszny and Daryl Smith fit the strong B and weak A as the Under fit assigns, and the relay worked through Smith, Posluszny and Stanford with the preceding communicator removed from the drill. Crennel tested the linebacker-on-back vertical in man for the first time: Daryl Smith carried Jones-Drew's wheel with the taught leverage and Lowery's post help arrived where the rule puts it. Stanford held his wheel leverage once against Jonathan Grimes and lost the top of the route once; Bush's correction was the June 18 leverage point, and the August 1 reps held against Anderson. Moore arrived as the next support defender on SPILL. Telvin Smith fit Counter with the flow on July 30 and the wrap came through the gap he left; Bush corrected the puller read, and he fit the same misdirection correctly on August 1. Christian Jones and Todd Davis were assignment-correct in comparable reps. Fit and wrap to the whistle was executed as instructed; no tackle to the ground was scored for anyone, by design.

### Talib, Verner, Harris, Poyer, Butler, Thomas and Phillips | Press, recovery and the rat

Rambo's motion trigger, Harris's rub path, Poyer's rat vision and Stanford's wheel leverage held on their camp retests, and the padded days added technique: Talib re-routed Nicks at the line on recorded reps and lost others to Nicks's hands; Verner lost leverage to Adams at the line once and recovered to the dig; Harris held his rub path with contact; Poyer's rat vision held under the live five-man rush. Butler's corrected transition from press was late once against Hurns, Oden corrected it, and the August 1 rep was on time. Thomas and Phillips were assignment-correct with mixed results. These are particular jobs in the called coverage, not a claim that any player can cover every matchup. Rambo's and Lowery's support fits were correct. The corner places of Brent Grimes and Ball and the reserve corner remain open.

## Special teams and reserve places

Cain and Kreiter both kept the operation clean, and Anger put the recorded situational punts to the instructed side. Against a controlled rush on July 30 both snappers' operations held and the interior protection passed an A-gap rusher late on one recorded rep; Westhoff corrected the exchange and the August 1 reps were clean in either snapping order. Coverage lanes held against a hold-up; several players fielded punts in the catch work. Neither snapper has separated; no returner is designated.

The reserve groups received meaningful padded work against veterans, with assignment-correct observations and mixed physical results for rookies who had no separate correction recorded: Lucas's set landmark held against the rookie rushers and he was beaten around the edge once by Branch, with his foot review item unchanged; Hurns and Gabriel had mixed release results. There is no basis to turn silence about an individual into failure or exceptional performance. All five named competitions remain open, and no reserve place was awarded. The [position review](../assessments/position_battles.md) connects each question to the next useful test. These are assessments through August 1; the coach fitness policy has no completed test result in this record.
