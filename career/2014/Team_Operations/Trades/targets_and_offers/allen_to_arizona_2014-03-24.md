Next action: Caldwell's office, at Posluszny's medical review.

## April 7, 2014: Allen trade with Arizona (completed)

The agreement of March 24 closed under its own conditions, with no further negotiation. Paul Posluszny was cleared on April 5. Allen passed Arizona's physical on April 7 and the trade was processed that day. Arizona's unconditional 2015 fourth is Jacksonville's. See [completed trades](../completed_trades/trades.md).
