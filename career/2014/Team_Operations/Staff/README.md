## Who calls what

| Phase | Calls on game day | Prepares | Also |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offense | Alex Stone, since the 2013 season | Mike Tice |  |
| Defense | Alex Stone, since September 28, 2014 | Romeo Crennel | Romeo Crennel sends in the call and the between-snap information; Daryl Smith declares the front |
| Special teams | Mike Westhoff, since February 11, 2014 | Mike Westhoff |  |

Before: Romeo Crennel called the defense from the 2013 season until September 28, 2014. Alex Stone called special teams on an interim basis from January 12, 2014 until February 11, 2014. Alan Lowry called special teams from the 2013 season until January 12, 2014.

Information to the caller on game day: Mike Tice, the offensive situation and the next eligible opening call; Jeremy Bates, quarterback and protection information; Charlie Skalaski, clock, timeout, fourth-down and challenge information.

What Alex Stone keeps in each phase, whoever calls it:

- Offense: final offensive menu, game-plan approval, situational direction, game management.
- Defense: final team principles, coordinator supervision, major situational/game-management decisions; the defensive calls.
- Special teams: final team-level priorities, roster/use decisions within Stone's authority, consequential game management.

If Stone is out: Mike Tice, then Romeo Crennel runs practice (run the approved schedule, keep the periods on time, coordinate the three phases and make ordinary situational adjustments; no depth-chart, install, role, disciplinary or personnel change). Game-day caller: not appointed.
