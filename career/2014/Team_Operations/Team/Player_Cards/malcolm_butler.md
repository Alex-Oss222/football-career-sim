## Staff assessment

Malcolm Butler is a rookie corner from a Division II program who has played in three games for Jacksonville. His strengths are his ball skills, change of direction and competitive press style, with kick-return experience in college, and in camp the correction of a late press transition held on the next rep. The jump from Division II competition is his biggest question, and his strength and technique are below an NFL starter's level.
