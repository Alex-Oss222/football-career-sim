## Staff assessment

Paul Posluszny has been a viable NFL starter at middle linebacker for Jacksonville, dressing for 11 regular-season games in 2013 and eight so far in 2014. He reads the run well, sets the fits, communicates the front and finishes tackles inside. He is stronger working downhill than in isolated man coverage, and his recovery range is limited.
