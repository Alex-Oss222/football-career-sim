## Staff assessment

Andrew Hawkins has been active for eight games in his first season with Jacksonville, with 12 catches for 143 yards, 25 kickoff returns and 15 punt returns. He is a quick underneath receiver who finds space, changes direction well and adds yards after the catch, and his best use is on slot routes. Releasing against press, contested catches and blocking are his weakest areas; in camp the depth of his dig held on later checks after it came up short once on spacing and once against Jordan Poyer's jam.
