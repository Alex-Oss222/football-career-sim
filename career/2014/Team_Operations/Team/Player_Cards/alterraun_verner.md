## Staff assessment

Alterraun Verner has played in six games in his first season with Jacksonville. He is an instinctive coverage corner who wins with route recognition, positioning and ball awareness, at his best in zone and off coverage. The staff's starting view favors his coverage judgment over matchups that depend on recovery speed, and strength, block defeat and penalty discipline are the weakest parts of his game.
