# Jacksonville 2014 cap changes

These are the working balances recorded when each transaction closed. They preserve the calculation at that date; they are not additional charges and do not replace the [current cap worksheet](../salary_cap/cap_worksheet.md) or the [contract schedules](../player_contracts/contracts.md). None was certified cap room. The league cap was $133,000,000; carryover, reserves and the worksheet's unresolved items remain separate.

The March 12 balance includes Talib's March 11 agreement and was closed with Tate's March 12 decision. The March 18 receiver balance includes Nicks's March 14 signing and Hawkins's March 18 binding contract. These are accounting checkpoints, not changed signing dates. The Washington pick exchange later on March 31 moved no player contract and left that day's cap balances unchanged.

The successive Top-51 exclusions were one $420,000 minimum on March 12; three $420,000 minimums after the receiver agreements; three $420,000 minimums and one $495,000 minimum after Te'o-Nesheim and after the Cain/Nwaneri transactions; two $420,000 minimums after the March 24 trades; three $420,000 minimums and one $495,000 minimum after the March 28 signings; and two $420,000 minimums after the Indianapolis trade and after the Henne/Allen transactions. After the rookie signings, 28 lowest bases totaling $12,060,000 fell outside the count. Rackley's departure brought one $495,000 base back inside: 27 displaced bases totaling $11,565,000. Bonus proration remained counted.

The rookie class added $15,440,282 in gross 2014 cap charges and $4,220,282 after Top-51 displacement. Its May 11 future commitments were $123,275,736 for 2015, $84,131,574 for 2016, $48,550,284 for 2017 and $18,900,000 for 2018. Those same future totals remained after Rackley's trade because his contract ended in 2014.

Other recorded future totals: after Monroe, Marks and Verner, 2016 $43,811,292, 2017 $24,600,000 and 2018 $9,100,000; after Talib, 2016 $53,111,292, 2017 $34,400,000 and 2018 $18,900,000; after Nicks and Hawkins, 2016 $56,511,292 and 2017 $37,800,000; after Te'o-Nesheim, 2016 $64,011,292. No unexecuted future signing was included.

## Reconstruction and rollover history

At the February 28 contract reconstruction, the 36 continuing contracts and six futures carried $56,140,166 of sourced or reconstructed 2014 charges and about $35,977,655 of estimates. Monroe's separate $11,654,000 tender brought the planning subtotal to about $103,771,821. Wilson and Jonathan Grimes then had $1.3 million of conditional retention allowances outside signed totals. The subsequent adopted completion terms replaced that uncertainty: 38 continuing signed players, six futures and Monroe's unsigned tender carried $105,071,821 in scheduled 2014 player cap, $74,805,137 in 2015 and $19,211,292 in 2016. The [original reconstruction](../../../../../library/2014_jaguars_original_contract_reconstruction.md) and [completion research](../../../../../library/2014_jaguars_contract_completion.md) own the assumptions; neither was a new signing or extension.

The surviving 2014 dead-money reconstruction counted Bray's $51,675 separately from his new futures contract. Marshall's original rookie bonus had accelerated after his 2012 waiver; Lane's original deal ended in 2013; Stanback's replacement contract was modeled without a signing bonus. Those three contributed no new 2014 bonus charge. Blackmon's gross schedule and suspension-guarantee treatment remained distinct from the still-unreconciled forfeiture credit.

On March 31 the working estimate of unused 2013 room was corrected to about $5.33 million to $6.00 million, replacing the earlier $5.4 million to $6.6 million note. It added a range, not a certified credit. With that estimate, the March 31 working difference was about $16.06 million to $16.73 million; April 7 $16,660,596 to $17,330,596; May 11 $12,440,314 to $13,110,314; and May 12 $13,376,314 to $14,046,314. The full calculation and unresolved adjustments remain in the [rollover calculation](../salary_cap/cap_worksheet.md#2013-rollover).
