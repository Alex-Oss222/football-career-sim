# Jacksonville current medical participation

**Through September 13, 2014.** Maurice Jones-Drew was reassessed September 8 with no change and cleared September 12, his projected date, without practising that day; he took the Saturday walkthrough in full and his game-day status is Stone's decision ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#september-8-to-12-jones-drew-reassessed-and-cleared-harris-and-lowery-to-reserveinjured)). Mike Harris was placed on ordinary reserve/injured and Dwight Lowery on reserve/injured designated for return on September 8 ([September 8 transactions](../Team/Roster/transactions_2014-09-08.md)); Alan Ball's September 8 physical communicated no restriction and he practiced in full all week. The Washington practice week produced no injury and no new restriction. The league's Week 2 report lists Nicks, Hoskins and Jones-Drew as non-participants Wednesday through Friday: Nicks and Hoskins out, Jones-Drew questionable. Earlier: the Week 1 game at Philadelphia generated three Jacksonville injuries ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#september-7-week-1-injuries-at-philadelphia)): Jones-Drew (minor), Harris and Lowery (long term). Gator Hoskins's September 3 and Hakeem Nicks's September 4 reassessments changed neither instruction ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#september-3-and-4-hoskins-and-nicks-reassessed)). The August 29 reassessments of A.J. Bouye and Jeris Pendleton changed neither instruction; at the August 30 reduction Pendleton was waived under his instruction, Cameron Brate and Jerrell Jackson were waived with the injured designation and reverted to reserve/injured on August 31, and Nicks and Hoskins were carried on the 53 under their instructions ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#august-29-to-31-reassessments-bouyes-clearance-and-the-reserve-placements)). Bouye cleared August 31, his projected date. Of the earlier injuries, Marcel Jensen's hold cleared August 28, Jordan Poyer cleared August 26, Richard Murphy was released August 18 (he was waived August 30 and signed to the practice squad August 31), C.J. Anderson cleared August 11 and Lane Johnson's hold cleared August 14, each on his projected date under the record's method.
