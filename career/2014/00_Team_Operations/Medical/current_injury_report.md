# Jacksonville current medical participation

**Through September 7, 2014.** The Week 1 game at Philadelphia generated three Jacksonville injuries ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#september-7-week-1-injuries-at-philadelphia)): Maurice Jones-Drew (lower extremity, minor; not removed; out, projected return September 12, reassessment September 8), Mike Harris (lower extremity, long term; removed in overtime; out, projected return December 8, reassessment September 14) and Dwight Lowery (lower extremity, long term; removed in overtime; out, projected return December 11, reassessment September 14). The roster dispositions on Harris and Lowery are Caldwell's decision with the user and are pending; the Week 2 secondary is Stone's decision after the September 8 review. Earlier: Gator Hoskins's September 3 and Hakeem Nicks's September 4 reassessments changed neither instruction ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#september-3-and-4-hoskins-and-nicks-reassessed)); both are Week 1 inactives by medical instruction and are listed out on the league's Week 1 practice report after three non-participation days. The Philadelphia practice week produced no injury and no new restriction. Earlier: The August 29 reassessments of A.J. Bouye and Jeris Pendleton changed neither instruction; at the August 30 reduction Pendleton was waived under his instruction, Cameron Brate and Jerrell Jackson were waived with the injured designation and reverted to reserve/injured on August 31, and Hakeem Nicks and Gator Hoskins were carried on the 53 under their instructions ([dated medical history](medical_history.md#august-29-to-31-reassessments-bouyes-clearance-and-the-reserve-placements)). Bouye cleared August 31, his projected date. No practice was held August 29 to 31 and no new injury or restriction arose. Of the earlier injuries, Marcel Jensen's hold cleared August 28, Jordan Poyer cleared August 26, Richard Murphy was released August 18 (he was waived August 30 and signed to the practice squad August 31), C.J. Anderson cleared August 11 and Lane Johnson's hold cleared August 14, each on his projected date under the record's method.
