# Jacksonville 2014 medical history

This record preserves communicated medical decisions and their dates. The [roster](../Team/Roster/roster.md) owns current availability. Coaches assign work within medical instructions; neither a projected return date nor a successful practice supplies a new clearance.

## April 5: Paul Posluszny cleared

Posluszny's head/neck hold from 2013 Week 13 cleared on April 5, 2014. No new restriction was communicated. The existing simulation method cleared every club's 2013 injuries on its recorded projected date without a separate staged-clearance model; Jacksonville's offseason players received that same treatment. This preserves that completed decision, not a rule permitting a coach to clear a future injury.

The clearance satisfied the medical condition of the March 24 Russell Allen agreement. Allen still had to pass Arizona's physical and complete processing; those occurred April 7 in the [completed Allen trade](../Trades/completed_trades/allen_to_arizona_2014-04-07.md).

## August 14: Johnson's projected date

Lane Johnson's projected return date from the August 8 trunk injury was August 14, and no further reassessment date or instruction was communicated after his August 10 reassessment. His hold cleared on that date under the method the record has applied to every injury in both seasons: a projected return clears on that date unless the staff communicates otherwise, and nothing else was communicated. He was inactive for the August 14 game by Stone's football decision in the [Chicago plan](../../04_Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Preseason_Games/Game_02/Preseason%20Game%202_%20HC.md), made before the reassessment and independent of the clearance. He is available for the August 15 to 21 practices with no communicated limitation. A clearance restores no role by itself: the right-tackle arrangement for August 22 is Stone's decision and is not made here.
