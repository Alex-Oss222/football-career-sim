## April 5: Paul Posluszny cleared

Posluszny's head/neck hold from 2013 Week 13 cleared on April 5, 2014. No new restriction was communicated. The existing simulation method cleared every club's 2013 injuries on its recorded projected date without a separate staged-clearance model; Jacksonville's offseason players received that same treatment. This preserves that completed decision, not a rule permitting a coach to clear a future injury.

The clearance satisfied the medical condition of the March 24 Russell Allen agreement. Allen still had to pass Arizona's physical and complete processing; those occurred April 7 in the [completed Allen trade](../Trades/completed_trades/allen_to_arizona_2014-04-07.md).
