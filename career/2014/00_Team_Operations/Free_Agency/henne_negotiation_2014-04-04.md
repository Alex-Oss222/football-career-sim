# Chad Henne: April 2014 negotiation

Recorded September 30, 2026. Branch dates: April 1 to 4, 2014.

**Method.** This is the same as the [Jones-Drew and C.J. Wilson negotiations](jones_drew_and_wilson_negotiation_2014-03-28.md). Caldwell sets the offers within Stone's instruction, the player and his representatives may accept, counter or decline, and there is no private draw. The exchanges are authored simulation events, not recovered conversations.

**Stone's instruction** ([memo amendment of September 30, 2026](../../01_Early_Offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-henne-ball-and-brent-grimes)): re-sign Henne as QB2. Open with the existing offer of two years, $3.75M with $1.65M guaranteed, and stay within $2.5M a year.

Henne's real March 7 re-signing with the real Jaguars (two years, $8M, $4.5M guaranteed) was for a club that planned to start him. It is not a market for a backup role and is not used.

## Exchanges

1. **Caldwell opens (April 1):** two years, $3.75M, $1.65M guaranteed.
2. **Representatives counter (April 2):** two years, $4.5M, $2.25M guaranteed. They cite Sanchez's guarantee and Henne's two seasons in the system. At $2.25M a year it is inside Stone's ceiling, but the guarantee is above any backup deal on the market except Sanchez's, and Sanchez had started for four seasons.
3. **Caldwell's final (April 3):** two years, $4.0M, $2.0M guaranteed. That is $2.0M a year, inside the ceiling.
4. **Henne accepts (April 4).** The clubs with open backup jobs have filled them, and a guaranteed $2.0M is above Hill's deal and close to Sanchez's. Jacksonville also offers continuity in a system he already knows. The same terms from any club, in the same market, would get the same answer.

## Executed contract

Figures in dollars.

| Season | Signing-bonus allocation | Base salary | Cash | Cap charge | Guaranteed at signing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 2014 | 600,000 | 800,000 | 2,000,000 | 1,400,000 | $1,200,000 signing bonus and the $800,000 base |
| 2015 | 600,000 | 2,000,000 | 2,000,000 | 2,600,000 | None |
| Total | 1,200,000 | 2,800,000 | 4,000,000 | 4,000,000 | $2,000,000 |

- **Minimum check:** the 2014 base is above the $730,000 minimum for his credited seasons.
- **Release exposure:** releasing him before 2015 leaves $600,000 of dead money and removes the $2,000,000 2015 base.
- **No other terms:** no void years, incentives, escalators or trade clauses, and no starting promise.
- **Role:** he returns as QB2 behind Kirk Cousins.
