## Process and evidence

Read [Stone's board](player_board.md), the [calendar](../../Calendar.md) and the [verified target pool](../../League/personnel/free_agent_pool.md). The February 17 to March 3 tag window, the March 8 to 11 negotiations and the March 11, 4 p.m. signing opening are distinct. Verify each player's branch rights and actual applicable transaction before acting. A retirement, trade or release is not a free-agent acquisition.

Caldwell resolves the memo's terms and contingencies. Jacksonville's own re-signings, tenders and tags are branch transactions, not real Jaguars imports. New targets require a sourced status and contract review. The March 8 to 11 period was replayed as a judgment-based negotiation (see below); outside targets not covered by that replay use the [one market draw per player](../../League/personnel/fa_draws.md) at the player's real signing date under the rails method. Do not reroll a loss or read later terms early.

### May 2 and May 7: Bradfield offer-sheet deadlines

The May 2 RFA offer-sheet deadline passed with no offer sheet for Cameron Bradfield on record. There was therefore nothing for Jacksonville to match at the May 7 matching deadline. His original $1,431,000 qualifying offer remained unsigned. Stone’s May 1 instruction kept Bradfield, Pasztor, Clemons and Brown outside football and social-program participation until they signed; their absence supplied no personnel assessment.

### June 16: Bradfield qualifying offer retained

On the adjusted June 15 tender date, Caldwell left Bradfield’s original $1,431,000 qualifying offer in place and substituted no reduced tender. No instruction requested a reduction. Bradfield’s 2013 salary remained unresolved, so no 110-percent alternative could be calculated. The adjusted date is directly verified in the [calendar research](../../../../library/2014_full_calendar_verification.md); the substitution mechanism itself rests on one unverified source in the [financial rules](../../../../library/2014_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md). No cap figure changed: the four unsigned tenders were already counted at $3,066,000. The source records no equivalent June 15 mechanism for exclusive-rights tenders; Clemons, Brown and Pasztor simply remained unsigned. The [contract status register](../Finances/player_contracts/contract_status.md) carries their current status.

A non-exclusive franchise player may negotiate with other clubs from the league year, and another club may sign him to an offer sheet; if Jacksonville declines to match, it receives two first-round picks from that club. Monroe's March 11 re-signing ended the tag before any offer sheet. His real Baltimore re-signing does not apply (rails method section 3).

Tender check (February 28, 2014). The required non-exclusive tender is the greater of the position figure and 120 percent of the player's prior-year salary. The only 2013 figures on record for Monroe are in the [initial cap sheet](../../../2013/offseason/initial_cap_sheet.md): $3,800,000 base, $1,742,500 proration and a $205,000 workout bonus, for a $5,747,500 cap number. That row is an older archive transcription its September 18, 2026 audit did not re-verify. One hundred twenty percent of the base is $4,560,000, and of the full cap number $6,897,000. Both are below $11,654,000, so the position figure applied whichever measure counted as his prior-year salary; it would have stopped applying only above a prior-year salary of $9,711,667.

## Recording rules for later entries

Record the actual dated offer or tender, authority, player response or draw reference where applicable, effective date, transaction type and actual status. Only a completed agreement belongs among signed contracts. Keep tendered rights, offer sheets, a matching period and an executed contract distinct.

For every completed contract, record term, base salaries, bonuses, guarantees, options and incentives when verified, cap and cash treatment and remaining unknowns. Link the dated agreement from the annual record and update the affected roster, control, contract accounting and current state. Do not copy the memo's estimated cap room into an authoritative balance. Actual departures and failed pursuits remain visible without becoming invented signings.
