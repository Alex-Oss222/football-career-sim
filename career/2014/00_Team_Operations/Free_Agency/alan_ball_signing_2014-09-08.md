# Alan Ball: signed September 8, 2014

Recorded October 2, 2026. Branch date: September 8, 2014.

**Instruction.** Stone's standing veteran-corner instruction: Ball is the first call if the corner room is hit, subject to a current physical ([May 12 conversation](alan_ball_2014-05-12.md); [memo amendment](../../01_Early_Offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-henne-ball-and-brent-grimes)). Mike Harris's long-term injury at Philadelphia on September 7 and his placement on reserve/injured on September 8 are the trigger. The [Week 2 packet](../../05_Regular_Season/Games/Week_02/Regular-season%20Week%202.md) names Ball for the first call and physical; Caldwell's authority over the signing is Document 3.

**Market.** None. Ball has been an unplaced free agent since May 12 under [the league rails](../../League/personnel/method.md) section 3: his real 2014 move was with the real Jaguars, which this club never made, so no other club's history signs him. No market draw (method section 4) applies, because there is no real contract to weigh Caldwell's offer against. The call list below him (none named after Brent Grimes, who is not pursued) was not reached.

**Physical.** September 8, the club physical; no restriction communicated. His 2013 trunk injury (Week 10 at Tennessee, long term) cleared at its January 22, 2014 projection under the method the record applies to every injury ([2013 medical history](../../../2013/medical_history.md)); the exit physical of January 14 recorded no finding here. Nothing is diagnosed beyond that.

**Terms.** Executed contract, Caldwell's offer accepted the same day:

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| Length | One year, 2014 |
| Base salary | $855,000 rate: the 2014 minimum for seven to nine credited seasons ([library section 2a](../../../../library/2014_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md), Confirmed). His credited count of seven is inferred from his 2007 entry (Dallas 2007 to 2011, Houston 2012, Jacksonville 2013) and is unverified, the label Jeremy Cain's March contract carried |
| Earned and charged in 2014 | **$804,706**: sixteen of the seventeen regular-season pay weeks (the standard player contract pays the base in equal weekly installments; the library records no proration rule, so this is a labelled reconstruction, 16/17 of $855,000 rounded to the dollar) |
| Signing bonus | None |
| Guarantees | None |
| Incentives, options, trade terms | None |
| Role | None promised. A place on the 53; the Week 2 chart place is Stone's |
| Minimum-salary benefit | Not applied until his eligibility is verified, the treatment of Cain's and C.J. Wilson's contracts; the charge is counted in full |
| Release exposure | $0 of dead money; the unpaid weekly installments leave |

**The exchange.** Caldwell opened with the terms above and said what Stone had said in May: the club had a job to compete for, not a job to hand him. Ball's agent asked for a roster-place assurance through the season or a small signing bonus; Caldwell declined both, Stone's instruction barring a role promise and the club having given no bonus to Cain on the same structure. Ball accepted, having told Stone in May he would take the best situation he found and having found no other. The same terms set against the same market (none) would give the same answer for any player in his position.

**Onboarding.** Compressed, as the framework provides for a late acquisition: the complete active books issued on September 8 at signing; Stone's and Tony Oden's calls that evening rather than on a second day; the welcome package handed over with the playbook. The compression is recorded, not backdated. He practiced from Wednesday, September 10.

**Cap effect.** $804,706 added to the full-count working total ([cap worksheet](../Finances/salary_cap/cap_worksheet.md#september-8-2014-reconciliation-after-the-week-2-transactions)). No 2015 commitment.
