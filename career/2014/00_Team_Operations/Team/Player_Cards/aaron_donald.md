## Dated coaching updates

### August 1, 2014 | Training camp

Donald's first step beat Turner on one one-on-one rep. The Brewster-Turner combination moved him from the strong A-gap and he split it once. Pleasant's double-team coaching point was pad level; a pass-rush win does not answer the anchor question.
