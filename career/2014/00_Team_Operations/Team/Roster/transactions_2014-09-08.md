## Alan Ball: the call and the physical

Harris's injury hit the room, which is the standing instruction's trigger ([Stone's May 12 conversation](../../Free_Agency/alan_ball_2014-05-12.md); [Stone's offseason decisions](../../../01_Early_Offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-henne-ball-and-brent-grimes)). Caldwell called Ball's agent on the morning of September 8. Ball was an unplaced free agent: under the [league rails](../../../League/personnel/method.md) section 3 no club's real 2014 history signs him (his own real 2014 move was with the real Jaguars, which this club never made), so there was no competing contract and no market draw (method section 4 applies only where a real contract competes). The three clubs his agent named in May had produced nothing on the record.

Ball flew in the same day. The club physical communicated no restriction: his November 2013 trunk injury had cleared at its January 22, 2014 projection under the method applied to every injury of both seasons ([2013 medical history](../../../../2013/medical_history.md)), and the September 8 examination added no finding. No diagnosis is invented. Caldwell's terms were the minimum structure the club used for Cain in March: one year at the minimum for his credited seasons, no bonus, no guarantee, no role promise. Ball's agent asked whether the job was the one he had held in 2013; Caldwell, under Stone's instruction, promised nothing beyond a place on the 53 and the chance to earn more through the week's work. Ball signed. The same terms offered to any unplaced veteran corner with the same record would have produced the same signing; persuasion was not an input.
