## How to read this page

Players are grouped by position. Pos is the roster position in Document 4. Status is `Active 53` (with the 2014 signing or draft note in parentheses), `Reserve/Injured` or `Practice squad`, each followed by at most one dated parenthetical; the weekly input build takes Jacksonville's game unit from the `Active 53` rows and treats every listed status as club control. Availability is the club's current medical communication; "No communicated restriction" means none has been communicated, and game-week status still requires fresh communication before each game. Role lists assignments decided in canon: the season depth chart Stone issued August 30, 2014 ([depth chart](../Depth_Chart/depth_chart.md)), the captains named August 31, and the carried 2013 roles Stone has not changed; "chart order" roles describe the player's place on the season chart, not a promise of snaps. The game depth chart (`game_depth_chart.json`) is the kernel's order; the Week 3 inactives are listed there until Stone re-sets them for Week 4.
