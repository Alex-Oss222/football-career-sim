**How to read the sheets:**
- **Cap savings** are net of Top-51 displacement. Today four minimum contracts sit outside the Top 51. Each player who leaves brings one of them back into the count, so the saving is the player's charge minus the returning minimum.
- **Pick values** use the Jimmy Johnson chart (nflverse `draft_values.csv`). A future pick is valued one round later for each year out, under the rule adopted for package I: a 2015 fifth counts as a mid-sixth (21 points) and a 2015 sixth or 2016 fifth as a mid-seventh (8 points). Anything lower is off the seven-round chart and counts as nominal.
- **2013 production** comes from the equal-strength engine. It shows opportunity and use, not talent, and a buying club would judge the player from his real public record.
- **The games condition** used on D and E: the player appears in at least half of the acquiring club's 2014 regular-season games (8 of 16), counted from that club's branch receipts.

- **Posluszny first:** the trade cannot close until Posluszny is actually cleared. His return is projected for April 5; the projection is not a clearance. If he is not cleared in time, the coverage question comes to Stone before the deadline.
