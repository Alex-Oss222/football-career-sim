# Jacksonville 2014 undrafted recruitment and signings

**Timing:** calls began immediately after the seventh round closed on Saturday, May 10, 2014, in the order of the [complete undrafted board](../01_Early_Offseason/stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md#september-30-2026-amendment-complete-undrafted-board); all 17 contracts were signed that day, before rookie minicamp.
**Status: COMPLETE.** 24 names on the board: 17 signed, six declined, one held (Hurst).
**Authority:** Caldwell executed the contracts under Document 3; Stone assigns football roles afterwards. Malcolm Butler was a draft target only and was taken at No. 241; he is not part of this file.
**Historical boundary:** every player on the board went undrafted in the real 2014 draft (checked against the cached real draft). Each call was a separate negotiation resolved by judgment, as in the March free-agency replay: the player's real undrafted club and real terms, where the contracts file recovers them, were the competing offer under [rails rule 6](../League/personnel/method.md); where no real terms were recovered, the comparison was on role and depth-chart fit only, and the file says so. Players Jacksonville won leave their real clubs under the rails; players Jacksonville lost went to their real clubs on their real terms. A player whose real club was the real Jaguars had no competing club offer (rails rule 4). No later NFL outcome was used.

## Jacksonville's offer

The standard offer was the same to every player: a three-year undrafted minimum contract ($420,000, $510,000 and $600,000 for 2014 to 2016, $1,530,000 in all) with no signing bonus, as in the 2013 precedent. Under the memo's lineman rule, Caldwell added a $15,000 signing bonus for each offensive lineman; that figure is a branch decision, set above every recovered 2014 undrafted-lineman bonus on the board ($7,000 to $12,000). Roster room: 79 of 90 after these signings (53 carried, nine drafted, 17 undrafted), so the 90-player limit never bound.
