## At each Jacksonville selection

Confirm actual owned asset, any condition/hold, final overall number and dated eligibility/scouting. Read only evidence available at this selection. Apply the [rails method](../League/personnel/method.md): the target is available at branch pick N only if his real pick is N or later, or he went undrafted. This check runs on draft day, not during today's board preparation. Apply the exact authorized fallback, if any; a comparison list does not authorize an invented priority or trade.

Record the selection and pairing before refreshing roster/control, dated record, draft accounting and current state. A selected player's negotiating rights and signed contract are distinct. Record the actual signed terms only when agreed, then execute permitted onboarding. Review package F2 only after two linemen are actually drafted. Do not count targets or use a trade-dependent pick until the trade closes.

Track Jacksonville's selection ordinal for the real-Jaguars pairing rule, including extra branch picks. Follow the method for an absent partner; do not invent a real selection or another club's compensating transaction. The outgoing player, receiving club and any unplaced result must be explicit. No roster may duplicate a Jacksonville-controlled player.

After Round 7, verify the actual undrafted/available pool and the permitted opening moment before signing anyone. The memo's 12–15 intention and watch list do not create offers, guarantee UDFA status or promise camp places. Close the draft and its contract/control effects through the normal atomic workflow; preserve all ex-ante plans.
