# Kirk Cousins: 2013 season review index

[Development plan](../player_development/kirk_cousins.md) | [Film workflow](README.md) | [Delivery log](delivery_log.md)

**Status:** source-play index prepared from existing branch receipts; cause review and real comparison/media assembly remain open. No video assets, player viewing or coach review are asserted. This index is the entry point for the regular-season sheet promised in Cousins’s January 13 [exit interview](../../../2013/exit_interviews/main_core/kirk_cousins.md).

**Reconciled scope:** 18 interceptions, 38 sacks and 2 lost fumbles in **57 distinct plays**. Week 10 sequence 155 is both a sack and a lost fumble; count it once as a play and in both event categories. The Week 4 completed pass followed by Toney Clemons’s fumble is not Cousins’s lost fumble and is excluded. Postseason and practice examples are separate comparison/review sources below, not added to the promised regular-season totals.

Each pointer is zero-based within the named receipt’s `play_ledger`. Sequence is the receipt’s own identifier. Clock is the receipt’s recorded game clock, not a media timecode or a verified pre-snap reading. No camera angle is implied. Concept labels are retrieval aids and do not establish the actual protection, receiver adjustment, coverage, footwork or fault. In particular, ignore the legacy randomly allocated `blocker` label for sack responsibility.

**Defaults for every event below:** cause **undetermined**; correct comparison **not yet established**; media **no asset supplied**; delivery **not recorded**; retention **not tested in 2014**. A source index is not a completed classified tape. Bates/Skalaski lead; Tice/Yarno handle protection; Drake and the relevant skill coach handle shared target assignments; Stone reviews his call.

Create the packet with [packet_template.md](packet_template.md). Reference these stable review IDs; do not replace the receipt. For each finding enter the expected job and its source, observed fact, cause with confidence, shared/staff contribution, confirmed comparison or missing evidence, correction cue and next legal retest. Undetermined findings remain open rather than becoming fabricated coverage or mechanics. Preserve the original source index when adding later review findings.
