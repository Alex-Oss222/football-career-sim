# Individual film and tape workflow

**Purpose:** give each player his own explanation, source examples and next teaching step, then close the loop on delivery and retention. The written indexes here are assembly and review records. No video assets were supplied for this build; a receipt's game clock is not a video timestamp, and a play ledger does not reveal unseen leverage, footwork or coverage.

## Assemble a useful player packet

1. Verify the player's eligibility/control and the existing contact/distribution gate. Internal preparation may proceed without claiming player participation. Use only branch evidence available at the checkpoint and the active playbooks.
2. State the assigned job and review question. Include what the player did correctly. Gather the question rep and the best documented comparison, not a highlights-only or failures-only compilation.
3. Give every source a stable locator: repository file, event ID and JSON pointer or output section. If actual media becomes available, also enter its authorized asset reference, angle and in/out markers. Never invent a timestamp, clip, angle or transcript.
4. Separate observed fact, expected assignment, outcome and cause. Classify the player's contribution, shared communication, other execution, opponent win and staff/call/design contribution. Retain uncertainty. The 2013 random lineman sack allocations, random returner assignments and absent coverage tackles cannot support individual fault or role findings.
5. Pair a genuinely comparable correct example where evidence supports it. State the comparison's limits. If none exists, label “comparison missing” and use a diagram or later lawful teaching demonstration. Do not classify an interception's cause or a completion's correctness from the result alone.
6. The position coach owns the explanation. Adjacent-room coaches reconcile shared jobs; Tice/Crennel/the special-teams lead resolves unit questions; Stone owns his calls and material menu decisions. The player receives one coherent correction.
7. Prepare a brief written takeaway, the source examples, one clear cue, a teach-back question, the immediate lawful repeat and a later changed-picture retest. Provide a written alternative if video is absent or inaccessible; label it honestly.

## Preparation, delivery and learning are separate

| Dimension | Permitted status examples | Evidence required to advance |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Queued; source index prepared; cause review open; coach-approved packet | Actual packet revision and reviewer finding, not a planned action |
| Media | No asset supplied; written-only; media reference verified | Play/source mapping and authorized asset that can actually be accessed |
| Contact gate | Unresolved; permitted for specified action/date/participant; deferred | Applicable sourced rule/club determination; medical and control conditions where relevant |
| Distribution | Not recorded; sent; receipt acknowledged; superseded | Actual date, sender, recipient, channel and packet revision |
| Teaching | Not observed; explained; immediate correction; delayed retention; next test open | Dated phase output and specific assignment/rep evidence |

Acknowledgment means receipt, not viewing, comprehension or mastery. Missing acknowledgment is not a commitment grade. An optional packet may be declined without a role penalty. A paper plan cannot establish that a player watched a tape.

## Handling the old promises

The [2013 exit-interview README](../../../2013/exit_interviews/README.md#follow-ups) records several January 31 cutups with no delivery receipt. Other interviews promise that week, that month, on request or when lawful. Preserve each exact source wording; do not convert all promises into one invented date or mark them all overdue in the same way.

The queue starts with **historical delivery unverified**. Check for an actual receipt first; if none is found, prepare the outstanding work at the honest current date. Never mark a past commitment fulfilled just because this folder now exists. A retired or departed player's archival question is not a current practice assignment.

## Distribution and phase handoff

Use the player's documented contact preference where one exists, after the relevant gate is resolved. This repository update sends no messages to players. When an in-simulation delivery actually occurs, record it in the [delivery log](delivery_log.md), link the event's primary output/history record and update the queue pointer in the same change. Store no private contact details, medical diagnosis or support disclosure in public packets.

At the next permitted teaching block, the coach checks the actual football job. Carry unobserved, medically restricted, corrected-on-repeat and later-retained work separately into the next phase. An evaluation that is parked must have a stated reason and next review opportunity communicated through a permitted channel; it does not silently disappear.
