## Open position and interim coverage

**Special teams coordinator is the only current vacancy.** Stone directs the kicking game on an interim basis under Entry 75 and Document 3 section 4. Crennel remains the defensive caller, Tice runs the offensive process, and Stone retains offensive play-calling. No additional assistant has been appointed to absorb the special-teams workload; emergency succession if Stone is unavailable remains unassigned.

The replacement role covers punt and punt return, kickoff and kickoff return, field-goal/PAT and block units, hands/onside units, specialist development and integration, coverage technique, returner evaluation, personnel recommendations, and unit planning. The current staff register's section 6 owns that role description. Stone retains team priorities and consequential game management; Caldwell retains final player-personnel authority. Existing player assignments stand until an authorized change.

**Planning judgment:** Stone's coverage is a workable vacancy arrangement at the current offseason checkpoint, but it adds a third phase to his head-coach and offensive-calling responsibilities. The search should resolve who will teach and run the unit before organized field work; no appointment date, delegation or practice result is presumed here.

## Salary commitments and offer capacity

The eleven remaining assistants' scheduled 2014 salaries total **$6,950,000**, reconciled to the [executed contract register](../../../2013/coaching_staff.md): the original $7,575,000 less Lowry's removed $625,000. This is branch-created contract compensation, not historical NFL salary data.

The head-coach agreement promises a funded staff budget but states no numeric ceiling. Therefore:

- $6,950,000 is the existing salary commitment, not the spending limit or available hiring money.
- Lowry's former $625,000 salary is a reference point, not an automatic offer ceiling or approved replacement allocation.
- Confirm the proposed replacement's annual salary, term, guarantees/offsets and total commitment within the funded budget before committing an offer. Stone selects and allocates within his contractual staff-budget authority; Caldwell's broader budget authority remains as established in Document 3.
- Assistant pay is separate from the player salary cap. These figures exclude Stone and the support departments identified in the staff register.
