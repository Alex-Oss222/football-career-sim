**What is canon today (Entries 75, 83 and 84).**
- **Alan Lowry**, special teams coordinator, left on January 12, 2014 to become Atlanta's head coach. Stone directed the kicking game under Entry 75's interim assignment until **Mike Westhoff** was hired on February 11, 2014 (Entry 84): $750,000 a season for 2014 and 2015, reporting to Stone.
- **Frank Bush**, linebackers coach, interviewed with Indianapolis for its defensive coordinator job on January 14 with Jacksonville's permission, and was not hired. Atlanta's request to interview him for its linebackers job on January 12 was refused (lateral).
- **February (Entry 83):** Chicago interviewed Bush for defensive coordinator on February 6 with permission and hired elsewhere on February 9. Minnesota's February 17 requests for Jeremy Bates (quarterbacks) and Mike Tice (offensive coordinator) were refused as lateral. No assistant left.
- The other assistants received no request and remain under contract (`career/2013/coaching_staff.md`).
- Five clubs changed head coaches by February 2: Atlanta, Cincinnati, Denver, Indianapolis and San Francisco. After the Super Bowl, Buffalo kept Doug Marrone and Minnesota changed head coach, with an external hire on February 17 (Entry 83).

## Interview permission: January 2014 policy

Entry 77 clarifies the existing policy without changing its decisions. Apply the researched [2013-14 rules](../../../../library/2014_coaching_hiring_and_anti_tampering_rules.md), including employment status and the relevant window, before applying a club preference.

- **Head-coach vacancy:** grant an eligible interview where the rules require it after Jacksonville's season. Within a permitted playoff window, Jacksonville's standing policy also grants permission, subject to the applicable scheduling and repeat-interview limits. "Grant" does not permit an interview outside the window.
- **Position coach to coordinator:** voluntarily grant an eligible interview after Jacksonville's season ends. Under the 2013-14 rules this is still an assistant-to-assistant move that the club could block, despite the greater responsibility. The grant is club policy, not a protected promotion under the later coordinator-interview rule.
- **Same-job request for an assistant under contract:** refuse under the standing club policy. This does not give Jacksonville a veto over a coach whose contract has expired or who has been released. Other role changes require the actual duties and existing authority to be checked; the current policy supplies no automatic answer for every possible title.

The user may change Jacksonville's discretionary policy prospectively. A policy change cannot waive league restrictions or undo a closed outcome. Interview permission, the coach's decision to participate, an offer, release/contract handling where applicable, and acceptance remain distinct. The frozen January method and result files are historical artifacts and are not rewritten by this clarification.
