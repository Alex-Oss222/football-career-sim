# Jacksonville: March 2014 negotiation replay

## Authority and method

Stone expressly directs the negotiations and player decisions to take place here, because the other execution environment is unavailable. For this replay, that instruction supersedes the former requirement to obtain each answer from the private draw service. These are fictional, judgment-based simulation outcomes, not actual contacts with players or agents, not private-engine receipts and not recovered historical conversations. No numerical acceptance probabilities or random draws are claimed.

Stone controls his own dialogue, tactics, depth chart and coaching promises. Caldwell controls financial offers and roster negotiations. The player and representatives can accept, counter, delay or reject. Stone's figures are baselines, not fixed offers or mandatory ceilings. Caldwell need not create a counteroffer where an opening agreement is reasonable, or a dispute simply to add drama.

The attached twelve-factor framework guides the inquiry. Its numerical weights, thresholds and broad claims about age are not treated as measured facts. NFL rules and March 2014 evidence govern. NBA options, tax aprons, modern NFLPA report cards and later career outcomes are not imported. Unknown preferences are left uncertain rather than converted into benefits or penalties. Team success, scheme familiarity and trust affect a player's assessment without creating automatic discounts or making promises on Stone's behalf. Disagreement does not automatically produce a holdout, grievance, public feud or coach firing.

The replay begins from the earlier cap commitments and franchise designation, with the first-pass March-window outcomes reopened. Other players' replacement outcomes remain pending until individually recorded. The March 7 Henne event requires its own chronology treatment; this log does not silently move it into March 8. Individual player transactions may be resolved in conversation order at the same March 11 checkpoint without deciding concurrent negotiations prematurely.
