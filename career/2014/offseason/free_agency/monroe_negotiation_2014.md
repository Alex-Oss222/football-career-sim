## Authority and Caldwell's decision

Stone supplied five years, $42.5 million maximum value and $20 million guaranteed as a baseline, and expressly delegated price, structure and negotiation to Caldwell. The baseline is neither a mandatory exact offer nor a hard ceiling. See the latest [decision-memo amendment](../stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md).

Caldwell selects that price and structure for the prepared offer. It commits $20 million of cash in two years to retain the left tackle, replaces the expensive one-year tender if accepted, and leaves only $3 million of unamortized signing bonus after 2015. No increase is warranted merely to make the proposal look different from Stone's baseline. No agent demand, counteroffer, concession or acceptance has been generated or received. Any further negotiation must remain distinguishable from an actual resolved player response.

The franchise designation predates the replay and remains in force. Jacksonville can negotiate a multiyear contract with its own franchise player; Article 10, section 2(k) allows a multiyear agreement before the July 15 deadline. The prepared contract covers the 2014–2018 seasons. Its proposed execution point for the replay is March 11, 2014; preparation does not advance the career clock or release the tag.

- **Signing bonus:** $5,000,000 paid at execution in 2014, allocated $1,000,000 to each of five seasons. The allocation column is not another annual cash payment.
- **Guarantees:** the signing bonus and the entire 2014 and 2015 base salaries are fully guaranteed for skill, injury and salary-cap termination at execution. Total: $5,000,000 + $6,500,000 + $8,500,000 = $20,000,000. No later vesting guarantee is proposed.
- **Roster bonuses:** $500,000 in each of 2016, 2017 and 2018, earned if on the roster on the fifth day of that league year. These are not guaranteed at signing.
- **Per-game active bonuses:** $31,250 for each regular-season game on the game-day active roster, up to 16 games and $500,000 per year in 2016–2018. No postseason payment is included in that ceiling.
- **Value:** $42,500,000 if every scheduled payment and active-game bonus is earned; maximum annual average $8,500,000. Scheduled compensation excluding active-game bonuses is $41,000,000, still subject to continued employment after the guaranteed years. Maximum cumulative cash is $11.5 million, $20 million, $27.5 million, $35 million and $42.5 million after successive seasons.
- **Other terms:** no option bonus, void years, escalators, workout bonuses, additional performance incentives or no-trade provision are included in this proposal. Standard contract and CBA provisions still apply.

### Cap treatment and exit costs

Article 13, section 6(b)(i) permits straight-line signing-bonus allocation over the five contract years. The proposed 2014 and 2015 cap charges are $7,500,000 and $9,500,000.

For 2016–2018, $8,500,000 is the full annual budget, not a certified initial cap charge. Base salary, signing-bonus allocation and the roster bonus total $8,000,000 each year; add the portion of the $500,000 active-game bonus treated as likely to be earned under the applicable prior-season participation rules. Actual earnings can produce later cap adjustments. Future participation is not known at this checkpoint, so no LTBE/NLTBE split is invented.

The following gross pre-June 1 release exposure assumes a release before that year's roster bonus is earned, no additional earned salary or bonus, and no offset, forfeiture recovery or other credit:

| Release year | Remaining signing-bonus allocation | Remaining fully guaranteed base | Gross dead charge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2015 | $4,000,000 | $8,500,000 | $12,500,000 |

| 2017 | $2,000,000 | $0 | $2,000,000 |
| 2018 | $1,000,000 | $0 | $1,000,000 |

The 2015 salary guarantee makes an early release expensive. The practical decision point is before the 2016 roster bonus is earned. Later earned amounts and any applicable CBA benefits must be added when evaluating a real transaction; these are not promises of a future certified cap saving.
