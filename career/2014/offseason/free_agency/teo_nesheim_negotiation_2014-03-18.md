# Daniel Te'o-Nesheim: March 2014 replay negotiation

Recorded September 29, 2026 (ledger Entry 98). Branch date: March 18, 2014. The method is the March 2014 replay's ([replay log](march_2014_replay_log.md)): Caldwell sets the offers, the player and his representatives may accept, counter or decline, and competing clubs are judged by the same criteria. There is no private draw and no acceptance probability. The exchanges below are authored simulation events, not recovered historical conversations.

## Evidence and player perspective

- Te'o-Nesheim is 26 and turns 27 on June 12. His 2013 branch line at Tampa Bay: 16 games, 85 tackles, 14 for loss, 9 sacks, 15 pressures, 4 passes defensed, 1 forced fumble. These totals come from the equal-strength engine; they show opportunity and production, not a talent grade.
- He is an unrestricted free agent from March 11 with no Tampa Bay re-signing. On the league rails no club signed him in 2014, so no competing contract exists to bid against. That is a fact about other clubs' real behavior, used here because the rails govern other clubs. It does not diagnose any medical or football reason, and none is invented.
- A week without a contract gives Caldwell leverage, but the player can still wait for a better market or an injury elsewhere. The negotiation treats that as a real option.
- Jacksonville's case: 10-6 with a playoff win, Crennel retained, an open edge role beside Babin, Mincey, Branch and Davis, with no promised starting job or snap share.

## Negotiation record

1. **Caldwell opens** at three years, $12,000,000, $5,000,000 fully guaranteed, with a 2014 cap charge of $2,500,000. The price reflects the cap position above and the absence of rival bids.
2. **His representatives counter** at three years, $16,500,000, $8,000,000 fully guaranteed, citing the 9-sack season and the memo-level market for an edge starter. This is a simulated asking price, not a sourced rival offer.
3. **Caldwell's final offer** is three years, up to $13,500,000, with $6,000,000 fully guaranteed. The 2014 and 2015 guaranteed money rises, and $500,000 of active-game bonuses is added in each of the first two years, so a healthy, active player earns $5.0M in 2014 cash. Caldwell keeps the 2014 cap charge at $3,000,000 and declines a guaranteed third year.
4. **Te'o-Nesheim accepts** on March 18. With no other club making an offer, a deal paying $5.0M in 2014 cash with $6.0M fully guaranteed beats waiting. The trade-off he accepts is a lower average and a non-guaranteed third year in which his cap charge rises, a keep-or-release year for the club. No starting promise is made.
