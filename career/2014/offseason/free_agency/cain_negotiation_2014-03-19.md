# Jeremy Cain: March 2014 replay negotiation

Recorded September 29, 2026 (ledger Entry 99). Branch date: March 19, 2014. Method: the March 2014 replay's judgment-based negotiation ([replay log](march_2014_replay_log.md)); no private draw. The exchanges are authored simulation events.

## Evidence

- Cain was Jacksonville's long snapper for all of 2013 in the branch and became an unrestricted free agent at 4 p.m. March 11, when his February 2011 contract expired. Entry 94's first-pass re-signing was superseded by the replay (Entry 95).
- On the league rails no club signed him until Chicago on September 1, 2014, so there is no rival offer in March.
- Stone's plan: one year at the minimum for his credited seasons, with Casey Kreiter (Iowa) to be signed after the draft as a camp competitor. Westhoff runs that evaluation; Caldwell makes the roster call.
- Cap position after Te'o-Nesheim: a $2,765,504 working difference (Entry 98), so the deal has to stay at the minimum.

## Negotiation record

1. **Caldwell offers** one year at the veteran minimum, $855,000 (the 2014 minimum for seven to nine credited seasons, inferred from his real September 2014 figure; single source), with no bonus or guarantee.
2. **Cain's representative asks** for a second year at the minimum, or a small signing bonus, and a statement that he is the incumbent.
3. **Caldwell declines both.** A second year would lock in a long snapper before the planned camp competition; a bonus would add guaranteed money with no rival bid to answer. He confirms that Cain enters camp as the 2013 incumbent in an open competition, which states the facts and makes no roster promise.
4. **Cain accepts** on March 19. His alternatives are waiting into the season with no known interest, or signing now with his own club at the minimum.
