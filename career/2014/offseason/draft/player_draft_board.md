## Amendment: Malcolm Butler (user-directed, September 28, 2026)

The user gave Claude autonomy to settle how the board treats Malcolm Butler (CB, West Alabama). The rule:

- **A draft target only.** Butler stays the first choice for Jacksonville's seventh-round pick (about #218), as the memo's board has him. He is not held for an undrafted signing, and no plan depends on his going undrafted.
- **If he is gone before the seventh-round pick:** Ross Cockrell, the board's comparison, takes the pick.
- **The undrafted spots go to other players:** the memo's ten-name watch list and its alternates stand unchanged. Butler is not added to them, even if he goes undrafted in the branch draft.
- **Evidence:** like every prospect, he is graded only on information published before the draft (`library/2014_draft_information_gates.md`), and a pre-draft scouting report must exist before the pick is made.
