## Phase One, April 21 to May 1

### Control, medical and participation

- **Controlled roster:** 53 players, 49 under signed contracts and 4 on unsigned tenders.
- **Unsigned tenders:** Bradfield, Clemons, Brown and Pasztor are not under signed 2014 contracts, and the repository has not established a basis for their taking part in the program. No program work is recorded for them. Caldwell's office owns the question. Bradfield's RFA offer-sheet deadline is May 2.
- **Participation:** the program was voluntary. The 49 signed players took part within their medical instructions. No absence was recorded, and attendance was not used as evidence of anything.
- **Medical:** Will Rackley's existing limit (upper extremity, minor) is a physical restriction only. Under the injured-player learning rule he did the full classroom work, and the medical staff set his strength work. Posluszny, cleared April 5, worked without restriction. No new injury or restriction was communicated.

### Onboarding

The same process was used for all eleven newcomers: the five veterans signed in March (Verner, Talib, Nicks, Hawkins and Te'o-Nesheim) and the six reserve/future players (Bray, Murphy, Jerrell Jackson, Long, D'Anthony Smith and Blake).

- **April 21 (Day 0):** each received the welcome package with the complete offensive and defensive Iteration I books and the 2014 phase roadmap. The package says the whole book is theirs to study and that only taught material is evaluated. No package went out before the program, because the verified rule bars pre-program playbook study.
- **April 23 to 25:** Stone and each position coach held the follow-up conversation in the building, as they were on site.
- **What came back:** football feedback was recorded as teaching notes, not grades. The common request from the five veterans was a translation of Jacksonville's protection and coverage words into those of their previous clubs. The relevant position coaches wrote a one-page word list for each room. Nothing private was recorded.
- **Newcomers' focus:** by Stone's rule each newcomer works on his primary Jacksonville job first. None of them was given a second position.

### What was taught

Every classroom session used Explain, Show, Walk (players explained the job back). Rep and the later steps need field work and wait for Phase Two. Examples came from the documented 2013 locators in the [Cousins review index](../film/kirk_cousins_2013_review.md) and the phase outputs, alongside labeled diagrams. Where the record lacks an angle or an assignment detail, the diagram says so.

**Offense (Tice; Bates, Yarno, Spencer, Drake, Zernhelt).**
- Review: Power, Counter, Inside Zone, Stick, Drive and the taught Mesh work, together with the other assignments shown during the season.
- Boot Flood returned to the classroom (active book 10.13, 11 and 11.3): the Outside Zone pairing, NAKED protection, the deep, intermediate and flat read, and what changes when a defender is outside the launch path. The examples were the good 2013 camp work and the Week 1 interception and Week 2 sack.
- Shared protection identification for quarterbacks, centers and backs (book 8 and 8.3): the center sets the point and others echo only what changes their rule.

**Defense (Crennel; Pleasant, Bush, Oden).**
- Review of the carried front, coverage and rush packages, with TAKE, GIVE and ANSWER written on each one. The motion answers are the book's TRAVEL, BUMP, ROLL and HOLD (book 6).
- Relay rotation: Crennel and Bush rotated the communication job among Daryl Smith, Posluszny and Stanford without changing anyone's defensive role. Stanford took full-call, echo and check reps. Moore learned the call structure for his own packages.

**Special teams (Westhoff).**
- The four punt families (field position, plus territory, backed up, end game) and three-part grading (operation, kick, coverage), taught with Anger, Cain, Scobee and the core coverage players.
- Anger's 2013 punts, starting with Week 12, were reviewed from written locators.
- No kicking or snapping was coached or observed.

**Cross-training observation lanes.** These opened in the classroom for Thielen, Kelce, Poyer, Moore, Prosinski and Trawick, with each player's primary job first. Bradfield's and Pasztor's lanes wait for their participation basis.

**Physical work.** The performance staff gave each signed player an individual plan (playing weight, strength, movement, conditioning and recovery) within his medical instructions. Coaches did not attend the field work.

- **Draft week:** Tice coordinated the veteran program on May 8 and 9 while Stone and Caldwell were in the draft room; the draft rounds ran in the evening on May 8 and 9 and at midday on Saturday, May 10, so no player session lost its assigned coach, and no temporary authority beyond the approved schedule was exercised.

### What was taught

Phase Two permits on-field individual and group instruction at the allowed speed, with no offense against defense and no live contact ([verified rules](../../../../library/2014_offseason_phase_rules_verification.md)). Every session used Explain, Show, Walk, then Rep at the permitted speed, Correct and Rep again; retention is checked next week.

**Offense (Tice; Bates, Yarno, Spencer, Drake, Zernhelt).** Stance and alignment, formation recognition, personnel and role labels, run footwork for Power, Counter and Inside Zone, route landmarks for Stick, Drive, the taught Mesh work and Boot Flood, ball security, protection technique on air, and the center, quarterback and back communication in movement. "Perfect play" reps: the offense walked and then ran each carried concept against no defense, with the coaches supplying the picture by card.

**Defense (Crennel; Pleasant, Bush, Oden).** Stance and alignment, front and leverage rules, pursuit landmarks, run-fit responsibility, coverage landmarks, rush-lane responsibility and communication, with TAKE, GIVE and ANSWER stated before each rep. Tackling approach and positioning were taught on bags and without contact.

**Special teams (Westhoff).** Stance and start mechanics, protection and release technique, lane and leverage rules, return-path landmarks, ball handling and substitution responsibilities. The four punt families moved from the classroom to the field with Anger, Cain, Scobee and the core coverage players; Westhoff began the individual operation record (operation, kick, coverage).

**Cross-training lanes.** The eight revised lanes opened in movement, each player's primary job first: Lane Johnson took a limited number of left-side sets after his right-tackle work; Asper took reps at both guard spots and echoed the interior communication; Thielen worked outside, slot and H; Kelce attached and detached; Poyer nickel and outside; Moore his packages plus the defined pressure work; Prosinski and Trawick secondary and coverage-unit jobs.

**Physical work.** Individual plans continued under the performance staff.
