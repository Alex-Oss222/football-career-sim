## Assessment: what he has demonstrated

Cousins has earned a retention-first starting point in core operation. The records repeatedly support huddle, cadence, protection-point communication and reduced-menu two-minute operation. He also corrected particular timing, outlet, protection and red-zone errors on repeat. Those strengths should be preserved rather than sending him through a beginner rebuild.

The evidence does **not** establish general mastery of closed-first-answer decisions under changed coverage, all protection adjustments, throw placement, ball carriage or the entire playbook. Several errors returned across contexts, some have only an immediate retest, and the season review has no complete cause classification. A late-season stretch without interceptions supports further testing; it does not identify which correction worked or prove every interception cause was fixed.

## Place him in the 2013 progression

The five stages are separate learning strands, not a single unlock ladder or whole-player grade.

| 2013 stage | Supported 2014 entry point | Advancement evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Core operation | Retain the demonstrated call/huddle/cadence and taught timing; sample rather than rebuild | Functioning feet, launch point and ball handling under the specific disruption. Operational strength alone does not certify every mechanical item in Stage 1 |
| 2. Protection and progression | Main development emphasis: shared protection answers and leaving a closed first option | Correct independent identification, timely alternative, secure ball and retained correction on later changed looks; isolate other players' and staff's contributions |
| 3. Simple packaged choices | No sufficient player-specific closure record for all packaged pre-snap answers | Confirm which active-book choices were actually assigned; explain and execute that choice with the surrounding assignments. Do not assume ordinary pass completions prove this stage |
| 4. Selective post-snap reads | Not established by the reviewed evidence | Only if Stone assigns an active-book read: secure exchange, correct conflict-defender read and timely decision. No new option/RPO family or automatic install is created |
| 5. Expanded command | Two-minute and some situational operation have supporting evidence | Additional checks, cadence variation, tempo and multi-answer work must each survive their own retention and transfer tests; no declaration that the entire stage is mastered |

## Teaching packets

Bates builds a short player-facing explanation with two or three genuinely relevant examples where they exist. Cousins explicitly said that paired examples work better for him than a lone correction phrase. Show the question rep, a documented correct answer, then change the presentation in the later lawful test. If no matching correct rep exists, label a diagram or teaching demonstration as such; do not rename a completion “correct” solely because it gained yards.

## Staff accountability and stop rules

- Stone's review includes Week 12 call balance, Week 13 calls relative to his stated re-anchor, the Divisional punt and menu, and Boot Flood removal. Those are staff questions, not automatic deductions from Cousins.
- Classify identification, progression, late decision, unseen defender, placement, pressure/mechanics, receiver error, deflection and justified risk using the 2013 plan. Multiple contributions can coexist. “Undetermined” is a valid outcome when the assignment or film is missing.
- Preserve functioning mechanics. If decision and feet separate only when the picture changes, test processing and instruction before imposing a cosmetic throwing rebuild.
- Reduce only the failing layer, teach it, retest it, then reconsider expansion. Read-ahead access to the full active book remains unrestricted; installed/evaluated work remains the taught subset.
- Participation is voluntary where the governing phase says so. Medical authority controls physical and cognitive work. No contract, draft, role or future signing promise is embedded in this plan.
