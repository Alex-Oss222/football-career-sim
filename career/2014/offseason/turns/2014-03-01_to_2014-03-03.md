## 3. Narrative, two parts

### 3A. Front-office closeout

Monday, March 3. Monroe's designation had been on file since February 18, and when the window closed at four there was nothing else to send. Caldwell initialed the deadline line on the calendar. The league's tag list came through afterward and he moved it to the top of the stack: Washington's franchise tag on Orakpo, Cleveland's transition tag on Mack, Pittsburgh's on Worilds. He ticked the board against it and found no tag on Verner, Talib or Edelman, wrote "untagged" beside the three names and put a question mark beside Te'o-Nesheim, whose club had filed nothing either way.

"Nobody we wanted got tagged," Stone said.

"Then the call sheet stays in the order you gave me. Verner, Talib, Tate, Te'o-Nesheim, with Allen behind him." Caldwell turned the sheet over. "Cain goes on the same page. I want that conversation opened at noon on the eighth, not after."

"And the tenders?"

"Bradfield at the lowest level, the three exclusive-rights players at their minimums. They go in before four on the eleventh, with the futures and Monroe's tender taking effect at the same hour."

Caldwell wrote March 25 at the foot of the sheet, the day Nwaneri's bonus was reported due, and asked whether package H could still be put to Minnesota before it. Stone took the sheet back and read the Minnesota line again.

### 3B. Coach's own team assessment

On offense, Eugene is the only name we sent the league, and he is still unsigned. Kirk starts. Maurice and Henne are conversations until the eleventh. Tate is the receiver I want from outside, and nobody tagged him. Adams is still the plan at 26, unless Arizona takes the Alualu package for 38, and then he is the plan there.

On defense, the corners I want were not tagged. Verner and Talib go to the market like everyone else, and we get the same noon on the eighth that every club gets. Te'o-Nesheim is the end, Allen behind him. Sen'Derrick is a negotiation with no tag behind it, and Paul is a projection until April 5.

In the kicking game, Cain gets the minimum offer, and if we sign Kreiter after the draft the two of them compete in camp. Mike runs that evaluation; Dave makes the roster call.

On the staff, nobody moved. The call sheet for the eighth is Dave's, in the order I gave him, and the tenders go in on his signature.
