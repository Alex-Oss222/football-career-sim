## 3. Narrative, two parts

### 3A. Front-office closeout

Monday, March 24. The league's release listed 32 picks: 12 after the third round, six after each of the fourth and fifth, five after the sixth and three after the seventh. Jacksonville was not on it. Caldwell had the club's 2013 file beside the list: Cox and Knighton out; Grimes, Miller, Marks and Ball in. He wrote "net gain 2, no award" at the top and handed it to the draft office.

The office renumbered the board. Round 4 went from 122 to 134, Detroit's fifth to 157, the club's own fifth to 172, the sixth to 210 and the seventh to 247. Nos. 13, 26, 38 and 90 did not move.

"Anything change on who we take?" Stone asked.

"Not from this. The names stay in the slots. Telvin Smith is 134 now instead of 122 plus whatever came in after the third." Caldwell turned to the second sheet. "The schedule is the one with a date on it. You wanted a choice by today so the office has a week before the thirty-first."

"I haven't signed off on the dates yet."

"Then it goes on tomorrow's sheet. Miami still hasn't called back on Babin."

Baltimore took four picks. Caldwell circled them and moved on to the League Meeting agenda.

### 3B. Coach's own team assessment

On offense, nothing moved this weekend. Kirk starts. The receiver room is Cecil, Adam, Justin, Hakeem and Andrew, and Adams is the plan at 38. Left guard is still open after Uche went to Arizona. That job is mine to settle, and I haven't settled it.

On defense, Verner and Talib are signed and not yet placed on the chart. Te'o-Nesheim is the new end. Paul is a projection until April 5.

In the kicking game, Cain is back for camp against Kreiter if Kreiter goes undrafted. Mike runs that.

On the staff, the dates for the spring are my decision and they are due. Dave's office files them once I choose. The first recommendation in front of me is the nine-week calendar with the May 16 to 18 rookie weekend.
