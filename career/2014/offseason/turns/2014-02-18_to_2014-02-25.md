## 3. Narrative, two parts

### 3A. Front-office closeout

Tuesday, February 18. Caldwell filled in the designation himself: Eugene Monroe, offensive tackle, non-exclusive franchise player. He checked the position against the register, signed it and filed the copy behind page two of the memo, the page that still said March 3. On the worksheet he left the tender line open and wrote "proj. 11.654, official 2/28" in the margin beside it.

"That's our one designation," Caldwell said. "Marks doesn't get one now."

"I know what it does to Marks."

"It costs us a number we don't have yet. The league publishes on the twenty-eighth, and nothing gets booked off a projection."

Stone asked whether another club could get to Monroe first. Caldwell said an offer sheet was possible from March 11, that matching would be his call if one came, and that declining meant two first-round picks coming back. The long-term talks went on under the tag, on the memo's terms, and he meant to keep having them.

"So we're where we'd have been on the third."

"We're there thirteen days sooner, and it's filed." Caldwell turned to Nwaneri's page and started reading the bonus clause again from the top, looking for the date nobody had given him.

### 3B. Coach's own team assessment

On offense, Kirk starts, and his job this spring is the interceptions. Eugene is ours for 2014 one way or the other now, which lets me plan the line from the left side in. Lane, Brewster and Rackley finished the year where the chart still has them. Maurice and Henne are still conversations, not contracts. At receiver I want Adams at 26. The combine gave us a 4.56 and a 39.5 vertical, and the combine numbers aren't a grade. I'll grade him from tape.

On defense, I'd have liked to keep the tag for Sen'Derrick, and I don't get to now. He's the best defender we have and the negotiation has to carry him. Donald ran 4.68 and put up 35 on the bench at 285. Lawrence ran 4.80. Neither of those numbers tells me what either man does against a double team. Paul is a projection until April 5 and not a day before.

In the kicking game, Mike Westhoff has the room. Cain is a pending free agent, and Dave has the minimum offer to make.

On the staff, nobody moved this week. Stork, Hurst and Lucas didn't work out in Indianapolis, and Turner skipped three drills, so the line list on the board is thinner on numbers than it looks.
