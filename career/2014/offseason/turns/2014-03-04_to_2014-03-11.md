## 3. Narrative, two parts

### 3A. Front-office closeout

Friday, March 7. Henne's agent had a number and it was not Jacksonville's. Caldwell had offered two years, $3.75 million, $1.65 million of it guaranteed, the memo's terms, and the reply asked for more than double. He did not counter. The walk-away line in the memo was $2.5 million a year, and it would not have closed the gap either.

Saturday at noon the window opened and the calls went out in the order Stone had written: Verner, Talib, Tate, Te'o-Nesheim. Cain's agent was on the same list. Marks's people already had Jacksonville's four-year offer.

Tuesday the tenders went in before four. Bradfield at the lowest level, the three exclusive-rights players at their minimums. Reisner and Rutland got nothing, as planned. At four o'clock the league year opened and the answers came in over the next hour. Te'o-Nesheim took the three years. Cain took the minimum. Marks did not. Monroe's side told Caldwell they would stay on the tender. Verner and Talib went elsewhere. Caldwell read the Tampa Bay and Denver terms off the wire later that evening: $14 million guaranteed for Verner, $26 million for Talib.

"Grimes is the corner call now," Caldwell said. "And Tarell Brown."

"Arizona?"

"Declined." He had sent the 2015 first, the 2015 fourth and the 2016 fifth for 38. "They didn't counter."

Stone asked about Nwaneri. The call to San Francisco had gone out at four. No answer. The bonus date was March 25.

### 3B. Coach's own team assessment

On offense, Chad is gone, so the backup quarterback is an open question: John Parker and Tyler are what we have under contract behind Kirk. Maurice is on the market with our offer still on the table. Eugene is ours for 2014 on the tag and nothing past it. Tate is tomorrow.

On defense, Daniel is signed. That was the edge rusher, and it means Babin is on the phone to Miami. We lost both corners we wanted, and Sen'Derrick, who was our best defender. Mike Harris starts outside until we find the other one. Grimes is a phone call Dave has to make.

In the kicking game, Jeremy is back on the minimum. Kreiter is still the plan after the draft.

On the staff, nobody moved.
