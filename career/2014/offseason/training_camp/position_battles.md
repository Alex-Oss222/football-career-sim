At a review, link source observations and contradictory evidence, available support/opposition, medical restrictions and meaningful opportunities for reserves. Invite player perspective when permitted. A shared-rule problem is not automatically an individual failure.

When Stone makes an actual football-role choice, record its date, scope, evidence, alternatives considered, authority and next review trigger in [roster decisions](roster_decisions.md), then update affected current-role views atomically. Keeping a role is also a valid result. Caldwell decides any resulting roster transaction; a learning opportunity is not a contract promise.
