## 1. How to read this table

- **Source and status labels.** Confirmed: two independent sources, or confirmed in the register or verifications. Single source: one public source. Branch record: a contract executed in this branch (its figures are canon). Inference: a figure applied from a sourced input by a stated rule (for example straight-line proration of a sourced 2013 proration). Unresolved: not established; never read as zero.
- **"2013 transcription"** means the 2013 row in the [initial cap sheet](../../2013/offseason/initial_cap_sheet.md): either an OverTheCap row the September 18, 2026 audit source-matched ("OTC row") or an older archive transcription the audit did not re-verify ("archive row").
- **Proration.** "Annual proration" is the 2013 figure. "Years left" counts 2014 through the final league year. Carrying the same annual figure into 2014 is an Inference under the straight-line proration rule (initial cap sheet, governing controls); no restructure is recorded, but clause-level terms are unverified for inherited contracts (verifications section 1).
- **2014 cap charge** is shown only when every component is sourced. It is a scheduled charge, not a Top-51 count and not club room.
- **Dead money if released before June 1** is shown only when both inputs (remaining proration and remaining guarantees) are sourced. Where only the proration is sourced, the cell gives that amount as a floor and marks the guarantee Unresolved.
- **Branch figures use millions** where the branch record states them in millions; other figures are exact dollars as sourced.
- **Planning figures** from Stone's [February 2 memo](stone_to_caldwell_2014_offseason_decisions.md) are not booked here; section 7 lists them as leads.

### 5d. 2013 carryover
