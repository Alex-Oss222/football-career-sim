# 2014 draft pairing

Filled at the draft, May 8 to 10, 2014, under [method section 6](method.md#6-draft). The [seven-round order](../../draft/draft_order.md) and [ownership register](../../draft/pick_ownership.json) controlled Jacksonville's nine ordinary picks (13, 26, 38, 90, 129, 153, 168, 205 and 241); Jacksonville's selections are k=1 to k=9 in that order. Other clubs' selections are their real selections. Jacksonville's k-th selection pairs with the real Jaguars' k-th selection: the real Jaguars' player goes to the club that really drafted Jacksonville's player and takes the depth slot he really held on his Week 1 chart; if Jacksonville's player went undrafted in reality, the real Jaguars' player goes to the club where Jacksonville's player really signed. A real Jaguars' selection with no partner is unplaced. Real selections are from the cached nflverse draft file (2014 draft_picks); real undrafted clubs are from the nflverse copy of the Over The Cap contracts file.

Real Jaguars' selections with no partner (unplaced): **Aaron Colvin, CB, Oklahoma (real pick 114)**. The method does not name the self-pairing case in words; it was resolved literally: the partner club would be Jacksonville, a real Jaguars move the branch never made does not happen, and a selection with no partner is unplaced.

The method's k=6 result (Telvin Smith to Green Bay) cannot apply because Smith is Jacksonville-controlled; Jacksonville control overrides the rails (rule 4). Green Bay therefore loses Adams and Linsley and gains only Allen Robinson. No compensating transaction is invented.

## Coverage

The other 247 real selections and the rest of the real undrafted class are applied club by club when each club's dated 2014 rails and Week 1 chart are built; only the pairing-affected moves above are recorded on the club pages now (St. Louis, Cleveland, Green Bay, Carolina, Chicago, New England and the undrafted clubs named). Jacksonville's own real 2014 selections other than the pairings above (Bortles, Lee, Robinson, Linder, Colvin, Chris Smith, Bowanko and Storm Johnson are all placed or unplaced here) do not join Jacksonville.
