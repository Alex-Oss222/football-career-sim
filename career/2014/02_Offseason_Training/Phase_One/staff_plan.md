## Player playbook access and evidence rule

Every participant may possess and study the **complete active 2013 Iteration I playbook**. The phase plan controls what the staff formally installs, practices, and evaluates together; it does not ration access to pages. A player may read ahead and ask about later material. He is evaluated on what has actually been assigned and taught for the phase.

No preset personnel percentage, snap share, rep quota, touch quota, or depth-chart probability determines the result of this phase. Reps and roles are adjusted from football evidence: assignment, communication, technique, physical execution, health, correction retention, matchup, special teams, and what the player demonstrates in practices and games. The plan creates opportunities to evaluate; it does not manufacture the answer.

## Physical preparation

The performance staff assigns playing-weight, strength, movement, conditioning and recovery work by position and individual need, within medical instructions. There is no universal body target. Position coaches neither direct nor observe the restricted field period. Classroom understanding does not establish physical execution, and no voluntary absence becomes an effort judgment.

## Handoff

Each position coach records what was taught, what the player could explain and what needs the field. The first checks in Phase Two concern the Boot Flood answer, shared protection language and defensive relay in movement. Individual teaching notes remain coaching records within the report and existing player card; they do not create another full annual assessment. Actual film distribution requires its own delivery receipt.

## Outstanding individual feedback and film responsibilities

Provide an individual written teaching note for every participating controlled player, including reserves and practice-squad/futures players, after each meaningful work block. State the assigned job, evidence, what was correct, the cause of any error, correction owner, next legal retest and whether an evaluation is open, parked or closed. No evidence means not observed, not failed. If several players miss the same instruction, review the teaching first.

For a needed correction, pair the player's rep with a correct example where one exists; never fabricate a matching good rep. Classify assignment, communication, technique, physical loss, processing delay, medical restriction and staff/design failure separately. Correct reps need no invented fault. Immediate re-reps occur only at the lawful speed/contact level; otherwise schedule the next permitted opportunity.
