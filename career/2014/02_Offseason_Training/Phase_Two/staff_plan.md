# Phase Two staff plan | Jacksonville Jaguars | 2014

**Work window:** May 5 to 22; staff reviews May 9 and 16, handoff May 23.  
**Purpose:** Carry classroom assignments into movement and individual technique before opposed team work begins. This is intended work; the [training report](training_report.md) records what occurred.

The [shared program instructions](../Phase_One/staff_plan.md#player-development-and-shared-program-instructions), [staff decisions](../staff_decisions.md), [April 18 direction](../stone_april_18_2014_decisions.md) and [verified phase rules](../../../../library/2014_offseason_phase_rules_verification.md) apply. Use active Iteration I, actual control and medical instructions, and the completed Phase One evidence. Possession of the full playbook does not establish installation or execution.

## Three weeks of field teaching

May 5 to 8 takes the classroom work onto the field. May 12 to 15 changes a meaningful feature while retaining the concept and checks prior corrections after a delay. May 19 to 22 integrates the eligible rookies after their separate minicamp, keeping their primary jobs first without resetting the veterans' work. Staff reviews are not extra practices. The May 8 to 10 draft and May 16 to 17 rookie camp retain their own records and participants.

No helmets, live contact or offense against defense are permitted. Run the offense, defense and special teams separately; a coach's card supplies a changed picture, not an opposing player. Use individual instruction, demonstration, rehearsal, correction and a later independent opportunity without turning those steps into whole-player grades.


As the calendar permits individual/group football teaching and walkthrough-speed work, connect physical training to football responsibilities.

### Offensive emphasis

- stance/alignment;
- formation recognition;
- personnel and role labels;
- run-footwork fundamentals;
- route landmarks;
- ball-security technique;
- protection technique;
- center/QB/back communication;
- shift/motion mechanics where permitted;
- concept teaching without pretending walkthrough success equals team-execution mastery.

### Defensive emphasis

- stance/alignment;
- front and leverage rules;
- pursuit landmarks;
- run-fit responsibility;
- coverage landmarks;
- communication;
- rush-lane responsibility;
- tackling approach/positioning taught without creating prohibited live-contact work;
- Nickel Even alignment and communication as the main sub-package teaching body;
- Cover 1 leverage, help location and man ownership for corners, nickel, safeties and linebackers;
- the existing 1 Robber / Robber-Rat family, with post-safety, underneath robber/rat and flat/force responsibilities stated before the rep;
- Cover 3 and Quarters from related presentations so the same alignment does not announce man coverage.

### Special-teams emphasis

- stance/start mechanics;
- protection/release technique;
- lane and leverage rules;
- return-path landmarks;
- ball handling;
- specialist operation;
- substitution responsibilities.

Phase Two is where the staff should find vocabulary problems before eleven-man work exposes them.

For the remaining May Phase Two work, teach the man-capable emphasis without creating prohibited offense-versus-defense drills. DBs work press/off leverage, releases and help rules separately; linebackers and safeties work back/tight-end ownership, post help, robber/rat landmarks and flat/force exchanges in permitted individual/group work. Bunch, stack and motion rules are taught on the board and at permitted walkthrough speed. The objective is common language before OTAs provide lawful non-contact opposition.

## Personnel and staff responsibility

Tice coordinates the offense, Crennel the defense and Westhoff special teams. Stone's actual May 12 starting-point decisions govern the later work: Nicks at WR1, Adams at WR3 when eligible, Bitonio at left guard, Turner at right guard and Mincey at Edge 1. These are starting points with open competition, not outcomes the plan can guarantee. The four unsigned tenders remain outside the program under the May 1 instruction. Cross-training stays secondary to each player's primary job and gives no automatic role.

The position coaches own individual corrections; coordinators check that adjacent positions received compatible instructions. Follow the existing [film queue](../../00_Team_Operations/Film/player_queue.md) and [delivery log](../../00_Team_Operations/Film/delivery_log.md): a prepared packet is not a delivered one. Retain the shared plan's coach responsibilities and outstanding 2013 feedback obligations.

## Handoff to OTAs

Record what held in unopposed movement, what needed correction and what opposition must test. Preserve the individual coaching note on the existing annual card when it materially changes the staff's view; no new full assessment is created at the phase boundary. Update an existing battle card when the comparison changes. OTAs must test live recognition and exchanges without importing contact conclusions from this work.
