# Rookie minicamp | Jacksonville Jaguars | 2014

<!-- sim-meta: {"kind": "phase_output", "status": "COMPLETE", "through": "2014-05-17", "event_ref": "2014-05-17-rookie-minicamp-complete"} -->

**Location:** Jacksonville club facilities; individual fields were not specified.  
**Dates:** May 16 and 17.  
**Practices completed:** Two practices.  
**Work addressed:** Introductory formation and fit language, primary-job technique, correction retention and special-teams assignments.

**May 16 and 17, 2014.** The two days gave the staff an initial look at how the 26 signed rookies learned their assignments. Linsley's protection calls, Telvin Smith's fit communication and Phillips's work between the secondary and coverage units were the strongest complete pieces of teaching evidence. Several of the useful corrections were less conspicuous: getting the right split, keeping the launch point and making sure two adjacent defenders understood the same fit.

This camp was unopposed and non-contact. The nine draft selections and 17 undrafted signings worked without veterans or tryout players added to the drills. Medical staff communicated no participation restriction; Lucas's foot remained a review item.

## First offensive install

Friday began with the huddle, cadence, personnel and formation language, then the jobs within Inside Zone, Power and Stick. Receivers and tight ends learned the X, Z, H, Y, R and F labels and the Normal, Reduced, Tight, Wide and Stack splits. Saturday kept the concept and changed a formation detail to check whether the player could still find his assignment.

The first split work exposed a problem with the explanation. Receivers and tight ends were taking different alignments for Reduced and Tight. Several had already asked to see the splits drawn against a defender. Drake and Zernhelt used that presentation and walked the alignments again before judging the next work. Hurns then had an individual error when a formation change moved him from X to Z. He corrected the assignment and kept it on Saturday when the modifier changed again. Adams kept his Stick landmark after the shared split teaching.

Shaw could run the huddle and state the first Stick progression and pressure answer. His launch point drifted when the carded protection point changed. Bates walked the footwork again, and Shaw kept the correction against Saturday's changed card. Linsley identified the point and used the combination calls with him; his first snap-to-set transition was late, but Saturday's changed front did not bring the timing problem back.

Yarno's line work remained specific. Bitonio corrected rising pad level during Power footwork. Turner needed a second walkthrough of the ME/YOU twist communication and got it right Saturday. Norwell carried his hand-placement correction to the other guard spot. Lucas corrected his set landmark, but there was not time for his Saturday retest. Brate learned the attached and wing alignments, Jensen repeated the combination communication correctly after another walk, and Hoskins handled the H and F move alignments. Contact blocking remains untested for all of them.

## First defensive install

Crennel's group started with the Under front, a primary run fit and coverage job, Cover 1 and Cover 3 leverage, contain and pursuit. Pleasant and Bush found that the ends and linebackers disagreed about the C-gap when SPILL changed to BOX. They restated the shared rule before Saturday's motion work. The rookies needed one answer from the coaches before they could be expected to give one another the right answer.

Donald explained his gap and the call's purpose, then corrected a rush lane that had widened during group work. Telvin Smith communicated his fit with the adjacent end and carried a pursuit-angle correction into the changed motion picture. Christian Jones was late reaching a coverage landmark Friday and reached it correctly on Saturday's repeat. Phillips took his corrected approach angle from a tackling-position drill into coverage-unit lane work without a reminder. Thomas had only a small sample at safety and nickel; there was no basis for deciding where he should play from these two days.

## Special teams and the next practice

Westhoff taught alignment, lane responsibility, release and protection assignments, ball security and substitution procedure. Gabriel handled the assigned return-path and communication work; no returner was designated. Kreiter snapped consistently into a net and to a coach. There was no eligible punter or holder in this group, so he could not establish operation timing with the veteran specialists here.

The rookies join Phase Two on May 19 with a written teaching note for each player. Lucas needs the delayed set-landmark check. Shaw and the centers need more work identifying the point together. Hurns needs the same assignment from a different label, and the defensive group needs to keep its fit and coverage calls when the presentation changes. Kreiter can begin working with Anger and Scobee.

Bitonio, Turner and Adams carry the rep starting points Stone already assigned on May 12. This camp added evidence about how to coach them; it did not decide those competitions or any other roster place. The complete individual notes for all 26 players remain in the [teaching record](#player-teaching-record).

**COMPLETE.** The historical two-day camp ran Friday, May 16 and Saturday, May 17, 2014, with the published field window of 1:10 to 3:15 p.m. each day and no May 18 practice. [Plan](staff_plan.md) owns intended work and is unchanged; the coaching findings are integrated below. The offensive and defensive Iteration I books were the only books in the building.

## Participants and control

Twenty-six players took part: the nine draft selections, whose rookie contracts were signed May 11, and the 17 undrafted rookies signed May 10 ([draftees](../../03_Draft/draftees.md); [undrafted signings](../../03_Draft/udfa_signings.md)). Caldwell's office verified each contract before the first football session.

- **Tryout players:** none. No tryout invitation is on record, and none was invented to fill a drill.
- **Other first-year players:** none. The record establishes no first-year eligibility for anyone outside the 26; the six reserve/future players and the 2013 rookies are not rookies and were not invited. No veteran was inserted to build an opposition group.
- **James Hurst** stays on the undrafted board pending a medical clearance. He received no contact beyond that hold.
- **Rookie development program (from May 12):** the individual school-date checks the plan requires are not on record, so no rookie field program work is claimed before May 16. May 12 to 15 was onboarding, medical processing and orientation only.

## Onboarding gate

The framework's welcome sequence was compressed into the post-draft window and dated as it happened, not backdated.

- **Packages, on signing:** the 17 undrafted rookies received the welcome package on May 10 and the nine draftees on May 11: Stone's letter, the Prowl pamphlet, the physical-readiness and medical-authority explanation, the support-resource map, the position material, the rookie-minicamp roadmap and the complete offensive and defensive Iteration I books. The package states that possessing the whole book does not mean the book is installed or testable.
- **Stone's calls, May 12 to 13:** Stone called all 26 individually, roughly two days after receipt. The calls confirmed receipt and asked how each player learns and what the staff should know about coaching him; nothing private became a note. The one football pattern across the calls was a request, from several receivers and tight ends, to see the split families drawn against a defender rather than described.
- **Position-coach calls, May 12 to 14:** Bates (Shaw), Drake (Adams, Hurns, Gabriel), Zernhelt (Brate, Jensen, Hoskins), Yarno (the nine linemen), Pleasant (Donald, Edebali, Jeffcoat), Bush (Telvin Smith, Christian Jones, Todd Davis), Oden (Thomas, Butler, Phillips) and Westhoff (Kreiter, and each rookie with a coverage-unit path). Every call was a receipt and communication conversation, not a quiz.
- **No promise:** no letter, call or player's enthusiasm created a role, depth place or roster expectation.

## Medical processing, May 13 to 15

Club physicals were completed before any field work. The medical staff communicated no restriction for the 26 for this event. That clearance is event-level; it is not a season-long prediction.

- **Cornelius Lucas:** his physical was the specific check the record required, because he was measured at the combine on crutches after a pre-combine foot stress fracture. The physical was his first Jacksonville medical event. No restriction was communicated for camp work, and the foot is listed as a performance-staff review item through the spring. No diagnosis beyond the pre-draft public fact is recorded here; the medical staff owns any later communication.
- No injury or new restriction was communicated during the two days.

## Work performed

Both days used Explain, Show, Walk, Rep, Correct, Rep again, then Retain on Saturday with one presentation changed. No contact, no offense against defense, no conditioning test and no running as punishment.

**Friday, May 16.** Teaching block A first: meeting-room terminology, huddle procedure, personnel and substitution communication, formation and strength language, cadence, where to stand, what question must be asked at once, how correction is delivered and how a medical limit is communicated. Then position fundamentals.

- **Offense (Tice; Bates, Drake, Zernhelt, Yarno):** formation, strength and eligible labels (X, Z, H, Y, R, F), the five split families (Normal, Reduced, Tight, Wide, Stack), Inside Zone and Power footwork, Stick, and the protection language that Stick and those runs need: the center sets the point, others echo only what changes their rule, ACE, DEUCE, MIKE and ME/YOU introduced only where the installed work used them. No second variant was added to any concept.
- **Defense (Crennel; Pleasant, Bush, Oden):** Under alignment and fit language, force language, one primary run responsibility and one primary coverage responsibility each, Cover 1 and Cover 3 leverage and communication, rush-lane and contain responsibility, pursuit, and tackling position on bags. TAKE, GIVE and ANSWER were stated on each taught call.
- **Special teams (Westhoff):** alignment, stance, release and protection assignment, lane and leverage responsibility, block avoidance, return-team communication, ball security and the substitution and emergency-backup procedure. Kreiter snapped into a net and to a coach's hands; no punter or holder was eligible, so operation timing waits for Phase Two.

**Saturday, May 17.** Each room repeated Friday's correction, then changed one presentation: a formation modifier on offense with the concept unchanged; a single motion on defense with the taught TRAVEL or BUMP answer; a different return-team alignment on special teams. Independent responses were sought where time allowed; where it did not, the item is carried to May 19.

## Staff review and observed standouts

The strongest complete teaching tapes belonged to **Corey Linsley** (point-setting language and a retained timing correction), **Telvin Smith** (fit communication and space movement, with the angle correction retained under motion) and **Adrian Phillips** (coverage communication and a correction carried into coverage-unit work without a prompt). "Standout" means the two-day evidence supported the next teaching opportunity. It does not establish a starter, a roster place or a future outcome, and it does not rank the draft.

Two shared slowdowns were treated as teaching-presentation issues, not player faults:

- **Split families.** Receivers and tight ends gave different alignments for Reduced and Tight on the first walk. Drake and Zernhelt redrew the families against a defender, as several players had asked on their calls, and re-walked before evaluating the next reps.
- **C-gap ownership under SPILL and BOX.** Ends and linebackers disagreed on who owned the C-gap when the edge tag changed. Pleasant and Bush restated the rule from the book's Under foundation before Saturday's motion picture.

Every participant leaves with a written baseline and next teaching step under the adopted individual-feedback policy. The notes are held by the position coaches and are teaching records, not grades. No film packet was issued, so the [delivery log](../../00_Team_Operations/Film/delivery_log.md) has no new receipt.

No depth chart, roster place, package share or permanent role was decided here; Bitonio, Turner and Adams work at rep starting points set by Stone's May 12 instruction. Hurst's medical hold is unchanged. The four unsigned tenders were not part of this event.
