# 2014 coaching staff: departures and hiring

**Start here:** [dated staff timeline](timeline.md), [replacement targets](staff_plan.md), [Jacksonville hiring record](hires.md), [current staff and contracts](../../00_team/coaching_staff/coaching_staff.md).

This folder holds Jacksonville's 2014 coaching-staff turnover: other clubs' requests to interview Stone's assistants, Jacksonville's answers, any departures and the hiring of replacements. It was created on September 28, 2026 at the user's request (branch date February 2, 2014).

**Authority:** Stone hires, retains, assigns and fires his own assistants within Caldwell's staff budget (Document 3, authority row 2). Whether another club may interview an assistant under contract follows the league's anti-tampering rules and, where those leave a choice, Jacksonville's decision.

## Current position

Resolved through February 17, 2014. Twelve assistants are under contract and there is no vacancy.

- Alan Lowry, special teams coordinator, left on January 12, 2014 to become Atlanta's head coach. Stone directed the kicking game on an interim basis until Mike Westhoff was hired as special teams coordinator on February 11, 2014: $750,000 a season for 2014 and 2015, reporting to Stone.
- Frank Bush, linebackers coach, interviewed for defensive coordinator with Indianapolis on January 14 and with Chicago on February 6, both with Jacksonville's permission, and was hired by neither (Chicago hired elsewhere on February 9). Atlanta's January 12 request to interview him for its linebackers job was refused as lateral.
- Minnesota's February 17 requests for Jeremy Bates (quarterbacks) and Mike Tice (offensive coordinator) were refused as lateral. No assistant left in February.
- The other assistants received no request and remain under contract.
- Five clubs changed head coaches by February 2: Atlanta, Cincinnati, Denver, Indianapolis and San Francisco. After the Super Bowl, Buffalo kept Doug Marrone and Minnesota made an external head-coach hire on February 17.

**Emergency practice lead (April 18):** Mike Tice runs practice if Stone is out, with Romeo Crennel second. Their authority is narrow: the approved schedule, no role, install or personnel change ([April 18 decisions](../../03_offseason_training/stone_april_18_2014_decisions.md#practice-if-stone-is-out)). Succession for a game-day caller is not covered and remains unassigned.

## Interview permission: January 2014 policy

This clarification (ledger Entry 77) explains the existing policy without changing its decisions. Apply the researched [2013-14 rules](../../../../library/2014_coaching_hiring_and_anti_tampering_rules.md), including employment status and the relevant window, before applying a club preference.

- **Head-coach vacancy:** grant an eligible interview where the rules require it after Jacksonville's season. Within a permitted playoff window, Jacksonville's standing policy also grants permission, subject to the applicable scheduling and repeat-interview limits. "Grant" does not permit an interview outside the window.
- **Position coach to coordinator:** voluntarily grant an eligible interview after Jacksonville's season ends. Under the 2013-14 rules this is still an assistant-to-assistant move that the club could block, despite the greater responsibility. The grant is club policy, not a protected promotion under the later coordinator-interview rule.
- **Same-job request for an assistant under contract:** refuse under the standing club policy. This does not give Jacksonville a veto over a coach whose contract has expired or who has been released. Other role changes require the actual duties and existing authority to be checked; the current policy supplies no automatic answer for every possible title.

The user may change Jacksonville's discretionary policy prospectively. A policy change cannot waive league restrictions or undo a closed outcome. Interview permission, the coach's decision to participate, an offer, release/contract handling where applicable, and acceptance remain distinct. The frozen January method and result files are historical artifacts and are not rewritten by this clarification.
