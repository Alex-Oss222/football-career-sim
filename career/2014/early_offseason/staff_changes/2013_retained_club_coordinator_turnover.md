# Coordinator turnover at clubs that kept their head coach: the January 2013 cycle

**Function:** the sourced base rate behind stage D2 of `carousel_deferred_method.json` (coordinator openings at clubs that kept their head coach, and who filled them). **Researched:** September 29, 2026, by a research pass and a separate skeptical verification pass, both limited to search-result text (page fetches were blocked by the network proxy). **Hindsight boundary:** only the January-February 2013 cycle (after the 2012 season, before the branch divergence) is recorded; no 2014-cycle fact is used.

## Verified findings

VERIFIED FINDINGS: 2013 COORDINATOR CYCLE (hires in January-February 2013, after the 2012 season). This is public before the in-sim date of January 2014 and is not future information. Labels: Confirmed = two independent sources; Single = one source; Unverified = no source found.

SCOPE: Eight clubs changed head coach and are excluded: KC, PHI, CHI, SD, BUF, CLE, ARI, JAX. That leaves 24 clubs that kept their head coach.

OC OPENINGS (5)
1. CAR. Chudzinski left to be CLE head coach. QB coach Mike Shula was promoted in-house on Jan 18. Confirmed: PFT 2013/01/18 https://profootballtalk.nbcsports.com/2013/01/18/panthers-promote-mike-shula-to-offensive-coordinator/ ; ESPN https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/8858189/mike-shula-carolina-panthers-promoted-offensive-coordinator ; NFL.com 0ap1000000128144.
2. DEN. McCoy left to be SD head coach. QB coach Adam Gase was promoted in-house on Jan 17. Confirmed: denverbroncos.com https://www.denverbroncos.com/news/gase-promoted-to-offensive-coordinator-9379229 ; Mile High Report 2013/1/17 ; ESPN 8853314.
3. IND. Arians left to be ARI head coach. Pep Hamilton was hired from Stanford (college OC) on Jan 18 [CORRECTED from Jan 19-22]. Confirmed: NFL.com https://www.nfl.com/news/indianapolis-colts-hire-pep-hamilton-to-run-offense-0ap1000000128145 ; NFL.com 0ap1000000128373 ; Wikipedia.
4. NYJ. Sparano was fired. Marty Mornhinweg, the ex-PHI OC displaced by Reid's exit, was hired on Jan 18 (lateral, displaced coordinator). Confirmed: ESPN 8858412 ; NFL.com 0ap1000000128168 ; SI wire 2013/01/19.
5. OAK. Knapp was fired. Greg Olson, JAX assistant head coach/QB coach under the outgoing staff, was hired around Jan 18-19 [refined]. He was a position coach displaced by JAX's head-coach change. Confirmed: Silver and Black Pride 2013/1/18 https://www.silverandblackpride.com/2013/1/18/3893266/ ; SI/AP 2013/01/19 ; Wikipedia.

OC CHANGES NOT COUNTED AS OPENINGS

- TEN: Loggains had been acting OC since late 2012 and was made permanent. Confirmed: tennesseetitans.com https://www.tennesseetitans.com/news/titans-announce-changes-to-coaching-staff-9382420 ; titansized.com 2013/07/08 ; Wikipedia.
- DAL: play-calling moved to Callahan with no change in coordinator title. Single: CBS.

DC OPENINGS (4)
1. DAL. Rob Ryan was fired Jan 8. Monte Kiffin was hired from USC (college DC) on Jan 11. Confirmed: dallascowboys.com https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/monte-kiffin-officially-hired-as-cowboys-d-coordinator-334101 ; Bucs Nation 2013/1/11 ; CBC.
2. SEA. Bradley left to be JAX head coach. Dan Quinn was hired from Florida (college DC) on Jan 17. Confirmed: Field Gulls 2013/1/17 ; Alligator Army 2013/1/17 ; PFT.
3. NO. Spagnuolo was fired on Jan 24 (the club was switching to a 3-4). Rob Ryan interviewed Feb 8 and was announced Feb 9 (lateral, displaced coordinator). Firing confirmed: PFT https://profootballtalk.nbcsports.com/2013/01/24/saints-fire-steve-spagnuolo-announce-plans-for-3-4-defense/ ; ESPN 8878344. Hire: NFL.com http://www.nfl.com/news/story/0ap1000000137607 plus secondary summaries (now two sources).
4. STL. The DC title had been vacant through 2012 because of Gregg Williams' suspension. Tim Walton, DET secondary coach 2009-12 and a sitting position coach at a club that kept its head coach, was agreed or reported Feb 12 and announced Friday Feb 15. Confirmed: NFL.com https://www.nfl.com/news/tim-walton-becomes-rams-new-defensive-coordinator-0ap1000000139528 ; Bleacher Report 1527557 ; Wikipedia.

STC OPENINGS (8)
1. DAL. DeCamillis left for CHI's new staff. Rich Bisaccia (SD STC 2011-12, briefly at Auburn in January) was reported around Jan 25 and made official Jan 30 (lateral). Confirmed: Blogging The Boys 2013/1/25 ; Dallas Morning News 2013/01/30 ; ESPN 8898770.
2. NYJ. Westhoff retired Dec 30, 2012. Assistant STC Ben Kotwica was promoted in-house. Confirmed [upgraded]: newyorkjets.com https://www.newyorkjets.com/news/kotwica-will-pick-up-where-mike-left-off-9314229 ; Wikipedia Ben Kotwica.
3. PIT. Amos Jones left for Arians' ARI staff. Danny Smith, WAS STC for nine seasons, was reported Jan 30 and made official Jan 31. This was a lateral poach of a sitting coordinator from a club that kept its head coach. Confirmed: Behind the Steel Curtain 2013/1/30 ; The Columbian 2013/jan/30 ; steelers.com ; Wikipedia.
4. WAS. Smith left. Keith Burns, DEN assistant STC for six seasons and a sitting position coach at a club that kept its head coach, was hired Feb 11 (reported Feb 11; AP carried it Feb 12). Confirmed: Washington Post 2013/02/11 ; PFT 2013/02/11 ; The Columbian 2013/feb/12.
5. IND. Maalouf left by mutual agreement around Jan 12. Tom McMahon, KC STC in 2012 and displaced when Reid brought in Toub, was hired (lateral). The move is confirmed (colts.com; Wikipedia). The hire date is UNVERIFIED.
6. DET. Crossman was fired. John Bonamego, JAX STC in 2012 [CORRECTED], was hired, a displaced coordinator after JAX's head-coach change. His 2012 role is confirmed by jaguars.com/NFL.com (Mularkey's 2012 staff hires) and Wikipedia. The Crossman firing is single-source (NFL.com 0ap1000000126490). The hire date is UNVERIFIED.
7. TB. Ligashesky was fired. Dave Wannstedt, BUF DC in 2012 and displaced, was reported Feb 1 and the staff announced Feb 7. Confirmed: PFT 2013/02/01 ; Bay News 9 2013/2/7 ; buccaneers.com staff release.
8. TEN. Lowry was not retained after 17 seasons. Nate Kaczor, TEN assistant OL coach in 2012 and a former JAX assistant STC, was promoted in-house. The role is confirmed (tennesseetitans.com; titansized.com; Wikipedia). The exact date is UNVERIFIED.

CLUBS WITH NO COORDINATOR OPENING (10): ATL, BAL, CIN, GB, HOU, MIA, MIN, NE, NYG, SF. These are UNVERIFIED as a set, because I did not search each club individually in either pass. Note that Titans' Jerry Gray stayed as DC, so TEN had no DC opening.

RATES (my own counts, not from a source), across the 24 clubs that kept their head coach
- Clubs with at least one opening: 14 of 24.
- Openings: 17 in total. That is 5 OC, 4 DC and 8 STC, or 21%, 17% and 33% of clubs.
- How the 17 were filled:
  - Sitting position coach from another club that kept its head coach: 2 (Walton, Burns).
  - In-house promotion: 4 (Shula, Gase, Kotwica, Kaczor).
  - College coordinator: 3 (Kiffin, Quinn, Hamilton).
  - Sitting or displaced NFL coordinator (lateral): 7 (Mornhinweg, Ryan, Bisaccia, D. Smith, McMahon, Wannstedt, Bonamego).
  - Displaced position coach promoted to coordinator: 1 (Olson, borderline).
  - Total: 2+4+3+7+1 = 17.
- Sitting coordinator poached laterally from a club that kept its head coach: 1 (Danny Smith, WAS→PIT). Every other coordinator who left came from a club with a head-coach change, or was fired first.
- Hires on or after Feb 1: 4 of 17. Those are Wannstedt (reported Feb 1, announced Feb 7), Ryan (Feb 9), Burns (Feb 11) and Walton (Feb 12-15). Both hires of a sitting position coach from another club came after Feb 1. The dates for McMahon, Bonamego and Kaczor are unknown.
- Relevance to Jacksonville's own coaches: in 2013 the realistic ways a coach was poached were:
  - a coordinator hired as a head coach elsewhere (Chudzinski, McCoy, Arians, Bradley: all at clubs changing head coach);
  - a position coach taking a coordinator title elsewhere (Walton, Burns, Olson);
  - rarely, a lateral coordinator move (Smith).

NOT DONE: the January 2012 cycle; any 2014-cycle facts (these are deliberately excluded because they would be contemporaneous or future for the sim); individual checks of the 10 no-opening clubs.

## Corrections made by the verification pass

- Bonamego (DET STC): his 2012 job was Jacksonville special teams coordinator under Mularkey, not unknown. Sources: jaguars.com and NFL.com 'Mularkey hires four more assistants' (2012), plus Wikipedia John Bonamego. He was displaced by Jacksonville's head-coach change, so he moves out of 'borderline position coach' and into displaced or lateral former coordinators. His DET hire date is still Unverified.
- Pep Hamilton (IND OC): hired Jan 18, 2013, not Jan 19-22. There are two NFL.com articles (0ap1000000128145 and 0ap1000000128373) and Wikipedia. The Stampede Blue piece dated Jan 22 is commentary written after the hire.
- Rich Bisaccia (DAL STC): the hire was first reported as official around Jan 25 (Blogging The Boys 2013/1/25). The Dallas Morning News and dallascowboys.com formal announcement was Jan 30. Auburn released him from his contract to let him go.
- Jim Caldwell (BAL OC): SI and other outlets reported Jan 21, and Wikipedia says Harbaugh announced it on Jan 22. It now has two sources (SI 2013/01/21 and hannibal.net AP 2013/01/21). It is still a non-opening, because he was the interim coordinator made permanent.
- Tim Walton (STL DC): Wikipedia gives Feb 12, 2013 as the hire date, and NFL.com says the team 'announced Friday', which was Feb 15, 2013. Best reading: agreed or reported Feb 12, announced Feb 15.
- Danny Smith (PIT STC): the move was reported Jan 30 (PFT, Behind the Steel Curtain, Hogs Haven, The Columbian) and made official Jan 31 (steelers.com, Wikipedia). He was Washington's sitting coordinator of nine seasons, and Washington kept its head coach. That makes this a lateral poach of a sitting coordinator from a club that kept its head coach, which matters when assessing poaching risk.
- Rob Ryan (NO DC): Feb 8 interview and Feb 9 announcement, now two sources (NFL.com and a 1079ishot summary). The Spagnuolo firing on Jan 24 is confirmed by PFT, ESPN and Canal Street Chronicles.
- Ben Kotwica (NYJ STC): upgraded from Single to Confirmed. Westhoff's retirement on Dec 30, 2012 and Kotwica's promotion appear in newyorkjets.com and Wikipedia Ben Kotwica.
- Nate Kaczor (TEN STC): the role is confirmed by Wikipedia and the Washington team site bio (TEN assistant OL coach 2012, then TEN STC 2013-15). The exact January 2013 date is still Unverified.
- Tom McMahon (IND STC): the move from KC STC in 2012 to IND STC in 2013 is confirmed by colts.com and Wikipedia. The exact hire date is still Unverified; no dated 2013 article was found.
- Greg Olson (OAK OC): Silver and Black Pride reported the hire on Jan 18, 2013 (article 2013/1/18), and Big Cat Country reported his interview on Jan 13. The hire is around Jan 18-19, not only the 19th.
