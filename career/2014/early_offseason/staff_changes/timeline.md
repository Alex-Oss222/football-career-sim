## Keeping this page current

After a recorded interview request, permission answer, interview, withdrawal, offer, departure, refusal or appointment, refresh the timeline and status rows from the actual event record in the same update. Link each new dated row to that record and its closed ledger entry. Keep the original January method and results intact; a later event gets its own dated record.

For Jacksonville's own search, [hires.md](hires.md) records what happened and [staff_plan.md](staff_plan.md) records Stone's choices before it happens. A staff appointment or departure also updates the current staff register, affected authority/delegation, salary commitments, state and calendar under the [repository update workflow](../../../../docs/update_workflow.md). This timeline is a summary, not a competing event ledger.
