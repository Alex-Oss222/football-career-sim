# Jacksonville 2014 draftees and rookie contracts

**Draft dates:** May 8 (Round 1), May 9 (Rounds 2 and 3) and May 10 (Rounds 4 to 7), 2014.
**Information ceiling:** May 8, 2014, 8 p.m. ET, then each selection's own time. Only evidence public before a selection was used for it.
**Status: COMPLETE.** Jacksonville exercised all nine selections and signed all nine rookie contracts on May 11, 2014, before the May 16 and 17 rookie minicamp, as the 2013 precedent did (all seven 2013 draftees signed before that year's minicamp).
**Historical boundary:** every selection below is branch canon. Other clubs' selections are their real selections under the [league rails](../league/personnel/method.md), and each target's availability was tested against the real draft at Jacksonville's branch pick: a player was available at branch pick N only if his real selection was N or later, or he went undrafted. No later NFL outcome was used to choose or grade anyone.

## Rookie contracts, executed May 11, 2014

Caldwell signed all nine on Sunday, May 11, before rookie minicamp, matching the 2013 precedent. Each is a four-year contract under the 2011 CBA rookie scale; the two first-round contracts carry the fifth-year club option for 2018, unexercised and not booked.

**Method: slot reconstruction from the same-slot 2014 contract.** The NFL-NFLPA rookie freeze held signing bonuses flat from 2012 to 2014 ([SI, May 22, 2014](https://www.si.com/nfl/2014/05/22/nfl-rookie-contract-negotiations)), so each pick is priced by its slot from the real 2014 contract signed at that same overall number, taken from the nflverse copy of the Over The Cap historical contracts file (fields draft_overall, years, value, guaranteed). The total and the guarantee are the slot contract's; the structure is the branch's: minimum-scale bases of $420,000, $510,000, $600,000 and $690,000 for 2014 to 2017, verified against the 2014 CBA minimum schedule in the [financial rules](../../../library/2014_league_calendar_and_financial_rules.md#2a-2014-minimum-salaries-by-credited-seasons-2011-cba-article-26-schedule), and a signing bonus equal to the remainder, prorated over four years. Where the slot contract's guarantee exceeds that bonus (picks 13, 26 and 38) the excess is guaranteed base salary from 2014 forward; where its total exceeds the bonus plus the minimum bases (pick 90, by $45,000) the excess sits in the 2017 base. These are branch contracts, not the players' real deals, and none of the slot comparators joins Jacksonville.

## Onboarding and medical

Signing creates control and an evaluation opportunity only. Each rookie's welcome package, complete Iteration I books and the Day-2 Stone and position-coach calls are scheduled for the May 12 to 15 onboarding window and the May 16 and 17 rookie minicamp, under the [rookie minicamp plan](../offseason_training/rookie_minicamp/staff_plan.md); receipts belong in that phase output, not here. No rookie has a communicated restriction; Cornelius Lucas's pre-combine foot stress fracture (undrafted, see [undrafted signings](udfa_signings.md)) and every rookie's physical are checked before field work. No depth position, rep share or roster place is promised by any contract above.
