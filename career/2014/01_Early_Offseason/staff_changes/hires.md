## Recording rules

For any later search, append dated entries for requests, permission decisions, interviews, candidate withdrawals or refusals, offers and acceptances, keeping those stages distinct. Identify the coach, role, current employer or rights holder where relevant, what was communicated, the result and the dated source record. An accepted appointment states the effective date, agreed terms, reporting line, operating responsibilities and the work transferred from any interim lead, and closes with its dependent staff, financial, authority and state updates. Preserve previous search entries rather than rewriting them to match the outcome.

Other clubs' hiring of Jacksonville assistants belongs in the dated outside-request/outcome records, linked from the [timeline](timeline.md). Lowry's Atlanta appointment is a departure, not a Jacksonville hire.
