- **Retained by Stone.** Alex Stone decides fourth downs, two-point tries, onside kicks, timeouts and clock management and replay challenges, with staff recommendations ([Document 3](../../foundation/03_Head_Coach_Organization_and_Authority_Canon.md#4-game-day-staff-responsibilities)). Stone's live calls: fourth downs (a live pause, unless the week's call sheet freezes a rule). From the week's frozen call sheet: two-point tries (the call sheet's frozen rule, or a live pause when the rule says ask); onside kicks (the call sheet's frozen kickoff rule, or a live pause when the rule says ask). Not Stone's live calls: timeouts and clock management (set by the game model with each replayed drive, not by Stone (defect register item 30); [record](../../runtime/defect_register.md)); replay challenges (no challenge decision exists in the game).
- **Game-day information.** Mike Tice: the offensive situation and the next eligible opening call; Jeremy Bates: quarterback and protection information; Charlie Skalaski: clock, timeout, fourth-down and challenge information ([record](05_Regular_Season/Games/Week_03/Week%203%20Packet_%20Indianapolis.md#decisions-needed-from-stone)).
- **Emergency leads.** Practice: Mike Tice, then Romeo Crennel, from April 18, 2014: run the approved schedule, keep the periods on time, coordinate the three phases and make ordinary situational adjustments; no depth-chart, install, role, disciplinary or personnel change. Unchanged: Crennel continues to run the defense and Westhoff special teams; medical staff retain all participation authority ([record](02_Offseason_Training/stone_april_18_2014_decisions.md#practice-if-stone-is-out)). Game-day caller: not appointed.

### Standing instructions
- **Calling arrangement (in force):** Stone can call, delegate or take back any phase at any time; continue the arrangement until he changes it, without making it a recurring planning question. [Record](00_Team_Operations/Staff/coaching_staff.md#phase-control)
- **Inactives (in force, from August 31, 2014):** The deepest healthy reserve on the chart in each of the five largest healthy position groups, never below a legal game-day unit, re-set each week. [Record](04_Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Roster_Decisions/roster_decisions.md#august-31-2014-the-week-1-inactives-stone-document-3-row-8)
- **Two-point tries (in force):** Kick, except go for two in the fourth quarter when the touchdown leaves Jacksonville down 2, up 1 or down 5. [Record](05_Regular_Season/Games/Week_05/call_sheet.json)
- **Kickoffs (in force):** Onside when trailing by 1 to 16 with 2:00 or less left; onside when trailing by 9 to 16 with 5:00 or less left; deep otherwise. [Record](05_Regular_Season/Games/Week_05/call_sheet.json)
- **Fourth downs (in force):** Fourth-and-2 or less, go, with Tice's first call on the matching menu; fourth-and-3 or 4, go on the opponent's side of midfield, otherwise the field goal or the punt by the field-goal line; fourth-and-5 or more, a field goal when the kick is 52 yards or less, otherwise a punt; frozen into each week's call sheet as its decisions.fourth_down block. [Record](05_Regular_Season/Games/Week_05/output.md#addendum-october-18-2026-the-fourth-down-rule-stands-for-every-game)
