## Follow the season

The numbered directories sort in the order their work begins. The draft overlaps spring training; follow the dated calendar within each phase. Season review follows Jacksonville's actual final game, including any postseason.

A player's opening assessment carries forward and receives dated updates during the year. The separate final assessment follows season close. Training reports supply observations; battle cards compare candidates for an established preseason job. Neither creates another whole-player assessment.

[League personnel](League/personnel/README.md) follows its dated information gates. [Simulation readiness](Supporting_Records/readiness.md) owns unresolved release checks. A scheduled game is not a completed result or game authorization.
