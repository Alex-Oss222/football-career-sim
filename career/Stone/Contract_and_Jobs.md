Signed authority and standards:

- Stone selects the coaching staff, with a funded assistant budget consistent with the staff he proposed; no dollar figure was stated.
- Stone controls the depth chart and game-day decisions.
- General manager David Caldwell has final authority over contracts, the salary cap, scouting administration, acquisitions and draft selections.
- Before a leading quarterback acquisition, disposition or draft commitment, Caldwell must obtain and consider Stone's written football projection and meet him to resolve any disagreement; Caldwell keeps the final call.
- No guarantee to Blaine Gabbert and no advance mandate to draft a quarterback second overall.
- First-season standard: a functioning program, credible player development, dependable preparation and communication, and an evidence-based direction at quarterback; no immediate playoff requirement.

Signed on January 15, 2013: the length, the guarantee and the authority terms. Completed from the wording of Jacksonville's own 2010 assistant contracts: the end date. Completed from comparable head-coach contracts of the time: the salary, the offset, the dismissal terms, the signing bonus and the options. ## Contacts from other clubs

None recorded: no rumor, call, permission request, interview request, offer or accepted job.

A club may not approach a head coach under contract without his club's permission. During Jacksonville's season, playoffs included, no club may ask to speak with him, and he may not discuss another job unless he is dismissed. After the season, a move to another club's head-coaching job needs Jacksonville's consent, which it may refuse ([the league's coaching hiring rules](../../library/2014_coaching_hiring_and_anti_tampering_rules.md#13-head-coaches-under-contract-to-another-club)).
