## 11. 2013 season archive

Jacksonville finished 10-6, second in the AFC South on division record behind Tennessee, and entered as the AFC's fifth seed. All 256 regular-season receipts and the eleven postseason receipts are preserved (`career/2013/stats/`); the standings are in `career/2013/standings.md` and the bracket in `career/2013/postseason/README.md`.
