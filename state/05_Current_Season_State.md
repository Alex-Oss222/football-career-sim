## 11. 2013 season archive

Jacksonville finished 10-6, second in the AFC South on division record behind Tennessee, and entered as the AFC's fifth seed. It won 38-14 at Kansas City in the Wild Card round and lost 20-13 at Tennessee in the Divisional round. Buffalo, the AFC's sixth seed, beat Minnesota 31-20 in Super Bowl XLVIII.

All 256 regular-season receipts and the eleven postseason receipts are preserved (`career/2013/stats/`); the standings are in `career/2013/standings.md` and the bracket in `career/2013/postseason/README.md`. Weekly and monthly league awards are complete in `career/2013/awards/`. Season honours: Maurice Jones-Drew was Comeback Player of the Year, Stone was shortlisted for Coach of the Year, C.J. Spiller was Super Bowl MVP, and Marcedes Lewis played in the Pro Bowl.
