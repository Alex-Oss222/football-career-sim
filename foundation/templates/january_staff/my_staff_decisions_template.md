**What the directions mean:**

- **Pursue renewal** authorizes the appropriate club representatives to begin or continue negotiation within their authority. It does not sign a contract, set compensation or force the coach to accept. A renewal to the same job at no more than his current salary, on the common terms of his contract, is within the promised staff budget once Caldwell confirms it before the offer; anything above returns to Caldwell as his decision.
- **Pursue a revised role** records the football structure you want to discuss. A material demotion or changed job may require agreement; never pretend the title change alone voids guaranteed salary.
- **Let the deal lapse** is a decision not to pursue another agreement, but the departure becomes effective only when the applicable contract actually ends. If the exact expiration is not established, leave that date open and obtain contract confirmation.
- **Do not offer renewal** closes your offer intent; the coach's current deal remains in force until its actual end or a separately authorized termination.
- **Hold for actual review** preserves the open decision and names what you need; no silent renewal or deadline bypass.
