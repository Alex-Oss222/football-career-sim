# [One event: Employment Check Result / Permission Result / Contact Result / Interview Result / Offer Result / Staff Hire Result / Search Closed Result]

*A form of the shared [event result](../event_inserts/event_result_template.md) for one concluded event of a staff search. The kinds are a closed list, each with its own file name in the search's `outcomes/` folder: `Employment_Check_Result_<date>_<candidate>.md` (an unknown employment fact settled), `Permission_Result_` (the employer club granted or refused), `Contact_Result_` (the candidate agreed or declined to talk or interview), `Interview_Result_` (held or cancelled), `Offer_Result_` (an offer that ended without a hire), `Staff_Hire_Result_` (accepted terms signed and effective) and `Search_Closed_Result_<date>.md` (closed without a hire on the head coach's authorized decision). A request still unanswered, a scheduled interview, a pending counter and the funding confirmation are process entries with no page of their own. The departure that opened the job is owned by its own record and linked.*

[The event-record line, on a line of its own under the title block, invisible on the page and without the backticks: `<!-- event-record: {"id": "[date]-[candidate]-[kind]", "date": "[YYYY-MM-DD resolved]", "status": "closed", "summary": "[One sentence in football words: who decided what, and its effect]"} -->`. Its summary is the event's line in the year's Record.md.]

**Event date:** [Actual in-career date and time if recorded; date only if no time]

**Resolved on:** [Date the deciding party answered, or the date the stage properly closed]

**Involving:** [The candidate, his employer club, Jacksonville's participants and anyone else actually involved, not everyone who might have been consulted]

**Search position:** [Actual vacancy, with a link to its Staff Hire page]

**What happened:** [One to three factual sentences on the specific request, call, interview, permission ruling or offer. State the exact role offered or discussed and who could decide. No fabricated quotations, invented interview answers or private motives.]

**Stone's answer:** [His My Hire answer for this stage, linked, or 'No answer from Stone required'. A staff hire is never closed by a standing instruction.]

**Final decision:** [Who had authority and what that party decided: the employer club granted or refused; the candidate agreed, declined or countered; Caldwell confirmed or withheld funding; the contract was signed. No automatic assent.]

**Outcome:** [Granted / refused / agreed / declined / held / cancelled / declined offer / withdrawn / lapsed / signed / search closed]

**Changes effective:** [Specific verified change to personnel, job duties, calls, contract or payroll, with its date; or 'No contract, staff or job change'. The larger search can remain open when this event ends.]

**Outstanding issue:** [The next search step actually authorized, with its link, or 'None']

**Source records:** [The permission correspondence, a dated interview summary if recorded, the authorized terms on My Hire, the accepted contract as the Coach page shows it, and the Staff page]

*One completed event gets one dated outcome. A declined offer does not become a departure; an unaccepted offer does not become a hire. If the same underlying appointment is already owned by an authoritative individual record, link to it rather than duplicating competing outcome pages. The page is football only: other machine data (fingerprints, draw state) stays in the search's data record, and the prose never explains how a result was produced.*
