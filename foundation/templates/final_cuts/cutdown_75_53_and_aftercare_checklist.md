# 2015 reduction to 75, final 53 and post-cutdown: implementation checklist

This is an internal acceptance checklist, not a request for the user to manage files or a career event. The dates are owned by the [2015 calendar](../../../career/2015/Calendar.md) and the rules by the library; the rules are in the [Final Roster Cuts design](../../../docs/final_roster_cuts.md#rules-this-design-follows), which this checklist follows.

## September 1, 2015, before 4 p.m. ET: reduce to 75

- [ ] As-of roster and rights reconciled: signed contracts, the count against the limit, PUP, NFI, reserve and exempt statuses and unsigned tenders distinguished.
- [ ] Actual players named and legally eligible; no future roster assumption and no 2014 count copied to 2015.
- [ ] Medical assessments from the medical staff; the right injured-transaction route checked.
- [ ] Count already at or under 75: a FOR YOUR INFORMATION line with the count, no cut, and the 75 section of My Cut Recommendations not requested.
- [ ] Count over 75: the Roster Cutdown Report asks for the 75 section; no missing answer treated as assent; Caldwell follows Stone's lawful list except on a constraint written into the report before Stone answered.
- [ ] Each concluded player case has its own dated result in its owner's folder, linked from the cutdown index.
- [ ] Count legally at or under 75 by the cutoff.
- [ ] The September 3 game freezes only from that roster.

## September 3: the last preseason game

- [ ] Jacksonville at Washington at 7:30 p.m. ET, as the calendar gives it; the completed game and its medical events verified.
- [ ] Its report does not resolve the September 5 reduction in advance.
- [ ] Camp reports, battle cards and roster advice changed only by new evidence, each at its own owner.

## September 5, 2015, before 4 p.m. ET: reduce to 53

- [ ] The 53-stage edition of the Roster Cutdown Report issued with the 75 count; its evidence includes only completed games, treatments, decisions and signed contracts.
- [ ] Stone fills the 53 section of My Cut Recommendations once; the 75 section is unchanged.
- [ ] Each departure classified by Plan credited service under Article 29: a vested veteran's contract terminated; an unresolved service count treated as a waiver, with the page saying a fourth credited year would make it a termination.
- [ ] Cap effects from the recovered or adopted schedules, reconstructions labelled; no termination pay at this reduction.
- [ ] PUP, NFI and reserve choices legal; the medical staff's conclusions not overridden.
- [ ] Exactly 53 **distinct** legally counted players if the list is filled.
- [ ] `Final_53.md` built once from the roster record at the filing, frozen with a digest, linking the cutdown index and each dated result.
- [ ] Every open battle card closed with Stone's season depth chart, a provisional call allowed; a role whose holder left goes to the next player in his standing order (FOR YOUR INFORMATION) or, with no ranked successor in a starting or specialist role, to Stone (ACTION REQUIRED) before the Week 1 Game Plan freezes.
- [ ] A player kept on the 53 but unavailable for Week 1 is not dressed.

## September 6: waivers, practice squad and designation

- [ ] Claims close at noon ET. Another club claims a Jacksonville waived player only where his real next move was a claim in that window; otherwise he clears.
- [ ] Jacksonville's claims on other clubs' waived players awarded by the waiver order then in force, Jacksonville's place from the career's 2014 standings; the order rule entered in the two-pass rules entry first.
- [ ] Reversion of a player waived with the injured designation, claims and later signings each closed at their own lawful times.
- [ ] No prohibited contact and no automatic return to Jacksonville before a waiver outcome.
- [ ] Practice squad formed only after the personnel notice: up to ten contracts, the two-player accrued-season allowance and prior service verified individually. An approach to a player who really signed elsewhere in the window is decided by the market draw on its real date; any other approached player signs. A proposed name is not a contract.
- [ ] Each failed claim, approach lost in the market draw or ended trade pursuit has its own dated result.
- [ ] After 4 p.m. ET only the period's designated-for-return rule applied, to a qualifying case.
- [ ] Final 53 unchanged; each later move only on its own result page and the roster page.

## Universal delivery and regression

- [ ] Every distinct event lives at its roster, free-agency, trade, medical or staff owner as its **own** dated page; Final 53 and the cutdown index only link it.
- [ ] People and contracts stored once; no duplicate editable roster or cap totals on the cutdown pages.
- [ ] The 2014 preseason games, the 2014 reduction records and the 2014 season files unchanged.
- [ ] All 68 proposed cutdown tests and the existing repository tests run in a future authorized build, not represented as passed by this design.
