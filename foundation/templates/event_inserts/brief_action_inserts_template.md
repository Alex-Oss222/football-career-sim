# Action Items: reusable briefing inserts

**Design status:** Adopted October 20, 2026 from the user's upload; built in step 5 of the [rebuild plan](../../../docs/rebuild_plan.md). This is a reusable insert inside the briefing already active at that time, NOT a new standalone brief. Substitute verified facts and only show relevant items. Do not display these instructions to the coach.

**Outcome rule:** When each inserted event resolves, it receives its **own separate dated output file immediately in that simulated period**. The weekly briefing and shared response page can hold multiple events; their result pages may not be combined. The week/phase index links to each distinct result. Trade offers, including rejected offers, follow the same rule.

## Placement inside the existing brief

Under the existing briefing's title and event/date summary, BEFORE the normal scouting or self-scout:

## ACTION REQUIRED

> **[DECISION TITLE]** | [Received: date/time ET] | **Response due: date/time ET**
>
> **From:** [GM / owner / agent / staff / medical authority / another club, through authorized channel]
>
> **What happened:** [One or two sentences naming the actual offer, request, roster transaction or event. No hypotheticals stated as facts.]
>
> **Why it needs you:** [The specific football recommendation, staff decision, consent or personal answer within your authority.]
>
> **The choices:** [Natural alternatives with factual conditions. Do not invent a closed option list.]
>
> **Staff position:** [Attributed, evidenced view; or “Not yet supplied.”]
>
> **If no agreement is reached:** [Known expiry or consequences; do not substitute silence for an answer or advance across an unresolved mandatory stop.]
>
> **Your answer:** [Link titled for the actual decision in My_Responses.md or the active career response surface.]
>
> **Supporting record:** [Link to one authorized canonical event source, not a second narrative.]
>
> **Individual event result:** [When closed, link this notice to **this event's own dated result**, not a shared results page. Before closure, show “Pending”.]

If there are multiple issues, repeat this compact insert in actual deadline order. Show one top-of-page index, e.g. **Two decisions need your answer**, without inventing a more urgent deadline. If nothing needs an answer, OMIT `ACTION REQUIRED` entirely.

## FOR YOUR INFORMATION

Use this for finalized transactions, ownership decisions, automatic standing-rule responses, confirmed legal eligibility changes, or publicly confirmed coaching vacancies that are not offers to you. This section never blocks and must not read as an open user question.

> **[News headline]** | [Effective date]
>
> **Known fact:** [What occurred and who decided it.]
>
> **Football consequence:** [Only what changes for your work, if anything.]
>
> **Record:** [Link to the authoritative result.]

## COMING UP

Use only for an actionable, dated window that is relevant to an actual person, expiring contract or club decision. A calendar date alone does not create a decision.

> **[Window or deadline]** | [Date/time ET]
>
> **Why it matters:** [Eligibility / trade deadline / contract expiration / coach-interview window.]
>
> **Current state:** [No offer; awaiting review; owner not decided; etc.]
>
> **Action now:** [No answer required, or a clearly identified preparatory task.]

### Event-specific facts to substitute into the standard insert

| Type | Include ONLY these essential facts |
|---|---|
| Injury / roster vacancy | Medical communication and who supplied it, whether an active 53-man roster vacancy *actually exists*, eligible replacements, special-teams and position depth consequences, who executes transaction |
| Trade approach | Requesting club, exact proposed assets, contract/cap implications if verified, offer validity, GM's view, any required franchise-QB consultation |
| Trade deadline | Exact league deadline, proposed live negotiations and their deadlines; if none, a short upcoming-window note, not an imaginary offer |
| Player extension | Player, original signed contract, eligibility date, remaining seasons, current football evaluation, sourced contract/cap range, agent interest only if known, GM's authority |
| Assistant contract nearing end | Assistant, actual expiry or unresolved expiry window, budget, renewal terms if offered, coaching assessment and successor implications |
| Other club wants your assistant | Club and role, exact permission rule for the season, Jacksonville season/playoff status, whether an in-force standing instruction already covers it |
| Coaching vacancy | Actual open job, confirmed or speculative interest distinguished, dates/permissions, whether your own candidacy is permitted; vacancy alone is not an interview request |
| Club offers YOU an extension | Written terms offered, owner/GM authority, existing contract, financial/authority changes, your accept/counter/decline deadline |
| Another club asks to talk to YOU | FOR YOUR INFORMATION while your standing answer is that you are not open to job offers: the requesting club, the date, and Shad Khan's answer (he alone decides: grant, refuse or ask for compensation). No imaginary in-season solicitation |
| Dismissal / head-coach contract expiry | Ownership notice or verified contract end, effective date, remaining guarantees/offset only from the signed contract, representation and actual offers. After dismissal route to the agent/career desk, not a Jaguars internal football briefing |

### Authoring requirements

- Bind to one event ID in the system metadata (do not print it on the coach-facing page) and one canonical source. The insert is a view of that event, never the owner of contract terms or transaction control.
- Every claim must be available to this coach through a legitimate channel at the event's date.
- An internal inquiry, an expression of interest, a formal offer, and a completed action are separate statuses.
- Keep the existing game or bye content beneath the insert without replacing it. Do not change frozen game decisions.
- Reissue under the one rule in the [event inserts design](../../../docs/event_inserts.md) (section A, item 6): a new event needing your answer, a dated finding that materially changes a pending choice, or an answer due before the next scheduled issue. Preserve the earlier issue as received.
