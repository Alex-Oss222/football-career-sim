# Assessing a player in the scheme

Use this method within phase and game reports, dated updates to an opening annual card, and actual [position battles](../position_battle_card_template.md). It supplies football reasoning, not another whole-player assessment or compulsory form. The [shared report instructions](../offseason_training/README.md) govern voice, evidence and the two annual assessments.

## Start with the job and the opportunity

Read the active playbook entry and the version actually taught. Explain where the player lines up, what he must recognize, what technique the assignment needs and who depends on him. Translate “scheme fit” into a usable responsibility. A depth label and a formation assignment are not interchangeable; a receiver's placement on the chart does not tell the reader which routes, motions or blocks he was asked to execute.

Establish the opportunity before drawing a comparison: classroom discussion, individual drill, group work, team period or game; teammates and opponent; support and help; situation; contact instruction; and material participation limits. Include exact counts only when recorded. Fewer reps, a narrower menu or a missing test limit the conclusion; they do not establish inability.

## Explain what the unit did together

Follow the connection that matters. A protection declaration affects the line, back and quarterback. Route spacing affects a teammate's window. A front or coverage check changes neighboring defenders' jobs. A specialist's result depends on the shared operation. Explain whether those responsibilities fitted together and what the player's work enabled or required from others.

Separate recognizing the assignment, communicating it, attempting the taught technique and winning the physical contest. Also consider the call, the coach's instruction and the help actually available. A rusher can win his isolated matchup and leave an escape lane; a completed pass does not certify sound protection. Credit ordinary correct work from observations, without inferring it from the result alone.

## Follow the evidence across the work

Give the current supported judgment, then the work that explains it. Compare with the relevant earlier observation: did the player retain the answer, handle a changed picture, show a new strength, repeat a problem or simply remain untested? Preserve evidence pointing in different directions. One successful repeat does not erase the original failure or establish consistency.

Describe the actual coaching response when it matters: a clearer cue, demonstration, film example, adjustment to the drill, different supporting player or a later opportunity. A correction is not required when the assignment was sound. Do not prescribe fixed weeks for footwork, months for hand placement or an automatic development arc. Later real-life success or failure supplies no private evidence.

For the next work, retain the [dated source, responsible coach and next test](../offseason_training/README.md#carry-the-work-forward). Explain what could change the interpretation. A proposed experiment, an approved rep arrangement and an awarded role remain distinct. A phase report can inform an annual-card update without another grade table.

## What each observer contributes

| Observer | Football question |
|---|---|
| Position coach | Did the player recognize his job and execute its technique? What instruction helped, and did the response last beyond the immediate repeat? |
| Coordinator | Did the assignments work together? What help or adjustment does this player or combination require, and what does that cost elsewhere? |
| Stone | Which combinations can the team trust for the situations it must play? Did the opportunity answer the intended question, and is the staff teaching a consistent answer? |
| Performance staff | What does documented physical work establish about the assigned workload? Is there evidence of fatigue, or only a technique or familiarity problem? |
| Medical staff | What participation is permitted? An independent restriction limits the opportunity; it is not an effort judgment. |

Use the actual names and duties for that season. Attribute recorded judgments and label staff-analysis reconstruction when interpreting evidence without an attributed statement. Never fill three perspectives by inventing a meeting, consensus, disagreement or Stone's preference. The [2014 scheme questions](../../../career/2014/04_Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Training_Camp/staff_plan.md#what-the-active-playbooks-ask-of-the-players) illustrate this method with that year's active books and staff.

## In a preseason game review

Assess the player's actual job and its effect on the unit, including both strengths and failures when the record supports them. Compare opening, relief and mixed groups using the teammates, opponent and situations they actually faced. A quiet assignment can matter without a box-score entry; missing individual evidence remains missing.

Explain what the game confirms, changes or leaves open from camp. Games test execution without resetting the script, while practice can supply combinations and situations that never arise in a game. Use both bodies of work. Keep the [game report](../preseason_output_template.md) focused on consequential findings; do not require an identical entry for everyone who dressed.

## Evidence appropriate to the phase

| Phase | What the recorded work can establish |
|---|---|
| Phase One | Classroom explanation and documented physical preparation. It cannot establish coached field timing or opposed football execution. |
| Phase Two | Unopposed alignment, movement, timing and separate-unit rehearsal. It cannot settle a physical matchup. |
| Rookie minicamp | Initial assignments, technique, movement and response to teaching within the actual invited group and permitted drills. A short sample leaves many jobs untested. |
| OTAs / mandatory minicamp | Recognition, communication, timing and spacing against non-contact opposition. Identification and physical execution remain separate questions. |
| Training camp | Retention and shared operation, plus the blocking, rush, release, fit and tackling work actually allowed and observed in each period. Opening non-contact sessions remain non-contact evidence. |
| Preseason | Game execution against the actual opponent, considered alongside practice exposure. The game may not present the situation the staff wanted to evaluate. |

Avoid “high football IQ,” “physical presence,” “versatile” or “struggled with the playbook” without the job and evidence that make the claim useful. State a provisional judgment directly, identify its basis and leave uncertainty where it belongs. No hidden score, preset rep share or automatic upgrade follows from this method.
