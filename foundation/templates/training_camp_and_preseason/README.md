# Training camp and preseason reports

Use these templates for the report in chat and the readable part of the saved output. The [shared report guidance](../offseason_training/README.md#write-the-football) governs connected unit narration, evidence, proportionate depth and follow-through. The [assessment method](player_assessment.md) explains how to interpret a player's work in the scheme.

| Report | Template | What the reader should learn |
|---|---|---|
| Camp practice or a block of practices | [Training camp](training_camp_report.md) | How the taught football held up at the actual pace and contact level, with the players and combinations used. |
| A played preseason game | [Preseason game](../preseason_output_template.md) | How the actual groups executed and communicated, who helped or struggled, and what the game added to the practice evidence. |
| Consolidated preseason turn | [Full preseason output](../preseason_output_template.md), with the [review guide](preseason_review.md) inside section 6 | What the combined work supports, which questions remain open and which roles were actually decided. |
| Findings about a player or contested spot | [Assessment method](player_assessment.md) and [battle card](../position_battle_card_template.md) | The assigned job, meaningful comparisons, staff interpretation and next useful test. |

## Before writing

Read the requested season's calendar, actual roster and participation instructions, Stone's [camp approach](../../../career/coaching_profiles/alex_stone_coaching_profile.md#how-he-wants-training-camp-run), the phase plan, completed work and relevant active-book passages. Check that year's staff responsibilities. Keep the format across seasons while rebuilding its content from the actual people, dates, taught menu and observations.

For camp, establish which work was on air, non-contact, controlled contact or live to the ground. Pads alone do not settle the last distinction. Report blocking, block defeat, protection, releases, fits, ball security and tackling only to the finish actually permitted and observed. Respect the quarterback's protection in the drill; a whistle is not a recorded sack, tackle or gain.

Stone expects eligible players to report and participate on their applicable dates, and players and coaches to meet appropriate physical standards. The [profile and plan](../../../career/coaching_profiles/alex_stone_coaching_profile.md#how-he-wants-training-camp-run) supply those expectations. Medical clearance, conditioning readiness and football execution remain separate findings; a reporting requirement supplies no test result. Mixing starters and reserves should answer a football question, not impose equal snap totals.

## Write the football, then the judgment

Use the shared method to explain the unit's performance through consequential work. Camp adds a comparison the spring could not provide: what changed when resistance, personnel or situations changed? Keep the familiar task and the new demand clear. Reinstallation after the summer gap can reveal a coaching ambiguity as well as a player's forgotten detail.

Show the actual exposure behind a comparison: the period, job, supporting personnel, opponent, help and coach involvement. A one-on-one, a team period and a scrimmage with coaches off the field answer different questions. Follow important issues across the practice block without writing a second whole account of every session. Carry the source, responsible coach and next test into the closing section using the [shared handoff guidance](../offseason_training/README.md#carry-the-work-forward).

## Use in chat and in the repository

Render the camp report directly in chat with its phase/team header, location, dates, actual practice count and work addressed. Distinguish days from sessions and padded from non-contact work when recorded. Length follows the work and evidence. Keep routine finance, coach-contract and draft tables out of practice reports.

Camp findings remain in `training_report.md`, with material dated updates to the existing opening annual cards and actual battle cards. Do not create phase `player_assessments.md` files. The [two-assessment lifecycle](../offseason_training/README.md#use-in-the-llm-chatbox) and [event-record instructions](../phase_record_template.md) apply. A review never advances the clock or supplies missing results.

Preseason games retain the complete eight-section [preseason output](../preseason_output_template.md), with the Report to render directly beneath the game narrative in section 4. Keep preparation, coach status, both media sessions, generated box scores, coaching takeaways, personnel and closure. The [consolidated review](preseason_review.md) supplies section 6 rather than replacing that layout. Regular-season and postseason turns retain [their complete format](../regular_season_output_template.md). Follow the existing bulk workflow, material-decision pauses and game release requirements.

Each played game keeps its own output and generated box score. The consolidated review links those sources and the role-decision records; it does not duplicate every game story or merge preseason and regular-season statistics. Compare practice and game evidence by the questions and opportunities they supplied, not a blanket rule that either always wins.
