# Consolidated preseason review template

Use the [report instructions](README.md), the individual [game format](preseason_game_report.md) and the [assessment method](player_assessment.md). This is the reader-facing bulk preseason report, not a replacement for each game's output. Report only the games and practices reached before a required pause. An unfinished preseason remains unfinished.

## Report to render

# [Team] | Camp and preseason review | [Evidence-through date]

[Lead with what the staff can now trust and the largest remaining football problem. Establish whether this is an interim review or the completed preseason.]

## Games and the questions they answered

| Game and linked report | Result | Material football finding | What remains open |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Played game] | [Receipt result] | [Supported finding tied to a camp question] | [Actual unresolved work] |

[Use only actual games. Link their generated box scores; do not duplicate every game story. Follow the table with a connected account of the team's play across the completed period: what carried the group, what repeatedly stopped it, and which game evidence changed the assessment. Describe the change without forcing steady improvement. The table cannot replace the narrative or named-player review in a bulk chat turn.]

## The offense the players can run

[Explain the dependable formations, personnel combinations, run/protection answers and passing relationships shown in the work. Include the actual opening, relief and mixed groups rather than using the starting quarterback's results as the offense's entire assessment. Identify the support a player or package still requires and the cost elsewhere. Describe the recurrent failures as well as the reliable work, with representative game evidence. Distinguish the whole active book from the menu prepared for games.]

## The defense the players can execute

[Explain front/fit reliability, rush and contain, coverage/help, tackling, motion exchanges and replacement communication. Say which personnel actually supported the calls, where a matchup needed a different answer and whether that answer was tested. Include reserve combinations and contributions that do not appear as sacks or interceptions when the game evidence establishes them.]

## Special teams and game operation

[Assess the actual specialist operation, coverage/return work, substitutions, clock management and emergency jobs. Treat untested work as untested. Identify a coaching or communication correction alongside player work where appropriate.]

## Communication across the games

[Explain whether the sideline, huddle and shared calls continued to work as personnel and situations changed. Identify a correction that held, a recurring problem or an unanswered test from the actual evidence. Distinguish communication from physical execution and avoid inferring clean operation from wins or few penalties. Consider the coaches' instruction and substitutions alongside the players' declarations and exchanges.]

## Position decisions and remaining work

[Give named assessments of the players whose work established something useful, exposed a limitation or changed an open comparison. Include meaningful strengths and failures in the same assessment where supported. Starters receive scrutiny as well as players competing for places; a roster bubble is not the only reason to review a performance. Compare the combined practice and game evidence, including contrary evidence and unequal opportunities.]

[State Stone's decisions only when made. Keep the actual general manager's transactions and roster deadlines in their proper records; a staff recommendation is not a roster place. Carry each material correction and its responsible coach into the next phase instead of closing every issue at cutdown. A quiet game or narrow opportunity cannot settle a question the player was never asked to answer.]
