# Individual position battle: decision and result

**Design note (not a coach-facing page):** This is the required result shape for **one specific genuine competition**, held at that contest's existing dated card owner, not a generic all-battles result. Example route: `career/Y/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Position_Battles/Offense/Guard/Left_Guard.md`. Its dated closure can be added to the same card only when the actual Stone decision has occurred. The card remains the one readable individual result and historical account; do not create a second near-identical `Battle_Result_...md` unless an existing owner cannot represent this actual independently closed decision. Its closure gets one unique `event-record` marker at its own canonical event owner in the career period where it happened. No duplicate `event-record` for this same resolved issue in `Depth_Chart.md` or `Roster_Decisions/roster_decisions.md`.

The `Depth_Chart.md` phase result and the team's current `Team_Operations/Team/Depth_Chart/depth_chart.md` derive from the same role decision and **link to every individual closure**. The battle card is the detailed decision-quality record, not another competing editable depth chart.

**Special cases:**

- A split role or genuinely provisional choice can be an actual Stone decision without falsely closing every related competition.
- If the question is decided solely because Caldwell releases a candidate, name that causal **sequence** accurately, without inventing superior practice evidence; his roster result is separately owned.
- If the contest ends without any decision or its job no longer exists, document why and when. Do not fabricate a "winner" or imply the old incumbent proved better.
- Six independently settled contests on August 30 generate **six individual card closures**, even if the current chart is issued once. A rejected trade or failed hiring search noticed during camp likewise produces its *own* individual dated result at the transaction or staff owner, not inside the battle file.
