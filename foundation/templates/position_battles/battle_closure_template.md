# Battle closure: the closing section of a position battle card

**Design status:** Adopted October 20, 2026 from the user's upload and corrected after the read-only check. A battle closes inside its own card, in the existing [position battle card template](../position_battle_card_template.md)'s form, as all six 2014 cards did ([2014 position battles](../../../career/2014/Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Position_Battles/README.md)). This page is the closure section that the build folds into that template; it is not a second card layout, not a separate result page and not an event result. Candidate names stay across the top of the card's table. Do not display these instructions on a card.

**Who owns what.** Stone's My Depth Calls edition is the record of his decision. Its applied record carries the change into the depth data with one dated entry in the depth chart's update log, and that applied set of depth calls carries the one line in the year's annual record, on the camp index. The card is the comparison record: its closure links his page and is not a second owner of the decision. The Depth Chart edition issued that day links the card. A roster move that bears on the battle (a release, signing or claim) has its own dated result page in the roster or free-agency owner's folder, built on the [event result template](../event_inserts/event_result_template.md) when it was an inserted event, and the closure links it.

## Closure lines to render

The three lines below are written into the card when Stone's applied depth calls decide the spot, or when the job ends. Every card still open closes with his season depth chart at the reduction to 53, at the latest.

**In the card's header:**

**Competition:** [Decided [date] / Decided [date] (conditional alternative closed, never triggered) / Ended without a decision [date]]

**At the head of "What separates the candidates":**

**Decision, [date] ([his depth calls], linked to the My Depth Calls edition):** [Who holds the spot and the next player in the order, with the defined duties; any condition Stone attached, in his recorded words or an attributed paraphrase of them; the recorded reason, or the plain statement that the record did not separate the candidates and the call is his judgment from the whole body of work; any link to a related roster result.] The rows above are preserved as dated history; this card is resolved and off the open list.

**At the head of "Next work and decision":**

**Resolved [date].** The decision above is recorded in [his depth calls] and on the [depth chart]; the text below is the card's history before the decision and names no further test.

> FILL-IN GUIDE (remove from the rendered card): Write the decision paragraph only from Stone's applied answer. Do not manufacture quotations, reasons he did not give, or an outcome-based grade; when he gave no reason beyond his judgment, say so, and credit any other point to the card row it comes from. Do not invent technical film details: an unassigned sack is not automatically the lineman's fault. Keep the card's earlier dated rows and its earlier "After ..." paragraphs exactly as written; the closure is added above them, never in place of them. Remove the closed card from the position battles index's open list and list it under the dated history.

## Statuses a card may show

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| Open | The contest is live; no applied depth call has decided it |
| Conditional alternative | A named alternative is held against a stated condition |
| Decided [date] | Stone's applied depth calls decided the spot on that date |
| Ended without a decision [date] | The job itself no longer exists, or the contest closed with no choice between the candidates |

Technical states may exist in the data. No status code appears on a reader page.

## Special cases

- A split role or genuinely provisional choice can be an actual Stone decision without falsely closing every related competition. Close only the card his answer decides.
- If the question is decided because the general manager releases a candidate, name that sequence accurately, without inventing superior practice evidence, and link the release's own dated result; the roster decision is separately owned, as the 2014 long-snapper card does.
- If the contest ends without any decision or its job no longer exists, write "Ended without a decision [date]" and say why. Do not fabricate a "winner" or imply the old incumbent proved better.
- Six contests settled by one set of depth calls give six card closures, one Depth Chart edition and one line in the annual record. A rejected trade or failed hiring search noticed during camp produces its *own* dated result page at the trade or staff owner, built on the event result template, never inside the battle card.
- A later change to a decided role is a new dated instruction on a new My Depth Calls edition, or after the cut to 53 an ordinary in-season depth decision. The decided card keeps its closure; a reopened job, if genuinely contested again, gets a new dated entry, not a rewrite of the old one.
