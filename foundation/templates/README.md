# Reports, assessments and teaching templates

Choose the template for the work actually completed. The same template supplies the chat response and the saved report; writing a second abbreviated account is not required. Replace its instructions with the football account, preserve the section order, and give the important findings the space they need. A short period with little evidence does not need padding. Game turns also follow the [game-output workflow](../../docs/game_output_workflow.md): retain the layouts and generated full statistics, author the football and media from established evidence, and review those separately. Prompts do not authorize invented activity or repeated filler.

All destinations below are relative to `career/<year>/` for 2014 onward. The season calendar controls dates and overlap; the folder order does not move rookie camp ahead of the draft. Create filled files when their work occurs, not empty future reports.

Keep two full annual player assessments. Preserve the opening baseline, add dated observations during the season and update statistics from closed receipts. The final assessment compares the completed season with that baseline. An exit interview records actual discussion and supplies evidence to that assessment. The [2013 player-sheet format](player_sheet_template.md) remains historical.

The regular-season output has unnumbered Coach info followed by seven sections, with Game in section 3. The preseason output has eight sections, with Game in section 4. Both put **Report to render** directly below the game narrative. Keep the surrounding preparation, media, personnel and closure material, and generate statistics from the recorded game.

## Carryover

End each substantive report with the work that continues: the football question, latest evidence, responsible coach and next legal opportunity. Link the existing plan, packet, medical record or decision that owns it. Do not create a competing carryover ledger. A completed task can close; an untested question remains open without an invented flaw or promised improvement.

At season close, use the existing `Season_Review/season_handoff.json` and [season continuity procedure](../../docs/season_structure.md). Previous final assessments inform new opening cards. Keep historical reports, resolved battle cards and old-season statistics in their original season; carry unfinished work by reviewed reference. Reconcile current contracts, medical permissions, staff authority and draft rights before they become next-season facts.
