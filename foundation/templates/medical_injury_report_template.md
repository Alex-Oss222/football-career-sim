# Medical incident report template

Create one dated incident record when an actual injury or medical restriction is established. Keep all later updates and clearance with that incident, including when it crosses a season boundary. A new injury is a new incident; an update is not. A routine physical or clearance without a new injury belongs in medical history. Do not create an empty injury card for every player.

Use the existing licensed photo registry if a picture helps identify the player; omit it when unavailable. The file reports communicated medical information in this simulation, not a claim about the real person's health. No invented diagnosis, severity, recovery estimate or clearance.

## Current medical information

- Injury or diagnosis: [Established wording, or evaluation pending]
- Practice participation: [Full, limited, did not participate, or not applicable]
- Game designation: [The season's official designation, or no designation issued]
- Roster designation: [Current roster/reserve category, linked to its owner]
- Communicated restrictions: [Permitted work and specific limits]
- Clearance: [Actual medical instruction and authority, or not established]
- Expected return: [Communicated estimate and its date, or unknown]
- Next medical review: [Actual appointment or stated review condition, if recorded]

For 2014, official game designations include Probable, Questionable, Doubtful and Out. Keep an internal availability description separate from an issued game designation. A forecast does not grant clearance. Do not infer a roster designation from whether the player practiced.
