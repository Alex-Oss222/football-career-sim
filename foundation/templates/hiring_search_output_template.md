# Hiring-search (candidacy) output template

**Status:** New 2026-09-17, filling a gap the user identified before starting play: Document 3 §10.2 describes what a hiring-search turn must contain, but never had a formal output shape the way the season/offseason turns do. This template is that shape, used only while Stone is a candidate (not yet hired). Once he's hired, `season_output_template.md` and `offseason_output_template.md` take over.

**Input contract (applies to every turn in this project, not just hiring):** there is no form to fill out. Document 1 §6.3/§16 bans closed-choice menus. At each decision point below, the output asks one specific, answerable question — you respond in your own words, and that answer is the input. Nothing is parsed against a rigid schema.

**The three turn types**, per Document 3 §10.2 — a full search should resolve in roughly 3-5 of these total, not one per team:

---

## 1. Opportunity turn (one, at the start of a search)

```
**[Coach Name] | Head-Coaching Candidate | [Date]**
**Phase:** Coaching search window | **Teams with real openings:** [N]

## The landscape

For each real opening actually in play for Stone (per Document 3 §1.4's established list, or any team that plausibly reaches out): team, why the job opened, decision-maker(s), what's known about the competing field, and the realistic timeline (per Document 3 §10.1's calendar gating).

## Your call

[The specific question: which of these do you want to pursue, and in what order/priority? Any conditions, non-negotiables, or approach you want on record before outreach begins?]
```

Routine outreach, permission requests, and scheduling that follow from your answer happen without a separate turn (Document 1 §3.2) and are only summarized in the next turn.

## 2. Market-update turn (as many as genuine developments require — usually 1-2)

```
**[Coach Name] | Head-Coaching Candidate | [Date]**
**Phase:** Coaching search window | **Teams still in play:** [N]

## What's happened

Batched since the last turn: interviews completed and how they went, teams that filled their jobs with someone else (with real market movement — see library/2013_coaching_market.md for the actual carousel), any new information about a team's situation, and any genuine complication (a leak, a competing bid, a rescinded permission).

## Your call

[Only if a genuine branch actually exists: e.g., a team wants a second interview, a rival bid surfaced, or a team asks a direct question needing an answer. If nothing requires your input, this section is omitted and the turn is read-only.]
```

## 3. Offer turn (one per real offer received)

```
**[Coach Name] | Head-Coaching Candidate | [Date]**
**Team:** [Team] | **Terms offered:** [What's actually on the table]

## The offer

Contract terms, authority/staffing commitments made or implied, timeline to respond, and anything else a real offer would include.

## Your call

Accept, negotiate (state what you want changed), or decline. If declined or if talks fail, the search continues with whatever teams remain in play.
```

---

Once an offer is accepted, the next turn is the season/offseason template pair, not this one — Document 3 §12's initialization gate becomes live at that point.
