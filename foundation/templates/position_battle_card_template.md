# Position battle card template

Create a card only for an identifiable contest during offseason training, training camp or preseason: a named spot, at least two actual candidates and a documented opportunity to compete. An open roster place or an unassigned player alone does not establish a battle. Do not create empty cards for every position, force a challenger, assign equal repetitions or infer a winner.

Store one Markdown card per spot under `career/<year>/04_Training_Camp_and_Preseason/Position_Battles/<Offense|Defense|Special_Teams>/<Position>/<Spot>.md`. Show only groups and positions with real cards in the navigation. A spring contest uses the same card and links its spring evidence; do not create a second spring copy. Close an actual decision in the roster/depth owner, preserve the card as dated preseason history, and remove it from the open-battles list. Do not generate a new card after preseason under this workflow.

Use the exact established role. WR1/WR2 are depth roles; X, Z, slot and the playbook's Y are assignments, not interchangeable labels. TE1 and TE2 need separate cards only when each is actually contested. One player may compete for two roles; each card explains that role's demands without copying the whole player profile.
